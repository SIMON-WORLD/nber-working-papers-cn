What Happens When Employers Can No Longer Discriminate in Job Ads?

当雇主无法再在招聘广告中歧视时会发生什么？

Peter J. Kuhn, Kailing Shen

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动

Abstract

When employers’ explicit gender requests were unexpectedly removed from a Chinese job board overnight, pools of successful applicants became more integrated: women’s (men’s) share of call-backs to jobs that had requested men (women) rose by 63 (146) percent. The removal ‘worked’ in this sense because it generated a large increase in gender-mismatched applications, and because those applications were treated surprisingly well by employers. The removal had little or no effect on aggregate matching frictions. The job titles that were integrated however, were not the most gendered ones, and were disproportionately lower-wage jobs.

中文摘要

当中国某招聘网站一夜之间意外移除雇主的明确性别要求后，成功申请者的群体变得更加融合：在原本要求男性的职位中，女性获得回应的比例上升了63%；在原本要求女性的职位中，男性获得回应的比例上升了146%。从这个意义上说，移除性别要求之所以“有效”，是因为它大幅增加了性别不匹配的申请数量，并且这些申请得到了雇主出人意料的良好对待。移除性别要求对总体匹配摩擦几乎没有影响。然而，实现融合的职位并非性别化最严重的那些，而且不成比例地集中在低薪职位上。

Optimal Tariffs and Trade Policy Formation: U.S. Evidence from the Smoot-Hawley Era

最优关税与贸易政策形成：来自斯穆特-霍利时代的美国证据

Douglas A. Irwin, Anson Soderbery

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

This paper examines the political economy of U.S. trade policy around the time of the Smoot-Hawley tariff of 1930, a period when policy was unconstrained by trade agreements. We consider a model of politically-optimal trade policy for a large country that can influence its terms of trade and where workers and firms lobby for protection. The predictions of the model hinge on import demand and export supply elasticities, which we estimate using detailed U.S. import data from 1927-35, as well as industry lobbying data. We find that tariff levels are largely determined by firm lobbies, but about about 5 percentage points of the tariffs are explained by terms of trade considerations. Decomposing the politically-optimal tariff in 1931 reveals an intensification of demand for protection by workers in the Smoot-Hawley tariff.

中文摘要

本文考察了1930年斯穆特-霍利关税法案前后美国贸易政策的政治经济学，这一时期政策不受贸易协定的约束。我们考虑了一个大国在可以影响其贸易条件且工人和企业游说寻求保护的情况下的政治最优贸易政策模型。该模型的预测取决于进口需求和出口供给弹性，我们利用1927-1935年间详细的美国进口数据以及行业游说数据对这些弹性进行了估计。我们发现，关税水平主要由企业游说决定，但大约5个百分点的关税可由贸易条件因素解释。对1931年政治最优关税的分解显示，斯穆特-霍利关税中工人对保护的需求有所加剧。

“Unusual, Unstable, Complicated, Unreliable and Temporary” Reinterpreting the Ebb and Flow of Globalization

“异常、不稳定、复杂、不可靠且暂时”：重新解读全球化的潮起潮落

Michael D. Bordo, Catherine R. Schenk

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

In 1919, John Maynard Keynes wrote his famous tract The Economic Consequences of the Peace. In that work, he anticipated the collapse of the first era of globalization that began in the mid-nineteenth century. He admonished the short-sighted assumption that these years of relative peace and prosperity for many was a permanent norm, interrupted only briefly by the Great War. The diplomatic failures, lapses in leadership, and promotion of narrow interests and vision outlined by Keynes underpinned his prediction of a backslash of economic nationalism, trade protectionism, and recession.\nThe paper revisits the turning points in the evolution of the global economic system since 1919 by focusing primarily on the evolution of the international monetary system and policy cooperation/coordination. We identify three disruptions and examine how each prompted a change in the underlying ideology about how the international monetary system should organize: World War I, Bretton Woods, 1970s Great Inflation and Managed Floating. Each turning point was characterized by different forms and institutions of cooperation, how rules (either explicit or implicit) were designed and implemented, and the crucial importance of the historical context.

中文摘要

1919年，约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯撰写了其著名著作《和平的经济后果》。在该书中，他预见了始于19世纪中叶的第一次全球化时代的崩溃。他告诫人们，那种认为对许多人而言相对和平与繁荣的岁月是永久常态、仅被世界大战短暂打断的短视假设是错误的。凯恩斯所概述的外交失败、领导力缺失以及狭隘利益与视野的鼓吹，支撑了他对经济民族主义、贸易保护主义和衰退反弹的预测。本文通过主要关注国际货币体系演变以及政策合作/协调，重新审视了1919年以来全球经济体系演变的转折点。我们识别出三次中断，并考察了每次中断如何促使关于国际货币体系应如何组织的潜在意识形态发生变化：第一次世界大战、布雷顿森林体系、1970年代的大通胀与管理浮动。每个转折点都以不同形式的合作与制度、规则（无论是显性还是隐性）的设计与实施方式，以及历史背景的至关重要性为特征。

Is Online Education Working?

在线教育有效吗？

Duha Tore Altindag, Elif S. Filiz, Erdal Tekin

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This study examines the impact of instruction modality on student learning outcomes, with a focus on disparities observed pre- and post-pandemic. Using administrative data from a public university spanning seven pre-pandemic and five post-pandemic semesters, the analysis controls for endogenous sorting using fixed effects. The findings suggest that face-to-face (FtF) instruction results in better student performance, such as higher grades and a lower withdrawal rate. Additionally, students with greater exposure to FtF instruction are less likely to repeat courses, more likely to graduate on time, and achieve higher GPAs. The study also shows that the FtF advantage has been decreasing over time, and the differences are smaller post-pandemic. The results are consistent across student and instructor characteristics, except for Honors and graduate students, where the FtF advantage is either smaller or statistically insignificant.

中文摘要

本研究考察了教学方式对学生学习成果的影响，重点关注疫情前后观察到的差异。利用一所公立大学涵盖七个疫情前学期和五个疫情后学期的行政数据，分析通过固定效应控制了内生性选择。研究结果表明，面对面教学能带来更好的学生表现，例如更高的成绩和更低的退课率。此外，接触更多面对面教学的学生更不容易重修课程，更有可能按时毕业，并获得更高的平均绩点。研究还显示，面对面教学的优势随时间推移而减小，且疫情后差异更小。除荣誉学生和研究生外，这一结果在不同学生和教师特征中保持一致，对于这些群体，面对面教学的优势要么较小，要么在统计上不显著。

Consistent Evidence on Duration Dependence of Price Changes

关于价格变动持续时间依赖性的稳健证据

Fernando E. Alvarez, Katarína Borovičková, Robert Shimer

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop an estimator and tests of a discrete time mixed proportional hazard (MPH) model of duration with unobserved heterogeneity. We allow for competing risks, observable characteristics, and censoring, and we use linear GMM, making estimation and inference straightforward. With repeated spell data, our estimator is consistent and robust to the unknown shape of the frailty distribution. We apply our estimator to the duration of price spells in weekly store data from IRI. We find substantial unobserved heterogeneity, accounting for a large fraction of the decrease in the Kaplan-Meier hazard with elapsed duration. Still, we show that the estimated baseline hazard rate is decreasing and a homogeneous firm model can accurately capture the response of the economy to a monetary policy shock even if there is significant strategic complementarity in pricing. Using competing risks and spell-specific observable characteristics, we separately estimate the model for regular and temporary price changes and find that the MPH structure describes regular price changes better than temporary ones.

中文摘要

我们开发了一个离散时间混合比例风险模型的估计量和检验方法，该模型考虑了未观测异质性。我们允许竞争风险、可观测特征和删失，并使用线性广义矩估计，使估计和推断变得简单。利用重复持续期数据，我们的估计量是一致的，并且对脆弱性分布的未知形状具有稳健性。我们将该估计量应用于IRI提供的每周商店数据中价格持续期的分析。我们发现存在显著的未观测异质性，这解释了卡普兰-迈耶风险率随持续期延长而下降的很大一部分。尽管如此，我们表明估计的基准风险率是递减的，并且一个同质企业模型可以准确捕捉经济对货币政策冲击的反应，即使定价中存在显著的策略互补性。利用竞争风险和持续期特定的可观测特征，我们分别估计了常规价格变动和临时价格变动的模型，发现混合比例风险结构对常规价格变动的描述优于临时价格变动。

Fairness in Incomplete Information Bargaining: Theory and Widespread Evidence from the Field

不完全信息谈判中的公平性：理论与来自实地的大范围证据

Daniel Keniston, Bradley J. Larsen, Shengwu Li, J.J. Prescott, Bernardo S. Silveira, Chuan Yu

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper uses detailed data on sequential offers from seven vastly different real-world bargaining settings to document a robust pattern: agents favor offers that split the difference between the two most recent offers on the table. Our settings include negotiations for used cars, insurance injury claims, a TV game show, auto rickshaw rides, housing, international trade tariffs, and online retail. We demonstrate that this pattern can arise in a perfect Bayesian equilibrium of an alternating-offer game with two-sided incomplete information, but this equilibrium is far from unique. We then provide a robust-inference argument to explain why agents may view the two most recent offers as corresponding to the potential surplus. Split-the-difference offers under this weaker, robust inference can then be viewed as fair. We present a number of other patterns in each data setting that point to split-the-difference offers as a strong social norm, whether in high-stakes or low-stakes negotiations.

中文摘要

本文利用来自七个截然不同的现实世界谈判场景中关于连续报价的详细数据，记录了一个稳健的模式：参与者倾向于选择平分桌上最近两个报价之间差额的报价。我们的场景包括二手车谈判、保险伤害索赔、电视游戏节目、自动人力车出行、住房、国际贸易关税和在线零售。我们证明，这种模式可以在一个具有双边不完全信息的交替出价博弈的完美贝叶斯均衡中出现，但该均衡远非唯一。然后，我们提供了一个稳健推断的论点，来解释为什么参与者可能将最近的两个报价视为对应潜在剩余。在这种较弱的稳健推断下，平分差额的报价可以被视为公平的。我们在每个数据场景中呈现了许多其他模式，这些模式表明，无论是在高 stakes 还是低 stakes 的谈判中，平分差额的报价都是一种强大的社会规范。

Economic Consequences of Hospital Closures

医院关闭的经济后果

Diane E. Alexander, Michael R. Richards

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Hospitals anchor much of US health care and receive a third of all medical spending, including various subsidies. Nevertheless, some become insolvent and exit the market. Research has documented subsequent access problems; however, less is understood about broader implications. We examine over 100 rural hospital closures spanning 2005-2017 to quantify the effects on the local economy. We find sharp and persistent reductions in employment, but these localize to health care occupations and are more impactful in areas experiencing complete closures. Aggregate consumer financial health and housing markets appear unaffected by the shuttering of a rural hospital.

中文摘要

医院是美国医疗保健的支柱，并接收了包括各种补贴在内的所有医疗支出的三分之一。尽管如此，一些医院仍会资不抵债并退出市场。研究已记录了随后的就医可及性问题；然而，对其更广泛影响的理解较少。我们考察了2005年至2017年间超过100家农村医院关闭的情况，以量化其对当地经济的影响。我们发现就业出现急剧且持续的减少，但这些影响局限于医疗保健职业，并且在经历完全关闭的地区影响更大。总体消费者金融健康和住房市场似乎未受农村医院关闭的影响。

No. 8论文详情NBER w29109

Intangibles, Markups, and the Measurement of Productivity Growth

无形资产、加成率与生产率增长的度量

Nicolas Crouzet, Janice C. Eberly

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

In recent years, measured TFP growth in the US has declined. We argue that two forces contributed to this decline: the mismeasurement of intangible capital, and rising markups. Markups affect input shares, while intangibles omitted from measures of investment affect measured capital growth, each potentially generating downward bias in measured TFP growth. Most importantly, when both forces are simultaneously present, their effects reinforce each other and amplify the downward bias in measured TFP growth. Using input-output data, we estimate that this mechanism could account for one-third to two-thirds of the decline in measured TFP growth.

中文摘要

近年来，美国测算的全要素生产率增长有所下降。我们认为有两种力量导致了这种下降：无形资本衡量的偏差，以及不断上升的加成率。加成率影响投入份额，而从投资衡量中遗漏的无形资产则影响测得的资本增长，每种力量都可能使测得的全要素生产率增长产生向下偏差。最重要的是，当这两种力量同时存在时，它们的效果会相互加强，并放大测得的全要素生产率增长的向下偏差。利用投入产出数据，我们估计这一机制可能解释了测得的全要素生产率增长下降的三分之一到三分之二。

Racial Disparities in the Health Effects from Air Pollution: Evidence from Ports

空气污染健康影响的种族差异：来自港口的证据

Kenneth Gillingham, Pei Huang

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This study examines the uneven effects of air pollution from maritime ports on physical and mental health across racial groups. We exploit quasi-random variation in vessels in port from weather events far out in the ocean to estimate how port traffic influences air pollution and human health. We find that one additional vessel in a port over a year leads to 3.0 hospital visits per thousand Black residents within 25 miles of the port and only 1.0 per thousand for whites. We assess a port-related environmental regulation and show that the policy can help alleviate racial inequalities in health outcomes.
