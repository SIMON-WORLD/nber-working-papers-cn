The Economic Case for Global Vaccinations: An Epidemiological Model with International Production Networks

全球疫苗接种的经济理由：一个具有国际生产网络的流行病学模型

Cem Çakmaklı, Selva Demiralp, Ṣebnem Kalemli-Özcan, Sevcan Yeşiltaş, Muhammed A. Yıldırım

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We present a global general equilibrium model that incorporates input-output linkages across multiple sectors and countries to analyze the impact of pandemic-related labor supply shocks. These shocks vary across industries due to differences in how occupations are affected by the disease, influenced by factors such as contact intensity and the feasibility of remote work. Using the model, we quantify the economic rationale for global vaccinations. Given empirically relevant trade and production elasticities, where labor and imported intermediate inputs complement each other, we examine several scenarios. If wealthy countries vaccinate only their own populations, global output could decline by nearly 1% compared to pre-pandemic levels. In contrast, investing in global vaccinations could yield a 178% return on investment for wealthy nations and potentially halve the global output decline. Vaccinating low-income countries could mitigate economic impacts on wealthy nations through sectoral trade and production linkages, significantly reducing losses by 70% without compromising rich countries own vaccination efforts.

中文摘要

我们提出了一个全球一般均衡模型，该模型纳入了多个部门和国家的投入产出联系，以分析疫情相关的劳动力供给冲击。这些冲击因行业而异，原因是不同职业受疾病影响的程度不同，这受到接触强度和远程工作可行性等因素的影响。利用该模型，我们量化了全球疫苗接种的经济理由。在具有经验相关性的贸易和生产弹性（即劳动力和进口中间投入品相互补充）的情况下，我们考察了几种情景。如果富裕国家仅为本国人口接种疫苗，全球产出可能比疫情前水平下降近1%。相比之下，投资于全球疫苗接种可为富裕国家带来178%的投资回报，并可能使全球产出下降幅度减半。为低收入国家接种疫苗可以通过部门贸易和生产联系减轻对富裕国家的经济影响，在不影响富裕国家自身疫苗接种努力的情况下，将损失显著减少70%。

Housing Precarity & the COVID-19 Pandemic: Impacts of Utility Disconnection and Eviction Moratoria on Infections and Deaths Across US Counties

住房不稳定与COVID-19疫情：断水断电和驱逐禁令对美国各县感染和死亡的影响

Kay Jowers, Christopher Timmins, Nrupen Bhavsar, Qihui Hu, Julia Marshall

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

The COVID-19 pandemic has necessitated the adoption of a number of policies that aim to reduce the spread of the disease by promoting housing stability. Housing precarity, which includes both the risk of eviction and utility disconnections or shut-offs, reduces a person’s ability to abide by social distancing orders and comply with hygiene recommendations. Our analysis quantifies the impact of these various economic policies on COVID-19 infection and death rates using panel regression techniques to control for a variety of potential confounders. We find that policies that limit evictions are found to reduce COVID-19 infections by 3.8% and reduce deaths by 11%. Moratoria on utility disconnections reduce COVID-19 infections by 4.4% and mortality rates by 7.4%. Had such policies been in place across all counties (i.e., adopted as federal policy) from early March 2020 through the end of November 2020, our estimated counterfactuals show that policies that limit evictions could have reduced COVID-19 infections by 14.2% and deaths by 40.7%. For moratoria on utility disconnections, COVID-19 infections rates could have been reduced by 8.7% and deaths by 14.8%. Housing precarity policies that prevent eviction and utility disconnections have been effective mechanisms for decreasing both COVID-19 infections and deaths.

中文摘要

COVID-19疫情促使采取了一系列旨在通过促进住房稳定来减少疾病传播的政策。住房不稳定，包括被驱逐以及公用事业断水断电或停供的风险，会降低个人遵守社交距离命令和遵循卫生建议的能力。我们的分析利用面板回归技术控制各种潜在混杂因素，量化了这些不同经济政策对COVID-19感染率和死亡率的影响。我们发现，限制驱逐的政策使COVID-19感染率降低3.8%，死亡率降低11%。断水断电禁令使COVID-19感染率降低4.4%，死亡率降低7.4%。如果这些政策从2020年3月初到2020年11月底在所有县实施（即作为联邦政策采纳），我们的反事实估计显示，限制驱逐的政策本可使COVID-19感染率降低14.2%，死亡率降低40.7%。对于断水断电禁令，COVID-19感染率本可降低8.7%，死亡率降低14.8%。防止驱逐和断水断电的住房不稳定政策是降低COVID-19感染和死亡的有效机制。

A Call for Structured Ethics Appendices in Social Science Papers

呼吁在社会科学论文中设立结构化伦理附录

Edward Asiedu, Dean Karlan, Monica P. Lambon-Quayefio, Christopher R. Udry

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Ethics in social science experimentation and data collection are often discussed but rarely articulated in writing as part of research outputs. Although papers typically reference human subjects research approvals from relevant institutional review boards, most recognize that such boards do not carry out comprehensive ethical assessments. We propose a structured ethics appendix to provide details on the following: policy equipoise, role of the researcher, potential harms to participants and nonparticipants, conflicts of interest, intellectual freedom, feedback to participants, and foreseeable misuse of research results. We discuss each of these, and some of the norms and challenging situations of each. We believe that discussing such issues explicitly in appendices of papers, even if briefly, will serve two purposes: more complete communication of ethics can improve discussions of papers and can clarify and improve the norms themselves.

中文摘要

社会科学实验和数据收集中的伦理问题经常被讨论，但很少在研究产出中以书面形式明确阐述。尽管论文通常会提及相关机构审查委员会对涉及人类受试者研究的批准，但大多数人都认识到，这些委员会并不进行全面的伦理评估。我们提议设立一个结构化的伦理附录，以提供以下方面的详细信息：政策均衡、研究者的角色、对参与者和非参与者的潜在伤害、利益冲突、学术自由、对参与者的反馈以及研究结果的可预见滥用。我们逐一讨论了这些问题，以及其中一些规范和具有挑战性的情况。我们相信，在论文附录中明确讨论这些问题，即使只是简要地讨论，也将服务于两个目的：更完整的伦理沟通可以改善对论文的讨论，并可以澄清和改进规范本身。

COVID-19 and Global Income Inequality

COVID-19与全球收入不平等

Angus Deaton

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 印度、美国

Abstract

There is a widespread belief that the COVID-19 pandemic has increased global income inequality, reducing per capita incomes by more in poor countries than in rich. This supposition is reasonable but false. Rich countries have experienced more deaths per head than have poor countries; their better health systems, higher incomes, more capable governments and better preparedness notwithstanding. The US did worse than some rich countries, but better than several others. Countries with more deaths saw larger declines in income. There was thus not only no trade-off between lives and income; fewer deaths meant more income. As a result, per capita incomes fell by more in higher-income countries. Country by country, international income inequality decreased. When countries are weighted by population, international income inequality increased, more in line with the original intuition. This was largely because Indian incomes fell, and because the disequalizing effect of declining Indian incomes was not offset by rising incomes in China, which is no longer a globally poor country. That these findings are a result of the pandemic is supported by comparing global inequality using IMF forecasts in October 2019 and October 2020.

中文摘要

人们普遍认为，COVID-19疫情加剧了全球收入不平等，使贫困国家的人均收入下降幅度大于富裕国家。这种假设看似合理，但却是错误的。富裕国家的人均死亡人数高于贫困国家；尽管它们拥有更好的卫生系统、更高的收入、更有能力的政府和更充分的准备。美国的表现比一些富裕国家差，但比其他几个国家好。死亡人数更多的国家收入下降幅度更大。因此，生命与收入之间不仅没有权衡取舍；更少的死亡意味着更多的收入。结果，高收入国家的人均收入下降幅度更大。按国家来看，国际收入不平等程度下降了。当按人口加权国家时，国际收入不平等程度上升，这更符合最初的直觉。这主要是因为印度收入下降，并且印度收入下降的不平等加剧效应未被中国（已不再是全球贫困国家）收入上升所抵消。通过比较国际货币基金组织2019年10月和2020年10月的预测得出的全球不平等，支持了这些发现是疫情结果的观点。

Trade and Domestic Distortions: the Case of Informality

贸易与国内扭曲：非正规经济案例

Rafael Dix-Carneiro, Pinelopi K. Goldberg, Costas Meghir, Gabriel Ulyssea

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We examine the effects of international trade in the presence of a set of domestic distortions giving rise to informality, a prevalent phenomenon in developing countries. In our quantitative model, the informal sector arises from burdensome taxes and regulations that are imperfectly enforced by the government. In equilibrium, smaller, less productive firms face fewer distortions than larger, more productive ones, potentially leading to substantial misallocation. We show that in settings with a large informal sector, the gains from trade are significantly amplified, as reductions in trade barriers imply a reallocation of resources from initially less distorted to more distorted firms. We confirm findings from earlier reduced-form studies that the informal sector mitigates the impact of negative labor demand shocks on unemployment. Nonetheless, the informal sector can exacerbate the adverse real income effects of economic downturns, amplifying misallocation. Last, our research sheds light on the relationship between trade openness and cross-firm wage inequality.

中文摘要

我们考察了在存在一系列导致非正规经济的国内扭曲（这是发展中国家的普遍现象）的情况下，国际贸易的影响。在我们的定量模型中，非正规部门源于政府执行不力的繁重税收和监管。在均衡状态下，规模较小、生产率较低的企业面临的扭曲少于规模较大、生产率较高的企业，这可能导致严重的资源错配。我们表明，在非正规部门规模较大的环境中，贸易收益被显著放大，因为贸易壁垒的降低意味着资源从最初扭曲较少的企业重新配置到扭曲较多的企业。我们证实了早期简化形式研究的发现，即非正规部门减轻了负面劳动力需求冲击对失业的影响。尽管如此，非正规部门可能加剧经济衰退对实际收入的不利影响，放大资源错配。最后，我们的研究揭示了贸易开放与跨企业工资不平等之间的关系。

Quality and Price Setting of High-Tech Goods

高科技产品的质量与定价

Yuriy Gorodnichenko, Oleksandr Talavera, Nam Hoai Vu

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper investigates the link between product quality and price setting for central processing units (CPUs). Using thousands of price quotes from a popular price-comparison website, we find that market fundamentals, such as the number of sellers, median price, share of convenient prices and level of seller stability, are important factors for explaining price stickiness and price dispersion. We demonstrate that calculations of price inflation require conditioning not only on CPU quality, but also on market fundamentals to ensure that CPU attributes are priced correctly. Failing to do so can result in an understatement of CPU price deflation in the sample period.

中文摘要

本文研究了中央处理器（CPU）的产品质量与定价之间的联系。利用来自一个流行的价格比较网站的数千条报价，我们发现市场基本面因素，如卖家数量、中位数价格、便利价格份额和卖家稳定程度，对于解释价格粘性和价格离散至关重要。我们证明，计算价格通胀不仅需要根据CPU质量进行调整，还需要根据市场基本面进行调整，以确保CPU属性被正确定价。如果未能做到这一点，可能会导致样本期内CPU价格通缩被低估。

Where is Pollution Moving? Environmental Markets and Environmental Justice

污染正在向何处转移？环境市场与环境正义

Joseph S. Shapiro, Reed Walker

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 西班牙、美国

Abstract

Do US air pollution offset markets disproportionately relocate pollution to or from low-income or minority communities? Concerns about an equal distribution of environmental quality across communities—environmental justice—have growing policy influence. We relate prices and quantities of offset transactions to demographics of the communities surrounding polluting plants. We find little association of offset prices or offset-induced movements in pollution with the share of a community that is Black, Hispanic, or with mean household income.
