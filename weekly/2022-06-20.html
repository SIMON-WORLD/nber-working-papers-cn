Age and the labor market for Hispanics in the United States

年龄与美国西班牙裔劳动力市场

Joanna Lahey, Roberto M. Mosquera

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 西班牙、美国

Abstract

We explore the labor market for Hispanic high school graduates in the United States by age using information from the US Census, American Community Survey, Current Population Survey, and three laboratory experiments. We find, in general, that the differences in outcomes for Hispanic and non-Hispanic high school graduates do not change across the lifecycle. Moving to a laboratory setting, we provided participants with randomized resumes for a clerical position that are on average equivalent except for name and age (as indicated by date of high school graduation). In all three experiments, hypothetical applicants with Hispanic and non-Hispanic names were generally treated the same across the lifecycle by a student population, a population of human resources managers, and a more general population from mTurk. These results stand in contrast to earlier results that find strong differences by age in how resumes with Black and White names are treated.

中文摘要

我们利用美国人口普查、美国社区调查、当前人口调查以及三项实验室实验的信息，按年龄探讨了美国西班牙裔高中毕业生的劳动力市场状况。我们发现，总体而言，西班牙裔与非西班牙裔高中毕业生在结果上的差异在整个生命周期中并未发生变化。在实验室环境中，我们向参与者提供了针对文职岗位的随机简历，这些简历除姓名和年龄（以高中毕业日期表示）外平均相当。在所有三项实验中，由学生群体、人力资源经理群体以及来自mTurk的更广泛群体进行的评估显示，具有西班牙裔和非西班牙裔姓名的假设求职者在整个生命周期中通常受到同等对待。这些结果与早期发现黑人和白人姓名简历因年龄而受到强烈区别对待的结果形成对比。

No. 2论文详情NBER w30170

Machine Learning Can Predict Shooting Victimization Well Enough to Help Prevent It

机器学习能够足够准确地预测枪击受害风险以帮助预防

Sara B. Heller, Benjamin Jakubowski, Zubin Jelveh, Max Kapustin

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using arrest and victimization records for almost 644,000 people from the Chicago Police Department, we train a machine learning model to predict the risk of being shot in the next 18 months. Out-of-sample accuracy is strikingly high: of the 500 people with the highest predicted risk, almost 17 percent are shot within 18 months, a rate 106 times higher than the average Chicagoan. A central concern with using police data is that predictions will “bake in” bias, overestimating risk for groups likelier to interact with police conditional on behavior. We show that Black male victims more often have enough police contact to generate predictions. But those predictions are not, on average, inflated; the demographic composition of predicted and actual shooting victims is almost identical. There are legal, ethical, and practical barriers to using these predictions to target law enforcement. But using them to target social services could increase the potential for prevention programs to reduce shootings: predictive accuracy among the top 500 people justifies spending up to $190,900 per person for an intervention that could cut the probability of being shot by half.

中文摘要

利用芝加哥警察局近644,000人的逮捕和受害记录，我们训练了一个机器学习模型来预测未来18个月内被枪击的风险。样本外准确性惊人地高：在预测风险最高的500人中，近17%的人在18个月内被枪击，这一比率是芝加哥平均水平的106倍。使用警方数据的一个核心担忧是预测会“固化”偏见，高估那些在行为相同条件下更可能与警方互动的群体的风险。我们表明，黑人男性受害者更经常有足够的警方接触以生成预测。但这些预测平均而言并未被夸大；预测的枪击受害者与实际枪击受害者的人口构成几乎相同。利用这些预测来指导执法存在法律、伦理和实践障碍。但利用它们来指导社会服务可能会增加预防项目减少枪击事件的潜力：对前500名高风险人群的预测准确性证明，对于一项能将枪击概率降低一半的干预措施，每人最高可花费190,900美元。

No. 3论文详情NBER w30169

Getting to the Core: Inflation Risks Within and Across Asset Classes

触及核心：资产类别内部及跨资产类别的通胀风险

Xiang Fang, Yang Liu, Nikolai Roussanov

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Do “real” assets protect against inflation? Core inflation betas of stocks are negative while energy betas are positive; currencies, commodities, and real estate also mostly hedge against energy inflation but not core. These hedging properties are reflected in the prices of inflation risks: only core inflation carries a negative risk premium, and its magnitude is consistent both within and across asset classes, uniquely among macroeconomic risk factors. While high core inflation tends to be followed by low real output, consumption, and dividend payouts, it impacts asset prices through both cash-flow and discount rate channels. The relative contribution of core and energy changes over time, helping explain the time-varying correlation between stock and bond returns. A two-sector New Keynesian model qualitatively accounts for these facts and implies that the changing stock-bond correlation can be attributed to the shifting importance of supply and demand shocks in driving energy inflation over time.

中文摘要

“实物”资产能抵御通胀吗？股票的核心通胀贝塔值为负，而能源贝塔值为正；货币、大宗商品和房地产也主要对冲能源通胀，而非核心通胀。这些对冲特性反映在通胀风险的价格上：只有核心通胀带有负的风险溢价，且其幅度在资产类别内部和跨资产类别之间一致，这在宏观经济风险因素中是独一无二的。虽然高核心通胀往往伴随着低实际产出、消费和股息支付，但它通过现金流和贴现率两个渠道影响资产价格。核心通胀与能源通胀的相对贡献随时间变化，这有助于解释股票和债券回报之间随时间变化的相关性。一个两部门新凯恩斯模型定性地解释了这些事实，并表明股票-债券相关性的变化可归因于供给和需求冲击在推动能源通胀中重要性的转变。

Was Pandemic Fiscal Relief Effective Fiscal Stimulus? Evidence from Aid to State and Local Governments

疫情财政救济是有效的财政刺激吗？来自对州和地方政府援助的证据

Jeffrey Clemens, Philip G. Hoxie, Stan Veuger

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use an instrumental-variables estimator reliant on variation in congressional representation to analyze the effects of federal aid to state and local governments across all four major pieces of COVID-19 response legislation. Through September 2021, we estimate that the federal government allocated $855,000 for each state or local government job-year preserved. Our baseline confidence interval allows us to rule out estimates of less than $433,000. Our estimates of effects on aggregate income and output are centered on zero and imply modest if any spillover effects onto the broader economy. We discuss aspects of the pandemic context, which include the surprising resilience of state and local tax revenues as well as of broader macroeconomic conditions, that may underlie the small employment and stimulative impacts we estimate in comparison with previous research.

中文摘要

我们利用依赖于国会代表权变动的工具变量估计量，分析了所有四项主要COVID-19应对立法中对州和地方政府的联邦援助的影响。截至2021年9月，我们估计联邦政府为每个保留的州或地方政府工作岗位年拨款855,000美元。我们的基准置信区间排除了低于433,000美元的估计值。我们对总收入和产出影响的估计以零为中心，表明对更广泛经济的溢出效应即使有也很小。我们讨论了疫情背景的各个方面，包括州和地方税收收入以及更广泛宏观经济状况出人意料的韧性，这些可能解释了与我们之前的研究相比，我们所估计的就业和刺激效应较小的原因。

No. 5论文详情NBER w30167

Surveillance of Repression: Theory and Implementation

镇压的监控：理论与实施

Veli Andirin, Yusuf Neggers, Mehdi Shadmehr, Jesse M. Shapiro

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

A repressive regime can suppress dissent (e.g., protest) after it manifests (e.g., by beating or arresting protesters) or prevent it beforehand (e.g., by obstructing public spaces). We separately study and measure these two forms of repression, focusing on the case of political protest. We show in a model that preventive repression makes protest less predictable. We introduce a new database of security alerts that includes advance warning of protest. We validate the database against objectively measured events and use it to produce global, monthly indices of repression grounded in our model. We show that these indices track salient episodes of repression and predict public expressions of human rights concerns. We find that additional government resources increase prevention but not suppression. We argue that these indices can be a valuable tool for surveillance by the international community and analysis by the research community.

中文摘要

一个压制性政权可以在异议（例如抗议）显现后予以压制（例如通过殴打或逮捕抗议者），也可以事先加以预防（例如通过封锁公共场所）。我们分别研究并衡量这两种形式的镇压，重点关注政治抗议的情形。我们在一个模型中表明，预防性镇压使抗议更难以预测。我们引入了一个新的安全警报数据库，其中包含对抗议的提前预警。我们根据客观测量的事件对该数据库进行了验证，并利用它基于我们的模型生成了全球性的月度镇压指数。我们表明，这些指数能够追踪显著的镇压事件，并预测对人权问题的公开表达。我们发现，额外的政府资源增加了预防性镇压，但并未增加压制性镇压。我们认为，这些指数可以成为国际社会进行监控和研究界进行分析的宝贵工具。

No. 6论文详情NBER w30166

Fighting Communism Supporting Collusion

打击共产主义，支持合谋

Sebastian Galiani, Jose Manuel Paz y Miño, Gustavo Torrens

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 美国

Abstract

We develop a simple model to explain why a powerful importer country like the United States may provide political support for international collusive agreements concerning certain imported commodities (e.g., coffee). We show that helping producer countries organize and enforce collusion might be an attractive instrument to advance important geopolitical goals; for example, to reduce the chances that the producer countries will align with a rival global power (e.g., the Soviet Union during the Cold War). Moreover, using this practice, the cost of collusion is shared with other importers (including allies). Thus, collusion might be a superior strategy to foreign aid (a priori a more direct and efficient instrument), which is riddled with free riding problems. The model sheds light on why the United States supported (or failed to support) international commodity agreements for coffee, sugar, and oil during and immediately after the Cold War period.

中文摘要

我们开发了一个简单模型来解释为什么像美国这样的强大进口国可能会为某些进口商品（例如咖啡）的国际合谋协议提供政治支持。我们表明，帮助生产国组织和执行合谋可能是推进重要地缘政治目标的一个有吸引力的工具；例如，降低生产国与竞争对手全球大国（例如冷战时期的苏联）结盟的可能性。此外，通过这种做法，合谋的成本与其他进口国（包括盟友）共同分担。因此，合谋可能是一种优于对外援助的策略（后者在理论上是一种更直接、更有效的工具），因为对外援助充斥着搭便车问题。该模型揭示了为什么美国在冷战期间及结束后立即支持（或未能支持）咖啡、糖和石油的国际商品协议。

No. 7论文详情NBER w30165中国相关

The Fertility Consequences of Air Pollution in China

中国空气污染的生育后果

Xuwen Gao, Ran Song, Christopher Timmins

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候

Abstract

We incorporate pollution exposure into Becker’s “Quantity-Quality” (Q-Q) model of fertility and quantify how air pollution distorts individuals’ fertility behaviors in China. We document a robust pattern in which increased pollution over time negatively affects the fertility of ethnic Han people, who comprise approximately 92% of the Chinese population. These patterns are evident in both cross-sectional and panel data, when instrumenting for pollution using distant coal-fired plants upwind of cities or thermal inversions that trap pollution. Consistent with the stylized Q-Q model of fertility, we find that increased pollution drives up the parental expenditure per child, which increases the shadow price associated with the number of children and reduces fertility. Consistent with the model, we also find that the fertility choices of people who tend to have higher demand for child quality are significantly more sensitive to pollution changes. Pollution does not have a meaningful effect on the fertility of ethnic minorities, which can also be explained under the Q-Q framework.

中文摘要

我们将污染暴露纳入贝克尔生育的“数量-质量”（Q-Q）模型，并量化了空气污染如何扭曲中国个体的生育行为。我们记录了一个稳健的模式：随着时间的推移，污染增加对汉族人的生育率产生负面影响，汉族人约占中国人口的92%。这些模式在横截面数据和面板数据中都很明显，当我们使用城市上风向的远距离燃煤电厂或捕获污染的热逆温层作为污染的工具变量时也是如此。与生育的经典Q-Q模型一致，我们发现污染增加推高了每个孩子的父母支出，这提高了与孩子数量相关的影子价格并降低了生育率。与模型一致，我们还发现，倾向于对孩子质量有更高需求的人群的生育选择对污染变化显著更敏感。污染对少数民族的生育率没有显著影响，这也可以在Q-Q框架下得到解释。

No. 8论文详情NBER w30164

Public Pension Reforms and Retirement Decisions: Narrative Evidence and Aggregate Implications

公共养老金改革与退休决策：叙事证据与总体影响

Huixin Bi, Sarah Zubairy

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We construct a database of public pension policy changes with motivation and implementation information for ten OECD countries. Structural pension reforms, motivated by long-run sustainability concerns, often come with prolonged phase-in periods. In response to pension retrenchments implemented immediately, people close to retirement stay in the work force longer. News about future pension retrenchments with implementation lags, however, is likely to lead this group to exit the labor market. This decline in the labor force participation rate is particularly strong for reforms with long lags, ones that introduce fundamental policy changes, and where citizens have lower trust in the government.

中文摘要

我们为十个OECD国家构建了一个包含动机和实施信息的公共养老金政策变化数据库。由长期可持续性担忧驱动的结构性养老金改革，通常伴随着漫长的逐步实施期。针对立即实施的养老金紧缩，接近退休的人群会延长工作年限。然而，关于未来实施滞后的养老金紧缩的消息，很可能导致这一群体退出劳动力市场。这种劳动力参与率的下降在以下改革中尤为显著：实施滞后时间长的改革、引入根本性政策变化的改革，以及公民对政府信任度较低的国家。

No. 9论文详情NBER w30163

The Size and Census Coverage of the U.S. Homeless Population

美国无家可归人口的规模与普查覆盖情况

Bruce D. Meyer, Angela Wyse, Kevin Corinth

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Despite widespread concern about homelessness, fundamental questions about the size and characteristics of this hard to study population are unresolved, in large part because it is unclear whether existing data are sufficiently complete and reliable. We examine these questions as well as the coverage of new microdata sources that are designed to be nationally representative and will allow pathbreaking new analyses. We compare three restricted use data sources that have been largely unused to study homelessness to less detailed public data. In doing this triangulation of sources, we examine the completeness and accuracy of available data and improve our understanding of the size of the homeless population and its inclusion in the Census and household surveys. Specifically, we compare restricted data from the 2010 Census, American Community Survey (ACS), and Homeless Management Information System (HMIS) to HUD's public-use point-in-time (PIT) estimates and the Housing Inventory Count (HIC) at the national, city and county, and person level. We also develop a new approach to estimating the size of the sheltered homeless population using linked Census and HMIS shelter microdata. Our analyses suggest that on a given night there are about 600,000 people experiencing homelessness in the U.S., about one-third of whom are sleeping on the streets and two-thirds in shelters. More than 90 percent of those in shelters were counted in the Census, although many were classified as housed or in other group quarters, a result that stems largely from ambiguity in the definition of a homeless shelter. By establishing the broad coverage and reliability of the new data sources, this paper lays the foundation for pathbreaking future work on the characteristics, income, safety net participation, mortality, migration, geographic distribution, and housing status transitions of the U.S. homeless population.

Unemployment Insurance, Starting Salaries, and Jobs

失业保险、起薪与工作

Gordon Dahl, Matthew M. Knepper

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the labor market effects of permanent 23-50% reductions in unemployment insurance benefits available in seven states. Leveraging linked firm-establishment data, we find that establishments based in reform states experience 1.5-2.4% faster employment growth relative to the same firm's establishments in other states. Using a similar multi-state firm design, starting salaries are 1.8-7.2% lower in reform states and posted salaries for the same job fall by 1.4-5.5%. These labor supply shocks yield an average labor demand elasticity of -1.0. Our results reveal a substantial decline in match quality and worker bargaining power as UI benefits become less generous.

中文摘要

Mothers as Insurance: Family Spillovers in WIC

母亲作为保险：WIC项目中的家庭溢出效应

Marianne Bitler, Janet Currie, Hilary W. Hoynes, Krista J. Ruffini, Lisa Schulkind, Barton Willage

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC) is a widely used program. Previous research shows that WIC improves birth outcomes, but evidence about impacts on children and families is limited. We use a regression discontinuity leveraging an age five when children become ineligible for WIC and examine nutritional and laboratory outcomes for adults and children. We find little impact on children who aged out of the program. But among adult women caloric intake falls and food insecurity increases, suggesting that mothers protect children by consuming less themselves. We find no effect on others in the household.

中文摘要

Microgiving with Digital Platforms

数字平台上的微捐赠

Xiheng Jiang, Jianwei Xing, Jintao Xu, Eric Zou

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济

Abstract

Microgiving, a new form of digital fundraising, operates by soliciting minuscule donation amounts that are typically not viable in traditional settings. This paper considers the scaling up of microgiving via platform integration, whereby small donations are bundled with high volume of economic activities that are naturally occurring on digital platforms. We evaluate a charity subscription program run by Alibaba, one of the world’s largest retail platforms, which allows sellers to pledge a tiny portion of a product’s revenue (2 cents per order at minimum) to charity, with donations made automatically as transactions take place. We document that, between 2018 and 2020, the program attracted more than 2 million Alibaba sellers and generated 1.2 billion yuan of charitable funds, representing one of China’s most successful online fundraisers. We pinpoint three forces that jointly create a self-fulfilling incentive for platform sellers to engage in microgiving: First, intense competition motivates sellers to link their products with charitable causes, even though the signaling value of the linkage is small; Second, due to low financial commitment, sellers rarely discontinue their giving once subscribed to the program, which ensures stable streams of charitable funds; Third, the program provides sellers with the joy of giving at a low cost, and this “warm glow” effect further reinforces participation. The success of the microgiving scheme hinges on features that are shared by many digital platforms, and its success is potentially applicable on a broader scale.

中文摘要

微捐赠是一种新型数字筹款方式，通过募集传统环境下通常不可行的小额捐款来运作。本文考虑了通过平台整合来扩大微捐赠规模，即将小额捐款与数字平台上自然发生的大量经济活动捆绑在一起。我们评估了由全球最大零售平台之一阿里巴巴运营的一项慈善订阅计划，该计划允许卖家将产品收入的一小部分（每笔订单至少2分钱）捐赠给慈善机构，捐款在交易发生时自动进行。我们记录到，在2018年至2020年间，该计划吸引了超过200万阿里巴巴卖家，并产生了12亿元人民币的慈善资金，成为中国最成功的在线筹款活动之一。我们指出了三种共同为平台卖家创造参与微捐赠自我实现激励的力量：首先，激烈的竞争促使卖家将他们的产品与慈善事业联系起来，尽管这种联系的信号价值很小；其次，由于财务承诺低，卖家一旦订阅该计划就很少停止捐赠，这确保了慈善资金的稳定来源；第三，该计划以低成本为卖家提供捐赠的快乐，这种“温暖光辉”效应进一步强化了参与。微捐赠计划的成功依赖于许多数字平台共有的特征，其成功可能适用于更广泛的规模。
