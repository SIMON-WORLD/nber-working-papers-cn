No. 1论文详情NBER w30614

Regression Discontinuity Evidence on the Effectiveness of the Minimum Legal E-Cigarette Purchasing Age

最低合法电子烟购买年龄有效性的断点回归证据

Jeffrey S. DeSimone, Daniel S. Grossman, Nicolas R. Ziebarth

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

Increases in youth vaping rates and concerns of a new generation of nicotine addicts recently prompted an increase in the federal minimum legal purchase age (MLPA) for tobacco products, including e-cigarettes, to 21 years. This study presents the first regression discontinuity evidence on the effectiveness of e-cigarette MLPA laws. Using data on 12th graders from Monitoring the Future, we obtain robust evidence that federal and state age-18 MLPAs decreased underage e-cigarette use by 15–20% and frequent use by 20–40%. These findings suggest that the age-21 federal MLPA could meaningfully reduce e-cigarette use among 18–20-year-olds.

中文摘要

近年来青少年电子烟使用率上升以及对新一代尼古丁成瘾者的担忧，促使美国将包括电子烟在内的烟草产品联邦最低合法购买年龄提高至21岁。本研究首次提供了关于电子烟最低合法购买年龄法律有效性的断点回归证据。利用“监测未来”项目中12年级学生的数据，我们获得了稳健的证据，表明联邦和州层面的18岁最低合法购买年龄将未成年人电子烟使用率降低了15%至20%，将频繁使用率降低了20%至40%。这些发现表明，联邦21岁最低合法购买年龄可能显著减少18至20岁人群的电子烟使用。

Understanding U.S. Inflation During the COVID Era

理解新冠疫情时期的美国通胀

Laurence M. Ball, Daniel Leigh, Prachi Mishra

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

This paper analyzes the dramatic rise in U.S. inflation since 2020, which we decompose into a rise in core inflation as measured by the weighted median inflation rate and deviations of headline inflation from core. We explain the rise in core with two factors, the tightening of the labor market as captured by the ratio of job vacancies to unemployment, and the pass-through into core from past shocks to headline inflation. The headline shocks themselves are explained largely by increases in energy prices and by supply chain problems as captured by backlogs of orders for goods and services. Looking forward, we simulate the future path of inflation for alternative paths of the unemployment rate, focusing on the projections of Federal Reserve policymakers in which unemployment rises only modestly to 4.4 percent. We find that this unemployment path returns inflation to near the Fed’s target only under optimistic assumptions about both inflation expectations and the Beveridge curve relating the unemployment and vacancy rates. Under less benign assumptions about these factors, the inflation rate remains well above target unless unemployment rises by more than the Fed projects.

中文摘要

本文分析了自2020年以来美国通胀的急剧上升，我们将其分解为以加权中位数通胀率衡量的核心通胀上升，以及总体通胀与核心通胀的偏离。我们用两个因素来解释核心通胀的上升：以职位空缺与失业率之比衡量的劳动力市场收紧，以及过去总体通胀冲击向核心通胀的传导。总体通胀冲击本身在很大程度上可由能源价格上涨以及以商品和服务订单积压衡量的供应链问题来解释。展望未来，我们模拟了不同失业率路径下通胀的未来走势，重点关注美联储政策制定者的预测，即失业率仅温和上升至4.4%。我们发现，只有在关于通胀预期以及连接失业率和职位空缺率的贝弗里奇曲线都持乐观假设的情况下，这一失业率路径才能使通胀回到接近美联储目标的水平。如果对这些因素的假设不那么有利，除非失业率上升幅度超过美联储的预测，否则通胀率将远高于目标水平。

AI, Skill, and Productivity: The Case of Taxi Drivers

人工智能、技能与生产率：以出租车司机为例

Kyogo Kanazawa, Daiji Kawaguchi, Hitoshi Shigeoka, Yasutora Watanabe

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on productivity in the context of taxi drivers. The AI we study assists drivers with finding customers by suggesting routes along which the demand is predicted to be high. We find that AI improves drivers’ productivity by shortening the cruising time, and such gain is accrued only to low-skilled drivers, narrowing the productivity gap between high- and low-skilled drivers by 14%. The result indicates that AI's impact on human labor is more nuanced and complex than a job displacement story, which was the primary focus of existing studies.

中文摘要

我们以出租车司机为背景，考察了人工智能对生产率的影响。我们研究的人工智能通过建议需求预计较高的路线来帮助司机寻找乘客。我们发现，人工智能通过缩短空驶时间提高了司机的生产率，而这种收益仅由低技能司机获得，使高技能与低技能司机之间的生产率差距缩小了14%。这一结果表明，人工智能对人类劳动的影响比现有研究主要关注的岗位替代故事更为微妙和复杂。

The Impact of Joint versus Separate Prediction Mode on Forecasting Accuracy

联合预测模式与单独预测模式对预测准确性的影响

Alex Imas, Minah H. Jung, Silvia Saccardo, Joachim Vosgerau

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Forecasters predicting how people change their behavior in response to a treatment or intervention often consider a set of alternatives. In contrast, those who are treated are typically exposed to only one of the treatment alternatives. For example, managers selecting a wage schedule consider a set of alternative wages while employees are hired at a given rate. We show that forecasts made in joint-prediction mode—which considers a set of alternatives—generate predictions that expect substantially larger behavioral responses than those made in separate-prediction mode—which considers the response to only one treatment realization in isolation. Results show the latter to be more accurate in matching people’s actual responses to interventions and treatment changes. We present applications to managerial decision-making and forecasting of scientific results.

中文摘要

预测人们如何改变其行为以应对某种治疗或干预的预测者，通常会考虑一系列备选方案。相比之下，接受治疗者通常只接触其中一种治疗方案。例如，选择工资方案的管理者会考虑一系列备选工资，而员工则以某一特定工资被雇佣。我们表明，在联合预测模式下做出的预测——即考虑一系列备选方案——所产生的预测，预期行为反应幅度显著大于在单独预测模式下做出的预测——即孤立地考虑对单一治疗实现的反应。结果显示，后者在匹配人们对干预和治疗变化的实际反应方面更为准确。我们展示了这些发现在管理决策和科学结果预测中的应用。

Parenting Promotes Social Mobility Within and Across Generations

养育方式促进代内与代际社会流动

Jorge Luis García, James J. Heckman

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

This paper compares early childhood enrichment programs that promote social mobility for disadvantaged children within and across generations. Instead of conducting a standard meta-analysis, we present a harmonized primary data analysis of programs that shape current policy. Our analysis is a template for rigorous syntheses and comparisons across programs. We analyze new long-run life-cycle data collected for iconic programs when participants are middle-aged and their children are in their twenties. The iconic programs are omnibus in nature and offer many services to children and their parents. We compare them with relatively low-cost more focused home-visiting programs. Successful interventions target both children and their caregivers. They engage caregivers and improve the home lives of children. They permanently boost cognitive and non-cognitive skills. Participants in programs that enrich home environments grow up with better skills, jobs, earnings, marital stability, and health, as well as reduced participation in crime. Long-run monetized gains are substantially greater than program costs for iconic programs. We investigate the mechanisms promoting successful family lives for participants and find intergenerational effects on their children. A study of focused home-visiting programs that target parents enables us to isolate a crucial component of successful programs: they activate and promote parenting skills of child caregivers. The home-visiting programs we analyze produce outcomes comparable to those of the iconic omnibus programs. National implementation of the programs with long-run follow up that we analyze would substantially shrink the overall US Black-White earnings gap.

中文摘要

本文比较了旨在促进弱势儿童代内与代际社会流动的早期儿童充实项目。我们没有进行标准的元分析，而是对塑造当前政策的标志性项目进行了统一的原始数据分析。我们的分析为跨项目的严格综合与比较提供了一个模板。我们分析了在参与者步入中年且其子女二十多岁时收集的新的长期生命周期数据。这些标志性项目本质上是综合性的，为儿童及其父母提供多种服务。我们将它们与成本相对较低、更聚焦的家访项目进行比较。成功的干预措施同时针对儿童及其照料者。它们吸引照料者参与，改善儿童的家庭生活，并永久性地提升认知与非认知技能。参与那些丰富家庭环境项目的儿童，成年后拥有更好的技能、工作、收入、婚姻稳定性、健康状况，并减少了犯罪参与。对于标志性项目而言，长期货币化收益远大于项目成本。我们研究了促进参与者成功家庭生活的机制，并发现了对其子女的代际影响。一项针对以父母为目标的聚焦家访项目的研究，使我们能够分离出成功项目的一个关键组成部分：它们激活并提升了儿童照料者的养育技能。我们分析的家访项目产生的结果与标志性综合项目相当。我们分析的这些具有长期跟踪研究的项目若在全国范围内实施，将大幅缩小美国黑人与白人之间的整体收入差距。

The U.S. Postal Savings System and the Collapse of B&Ls During the Great Depression

美国邮政储蓄系统与大萧条时期建筑贷款协会的崩溃

Sebastián Fleitas, Matthew S. Jaremski, Steven Sprick Schuster

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

Building and Loan Associations (B&Ls) financed over half of new houses constructed in the U.S. during the 1920s but they lost their predominance within the following decades as they were pushed to convert into Savings and Loans (S&Ls). This study examines whether the U.S. government-insured Postal Savings System attracted funds away from B&Ls precisely when they needed them the most in the Great Depression. Annual town- and county-level data from 1920 through 1935 for 3 states show that the sudden rise in local postal savings was associated with local downturns in B&Ls. Using a panel vector autoregression, we find that postal savings significantly reduced the amount of money in B&Ls, yet B&Ls had no significant effect on postal savings banks. Alternatively, postal savings had no significant effect on commercial banks. The results suggest that this competitive dynamic prevented B&Ls from rebounding in the mid-1930s and helped contribute to Great Depression’s local real estate lending decline.

中文摘要

在20世纪20年代，建筑贷款协会为美国一半以上的新建房屋提供了融资，但在随后的几十年里，由于被迫转变为储蓄贷款协会，它们失去了主导地位。本研究考察了美国政府担保的邮政储蓄系统是否在大萧条时期，恰恰在建筑贷款协会最需要资金的时候，吸走了它们的资金。来自三个州1920年至1935年的年度城镇和县级数据显示，当地邮政储蓄的突然增加与当地建筑贷款协会的衰退相关。使用面板向量自回归模型，我们发现邮政储蓄显著减少了建筑贷款协会中的资金量，而建筑贷款协会对邮政储蓄银行没有显著影响。另外，邮政储蓄对商业银行没有显著影响。结果表明，这种竞争动态阻止了建筑贷款协会在20世纪30年代中期复苏，并加剧了大萧条时期地方房地产贷款的下降。

The Productivity of Professions: Evidence from the Emergency Department

专业人员的生产率：来自急诊科的证据

David C. Chan Jr, Yiqun Chen

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper studies the productivity of nurse practitioners (NPs) and physicians, two professions performing overlapping tasks but with starkly different backgrounds, training, and pay. Using quasi-experimental variation in patient assignment to NPs versus physicians in Veterans Health Administration emergency departments, we find that, on average, NPs use more resources and exhibit a higher 30-day preventable hospitalization rate than physicians. However, the NP-physician performance difference varies by case complexity and severity. Importantly, even larger productivity variation exists within each profession, leading to substantial overlap between the productivity distributions of the two professions; NPs outperform physicians in 38 percent of random pairs.

中文摘要

本文研究了执业护士和医生这两类从事重叠任务但背景、培训和薪酬截然不同的专业人员的生产率。利用退伍军人健康管理局急诊科中患者被随机分配给执业护士或医生的准实验变异，我们发现，平均而言，执业护士比医生使用更多资源，并且30天可预防住院率更高。然而，执业护士与医生的绩效差异因病例复杂性和严重程度而异。重要的是，每个专业内部的生产率差异甚至更大，导致两个专业的生产率分布存在大量重叠；在38%的随机配对中，执业护士的表现优于医生。

Artificial Intelligence, the Evolution of the Healthcare Value Chain, and the Future of the Physician

人工智能、医疗价值链的演变与医生的未来

David Dranove, Craig Garthwaite

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming production across all sectors of the economy, with the potential to both complement and substitute for traditional labor inputs. Healthcare is no exception. Dozens of recent academic studies demonstrate that AI can contribute to the healthcare value chain, by improving both diagnostic accuracy and treatment recommendations. In these ways, AI may wither complement or substitute for physicians. We argue that AI represents the culmination of decades of efforts to enhance medical decision making. Using an historical lens that considers long-standing institutional features of healthcare markets, we identify numerous obstacles to the implementation of AI in medical care, and identify which specialties are most at risk for substitution by AI.

中文摘要

人工智能正在改变经济所有部门的生产方式，并有可能补充和替代传统的劳动力投入。医疗保健也不例外。最近数十项学术研究表明，人工智能可以通过提高诊断准确性和治疗建议来为医疗价值链做出贡献。在这些方面，人工智能可能补充或替代医生。我们认为，人工智能代表了数十年来改进医疗决策努力的高潮。通过一个考虑医疗市场长期制度特征的历史视角，我们识别了在医疗保健中实施人工智能的众多障碍，并指出了哪些专科最有可能被人工智能替代。

Should Mothers Work? How Perceptions of the Social Norm Affect Individual Attitudes Toward Work in the U.S.

母亲应该工作吗？社会规范认知如何影响美国个体对工作的态度

Patricia Cortés, Gizem Koşar, Jessica Pan, Basit Zafar

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We study how peer beliefs shape individual attitudes toward maternal labor supply using realistic hypothetical scenarios that elicit recommendations on the labor supply choices of a mother with a young child and an information treatment embedded within representative surveys. Across the scenarios, we find that individuals systematically overestimate the extent of gender conservativeness of the people around them. Exposure to information on peer beliefs leads to a shift in recommendations, driven largely by information-based belief updating. The information treatment also increases (intended and actual) donations to a non-profit organization advocating for women in the workplace.

中文摘要

我们利用现实假设情景（这些情景引发关于有幼儿母亲劳动供给选择的建议）以及嵌入在代表性调查中的信息处理，研究同伴信念如何塑造个体对母亲劳动供给的态度。在各种情景中，我们发现个体系统性地高估了周围人群的性别保守程度。接触同伴信念信息会导致建议的转变，这主要由基于信息的信念更新驱动。信息处理还增加了向倡导职场女性权益的非营利组织的（意向和实际）捐赠。

No. 10论文详情NBER w30605

Epidemiological Expectations

流行病学预期

Christopher D. Carroll, Tao Wang

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

‘Epidemiological’ models of belief formation put social interactions at their core; such models are widely used by scholars who are not economists to study the dynamics of beliefs in populations. We survey the literature in which economists attempting to model the consequences of beliefs about the future – ‘expectations’ – have employed a full-fledged epidemiological approach to explore an economic question. We draw connections to related work on ‘contagion,’ narrative economics, news/rumor spreading, and the spread of internet memes. 大量文献表明，早期生活条件塑造了后期生活结果，包括健康和迁移事件。然而，对死亡率结果地理差异的分析几乎完全集中在同期测量的地理位置（例如，死亡时的居住州），从而可能混淆了早期生活条件、迁移模式和目的地影响的作用。我们利用新近可用的美国社区死亡率差异（MDAC）数据集，该数据集将2008年美国社区调查（ACS）的受访者与官方死亡记录相链接，并估计了不同聚合方法导致的显著差异；按出生州与按居住州测量的50岁时预期寿命差异的平均绝对偏差，男性为0.58（0.50）年，女性为0.40（0.29）年。这些差异也存在空间聚类，我们表明基于出生州预期寿命的区域不平等更高，这意味着州际迁移缓解了死亡率结果的基线地理不平等。最后，我们评估了迁入、迁出和非迁移的州特定特征如何共同塑造按州（居住地）测量的死亡率差异指标，进一步证明了清晰解释这些广泛使用指标的困难。
