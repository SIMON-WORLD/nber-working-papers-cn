Preferred Habitats and Timing in the World’s Safe Asset

全球安全资产中的偏好栖息地与时机选择

Alexandra M. Tabova, Francis E. Warnock

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We build the first-ever comprehensive security-level dataset on the size, flows, coupon payments and returns of foreign and U.S. investors’ Treasury portfolios. Private U.S. and private foreign investors hold longer-duration higher-return Treasuries, whereas foreign governments hold shorter-duration lower-return Treasuries, so all else equal private investors should earn higher returns than foreign governments. But all else is not equal. Foreign governments, even with their low-returns low-volatility portfolios, actually earn higher returns because U.S. and foreign private investors’ returns are substantially reduced by poor timing. Moreover, we find that foreigners beat the (non- Fed) market, while U.S. investors earn a small and insignificant 19 basis points less than market returns. From our analysis, no investor-type earns significantly less than the (non-Fed) Treasury market, indicating that differential investors are not the source of the convenience yield. In terms of investor behavior more broadly, our novel dataset allows a direct comparison of the different investors in the Treasury market. Foreign private investors are similar to U.S. private investors and both behave differently and have different preferred habitats than governments.

中文摘要

我们构建了首个全面的证券层面数据集，涵盖外国和美国投资者国债投资组合的规模、流量、票息支付和回报。美国私人投资者和外国私人投资者持有期限更长、回报更高的国债，而外国政府持有期限更短、回报更低的国债，因此，在其他条件相同的情况下，私人投资者应获得比外国政府更高的回报。但其他条件并不相同。外国政府即使拥有低回报、低波动的投资组合，实际上却获得了更高的回报，因为美国和外国私人投资者的回报因时机选择不佳而大幅降低。此外，我们发现外国投资者跑赢了（非美联储）市场，而美国投资者的回报比市场回报低19个基点，且这一差异较小且不显著。根据我们的分析，没有一类投资者的回报显著低于（非美联储）国债市场，这表明不同的投资者并非便利收益的来源。从更广泛的投资者行为来看，我们的新颖数据集允许直接比较国债市场中的不同投资者。外国私人投资者与美国私人投资者相似，两者行为不同，且与政府相比具有不同的偏好栖息地。

Mutual Fund Revenue Sharing in 401(k) Plans

401(k)计划中的共同基金收入分成

Veronika K. Pool, Clemens Sialm, Irina Stefanescu

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Recordkeepers in DC pension plans are often paid indirectly in the form of revenue sharing from third-party funds on the menu. We show that these arrangements affect the investment menu of 401(k) plans. Revenue-sharing funds are more likely to be added to the menu and are less likely to be deleted. Overall, revenue-sharing plans are more expensive as higher expense ratios are not offset by lower direct fees or by superior performance. Rebates increase with the market power of the recordkeeper suggesting that third-party funds may revenue share to gain access to retirement assets.

中文摘要

固定缴款养老金计划中的记录保管人通常通过从菜单中的第三方基金获得收入分成来间接获得报酬。我们表明，这些安排会影响401(k)计划的投资菜单。收入分成基金更有可能被添加到菜单中，并且不太可能被删除。总体而言，收入分成计划更昂贵，因为较高的费用比率并未被较低的直接费用或更优的业绩所抵消。回扣随着记录保管人的市场力量而增加，这表明第三方基金可能通过收入分成来获取退休资产。

The Macroeconomic Implications of US Market Power in Safe Assets

美国在安全资产中的市场力量的宏观经济影响

Jason Choi, Rishabh Kirpalani, Diego J. Perez

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

The US government is the dominant supplier of global safe assets and faces a downward-sloping demand for its debt. In this paper, we ask if the US exercises its market power when issuing debt and study its macroeconomic consequences. We develop a model of the global economy in which US public debt generates a non-pecuniary value for its holders, analyze the equilibrium in which the US government is the monopoly provider of this safe asset, and contrast this case with the one in which the US government acts as a price taker. We use variation in estimated demand elasticities for US debt during high- and low-volatility regimes to empirically distinguish between these two models and find that the data reject the price-taking behavior in favor of the monopoly one. We then quantify the distortions due to market power and find that it generates a significant underprovision of safe assets, a sizable markup in the convenience yield, and large welfare benefits for the US to the detriment of the rest of the world. Finally, we study the implications of increasing competition in safe assets from other sovereigns and private institutions.

中文摘要

美国政府是全球安全资产的主要供应者，并面临对其债务向下倾斜的需求。在本文中，我们探讨美国在发行债务时是否行使了其市场力量，并研究其宏观经济后果。我们建立了一个全球经济模型，其中美国公共债务为其持有者产生非金钱价值，分析了美国政府作为这种安全资产的垄断提供者时的均衡，并将此情况与美国政府作为价格接受者时的情形进行对比。我们利用高波动和低波动制度下美国债务需求弹性的估计变化，从实证上区分这两种模型，发现数据拒绝了价格接受行为，支持了垄断行为。然后，我们量化了市场力量造成的扭曲，发现它导致了安全资产的显著供应不足、便利收益的大幅加价，以及美国以世界其他地区为代价的巨大福利收益。最后，我们研究了来自其他主权国家和私人机构在安全资产领域日益激烈的竞争的影响。

Adverse Selection and Network Design Under Regulated Plan Prices: Evidence from Medicaid

受监管计划价格下的逆向选择与网络设计：来自医疗补助计划的证据

Amanda R. Kreider, Timothy J. Layton, Mark Shepard, Jacob Wallace

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

Health plans for the poor increasingly limit access to specialty hospitals. We investigate the role of adverse selection in generating this equilibrium among private plans in Medicaid. Studying a network change, we find that covering a top cancer hospital causes severe adverse selection, increasing demand for a plan by 50% among enrollees with cancer versus no impact for others. Medicaid’s fixed insurer payments make offsetting this selection, and the contract distortions it induces, challenging, requiring either infeasibly high payment rates or near-perfect risk adjustment. By contrast, a small explicit bonus for covering the hospital is sufficient to make coverage profitable.

中文摘要

针对贫困人群的健康计划日益限制对专科医院的准入。我们研究了逆向选择在医疗补助计划中私人计划之间形成这种均衡的作用。通过研究一次网络变更，我们发现覆盖一家顶级癌症医院会导致严重的逆向选择，使参保癌症患者对该计划的需求增加50%，而对其他人群则无影响。医疗补助计划固定的保险公司支付使得抵消这种选择及其引发的合同扭曲变得困难，要么需要高得不可行的支付率，要么需要近乎完美的风险调整。相比之下，为覆盖该医院提供一个小额的明确奖金就足以使覆盖变得有利可图。

Corporate Governance Implications of the Growth in Indexing

指数化增长对公司治理的影响

Alon Brav, Nadya Malenko, Andrey Malenko

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Passively managed funds have grown to become some of the largest shareholders in publicly traded companies, but there is considerable debate about the effects of this growth on corporate governance. The goal of this paper is to review the literature on the governance implications of passive fund growth and discuss directions for future research. In particular, we present a framework to understand the incentives of passive and actively managed funds to engage in governance, review the empirical evidence in the context of this framework, and highlight the questions that remain unanswered.

中文摘要

被动管理型基金已成长为上市公司中一些最大的股东，但关于这种增长对公司治理的影响存在相当大的争论。本文旨在回顾关于被动基金增长对治理影响的文献，并讨论未来研究的方向。具体而言，我们提出了一个框架来理解被动和主动管理型基金参与治理的激励，在此框架下回顾实证证据，并强调尚未解答的问题。

Missing Workers and Missing Jobs Since the Pandemic

疫情以来缺失的工人与缺失的就业岗位

Bart Hobijn, Ayşegül Şahin

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Since the start of the pandemic the U.S. labor market has been characterized as being plagued by missing jobs, i.e. payroll employment has fallen more than five million jobs short of its pre-pandemic trend, and missing workers, i.e. the participation rate has declined by 1.2 percentage points: A pandemic-induced shortage of workers has restrained job creation and, as a result, been a substantial drag on post-pandemic job growth. In this paper, we show that this is a misinterpretation of the data for two reasons. The first is that the number of missing jobs is inflated because it is based on the unrealistic assumption that the pre-pandemic tailwinds for job growth from the decline in the unemployment rate and cyclical upward pressures on participation would have continued in 2020 and beyond if the pandemic would not have occurred. Second, the number of workers missing due to COVID is overstated because the bulk of the 1.2 percentage-point decline in the participation rate since the start of the pandemic reflects a continuation of its long-run downward trend that was already part of projections before the pandemic broke out. Instead, our payroll jobs accounting yields an 810 thousand cyclical shortfall in payroll jobs in October 2022 compared to right before the pandemic. At the recent pace of job growth, even without monetary and fiscal tightening, we expect a substantial deceleration of payroll growth in the coming months.

中文摘要

自疫情开始以来，美国劳动力市场的特征被描述为受到缺失就业岗位的困扰，即薪资就业人数比疫情前趋势少了超过500万个岗位；以及缺失工人，即劳动参与率下降了1.2个百分点：疫情引发的工人短缺抑制了就业创造，从而成为疫情后就业增长的重大拖累。在本文中，我们表明这是对数据的误解，原因有二。第一，缺失就业岗位的数量被夸大了，因为它基于一个不现实的假设，即如果疫情没有发生，疫情前失业率下降和参与率周期性上行压力对就业增长的有利因素本会在2020年及以后持续。第二，因疫情缺失的工人数量被高估了，因为自疫情开始以来参与率下降的1.2个百分点中，大部分反映了其长期下降趋势的延续，这一趋势在疫情爆发前就已包含在预测中。相反，我们的薪资就业核算显示，与疫情前相比，2022年10月薪资就业的周期性缺口为81万个。按照近期的就业增长速度，即使没有货币和财政紧缩，我们预计未来几个月薪资增长将大幅放缓。

Tax-Loss Harvesting with Cryptocurrencies

加密货币的税收损失收割

Lin William Cong, Wayne Landsman, Edward Maydew, Daniel Rabetti

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

We describe the landscape of taxation in the crypto markets, especially that concerning U.S. taxpayers, and examine how recent increases in tax scrutiny have led to changes in trading behavior by crypto traders. We predict under a simple theoretical framework and then empirically document that increased tax scrutiny leads crypto investors to utilize legal tax planning with tax-loss harvesting as an alternative to non-compliance. In particular, domestic traders increase tax-loss harvesting following the increase in tax scrutiny, and U.S. exchanges exhibit a significantly greater amount of wash trading. Additional findings suggest that broad-based and targeted changes in tax scrutiny can differentially affect crypto traders' preference for U.S.-based exchanges. We also discuss other gray areas for tax regulation related to new crypto assets such as Non-Fungible Tokens and Decentralized Finance protocols that further highlight the importance of coordinating tax policy and other regulations.

中文摘要

我们描述了加密市场的税收格局，特别是涉及美国纳税人的部分，并考察了近期税收审查加强如何导致加密交易者交易行为的变化。我们在一个简单的理论框架下预测，并通过实证记录，税收审查加强促使加密投资者利用税收损失收割进行合法税务规划，以此替代不合规行为。具体而言，国内交易者在税收审查加强后增加了税收损失收割，美国交易所表现出显著更多的虚假交易。其他发现表明，广泛和针对性的税收审查变化可能对加密交易者选择美国交易所的偏好产生不同影响。我们还讨论了与新型加密资产（如非同质化代币和去中心化金融协议）相关的其他税收监管灰色地带，这进一步凸显了协调税收政策与其他监管的重要性。

Heads Up: Does Air Pollution Cause Workplace Accidents?

抬头注意：空气污染会导致工作场所事故吗？

Victor Lavy, Genia Rachkovski, Omry Yoresh

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 以色列

Abstract

Air pollution can adversely affect physiological and cognitive performance. This study estimates the causal effect of increased nitrogen dioxide (NO2), a primary air pollutant, on construction work accidents, a significant factor related to labor market productivity losses. Using data from all construction sites and pollution monitoring stations in Israel, we find a strong and significant effect on accidents, with a 377% (138%) increase on high (moderate) NO2 pollution days compared to clean air levels. Our mechanism analysis suggests the effect is exacerbated under cognitive strain or worker fatigue. A cost-benefit analysis, supported by a nonparametric estimation, examines subsidizing site closures on highly polluted days.

中文摘要

空气污染会对生理和认知表现产生不利影响。本研究估计了主要空气污染物二氧化氮（NO2）增加对建筑工作事故的因果效应，这是与劳动力市场生产力损失相关的一个重要因素。利用以色列所有建筑工地和污染监测站的数据，我们发现对事故有强烈且显著的影响：与清洁空气水平相比，高（中等）NO2污染日的事故增加了377%（138%）。我们的机制分析表明，这种效应在认知压力或工人疲劳下会加剧。一项由非参数估计支持的成本效益分析，考察了在高污染日补贴工地关闭的情况。

Preferences, Inequities, and Incentives in the Substitute Teacher Labor Market

代课教师劳动力市场中的偏好、不平等与激励

Matthew A. Kraft, Megan Lane Conklin, Grace T. Falken

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 西班牙

Abstract

We examine the labor supply decisions of substitute teachers – a large, on-demand market with broad shortages and inequitable supply. In 2018, Chicago Public Schools implemented a targeted bonus program designed to reduce unfilled teacher absences in largely segregated Black schools with historically low substitute coverage rates. Using a regression discontinuity design, we find that incentive pay substantially improved coverage equity and raised student achievement. Changes in labor supply were concentrated among Black and Hispanic substitutes from nearby neighborhoods with experience in incentive schools. Wage elasticity estimates suggest incentives would need to be 50% of daily wages to close fill-rate gaps.

中文摘要

我们研究了代课教师的劳动力供给决策——这是一个庞大的、按需的市场，存在广泛的短缺和不公平的供给。2018年，芝加哥公立学校实施了一项有针对性的奖金计划，旨在减少在历史上代课覆盖率低、高度隔离的黑人学校中未填补的教师缺勤。利用断点回归设计，我们发现激励性薪酬显著改善了覆盖公平性并提高了学生成绩。劳动力供给的变化集中在来自附近社区、在激励学校有经验的黑人和西班牙裔代课教师中。工资弹性估计表明，要填补覆盖率差距，激励措施需要达到日工资的50%。

Pros and Cons of Globalization: Income-Based Attitudes

全球化的利弊：基于收入的态度的分析

Assaf Razin

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Regulating Untaxable Externalities: Are Vehicle Air Pollution Standards Effective and Efficient?

监管不可征税的外部性：车辆空气污染标准是否有效且高效？

Mark R. Jacobsen, James M. Sallee, Joseph S. Shapiro, Arthur A. van Benthem

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

The world has 1.4 billion passenger vehicles. How should governments regulate their air pollution emissions? A Pigouvian tax is technologically infeasible. Most countries instead rely on exhaust standards that limit air pollution emissions per mile for new vehicles. We assess the effectiveness and efficiency of these standards, which are the centerpiece of US Clean Air Act regulation of transportation, and counterfactual policies. We show that the air pollution emissions per mile of new US vehicles has fallen spectacularly, by over 99 percent, since standards began in 1967. Several research designs with a half century of data suggest that exhaust standards have caused most of this decline. Yet exhaust standards are not cost-effective in part because they fail to encourage scrap of older vehicles, which account for the majority of emissions. To study counterfactual policies, we develop an analytical and a quantitative model of the vehicle fleet. Analysis of these models suggests that tighter exhaust standards increase social welfare and that increasing registration fees on dirty vehicles yields even larger gains by accelerating scrap, though both reforms have complex effects on inequality.

中文摘要

全球有14亿辆乘用车。政府应如何监管其空气污染排放？庇古税在技术上不可行。大多数国家转而依赖排放标准，限制新车每英里的空气污染排放量。我们评估了这些标准（美国《清洁空气法》对交通运输监管的核心内容）以及反事实政策的有效性和效率。我们表明，自1967年标准开始实施以来，美国新车的每英里空气污染排放量已惊人地下降了超过99%。利用半个世纪数据的多项研究设计表明，排放标准是造成这一下降的主要原因。然而，排放标准并非成本有效，部分原因在于它们未能鼓励淘汰占排放多数的老旧车辆。为了研究反事实政策，我们开发了一个分析模型和一个车辆保有量定量模型。对这些模型的分析表明，更严格的排放标准能提高社会福利，而提高高污染车辆的注册费通过加速淘汰能带来更大的收益，尽管这两项改革都会对不平等产生复杂的影响。

What Do Changes in State Test Scores Imply for Later Life Outcomes?

州考试成绩变化对后期生活结果意味着什么？

Elena Doty, Thomas J. Kane, Tyler Patterson, Douglas O. Staiger

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

In the three decades before the pandemic, mean achievement of U.S. 8th graders in math rose by more than half a standard deviation on the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP). Between 2019 and 2022, U.S. students had forfeited 40 percent of that rise. To anticipate the consequences of the recent decline, we investigate the past relationship between NAEP scores and students’ later life outcomes by year and state of birth. We find that a standard deviation improvement in a birth cohort’s 8th grade math achievement was associated with an 8 percent rise in income, as well as improved educational attainment and declines in teen motherhood, incarceration and arrest rates. If allowed to become permanent, our findings imply that the recent losses would represent a 1.6 percent decline in present value of lifetime earnings for the average K-12 student (or $19,400), totaling $900 billion for the 48 million students enrolled in public schools during the 2020-21 school year.

中文摘要

在疫情前的三十年里，美国八年级学生在国家教育进步评估（NAEP）中的数学平均成绩提高了超过半个标准差。在2019年至2022年间，美国学生损失了这一增长幅度的40%。为了预测近期下降的后果，我们按出生年份和州调查了NAEP分数与学生后期生活结果之间的历史关系。我们发现，一个出生队列八年级数学成绩提高一个标准差，与其收入增长8%相关，同时也与教育程度提高以及青少年生育率、监禁率和逮捕率下降相关。如果这些近期损失成为永久性的，我们的研究结果表明，对于普通K-12学生而言，这将意味着终身收入现值下降1.6%（即19,400美元），对于2020-21学年入学的4800万公立学校学生来说，总计达9000亿美元。

Using Measures of Race to Make Clinical Predictions: Decision Making, Patient Health, and Fairness

使用种族指标进行临床预测：决策、患者健康与公平性

Charles F. Manski, John Mullahy, Atheendar Venkataramani

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The use of race measures in clinical prediction models and algorithms has become a highly contentious issue, driven by concerns that inclusion of race as a covariate exacerbates and perpetuates long-standing disparities in quality of health care provided to racial and ethnic minority patients. We seek to inform and ground this debate by evaluating the inclusion of race—even if imperfectly measured—in probabilistic predictions of illness that aim to inform clinical decision making. First, adopting a utilitarian framework to formalize social welfare, our analysis reveals that patients of all races are better off when clinical decisions are jointly guided by patient race and other observable covariates. In this sense, race is not a particularly special covariate: any covariate with predictive power (i.e., one that changes conditional probabilities of illness) should be used to optimize clinical decisions. We then extend the model to a two-period setting where prevention activities that address systemic drivers of disease are relevant and find that the same basic conclusions emerge. Finally, we discuss formal non-utilitarian concepts of fairness and disparity-aversion that have been proposed to guide societal allocation of health care resources.

中文摘要

在临床预测模型和算法中使用种族指标已成为一个高度争议的问题，其驱动力是担心将种族作为协变量会加剧并延续向种族和族裔少数患者提供的医疗服务质量方面的长期差异。我们旨在通过评估在旨在为临床决策提供信息的疾病概率预测中包含种族指标（即使测量不完美）来为这场辩论提供信息和基础。首先，采用功利主义框架来形式化社会福利，我们的分析表明，当临床决策由患者种族和其他可观察协变量共同指导时，所有种族的患者都会受益。从这个意义上说，种族并不是一个特别特殊的协变量：任何具有预测能力的协变量（即改变疾病条件概率的协变量）都应用于优化临床决策。然后，我们将模型扩展到两期设定，其中涉及解决疾病系统性驱动因素的预防活动是相关的，并发现相同的基本结论出现。最后，我们讨论了旨在指导医疗资源社会分配的正式的非功利主义公平和差异厌恶概念。

Picking Winners? Government Subsidies and Firm Productivity in China

挑选赢家？中国政府补贴与企业生产率

Lee G. Branstetter, Guangwei Li, Mengjia Ren

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政

Abstract

Are Chinese industrial policies making the targeted Chinese firms more productive? Alternatively, are efforts to promote productivity undercut by efforts to maintain or expand employment in less productive enterprises? In this paper, we attempt to shed light on these questions through the analysis of previously underutilized microdata on direct government subsidies provided to China’s publicly traded firms. We categorize subsidies into different types. We then estimate total-factor productivity (TFP) for Chinese listed firms and investigate the relationship between these estimates of TFP and the allocation of government subsidies. We find little evidence that the Chinese government consistently “picks winners”. Firms’ ex-ante productivity is negatively correlated with subsidies received by firms, and subsidies appear to have a negative impact on firms’ ex-post productivity growth throughout our data window, 2007 to 2018. Neither subsidies given out under the name of R&D and innovation promotion nor industrial and equipment upgrading positively affect firms’ productivity growth. On the other hand, we find a positive impact of subsidy on current year employment, both for the aggregated and employment-related subsidies. These findings suggest that China’s increasingly prescriptive industrial policies may have generated limited effects in promoting productivity.

中文摘要

中国的产业政策是否让目标中国企业变得更具生产力？或者，提高生产力的努力是否被维持或扩大低生产率企业就业的努力所削弱？在本文中，我们试图通过分析先前未充分利用的关于中国上市公司直接政府补贴的微观数据来阐明这些问题。我们将补贴分为不同类型。然后，我们估计了中国上市公司的全要素生产率（TFP），并研究了这些TFP估计值与政府补贴分配之间的关系。我们几乎没有发现中国政府一贯“挑选赢家”的证据。企业的事前生产率与所获补贴呈负相关，并且在整个数据窗口期（2007年至2018年），补贴似乎对企业的事后生产率增长产生了负面影响。无论是名义上用于研发和创新促进的补贴，还是用于工业和设备升级的补贴，都没有对企业的生产率增长产生积极影响。另一方面，我们发现补贴对当年就业有积极影响，无论是总体补贴还是与就业相关的补贴。这些发现表明，中国日益指令性的产业政策在促进生产率方面可能效果有限。

Taxes and the Labor Supply of the Stars

税收与明星的劳动供给

Daniel Keniston, Abigail Allison M. Peralta

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Do high taxes cause superstars to work less? We test this hypothesis using complete data on Hollywood movie stars' labor supply from 1927 to 2014. Changes to marginal tax rates in high tax brackets have no significant effect on the number of films a movie star makes each year. However, in years with high taxes, stars produce more highly rated movies with award-winning directors, potentially substituting prestigious films for pecuniary gains

Funding Black High-Growth Startups

资助黑人高增长初创企业

Lisa D. Cook, Matt Marx, Emmanuel Yimfor

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We analyze determinants of access to venture capital for Black founders of high-growth startups. We combine image- and name-processing algorithms with clerical review to identify race for over 100,000 startup founders “at risk” for venture funding. Black founders raise roughly one-third as much venture capital in the five years after founding vs. other startups formed in the same year, industry, and state. What explains the gap? We attribute about a third of the gap to four factors: Black startups have smaller founding teams; Black founders are less likely to have worked at the same companies or attended the same schools as investors who fund startups in the same industry and geography; Black startups are less likely to be located in geographies with plentiful venture capital, and Black startups are less likely to have a patent. We then bound our estimates of the funding gap and show that, to explain the gap, omitted variables would have to be nearly four times as important as the variables we fix. The funding gap is not statistically different from zero in later funding stages (post Series B), suggesting that some investors initially hold—but later amend—incorrect beliefs about Black-founded startups. We also exploit the hiring of Black partners and show that they are more likely to fund successful Black startups, consistent with segmented networks.

中文摘要

我们分析了黑人创始人创办的高增长初创企业获得风险投资的决定因素。我们将图像和姓名处理算法与文书审查相结合，以识别超过10万名“有风险”获得风险投资的初创企业创始人的种族。在成立后的五年内，黑人创始人筹集的风险资本大约是同一年、同一行业和同一州成立的其他初创企业的三分之一。是什么造成了这一差距？我们将大约三分之一的差距归因于四个因素：黑人初创企业的创始团队规模较小；黑人创始人不太可能曾在与投资同一行业和地理区域初创企业的投资者相同的公司工作或就读相同的学校；黑人初创企业不太可能位于风险投资充足的地理区域；以及黑人初创企业不太可能拥有专利。然后，我们界定了资金缺口估计值的范围，并表明，要解释这一缺口，遗漏变量的重要性必须是我们所固定变量的近四倍。在后期融资阶段（B轮之后），资金缺口在统计上与零无显著差异，这表明一些投资者最初对黑人创办的初创企业持有错误的信念，但后来修正了这些信念。我们还利用了黑人合伙人的招聘情况，并表明他们更有可能资助成功的黑人初创企业，这与细分网络的观点一致。

The Regulation of Medical AI: Policy Approaches, Data, and Innovation Incentives

医疗人工智能的监管：政策方法、数据与创新激励

Ariel Dora Stern

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

For those who follow health and technology news, it is difficult to go more than a few days without reading about a compelling new application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) to health care. AI has myriad applications in medicine and its adjacent industries, with AI-driven tools already in use in basic science, translational medicine, and numerous corners of health care delivery, including administrative work, diagnosis, and treatment. In diagnosis and treatment, a large and growing number of AI tools meet the statutory definition of a medical device or that of an in-vitro diagnostic. Those that do are subject to regulation by local authorities, resulting in both practical and strategic implications for manufacturers, along with a more complex set of innovation incentives. This chapter presents background on medical device regulation—especially as it relates to software products—and quantitatively describes the emergence of AI among FDA-regulated products. The empirical section of this chapter explores characteristics of AI-supported/driven medical devices (“AI devices”) in the United States. It presents data on their origins (by firm type and country), their safety profiles (as measured by associated adverse events and recalls), and concludes with a discussion of the implications of regulation for innovation incentives in medical AI.

中文摘要

对于那些关注健康和技术新闻的人来说，很难超过几天不读到关于人工智能在医疗保健领域令人信服的新应用。人工智能在医学及其相关行业有无数应用，人工智能驱动的工具已用于基础科学、转化医学以及医疗保健服务的众多角落，包括行政工作、诊断和治疗。在诊断和治疗方面，大量且不断增长的人工智能工具符合医疗器械或体外诊断的法定定义。那些符合定义的器械需接受当地监管机构的监管，这给制造商带来了实际和战略上的影响，以及一套更复杂的创新激励。本章介绍了医疗器械监管的背景——特别是与软件产品相关的部分，并定量描述了人工智能在FDA监管产品中的出现。本章的实证部分探讨了美国人工智能支持/驱动的医疗器械（“人工智能设备”）的特征。它提供了关于其来源（按公司类型和国家）、安全概况（以相关不良事件和召回衡量）的数据，并最后讨论了监管对医疗人工智能创新激励的影响。
