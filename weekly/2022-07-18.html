No. 1论文详情NBER w30268

What Motivates Leaders to Invest in Nation-Building?

什么激励领导者投资于国家建设？

Paola Giuliano, Bryony Reich, Alessandro Riboni

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Why do some leaders invest in significant nation-building policies and others do not? Why does nation-building occur at certain junctures in time and not others? In our research, we investigate what motivates leaders to nation build. We argue that threats to their regime motivate rulers to invest in significant nation-building and that the type of threats that provoke nation-building have largely materialized since the 19th century.

中文摘要

为什么有些领导者会投资于重大的国家建设政策，而另一些则不会？为什么国家建设会在某些历史节点发生，而在其他时候则不会？在我们的研究中，我们调查了是什么激励领导者进行国家建设。我们认为，对其政权的威胁促使统治者投资于重大的国家建设，而自19世纪以来，引发国家建设的威胁类型已基本显现。

No. 2论文详情NBER w30267

A New Interpretation of Productivity Growth Dynamics in the Pre-Pandemic and Pandemic Era U.S. Economy, 1950-2022

对疫情前和疫情时代美国经济（1950-2022）生产率增长动态的新解释

Robert J. Gordon, Hassan Sayed

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

The dismal decade of 2010-19 recorded the slowest productivity growth of any decade in U.S. history, only 1.1 percent per year in the business sector. Yet the pandemic appears to have created a resurgence in productivity growth with a 4.1 percent rate achieved in the four quarters of 2020. This paper provides a unified framework that explains productivity growth in both the pre-pandemic and pandemic-era U.S. economy. The key insight is that in their panicked reaction to the collapse of output in the 2008-09 recession, business firms overreacted with “excess layoffs,” adjusting hours to the output decline with a far higher elasticity than normal. Our regression analysis, which allows post-recession rehiring that gradually unwinds the excess layoffs, explains why productivity growth was countercyclical in 2009 and why it was so slow in 2010-16 as rehiring boosted hours growth. Post-sample simulations explain why productivity growth was so high in 2020 and why it fell to only 0.6 percent in the five quarters of 2021-22. The paper includes implications for the future long-term evolution of productivity growth in the business sector and total economy. A new data file on quarterly productivity levels and changes for 17 industries provides new perspectives for 2006-22 and particularly for the nine pandemic quarters of 2020-22. Positive pandemic-era productivity growth can be entirely explained by a surge in the performance of work-from-home service industries, while goods industries soared and then slumped, while contact services recorded strongly negative productivity growth throughout 2020-22.

中文摘要

2010-19年这惨淡的十年是美国历史上生产率增长最慢的十年，商业部门年均增长率仅为1.1%。然而，疫情似乎引发了生产率增长的复苏，2020年四个季度的增长率达到4.1%。本文提供了一个统一的框架来解释疫情前和疫情时代美国经济的生产率增长。关键见解在于，在2008-09年经济衰退中产出崩溃的恐慌反应下，企业过度反应，进行了“过度裁员”，以远高于正常水平的弹性将工时调整到产出下降。我们的回归分析允许衰退后的重新雇佣逐步消除过度裁员，这解释了为什么2009年生产率增长是逆周期的，以及为什么2010-16年生产率增长如此缓慢，因为重新雇佣推动了工时增长。样本外模拟解释了为什么2020年生产率增长如此之高，以及为什么在2021-22年的五个季度中生产率增长降至仅0.6%。本文还包含了对商业部门和整体经济未来长期生产率增长演变的启示。一个关于17个行业季度生产率水平和变化的新数据文件为2006-22年，特别是2020-22年九个疫情季度提供了新的视角。疫情时代的正生产率增长可以完全归因于居家办公服务业表现的激增，而商品行业先飙升后下滑，接触性服务业在2020-22年全年则录得强烈的负生产率增长。

No. 3论文详情NBER w30266

Firms and Unemployment Insurance Take-up

企业与失业保险申领行为

Marta Lachowska, Isaac Sorkin, Stephen A. Woodbury

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use administrative data to quantify the employer’s role in unemployment insurance (UI) take-up. First, there are employer effects in both claiming and appeals, and, consistent with deterrence effects, these are negatively correlated. Second, low-wage workers are less likely to claim and more likely to have their claims appealed than median-wage workers, and employer effects explain a large share of these income gradients. Third, high-claiming and low-appealing employers are desirable employers: they are higher-paying and have lower separation rates. Finally, the dominant source of targeting error in the UI system is that eligible workers do not apply. Our findings emphasize a novel dimension of the role of employers in the labor market, and have implications for the financing of UI.

中文摘要

我们利用行政数据量化了雇主在失业保险申领中的作用。首先，雇主效应同时存在于申领和申诉环节，且与威慑效应一致，两者呈负相关。其次，与中位工资工人相比，低工资工人申领失业保险的可能性更低，而其申领被申诉的可能性更高，雇主效应解释了这些收入梯度的很大一部分。第三，高申领率和低申诉率的雇主是理想的雇主：他们支付更高的工资，且离职率更低。最后，失业保险体系中目标错误的主要来源是符合条件的工人没有提出申请。我们的研究结果强调了雇主在劳动力市场中作用的一个新维度，并对失业保险的融资具有启示意义。

No. 4论文详情NBER w30265

Fighting Climate Change: International Attitudes Toward Climate Policies

应对气候变化：国际社会对气候政策的态度

Antoine Dechezleprêtre, Adrien Fabre, Tobias Kruse, Bluebery Planterose, Ana Sanchez Chico, Stefanie Stantcheva

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper explores global perceptions and understanding of climate change and policies, examining factors that influence support for climate action and the impact of different types of information. We conduct large-scale surveys with 40,000 respondents from 20 countries, providing new international data on attitudes towards climate change and respondents’ socioeconomic backgrounds and lifestyles. We identify three key perceptions affecting policy support: perceived effectiveness of policies in reducing emissions, their impact on low-income households, and their effect on respondents’ households (self-interest). Educational videos clarifying policy mechanisms increase support for climate policies; those merely highlighting climate change’s impacts do not.

中文摘要

本文探讨了全球对气候变化及政策的认知与理解，考察了影响气候行动支持度的因素以及不同类型信息的影响。我们对来自20个国家的40,000名受访者进行了大规模调查，提供了关于气候变化态度以及受访者社会经济背景和生活方式的新国际数据。我们识别出影响政策支持的三个关键认知：政策在减排方面的感知有效性、对低收入家庭的影响，以及对受访者自身家庭的影响（自利动机）。阐明政策机制的教育视频增加了对气候政策的支持；而仅仅强调气候变化影响的视频则没有这种效果。

New Pricing Models, Same Old Phillips Curves?

新定价模型，相同的旧菲利普斯曲线？

Adrien Auclert, Rodolfo D. Rigato, Matthew Rognlie, Ludwig Straub

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We show that, in a broad class of menu cost models, the first-order dynamics of aggregate inflation in response to arbitrary shocks to aggregate costs are nearly the same as in Calvo models with suitably chosen Calvo adjustment frequencies. We first prove that the canonical menu cost model is first-order equivalent to a mixture of two time-dependent models, which reflect the extensive and intensive margins of price adjustment. We then show numerically that, in any plausible parameterization, this mixture is well-approximated by a single Calvo model. This close numerical fit carries over to other standard specifications of menu cost models. Thus, for shocks that are not too large, the Phillips curve for a menu cost model looks like the New Keynesian Phillips curve, but with a higher slope.

中文摘要

我们证明，在一大类菜单成本模型中，总通胀对总成本任意冲击的一阶动态与具有适当选择的Calvo调整频率的Calvo模型几乎相同。我们首先证明，经典的菜单成本模型在一阶上等价于两个时间依赖模型的混合，这两个模型分别反映了价格调整的广延边际和集约边际。然后，我们通过数值模拟表明，在任何合理的参数化下，这种混合可以被一个单一的Calvo模型很好地近似。这种紧密的数值拟合也适用于菜单成本模型的其他标准设定。因此，对于不太大的冲击，菜单成本模型的菲利普斯曲线看起来像新凯恩斯菲利普斯曲线，但具有更高的斜率。

No. 6论文详情NBER w30263

Carbon Pricing, Clean Electricity Standards, and Clean Electricity Subsidies on the Path to Zero Emissions

通往零排放道路上的碳定价、清洁电力标准与清洁电力补贴

Severin Borenstein, Ryan Kellogg

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

We categorize the primary incentive-based mechanisms under consideration for addressing greenhouse gas emissions from electricity generation—pricing carbon, setting intensity standards, and subsidizing clean energy—and compare their market outcomes under similar expansions of clean electricity generation. While pricing emissions gives strong incentives to first eliminate generation with the highest social cost, a clean energy standard incentivizes earliest phaseout of the generation with the highest private cost. We show that the importance of this distinction depends on the correlation between private costs and emissions rates. We then estimate this correlation for US electricity generation and fuel prices as of 2019. The results indicate that the emissions difference between a carbon tax and clean energy standard that phase out fossil fuel generation over the same timeframe may actually be quite small, though it depends on fossil fuel prices during the phaseout. We also discuss how each of these policy options is likely to impact electricity prices, quantity demanded, government revenue, and economic efficiency. Large pre-existing markups of retail electricity prices over marginal costs are likely to considerably weaken or even reverse the usual assumed efficiency advantage of carbon pricing policies over alternatives, including direct subsidization of clean electricity generation.

中文摘要

我们将目前考虑用于解决电力生产温室气体排放的主要基于激励的机制——碳定价、设定强度标准和补贴清洁能源——进行分类，并在清洁电力发电量类似扩张的情况下比较它们的市场结果。虽然排放定价强烈激励首先淘汰社会成本最高的发电方式，但清洁能源标准则激励首先淘汰私人成本最高的发电方式。我们表明，这种区别的重要性取决于私人成本与排放率之间的相关性。然后，我们估算了截至2019年美国电力生产和燃料价格的这种相关性。结果表明，在相同时间框架内逐步淘汰化石燃料发电的碳税和清洁能源标准之间的排放差异实际上可能非常小，尽管这取决于淘汰期间的化石燃料价格。我们还讨论了每种政策选项可能如何影响电价、需求量、政府收入和经济效率。零售电价相对于边际成本存在巨大的预先加价，这很可能大大削弱甚至逆转通常假设的碳定价政策相对于其他替代方案（包括直接补贴清洁电力发电）的效率优势。

Confidence, Self-Selection and Bias in the Aggregate

信心、自我选择与总体偏差

Benjamin Enke, Thomas Graeber, Ryan Oprea

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The influence of behavioral biases on aggregate outcomes like prices and allocations depends in part on self-selection: whether rational people opt more strongly into aggregate interactions than biased individuals. We conduct a series of betting market, auction and committee experiments using 15 classic cognitive bias tasks. We document that some cognitive errors are strongly reduced through self-selection, while others are not affected at all. A large part of this variation is explained by the quality of people's meta-cognition. In some cognitive tasks, confidence and performance are strongly positively correlated, while for others this link is absent or even negative.

中文摘要

行为偏差对价格和配置等总体结果的影响部分取决于自我选择：理性人是否比有偏差的个体更强烈地选择参与总体互动。我们利用15个经典认知偏差任务进行了一系列博彩市场、拍卖和委员会实验。我们记录到，一些认知错误通过自我选择被大大减少，而另一些则完全不受影响。这种差异的很大一部分可以通过人们元认知的质量来解释。在某些认知任务中，信心和表现呈强正相关，而在其他任务中，这种联系不存在甚至是负的。

No. 8论文详情NBER w30261

New Evidence on the Demand for Advice within Retirement Plans

退休计划内咨询需求的新证据

Jonathan Reuter, David P. Richardson

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study demand for advice within defined contribution retirement plans offered by 23 institutions where TIAA is sole recordkeeper. Advice seeking increases with age, account balance, annual contribution level, web access, and changes in marital status. More provocatively, participants who invest solely through target date funds—the dominant default investment option—are significantly less likely to seek any form of advice throughout the age distribution, raising the possibility that reliance upon defaults crowds out advice seeking. Advice seeking increases significantly following the introduction of online tools, but is only weakly correlated with market returns and investment menu changes.

中文摘要

我们研究了由23家机构提供的固定缴款退休计划中对咨询的需求，这些机构中TIAA是唯一的记录保管人。寻求咨询的行为随年龄、账户余额、年度缴款水平、网络访问权限以及婚姻状况的变化而增加。更具争议性的是，仅通过目标日期基金（主要的默认投资选项）进行投资的参与者，在整个年龄分布中寻求任何形式咨询的可能性都显著降低，这增加了依赖默认选项会挤出咨询寻求行为的可能性。在线工具的引入显著增加了寻求咨询的行为，但该行为与市场回报和投资菜单变化仅呈弱相关。

No. 9论文详情NBER w30260

Expectations and the Rate of Inflation

预期与通货膨胀率

Iván Werning

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

What is the effect of higher expectations of future inflation on current inflation? I compute this passthrough for a series of canonical firm-pricing models, but allowing for arbitrary (non-rational) expectations. In the Calvo model, the expectational-passthrough can be made arbitrarily close to zero for sufficiently high stickiness, but in practice, for reasonable parameters, passthrough is close to its upper bound of 1. In the Taylor model, in contrast, the upper bound for passthrough is ½ instead of 1. For a general time-dependent model I show that: (i) passthrough is given by a measurable sufficient statistic: the ratio of the average duration of ongoing price spells to that of completed price spells; (ii) the lowest theoretically possible passthrough equals ½ by Taylor pricing; and (iii) passthrough can be theoretically greater than 1 with hazards that decrease over time; (iv) breaking down the passthrough across horizons, it is expectations in the near future that matters the most, expectations of long-run inflation are completely irrelevant; (v) I provide a generalized Phillips curve for current inflation as a linear function of expectations of future inflation and realized past inflations; (vi) I show that the sum of all coefficients, both past and future, sums to one, so that the long-run Phillips curve is vertical. Finally, I study state-dependent “menu cost” models and show that passthrough in these models can be extremely low or extremely high, depending on the exact specification and inflation rate. I suggest a model where firms must pay a fixed cost for changing their sS pricing policy bands. This extension gives a passthrough of 0 for small enough changes in expectations.

Abstract

GDP is a closely watched indicator of the current health of the economy and an indispensable tool of economic policy. It is not, however, a persuasive indicator of individual wellbeing or collective economic progress. There have been calls to refocus or replace GDP with a metric that better reflects the welfare dimension. In response, the U.S. agency responsible for the GDP accounts recently launched a “GDP and Beyond” program.
