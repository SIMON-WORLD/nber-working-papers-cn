How Important are Minimum Wage Increases in Increasing the Wages of Minimum Wage Workers?

最低工资上涨对提高最低工资工人工资有多重要？

Jeffrey Clemens, Michael R. Strain

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Popular discussion commonly presumes an outsized role for minimum wage increases as a driver of wage increases for minimum wage workers. In this paper, we investigate the accuracy of this presumption using data from the earnings studies of the Current Population Survey (CPS). CPS wage and earnings data enable us to assess the fraction of minimum wage workers who receive a raise within 12 months of their initial appearance as a minimum wage worker. On average from 2010 to 2019, we find that roughly 75 percent of minimum wage workers who remain employed experience a wage increase within 12 months. This fraction is higher during the later years of the sample, when the labor market has been strong, than in the earlier years. The fraction of minimum wage workers receiving wage increases is moderately higher when states enact minimum wage increases than when they do not. We also find that the fraction of minimum wage workers receiving wage increases is correlated with several measures of labor market tightness. Finally, wage gains are quite commonly associated with industry and/or occupation switches. This highlights the importance of career progression for the growth of earnings among entry-level workers. The vast majority of the wage gains realized by minimum wage workers thus appear to be driven by career progression and increases in labor demand. Minimum wage increases play a modest role as a driver of earnings trajectories beyond shaping the initial, typically short-lived, minimum wage job itself.

中文摘要

公众讨论通常假定最低工资上涨在推动最低工资工人工资增长中扮演着过大的角色。在本文中，我们利用当前人口调查（CPS）的工资研究数据来调查这一假定的准确性。CPS的工资和收入数据使我们能够评估在首次成为最低工资工人后12个月内获得加薪的最低工资工人比例。平均而言，从2010年到2019年，我们发现大约75%的仍受雇用的最低工资工人在12个月内经历了工资增长。这一比例在样本后期（劳动力市场强劲时）高于早期。当各州实施最低工资上涨时，获得加薪的最低工资工人比例略高于未实施时。我们还发现，获得加薪的最低工资工人比例与几个劳动力市场紧张程度的衡量指标相关。最后，工资增长通常与行业和/或职业转换相关。这凸显了职业发展对入门级工人收入增长的重要性。因此，最低工资工人实现的绝大多数工资增长似乎是由职业发展和劳动力需求增加驱动的。最低工资上涨在塑造最初的、通常是短暂的最低工资工作本身之外，对收入轨迹的推动作用较为温和。

Effects of the Expanded Child Tax Credit on Employment Outcomes: Evidence from Real-World Data from April to December 2021

扩大儿童税收抵免对就业结果的影响：来自2021年4月至12月真实世界数据的证据

Elizabeth Ananat, Benjamin Glasner, Christal Hamilton, Zachary Parolin

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

Studies have established that the expanded Child Tax Credit (CTC), which provided monthly cash payments to most U.S. families with children from July to December 2021, substantially reduced poverty and food hardship. Other studies posit, however, that the CTC payments may generate negative employment effects that could offset its potential poverty-reduction effects. Scholars have simulated employment scenarios assuming various labor supply elasticities, but less work has empirically assessed how the monthly payments affected employment outcomes using real-world data. To evaluate employment effects, we apply a series of difference-in-differences analyses using data from the monthly Current Population Survey and the Census Pulse, both from April through December 2021. Across both samples and several model specifications, we find very small, inconsistently signed, and statistically insignificant impacts of the CTC both on employment in the prior week and on active participation in the labor force among adults living in households with children. Further, labor supply responses to the policy change do not differ for households for whom the CTC’s expansion eliminated a previous work incentive. Thus, our analyses of real-world data suggest that the expanded CTC did not have negative short-term employment effects that offset its documented reductions in poverty and hardship.

中文摘要

已有研究证实，扩大儿童税收抵免（CTC）——该政策于2021年7月至12月向大多数有孩子的美国家庭提供每月现金支付——显著减少了贫困和食品困难。然而，其他研究认为，CTC支付可能产生负面就业效应，从而抵消其潜在的减贫效果。学者们假设了各种劳动供给弹性来模拟就业情景，但利用真实世界数据实证评估每月支付如何影响就业结果的研究较少。为了评估就业效应，我们使用2021年4月至12月的月度当前人口调查和人口普查脉冲数据，应用了一系列双重差分分析。在两个样本和多种模型设定中，我们发现CTC对生活在有孩子家庭中的成年人过去一周的就业以及积极参与劳动力市场的影响非常小、符号不一致且统计上不显著。此外，对于CTC扩大消除了先前工作激励的家庭，其对政策变化的劳动供给反应并无差异。因此，我们对真实世界数据的分析表明，扩大CTC并未产生负面的短期就业效应，从而抵消其已证实的贫困和困难减少效果。

School Choice

择校

Atila Abdulkadiroglu, Tommy Andersson

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

School districts in the US and around the world are increasingly moving away from traditional neighborhood school assignment, in which pupils attend closest schools to their homes. Instead, they allow families to choose from schools within district boundaries. This creates a market with parental demand over publicly-supplied school seats. More frequently than ever, this market for school seats is cleared via market design solutions grounded in recent advances in matching and mechanism design theory. The literature on school choice is reviewed with emphasis placed on the trade-offs among policy objectives and best practices in the design of admissions processes. It is concluded with a brief discussion about how data generated by assignment algorithms can be used to answer contemporary empirical questions about school effectiveness and policy interventions.

中文摘要

美国及世界各地的学区正日益摆脱传统的就近入学分配模式（即学生就读离家最近的学校），转而允许家庭在学区范围内选择学校。这形成了一个由家长对公立学校座位需求构成的市场。如今，这一学校座位市场比以往任何时候都更频繁地通过基于匹配与机制设计理论最新进展的市场设计解决方案来出清。本文回顾了关于择校的文献，重点阐述了政策目标之间的权衡以及招生流程设计中的最佳实践。最后，简要讨论了如何利用分配算法产生的数据来回答关于学校效能和政策干预的当代实证问题。

Dynamic Trading with Realization Utility

基于实现效用的动态交易

Min Dai, Cong Qin, Neng Wang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

An investor receives utility bursts from realizing gains and losses at the individual-stock level (Barberis and Xiong, 2009, 2012; Ingersoll and Jin, 2013) and dynamically allocates his mental budget between risky and risk-free assets at the trading-account level. Using savings, he reduces his stockholdings and is more willing to realize losses. Using leverage, he increases his stockholdings beyond his mental budget and is more reluctant to realize losses. While leverage strengthens the disposition effect, introducing leverage constraints mitigates it. Our model predicts that investors with stocks in deep losses sell them either immediately or after stocks rebound a little.

中文摘要

一位投资者从个股层面实现收益和损失中获得效用爆发（Barberis and Xiong, 2009, 2012; Ingersoll and Jin, 2013），并在交易账户层面动态地在风险资产和无风险资产之间分配其心理预算。通过储蓄，他减少股票持有量，并更愿意实现损失。通过杠杆，他增加股票持有量超出其心理预算，并更不愿意实现损失。虽然杠杆强化了处置效应，但引入杠杆约束则缓解了该效应。我们的模型预测，持有深度亏损股票的投资者要么立即卖出这些股票，要么在股票小幅反弹后卖出。

Systemic Discrimination: Theory and Measurement

系统性歧视：理论与测度

J. Aislinn Bohren, Peter Hull, Alex Imas

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Economists often measure discrimination as disparities arising from the direct effects of group identity. We develop new tools to model and measure systemic discrimination, which instead captures how discrimination in other decisions indirectly contributes to disparities. We propose an experimental design, the Iterated Audit, to identify systemic discrimination. We then illustrate these new tools in two field experiments. The first experiment shows how racial discrimination accumulates across multiple rounds of hiring through the interaction of two forces: greater discrimination against inexperienced workers—which affects the opportunity to obtain experience—and high subsequent returns to experience. The second experiment shows how gender-based differences in the language of recommendation letters can translate into systemic gender discrimination in STEM hiring. We discuss how our findings qualify previous results on direct discrimination and outline how our tools can be used to target policy interventions.

中文摘要

经济学家通常将歧视测度为源于群体身份直接效应的差异。我们开发了新的工具来建模和测度系统性歧视，后者则捕捉其他决策中的歧视如何间接导致差异。我们提出了一种实验设计——迭代审计——来识别系统性歧视。然后，我们在两个实地实验中展示了这些新工具。第一个实验展示了种族歧视如何通过两种力量的相互作用在多次招聘轮次中累积：对缺乏经验工人的更大歧视（这影响了获得经验的机会）以及经验随后带来的高回报。第二个实验展示了推荐信语言中的性别差异如何转化为STEM招聘中的系统性性别歧视。我们讨论了我们的发现如何对先前关于直接歧视的研究结果进行限定，并概述了我们的工具如何用于针对政策干预。

Capital Taxation, Development, and Globalization: Evidence from a Macro-Historical Database

资本税、发展与全球化：来自宏观历史数据库的证据

Pierre Bachas, Matthew H. Fisher-Post, Anders Jensen, Gabriel Zucman

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper builds and analyzes a new global macro-historical database of effective tax rates on capital and labor in 154 countries. We establish a new stylized fact: while effective capital tax rates fell in developed countries between 1965 and 2018, they rose in developing countries since 1990. Multiple research designs at the country, sector and firm-level suggest that trade openness contributed to this rise, by increasing the share of output produced in corporations and larger firms, where effective capital taxation is higher. In contrast to a common view, globalization appears in many countries to have supported governments’ ability to tax capital.

中文摘要

本文构建并分析了一个新的全球宏观历史数据库，涵盖了154个国家对资本和劳动的有效税率。我们确立了一个新的典型事实：在1965年至2018年间，发达国家的有效资本税率下降，而发展中国家的有效资本税率自1990年以来却上升了。基于国家、行业和企业层面的多种研究设计表明，贸易开放通过提高产出在公司和大企业中的份额（这些地方的有效资本税率更高）促进了这一上升。与普遍观点相反，全球化在许多国家似乎支持了政府对资本征税的能力。

Economic Crises and Mental Health: Effects of the Great Recession on Older Americans

经济危机与心理健康：大衰退对美国老年人的影响

David M. Cutler, Noémie Sportiche

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 西班牙、美国

Abstract

We examine the effect of the Great Recession of 2007-2009 on the mental health of older adults, using longitudinal Health and Retirement Study data linked to area-level data on house prices. We use a variety of measures to capture mental health and rely on the very large cross-sectional variation in falling house prices to identify the impact of the Great Recession on those outcomes. We also account for people who moved in response to falling prices by fixing each person’s location immediately prior to the house price collapse. Our central finding is that the Great Recession had heterogeneous effects on health. While mental health was not affected for the average older adult, mental health declined among homeowners with few financial assets, who were therefore more vulnerable to falling house prices. Importantly, health impacts in this group differed by race and ethnicity: depression and functional limitations worsened among Black and other non-white homeowners and medication use increased among white homeowners. There were no measurable impacts for Hispanic homeowners. These results highlight the importance of examining heterogeneity across multiple dimensions when examining the health impacts of economic conditions.

中文摘要

我们利用与地区层面房价数据相关联的纵向健康与退休研究数据，考察了2007-2009年大衰退对老年人心理健康的影响。我们使用多种指标来捕捉心理健康状况，并依靠房价下跌的巨大横截面差异来识别大衰退对这些结果的影响。我们还通过将每个人的位置固定在其房价暴跌前的时点，来考虑因房价下跌而搬家的人群。我们的核心发现是，大衰退对健康产生了异质性影响。虽然平均而言老年人的心理健康未受影响，但在金融资产较少的房主中，心理健康状况下降，因为他们更容易受到房价下跌的影响。重要的是，该群体中的健康影响因种族和民族而异：黑人和其它非白人房主的抑郁和功能限制恶化，而白人房主的药物使用增加。对西班牙裔房主没有可测量的影响。这些结果强调了在考察经济状况对健康影响时，审视多个维度异质性的重要性。

Investing in the Next Generation: The Long-Run Impacts of a Liquidity Shock

投资下一代：流动性冲击的长期影响

Patrick Agte, Arielle Bernhardt, Erica M. Field, Rohini Pande, Natalia Rigol

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

How do poor entrepreneurs trade off investments in business enterprises versus children's human capital, and how do these choices influence intergenerational socio-economic mobility? To examine this, we exploit experimental variation in household income resulting from a one-time relaxation of household liquidity constraints (Field et al., 2013), and track schooling and business outcomes over the subsequent 11 years. On average, treatment households, who were made wealthier through the experiment, increase human capital investment such that their children are 35% more likely to attend college. However, schooling gains only accrue to children with literate parents, among whom college attendance nearly doubles. In contrast, treatment effects on investment among the illiterate accrue only on the business margin and are accompanied by adverse educational outcomes for children. As a result, treatment lowers relative educational mobility. In a forecasting exercise, we find that earnings gains for literate households are four times larger than the earnings gains for illiterate households, raising earnings inequality. Our findings highlight how parental investment choices can contribute to a growth in intergenerational earnings inequality despite reductions in urban poverty.

中文摘要

贫困企业家如何在企业投资与子女人力资本投资之间进行权衡？这些选择如何影响代际社会经济流动性？为考察这一点，我们利用一次性放松家庭流动性约束所产生的家庭收入实验性变化（Field et al., 2013），并追踪随后11年的教育和商业结果。平均而言，通过实验变得更富裕的处理组家庭增加了人力资本投资，使其子女上大学的概率提高了35%。然而，教育收益仅发生在父母有读写能力的儿童中，这些儿童的大学入学率几乎翻了一番。相比之下，对无读写能力家庭的处理效应仅体现在商业投资方面，并伴随着子女教育的不良后果。因此，处理降低了相对教育流动性。在一项预测练习中，我们发现，有读写能力家庭的收入增长是无读写能力家庭的四倍，从而加剧了收入不平等。我们的发现强调了尽管城市贫困减少，但父母投资选择如何可能导致代际收入不平等的加剧。

A Note on Temporary Supply Shocks with Aggregate Demand Inertia

关于具有总需求惯性的暂时性供给冲击的注记

Ricardo J. Caballero, Alp Simsek

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study optimal monetary policy during temporary supply contractions when aggregate demand has inertia and the central bank is concerned about future constraints on expansionary policy. In this environment, it is optimal to run the economy hot until supply recovers. However, the policy does not remain loose throughout the low-supply phase. Overall, when the initial aggregate demand is low, the goal is to frontload the rate cuts to raise demand in anticipation of the recovery of supply. If inflation also has inertia, the central bank still overheats the economy during the low-supply phase but gradually cools it down over time.

中文摘要

我们研究了在总需求具有惯性且央行担忧未来扩张性政策受限的情况下，面对暂时性供给收缩时的最优货币政策。在这种环境下，最优策略是在供给恢复前让经济过热运行。然而，政策在整个低供给阶段并非始终保持宽松。总体而言，当初始总需求较低时，目标是提前降息，以在供给复苏预期到来前提升需求。如果通胀也具有惯性，央行仍会在低供给阶段让经济过热，但会随时间推移逐步降温。

Improving Willingness-to-Pay Elicitation by Including a Benchmark Good

通过纳入基准商品改进支付意愿的诱导方法

Rebecca Dizon-Ross, Seema Jayachandran

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 乌干达

Abstract

We propose and validate a simple way to augment the standard Becker-DeGroot-Marschak method that researchers use to elicit willingness to pay (WTP) for a good. The augmentation is to measure WTP for another good ("benchmark good"), one unrelated to both the good the researcher is interested in and the independent variables of interest, and to use WTP for the benchmark good as a control variable in analyses. We illustrate the method and how it can eliminate noise in measured WTP using data collected in Uganda.

中文摘要

我们提出并验证了一种简单的方法，用于增强研究者常用的贝克尔-德格鲁特-马沙克方法，以诱导对某商品的支付意愿。该增强措施是测量另一种商品（“基准商品”）的支付意愿，这种商品既与研究者感兴趣的商品无关，也与所关注的自变量无关，并在分析中将基准商品的支付意愿作为控制变量使用。我们利用在乌干达收集的数据，展示了该方法及其如何消除测量支付意愿中的噪声。

No. 11论文详情NBER w29813中国相关

Indirect Effects of Access to Finance

融资可及性的间接效应

Jing Cai, Adam Szeidl

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价

Abstract

We created experimental variation across local markets in China in the share of firms having access to a new loan product, to measure the direct and indirect effects of access to finance. We find that: (1) Access to finance had a large positive direct effect on the performance of treated firms. (2) Access to finance had a similar-sized negative indirect effect on the performance of firms with treated competitors. The two effects offset in the aggregate and imply no detectable gains in producer surplus. (3) Access to finance had a positive direct effect on business practices, service quality, and consumer satisfaction, and a negative effect on price. None of these effects were offset by indirect effects, suggesting net gains in consumer surplus. (4) Two additional indirect effects were active: diffusion of borrowing to firms with treated peers, and diffusion of demand to firms with treated neighbors. (5) Combining several effects in a model-based evaluation, we estimate that the loan had a private return of 74%, most of which was offset by losses to competitors, and a social return of 60%, most of which was driven by gains to consumers.

中文摘要

我们在中国地方市场中实验性地创造了企业获得新贷款产品比例的差异，以衡量融资可及性的直接和间接效应。我们发现：（1）融资可及性对受处理企业的绩效有较大的正向直接效应。（2）融资可及性对与受处理企业存在竞争关系的企业绩效有同等规模的负向间接效应。这两种效应在总体上相互抵消，意味着生产者剩余没有可检测的增益。（3）融资可及性对商业实践、服务质量和消费者满意度有正向直接效应，对价格有负向效应。这些效应均未被间接效应抵消，表明消费者剩余有净增益。（4）还存在两种额外的间接效应：借款行为向有受处理同行的企业扩散，以及需求向有受处理邻居的企业扩散。（5）通过基于模型的评估综合多种效应，我们估计该贷款的私人回报率为74%，其中大部分被竞争对手的损失所抵消，社会回报率为60%，其中大部分由消费者收益驱动。

European Recessions and Native American Conflict

欧洲经济衰退与美洲原住民冲突

Marco Del Angel, Gregory D. Hess, Marc D. Weidenmier

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

We investigate the extent to which conflicts between Native American tribes and U.S. Army troops were caused by poor economic conditions in Europe from 1869 to 1890. We hypothesize that contractions in economic activity pushed many Europeans to move to the western United States in search of better economic opportunity. The empirical analysis demonstrates that immigration, interacted with US railroad access, caused the probability of a Native American conflict to increase by approximately 46 percent.

中文摘要

我们研究了1869年至1890年间，美洲原住民部落与美国陆军部队之间的冲突在多大程度上是由欧洲糟糕的经济状况引起的。我们假设，经济活动的收缩促使许多欧洲人迁移到美国西部寻找更好的经济机会。实证分析表明，移民与美国铁路通达性的交互作用，使美洲原住民冲突发生的概率增加了约46%。

When Can Benefit Cost Analyses Ignore Secondary Markets?

何时效益成本分析可以忽略次级市场？

Matthew Kotchen, Arik Levinson

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We make four main contributions in this paper related to the theory and practice of benefit cost analysis (BCA). First, we show that most BCAs of policy interventions do not consider the welfare consequences in secondary markets, where goods or services can be complements or substitutes to those in the directly regulated markets. Second, we provide a general theoretical analysis for examining the sign of welfare effects in secondary markets, showing how the results depend on the welfare measure of interest and on whether the goods are complements or substitutes. In doing so, we conclude that the welfare effects in secondary markets will typically be negative in cases most relevant for policy analysis. Third, we develop a straightforward tool that BCA analysts can use to evaluate the potential magnitude of secondary market effects in particular applications. The tool itself highlights how secondary markets are likely to be relatively small in most circumstances. Finally, we illustrate use of the tool in different applications that provide further evidence that secondary market effects are likely to be small.

中文摘要

本文在效益成本分析的理论与实践方面做出了四项主要贡献。首先，我们表明，大多数政策干预的效益成本分析并未考虑次级市场中的福利后果，在这些市场中，商品或服务可能是直接受监管市场中商品或服务的互补品或替代品。其次，我们提供了一个一般性理论分析，用于检验次级市场中福利效应的符号，展示了结果如何取决于所关注的福利度量以及商品是互补品还是替代品。在此过程中，我们得出结论，在政策分析最相关的情况下，次级市场中的福利效应通常为负。第三，我们开发了一个直观的工具，效益成本分析人员可以在特定应用中评估次级市场效应的潜在规模。该工具本身突显了在大多数情况下次级市场可能相对较小。最后，我们通过不同应用展示了该工具的使用，进一步证明了次级市场效应可能很小的证据。

Intergenerational Persistence in Child Mortality

儿童死亡率的代际持续性

Frances R. Lu, Tom Vogl

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We study the intergenerational persistence of inequality by estimating grandmother-mother associations in the loss of a child, using pooled data from 119 Demographic and Health Surveys in 44 developing countries. Compared with compatriots of the same age, women with at least one sibling who died in childhood face 39% higher odds of having experienced at least one own-child death, or 7 percentage points at age 49. Place fixed effects reduce estimated mortality persistence by 47%; socioeconomic covariates explain far less. Within countries over time, persistence falls with aggregate child mortality, so that mortality decline disproportionately benefits high-mortality lineages.

我们利用来自44个发展中国家119项人口与健康调查的合并数据，通过估计祖母与母亲在子女死亡方面的关联，研究了不平等的代际持续性。与同龄同胞相比，至少有一个兄弟姐妹在童年时期去世的女性，其经历过至少一次自身子女死亡的概率高出39%，或在49岁时高出7个百分点。地区固定效应使估计的死亡率持续性降低了47%；社会经济协变量解释的差异要少得多。在国家内部随时间推移，持续性随着总体儿童死亡率的下降而下降，因此死亡率下降不成比例地惠及高死亡率谱系。
