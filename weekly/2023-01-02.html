The Dynamics of Networks and Homophily

网络与同质性的动态变化

Matthew O. Jackson, Stephen M. Nei, Erik Snowberg, Leeat Yariv

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine friendships and study partnerships among university students over several years. At the aggregate level, connections increase over time, but homophily on gender and ethnicity is relatively constant across time, university residences, and different network layers. At the individual level, homophilous tendencies are persistent across time and network layers. Furthermore, we see assortativity in homophilous tendencies. There is weaker, albeit significant, homophily over malleable characteristics---risk preferences, altruism, study habits, and so on. We find little evidence of assimilation over those characteristics. We also document the nuanced impact of network connections on changes in Grade Point Average.

中文摘要

我们研究了大学生在数年间的友谊和学习伙伴关系。在总体层面，联系随时间增加，但基于性别和种族的同质性在不同时间、大学宿舍以及不同网络层级中相对稳定。在个体层面，同质性倾向在时间和网络层级上具有持续性。此外，我们观察到同质性倾向存在同配性。在可塑性特征（如风险偏好、利他主义、学习习惯等）上，同质性较弱但依然显著。我们几乎没有发现这些特征同化的证据。我们还记录了网络联系对平均绩点变化的微妙影响。

Disparities in Pollution Capitalization Rates: The Role of Direct & Systemic Discrimination

污染资本化率的差异：直接歧视与系统性歧视的作用

Joshua S. Graff Zivin, Gregor Singer

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine how exogenous changes in air pollution exposure over the past two decades have altered disparities in home values between Black and White homeowners. We find that air quality capitalization rates are significantly lower for Black homeowners, so much lower that, despite secular reductions in the Black-White pollution exposure gap, housing value disparities increased. An exploration of mechanisms suggests 60% of this difference is attributable to seller race and 40% to racial neighborhood composition, robust to a range of seller, property and neighborhood characteristics. We discuss our findings through the lens of direct and systemic discrimination in housing markets.

中文摘要

我们研究了过去二十年空气污染暴露的外生变化如何改变了黑人和白人房主之间房屋价值的差异。我们发现，黑人房主的空气质量资本化率显著较低。事实上，这一差异如此之大，以至于尽管黑人与白人之间的污染暴露差距在长期内有所缩小，但住房价值的差距在此期间反而扩大了。对机制的研究表明，这种差异中约60%源于直接歧视，其余40%可归因于系统性歧视。

The Effects of Recreational Marijuana Legalization on Employment and Earnings

娱乐用大麻合法化对就业和收入的影响

Dhaval M. Dave, Yang Liang, Caterina Muratori, Joseph J. Sabia

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Despite nearly 70 percent of the American public supporting legalization of recreational marijuana, opponents argue that increased marijuana use may diminish motivation, impede cognitive function, and harm health, each of which could adversely affect adults’ economic wellbeing. This study is the first to explore the impacts of recreational marijuana laws (RMLs) on employment and wages. Difference-in-differences estimates show little evidence that RMLs adversely affect labor market outcomes among most working-age individuals. Rather, our estimates show that RML adoption is associated with an increase in agricultural employment, consistent with the opening of a new licit market. A causal interpretation of our findings is supported by (1) event-study analyses using dynamic difference-in-differences estimates designed to expunge bias due to heterogeneous and dynamic treatment effects, and (2) alternative policy estimates generated using a synthetic control design.

中文摘要

尽管近70%的美国公众支持娱乐用大麻合法化，但反对者认为，大麻使用增加可能会削弱动力、损害认知功能并危害健康，这些都可能对成年人的经济福祉产生不利影响。本研究首次探讨了娱乐用大麻法律对就业和工资的影响。双重差分估计显示，几乎没有证据表明娱乐用大麻法律对大多数劳动年龄个体的劳动力市场结果产生不利影响。相反，我们的估计表明，娱乐用大麻法律的实施与农业就业的增加相关，这与新合法市场的开放一致。我们研究结果的因果解释得到了以下两点的支持：（1）使用动态双重差分估计的事件研究分析，旨在消除因异质性和动态处理效应导致的偏差；（2）使用合成控制设计生成的替代政策估计。

Fundamentally Reforming the DI System: Evidence from German Notch Cohorts

从根本上改革伤残保险体系：来自德国断点队列的证据

Yaming Cao, Bjoern Fischer, Johannes Micha Geyer, Nicolas R. Ziebarth

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 德国

Abstract

In 2001, Germany abolished public occupational disability insurance (ODI)—the second tier of public DI—for post-1960 cohorts. First, overall DI inflows decreased by a third in the long run. Second, representative data show at best modest increases in private ODI take-up in an individual risk-rated market without guaranteed issue. Third, an equilibrium model featuring interactions between the safety net, the first tier of public DI, and the private market illustrates that coverage denials and weak insurance demand due to complementary social insurance produce modest private ODI take-up with steep gradients by income and health. Finally, we simulate complementary reforms.

中文摘要

2001年，德国废除了针对1960年后出生队列的公共职业伤残保险——公共伤残保险的第二层级。首先，长期来看，总体伤残保险流入减少了三分之一。其次，代表性数据显示，在个人风险定价且无保证发行的市场中，私人职业伤残保险的参保率最多只有适度增长。第三，一个包含安全网、公共伤残保险第一层级和私人市场之间相互作用的均衡模型表明，由于补充性社会保险导致的承保拒绝和保险需求疲软，使得私人职业伤残保险的参保率较低，且按收入和健康水平呈现陡峭梯度。最后，我们模拟了补充性改革。

Changes in International Immigration and Internal Native Mobility after Covid-19 in the US

新冠疫情后美国国际移民流入与国内本地人口流动的变化

Giovanni Peri, Reem Zaiour

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

From the beginning of the Covid-19 pandemic to late 2021, international immigration flows to the US decreased significantly. We document the timing and the characteristics of these significant changes in flows, their evolution until late 2022 and their geographic and sector distribution. We consider, in a similar way, changes in internal native mobility in the US, before and after Covid-19. We then connect cross-state native mobility to foreign immigration, the emergence of remote-work options, and changes in labor demand, before and after Covid. In spite of the large changes in labor markets and international migration, we do not measure any significant changes in native internal mobility. Then, using a panel regression and a shift-share IV, we find that the post-Covid drop in immigration and differential increase in remote-work options across sectors and states were not associated with changes in natives' cross-state mobility. We discuss possible implications of the decline in immigration and low native mobility on unfilled jobs in local labor markets.

中文摘要

从新冠疫情开始到2021年底，流入美国的国际移民显著减少。我们记录了这些流量显著变化的时间、特征、直至2022年底的演变情况，以及其地理和行业分布。我们以类似方式考察了新冠疫情前后美国国内本地人口流动的变化。然后，我们联系新冠疫情前后各州之间的本地人口流动与外国移民、远程工作选项的出现以及劳动力需求的变化。尽管劳动力市场和国际移民发生了巨大变化，但我们并未发现本地人口流动有任何显著变化。接着，使用面板回归和份额转移工具变量，我们发现，新冠疫情后移民的减少以及不同行业和州之间远程工作选项的差异化增加，与本地人口跨州流动的变化无关。我们讨论了移民减少和本地人口流动低迷对地方劳动力市场空缺职位的可能影响。

Getting on the Map: The Impact of Online Listings on Business Performance

登上地图：在线列表对企业绩效的影响

Michael Luca, Abhishek Nagaraj, Gauri Subramani

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We evaluate the extent to which small businesses maintain an online presence, looking at restaurant listings on a major online review platform. While the majority of restaurants have an online presence, we find that roughly 18 percent in our sample have no presence as of the end of 2017, despite being able to list their business for free. Using temporal variation in a restaurant's online presence as well as a natural experiment arising from a data acquisition that led over a thousand businesses to be added to the platform, we find that maintaining an online presence is valuable for small businesses. Establishing an online presence leads to a 5% revenue increase. Using data on customer reviews post-add, we explore potential channels that could explain why restaurants continue to remain offline despite potential benefits from digital representation. Consistent with models of voluntary disclosure, we find that businesses that had stayed offline until the acquisition ended up having lower ratings, on average, than those that added themselves or were added by customers.

中文摘要

我们评估了小企业维持在线存在的程度，重点关注一个主要在线评论平台上的餐厅列表。虽然大多数餐厅都有在线存在，但我们发现样本中约18%的餐厅截至2017年底没有任何在线存在，尽管它们可以免费列出自己的业务。利用餐厅在线存在的时间变化，以及一次导致超过一千家企业被添加到平台的数据收购所产生的自然实验，我们发现维持在线存在对小企业是有价值的。建立在线存在导致收入增加5%。利用添加后的客户评论数据，我们探讨了可能解释为什么餐厅尽管有数字化的潜在好处却仍保持离线的渠道。与自愿披露模型一致，我们发现，在收购前一直保持离线的企业，其平均评分低于那些自行添加或被客户添加的企业。

Estimating Gravity from the Short to the Long Run: A Simple Solution to the 'International Elasticity Puzzle'

从短期到长期估计引力模型：解决“国际弹性之谜”的简单方法

James E. Anderson, Yoto V. Yotov

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We propose a simple and flexible reduced-form econometric approach to estimate gravity models in the short and the long run. The theoretical lens for interpreting our methods amends the canonical Lucas-Prescott adjustment formulation to allow for time-interval-varying depreciation-cum-adjustment. A time-varying trade elasticity in the structural gravity model is implied. Our methods explain the 'international elasticity puzzle,' the discrepancy between trade elasticity estimates from the trade literature and the international real business cycle literature. The same theory-motivated estimating equation applied to the same data generates a distribution of trade elasticity estimates that vary from 0.4 in the short run to 4.8 in the long run. The results offer support for some existing theories of dynamic adjustment in trade costs and imply that the long-run equilibrium in our sample is reached in about 16 to 17 years.

中文摘要

我们提出了一种简单且灵活的简化形式计量经济学方法，用于估计短期和长期的引力模型。解释我们方法的理论视角修正了经典的卢卡斯-普雷斯科特调整公式，允许时间间隔变化的折旧-调整参数。这隐含了结构性引力模型中随时间变化的贸易弹性。我们的方法解释了“国际弹性之谜”，即贸易文献与国际实际商业周期文献中贸易弹性估计值之间的差异。将同一理论驱动的估计方程应用于同一数据，会产生一系列贸易弹性估计值，从短期的0.4变化到长期的4.8。这些结果为一些现有的贸易成本动态调整理论提供了支持，并表明我们样本中的长期均衡大约在16到17年内达到。

Dividend Taxation and Firm Performance with Heterogeneous Payout Responses

股息税与企业绩效：基于异质性派息反应的分析

Katarzyna A. Bilicka, Irem Guceri, Evangelos Koumanakos

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We analyze the short and long-run performance of firms that were differentially affected by a new tax on dividends in the lead-up to the Global Financial Crisis. We use exogenous policy variation for firms with different legal statuses and financial year-end dates to causally identify the policy impact. Consistent with intertemporal tax arbitrage, immediately-affected firms significantly reduce payouts. At a time of severe liquidity shortage, the average firm uses the undistributed cash to pay back debt. In the long run, the allocation of undistributed cash to investment, retained earnings, and debt repayment predicts growth and the likelihood of bankruptcy.

中文摘要

我们分析了在全球金融危机前夕，受新股息税影响程度不同的企业的短期和长期绩效。我们利用不同法律地位和财务年度截止日期企业的外生政策变化来因果识别政策影响。与跨期税收套利一致，立即受影响的企业显著减少了派息。在严重流动性短缺时期，平均而言，企业将未分配的现金用于偿还债务。长期来看，未分配现金用于投资、留存收益和债务偿还的分配方式预测了企业增长和破产可能性。

Non-parametric Gravity

非参数引力模型

James E. Anderson

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

Non-parametric gravity as deﬁned in this paper encompasses previous parametric forms. The model generates non-parametric suﬃcient statistics for arbitrage gains from trade and terms of trade, and a non-parametric elasticity of terms of trade with respect to supply. For world manufacturing trade 2000-2014, China’s gains rose 2%yearly and terms of trade fell 8.3%. US gains fell 2% yearly and terms of trade rose 5.5%. Counterfactual industrial policy that raises US 2014 world sales share by 1% lowers US gains per unit 0.72%, leaving a surplus of 0.28%. A novel minimum distance estimator generates the CES trade elasticity required.

中文摘要

本文定义的非参数引力模型涵盖了以往的参数形式。该模型为贸易套利收益和贸易条件生成了非参数的充分统计量，并给出了贸易条件相对于供给的非参数弹性。针对2000-2014年全球制造业贸易，中国的收益每年增长2%，而贸易条件下降了8.3%。美国的收益每年下降2%，贸易条件上升了5.5%。反事实的产业政策若将美国2014年全球销售份额提高1%，则会使美国单位收益下降0.72%，产生0.28%的盈余。一种新颖的最小距离估计量能够估算出所需的CES贸易弹性。

Negotiations of Oil and Gas Auxiliary Lease Clauses: Evidence from Pennsylvania’s Marcellus Shale

油气辅助租赁条款的谈判：来自宾夕法尼亚州马塞勒斯页岩的证据

Max Harleman, Pramod Manohar, Elaine L. Hill

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Oil and gas lease negotiations provide mineral owners the opportunity to negotiate for both compensation and clauses that may protect their health and the enjoyment of their properties. We use optical character recognition to assemble the most comprehensive dataset to date on royalty rates and clauses in nearly 60,000 leases signed in Pennsylvania’s Marcellus Shale. We leverage our data to produce three descriptive findings. First, we find a positive relationship between royalty rates and the prevalence of protective clauses. Second, we find that as development of the shale play progressed over time, royalty rates rose and leases became more likely to contain several protective clauses. Third, we find that royalty rates and the presence of protective clauses bear a weak relationship with the geologic productivity of nearby wells, explained by few firms competing in geographically segregated leasing markets. Some leases simultaneously containing higher royalty rates and more protective clauses suggests that there is a bargaining surplus in leasing markets, but the division of this surplus does not depend on the productivity of the mineral estate. Instead, it may reflect differing preferences, as well as differing negotiating skills, legal resources, and access to information. By documenting 43 clauses found in shale leases and their prevalence—more than double the number identified in past research—we provide critical information that can help mineral owners overcome information asymmetries and increase transparency and equity in leasing markets.

中文摘要

油气租赁谈判为矿权所有者提供了就补偿以及可能保护其健康和财产享受的条款进行谈判的机会。我们利用光学字符识别技术，构建了迄今为止最全面的数据集，涵盖了宾夕法尼亚州马塞勒斯页岩地区近6万份已签署租赁合同中的特许权使用费率和条款。我们利用这些数据得出了三个描述性发现。第一，我们发现特许权使用费率与保护性条款的普遍性之间存在正相关关系。第二，我们发现，随着页岩气开发随时间推移而推进，特许权使用费率上升，且租赁合同更可能包含若干保护性条款。第三，我们发现，特许权使用费率和保护性条款的存在与附近井的地质生产力关系微弱，这可以解释为在地理上分割的租赁市场中，只有少数公司参与竞争。一些租赁合同同时包含更高的特许权使用费率和更多的保护性条款，这表明租赁市场存在谈判盈余，但该盈余的分配并不取决于矿产权益的生产力。相反，它可能反映了不同的偏好，以及不同的谈判技巧、法律资源和信息获取渠道。通过记录页岩租赁合同中发现的43种条款及其普遍性——这一数量是过去研究中识别出的两倍多——我们提供了关键信息，有助于矿权所有者克服信息不对称，提高租赁市场的透明度和公平性。

Growing the Efficient Frontier on Panel Trees

在面板树上增长有效前沿

Lin William Cong, Guanhao Feng, Jingyu He, Xin He

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We develop a new class of tree-based models (P-Trees) for analyzing (unbalanced) panel data using economically guided, global (instead of local) split criteria that guard against overfitting while preserving sparsity and interpretability. To generalize security sorting and better estimate the efficient frontier, we grow a P-Tree top-down to split the cross section of asset returns to construct test assets and re-cover the stochastic discount factor under the mean-variance efficient framework, visualizing (asymmetric) nonlinear interactions among firm characteristics. When applied to U.S. equities, boosted (multi-factor) P-Trees significantly advance the efficient frontier relative to those constructed with established factors and common test assets. P-Tree test assets are diversified and exhibit significant unexplained alphas against benchmark models. The unified P-Tree factors outperform most known observable and latent factor models in pricing cross-sectional returns, delivering transparent and effective trading strategies. Beyond asset pricing, our framework offers a more interpretable and computationally efficient alternative to recent machine learning and AI models for analyzing panel data through goal-oriented, high-dimensional clustering.

中文摘要

我们开发了一类新的基于树的模型（P-Trees），用于分析（非平衡）面板数据。该模型采用经济引导的、全局（而非局部）的分裂准则，以防止过拟合，同时保持稀疏性和可解释性。为了推广证券分类并更好地估计有效前沿，我们自上而下地生长一棵P-Tree，以分割资产收益的横截面，从而构建测试资产，并在均值-方差有效框架下恢复随机贴现因子，可视化公司特征之间（非对称的）非线性交互作用。当应用于美国股票时，增强型（多因子）P-Trees相对于使用已建立因子和常见测试资产构建的有效前沿，显著推进了有效前沿。P-Tree测试资产是多样化的，并且相对于基准模型表现出显著无法解释的阿尔法。统一的P-Tree因子在定价横截面收益方面优于大多数已知的可观测和潜在因子模型，提供了透明且有效的交易策略。除了资产定价之外，我们的框架通过目标导向的高维聚类，为分析面板数据提供了一种比近期机器学习和人工智能模型更具可解释性和计算效率的替代方案。

Picture This: Social Distance and the Mistreatment of Migrant Workers

想象一下：社会距离与对移民工人的虐待

Toman Barsbai, Vojtech Bartos, Victoria Licuanan, Andreas Steinmayr, Erwin Tiongson, Dean Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 香港、菲律宾、沙特阿拉伯

Abstract

International migrant workers are vulnerable to abuses by their employers. We implemented a randomized controlled trial of an intervention to reduce mistreatment of Filipino women working as domestic workers (DWs) by their household employers in Hong Kong and Saudi Arabia. The intervention -- encouraging DWs to show their employers a photo of their family while providing a small gift when starting employment -- caused DWs to experience less mistreatment, have higher satisfaction with the employer, and be more likely to stay with the employer. DWs' families in the Philippines also come to view international labor migration more positively, while they generally remain unaware of the intervention. An online experiment with potential employers in Hong Kong and the Middle East suggests that a mechanism behind the treatment effect is a reduction in the employer's perceived social distance from the employee.

中文摘要

国际移民工人容易受到雇主的虐待。我们实施了一项随机对照试验，评估一项旨在减少在香港和沙特阿拉伯担任家政工人的菲律宾女性遭受其家庭雇主虐待的干预措施。该干预措施——鼓励家政工人在开始工作时向雇主展示其家人的照片并赠送小礼物——导致家政工人遭受的虐待减少，对雇主的满意度提高，并且更有可能继续为雇主工作。在菲律宾的家政工人家庭也因此对国际劳务移民的看法更加积极，尽管他们通常不知道这项干预措施。一项针对香港和中东潜在雇主的在线实验表明，处理效应背后的一个机制是雇主感知到的与雇员之间的社会距离减小。
