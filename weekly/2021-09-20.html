The Consequences of Chronic Pain in Mid-Life: Evidence from the National Child Development Survey

中年慢性疼痛的后果：来自英国全国儿童发展调查的证据

David G. Blanchflower, Alex Bryson

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 英国

Abstract

Using data from all those born in a single week in 1958 in Britain we track the consequences of short pain and chronic pain in mid-life (age 44) on health, wellbeing and labor market outcomes in later life. We examine data taken at age 50 in 2008, when the Great Recession hit and then five years later at age 55 in 2013. We find those suffering both short-term and chronic pain at age 44 continue to report pain and poor general health in their 50s. However, the associations are much stronger for those with chronic pain. Furthermore, chronic pain at age 44 is associated with a range of poor mental health outcomes, pessimism about the future and joblessness at age 55 whereas short-duration pain at age 44 is not. Uniquely, we also show that pain experienced in childhood, at ages 11 and 16, reported by a parent and a teacher respectively, collected decades earlier, predicts pain in mid-life, indicating just how persistent pain can be over the life-course.

中文摘要

利用1958年英国同一周内出生的所有人的数据，我们追踪了中年（44岁）短期疼痛和慢性疼痛对后期健康、福祉和劳动力市场结果的影响。我们考察了2008年（大衰退爆发时）50岁时的数据，以及五年后2013年55岁时的数据。我们发现，44岁时遭受短期和慢性疼痛的人，在50多岁时仍报告疼痛和总体健康状况不佳。然而，这种关联对于慢性疼痛者更为强烈。此外，44岁时的慢性疼痛与55岁时的一系列不良心理健康结果、对未来悲观以及失业相关，而44岁时的短期疼痛则不然。独特的是，我们还表明，童年时期（11岁和16岁）由父母和老师分别报告的疼痛，在数十年后收集的数据中，能预测中年疼痛，这表明疼痛在生命历程中可能具有极强的持续性。

External Balance Sheets and the COVID-19 Crisis

外部资产负债表与COVID-19危机

Galina Hale, Luciana Juvenal

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

At the onset of the COVID-19 economic crisis, as in other crisis episodes, the flight to safety was accompanied by a rapid appreciation of “safe haven” currencies. We quantify currency-induced balance sheet effects for total external positions as well as for individual asset classes using new data on the currency composition of cross-border stocks for 48 countries for the first quarter as well as for the full year 2020. We also conduct the stock-flow reconciliation of net international investment positions to measure overall valuation effects. We show that for many countries currency-induced valuation gains mitigated losses that resulted from declining asset prices in the first quarter of 2020. Moreover, for countries with excess capital out flows during this period, the impact on external balance sheet positions was mitigated by valuation gains. This is because, in contrast with past financial crises, many emerging markets did not experience negative external balance sheet effects from their currency depreciation, partly due to currency-induced valuation gains on equity positions offsetting losses on debt positions, partly due to reduced currency mismatch on their external debt positions.

中文摘要

在COVID-19经济危机爆发之初，与其他危机时期一样，避险行为伴随着“避险”货币的快速升值。我们利用48个国家2020年第一季度及全年跨境存量的货币构成新数据，量化了总外部头寸以及各类资产因货币变动引起的资产负债表效应。我们还进行了净国际投资头寸的流量-存量调整，以衡量总体估值效应。我们表明，对于许多国家而言，2020年第一季度因货币变动产生的估值收益抵消了资产价格下跌造成的损失。此外，对于在此期间出现资本过度外流的国家，估值收益缓解了对外部资产负债表头寸的影响。这是因为，与以往的金融危机不同，许多新兴市场并未因本币贬值而遭受负面的外部资产负债表效应，部分原因是股权头寸的货币估值收益抵消了债务头寸的损失，部分原因是其外债头寸的货币错配有所减少。

The Returns to College(s): Relative Value-Added and Match Effects in Higher Education

大学（教育）的回报：高等教育的相对增值与匹配效应

Jack Mountjoy, Brent R. Hickman

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Students who attend different colleges in the U.S. end up with vastly different economic outcomes. We study the role of relative value-added across colleges within student choice sets in producing these outcome disparities. Linking high school, college, and earnings registries spanning the state of Texas, we identify relative college value-added by comparing the outcomes of students who apply to and are admitted by the same set of institutions, as this approach strikingly balances observable student potential across college treatments and renders our extensive set of covariates irrelevant as controls. Methodologically, we develop a framework for identifying and interpreting value-added under varying assumptions about match effects and sorting gains. Empirically, we estimate a relatively tight, though non-degenerate, distribution of relative value-added across the wide diversity of Texas public universities. Selectivity poorly predicts value-added within student choice sets, with only a fleeting selectivity earnings premium fading to zero after a few years in the labor market. Non-peer college inputs like instructional spending more strongly predict value-added, especially conditional on selectivity. Colleges that boost BA completion, especially in STEM majors, also tend to boost earnings. Finally, we probe the potential for (mis)match effects by allowing value-added schedules to vary by student characteristics.

中文摘要

在美国，就读不同大学的学生最终获得的经济结果差异巨大。我们研究了学生选择集内大学间相对增值在产生这些结果差异中的作用。通过链接涵盖德克萨斯州的高中、大学和收入登记数据，我们通过比较申请并被同一组院校录取的学生的结果来识别相对大学增值，因为这种方法显著平衡了不同大学处理组中学生的可观测潜力，并使我们广泛使用的协变量作为控制变量变得无关紧要。在方法论上，我们建立了一个框架，用于在关于匹配效应和分类收益的不同假设下识别和解释增值。在实证上，我们估计了德克萨斯州公立大学多样性中相对增值的分布，该分布虽然非退化，但相对紧凑。在选择集内，选择性对增值的预测能力较差，仅存在短暂的选择性收入溢价，在劳动力市场几年后便降至零。非同伴大学投入（如教学支出）更能预测增值，尤其是在控制选择性后。那些能提高学士学位完成率（尤其是STEM专业）的大学也倾向于提高收入。最后，我们通过允许增值计划随学生特征变化，探讨了（错）匹配效应的可能性。

Diverse Policy Committees Can Reach Underrepresented Groups

多元化的政策委员会能够触及代表性不足的群体

Francesco D’Acunto, Andreas Fuster, Michael Weber

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Increasing the diversity of policy committees has taken center stage worldwide, but whether and why diverse committees are more effective is still unclear. In a randomized control trial that varies the salience of female and minority representation on the Federal Reserve’s monetary policy committee, the FOMC, we test whether diversity affects how Fed information influences consumers’ subjective beliefs. Women and Black respondents form unemployment expectations more in line with FOMC forecasts and trust the Fed more after this intervention. Women are also more likely to acquire Fed-related information when associated with a female official. White men, who are overrepresented on the FOMC, do not react negatively. Heterogeneous taste for diversity can explain these patterns better than homophily. Our results suggest more diverse policy committees are better able to reach underrepresented groups without inducing negative reactions by others, thereby enhancing the effectiveness of policy communication and public trust in the institution.

中文摘要

提高政策委员会的多元化已成为全球焦点，但多元化委员会是否以及为何更有效仍不清楚。在一项随机对照试验中，我们通过改变美联储货币政策委员会（FOMC）中女性和少数族裔代表的显著性，测试了多元化是否影响美联储信息对消费者主观信念的作用。在此干预后，女性和黑人受访者形成的失业预期更符合FOMC预测，并且对美联储的信任度更高。当信息与女性官员相关联时，女性也更有可能获取与美联储相关的信息。在FOMC中代表性过高的白人男性并未产生负面反应。对多元化的异质性偏好比同质性更能解释这些模式。我们的结果表明，更多元化的政策委员会能够更好地触及代表性不足的群体，而不会引发其他人的负面反应，从而增强政策沟通的有效性和公众对机构的信任。

Salience

显著性

Pedro Bordalo, Nicola Gennaioli, Andrei Shleifer

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We review the fast-growing work on salience and economic behavior. Psychological research shows that salient stimuli attract human attention “bottom up” due to their high contrast with surroundings, their surprising nature relative to recalled experiences, or their prominence. The Bordalo, Gennaioli and Shleifer (2012, 2013, 2020) models of salience show how bottom up attention can distort economic choice by distracting decision makers from their immediate goals or from certain choice attributes. We show that this approach explains many puzzles: separately treated departures from “rationality” such as probability weighting, menu effects, reference point effects, and framing, emerge as distinct manifestations of the same principle of bottom up attention to salient stimuli. We highlight new predictions and discuss open conceptual questions, as well as potential applications in finance, industrial organization, advertising, and politics.

中文摘要

我们回顾了关于显著性与经济行为的快速发展的研究。心理学研究表明，显著的刺激物因其与周围环境的高对比度、相对于回忆经验的令人惊讶的性质或突出性，而“自下而上”地吸引人类注意力。Bordalo、Gennaioli和Shleifer（2012、2013、2020）的显著性模型展示了自下而上的注意力如何通过分散决策者对其直接目标或某些选择属性的注意力来扭曲经济选择。我们表明，这种方法解释了许多谜题：那些被单独处理的偏离“理性”的行为，如概率加权、菜单效应、参考点效应和框架效应，都表现为对显著刺激物自下而上注意力的同一原则的不同表现形式。我们强调了新的预测，并讨论了开放的概念性问题，以及在金融、产业组织、广告和政治中的潜在应用。

Memory and Probability

记忆与概率

Pedro Bordalo, John J. Conlon, Nicola Gennaioli, Spencer Yongwook Kwon, Andrei Shleifer

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 爱尔兰

Abstract

People often estimate probabilities, such as the likelihood that an insurable risk will materialize or that an Irish person has red hair, by retrieving experiences from memory. We present a model of this process based on two established regularities of selective recall: similarity and interference. The model accounts for and reconciles a variety of conflicting empirical findings, such as overestimation of unlikely events when these are cued vs. neglect of non-cued ones, the availability heuristic, the representativeness heuristic, as well as over vs. underreaction to information in different situations. The model makes new predictions on how the content of a hypothesis (not just its objective probability) affects probability assessments by shaping the ease of recall. We experimentally evaluate these predictions and find strong experimental support.

中文摘要

人们通常通过从记忆中检索经验来估计概率，例如可保风险发生的可能性或爱尔兰人拥有红头发的概率。我们基于选择性回忆的两个既定规律——相似性和干扰——提出了一个关于此过程的模型。该模型解释并调和了各种相互矛盾的实证发现，例如当罕见事件被提示时的高估与非提示事件的忽视、可得性启发式、代表性启发式，以及在不同情境下对信息的过度反应与反应不足。该模型做出了新的预测，即假设的内容（而不仅仅是其客观概率）如何通过塑造回忆的难易程度来影响概率评估。我们通过实验评估了这些预测，并发现了强有力的实验支持。

Red Scare? A Study of Ethnic Prejudice in the Prosecutions under the Economic Espionage Act

红色恐慌？《经济间谍法》起诉中的种族偏见研究

Hanming Fang, Ming Li

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We empirically test whether the Department of Justice (DOJ) engages in ethnic prejudice against Chinese in its prosecutorial decisions under the Economic Espionage Act (EEA) of 1996. Using data of EEA cases from November 1996 to June 2021, we conduct Becker's outcome test for evidence of ethnic prejudice. We find that Chinese-named defendants were more likely to be dismissed by trial or acquitted by jury, and were found guilty on fewer counts, and on average received harsher indictments. These results are robust regardless of whether we consider all cases or only arguably ``marginal'' cases. We also find that, for those publicly listed victim firms whose trade secrets were allegedly stolen by the charged defendants, the stock market reaction was much more muted to the news on the case filing date if the charged defendants are of Chinese descent. Our study provides the first systematic evidence that the DOJ's prosecutorial decisions in the application of the EEA may have been tainted by ethnic prejudice against Chinese, including American citizens of Chinese descent.

中文摘要

我们实证检验了司法部在1996年《经济间谍法》下的起诉决定中是否对华人存在种族偏见。利用1996年11月至2021年6月期间EEA案件的数据，我们进行了贝克尔结果检验以寻找种族偏见的证据。我们发现，具有中文姓名的被告更有可能在审判中被驳回或由陪审团宣告无罪，被判有罪的罪名数量更少，并且平均受到更严厉的起诉。无论我们考虑所有案件还是仅考虑可能属于“边缘”的案件，这些结果都是稳健的。我们还发现，对于声称被指控被告窃取了商业秘密的公开上市受害公司，如果被指控被告是华裔，股票市场在案件立案日期对该消息的反应要弱得多。我们的研究首次提供了系统性证据，表明司法部在适用EEA时的起诉决定可能受到了针对华人（包括华裔美国公民）的种族偏见的影响。

Life Course Effects Of The Lanham Preschools: What The First Government Preschool Effort Can Tell Us About Universal Early Care And Education Today

兰汉姆学前教育的生命周期效应：首个政府学前教育努力对当今普遍早期保育与教育的启示

Taletha M. Derrington, Alison Huang, Joseph P. Ferrie

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine the effects WWII Lanham Act Nursery Schools (LNS) on high school and young adult educational and labor outcomes of participants in the landmark Project Talent (PT) study. All PT places that received funding for LNS schools and all PT places that did not were identified by examining program records and contemporaneous newspaper accounts. Focusing on students who in 1960 attended high school in the same city or town where they were born, we estimate intent to treat effects of access to LNS preschool on high school academic and social emotional outcomes and on educational attainment and labor outcomes at five and eleven years following high school graduation. Preschool boosts high school academic outcomes for men and (in at least one specification) income 11 years after high school graduation. For women, preschool exposure had a negative effect on some social emotional outcomes in high school. We found no or inconsistent effects for other outcomes. The Lanham experience demonstrates that even with the less sophisticated understanding of child development of the early 1940s, the first universal, government-funded preschool program had positive impacts on boys’ outcomes at least through high school. Given today’s expanded understanding of child development and focus on the quality of early care and education programming, these findings provide some optimism as communities, states, and the federal government contemplate expanding funding for today’s early learning environments.
