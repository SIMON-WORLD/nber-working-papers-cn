No. 1论文详情NBER w30120

(Co-)Working in Close Proximity: Knowledge Spillovers and Social Interactions

近距离（共）同工作：知识溢出与社交互动

Maria P. Roche, Alexander Oettl, Christian Catalini

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

We examine the influence of physical proximity on between-startup knowledge spillovers at one of the largest technology co-working hubs in the United States. Relying on the random assignment of office space to the hub's 251 startups, we find that proximity positively influences knowledge spillovers as proxied by the likelihood of adopting an upstream web technology already used by a peer startup. This effect is largest for startups within close proximity of each other and quickly decays: startups more than 20 meters apart on the same floor are indistinguishable from startups on different floors. The main driver of the effect appears to be social interactions. While startups in close proximity are most likely to participate in social co-working space events together, knowledge spillovers are greatest between startups that socialize but are dissimilar. Ultimately, startups that are embedded in environments that have neither too much nor too little diversity perform better, but only if they socialize.

中文摘要

我们考察了物理距离对美国最大科技共享办公空间之一内初创企业间知识溢出的影响。基于该空间内251家初创企业办公空间的随机分配，我们发现，物理距离正向影响知识溢出，其代理指标是采用同行初创企业已使用的上游网络技术的可能性。这种效应在彼此距离很近的初创企业中最大，并迅速衰减：同一楼层相距超过20米的初创企业与不同楼层的初创企业之间没有区别。该效应的主要驱动因素似乎是社交互动。虽然距离很近的初创企业最有可能一起参加共享办公空间的社交活动，但知识溢出在那些进行社交但彼此不相似的初创企业之间最大。最终，嵌入在既不过度多样化也不过度同质化环境中的初创企业表现更好，但这仅当它们进行社交时才成立。

Globalization, Fertility and Marital Behavior in a Lowest-Low Fertility Setting

全球化、生育行为与婚姻行为：基于极低生育率环境的研究

Osea Giuntella, Lorenzo Rotunno, Luca Stella

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 德国

Abstract

Using longitudinal data from the German Socio-Economic Panel, we analyze the effects of exposure to globalization on the fertility and marital behavior in Germany, until recently a lowest-low fertility setting. We find that exposure to greater import competition from Eastern Europe led to worse labor market outcomes and lower fertility rates. In contrast, workers in industries that benefited from increased exports had better employment prospects and higher fertility. These effects are driven by low-educated, married men, and full-time workers and reflect changes in the likelihood of having any child (extensive margin). While there is evidence of some fertility postponement, we find significant effects on completed fertility. There is instead little evidence of any significant impact on marital behavior.

中文摘要

利用德国社会经济面板数据的纵向追踪，我们分析了全球化暴露对德国生育与婚姻行为的影响——直至近期，德国仍处于极低生育率环境。研究发现，东欧进口竞争加剧导致劳动力市场表现恶化及生育率下降；相反，受益于出口扩张的行业从业者则获得更优就业前景与更高生育率。这些效应主要由低学历已婚男性及全职工作者驱动，并反映在生育可能性（广延边际）的变化上。尽管存在生育推迟的证据，但我们发现对终身生育率具有显著影响。相比之下，婚姻行为未受显著冲击。

How Undervalued is the Covid-19 Vaccine? Evidence from Discrete Choice Experiments and VSL Benchmarks

新冠疫苗被低估了多少？来自离散选择实验与统计生命价值基准的证据

Patrick Carlin, Brian E. Dixon, Kosali I. Simon, Ryan Sullivan, Coady Wing

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Two discrete choice experiments conducted early in the Covid-19 vaccination campaign show that people dramatically undervalue the Covid-19 vaccine, relative to benchmarks implied by the value of a statistical life (VSL). Our first experiment found that median willingness to pay (WTP) for initial vaccination is around $50, only 2 percent of the WTP implied by standard VSL calculations. Our second experiment found the median person was willing to accept (WTA) about $200 to delay the second dose, only 32 percent of the WTA implied by standard VSL calculations. While standard economic models imply that vaccines are undervalued because of their large externalities, we interpret the finding that WTP estimates are well below the VSL benchmarks as evidence that internalities play a substantial role. This evidence that people undervalue even the private benefits of vaccination suggests that there may be a role for government beyond conventional efforts to correct externalities.

中文摘要

在新冠疫苗接种运动早期开展的两项离散选择实验表明，相对于统计生命价值（VSL）隐含的基准，人们对新冠疫苗的估值显著偏低。第一项实验发现，初始疫苗接种意愿支付中位数约为50美元，仅占标准VSL计算隐含意愿支付的2%。第二项实验显示，中位受访者愿意接受约200美元以推迟第二剂接种，仅占标准VSL计算隐含意愿接受的32%。虽然标准经济模型认为疫苗因巨大外部性而被低估，但我们发现意愿支付估计值远低于VSL基准，这证明内部性发挥了重要作用。该证据表明人们甚至低估了疫苗接种的私人收益，暗示政府可能需要超越传统纠正外部性的努力来发挥作用。

Escaping Secular Stagnation with Unconventional Monetary Policy

以非常规货币政策逃离长期停滞

Luba Petersen, Ryan Rholes

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We design a new experimental framework to study policy interventions to combat secular stagnations and liquidity traps in an overlapping-generations environment where participants form expectations and make real economic decisions. We observe that participants can learn to coordinate on high inflation full-employment equilibria. Permanent deleveraging shocks induce pessimistic, backward-looking expectations and considerable consumption heterogeneity as the economies experience persistent deflation. We explore the ability of unconventional monetary policy to lead economies out of deflationary traps. Permanently increasing the central bank's inflation target is insufficient to generate inflationary expectations due to low central bank credibility. Negative interest rates stimulate spending and generate the necessary inflation for the economies to escape the zero lower bound. Negative interest rates are more potent than raising the inflation target at shifting consumption to the present.

中文摘要

我们设计了一个新的实验框架，研究在代际交叠环境中应对长期停滞与流动性陷阱的政策干预——该环境中参与者形成预期并做出真实经济决策。我们观察到参与者能够学会协调于高通胀充分就业均衡。永久性去杠杆冲击引发悲观的后顾型预期，并在经济经历持续通缩时产生显著的消费异质性。我们探索了非常规货币政策引导经济脱离通缩陷阱的能力。由于央行可信度低，永久性提高通胀目标不足以产生通胀预期。负利率刺激支出并产生必要通胀，使经济摆脱零下限约束。在将消费转移至当前方面，负利率比提高通胀目标更为有效。

No. 5论文详情NBER w30116

Comparing Past and Present Inflation

过去与当前通胀的比较

Marijn A. Bolhuis, Judd N. L. Cramer, Lawrence H. Summers

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

There have been important methodological changes in the Consumer Price Index (CPI) over time. These distort comparisons of inflation from different periods, which have become more prevalent as inflation has risen to 40-year highs. To better contextualize the current run-up in inflation, this paper constructs new historical series for CPI headline and core inflation that are more consistent with current practices and expenditure shares for the post-war period. Using these series, we find that current inflation levels are much closer to past inflation peaks than the official series would suggest. In particular, the rate of core CPI disinflation caused by Volcker-era policies is significantly lower when measured using today’s treatment of housing: only 5 percentage points of decline instead of 11 percentage points in the official CPI statistics. To return to 2 percent core CPI inflation today will thus require nearly the same amount of disinflation as achieved under Chairman Volcker.

中文摘要

消费者价格指数（CPI）在方法上经历了重要变革。这些变革扭曲了不同时期通胀率的比较——随着通胀升至40年高位，此类比较愈发普遍。为更好地理解当前通胀飙升的背景，本文构建了战后时期与现行做法及支出份额更一致的CPI总体与核心通胀新历史序列。使用这些序列，我们发现当前通胀水平比官方序列所暗示的更接近历史通胀峰值。具体而言，若采用当今对住房的处理方式，沃尔克时代政策导致的核心CPI反通胀率显著更低：仅下降5个百分点，而非官方CPI统计中的11个百分点。因此，要使当前核心CPI通胀回归2%，所需的反通胀幅度几乎与沃尔克主席时期相当。

Factors Influencing the Choice of Pension Distribution at Retirement

影响退休时养老金分配选择的因素

Robert L. Clark, Olivia S. Mitchell

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

One of the most important financial decisions that pension participants make concerns how they access their pension assets when they terminate employment with their plan sponsor. Their choices depend both on own preferences and the options offered by their retirement plan. This paper examines both past and future pension withdrawal choices for those with defined benefit and defined contribution pensions, separately. Our data are drawn from a set of pension distribution questions we fielded in the Understanding American Study. Results show significant differences in distribution choices based on the type of retirement plan, with individuals covered by defined benefit plans significantly more likely to select annuities compared to similar employees covered by defined contribution plans. We also find differences in how higher annual income affects annuity choices based on coverage by DB plans. Individuals with lower levels of financial literacy and lower annual income have less knowledge of basic pension characteristics.

中文摘要

养老金参与者在终止与计划发起人的雇佣关系时，最重要的财务决策之一涉及如何获取养老金资产。其选择既取决于个人偏好，也取决于退休计划提供的选项。本文分别考察了固定收益型与固定缴款型养老金参与者的过去及未来养老金提取选择。数据来自我们在“理解美国研究”中设置的一组养老金分配问题。结果显示，基于退休计划类型的分配选择存在显著差异：与固定缴款计划覆盖的类似员工相比，固定收益计划覆盖的个人选择年金的概率显著更高。我们还发现，基于固定收益计划覆盖情况，较高年收入对年金选择的影响存在差异。金融素养较低且年收入较低的个人对基本养老金特征的了解更少。

The Race Between Tax Enforcement and Tax Planning: Evidence From a Natural Experiment in Chile

税收执法与税收筹划的竞赛：来自智利自然实验的证据

Sebastián Bustos, Dina Pomeranz, Juan Carlos Suárez Serrato, José Vila-Belda, Gabriel Zucman

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 智利

Abstract

Profit shifting by multinational corporations is thought to reduce tax revenue around the world. This paper provides a comprehensive analysis of the introduction of standard regulations to limit profit shifting. Using administrative tax and customs data from Chile, we find that the reform was ineffective in reducing multinationals’ transfers to lower-tax countries and did not significantly raise tax payments. Interviews with tax advisors and employment history data reveal a drastic increase in consulting services. Our results illustrate that when enforcement can be circumvented by sophisticated tax planning, it can benefit tax consultants at the expense of tax authorities and taxpayers.

中文摘要

跨国公司的利润转移被认为减少了全球税收收入。本文对限制利润转移的标准法规引入进行了全面分析。利用智利的行政税收与海关数据，我们发现改革在减少跨国公司向低税国转移方面无效，且未显著提高税收收入。对税务顾问的访谈及就业历史数据揭示了咨询服务的急剧增长。我们的结果表明，当执法可以被复杂的税收筹划规避时，执法可能以税务机关和纳税人的代价使税务顾问受益。

No. 8论文详情NBER w30113

Who Benefits from Meritocracy?

谁从精英选拔中受益？

Diana Moreira, Santiago Pérez

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Individuals from lower-income backgrounds are underrepresented in high-status occupations. This underrepresentation has coincided with increasing scrutiny of the “meritocratic” criteria shaping access to these positions. We study the equity impacts of a prominent example of meritocratic selection: civil service exams. To do so, we use evidence from the Pendleton Act, a historical reform that introduced such exams to select U.S. federal employees. We find that, although the reform increased the representation of “educated outsiders” (individuals with high education but limited connections), it reduced the representation of lower-SES individuals. This reduction was stronger among applicants from states with high educational inequality.

中文摘要

低收入背景个体在高地位职业中代表性不足。这种不足与塑造这些职位准入的“精英选拔”标准日益受到审视相伴而生。我们研究了一个精英选拔的突出案例——公务员考试——的公平性影响。为此，我们利用《彭德尔顿法案》这一历史改革——该法案引入此类考试以选拔美国联邦雇员。我们发现，尽管改革增加了“受过教育的外部人士”（高学历但关系有限者）的代表性，但它降低了低社会经济地位个体的代表性。这种降低在教育不平等程度高的州的申请者中更为显著。

No. 9论文详情NBER w30111

Near-Rational Equilibria in Heterogeneous-Agent Models: A Verification Method

异质性主体模型中的近理性均衡：一种验证方法

Leonid Kogan, Indrajit Mitra

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose a general simulation-based procedure for estimating quality of approximate policies in heterogeneous-agent equilibrium models, which allows to verify that such approximate solutions describe a near-rational equilibrium. Our procedure endows agents with superior knowledge of the future path of the economy, while imposing a suitable penalty for such foresight. The relaxed problem is more tractable than the original, and results in an upper bound on agents’ welfare. Our method applies to various solution algorithms. We illustrate our approach in two applications: the incomplete-markets model of Krusell and Smith (1998) and the heterogeneous firm model of Khan and Thomas (2008).

中文摘要

我们提出了一种通用的基于模拟的程序，用于评估异质性主体均衡模型中近似策略的质量，该程序能够验证此类近似解是否描述了一个近理性均衡。我们的程序赋予主体对未来经济路径的优越知识，同时对此类预见施加适当的惩罚。这个松弛问题比原始问题更易处理，并得出了主体福利的上界。我们的方法适用于多种求解算法。我们通过两个应用实例来说明该方法：Krusell和Smith（1998）的不完全市场模型以及Khan和Thomas（2008）的异质性企业模型。

Opioid Use and Employment Outcomes: Evidence from the U.S. Military

阿片类药物使用与就业结果：来自美国军方的证据

Abby E. Alpert, Steve Schwab, Benjamin D. Ukert

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

There is significant interest in understanding the labor market consequences of the opioid epidemic, but little is known about how opioid use impacts on-the-job productivity. We analyze the impact of opioid initiation in the emergency department (ED) on workforce outcomes in the Military using linked medical and administrative personnel data for active duty service members from 2008 to 2017. Exploiting quasi-random assignment of patients to physicians in the ED, we find that assignment to a high-intensity opioid prescribing physician increases the probability of long-term opioid use and leads to subsequent negative effects on work capacity, job performance, and productivity. We also analyze the mechanisms underlying these negative workforce outcomes. While opioid use does not negatively affect measures of physical job performance, we find large increases in behavioral problems which lead to disciplinary actions and job separation.

中文摘要

人们对于理解阿片类药物流行对劳动力市场的影响有浓厚兴趣，但关于阿片类药物使用如何影响在职生产率却知之甚少。我们利用2008年至2017年现役军人的关联医疗和行政人事数据，分析了急诊科中阿片类药物初始使用对军队劳动力结果的影响。利用急诊科患者被准随机分配给医生的特点，我们发现，被分配给高阿片类药物处方强度医生的患者，其长期使用阿片类药物的概率增加，并导致随后在工作能力、工作表现和生产率方面的负面效应。我们还分析了这些负面劳动力结果背后的机制。虽然阿片类药物使用并未对体力工作表现指标产生负面影响，但我们发现行为问题大幅增加，这导致了纪律处分和离职。

No. 11论文详情NBER w30109中国相关

Cutting Out the Middleman: The Structure of Chains of Intermediation

去掉中间商：中介链的结构

Matthew Grant, Meredith Startz

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 尼日利亚

Abstract

Distribution of goods often involves multiple intermediaries engaged in sequential buying and reselling. Why do these chains of intermediation exist, and what are their implications for consumers? We show that multi-intermediary chains arise in response to internal economies of scale in trade costs. This suggests that chains will be longer on average in developing countries, and can account for empirical patterns in firm size and prices that we document using original data on imported consumer goods in Nigeria. While policy wisdom often calls for shortening chains, we show that this has ambiguous welfare implications. Equilibrium distribution structures are not generally efficient, and policies and technologies that lead to shorter chains will not necessarily benefit consumers, even when intermediaries hold market power. Instead, there is a fundamental trade-off: shorter chains have lower marginal cost but also fewer sellers, which can reduce competition, product availability, and access to retailers. Contamination Bias in Linear Regressions

Paul Goldsmith-Pinkham, Peter Hull, Michal Kolesár

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study regressions with multiple treatments and a set of controls that is flexible enough to purge omitted variable bias. We show that these regressions generally fail to estimate convex averages of heterogeneous treatment effects—instead, estimates of each treatment’s effect are contaminated by non-convex averages of the effects of other treatments. We discuss three estimation approaches that avoid such contamination bias, including the targeting of easiest-to-estimate weighted average effects. A re-analysis of nine empirical applications finds economically and statistically meaningful contamination bias in observational studies; contamination bias in experimental studies is more limited due to smaller variability in propensity scores.

Abstract

We examine patterns of work in the U.S. from 1973-2018 with the novel focus on days per week, using intermittent CPS samples and one ATUS sample. Abstract

The racial wealth gap is the largest of the economic disparities between Black and white Americans, with a white-to-Black per capita wealth ratio of 6 to 1. It is also among the most persistent. Abstract

This paper uses two complementary approaches to estimate the effect of right-to-work (RTW) laws on wages and unionization rates.
