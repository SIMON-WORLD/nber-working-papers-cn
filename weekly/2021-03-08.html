No. 1论文详情NBER w28541

Does Mobility Explain Why Slums Were Hit Harder by COVID-19 in Mumbai, India?

流动性能否解释为何印度孟买的贫民窟受COVID-19冲击更严重？

Jaymee Sheng, Anup Malani, Ashish Goel, Purushotham Botla

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 印度

Abstract

SARS-CoV-2 has had a greater burden, as measured by rate of infection, in poorer communities within cities. For example, 55% of Mumbai slums residents had antibodies to COVID-19, 3.2 times the seroprevalence in non-slum areas of the city according to a sero-survey done in July 2020. One explanation is that government suppression was less severe in poorer communities, either because the poor were more likely to be exempt or unable to comply. Another explanation is that effective suppression itself accelerated the epidemic in poor neighborhoods because households are more crowded and residents share toilet and water facilities. We show there is little evidence for the first hypothesis in the context of Mumbai. Using location data from smart phones, we find that slum residents had nominally but not significantly (economically or statistically) higher mobility than non-slums prior to the sero-survey. We also find little evidence that mobility in non-slums was lower than in slums during lockdown, a subset of the period before the survey.

中文摘要

SARS-CoV-2在城市内较贫困社区造成了更大的负担，以感染率衡量。例如，根据2020年7月进行的一项血清调查，孟买55%的贫民窟居民拥有COVID-19抗体，是非贫民窟地区血清阳性率的3.2倍。一种解释是，政府在较贫困社区的压制措施不那么严厉，要么是因为穷人更可能被豁免，要么是无法遵守。另一种解释是，有效的压制本身加速了贫困社区的疫情，因为家庭更拥挤，居民共用厕所和供水设施。我们表明，在孟买的背景下，几乎没有证据支持第一种假设。利用智能手机的位置数据，我们发现，在血清调查之前，贫民窟居民的流动性名义上高于非贫民窟，但并不显著（经济上或统计上）。我们还发现，几乎没有证据表明，在封锁期间（调查前的一个子时期），非贫民窟的流动性低于贫民窟。

A Model of Credit, Money, Interest, and Prices

一个关于信贷、货币、利率和价格的模型

Saki Bigio, Yuliy Sannikov

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper integrates a realistic implementation of monetary policy through the banking system into an incomplete-markets economy with wage rigidity. Monetary policy sets policy rates and alters the supply of reserves. These tools grant independent control over credit spreads and an interest target. Through these tools, monetary policy affects the evolution of real interests rates, credit, output, and the wealth distribution—both in the long and in the short run. We decompose the effects through a combination of the interest and credit channels that depend on the size of the central bank’s balance sheet. Monetary policy reaches an expansionary limit when it enters a liquidity trap. The model highlights a trade-off between worse microeconomic insurance (insurance across agents) and greater macroeconomic insurance (insurance across states). The model prescribes that monetary policy should operate with a small balance sheet which tightens credit during booms, and should expand its balance sheet and lower policy rates during busts.

中文摘要

本文将货币政策通过银行体系的现实实施方式，整合到一个具有工资刚性的不完全市场经济中。货币政策设定政策利率并改变准备金供给。这些工具赋予了对信贷利差和利率目标的独立控制权。通过这些工具，货币政策影响实际利率、信贷、产出和财富分配的演变——无论是在长期还是短期。我们通过利率和信贷渠道的组合来分解这些效应，这取决于中央银行资产负债表的规模。当货币政策进入流动性陷阱时，它达到扩张极限。该模型强调了微观经济保险（跨代理人保险）恶化与宏观经济保险（跨状态保险）增强之间的权衡。该模型规定，货币政策应以小资产负债表运作，在繁荣时期收紧信贷，并在萧条时期扩大资产负债表并降低政策利率。

The Value of Privacy in Cartels: An Analysis of the Inner Workings of a Bidding Ring

卡特尔中隐私的价值：对投标圈内部运作的分析

Kei Kawai, Jun Nakabayashi, Juan M. Ortner

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 日本

Abstract

We study the inner workings of a bidding cartel focusing on the way in which bidders communicate with one another regarding how each bidder should bid. We show that the designated winner of the cartel can attain higher payoffs by randomizing its bid and keeping it secret from other bidders when defection is a concern. Intuitively, randomization makes defection less attractive as potential defectors face the risk of not winning the auction even if they deviate. We illustrate how our theoretical predictions are borne out in practice by studying a bidding cartel that operated in the town of Kumatori, Japan.

中文摘要

我们研究了一个投标卡特尔（bidding cartel）的内部运作，重点关注投标人之间就每个投标人应如何投标进行沟通的方式。我们表明，当存在背叛风险时，卡特尔指定的赢家可以通过随机化其投标并对其他投标人保密来获得更高的收益。直观地说，随机化降低了背叛的吸引力，因为潜在的背叛者即使偏离约定，也面临无法赢得拍卖的风险。我们通过研究一个在日本熊取町运作的投标卡特尔，说明了我们的理论预测如何在实践中得到验证。

Market Power in Neoclassical Growth Models

新古典增长模型中的市场势力

Laurence M. Ball, N. Gregory Mankiw

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

This paper examines the optimal accumulation of capital and the effects of government debt in neoclassical growth models in which firms have market power and therefore charge prices above marginal cost. In this environment, the real interest rate earned by savers is less than the net marginal product of capital. We establish a new method for evaluating dynamic efficiency that can be applied in such economies. A plausible calibration suggests that the wedge between the real interest rate and the marginal product of capital is more than 4 percentage points and that the U.S. economy is dynamically efficient. In addition, government Ponzi schemes can have different implications for welfare than they do under competition. Even if the government can sustain a perpetual rollover of debt and accumulating interest, the policy may nonetheless reduce welfare by depressing steady-state capital and aggregate consumption. These findings suggest that even with low interest rates, as have been observed recently, fiscal policymakers should still be concerned about the crowding-out effects of government debt.

中文摘要

本文研究了企业拥有市场势力并因此收取高于边际成本的价格时，新古典增长模型中的最优资本积累以及政府债务的影响。在这种环境下，储蓄者获得的实际利率低于资本的净边际产出。我们建立了一种新的评估动态效率的方法，可应用于此类经济体。一个合理的校准表明，实际利率与资本边际产出之间的楔子超过4个百分点，并且美国经济是动态有效的。此外，政府庞氏骗局（Ponzi schemes）对福利的影响可能与竞争条件下的情况不同。即使政府能够维持债务的永久展期并积累利息，该政策仍可能通过压低稳态资本和总消费来降低福利。这些发现表明，即使利率较低（如近期所观察到的），财政政策制定者仍应关注政府债务的挤出效应。

‘You Will:’ A Macroeconomic Analysis of Digital Advertising

“你会的：”数字广告的宏观经济分析

Jeremy Greenwood, Yueyuan Ma, Mehmet Yorukoglu

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济 · 美国

Abstract

An information-based model is developed where traditional and digital advertising finance the provision of free media goods and affect price competition. Digital advertising is directed toward consumers while traditional advertising is undirected. The equilibrium is suboptimal. Media goods are under provided with both types of advertising. Additionally, traditional advertising is excessive because it is undirected. The tax-cum-subsidy policy that overcomes these inefficiencies is characterized. The model is calibrated to the U.S. economy. Digital advertising increases welfare significantly and is disproportionately financed by better-off consumers. The welfare gain from the optimal policy is much smaller than the gain from digital advertising.

中文摘要

我们开发了一个基于信息的模型，其中传统广告和数字广告为免费媒体商品的提供提供资金，并影响价格竞争。数字广告针对消费者，而传统广告则是非定向的。均衡状态是次优的。两种类型的广告都导致媒体商品供给不足。此外，传统广告由于是非定向的而过度供给。我们描述了能够克服这些低效率的税收-补贴政策。该模型根据美国经济进行了校准。数字广告显著提高了福利，并且其资金不成比例地由较富裕的消费者提供。最优政策带来的福利收益远小于数字广告带来的收益。

Do Monetary Policy Frameworks Matter in Low Income Countries?

货币政策框架在低收入国家重要吗？

Alina Carare, Carlos de Resende, Andrew T. Levin, Chelsea Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 中非共和国

Abstract

Microeconomic evidence indicates a very high frequency of price adjustment in low income countries (LICs), raising the question of whether LICs may be reasonably characterized as exhibiting monetary neutrality. To address this question, we analyze a cross-country panel dataset of 79 LICs over the period 1990 to 2015 to assess the impact of external shocks on real GDP growth, and we find highly significant differences between LICs where the central bank targets monetary aggregates or inflation compared to LICs that maintain rigid nominal exchange rates. We also conduct an event study of the surprise devaluation of the Central African Franc (CFA) in January 1994 and find that it had highly significant effects on the output growth of 10 CFA countries relative to 18 similar countries outside the CFA zone. Consequently, the hypothesis of monetary neutrality is decisively rejected, and these findings provide strong support for the role of monetary policy frameworks in fostering price stability and macroeconomic stability in LICs.

中文摘要

微观经济证据表明，低收入国家（LICs）的价格调整频率非常高，这引发了关于低收入国家是否可以被合理描述为表现出货币中性的问题。为了解决这个问题，我们分析了1990年至2015年间79个低收入国家的跨国面板数据集，以评估外部冲击对实际GDP增长的影响。我们发现，在中央银行以货币总量或通胀为目标的低收入国家，与维持刚性名义汇率的低收入国家之间，存在高度显著的差异。我们还对1994年1月中非法郎（CFA）的意外贬值进行了事件研究，发现相对于CFA区外的18个类似国家，它对10个CFA国家的产出增长产生了高度显著的影响。因此，货币中性的假设被明确拒绝，这些发现为货币政策框架在促进低收入国家价格稳定和宏观经济稳定方面的作用提供了强有力的支持。

Artificial Intelligence and Pricing: The Impact of Algorithm Design

人工智能与定价：算法设计的影响

John Asker, Chaim Fershtman, Ariel Pakes

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The behavior of artificial intelligences algorithms (AIAs) is shaped by how they learn about their environment. We compare the prices generated by AIAs that use different learning protocols when there is market interaction. Asynchronous learning occurs when the AIA only learns about the return from the action it took. Synchronous learning occurs when the AIA conducts counterfactuals to learn about the returns it would have earned had it taken an alternative action. The two lead to markedly different market prices. When future profits are not given positive weight by the AIA, synchronous updating leads to competitive pricing, while asynchronous can lead to pricing close to monopoly levels. We investigate how this result varies when either counterfactuals can only be calculated imperfectly and/or when the AIA places a weight on future profits.

中文摘要

人工智能算法（AIAs）的行为由其学习环境的方式塑造。我们比较了在市场互动中使用不同学习协议的AIAs所产生的价格。异步学习发生在AIA仅从其所采取的行动中了解回报时。同步学习发生在AIA进行反事实推断以了解如果采取替代行动本应获得的回报时。两者导致显著不同的市场价格。当未来利润不被AIA赋予正权重时，同步更新导致竞争性定价，而异步更新可能导致接近垄断水平的定价。我们研究了当反事实只能被不完美地计算和/或当AIA对未来利润赋予权重时，这一结果如何变化。

Science and the Market for Technology

科学与技术市场

Ashish Arora, Sharon Belenzon, Jungkyu Suh

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Well functioning Markets for Technology (MFT) allow inventors to sell their inventions to others that may derive more value from them. We argue that the growing reliance on science in inventions enhances MFT. In addition to higher quality inventions, reliance on science may enhance gains from trade and reduce the transfer cost of knowledge and other transaction costs. Using large scale data, we show that patents citing science are more likely to be traded, especially for novel patents and for smaller inventors. Leveraging the fall of the Berlin Wall as a source of exogenous variation in the relevant scientific knowledge to technological fields, we confirm reliance on science increases the likelihood that the invention will be traded

中文摘要

运作良好的技术市场（MFT）允许发明者将其发明出售给可能从中获得更多价值的其他人。我们认为，发明中对科学的日益依赖增强了技术市场。除了更高质量的发明外，对科学的依赖可能增加贸易收益，并降低知识的转移成本和其他交易成本。利用大规模数据，我们表明引用科学的专利更有可能被交易，尤其是对于新颖专利和较小的发明者。利用柏林墙倒塌作为相关科学知识对技术领域的外生变化来源，我们确认对科学的依赖增加了发明被交易的可能性。

First-mover Advantage and the Private Value of Public Science

先发优势与公共科学的私人价值

Ashish Arora, Sharon Belenzon, Bernardo Dionisi

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Technical progress increasingly relies on the use of scientific knowledge. But if much of this knowledge is in the public domain, can it be a source of private value? We find that average private returns to using public science are small, especially in crowded technical fields, consistent with the view that the expected profit from an input that others can easily access is low. However, private value is higher when a firm is the first to use science, partly because it can secure broader patents relative to later users. Finally, corporate participation in scientific research is a strong predictor of first use, especially of relevant science. This is consistent with the view that participation in science raises familiarity with relevant scientific advances.

中文摘要

技术进步越来越依赖于科学知识的使用。但如果这些知识大部分属于公共领域，它能否成为私人价值的来源？我们发现，使用公共科学的平均私人回报很小，尤其是在拥挤的技术领域，这与一种观点一致，即其他人容易获取的投入的预期利润较低。然而，当企业是第一个使用科学时，私人价值更高，部分原因是相对于后来的使用者，它可以获得更广泛的专利。最后，企业参与科学研究是首次使用（尤其是相关科学）的有力预测因素。这与参与科学研究能提高对相关科学进展的熟悉程度的观点一致。

No. 10论文详情NBER w28532

Deterrence and Compellence in the Parliament

议会中的威慑与强制

Duha T. Altindag, Naci H. Mocan, Jie Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 土耳其

Abstract

In most countries, Parliamentary immunity protects lawmakers from civil or criminal charges while in office, and it shields them from prosecution for their political speech or political actions. This paper presents the first empirical analysis in the literature of the impact of Parliamentary immunity on the behavior and performance of politicians. Leveraging a Constitutional Amendment, the adoption of which lifted the immunity of 24 percent of the Members of the Turkish Parliament (MPs), we find that immunity from prosecution impacts how the MPs act and perform their duties in the Parliament. Losing immunity pacifies the MPs of the opposition parties, who become less diligent in the Parliament (drafting fewer pieces of legislation, initiating fewer investigation inquiries, delivering fewer and shorter speeches) and become less aggressive (interrupting other MPs less frequently). They also reduce their tendency to cast dissenting votes against the government. These MPs are less likely to get re-nominated by their parties in the next election, and they are less likely to get re-elected. We find no evidence that more outspoken and active opposition MPs or those who are more valuable for their parties have been targeted for immunity revocation. The results are robust to limiting the analysis to the sample of opposition MPs who had the same intensity of pre-treatment Parliamentary activity. There is no evidence that the MPs, who retained immunity, have increased their Parliamentary efforts in reaction to their same-party colleagues losing immunity. We find that laws are passed faster after the Constitutional Amendment was adopted, possibly as a consequence of reduced opposition and deliberation. Using Eurobarometer surveys, we find that citizens’ reactions to the revocation of MP immunity are polarized. An individual’s trust in the Parliament is lower or higher based on whether an MP from the individual’s province lost immunity and if that MP subscribes to the same or opposing ideology as the individual.

中文摘要

众所周知，政府支出在工业国家通常是逆周期的，而在发展中经济体则是顺周期的。大部分文献集中于分析总政府支出或可自由支配的支出类别，如政府消费和政府投资。然而，关于自动政府支出（包括失业保险、家庭项目和社会保障转移支付）的周期性行为，我们知之甚少。自动政府支出源于法律甚至宪法条款，使符合特定资格标准的个人受益。原则上，自动政府支出的主要类别预计要么是逆周期的（尤其是失业保险和其他减震项目），要么是非周期的（特别是社会保障和其他结构性项目）。我们发现，虽然自动政府支出在工业国家如预期般是逆周期的，但在发展中国家却令人惊讶地是顺周期的。我们将这种令人困惑的顺周期行为归因于：（i）有效缺乏像失业保险这样的自动稳定器，以及（ii）更有趣的是，社会保障支出中存在反常的自动去稳定机制（特别是在缺乏指数化机制的情况下）。我们还表明，这两个社会项目的存在和性质是总政府支出周期性和宏观经济波动性的关键新决定因素，即使在控制了其他已知决定因素并解决了潜在的内生性问题之后也是如此。

Exchange Rate Pass-Through, Monetary Policy, and Real Exchange Rates: Iceland and the 2008 Crisis

汇率传递、货币政策与实际汇率：冰岛与2008年危机

Sebastian Edwards, Luis Cabezas

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 冰岛

Abstract

We use detailed data for Iceland to examine two often-neglected aspects of the “exchange rate pass-through” problem. First, we investigate whether the pass-through coefficient varies with the degree of “international tradability” of goods. Second, we analyze if the pass-through coefficient depends on the monetary policy framework. We consider 12 disaggregated price indexes in Iceland for 2003-2019, a period that includes Iceland’s banking and currency crisis of 2008. We find that the pass-through declined around the time Iceland reformed its “flexible inflation targeting,” and that the coefficients are significantly higher for tradable than for nontradable goods.

中文摘要

The Trade-off Between Prioritization and Vaccination Speed Depends on Mitigation Measures

优先接种与接种速度之间的权衡取决于缓解措施

Nikhil Agarwal, Andrew Komo, Chetan A. Patel, Parag A. Pathak, M. Utku Ünver

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Calls for eliminating prioritization for SARS-CoV-2 vaccines are growing amid concerns that prioritization reduces vaccination speed. We use an SEIR model to study the effects of vaccination distribution on public health, comparing prioritization policy and speed under mitigation measures that are either eased during the vaccine rollout or sustained through the end of the pandemic period. NASEM's recommended prioritization results in fewer deaths than no prioritization, but does not minimize total deaths. If mitigation measures are eased, abandoning NASEM will result in about 134,000 more deaths at 30 million vaccinations per month. Vaccination speed must be at least 53% higher under no prioritization to avoid increasing deaths. With sustained mitigation, discarding NASEM prioritization will result in 42,000 more deaths, requiring only a 26% increase in speed to hold deaths constant. Therefore, abandoning NASEM's prioritization to increase vaccination speed without substantially increasing deaths may require sustained mitigation.

中文摘要

Biosimilar Competition: Early Learning

生物类似药竞争：早期经验

Richard G. Frank, Mahnum Shahzad, William B. Feldman, Aaron S. Kesselheim

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Biologics accounted for roughly $145 billion in spending in 2018 (IQVIA, 2019). They are also the fastest growing segment of the pharmaceutical industry. The Biological Price Competition and Innovation Act (BPCIA) of 2010 created an abbreviated pathway for biosimilar products to promote price competition in the market for biological drugs. There was great anticipation that the BPCIA would lead to a moderation in drug prices driven by market competition. The observed levels of competition and the accompanying savings have not reached those expected levels. we investigate the early impacts of entry of potential biosimilar competitors on use of biosimilars and prices for biological products. We focus especially on entry by biosimilars and how altered market structures stemming from the implementation of the BPCIA are affecting the prices for biological products subject to biosimilar competition. We do so by studying 7 products that have recently faced biosimilar competition. We estimate fixed effects and Instrument Variables models to estimate the impact of market competition on prices. Our results indicate that in the range of 1 to 3 entrants each additional marketed product results in a reduction in weighted average market prices of between 5.4 and 7 percentage points.

中文摘要

2018年，生物制剂支出约为1450亿美元（IQVIA，2019）。它们也是制药行业中增长最快的部分。2010年的《生物制品价格竞争与创新法案》（BPCIA）为生物类似药创建了一条简化的审批途径，以促进生物药品市场的价格竞争。人们曾高度期待BPCIA将通过市场竞争导致药品价格趋于温和。然而，观察到的竞争水平以及随之而来的节省并未达到预期水平。我们研究了潜在生物类似药竞争者进入对生物类似药使用和生物制品价格的早期影响。我们特别关注生物类似药的进入，以及BPCIA实施所导致的市场结构变化如何影响面临生物类似药竞争的生物制品的价格。为此，我们研究了近期面临生物类似药竞争的7种产品。我们估计了固定效应模型和工具变量模型，以评估市场竞争对价格的影响。我们的结果表明，在1到3个进入者的范围内，每增加一个上市产品，加权平均市场价格将下降5.4到7个百分点。
