The Impact of Healthcare IT on Clinical Quality, Productivity and Workers

医疗信息技术对临床质量、生产率和劳动力的影响

Ari Bronsoler, Joseph J. Doyle Jr., John Van Reenen

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Adoption of health information and communication technologies (“HICT”) has surged over the past two decades. We survey the medical and economic literature on HICT adoption and its impact on clinical outcomes, productivity and labor. We find that HICT improves clinical outcomes and lowers healthcare costs, but (i) the effects are modest so far, (ii) it takes time for these effects to materialize, and (iii) there is much variation in the impact. More evidence on the causal effects of HICT on productivity is needed to guide further adoption. There is little econometric work directly investigating the impact of HICT on labor, but what there is suggests no substantial negative effects on employment and earnings. Overall, while healthcare is “exceptional” in many ways, we are struck by the similarities to the wider findings on ICT and productivity stressing the importance of complementary factors (e.g. management and skills) in determining HICT impacts.

中文摘要

过去二十年，健康信息与通信技术（HICT）的采用激增。我们调查了关于HICT采用及其对临床结果、生产率和劳动力影响的医学和经济学文献。我们发现，HICT改善了临床结果并降低了医疗成本，但（i）目前效果温和，（ii）这些效果需要时间才能显现，（iii）影响存在很大差异。需要更多关于HICT对生产率因果效应的证据来指导进一步采用。直接调查HICT对劳动力影响的经济计量研究很少，但现有研究表明对就业和收入没有显著的负面影响。总体而言，尽管医疗保健在许多方面“特殊”，但我们对其与ICT和生产率更广泛研究结果的相似性感到惊讶，这些研究强调了互补因素（如管理和技能）在决定HICT影响中的重要性。

A Theory of the Global Financial Cycle

全球金融周期理论

J. Scott Davis, Eric van Wincoop

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We develop a theory to account for changes in prices of risky and safe assets and gross and net capital flows over the global financial cycle (GFC). The multi-country model features global risk-aversion shocks and heterogeneity of investors both within and across countries. Within-country heterogeneity is needed to account for the drop in gross capital flows during a negative GFC shock (higher global risk-aversion). Cross-country heterogeneity is needed to account for the differential vulnerability of countries to a negative GFC shock. The key vulnerability is associated with leverage. In both the data and the theory, leveraged countries (net borrowers of safe assets) deleverage through negative net outflows of risky assets and positive net outflows of safe assets, experience a rise in the current account and a greater than average drop in risky asset prices. The opposite is the case for non-leveraged countries (net lenders of safe assets).

中文摘要

我们发展了一个理论来解释全球金融周期（GFC）中风险资产和安全资产价格以及总资本流动和净资本流动的变化。该多国模型具有全球风险厌恶冲击以及国家内部和国家之间投资者的异质性。国家内部的异质性对于解释负面GFC冲击（更高的全球风险厌恶）期间总资本流动的下降是必要的。国家间的异质性对于解释各国对负面GFC冲击的不同脆弱性是必要的。关键脆弱性与杠杆相关。在数据和理论中，杠杆国家（安全资产的净借款人）通过风险资产的净流出和安全资产的净流入去杠杆化，经历经常账户上升和风险资产价格高于平均水平的下降。非杠杆国家（安全资产的净贷款人）则相反。

Intended and Unintended Effects of E-cigarette Taxes on Youth Tobacco Use

电子烟税对青少年烟草使用的预期与非预期效应

Rahi Abouk, Charles J. Courtemanche, Dhaval M. Dave, Bo Feng, Abigail S. Friedman, Johanna Catherine Maclean, Michael F. Pesko, Joseph J. Sabia, Samuel Safford

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Over the past decade, rising youth use of e-cigarettes and other electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS) has contributed to aggressive regulation by state and local governments. Between 2010 and mid-2019, ten states and two large counties adopted ENDS taxes. We use two large national surveys (Monitoring the Future and the Youth Risk Behavior Surveillance System) to estimate the impact of ENDS taxes on youth tobacco use. We find that ENDS taxes reduce youth ENDS consumption, with estimated ENDS tax elasticities of -0.06 to -0.21. However, we estimate sizable positive cigarette cross-tax effects, suggesting economic substitution between cigarettes and ENDS for youth. These substitution effects are particularly large for frequent cigarette smoking. We conclude that the unintended effects of ENDS taxation may considerably undercut or even outweigh any public health gains.

中文摘要

在过去十年中，青少年使用电子烟和其他电子尼古丁输送系统（ENDS）的增加促使州和地方政府采取严格监管。2010年至2019年中，十个州和两个大县通过了ENDS税。我们使用两项大型全国调查（“监测未来”和“青少年风险行为监测系统”）来估计ENDS税对青少年烟草使用的影响。我们发现，ENDS税减少了青少年ENDS消费，估计的ENDS税收弹性为-0.06至-0.21。然而，我们估计出显著的正向香烟交叉税效应，表明青少年在香烟和ENDS之间存在经济替代。这些替代效应对于频繁吸烟尤为显著。我们得出结论，ENDS税收的非预期效应可能大大削弱甚至超过任何公共健康收益。

Matching in the Dark? Inequalities in student to degree match

暗中的匹配？学生与学位质量之间的不平等

Stuart Campbell, Lindsey Macmillan, Richard Murphy, Gillian Wyness

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper examines inequalities in the match between student and degree quality using linked administrative data from schools, universities and tax authorities. We analyse two measures of match at the university-subject level: undergraduate enrollment qualifications, and graduate earnings. We find for both that disadvantaged students match to lower quality degrees across the entire distribution of achievement, in a setting with uniform fees and a generous financial aid system. While there are negligible gender gaps in academic match, high-attaining women systematically undermatch in terms of expected earnings, driven by subject choice. These inequalities in match are largest among the most undermatched.

中文摘要

本文利用来自学校、大学和税务部门的关联行政数据，研究了学生与学位质量之间匹配的不平等问题。我们分析了大学-学科层面的两种匹配度量：本科入学资格和毕业生收入。我们发现，在统一学费和慷慨的助学金体系下，弱势学生在整个成绩分布中都匹配到较低质量的学位。虽然在学术匹配方面性别差距可以忽略不计，但高成就女性在预期收入方面系统性低配，这由学科选择驱动。这些匹配不平等在低配最严重的群体中最为显著。

Opportunity Unraveled: Private Information and the Missing Markets for Financing Human Capital

机会瓦解：私人信息与人力资本融资的缺失市场

Daniel Herbst, Nathaniel Hendren

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Investing in college carries high returns but comes with considerable risk. Financial products like equity contracts can mitigate this risk, yet college is typically financed through non-dischargeable, government-backed student loans. This paper argues that adverse selection has unraveled private markets for college-financing contracts that mitigate risk. We use survey data on students’ expected post-college outcomes to estimate their knowledge about future outcomes and quantify the threat of adverse selection in markets for equity contracts and several state-contingent debt contracts. We find students hold significant private knowledge of their future earnings, academic persistence, employment, and loan repayment likelihood, beyond what is captured by observable characteristics. Our empirical results imply that a typical college-goer must expect to pay back $1.64 in present value for every $1 of equity financing to cover the financier’s costs of covering those who would adversely select their contract. We estimate that college-goers are not willing to accept these terms so that private markets unravel. Nonetheless, our framework quantifies significant welfare gains from government subsidies that would open up these missing markets and partially insure college-going risks.

中文摘要

大学投资回报率高，但伴随相当大的风险。股权合约等金融产品可以缓解这种风险，然而大学通常通过不可免除的政府支持学生贷款来融资。本文认为，逆向选择已经瓦解了缓解风险的大学融资合约的私人市场。我们利用学生对大学毕业后结果的预期调查数据，来估计他们对未来结果的了解程度，并量化股权合约和几种状态依存债务合约市场中逆向选择的威胁。我们发现，学生对其未来收入、学业持续性、就业和贷款偿还可能性拥有显著的私人知识，超出了可观测特征所能捕捉的信息。我们的实证结果表明，一个典型的大学就读生必须预期每获得1美元股权融资，就要偿还现值1.64美元，才能覆盖金融家因那些逆向选择合约者而产生的成本。我们估计大学就读生不愿接受这些条款，因此私人市场瓦解。尽管如此，我们的框架量化了政府补贴带来的显著福利收益，这些补贴可以开放这些缺失市场，并部分保险大学就读风险。

The Employment and Redistributive Effects of Reducing or Eliminating Minimum Wage Tip Credits

减少或取消最低工资小费抵免的就业与再分配效应

David Neumark, Maysen Yen

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Recent policy debate on minimum wages has focused not only on raising the minimum wage, but on eliminating the tip credit for restaurant workers. We use data on past variation in tip credits—or minimum wages for restaurant workers—to provide evidence on the potential impacts of eliminating (or reducing) the tip credit. Our evidence points to higher tipped minimum wages (smaller tip credits) reducing jobs among tipped restaurant workers, without earnings effects on those who remain employed sufficiently large to raise total earnings in this sector. And most of our evidence provides no indication that higher tipped minimum wages would be well targeted to poor or low-income families or reduce the likelihood of being poor or very low income.

中文摘要

近期关于最低工资的政策辩论不仅关注提高最低工资，还关注取消餐厅工人的小费抵免。我们利用过去小费抵免（即餐厅工人最低工资）变动的数据，为取消（或减少）小费抵免的潜在影响提供证据。我们的证据表明，更高的有最低工资（更小的小费抵免）减少了有餐厅工人的就业，而继续就业者的收入效应不足以大到提高该部门的总收入。而且，我们的大部分证据并未表明更高的有最低工资能精准针对贫困或低收入家庭，或降低陷入贫困或极低收入的可能性。

Corporate Actions and the Manipulation of Retail Investors in China: An Analysis of Stock Splits

公司行为与中国散户投资者的操纵：股票拆分分析

Sheridan Titman, Chishen Wei. Wei, Bin Zhao

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价

Abstract

We identify a group of “suspicious” firms that use stock splits—perhaps, along with other activities—to artificially inflate their share prices. Following the initiation of suspicious splits, share prices temporarily increase, and subsequently decline below their pre-split levels. Using account level data from the Shanghai Stock Exchange, we find that small retail investors acquire shares in firms initiating suspicious splits, while more sophisticated investors accumulate positions before suspicious split announcements and sell in the post-split period. We also find that insiders sell large blocks of shares and obtain loans using company stock as collateral around the initiation of suspicious splits.

中文摘要

我们识别出一组“可疑”公司，它们利用股票拆分——可能还伴随其他活动——来人为抬高股价。在可疑拆分启动后，股价暂时上涨，随后下跌至拆分前水平以下。利用上海证券交易所的账户级数据，我们发现小型散户投资者购买启动可疑拆分的公司的股票，而更精明的投资者在可疑拆分公告前积累头寸，并在拆分后期间卖出。我们还发现，内部人士在可疑拆分启动前后出售大额股票，并以公司股票为抵押获得贷款。

No. 8论文详情NBER w29211

Venture Capital and Startup Agglomeration

风险投资与初创企业集聚

Jun Chen, Michael Ewens

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

The paper studies venture capital’s (VC) role in the geographic clustering of high-growth startups. We exploit a rule change that disproportionately impacted U.S. regions that historically lacked VC financing via a restriction of banks to invest in the asset class. A one-standard-deviation increase in VCs’ exposure to the rule led to a 20% decline in fund size and a 10% decrease in the likelihood of raising a follow-on fund. Startups were not wholly cushioned: financing and valuations declined. Startups also moved out of impacted states after the rule change, likely exacerbating existing geographic disparity in entrepreneurship.

中文摘要

本文研究风险投资在高增长初创企业地理集聚中的作用。我们利用一项规则变更，该变更通过限制银行投资于该资产类别，对历史上缺乏风险投资融资的美国地区产生了不成比例的影响。风险投资对该规则暴露程度的一个标准差增加，导致基金规模下降20%，以及筹集后续基金的可能性下降10%。初创企业并未完全得到缓冲：融资和估值下降。规则变更后，初创企业也迁出受影响的州，这可能加剧了创业方面已有的地理差异。

Market Freezes

市场冻结

Chao Gu, Guido Menzio, Randall Wright, Yu Zhu

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

During the financial crisis apparently centralized markets continued to function while trade in OTC markets froze. We use search-and-bargaining theory to ascertain conditions that allow trade to temporarily freeze in decentralized markets, focusing on the roles of liquidity and self-fulfilling prophecies. We show standard models can have recurrent, belief-driven hot and cold spells, but not freezes and thaws. A simple specification that has freezes assumes negative returns. A more realistic one incorporates information frictions (costly asset-quality verification). Another uses different frictions to get credit freezes. We also discuss policy implications, and go into detail on the nature of OTC markets.

中文摘要

在金融危机期间，集中市场显然继续运转，而场外市场的交易却冻结了。我们利用搜索与讨价还价理论来确定允许交易在分散市场中暂时冻结的条件，重点关注流动性和自我实现预言的作用。我们表明，标准模型可以出现反复的、信念驱动的热期和冷期，但不能出现冻结和解冻。一个具有冻结的简单设定假设负回报。一个更现实的设定包含信息摩擦（昂贵的资产质量验证）。另一个设定利用不同的摩擦来获得信贷冻结。我们还讨论了政策含义，并详细阐述了场外市场的性质。

What Can Stockouts Tell Us About Inflation? Evidence from Online Micro Data

缺货能告诉我们关于通胀的什么？来自在线微观数据的证据

Alberto Cavallo, Oleksiy Kryvtsov

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use a detailed micro dataset on product availability to construct a direct high-frequency measure of consumer product shortages during the 2020–2021 pandemic. We document a widespread multi-fold rise in shortages in nearly all sectors early in the pandemic. Over time, the composition of shortages evolved from many temporary stockouts to mostly discontinued products, concentrated in fewer sectors. We show that unexpected product shortages have signiﬁcant inﬂationary eﬀects within three months. We develop a model of inventories in a sector facing both demand and cost disturbances, and use the observed joint dynamics of stockouts and prices to show that these eﬀects can be associated with elevated cost of replenishing inventories.

中文摘要

我们利用关于产品可用性的详细微观数据集，构建了2020-2021年疫情期间消费者产品短缺的直接高频度量。我们记录了疫情初期几乎所有部门普遍出现的数倍短缺增长。随着时间的推移，短缺的构成从许多临时缺货演变为主要是停产产品，集中在较少的部门。我们表明，意外的产品短缺在三个月内会产生显著的通货膨胀效应。我们开发了一个面临需求和成本扰动的部门库存模型，并利用观察到的缺货和价格的联合动态来表明，这些效应可能与库存补货成本上升有关。

No. 11论文详情NBER w29208

Who Cares More? Allocation with Diverse Preference Intensities

谁更在意？具有不同偏好强度的分配

Pietro Ortoleva, Evgenii Safonov, Leeat Yariv

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Goods and services---public housing, medical appointments, schools---are often allocated to individuals who rank them similarly but differ in their preference intensities. We characterize optimal allocation rules when individual preferences are known and when they are not. Several insights emerge. First-best allocations may involve assigning some agents "lotteries" between high- and low-ranked goods. When preference intensities are private information, second-best allocations always involve such lotteries and, crucially, may coincide with first-best allocations. Furthermore, second-best allocations may entail disposal of services. We discuss a market-based alternative and show how it differs.

中文摘要

商品和服务——公共住房、医疗预约、学校——通常分配给那些排名相似但偏好强度不同的个体。我们刻画了当个体偏好已知和未知时的最优分配规则。出现了几个见解。第一最优分配可能涉及将一些代理人分配到高排名和低排名商品之间的“彩票”。当偏好强度是私人信息时，第二最优分配总是涉及此类彩票，并且关键的是，可能与第一最优分配一致。此外，第二最优分配可能涉及服务的处置。我们讨论了一种基于市场的替代方案，并展示了其不同之处。

No. 12论文详情NBER w29207

Hedging and Competition

对冲与竞争

Erasmo Giambona, Anil Kumar, Gordon M. Phillips

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study how risk management through hedging impacts firms and competition among firms in the life insurance industry - an industry with over 7 Trillion in assets and over 1,000 private and public firms. We show that firms that are likely to face costly external finance increase hedging after staggered state-level financial reform that reduces the costs of hedging. Post reform impacted firms have lower risk and fewer negative income shocks. Product market competition is also impacted. Firms that previously are more likely to face costly external finance, lower price, increase policy sales and increase their market share post reform. The results are consistent with hedging allowing firms that face potential costly financial distress to decrease risk and become more competitive.

中文摘要

我们研究了对冲风险管理如何影响人寿保险行业中的企业及其竞争——该行业资产超过7万亿美元，拥有超过1000家私营和上市公司。我们发现，在分阶段实施的州级金融改革降低了对冲成本后，那些可能面临昂贵外部融资的企业增加了对冲操作。改革后，受影响的企业风险降低，负面收入冲击减少。产品市场竞争也受到影响。此前更可能面临昂贵外部融资的企业，在改革后降低了价格，增加了保单销售，并扩大了市场份额。这些结果与以下观点一致：对冲使可能面临昂贵财务困境的企业能够降低风险并变得更具竞争力。

Markups and Fixed Costs in Generic and Off-Patent Pharmaceutical Markets

仿制药和专利过期药品市场的加价与固定成本

Sharat Ganapati, Rebecca McKibbin

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

There is wide dispersion in pharmaceutical prices across countries with comparable quality standards. Under monopoly, off-patent and generic drug prices are at least four times higher in the United States than in comparable English-speaking high income countries. With five or more competitors, off-patent drug prices are similar or lower. Our analysis shows that differential US markups are largely driven by the market power of drug suppliers and not due to wholesale intermediaries or pharmacies. Furthermore, we show that the traditional mechanism of reducing market power – free entry – is limited because implied entry costs are substantially higher in the US.

中文摘要

Models Behaving Badly: The Limits of Data-Driven Lending

行为失常的模型：数据驱动贷款的局限性

Itzhak Ben-David, Mark J. Johnson, René M. Stulz

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Data-driven lending relies on the calibration of models using training periods. We find that this type of lending is not resilient in the presence of economic conditions that are materially different from those experienced during the training period. Using data from a small business fintech lending platform, we document that the small business credit supply collapsed during the COVID-19 crisis of March 2020 even though the demand for loans doubled relative to pre-pandemic levels. As the month progressed, most lenders significantly reduced or halted their lending activities, likely due to the heightened risk of model miscalibration under the new economic conditions.

中文摘要

数据驱动贷款依赖于使用训练期对模型进行校准。我们发现，当经济条件与训练期经历的条件存在显著差异时，这种贷款方式缺乏韧性。利用一家小型企业金融科技贷款平台的数据，我们记录到，在2020年3月COVID-19危机期间，小型企业信贷供应崩溃，尽管贷款需求相比疫情前翻了一番。随着该月的推进，大多数贷款机构大幅减少或停止了贷款活动，这很可能是由于新经济条件下模型校准错误的风险增加。

Are Collateral-Constraint Models Ready for Macroprudential Policy Design?

抵押品约束模型是否已准备好用于宏观审慎政策设计？

Pablo Ottonello, Diego J. Perez, Paolo Varraso

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the design of macroprudential policies based on quantitative collateral-constraint models. We show that the desirability of macroprudential policies critically depends on the specific form of collateral used in debt contracts: While inefficiencies arise when current prices affect collateral---a frequent benchmark used to guide policies---they do not when only future prices affect collateral. Since the microfoundations and quantitative predictions of models with future-price collateral constraints do not appear less plausible than those using current prices, we conclude that additional empirical work is essential for the use of these models in macroprudential policy design.

中文摘要

我们研究了基于定量抵押品约束模型的宏观审慎政策设计。我们表明，宏观审慎政策的可取性关键取决于债务合同中使用的抵押品具体形式：当当前价格影响抵押品时——这是指导政策的常用基准——会出现效率低下；而当只有未来价格影响抵押品时，则不会出现效率低下。由于采用未来价格抵押品约束的模型的微观基础与定量预测似乎并不比使用当前价格的模型更不可信，我们得出结论，需要额外的实证工作才能将这些模型用于宏观审慎政策设计。

Hannes Schwandt, Janet Currie, Marlies Bär, James Banks, Paola Bertoli, Aline Bütikofer, Sarah Cattan, Beatrice Zong-Ying Chao, Claudia Costa, Libertad Gonzalez, Veronica Grembi, Kristiina Huttunen, René Karadakic, Lucy Kraftman, Sonya Krutikova, Stefano Lombardi, Peter Redler, Carlos Riumallo-Herl, Ana Rodríguez-González, Kjell Salvanes, Paula Santana, Josselin Thuilliez, Eddy van Doorslaer, Tom Van Ourti, Joachim Winter, Bram Wouterse, Amelie Wuppermann

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Although there is a large gap between Black and White American life expectancies, the gap fell 48.9% between 1990-2018, mainly due to mortality declines among Black Americans. We examine age-specific mortality trends and racial gaps in life expectancy in rich and poor U.S. areas and with reference to six European countries. \nInequalities in life expectancy are starker in the U.S. than in Europe. In 1990 White Americans and Europeans in rich areas had similar overall life expectancy, while life expectancy for White Americans in poor areas was lower. But since then even rich White Americans have lost ground relative to Europeans. Meanwhile, the gap in life expectancy between Black Americans and Europeans decreased by 8.3%. \nBlack life expectancy increased more than White life expectancy in all U.S. areas, but improvements in poorer areas had the greatest impact on the racial life expectancy gap. The causes that contributed the most to Black mortality reductions included: Cancer, homicide, HIV, and causes originating in the fetal or infant period. \nLife expectancy for both Black and White Americans plateaued or slightly declined after 2012, but this stalling was most evident among Black Americans even prior to the COVID-19 pandemic. If improvements had continued at the 1990-2012 rate, the racial gap in life expectancy would have closed by 2036. European life expectancy also stalled after 2014. Still, the comparison with Europe suggests that mortality rates of both Black and White Americans could fall much further across all ages and in both rich and poor areas.
