Reconsidering Risk Aversion

重新审视风险厌恶

Daniel J. Benjamin, Mark Alan Fontana, Miles S. Kimball

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Risk aversion is typically inferred from real or hypothetical choices over risky lotteries, but such “untutored” choices may reflect mistakes rather than preferences. We develop a procedure to obtain a better measure of normatively relevant preferences: after eliciting untutored choices, we confront participants with their choices that are inconsistent with intertemporal-expected-utility axioms and allow them to reconsider their choices. We demonstrate this procedure via a survey about hypothetical retirement investment choices administered to 596 Cornell students. We find that, on average, reconsidered choices are more consistent with almost all axioms, with one exception related to a counterfactual reference point.

中文摘要

风险厌恶通常从对风险彩票的真实或假设选择中推断出来，但这种“未经指导的”选择可能反映的是错误而非偏好。我们开发了一个程序来获得规范性相关偏好的更好度量：在引出未经指导的选择后，我们让参与者面对其与跨期期望效用公理不一致的选择，并允许他们重新考虑自己的选择。我们通过对596名康奈尔学生进行的关于假设退休投资选择的调查来演示这一程序。我们发现，平均而言，重新考虑后的选择与几乎所有公理更加一致，但有一个与反事实参照点相关的例外。

How Should Tax Progressivity Respond to Rising Income Inequality?

税收累进性应如何应对收入不平等加剧？

Jonathan Heathcote, Kjetil Storesletten, Giovanni L. Violante

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

We address this question in a heterogeneous-agent incomplete-markets model featuring exogenous idiosyncratic risk, endogenous skill investment, and flexible labor supply. The tax and transfer schedule is restricted to be log-linear in income, a good description of the US system. Rising inequality is modeled as a combination of skill-biased technical change and growth in residual wage dispersion. When facing shifts in the income distribution like those observed in the US, a utilitarian planner chooses higher progressivity in response to larger residual inequality but lower progressivity in response to widening skill price dispersion reflecting technical change. Overall, optimal progressivity is approximately unchanged between 1980 and 2016. We document that the progressivity of the actual US tax and transfer system has similarly changed little since 1980, in line with the model prescription.

中文摘要

我们在一个具有外生异质性风险、内生技能投资和弹性劳动供给的异质性代理人不完全市场模型中探讨这一问题。税收和转移支付计划被限制为收入的对数线性形式，这很好地描述了美国体系。不平等加剧被建模为技能偏向型技术变化和残差工资离散度增长的组合。当面临类似美国观察到的收入分布变化时，功利主义规划者会选择更高的累进性以应对更大的残差不平等，但会选择更低的累进性以应对反映技术变化的技能价格离散度扩大。总体而言，1980年至2016年间最优累进性大致保持不变。我们记录到，自1980年以来，美国实际税收和转移支付体系的累进性同样变化甚微，这与模型建议一致。

The Slope of the Phillips Curve: Evidence from U.S. States

菲利普斯曲线的斜率：来自美国各州的证据

Jonathon Hazell, Juan Herreño, Emi Nakamura, Jón Steinsson

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

We estimate the slope of the Phillips curve in the cross section of U.S. states using newly constructed state-level price indexes for non-tradeable goods back to 1978. Our estimates indicate that the slope of the Phillips curve is small and was small even during the early 1980s. We estimate only a modest decline in the slope of the Phillips curve since the 1980s. We use a multi-region model to infer the slope of the aggregate Phillips curve from our regional estimates. Applying our estimates to recent unemployment dynamics yields essentially no missing disinflation or missing reinflation over the past few business cycles. Our results imply that the sharp drop in core inflation in the early 1980s was mostly due to shifting expectations about long-run monetary policy as opposed to a steep Phillips curve, and the greater stability of inflation since the 1990s is mostly due to long-run inflationary expectations becoming more firmly anchored.

中文摘要

我们利用新构建的、可追溯至1978年的美国各州非贸易品价格指数，在截面数据上估计了菲利普斯曲线的斜率。我们的估计表明，菲利普斯曲线的斜率很小，即使在20世纪80年代初期也是如此。我们估计自20世纪80年代以来，菲利普斯曲线的斜率仅出现了适度下降。我们使用一个多区域模型，从我们的区域估计中推断出总体菲利普斯曲线的斜率。将我们的估计应用于近期的失业动态，结果显示在过去几个商业周期中，基本上不存在缺失的通缩或缺失的再通胀。我们的结果表明，20世纪80年代初核心通胀率的急剧下降主要是由于对长期货币政策的预期转变，而非陡峭的菲利普斯曲线；而自20世纪90年代以来通胀率更加稳定，主要是由于长期通胀预期变得更加稳固。

Macroeconomic Outcomes and COVID-19: A Progress Report

宏观经济结果与COVID-19：进展报告

Jesús Fernández-Villaverde, Charles I. Jones

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 德国、日本、挪威、韩国、瑞典、英国、美国

Abstract

This paper combines data on GDP, unemployment, and Google's COVID-19 Community Mobility Reports with data on deaths from COVID-19 to study the macroeconomic outcomes of the pandemic. We present results from an international perspective using data at the country level as well as results for individual U.S. states and key cities throughout the world. The data from these different levels of geographic aggregation offer a remarkably similar view of the pandemic despite the substantial heterogeneity in outcomes. Countries like Korea, Japan, Germany, and Norway and cities such as Tokyo and Seoul have comparatively few deaths and low macroeconomic losses. At the other extreme, New York City, Lombardy, the United Kingdom, and Madrid have many deaths and large macroeconomic losses. There are fewer locations that seem to succeed on one dimension but suffer on the other, but these include California and Sweden. The variety of cases potentially offers useful policy lessons regarding how to use non-pharmaceutical interventions to support good economic and health outcomes.

中文摘要

本文结合GDP、失业率、谷歌COVID-19社区流动性报告以及COVID-19死亡数据，研究疫情对宏观经济的影响。我们利用国家层面的数据从国际视角呈现结果，同时也分析了美国各州和全球主要城市的情况。尽管结果存在显著异质性，但这些不同地理聚合层面的数据对疫情提供了极为相似的看法。韩国、日本、德国和挪威等国家，以及东京和首尔等城市，死亡人数相对较少，宏观经济损失也较低。在另一个极端，纽约市、伦巴第、英国和马德里死亡人数众多，宏观经济损失巨大。似乎在一方面成功但在另一方面遭受损失的地点较少，但其中包括加利福尼亚和瑞典。这些多样化的案例可能为如何利用非药物干预措施支持良好的经济和健康结果提供有用的政策教训。

Impact of Consequence Information on Insurance Choice

后果信息对保险选择的影响

Anya Samek, Justin R. Sydnor

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Insurance choices are often hard to rationalize by standard theory and frequently appear sub-optimal. A key reason may be that people are unable to map the cost-sharing features of plans to their distribution of financial consequences. We develop and experimentally test a decision aid that provides this mapping to simplify comparisons of plan options. In two experiments mirroring typical health insurance decisions, we find that when people choose plans using standard feature-based information, they violate dominance at high rates. Our distribution-based decision aid substantially reduces dominance violations, and also changes choice patterns in situations where there is no dominant option. Choice patterns under feature-based menus can be most easily rationalized by models of heuristic choices, such as minimizing premium or deductible. With the decision aid, though, significantly more people have choice patterns that are better explained by expected utility theory. We compare our distribution-based approach to an alternative of providing estimates of the expected value of costs, which is the most common decision-support available in most insurance markets. Providing expected values affects choices in a similar direction as our consequence-based approach, but in a more muted fashion, and is only about half as effective at reducing dominance violations.

中文摘要

保险选择通常难以用标准理论合理化，并且经常显得次优。一个关键原因可能是人们无法将计划的成本分摊特征映射到其财务后果的分布上。我们开发并实验测试了一种决策辅助工具，该工具提供了这种映射，以简化计划选项的比较。在两项模拟典型健康保险决策的实验中，我们发现，当人们使用基于标准特征的信息选择计划时，他们以高比率违反优势原则。我们的基于分布的决策辅助工具显著减少了优势违反，并且在没有主导选项的情况下也改变了选择模式。基于特征菜单下的选择模式最容易通过启发式选择模型（如最小化保费或免赔额）来合理化。然而，有了决策辅助工具，显著更多人的选择模式能更好地由预期效用理论解释。我们将基于分布的方法与提供成本预期值估计的替代方法进行比较，后者是大多数保险市场中最常见的决策支持。提供预期值对选择的影响方向与我们的基于后果的方法相似，但影响更为温和，并且在减少优势违反方面的效果仅约为后者的一半。

r Minus g

r 减去 g

Robert J. Barro

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Long-term data show that the dynamic efficiency condition `r>g` holds when `g` is represented by the average growth rate of real GDP if `r` is the average real rate of return on equity, `E(r^e)`, but not if `r` is the risk-free rate, `r^f`. This pattern accords with a simple disaster-risk model calibrated to fit observed equity premia. If Ponzi (chain-letter) finance by private agents and the government are precluded, the equilibrium can feature `r^f≤E(g)`, a result that does not signal dynamic inefficiency. In contrast, `E(r^e)>E(g)` is required for dynamic efficiency, implied by the model, and consistent with the data. The model satisfies Ricardian Equivalence because, without Ponzi finance by the government, a rise in safe assets from increased public debt is matched by an increase in the safe (that is, certain) present value of liabilities associated with net taxes.\n\n

中文摘要

长期数据显示，当g以实际GDP平均增长率表示，且r为权益平均实际回报率E(r^e)时，动态效率条件“r>g”成立；但如果r为无风险利率r^f，则不成立。这一模式符合一个简单的灾难风险模型，该模型经过校准以拟合观察到的股权溢价。如果禁止私人主体和政府进行庞氏（连锁信）融资，均衡可能呈现r^f≤E(g)的特征，这一结果并不表明动态无效率。相比之下，动态效率要求E(r^e)>E(g)，这由模型隐含且与数据一致。该模型满足李嘉图等价，因为在没有政府庞氏融资的情况下，公共债务增加带来的安全资产上升，与净税收相关负债的安全（即确定的）现值增加相匹配。

Searching for the Equity Premium

寻找股权溢价

Hang Bai, Lu Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Labor market frictions are crucial for the equity premium in production economies. A dynamic stochastic general equilibrium model with recursive utility, search frictions, and capital accumulation yields a high equity premium of 4.26% per annum, a stock market volatility of 11.8%, and a low average interest rate of 1.59%, while simultaneously retaining plausible business cycle dynamics. The equity premium and stock market volatility are strongly countercyclical, while the interest rate and consumption growth are largely unpredictable. Because of wage inertia, dividends are procyclical despite consumption smoothing via capital investment. The welfare cost of business cycles is huge, 29%.

中文摘要

劳动力市场摩擦对于生产经济中的股权溢价至关重要。一个具有递归效用、搜索摩擦和资本积累的动态随机一般均衡模型，产生了每年4.26%的高股权溢价、11.8%的股市波动率和1.59%的低平均利率，同时保持了合理的商业周期动态。股权溢价和股市波动率呈现强烈的逆周期性，而利率和消费增长在很大程度上不可预测。由于工资惯性，尽管通过资本投资进行消费平滑，股息仍呈顺周期性。商业周期的福利成本巨大，达到29%。

Industrial Clusters, Networks and Resilience to the Covid-19 Shock in China

产业集群、网络与中国对COVID-19冲击的韧性

Ruochen Dai, Dilip Mookherjee, Yingyue Quan, Xiaobo Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题

Abstract

We examine how exposure of Chinese firms to the Covid-19 shock varied with a cluster index (measuring spatial agglomeration of firms in related industries) at the county level. Two data sources are used: entry flows of newly registered firms in the entire country, and an entrepreneur survey regarding operation of existing firms. Both show greater resilience in counties with a higher cluster index, after controlling for industry dummies and local infection rates, besides county and time dummies in the entry data. Reliance of clusters on informal entrepreneur hometown networks and closer proximity to suppliers and customers help explain these findings.

中文摘要

我们考察了中国企业受COVID-19冲击的程度如何随县级集群指数（衡量相关行业企业空间集聚程度的指标）而变化。使用了两个数据来源：全国新注册企业的进入流量，以及关于现有企业运营的企业家调查。在控制行业虚拟变量和本地感染率，以及进入数据中的县和时间虚拟变量后，两者均显示集群指数较高的县具有更强的韧性。集群对非正式企业家同乡网络的依赖以及与供应商和客户更近的距离有助于解释这些发现。

The Long-Run Effects of the Affordable Care Act: A Pre-Committed Research Design Over the COVID-19 Recession and Recovery

《平价医疗法案》的长期效应：跨越COVID-19衰退与复苏的预先承诺研究设计

Jeffrey Clemens, Drew McNichols, Joseph J. Sabia

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The long-run costs and benefits of social insurance expansions may not be realized until a program has been in place through a cycle of boom, bust, and recovery. In the case of the Affordable Care Act (ACA), the arrival of the program's inaugural bust and recovery have been hastened by the COVID-19 pandemic. In this context, our analysis begins by developing two facts. First, during the pre-pandemic boom, we show that the ACA's effects had largely stabilized by 2016. Second, we develop a new fact involving variations in the ACA's effects across industries.
