The Economic Impacts of the US-China Trade War

中美贸易战的经济影响

Pablo Fajgelbaum, Amit Khandelwal

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

In 2018, the US launched a trade war with China, an abrupt departure from its historical leadership in integrating global markets. By late 2019, the US had imposed tariffs on roughly $350 billion of Chinese imports, and China had retaliated on $100 billion US exports. Economists have used a diversity of data and methods to assess the impacts of the trade war on the US, China and other countries. This article reviews what we have learned to date from this work.

中文摘要

2018年，美国对中国发起了贸易战，这与其在整合全球市场方面的历史领导地位截然不同。到2019年底，美国已对约3500亿美元的中国进口商品加征关税，中国也对1000亿美元的美国出口商品进行了报复。经济学家们运用多种数据和方法评估了贸易战对美国、中国及其他国家的影响。本文回顾了迄今为止我们从这些研究中了解到的内容。

The Role of Trade in Economic Development

贸易在经济发展中的作用

David Atkin, Dave Donaldson

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This chapter (to appear in the forthcoming Handbook of International Economics, Vol. 5) develops a framework with which to interpret and survey answers to the question: how does increased openness affect aggregate welfare in a typical developing country? We decompose answers into four mechanisms: (i) an effect akin to technological progress that is purely mechanical; (ii) an effect on factoral terms of trade; (iii) a distortion revenue effect that exacerbates or mitigates the consequences of domestic distortions; and (iv) an effect of trade on the magnitude of those distortions themselves. Our focus lies in the last two mechanisms as these are especially important for the study of low-income settings where domestic distortions are thought to be rife. Throughout, we provide both a review of existing work on these topics and quantitative calculations that aim to gauge the magnitudes involved in a global model that is calibrated to match firm- and industry-level data on trade flows, production techniques, and a host of distortions (tariffs, other taxes, markups, bribes, theft, credit constraints, contracting failures, labor regulations, and public utility provision) that have featured in the literature.

中文摘要

本章（将收录于即将出版的《国际经济学手册》第五卷）构建了一个框架，用以解释和综述以下问题的答案：在一个典型的发展中国家，开放程度的提高如何影响总体福利？我们将答案分解为四种机制：（i）类似于技术进步且纯粹机械性的效应；（ii）对要素贸易条件的影响；（iii）扭曲收入效应，该效应会加剧或减轻国内扭曲的后果；以及（iv）贸易对这些扭曲本身规模的影响。我们的重点在于最后两种机制，因为它们对于研究国内扭曲被认为普遍存在的低收入环境尤为重要。在整个过程中，我们既回顾了关于这些主题的现有研究，也进行了定量计算，旨在评估一个全球模型中所涉及的数量级，该模型经过校准，以匹配关于贸易流动、生产技术以及一系列扭曲（关税、其他税收、加成率、贿赂、盗窃、信贷约束、合同失败、劳动法规和公共事业供给）的企业和行业层面数据，这些扭曲在文献中均有涉及。

Foreign Investors and US Treasuries

外国投资者与美国国债

Alexandra M. Tabova, Francis E. Warnock

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We build, from confidential security-level surveys, a novel dataset on the size, flows, coupon payments, and returns of the US Treasuries portfolios of foreign and US investors. The internally consistent dataset provides evidence on foreigners’ Treasuries portfolios that sharply contrasts with what is portrayed in the literature. Foreigners do not earn spectacularly low returns; they have higher average annual risk-adjusted returns than US investors (although the differences are well within the bounds of standard confidence intervals), as US investors’ strong mean returns are accompanied by higher volatility. Foreigners do not buy Treasuries when they are expensive; dollar-weighted returns that capture the timing and volume of purchases show that both private and official foreigners outperform US investors. And private foreign investors do not have inelastic demand; they are price sensitive, increasing purchases of Treasuries and the duration of their Treasury portfolios when non-US sovereign yields are low or decrease relative to Treasury yields. Our results should inform many literatures, including any that use data on international positions and flows. In fact, an important practical contribution is that the dataset shows which publicly available data on foreigners’ flows should (and should not) be used.

中文摘要

我们利用保密的证券层面调查，构建了一个关于外国和美国投资者持有的美国国债组合的规模、流动、息票支付和回报的新数据集。这个内部一致的数据集提供了关于外国人美国国债组合的证据，与文献中所描绘的情况形成鲜明对比。外国人并未获得极低的回报；他们的平均年化风险调整后回报高于美国投资者（尽管差异在标准置信区间范围内），因为美国投资者的强劲平均回报伴随着更高的波动性。外国人并非在国债昂贵时买入；捕捉购买时机和数量的美元加权回报显示，私人和官方外国投资者的表现均优于美国投资者。私人外国投资者并非需求缺乏弹性；他们对价格敏感，当非美国主权收益率较低或相对于美国国债收益率下降时，他们会增加对美国国债的购买以及其国债组合的久期。我们的研究结果应为众多文献提供信息，包括任何使用国际头寸和流动数据的文献。事实上，一个重要的实际贡献是，该数据集显示了哪些公开可用的外国人流动数据应该（以及不应该）被使用。

Task Allocation and On-the-job Training

任务分配与在职培训

Mariagiovanna Baccara, SangMok Lee, Leeat Yariv

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study dynamic task allocation when providers' expertise evolves endogenously through training. We characterize optimal assignment protocols and compare them to discretionary procedures, where it is the clients who select their service providers. Our results indicate that welfare gains from centralization are greater when tasks arrive more rapidly, and when training technologies improve. Monitoring seniors' backlog of clients always increases welfare but may decrease training. Methodologically, we explore a matching setting with endogenous types, and illustrate useful adaptations of queueing theory techniques for such environments.

中文摘要

我们研究了当服务提供者的专业知识通过培训内生演化时的动态任务分配问题。我们刻画了最优分配协议，并将其与自由裁量程序（即由客户选择其服务提供者）进行比较。我们的结果表明，当任务到达更频繁且培训技术改进时，集中化带来的福利收益更大。监控资深服务提供者的客户积压总是能增加福利，但可能会减少培训。在方法论上，我们探索了一个具有内生类型的匹配设定，并展示了排队论技术在此类环境中的有用适应性。

Big Loans to Small Businesses: Predicting Winners and Losers in an Entrepreneurial Lending Experiment

小企业的大额贷款：预测创业贷款实验中的赢家和输家

Gharad T. Bryan, Dean Karlan, Adam Osman

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 埃及

Abstract

We experimentally study the impact of relatively large enterprise loans in Egypt. Larger loans generate small average impacts, but machine learning using psychometric data reveals that ”top-performers” (those with the highest predicted treatment effects) substantially increase profits, while profits drop for poor-performers. The large differences imply that lender credit allocation decisions matter for aggregate income, yet we find that existing practice leads to substantial misallocation. We argue that some entrepreneurs are over-optimistic and squander the opportunities presented by larger loans by taking on too much risk, and show the promise of allocations based on entrepreneurial type relative to firm characteristics.

中文摘要

我们通过实验研究了埃及相对较大规模企业贷款的影响。较大规模的贷款产生的平均影响很小，但使用心理测量数据的机器学习揭示，“表现最佳者”（那些具有最高预测处理效应的企业）的利润大幅增加，而表现不佳者的利润则下降。这种巨大差异意味着贷款人的信贷分配决策对总收入至关重要，然而我们发现现有做法导致了严重的错配。我们认为一些企业家过度乐观，通过承担过多风险浪费了较大贷款带来的机会，并展示了基于企业家类型而非企业特征的分配方案的前景。

Dynamic Strategic Corporate Finance: A Tug of War with Financial Frictions

动态战略公司金融：带有金融摩擦的拉锯战

Ulrich Doraszelski, João F. Gomes, Felix Nockher

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Access to capital markets plays a key role for the evolution of an industry over time. We develop a benchmark theoretical setting that integrates core corporate finance insights about the impact of financial frictions on investment with those from industrial organization on dynamic competition and industry evolution under perfect capital markets. Using state of the art computational tools to systematically detect and evaluate multiple equilibria and to thoroughly explore the parameter space of our model, we show how, depending on key industry characteristics such as market size, financial frictions impact firms' pricing and investment strategies and the degree of industry concentration.

中文摘要

进入资本市场的能力对行业随时间的演变起着关键作用。我们建立了一个基准理论框架，将关于金融摩擦对投资影响的核心公司金融见解与产业组织关于完美资本市场下动态竞争和行业演变的见解整合起来。利用最先进的计算工具系统地检测和评估多重均衡，并彻底探索我们模型的参数空间，我们展示了根据关键行业特征（如市场规模），金融摩擦如何影响企业的定价和投资策略以及行业集中度。

Why Don’t Elite Colleges Expand Supply?

精英大学为何不扩大招生？

Peter Q. Blair, Kent Smetters

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

While college enrollment has more-than doubled since 1970, elite colleges have barely increased supply, instead reducing admit rates. We show that straightforward reasons cannot explain this behavior. We propose a model where colleges compete on prestige, measured using relative selectivity or relative admit rates. A key comparative static of the model is that higher demand decreases [increases] the admit rate when the weight on prestige is above [below] a critical value, consistent with experience in elite [non-elite] colleges. A calibrated version of the model closely replicates the pattern in the data of declining admit rates at elite colleges while counter-factual simulations without prestige fail. Prestige competition is inefficient. Allowing elite colleges to collude on admissions strategy internalizes the non-pecuniary prestige externality and is Pareto improving.

中文摘要

尽管自1970年以来大学入学人数增加了一倍多，但精英大学几乎没有增加招生名额，反而降低了录取率。我们表明，简单的理由无法解释这种行为。我们提出了一个模型，其中大学在声望上竞争，声望通过相对选择性或相对录取率来衡量。该模型的一个关键比较静态是，当声望权重高于[低于]临界值时，更高的需求会降低[提高]录取率，这与精英[非精英]大学的经验一致。该模型的校准版本密切复制了数据中精英大学录取率下降的模式，而没有声望的反事实模拟则无法做到这一点。声望竞争是低效的。允许精英大学在招生策略上合谋，可以内部化非金钱的声望外部性，并且是帕累托改进的。

Why Does Education Increase Voting? Evidence from Boston’s Charter Schools

教育为何能提高投票率？来自波士顿特许学校的证据

Sarah Cohodes, James J. Feigenbaum

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Americans with more education vote more, but we know little about whether this effect on civic participation arises from educational quality or quantity. Using admissions lotteries at Boston charter schools, we estimate the impacts of charter attendance on academic and voting outcomes. We first confirm that there are large academic gains from charter school attendance. Second, we find that charter attendance boosts voter participation, substantially increasing voter participation in the first presidential election after a student turns 18 by six percentage points from a baseline of 35 percent. This effect operates through increased turnout, as there is no increase in voter registration. Rich data enable us to explore multiple potential channels of this voting impact, and our evidence suggests that charters increase voting by increasing noncognitive skills.

中文摘要

受教育程度更高的美国人投票更多，但我们对于这种对公民参与的影响是源于教育质量还是教育数量知之甚少。利用波士顿特许学校的入学抽签，我们估算了特许学校出勤对学业和投票结果的影响。我们首先确认，特许学校出勤带来了巨大的学业收益。其次，我们发现特许学校出勤提高了选民参与率，使学生年满18岁后的第一次总统选举的选民参与率从35%的基线水平大幅提高了6个百分点。这种效应是通过提高投票率实现的，因为选民登记没有增加。丰富的数据使我们能够探索这种投票影响的多个潜在渠道，我们的证据表明，特许学校通过提高非认知技能来增加投票率。

Recovery from the COVID-19 Recession: Uneven Effects among Young Workers?

新冠疫情衰退后的复苏：年轻工人中的不均衡影响？

Pinka Chatterji, Yue Li

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In this paper, we examine the labor market recovery from the COVID-19 recession and test for effects of termination of pandemic unemployment insurance programs among 15-24-year-olds. We use data from the January 2016-October 2022 Current Population Survey. Using regression-based methods, we show that while 15-19-year-olds experienced a brisk, full recovery in labor market outcomes from the COVID-19 recession, the recovery was sluggish and incomplete among 20-24-year-olds, with some work outcomes lagging below pre-pandemic norms well into 2022. Banking-Crisis Interventions Across Time and Space

Andrew Metrick, Paul Schmelzing

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We present a new database of banking-crisis interventions from the Roman Empire to the present, covering 1,946 interventions in 20 categories across 143 countries. We demonstrate that crisis-intervention patterns are significantly related to income and fiscal variables and to measures of the political system and currency regime. GDP losses following crises are economically significant and are larger for wealthier countries, with some evidence that these losses are mitigated by democratic political systems and liberal currency regimes. Finally, intervention frequencies reached an apex during the post-Bretton Woods era, continuing a secular increase since at least the late 17th century.

Political Ideology and International Capital Allocation

Elisabeth Kempf, Mancy Luo, Larissa Schäfer, Margarita Tsoutsoura

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

Does investors' political ideology shape international capital allocation? We provide evidence from two settings–syndicated corporate loans and equity mutual funds–to show ideological alignment with foreign governments affects the cross-border capital allocation by U.S. institutional investors. Ideological alignment on both economic and social issues plays a role. Our empirical strategy ensures direct economic effects of foreign elections or government ties between countries are not driving the result. Ideological distance between countries also explains variation in bilateral investment. Combined, our findings imply ideological alignment is an important, omitted factor in models of international capital allocation.

Human Frictions in the Transmission of Economic Policies

经济政策传导中的人类摩擦

Francesco D’Acunto, Daniel Hoang, Maritta Paloviita, Michael Weber

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Many consumers below the top of the distribution of a representative population by cognitive abilities barely react to monetary and fiscal policies that aim to stimulate consumption and borrowing, even when they are financially unconstrained and despite substantial debt capacity. Differences in income, formal education levels, economic expectations, and a large set of registry-based demographics do not explain these facts. Heterogeneous cognitive abilities thus act as human frictions in the transmission of economic policies that operate through the household sector and might imply redistribution from low- to high-cognitive-ability agents. We conclude by discussing how our findings inform the microfoundation of behavioral macroeconomic theory.
