The Affordable Care Act After a Decade: Industrial Organization of the Insurance Exchanges

《平价医疗法案》十年后：保险交易所的产业组织分析

Benjamin R. Handel, Jonathan T. Kolstad

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The regulated insurance exchanges set up in the Affordable Care Act (ACA) were designed to deliver affordable, efficient health coverage through private insurers. It is crucial to study the complex industrial organization (IO) of these exchanges in order to assess their impacts to date, during the first decade of the ACA, and in order to project their impacts going forward. We revisit the inherent market failures in health care markets that necessitate key ACA exchange regulations and investigate whether they have succeeded in their goals of expanding coverage, creating robust marketplaces, providing product variety, and generating innovation in health care delivery. We discuss empirical IO research to date and also highlight shortcomings in the existing research that can be addressed moving forward. We conclude with a discussion of IO research-based policy lessons for the ACA exchanges and, more generally, for managed competition of private insurance in health care.

中文摘要

《平价医疗法案》（ACA）设立的受监管保险交易所旨在通过私人保险公司提供可负担、高效的医疗保障。研究这些交易所复杂的产业组织（IO）对于评估其在ACA第一个十年期间至今的影响，以及预测其未来影响至关重要。我们重新审视医疗保健市场中固有的市场失灵问题，这些失灵使得ACA的关键交易所监管成为必要，并调查这些监管是否成功实现了扩大覆盖范围、创建稳健的市场、提供产品多样性以及推动医疗保健服务创新的目标。我们讨论了迄今为止的实证产业组织研究，并指出了现有研究中未来可以解决的不足之处。最后，我们讨论了基于产业组织研究的政策经验教训，这些教训适用于ACA交易所，更广泛地说，也适用于医疗保健领域私人保险的管理式竞争。

Does Money Still Matter? Attainment and Earnings Effects of Post-1990 School Finance Reforms

金钱仍然重要吗？1990年后学校财政改革的成就与收入效应

Jesse Rothstein, Diane Whitmore Schanzenbach

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Card and Krueger (1992a,b) used labor market outcomes to study the productivity of school spending. Following their lead, we examine effects of post-1990 school finance reforms on students’ educational attainment and labor market outcomes. Lafortune et al. (2018) show that these reforms increased school spending and narrowed spending and achievement gaps between high- and low-income districts. Using a state-by-cohort panel design, we find that reforms increased high school completion and college-going, concentrated among Black students and women, and raised annual earnings. They also increased the return to education, particularly for Black students and men and driven by the return to high school.

中文摘要

Card和Krueger（1992a,b）利用劳动力市场结果研究了学校支出的生产力。遵循他们的思路，我们考察了1990年后的学校财政改革对学生教育成就和劳动力市场结果的影响。Lafortune等人（2018）表明，这些改革增加了学校支出，并缩小了高收入与低收入学区之间的支出和成就差距。使用州-队列面板设计，我们发现改革提高了高中毕业率和大学入学率，这一效应集中在黑人学生和女性群体中，并提高了年收入。改革还增加了教育回报率，尤其是对黑人学生和男性而言，且主要由高中教育回报率驱动。

Where the Rubber Meets the Road: Pavement Damage Reduces Traffic Safety and Speed

关键之处：路面损坏降低交通安全与速度

Margaret Bock, Alexander Cardazzi, Brad R. Humphreys

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Road maintenance constitutes a significant component of public transportation spending at all levels of government. Formulation of efficient transportation infrastructure policy requires information about factors affecting road and traffic conditions. We generate the first causal evidence that decreasing pavement quality impacts vehicle crash rates and decreases average speed. Results from Instrumental Variable models using spatially and temporally disaggregated data from Federal-Aid Highway System (FAHS) roads in California show statistically and economically significant increases in vehicle crash rates and decreases in average vehicle speed caused by road damage. These impacts imply significant increases in social costs attributable to road damage.

中文摘要

道路维护是各级政府公共交通支出的重要组成部分。制定高效的交通基础设施政策需要了解影响道路和交通状况的因素。我们首次提供了因果证据，表明路面质量下降会影响车辆碰撞率并降低平均速度。使用来自加利福尼亚州联邦援助公路系统（FAHS）道路的空间和时间分解数据，通过工具变量模型得出的结果显示，道路损坏导致车辆碰撞率在统计和经济意义上显著增加，平均车速显著下降。这些影响意味着道路损坏导致了显著的社会成本增加。

No. 4论文详情NBER w29175

Collusion, Mergers, and Related Antitrust Issues

合谋、并购及相关反垄断问题

John Asker, Volker Nocke

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This survey examines recent developments in economic research relating to antitrust, paying specific attention to research in the areas of collusion and merger enforcement. Research relating to both collusion and mergers has made significant advances in the last twenty years. With respect to collusion, this includes important theoretical and empirical work on the sustainability, structure, and impact of collusive schemes. With respect to mergers, this includes important work on the impact of enforcement institutions, both theoretical and empirical work on unilateral effects, and theoretical work on the selection of which mergers get proposed to antitrust agencies and optimal policy in the face of that selection. A feature of recent research is the increasing complementarity between empirical work (ranging from observational studies to model-based measurement) and theoretical work in advancing our understanding of collusive and merger-related phenomena.

中文摘要

本综述考察了反垄断领域经济研究的最新进展，特别关注合谋与并购执法方面的研究。过去二十年中，关于合谋和并购的研究都取得了显著进展。在合谋方面，这包括关于合谋方案可持续性、结构和影响的重要理论和实证工作。在并购方面，这包括关于执法机构影响的重要工作，关于单边效应的理论和实证工作，以及关于哪些并购会被提交给反垄断机构的选择问题以及面对这种选择时的最优政策的理论工作。近期研究的一个特点是，实证工作（从观察性研究到基于模型的测量）与理论工作之间的互补性日益增强，这增进了我们对合谋和并购相关现象的理解。

Specialized Investments and Firms’ Boundaries: Evidence from Textual Analysis of Patents

专用性投资与企业边界：来自专利文本分析的证据

Jan Bena, Isil Erel, Daisy Wang, Michael S. Weisbach

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Inducing firms to make specialized investments through bilateral contracts can be challenging because of potential hold- up problems. Such contracting difficulties have long been argued to be an important reason for acquisitions. To evaluate the extent to which this motivation leads to mergers, we perform a textual analysis of the patents filed by the same lead inventors of the target firms before and after the mergers. We find that patents of inventors from target firms become 28.9% to 46.8% more specific to those of acquirers’ inventors following completed mergers, benchmarked against patents filed by targets and a group of counterfactual acquirers. This pattern is stronger for vertical mergers that are likely to require specialized investments. There is no change in the specificity of patents for mergers that are announced but not consummated. Overall, we provide empirical evidence that contracting issues in motivating specialized investment can be a motive for acquisitions.

中文摘要

通过双边合同促使企业进行专用性投资可能因潜在的敲竹杠问题而具有挑战性。长期以来，这种合同困难被认为是收购的重要原因。为了评估这一动机在多大程度上导致并购，我们对目标公司在并购前后由同一主要发明人提交的专利进行了文本分析。我们发现，在并购完成后，目标公司发明人的专利相对于收购方发明人的专利，其专用性提高了28.9%至46.8%，这一结果是以目标公司提交的专利与一组反事实收购方为基准进行比较得出的。这种模式在可能需要专用性投资的纵向并购中更为显著。对于已宣布但未完成的并购，专利的专用性没有变化。总体而言，我们提供了实证证据，表明在激励专用性投资方面的合同问题可能是收购的一个动机。

Innovation: Market Failures and Public Policies

创新：市场失灵与公共政策

Kevin A. Bryan, Heidi L. Williams

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This is an invited chapter for the forthcoming Volume 4 of the Handbook of Industrial Organization. We summarize the state of the literature on the economics of innovation and highlight open policy questions. We first articulate the key market failures in markets for innovation, and then discuss how both scientific norms and market-oriented policies help overcome those market failures. We close by discussing recent work on the diffusion of inventions as well as on the links between innovation and inequality.

中文摘要

这是为即将出版的《产业组织手册》第4卷撰写的一章。我们总结了创新经济学文献的现状，并强调了尚未解决的政策问题。我们首先阐述了创新市场中关键的市场失灵，然后讨论了科学规范和市场导向政策如何帮助克服这些市场失灵。最后，我们讨论了关于发明扩散以及创新与不平等之间联系的近期研究。

The Economics of Walking About and Predicting Unemployment

“行走经济学”与失业预测

David G. Blanchflower, Alex Bryson

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Unemployment is notoriously difficult to predict. In previous studies, once country fixed effects are added to panel estimates, few variables predict changes in unemployment rates. Using panel data for 29 European countries over 439 months between 1985 and 2021 in an unbalanced country*month panel of just over 10000 observations, we predict changes in the unemployment rate 12 months in advance based on individuals’ fears of unemployment, their perceptions of the economic situation and their own household financial situation. Fear of unemployment predicts subsequent changes in unemployment 12 months later in the presence of country fixed effects and lagged unemployment. Individuals’ perceptions of the economic situation in the country and their own household finances also predict unemployment 12 months later. Business sentiment (industry fear of unemployment) is also predictive of unemployment 12 months later. The findings underscore the importance of the “economics of walking about”. The implication is that these social survey data are informative in predicting economic downturns and should be used more extensively in forecasting. We also generate a 29 country-level annual panel on life satisfaction from 1985-2020 from the World Database of Happiness and show that the consumer level fear of unemployment variable lowers well-being over and above the negative impact of the unemployment rate itself. Qualitative survey metrics were able to predict the Great Recession and the economic slowdown in Europe just prior to the COVID19 pandemic.

中文摘要

失业率历来难以预测。在以往的研究中，一旦在面板估计中加入国家固定效应，很少有变量能够预测失业率的变化。我们使用1985年至2021年间29个欧洲国家439个月的面板数据，构建了一个包含略超过10000个观测值的非平衡国家*月面板，基于个人对失业的恐惧、对经济形势的看法以及自身家庭财务状况，预测12个月后的失业率变化。在控制国家固定效应和滞后失业率的情况下，对失业的恐惧能够预测12个月后的失业率变化。个人对国家经济形势和自身家庭财务状况的看法也能预测12个月后的失业率。商业情绪（行业对失业的恐惧）同样能预测12个月后的失业率。这些发现强调了“行走经济学”的重要性。其含义是，这些社会调查数据在预测经济衰退方面具有信息价值，应在预测中更广泛地使用。我们还从世界幸福数据库生成了1985-2020年29个国家的年度生活满意度面板数据，并表明消费者层面的失业恐惧变量会降低幸福感，其影响超过了失业率本身的负面效应。定性调查指标能够预测大衰退以及COVID-19大流行前夕欧洲的经济放缓。

Central Bank Digital Currency in Historical Perspective: Another Crossroad in Monetary History

历史视角下的中央银行数字货币：货币史上的又一个十字路口

Michael D. Bordo

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 巴哈马

Abstract

Digitalization of Money is a crossroad in monetary history. Advances in technology has led to the development of new forms of money: virtual (crypto) currencies like bitcoin; stable coins like libra/diem; and central bank digital currencies (CBDC) like the Bahamian sand dollar. These innovations in money and finance have resonance to earlier shifts in monetary history: 1) The shift in the eighteenth and nineteenth century from commodity money (gold and silver coins) to convertible fiduciary money and inconvertible fiat money; 2) the shift in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries from central bank notes to a central bank monopoly; 3) Then evolution since the seventeenth century of central banks and the tools of monetary policy. \n\nThis paper analyzes the arguments for a CBDC through the lens of monetary history. The bottom line is that the history of transformations in monetary systems suggests that technical change in money is inevitably driven by the financial incentives of a market economy. Government has always had a key role in the provision of outside money, which is a public good. Government has also regulated inside money provided by the private sector. This held for fiduciary money and will likely hold for digital money. CBDC could make monetary policy more efficient, and it could transform the international monetary and payments systems.

中文摘要

货币数字化是货币史上的一个十字路口。技术进步导致了新货币形式的发展：虚拟（加密）货币如比特币；稳定币如Libra/Diem；以及中央银行数字货币（CBDC）如巴哈马沙元。这些货币和金融领域的创新与货币史上早期的转变有共鸣之处：1）18和19世纪从商品货币（金币和银币）向可兑换信用货币和不可兑换法定货币的转变；2）19和20世纪从中央银行纸币向中央银行垄断的转变；3）自17世纪以来中央银行及货币政策工具的演变。本文通过货币史的视角分析支持CBDC的论点。底线是，货币体系转型的历史表明，货币的技术变革不可避免地由市场经济的金融激励驱动。政府在提供外部货币（一种公共品）方面始终扮演着关键角色。政府也对私人部门提供的内部货币进行监管。这适用于信用货币，也可能适用于数字货币。CBDC可以使货币政策更有效率，并可能改变国际货币和支付体系。

Visualization, Identification, and Estimation in the Linear Panel Event-Study Design

线性面板事件研究设计中的可视化、识别与估计

Simon Freyaldenhoven, Christian Hansen, Jorge Pérez Pérez, Jesse M. Shapiro

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Linear panel models, and the “event-study plots” that often accompany them, are popular tools for learning about policy effects. We discuss the construction of event-study plots and suggest ways to make them more informative. We examine the economic content of different possible identifying assumptions. We explore the performance of the corresponding estimators in simulations, highlighting that a given estimator can perform well or poorly depending on the economic environment. An accompanying Stata package, xtevent, facilitates adoption of our suggestions.

中文摘要

线性面板模型及其常伴随的“事件研究图”是了解政策效应的流行工具。我们讨论了事件研究图的构建，并提出了使其更具信息量的方法。我们审视了不同可能识别假设的经济内涵。我们通过模拟探索了相应估计量的表现，强调特定估计量的表现好坏取决于经济环境。配套的Stata软件包xtevent有助于采纳我们的建议。

No. 10论文详情NBER w29169

Competition and Selection in Credit Markets

信贷市场中的竞争与筛选

Constantine Yannelis, Anthony Lee Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Screening in consumer credit markets is often associated with large fixed costs. We present both theory and evidence that, when lenders use fixed-cost technologies to screen borrowers, increased competition may increase rather than decrease interest rates in subprime consumer credit markets. In more competitive markets, lenders have lower market shares, and thus lower incentives to invest in screening. Thus, when markets are competitive, all lenders face a riskier pool of borrowers, which can lead interest rates to be higher. We provide evidence for the model’s predictions in the auto loan market using administrative credit panel data.

中文摘要

消费者信贷市场中的筛选通常与高固定成本相关。我们提出理论和证据表明，当贷款人使用固定成本技术筛选借款人时，竞争加剧可能提高而非降低次级消费者信贷市场的利率。在竞争更激烈的市场中，贷款人市场份额较低，因此投资于筛选的激励也较低。因此，当市场竞争激烈时，所有贷款人都面临风险更高的借款人池，这可能导致利率上升。我们利用行政信贷面板数据为汽车贷款市场中模型的预测提供了证据。

No. 11论文详情NBER w29168

Misperceptions about Others

对他人的误解

Leonardo Bursztyn, David Y. Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

People’s perceptions about others play an important role in shaping their own attitudes and behaviors, as well as social norms more broadly. This review presents a meta-analysis of the recent empirical literature that examines perceptions about others in the field. We document a number of stylized facts. Misperceptions about others are widespread, asymmetric, much larger when about out-group members, and positively associated with one’s own attitudes. Experimental treatments to re-calibrate misperceptions generally work as intended; they sometimes lead to meaningful changes in behaviors, though this often occurs only immediately after the treatments. We discuss different conceptual frameworks that could explain the origin, persistence, and rigidity of misperceptions about others. We point to several directions for future research.

中文摘要

人们对他人的看法在塑造自身态度和行为以及更广泛的社会规范方面发挥着重要作用。本综述对近期关于实地环境中他人看法的实证文献进行了元分析。我们记录了一些典型事实：对他人的误解普遍存在、具有不对称性、在涉及外群体成员时更为严重，并且与自身态度正相关。旨在纠正误解的实验性处理通常按预期发挥作用；它们有时会导致行为的有意义变化，尽管这通常仅发生在处理之后。我们讨论了可能解释他人误解的起源、持续性和僵化性的不同概念框架。我们指出了未来研究的几个方向。

No. 12论文详情NBER w29167

(Successful) Democracies Breed Their Own Support

（成功的）民主制度孕育自身支持

Daron Acemoglu, Nicolás Ajzenman, Cevat Giray Aksoy, Martin Fiszbein, Carlos A. Molina

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Using large-scale survey data covering more than 110 countries and exploiting within-country variation across cohorts and surveys, we show that individuals with longer exposure to democracy display stronger support for democratic institutions. We bolster these baseline findings using an instrumental-variables strategy exploiting regional democratization waves and focusing on immigrants’ exposure to democracy before migration. In all cases, the timing and nature of the effects are consistent with a causal interpretation. We also establish that democracies breed their own support only when they are successful: all of the effects we estimate work through exposure to democracies that are successful in providing economic growth, peace and political stability, and public goods.

中文摘要

The Elusive Explanation for the Declining Labor Share

劳动份额下降的难以捉摸的解释

Gene M. Grossman, Ezra Oberfield

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

A vast literature seeks to measure and explain the apparent decline in the labor share in national income that has occurred in recent times in the United States and elsewhere. The culprits include technological change, increased globalization and the rise of China, the enhanced exercise of market power by large firms in concentrated product markets, the decline in unionization rates and the erosion in the bargaining power of workers in labor markets, and the changing composition of the workforce due to a slowdown in population growth and a rise in educational attainment. We review this literature, with special emphasis on the pitfalls associated with using cross-sectional data to assess this phenomenon and the reasons why the body of papers collectively explains the phenomenon many times over.

中文摘要

Exchange Rate Volatility and Global Food Supply Chains

汇率波动与全球食品供应链

Sandro Steinbach

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper analyzes the impact of exchange rate risk on global food supply chains. Although the theoretical literature suggests ambivalence regarding the sign and magnitude of this effect, most empirical studies indicate a negative association between exchange rate volatility and international trade flows. I contribute to the ongoing debate by investigating the relationship at the product-level using a sectoral gravity model and relying on detailed retrospective trade and exchange rate data for a balanced panel of 159 countries for 2001 to 2017. I study the relationship for 781 agricultural and food products and estimate the trade effects of short-run and long-run exchange rate volatility. My findings indicate significant heterogeneity in the trade effects of exchange rate risk. While the mean trade effects are positive for short-run and long-run volatility, these effects vary substantially according to product and industry characteristics. I find a positive association between exchange rate volatility and the trade effects for upstreamness and a negative association for downstreamness of the traded products. I show that the significant and adverse trade effects in earlier studies result from model misspecification and measurement errors. This research enhances the understanding of the implications of exchange rate volatility which is a primary source of international risk exposure for global food supply chains.

中文摘要

本文分析了汇率风险对全球食品供应链的影响。尽管理论文献表明对此效应的符号和大小存在矛盾，但大多数实证研究表明汇率波动性与国际贸易流量之间存在负相关关系。我通过使用部门引力模型在产品层面研究这种关系，并依赖2001年至2017年159个国家的平衡面板的详细回溯贸易和汇率数据，为正在进行的辩论做出贡献。我研究了781种农业和食品产品的关系，并估计了短期和长期汇率波动的贸易效应。我的发现表明汇率风险的贸易效应存在显著的异质性。虽然短期和长期波动的平均贸易效应为正，但这些效应根据产品和行业特征而有很大差异。我发现汇率波动性与上游度产品的贸易效应之间存在正相关关系，与下游度产品的贸易效应之间存在负相关关系。我表明，早期研究中显著的不利贸易效应源于模型设定错误和测量误差。本研究增强了对汇率波动影响的理解，汇率波动是全球食品供应链国际风险暴露的主要来源。

Bureaucracy and Development

官僚机构与发展

Timothy J. Besley, Robin Burgess, Adnan Khan, Guo Xu

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In recent years, there has been increasing interest in whether and how bureaucratic effectiveness contributes to development. Just what makes for an effective bureaucracy and what are the building blocks of state capacity remain subject to debate. This paper reviews the arguments connecting contemporary research using administrative data and field experiments to wider discussions of the origins of state capacity. Most current research has been focused on understanding specific features of the environment in which bureaucrats operate. We connect this to discussions of bureaucratic systems, specifically the relationship to politics, citizens, firms and NGOs.

Ageing and Welfare-State Policy Making: Macroeconomic Perspective

老龄化与福利国家政策制定：宏观经济学视角

Assaf Razin, Alexander Horst Schwemmer

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

It has been well recognized that population ageing could generate structural changes centered around the dwindling labor force, on one hand, and the expanding dependency on the generosity of the welfare state, on the other hand. Ageing-related welfare state policy entails both fiscal issues and migration issues. The paper employs a general-equilibrium model with a policy-making focus, to help understand the mechanism governing the provision of social benefits, labor income taxation, capital income taxation, migration curbs on low skilled and high skilled, driven by the ageing of the population. Greater generosity of the welfare state comes together with policy of a more liberal migration policy — incentive compatible with the interests of the majority voters.

中文摘要

人们普遍认识到，人口老龄化可能引发结构性变化，一方面表现为劳动力萎缩，另一方面则是对福利国家慷慨程度的依赖日益扩大。与老龄化相关的福利国家政策既涉及财政问题，也涉及移民问题。本文采用一个以政策制定为重点的一般均衡模型，旨在帮助理解由人口老龄化驱动的社会福利供给、劳动收入税、资本收入税、对低技能和高技能移民的限制等政策的运行机制。福利国家慷慨程度的提高伴随着更自由的移民政策——这与多数选民的利益激励相容。
