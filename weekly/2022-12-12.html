Quantifying the Costs and Benefits of Quantitative Easing

量化宽松的成本与收益量化

Andrew T. Levin, Brian L. Lu, William R. Nelson

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

We conduct a systematic analysis of the costs and benefits of large-scale securities purchases, using the Federal Reserve’s QE4 program as a concrete example. This program was initiated at the onset of the pandemic in March 2020 and continued for two years, leading to a doubling of the Fed’s securities holdings to about $8.5 trillion as of March 2022. QE4 was initially aimed at mitigating strains in markets for Treasuries and agency mortgage-backed securities but was subsequently aimed more broadly at supporting market functioning and providing monetary stimulus. Nonetheless, QE4 did not have any notable benefits in reducing term premiums. Moreover, since the securities purchases were financed by expanding the Fed’s short-term liabilities, QE4 amplified the interest rate risk associated with the publicly-held debt of the consolidated federal government. Our simulation analysis indicates that QE4 is likely to reduce the Federal Reserve’s remittances to the U.S. Treasury by about $760 billion over the next ten years.

中文摘要

我们以美联储的 QE4 计划为具体案例，对大规模证券购买的成本和收益进行了系统分析。该计划于 2020 年 3 月疫情爆发时启动，并持续了两年，导致美联储的证券持有量翻倍，截至 2022 年 3 月达到约 8.5 万亿美元。QE4 最初旨在缓解国债和机构抵押贷款支持证券市场的压力，但随后其目标更广泛地转向支持市场运作和提供货币刺激。尽管如此，QE4 在降低期限溢价方面并未带来显著收益。此外，由于证券购买是通过扩大美联储的短期负债来融资的，QE4 放大了与合并联邦政府公众持有债务相关的利率风险。我们的模拟分析表明，QE4 在未来十年内可能会使美联储向美国财政部的汇款减少约 7600 亿美元。

The Disappearing Index Effect

消失的指数效应

Robin Greenwood, Marco C. Sammon

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The abnormal return associated with a stock being added to the S&P 500 has fallen from an average of 3.4% in the 1980s and 7.6% in the 1990s to 0.8% over the past decade. This has occurred despite a significant increase in the percentage of stock market assets linked to the index. A similar pattern has occurred for index deletions, with large negative abnormal returns on average during the 1980s and 1990s, but only -0.6% between 2010 and 2020. We investigate potential drivers of this surprising phenomenon and discuss the implications for market efficiency.

中文摘要

股票被纳入标普500指数所带来的异常回报率已从1980年代的平均3.4%和1990年代的7.6%下降至过去十年的0.8%。尽管与该指数挂钩的股票市场资产比例显著增加，这种情况仍然发生。指数剔除也出现了类似模式，在1980年代和1990年代平均出现较大的负异常回报，但在2010年至2020年间仅为-0.6%。我们调查了这一令人惊讶现象的可能驱动因素，并讨论了其对市场效率的影响。

Children and Grandchildren of Union Army Veterans: New Data Collections to Study the Persistence of Longevity and Socioeconomic Status Across Generations

联邦军退伍军人的子女和孙辈：用于研究长寿和社会经济地位跨代持续性的新数据收集

Dora Costa, CoraLee Lewis, Noelle Yetter

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper introduces four new intergenerational and multigenerational datasets which follow both sons and daughters and which can be used to study the persistence of longevity, socioeconomic status, family structure, and geographic mobility across generations. The data follow the children of Black and White Union Army veterans from birth to death, linking them to the available censuses. The White samples include an over-sample of children of ex-POWs. A separate collection links grandchildren of White Union Army veterans to their death records. The data were created with high quality manual linkage procedures utilizing a wide variety of records to establish links.

中文摘要

本文介绍了四个新的跨代和多代数据集，这些数据集追踪了儿子和女儿，可用于研究长寿、社会经济地位、家庭结构和地理流动性在代际间的持续性。这些数据追踪了黑人和白人联邦军退伍军人的子女从出生到死亡的过程，并将其与可用的人口普查数据相链接。白人样本包括前战俘子女的过度抽样。另一个独立的数据收集将白人联邦军退伍军人的孙辈与其死亡记录相链接。这些数据是通过高质量的手动链接程序创建的，利用了各种记录来建立链接。

How Regressive are Mobility-Related User Fees and Gasoline Taxes?

与出行相关的用户费和汽油税有多累进？

Edward L. Glaeser, Caitlin S. Gorback, James M. Poterba

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Pigouvian taxes and user fees can address environmental externalities and efficiently fund transportation infrastructure, but these policies may place burdens on poorer households. This paper presents new evidence on the distributional consequences of the gasoline tax, bus and light rail charges and a vehicle miles traveled (VMT) tax. Gas taxes have become more regressive over time, partially because of environmentally-oriented technological change, although the share of expenditures on gas taxes declines with expenditures much less than the share of income spent on gas taxes declines with income. Replacing the gasoline tax with a household-level VMT tax would increase the average tax burden on households in the top income and expenditure deciles, because of their greater use of hybrid-electric and battery-electric vehicles. This progressive shift would be small given current levels of hybrid and electric vehicle ownership, but will be larger in the future if such vehicles continue to be more common among higher than lower income households. An expanded commercial VMT would place a larger burden, as a share of expenditures, on lower income or expenditure households, because better-off households consume more non-tradable goods that do not require transportation. User charges for airports, subways and commuter rail are progressive, while bus fees loom much larger for lower income households.

中文摘要

庇古税和用户费可以解决环境外部性并为交通基础设施提供有效资金，但这些政策可能会给较贫困家庭带来负担。本文提供了关于汽油税、公交和轻轨收费以及车辆行驶里程税（VMT）分配后果的新证据。随着时间的推移，汽油税变得更加累退，部分原因是环境导向的技术变革，尽管汽油税支出占总支出的份额随支出下降的幅度远小于汽油税支出占收入的份额随收入下降的幅度。将汽油税替换为家庭层面的VMT税将增加收入最高和支出最高十分位家庭的平均税收负担，因为他们更多地使用混合动力电动汽车和纯电动汽车。鉴于当前混合动力和电动汽车的拥有水平，这种累进性转变幅度较小，但如果这类车辆在未来继续在高收入家庭中比在低收入家庭中更普遍，这种转变将会更大。扩大的商业VMT税将给较低收入或支出家庭带来更大的负担（按支出份额计算），因为较富裕家庭消费更多不需要运输的非贸易商品。机场、地铁和通勤铁路的用户费是累进的，而公交费用对低收入家庭来说则显得更为沉重。

The Cost of Imbalance in Clinical Trials

临床试验中不平衡的成本

Sylvain Chassang, Rong Feng

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Clinical trials following the “gold standard” of random assignment frequently use independent lotteries to allocate patients to treatment and control arms. However, independent assignment can generate treatment and control arms that are unbalanced (i.e. treatment and control populations with significantly different demographics), which reduces power. Other assignment methods such as matched pair designs ensure balance across arms while maintaining randomization and permitting inference. \nThis paper seeks to measure the cost of imbalance with respect to gender in a sample of roughly 2000 clinical studies. We document significant imbalance: 25% of experiments have at least 26% more men in one treatment arm than in the other. In addition, clinical trials with greater imbalance have more dispersed treatment effects, indicating that imbalance reduces the informativeness of experiments. A simple structural model suggests that for a typical experiment, using a balanced random design could deliver informativeness gains equivalent to increasing the sample size by 18%.

中文摘要

遵循随机分配“金标准”的临床试验通常使用独立抽签将患者分配到治疗组和对照组。然而，独立分配可能导致治疗组和对照组不平衡（即治疗组和对照组人群的人口统计学特征显著不同），这会降低统计功效。其他分配方法，如配对设计，可以在保持随机化并允许推断的同时确保各组间的平衡。本文旨在衡量约2000项临床研究样本中性别不平衡的成本。我们记录了显著的不平衡：25%的实验中，一个治疗组的男性比例比另一个治疗组至少高出26%。此外，不平衡程度更大的临床试验具有更分散的治疗效果，表明不平衡降低了实验的信息量。一个简单的结构模型表明，对于一个典型的实验，使用平衡的随机设计可以带来相当于将样本量增加18%的信息量增益。

Industrial Clusters in the Long Run: Evidence from Million-Rouble Plants in China

Stephan Heblich, Marlon Seror, Hao Xu, Yanos Zylberberg

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题

Abstract

We study the impact of large, successful manufacturing plants on other local producers in China, focusing on “Million-Rouble Plants” built in the 1950s during a brief alliance with the U.S.S.R. The ephemeral geopolitical situation and the locations of allied and enemy airbases provide exogenous variation in plant siting. We find a boom-and-bust pattern: Counties hosting these plants were 80% more productive than control counties in 1982 but 20% less productive by 2010. This decline reflects the performance of local establishments, which exhibit low productivity, limited innovation, and high markup. Specialization hindered spillovers, preventing the emergence of new clusters and local entrepreneurship.

The Effect of Financial Resources on Homeownership, Marriage, and Fertility: Evidence from State Lotteries

金融资源对住房拥有率、婚姻和生育的影响：来自州彩票的证据

George Bulman, Sarena Goodman, Adam Isen

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

This paper leverages the universe of U.S. tax data and state lottery wins between 2000 and 2019 to estimate the causal effect of financial resources on three key lifecycle outcomes for young adults. We find large and persistent effects on homeownership, with a response function that exhibits substantial concavity but also an extremely high upper bound, and larger responses among higher-income individuals. Resources generate persistent increases in marriage for single men and women but do not increase the likelihood existing marriages are preserved. Fertility is modestly accelerated by a lottery win, but there is little effect on total fertility. Our results support a causal pathway behind differences in homeownership and marriage by socioeconomic status and inform theories of household formation and the family.

中文摘要

本文利用2000年至2019年间美国税务数据和州彩票中奖的完整样本，估计了金融资源对年轻人三个关键生命周期结果的因果效应。我们发现，金融资源对住房拥有率产生了巨大且持续的影响，其响应函数表现出显著的凹性，但也存在极高的上限，且高收入个体的响应更大。金融资源能持续提高单身男性和女性的结婚率，但不会增加现有婚姻存续的可能性。彩票中奖会适度加速生育，但对总生育率影响甚微。我们的结果支持了社会经济地位差异导致住房拥有率和婚姻差异的因果路径，并为家庭形成和家庭理论提供了依据。

Immigration and Work Schedules: Theory and Evidence

移民与工作时间安排：理论与证据

Timothy N. Bond, Osea Giuntella, Jakub Lonsky

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop a theoretical framework to analyze the effects of immigration on native job amenities, focusing on work schedules. Immigrants have a comparative advantage in production at, and lower disamenity cost for nighttime work, which leads them to disproportionately choose nighttime employment. Because day and night tasks are imperfect substitutes, the relative price of day tasks increases as their supply becomes relatively more scarce. We provide empirical support for our theory. Native workers in local labor markets that experienced higher rates of immigration are more likely to work day shifts and receive a lower compensating differential for nighttime work.

中文摘要

我们构建了一个理论框架来分析移民对本地居民工作福利的影响，重点关注工作时间安排。移民在夜间工作的生产上具有比较优势，且对夜间工作的不适成本较低，这导致他们不成比例地选择夜间工作。由于白天和夜间任务是不完全替代的，随着白天任务的供给相对变得更加稀缺，其相对价格上升。我们为理论提供了实证支持。在移民率较高的本地劳动力市场中，本地工人更有可能上白班，并因夜间工作而获得较低的补偿性差异。

The Employment Effects of Mobile Internet in Developing Countries

发展中国家移动互联网的就业效应

Gaurav Chiplunkar, Pinelopi Koujianou Goldberg

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We examine the employment effects of 3G mobile internet expansion in developing countries. We find that 3G significantly increases the labor force participation rate of women and the employment rates of both men and women. Our results suggest that 3G affects the type of jobs and there is a distinct gender dimension to these effects. Men transition away from unpaid agricultural work into operating small agricultural enterprises, while women take more unpaid jobs, especially in agriculture, and operate more small businesses in all sectors. Both men and women are more likely to work in wage jobs in the service sector.

中文摘要

我们研究了3G移动互联网扩展在发展中国家的就业效应。我们发现，3G显著提高了女性的劳动力参与率以及男性和女性的就业率。我们的结果表明，3G影响了工作类型，并且这些效应具有明显的性别维度。男性从无酬农业工作转向经营小型农业企业，而女性则承担更多无酬工作，尤其是在农业领域，并在所有部门经营更多小型企业。男性和女性都更有可能在服务部门从事有薪工作。

When do "Nudges" Increase Welfare?

“助推”何时能提高福利？

Hunt Allcott, Daniel Cohen, William Morrison, Dmitry Taubinsky

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use public finance sufficient statistic approaches to characterize the welfare effects of “nudges,” such as simplified information and warning labels, in markets with taxes and endogenous prices. While many studies focus on average effects, we show that welfare also depends on how the nudge affects the variance of choice distortions, and average effects become irrelevant with zero pass-through or optimal taxes. We implement the framework with experiments evaluating automotive fuel economy labels and sugary drink health labels. Labels decrease purchases of low-fuel economy cars and sugary drinks but may decrease welfare, because they increase the variance of choice distortions.

中文摘要

我们使用公共财政充分统计量方法来描述在存在税收和内生产品价格的市场中，“助推”（例如简化信息和警告标签）的福利效应。虽然许多研究关注平均效应，但我们表明福利还取决于助推如何影响选择扭曲的方差，并且在零传导或最优税收的情况下，平均效应变得无关紧要。我们通过评估汽车燃油经济性标签和含糖饮料健康标签的实验来实施该框架。标签减少了低燃油经济性汽车和含糖饮料的购买，但可能会降低福利，因为它们增加了选择扭曲的方差。

The Impact of Paid Family Leave on Families with Health Shocks

带薪家庭假对遭受健康冲击家庭的影响

Courtney Coile, Maya Rossin-Slater, Amanda Su

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the impact of paid family leave (PFL) policies on individuals’ labor market and mental health-related responses to their spouses’ and children’s health shocks using data from the Medical Expenditure Panel Survey and event-study models. We find that PFL access improves job continuity among wives of individuals who are hospitalized or have surgery. Additionally, while PFL access does not affect the wives’ self-reported mental health, it reduces their use of mental health-related medication. We find no effects of PFL on parents of children who experience health shocks, suggesting that this policy is less fitting for this group of caregivers.

Noise-Robust De-Duplication at Scale

大规模噪声鲁棒的去重方法

Emily Silcock, Luca D'Amico-Wong, Jinglin Yang, Melissa Dell

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Identifying near duplicates within large, noisy text corpora has a myriad of applications that range from de-duplicating training datasets, reducing privacy risk, and evaluating test set leakage, to identifying reproduced news articles and literature within large corpora. Across these diverse applications, the overwhelming majority of work relies on N-grams. Limited efforts have been made to evaluate how well N-gram methods perform, in part because it is unclear how one could create an unbiased evaluation dataset for a massive corpus. This study uses the unique timeliness of historical news wires to create a 27,210 document dataset, with 122,876 positive duplicate pairs, for studying noise-robust de-duplication. The time-sensitivity of news makes comprehensive hand labelling feasible - despite the massive overall size of the corpus - as duplicates occur within a narrow date range. The study then develops and evaluates a range of de-duplication methods: hashing and N-gram overlap (which predominate in the literature), a contrastively trained bi-encoder, and a ``re-rank'' style approach combining a bi- and cross-encoder. The neural approaches significantly outperform hashing and N-gram overlap. We show that the bi-encoder scales well, de-duplicating a 10 million article corpus on a single GPU card in a matter of hours. We also apply our pre-trained model to the RealNews and patent portions of C4 (Colossal Clean Crawled Corpus), illustrating that a neural approach can identify many near duplicates missed by hashing, in the presence of various types of noise. The public release of our NEWS-COPY de-duplication dataset, de-duplicated RealNews and patent corpuses, and the pre-trained models will facilitate further research and applications.

中文摘要

在大型、嘈杂的文本语料库中识别近似重复项具有众多应用，包括去重训练数据集、降低隐私风险、评估测试集泄露，以及在大型语料库中识别重复的新闻文章和文献。在这些不同的应用中，绝大多数工作依赖于N-gram方法。评估N-gram方法表现如何的努力有限，部分原因在于不清楚如何为大规模语料库创建无偏评估数据集。本研究利用历史新闻专线的独特时效性，创建了一个包含27,210篇文档、122,876个正例重复对的数据集，用于研究噪声鲁棒的去重。新闻的时效敏感性使得全面的手工标注成为可能——尽管语料库整体规模巨大——因为重复发生在狭窄的日期范围内。随后，本研究开发并评估了一系列去重方法：哈希和N-gram重叠（文献中占主导地位）、对比训练的双编码器，以及结合双编码器和交叉编码器的“重排序”风格方法。神经方法显著优于哈希和N-gram重叠。我们展示了双编码器具有良好的可扩展性，能够在单个GPU卡上在数小时内对1000万篇文章的语料库进行去重。我们还将预训练模型应用于C4（大规模清洗爬取语料库）的RealNews和专利部分，说明神经方法能够在存在各种类型噪声的情况下，识别出哈希方法遗漏的许多近似重复项。我们公开发布的NEWS-COPY去重数据集、去重后的RealNews和专利语料库以及预训练模型，将促进进一步的研究和应用。

The Importance of Modeling Income Taxes Over time. U.S. Reforms and Outcomes

随时间建模所得税的重要性：美国改革与结果

Margherita Borella, Mariacristina De Nardi, Michael Pak, Nicolo Russo, Fang Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

While “’Tis impossible to be sure of any thing but Death and Taxes” Bullock (1716), the structure of taxes and their burden have undergone large and frequent changes over time. We provide a brief history of U.S. federal income tax reforms since the 1960s, calculate effective federal income tax rates for each wave of the Panel Study of Income Dynamics, and discuss how effective taxation changed from 1969 to 2016. We show that most tax regimes are short-lived and that the variation in taxes over time and across groups is large. We also use an estimated dynamic model of couples and singles to show that the various tax regimes that we estimate imply very different labor market and saving behavior. These findings stress the importance of studying and modeling tax changes over time and across groups.

Wage Garnishment in the United States: New Facts from Administrative Payroll Records

美国的工资扣押：来自行政工资记录的新事实

Anthony A. DeFusco, Brandon M. Enriquez, Margaret B. Yellen

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Wage garnishment allows creditors to deduct money directly from workers’ paychecks to re-pay defaulted debts. We document new facts about wage garnishment between 2014–2019 using data from a large payroll processor who distributes paychecks to approximately 20%of U.S. private-sector workers. As of 2019, over one in every 100 workers was being garnished for delinquent debt. The average garnished worker experiences garnishment for five months, during which approximately 11% of gross earnings is remitted to their creditor(s). The beginning of a new garnishment is associated with an increase in job turnover rates but no intensive margin change in hours worked.

Are Retirement Planning Tools Substitutes or Complements to Financial Capability?

退休规划工具是金融能力的替代品还是补充品？

Gopi Shah Goda, Matthew R. Levy, Colleen Flaherty Manchester, Aaron Sojourner, Joshua Tasoff, Jiusi Xiao

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We conduct a randomized controlled trial to understand how a web-based retirement saving calculator affects workers' retirement-savings decisions. In both conditions, the calculator projects workers' retirement income goal. In the treatment condition, it also projects retirement income based on defined-contribution savings, prominently displays the gap between projected goal and actual retirement income, and allows users to interactively explore how alternative, future contribution choices would affect the gap. The treatment increased average annual retirement contributions by $174 (2.3 percent). However, effects were larger for those with greater financial knowledge, suggesting this type of tool complements, rather than substitutes for, underlying financial capability.

中文摘要
