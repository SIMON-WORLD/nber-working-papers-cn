The Economics of Civil Service Protections

公务员保护的经济学

Guo Xu

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The study of bureaucracy as a key dimension of state capacity has gained increasing attention in economics. What organizational features create an effective bureaucracy, however, remains subject to debate. This paper focuses on the central role of civil service protections in delineating the boundary between politics and administration. I introduce a simple principal-agent framework to discuss the key theoretical channels through which political insulation can shape bureaucrat performance. Guided by the framework, I establish stylized facts and organize evidence from historical and contemporary civil service reforms. Overall, the micro-level evidence documents a positive effect of civil service protections on the quality of public service delivery. I conclude with open questions about the political economy of reform adoption.

中文摘要

官僚机构作为国家能力的一个关键维度，其研究在经济学中日益受到关注。然而，何种组织特征能造就有效的官僚机构，仍存在争议。本文聚焦于公务员保护在界定政治与行政边界中的核心作用。我引入一个简单的委托-代理框架，讨论政治隔离可能塑造官僚绩效的关键理论渠道。在该框架的指导下，我建立了典型化事实，并整理了来自历史和当代公务员改革的相关证据。总体而言，微观层面的证据表明，公务员保护对公共服务供给质量具有正向效应。最后，我就改革采纳的政治经济学提出了开放性问题。

The Value of Behavioral Policies

行为政策的价值

John A. List, Matthias Rodemeier, Sutanuka Roy, Gregory K. Sun

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Behavioral interventions have become central to modern public policy, but their empirical promise remains contested because estimated treatment effects often appear small. We argue that a policy response is economically meaningful only relative to the response generated by alternative policies. We assemble more than 1,200 estimates from over 600 studies comparing “nudges” and traditional price interventions in the markets for cigarettes, alcohol, influenza vaccination, electricity, and residential water. Translating nudge effects into equivalent price changes, we find that behavioral interventions often correspond to enormous fiscal interventions, from an 11% tax on electricity to a 100% subsidy on influenza vaccinations. Nudges are also more cost-effective than price instruments in all markets, but cost-effectiveness does not predict the welfare ranking of policies. Using a behavioral extension of the Marginal Value of Public Funds, we show that nudges have high welfare returns at the margin, while price instruments often generate larger total surplus at scale.

中文摘要

行为干预已成为现代公共政策的核心，但其实证前景仍存争议，因为估计的处理效应往往显得较小。我们认为，一项政策回应只有在相对于替代政策所产生的回应时，才具有经济意义。我们汇集了来自600多项研究的超过1200个估计值，比较了香烟、酒精、流感疫苗接种、电力和居民用水市场中“助推”与传统价格干预的效果。将助推效应转化为等效价格变化，我们发现行为干预往往对应着巨大的财政干预，从对电力征收11%的税到对流感疫苗接种提供100%的补贴。在所有市场中，助推也比价格工具更具成本效益，但成本效益并不能预测政策的福利排序。利用行为公共资金边际价值的扩展，我们表明助推在边际上具有较高的福利回报，而价格工具在规模上通常产生更大的总剩余。

Childhood Cognitive and Behavioral Impairments and Adult Health

童年认知和行为障碍与成年健康

Hope Corman, Kelly Noonan, Nancy Reichman

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

We use U.S. nationally representative longitudinal data to estimate the effects of cognitive and behavioral impairments in childhood on health in adulthood. We focus on work-limiting health conditions and fair or poor self-rated health but also consider adequacy of sleep, body weight, and health behaviors (use of various substances and preventive health care) as potential pathways. In household fixed effects models, we find that both impairments are strongly associated with having a work-limiting health condition in adulthood; specifically, cognitive impairments increase the probability of a work-limiting condition by 70% and behavioral impairments increase the probability by 60%. Individuals with cognitive impairments in childhood are 43% more likely to report being in fair or poor health in adulthood compared to individuals without cognitive impairments. The effects of cognitive impairments on health do not appear to operate through insufficient sleep, obesity, or health behaviors. Individuals with childhood behavioral impairments are no more likely to report being in fair or poor health but are more likely to get insufficient sleep and to be obese, compared to individuals without behavioral impairments, suggesting that insufficient sleep and obesity could potentially lie on the pathway from behavioral impairments in childhood to work-limiting health conditions in adulthood.

中文摘要

我们使用美国全国代表性的纵向数据，估计童年期认知和行为障碍对成年期健康的影响。我们重点关注限制工作的健康状况和自评健康为一般或较差，但也考虑睡眠充足度、体重和健康行为（使用各种物质和预防性医疗保健）作为潜在途径。在家庭固定效应模型中，我们发现这两种障碍都与成年期存在限制工作的健康状况密切相关；具体而言，认知障碍使限制工作状况的概率增加70%，行为障碍使其概率增加60%。与没有认知障碍的个体相比，童年期有认知障碍的个体在成年期报告自评健康为一般或较差的可能性高出43%。认知障碍对健康的影响似乎并非通过睡眠不足、肥胖或健康行为起作用。与没有行为障碍的个体相比，童年期有行为障碍的个体报告自评健康为一般或较差的可能性并不更高，但更可能睡眠不足和肥胖，这表明睡眠不足和肥胖可能处于从童年期行为障碍到成年期限制工作的健康状况的路径上。

Common Ownership and Collusion

共同所有权与合谋

Vincent Abraham, Florian Ederer, Catarina Marvao

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 瑞典

Abstract

We provide the first empirical evidence that common ownership can facilitate explicit collusion. Our unique setting, the population of legal cartels in Sweden, lets us observe cartel formation, duration, and dissolution without the sample-selection bias that plagues studies of detected cartels. Combining hand-collected ownership data with the universe of registered cartels, we compute firm-pair profit weights (κ) that measure how much one firm internalizes a rival's profits. Higher profit weights are associated with a greater likelihood of cartel participation, predict future cartel involvement, and are linked to longer cartel duration. Firms also reduce their profit weights immediately after a cartel dissolves. However, this relationship is concentrated among pairs in which the firm that internalizes the rival firm's profits colludes while the rival does not. Common ownership therefore complements a firm’s own participation in explicit collusion but substitutes for cartel participation by the rival whose profits it internalizes.

中文摘要

我们提供了首个实证证据，表明共同所有权可以促进显性合谋。我们独特的研究背景——瑞典合法卡特尔的全样本——使我们能够观察卡特尔的组建、持续和解散，而不会受到困扰已侦破卡特尔研究的样本选择偏差的影响。结合手工收集的所有权数据和所有注册卡特尔的总体，我们计算了衡量一家企业在多大程度上内化竞争对手利润的企业对利润权重（κ）。更高的利润权重与更大的卡特尔参与可能性相关，能预测未来的卡特尔参与，并与更长的卡特尔持续时间相关。企业也会在卡特尔解散后立即降低其利润权重。然而，这种关系集中在那些内化竞争对手利润的企业参与合谋而竞争对手不参与的企业对中。因此，共同所有权补充了企业自身对显性合谋的参与，但替代了其利润被内化的竞争对手对卡特尔的参与。

Improving Financial Literacy: From Corporate Finance to Personal Finance

提高金融素养：从公司金融到个人金融

Annamaria Lusardi

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

This article provides an overview of the literature on financial literacy. It covers the initial measurement that created the Big Three questions and measures of personal finance knowledge featuring 28 questions. It shows that levels of financial literacy are low and have not been improving over time. This is true not only in the United States but also in countries worldwide. These findings matter because financial literacy is conducive to savvy financial behaviors, from holding precautionary savings to planning for retirement to many other financial decisions. Financial education programs have grown exponentially over time, providing important insights into their cost-effectiveness. Personal finance courses have been added to high school and college curricula, and the evidence indicates that they are useful initiatives to improve financial knowledge and downstream behavior.

中文摘要

本文概述了关于金融素养的文献。它涵盖了最初创建“三大问题”的测量，以及包含28个问题的个人金融知识测量。研究表明，金融素养水平较低，且并未随时间改善。这不仅在美国如此，在世界各国也是如此。这些发现之所以重要，是因为金融素养有助于形成精明的金融行为，从持有预防性储蓄到为退休做规划，再到许多其他金融决策。随着时间的推移，金融教育项目呈指数级增长，为其成本效益提供了重要见解。个人理财课程已被纳入高中和大学课程，证据表明它们是改善金融知识和下游行为的有效举措。

Incomplete Information and Self-Fulfilling Inflation Dynamics—Lucas meets Keynes

不完全信息与自我实现的通胀动态——卢卡斯遇见凯恩斯

Jess Benhabib, Pengfei Wang, Yi Wen

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 冰岛

Abstract

This paper incorporates the Lucas (1973) island model into a standard DSGE framework. It demonstrates that self-fulfilling stochastic inflation equilibria, which are driven by intrinsic uncertainty or pure sentiments, can exist under rational expectations. Furthermore, it shows that these sentiment-driven stochastic equilibria exhibit monetary non-neutrality, even when the fundamental equilibrium is unique and monetarily neutral. As the aggregate price or inflation rate can appear to fluctuate independently of the money supply, our model explains the complex relationship between inflation and the money supply in the real world, where the basic quantity theory of money often seems invalid.

中文摘要

本文将卢卡斯（1973）岛屿模型纳入标准DSGE框架。它证明了在理性预期下，由内在不确定性或纯粹情绪驱动的自我实现随机通胀均衡可能存在。此外，它表明这些情绪驱动的随机均衡表现出货币非中性，即使基本均衡是唯一的且货币中性的。由于总价格或通胀率可能表现为独立于货币供应量而波动，我们的模型解释了现实世界中通胀与货币供应量之间的复杂关系，而基本货币数量论在这种关系中往往显得失效。

Monetary Policy in Mandarin Capitalism

儒家资本主义下的货币政策

Jeffery (Jinfan) Chang, Wei Xiong

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政

Abstract

This paper investigates why China’s recurrent credit expansions have coincided with persistently weak inflation. We argue that this pattern reflects the country’s production-oriented monetary regime. At the aggregate level, faster monetary-financial expansion temporarily raises PPI inflation but depresses it over longer horizons. At the sectoral level, liability growth among listed industrial firms is followed by weaker producer prices, lower profitability, higher leverage, rising inventories, and reduced capacity utilization. We also find asymmetric supply-chain transmission: downstream liability growth raises upstream PPI inflation, while upstream liability growth does not generate a corresponding downstream price response. These findings indicate that credit expansion in China tends to sustain production and balance sheets rather than stimulate final demand. As a result, monetary policy operates less as a conventional tool for demand management and durable reflation, and more as a mechanism for preserving production capacity and supporting growth.

中文摘要

本文研究为何中国反复出现的信贷扩张与持续疲弱的通胀并存。我们认为，这一模式反映了该国以生产为导向的货币体制。在总体层面，更快的货币金融扩张在短期内推高生产者价格指数（PPI）通胀，但在更长期限内则压低通胀。在行业层面，上市工业企业负债增长之后，会出现生产者价格走弱、盈利能力下降、杠杆率上升、库存增加以及产能利用率下降。我们还发现了不对称的供应链传导：下游负债增长推高上游PPI通胀，而上游负债增长并未引发相应的下游价格反应。这些发现表明，中国的信贷扩张倾向于维持生产和资产负债表，而非刺激最终需求。因此，货币政策的作用更多是作为一种维持生产能力和支持增长的机制，而非传统的需求管理和持久再通胀工具。

The Anatomy of Tariff Pass-through into Consumer Prices

关税向消费者价格传导的剖析

Mary Amiti, Sebastian Heise, David Weinstein

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

Tariffs raise the prices of goods made at home, not just the imports they tax—an effect that standard pass-through estimates largely miss. Studying the 2025 U.S. tariffs, we find that about 26 percent of the tariff increase passes through to consumer prices. These estimates are measured relative to less-exposed goods and hold aggregate conditions fixed. The direct effect accounts for 64 percent of this increase, as tariffs raise the consumer prices of foreign varieties of a good. The remaining 36 percent arises indirectly—tariffs raise the cost of imported inputs used by U.S. producers, and domestic producers raise their markups because they face less competition from higher-priced imports. The direct effect passes through quickly, since tariffs raise import prices almost immediately, but the indirect effect takes nine to twelve months to work its way through supply chains. As a result, tariffs have a larger and more drawn-out impact on consumers than the direct effect alone would suggest.

中文摘要

关税不仅会提高其所征税的进口商品的价格，还会提高国内生产的商品的价格——这一效应是标准传导估计在很大程度上忽略的。通过研究2025年美国关税，我们发现大约26%的关税增幅传导至消费者价格。这些估计是相对于受冲击较小的商品进行衡量的，并保持总体条件不变。直接效应占这一增幅的64%，因为关税提高了某种商品外国品种的消费者价格。其余36%来自间接效应——关税提高了美国生产商使用的进口投入品的成本，而国内生产商因面临来自高价进口商品的竞争减少而提高了加成率。直接效应传导迅速，因为关税几乎立即推高了进口价格，但间接效应需要九到十二个月才能通过供应链发挥作用。因此，关税对消费者的影响比仅直接效应所显示的要更大且更持久。

Long-Run Effects of H-1B Immigration on the U.S. Economy

H-1B移民对美国经济的长期影响

Ran Abramitzky, Leah Platt Boustan, Ahmet Gulek, Jens Hainmueller

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We study the effects of H-1B immigration on U.S. industries that employ H-1B workers and their trading partners. Using a novel cross-industry design and the 1999–2003 expansion of the H-1B visa cap for identification, we find that H-1B exposure raised incomes for natives and pre-existing immigrants, with gains concentrated in non-STEM occupations. Income gains propagate forward through supply chains to downstream industries but not backward to upstream industries, consistent with a productivity shock rather than a labor supply shock. 我们分析了荷兰65岁以上人口中认知障碍与长期护理使用之间的关联。为此，我们使用了Lifelines队列研究的数据，这些数据与荷兰统计局提供的行政记录相关联。我们发现，在认知障碍人群中，长期护理使用集中在一个亚组中：42%的认知障碍者不使用任何长期护理。同时，使用长期护理的认知障碍者支出较高，尤其是在养老院居住时。进一步分析显示，许多认知障碍者报告存在功能限制，且认知障碍与长期护理使用的关联超出了这些限制本身。这些发现强调了在研究中解释长期护理使用决定因素时考虑认知障碍的重要性，以及在考虑政策改革时根据老年人需求定制长期护理计划的重要性。
