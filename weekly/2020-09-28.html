SME Failures Under Large Liquidity Shocks: An Application to the COVID-19 Crisis

大型流动性冲击下的中小企业倒闭：以新冠疫情危机为例

Pierre-Olivier Gourinchas, Ṣebnem Kalemli-Özcan, Veronika Penciakova, Nick Sander

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We study the effects of financial frictions on firm exit when firms face large liquidity shocks. We develop a simple model of firm cost-minimization, where firms’ borrowing capacity to smooth temporary shocks to liquidity is limited. In this framework, firm exit arises from the interaction between this financial friction and fluctuations in cash flow due to aggregate and sectoral changes in demand conditions, as well as more traditional shocks to productivity. To evaluate the implications of our model, we use firm level data on small and medium sized enterprises (SMEs) in 11 European countries. We confirm that our framework is consistent with official failure rates in 2017-2019, a period characterized by standard business cycle fluctuations in demand. To capture a large liquidity shock, we apply our framework to the COVID-19 crisis. We find that, absent government support, SME failure rates would have increased by 6.01 percentage points, putting 3.1 percent of employment at risk. Our results also show that in the presence of financial frictions and in the absence of government support, the firms failing due to COVID have similar productivity and growth to firms that survive COVID.

中文摘要

我们研究了当企业面临大规模流动性冲击时，金融摩擦对企业退出的影响。我们构建了一个简单的企业成本最小化模型，在该模型中，企业为平滑暂时性流动性冲击而进行借贷的能力是有限的。在此框架下，企业退出源于这种金融摩擦与因总体和行业需求状况变化以及更传统的生产率冲击所导致的现金流波动之间的相互作用。为了评估我们模型的含义，我们使用了11个欧洲国家中小企业的企业层面数据。我们确认，我们的框架与2017-2019年（一个以标准商业周期需求波动为特征的时期）的官方倒闭率是一致的。为了捕捉一次大规模的流动性冲击，我们将我们的框架应用于新冠疫情危机。我们发现，如果没有政府支持，中小企业的倒闭率将上升6.01个百分点，使3.1%的就业面临风险。我们的结果还表明，在存在金融摩擦且缺乏政府支持的情况下，因新冠疫情而倒闭的企业与幸存下来的企业具有相似的生产率和增长水平。

Modeling the Consumption Response to the CARES Act

模拟对《CARES法案》的消费反应

Christopher D. Carroll, Edmund Crawley, Jiri Slacalek, Matthew N. White

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 美国

Abstract

To predict the effects of the 2020 U.S. CARES Act on consumption, we extend a model that matches responses to past consumption stimulus packages. The extension allows us to account for two novel features of the coronavirus crisis. First, during lockdowns, many types of spending are undesirable or impossible. Second, some of the jobs that disappear during the lockdown will not reappear. We estimate that, if the lockdown is short-lived (the median point of view as we are writing in April 2020), the combination of expanded unemployment insurance benefits and stimulus payments should be sufficient to allow a swift recovery in consumer spending to pre-crisis levels. If the lockdown lasts longer (or there is a ‘second wave’), an extension of enhanced unemployment benefits will likely be necessary for consumption spending to recover quickly.

中文摘要

为了预测2020年美国《CARES法案》对消费的影响，我们扩展了一个能够匹配过去消费刺激计划反应的模型。这一扩展使我们能够考虑新冠疫情危机的两个新特征。首先，在封锁期间，许多类型的消费是不被期望或不可能的。其次，封锁期间消失的部分工作岗位将不会重新出现。我们估计，如果封锁是短暂的（截至2020年4月撰写本文时的中位数观点），扩大的失业保险福利和刺激性支付的组合应该足以使消费者支出迅速恢复到危机前的水平。如果封锁持续时间更长（或者出现“第二波”），那么为了消费支出能够快速复苏，延长增强型失业救济金可能是必要的。

Nudging Timely Wage Reporting: Field Experimental Evidence from the United States Social Supplementary Income Program

助推及时工资报告：来自美国社会补充收入计划的实地实验证据

C. Yiwei Zhang, Jeffrey Hemmeter, Judd B. Kessler, Robert D. Metcalfe, Robert Weathers

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We study a large-scale (n=50,000) natural field experiment implemented by the U.S. Social Security Administration that was aimed at increasing the timely and accurate self-reporting of wages by Supplemental Security Income (SSI) recipients. Sending a letter reminding SSI recipients of their wage reporting responsibilities significantly increased both the likelihood of reporting any earnings and the total amount of earnings reported, though this effect decays slightly over time. However, the specific letter content—providing social information or highlighting the salience of penalties—had no systematic effect. We develop a conservative estimate that the letters generated roughly $5.91 in savings on average per dollar spent for the U.S. government.

中文摘要

我们研究了一项由美国社会保障局实施的大规模（n=50,000）自然实地实验，该实验旨在提高补充保障收入领取者及时准确地自我报告工资的水平。向补充保障收入领取者发送一封提醒其工资报告责任的信件，显著增加了报告任何收入的可能性以及报告的收入总额，尽管这种效应会随时间略有衰减。然而，具体的信件内容——提供社会信息或强调惩罚的显著性——没有产生系统性影响。我们得出一个保守估计，即这些信件平均每花费1美元，为美国政府节省了大约5.91美元。

The International Propagation of Economic Downturns Through Multinational Companies: The Real Economy Channel

经济衰退通过跨国公司的国际传导：实体经济渠道

Jan Bena, Serdar Dinc, Isil Erel

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We study how non-financial multinational companies propagate economic declines from their subsidiaries located in countries experiencing an economic downturn to subsidiaries in countries not experiencing one. We find that investment is 18% lower in subsidiaries of these parents relative to the same-industry, same-country subsidiaries of parents that are headquartered in the same parent country but do not have a subsidiary in a country experiencing an economic downturn. The employment growth rate in the affected subsidiaries is zero or negative while it is 1.4% in the subsidiaries of unaffected parents. The aggregate industry-level sales and employment are also negatively impacted in the countries of the affected subsidiaries.

中文摘要

我们研究了非金融跨国公司如何将经济衰退从其位于经历经济衰退国家的子公司传导至位于未经历经济衰退国家的子公司。我们发现，与那些总部位于同一母公司国家、但在经历经济衰退国家没有子公司、且处于相同行业和相同国家的母公司子公司相比，这些母公司子公司的投资要低18%。受影响子公司的就业增长率为零或负值，而未受影响母公司子公司的就业增长率为1.4%。在受影响子公司所在国家，总体行业层面的销售额和就业也受到负面影响。

A Theory of Foreign Exchange Interventions

外汇干预理论

Sebastián Fanelli, Ludwig Straub

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We study a real small open economy with two key ingredients: (i) partial segmentation of home and foreign bond markets and (ii) a pecuniary externality that makes the real exchange rate excessively volatile in response to capital flows. Partial segmentation implies that, by intervening in the bond markets, the central bank can affect the exchange rate and the spread between home- and foreign-bond yields. Such interventions allow the central bank to address the pecuniary externality, but they are also costly, as foreigners make carry-trade profits. We analytically characterize the optimal intervention policy that solves this trade-off: (a) the optimal policy leans against the wind, stabilizing the exchange rate; (b) it involves smooth spreads but allows exchange rates to jump; (c) it partly relies on “forward guidance”, with non-zero interventions even after the shock has subsided; (d) it requires credibility, in that central banks do not intervene without commitment. Finally, we shed light on the global consequences of widespread interventions, using a multi-country extension of our model. We find that, left to themselves, countries over-accumulate reserves, reducing welfare and leading to inefficiently low world interest rates.

中文摘要

我们研究了一个具有两个关键特征的真实小型开放经济体：（i）国内和国外债券市场的部分分割，以及（ii）一种货币外部性，使得实际汇率对资本流动的反应过度波动。部分分割意味着，通过干预债券市场，中央银行可以影响汇率以及国内和国外债券收益率之间的利差。这种干预使中央银行能够解决货币外部性，但也是有代价的，因为外国投资者会进行套利交易获利。我们分析性地刻画了解决这一权衡的最优干预政策：（a）最优政策是逆风而行，稳定汇率；（b）它涉及平滑的利差，但允许汇率跳跃；（c）它部分依赖于“前瞻性指引”，即使在冲击消退后仍进行非零干预；（d）它需要可信度，即没有承诺的中央银行不会进行干预。最后，我们利用模型的多国扩展，揭示了广泛干预的全球后果。我们发现，如果放任自流，各国会过度积累储备，降低福利并导致世界利率低效地偏低。

Impediments to the Schumpeterian Process in the Replacement of Large Firms

大型企业更替中熊彼特过程的障碍

Mara Faccio, John J. McConnell

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Using newly-assembled data encompassing up to 75 countries and starting circa 1910, we find that the Schumpeterian process of creative destruction aptly describes the replacement of large firms by other firms, but exceptions to the norm of replacement are not rare and replacement is often not by new firms. Initial firm size and political connections represent the main obstacles to the Schumpeterian process while board interlocks and a corporate culture of innovation play modest roles. Consistent with a theory of political capture, when accompanied by regulations that restrict entry, political connections play a formidable role in abetting large firms remaining large.

中文摘要

利用新汇编的、涵盖多达75个国家且起始于1910年左右的数据，我们发现熊彼特的创造性破坏过程恰当地描述了大型企业被其他企业所取代的现象，但不符合更替常态的例外情况并不罕见，而且更替往往不是由新企业完成的。初始企业规模和政治关联是熊彼特过程的主要障碍，而董事会连锁和企业创新文化的作用则较为温和。与政治俘获理论一致，当伴随着限制进入的监管时，政治关联在助长大型企业保持其规模方面发挥着巨大作用。

Long-run Returns to Private Equity in Emerging Markets

新兴市场私募股权的长期回报

Shawn Cole, Martin Melecky, Florian Mölders, Tristan Reed

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We provide the first evidence on the long-run returns to private equity in emerging and frontier markets using the cash flows from every equity investment made by the International Finance Corporation across 130 countries over 58 years. Risk-adjusted returns are comparable to the S&P 500, at least from 1961 until 2010. Returns improve with economic growth, but decline as banking systems deepen and countries relax capital controls. These results are consistent with the thesis that financial frictions have prevented the flow of capital from rich to poor countries, and that the persistence of impact investors’ performance may rely on identifying or creating new markets that lack access to capital.

中文摘要

我们利用国际金融公司在130个国家58年间每笔股权投资产生的现金流，首次提供了新兴市场和前沿市场私募股权长期回报的证据。至少从1961年到2010年，风险调整后的回报与标普500指数相当。回报随着经济增长而改善，但随着银行体系深化和各国放松资本管制而下降。这些结果与以下论点一致：金融摩擦阻碍了资本从富国流向穷国，并且影响力投资者的持续表现可能依赖于识别或创造缺乏资本准入的新市场。

Is the Selfish Life-Cycle Model More Applicable in Japan and, If So, Why? A Literature Survey

自私的生命周期模型在日本是否更适用？如果是，为什么？一项文献综述

Charles Yuji Horioka

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 日本、美国

Abstract

The selfish life-cycle model or hypothesis is, together with the dynasty or altruism model, the most widely used theoretical model of household behavior in economics, but does this model apply in the case of a country like Japan, which is said to have closer family ties than other countries? In this paper, we first provide a brief exposition of the simplest version of the selfish life-cycle model and then survey the literature on household saving and bequest behavior in Japan in order to answer this question. The paper finds that almost all of the available evidence suggests that the selfish life-cycle model applies to at least some extent in all countries but that there is more consistent support for this model in Japan than in the United States and other countries. It then explores possible explanations for why the life-cycle model is more consistently supported in Japan than in other countries, attributing this finding to government policies, institutional factors, economic factors, demographic factors, and cultural factors. Finally, it shows that the findings of the paper have many important implications for economic modeling and for government tax and expenditure policies.

中文摘要

自私的生命周期模型或假说，与王朝模型或利他主义模型一起，是经济学中最常用的家庭行为理论模型，但这个模型是否适用于像日本这样据说家庭纽带比其他国更紧密的国家？在本文中，我们首先简要阐述了最简单的自私生命周期模型版本，然后为了回答这个问题，我们调查了关于日本家庭储蓄和遗赠行为的文献。本文发现，几乎所有可获得的证据都表明，自私的生命周期模型至少在一定程度上适用于所有国家，但该模型在日本得到的支持比在美国和其他国家更为一致。然后，本文探讨了生命周期模型在日本比在其他国家得到更一致支持的可能解释，将此发现归因于政府政策、制度因素、经济因素、人口因素和文化因素。最后，本文表明，该研究的发现对经济建模以及政府税收和支出政策具有许多重要启示。

Health Insurance Menu Design for Large Employers

大型雇主的健康保险菜单设计

Kate Ho, Robin S. Lee

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We provide a framework for large employers designing a menu of health plan offerings that differ on both financial and non-financial dimensions. Using detailed administrative data from Harvard University, we estimate a model of plan choice and utilization, and evaluate the benefits of cost sharing and plan variety. For this population of employed consumers, and a single plan with a generous out-of-pocket maximum and zero deductible, we find that modest cost sharing of approximately 30% maximizes average employee surplus. 当前的经济危机凸显了对既及时又本地化的数据的需求，以便能够更即时地评估财政政策选项对地方经济的影响。本文强调了利用两种新的近实时数据源来为实施适当刺激方法的决策提供信息的潜在价值。第一个数据源是提供每周、普遍性的失业索赔人信息的行政记录。第二个数据源是按日可获取的经济活动交易层面数据。我们利用刺激支付的离散变化构建了一个评估财政政策对地方经济活动实时影响的框架。具体而言，我们利用2020年3月至9月间联邦大流行失业补偿（FPUC）新冠疫情失业保险补助的相对规模——从0美元到600美元再到300美元——在县际和时间上的变化，来估算失业、收入替代以及两者交互作用对地方经济的影响。我们发现，即使失业索赔率上升，更高的收入替代率也会显著增加消费者支出，这与财政刺激的目标一致。
