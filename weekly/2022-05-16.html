Voting on a Trade Agreement: Firm Networks and Attitudes Toward Openness

对贸易协定的投票：企业网络与对开放的态度

Esteban Méndez, Diana Van Patten

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 哥斯达黎加、美国

Abstract

We exploit a unique event to study the extent to which popular attitudes toward trade are driven by economic fundamentals. In 2007, Costa Rica put a free trade agreement (FTA) to a national referendum. With a single question on the ballot, 59% of Costa Rican adult citizens cast a vote on whether they wanted an FTA with the United States to be ratified or not. We merge disaggregated referendum results, which break new ground on anonymity-compatible voting data, with employer-employee, customs, and firm-to-firm transactions data, and data on household composition and expenditures. We document that a firm’s exposure to the FTA, directly and via input-output linkages, significantly influences the voting behavior of its employees. This effect dominates that of sector-level exposure and is greater for voters aligned with pro-FTA political candidates. We also show that citizens considered the expected decrease in consumer prices when exercising their vote. Overall, economic factors explain 7% of the variation in voting patterns, which cannot be accounted for by non-economic factors such as political ideology, and played a pivotal role in this vote.

中文摘要

我们利用一个独特的事件来研究公众对贸易的态度在多大程度上由经济基本面驱动。2007年，哥斯达黎加将一项自由贸易协定提交全国公投。选票上只有一个问题，59%的哥斯达黎加成年公民投票决定是否批准与美国的一项自由贸易协定。我们将细分的公投结果（这为匿名兼容的投票数据开辟了新天地）与雇主-雇员数据、海关数据、企业间交易数据以及家庭构成和支出数据合并。我们记录到，一家企业直接或通过投入产出联系对自由贸易协定的暴露程度，会显著影响其员工的投票行为。这种效应超过了行业层面暴露程度的影响，并且对于支持自由贸易协定的政治候选人的选民来说更为显著。我们还表明，公民在投票时考虑了预期的消费者价格下降。总体而言，经济因素解释了投票模式中7%的变异，这无法由政治意识形态等非经济因素解释，并且在此次投票中发挥了关键作用。

Financial Inclusion, Economic Development, and Inequality: Evidence from Brazil

金融包容性、经济发展与不平等：来自巴西的证据

Julia Fonseca, Adrien Matray

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 巴西

Abstract

We study a financial inclusion policy targeting Brazilian cities with low bank branch coverage using data on the universe of employees from 2000–2014. The policy leads to bank entry and to similar increases in both deposits and lending. It also fosters entrepreneurship, employment, and wage growth, especially for cities initially in banking deserts. These gains are not shared equally and instead increase with workers’ education, implying a substantial increase in wage inequality. The changes in inequality are concentrated in cities where the initial supply of skilled workers is low, indicating that talent scarcity can drive how financial development affects inequality.

中文摘要

我们利用2000-2014年全体雇员的数据，研究了一项针对巴西银行网点覆盖率低城市的金融包容性政策。该政策导致银行进入，并使得存款和贷款均出现类似增长。它还促进了创业、就业和工资增长，尤其是在最初处于“银行沙漠”的城市。但这些收益并非平等分享，而是随着工人教育水平的提高而增加，这意味着工资不平等显著加剧。不平等的变化集中在初始熟练工人供给较低的城市，这表明人才稀缺性可能驱动金融发展如何影响不平等。

The Supplemental Poverty Measure: A New Method for Measuring Poverty

补充贫困衡量指标：一种衡量贫困的新方法

John Fitzgerald, Robert A. Moffitt

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose a new measure of the rate of poverty we call the Supplemental Expenditure Poverty Measure (SEPM) based on expenditure in the Consumer Expenditure survey. It treats household expenditure as a measure of resources available to purchase the minimum bundle necessary to meet basic needs. Our measure differs from conventional income and consumption poverty in both concept and measurement and it has advantages relative to both. Poverty rates using our basic measure are very close in level and recent trend to those of the most preferred income-based poverty rate produced by the Census Bureau. But our SEPM poverty rate differs from the Census measure at different levels of the poverty line. For example, that the number of individuals living in either poor or “almost” poor households is 5 percentage points greater (about 16 million individuals) using our measure. We also construct an augmented measure that adds additional potential liquid resources. This “maximal resources” measure indicates that if disadvantaged households used up all their bank balances and maximized their credit card borrowing, 9.6 percent of the population (over 31 million individuals) would still be poor and unable to purchase the goods necessary for the basic needs of life.

中文摘要

我们提出了一种新的贫困率衡量指标，称为补充性支出贫困衡量指标（SEPM），该指标基于消费者支出调查中的支出数据。它将家庭支出视为可用于购买满足基本需求所需最低限度商品组合的资源衡量指标。我们的指标在概念和测量上均不同于传统的收入贫困和消费贫困，并且相对于两者都有优势。使用我们基本指标得出的贫困率，在水平和近期趋势上与人口普查局最常用的基于收入的贫困率非常接近。但我们的SEPM贫困率在贫困线的不同水平上与人口普查指标存在差异。例如，根据我们的指标，生活在贫困或“近乎”贫困家庭中的个体数量多出5个百分点（约1600万人）。我们还构建了一个增强型指标，增加了额外的潜在流动性资源。这个“最大资源”指标表明，如果弱势家庭用尽所有银行存款并最大化信用卡借贷，仍有9.6%的人口（超过3100万人）将处于贫困状态，无法购买维持基本生活所需的商品。

No. 4论文详情NBER w30055

Economic Integration and the Transmission of Democracy

经济一体化与民主的传播

Marco Tabellini, Giacomo Magistretti

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

In this paper, we study the effects of economic integration with democracies on individuals’ democratic values and on countries’ institutions. We combine survey data with country level measures of democracy from 1960 to 2015, and exploit improvements in air, relative to sea, transportation to derive a time-varying instrument for trade. We find that economic integration with democracies increases both citizens’ support for democracy and countries’ democracy scores. Instead, trade with non-democracies has no impact on either attitudes or institutions. The effects of trade with democracies are stronger when partners have a longer history of democracy, grow faster, spend more on public goods, and are culturally closer. They are also driven by imports, rather than exports, and by integration with partners that export higher quality goods and that account for a larger share of a country’s trade in institutionally intensive, cultural, and consumer goods as well as in goods that involve more face-to-face interactions and entail higher levels of bilateral trust. These patterns are consistent with trade in goods favoring the transmission of democracy by signaling the (actual or perceived) desirability of the latter. We examine alternative mechanisms, and conclude that none of them can, alone, explain our findings.

中文摘要

在本文中，我们研究了与民主国家的经济一体化对个人民主价值观和国家制度的影响。我们将1960年至2015年的调查数据与国家层面的民主衡量指标相结合，并利用航空运输相对于海运的改善来推导贸易的时变工具变量。我们发现，与民主国家的经济一体化既增加了公民对民主的支持，也提高了国家的民主得分。相反，与非民主国家的贸易对态度或制度均无影响。当贸易伙伴具有更长的民主历史、增长更快、在公共品上支出更多且文化上更接近时，与民主国家贸易的影响更强。这些影响还由进口而非出口驱动，并且是由与那些出口更高质量商品、在一国制度密集型、文化及消费品贸易中占更大份额、以及涉及更多面对面互动和更高双边信任水平的伙伴进行一体化所驱动的。这些模式与商品贸易通过传递民主的（实际或感知的）可取性信号来促进民主传播是一致的。我们考察了其他机制，并得出结论，没有任何一种机制能单独解释我们的发现。

Addressing Non-Financial Barriers to College Access and Success: Evidence and Policy Implications

应对大学入学与成功的非金融障碍：证据与政策启示

Susan Dynarski, Aizat Nurshatayeva, Lindsay C. Page, Judith Scott-Clayton

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Non-financial barriers to college are an important possible explanation for socioeconomic, racial, gender, and other gaps in college access and success. A sizeable economic literature documents policy efforts to understand and address these barriers. We review this literature on non-financial interventions for improving college access and success ranging from discreet and narrowly defined interventions to comprehensive and multifaceted programs and systemic-level solutions.

中文摘要

大学的非金融障碍是解释社会经济、种族、性别及其他群体在大学入学与成功方面差距的一个重要可能原因。大量经济学文献记录了旨在理解和应对这些障碍的政策努力。我们回顾了关于改善大学入学与成功的非金融干预措施的文献，这些干预措施范围从具体且狭义定义的干预措施到全面且多方面的项目以及系统性层面的解决方案。

Bargaining and International Reference Pricing in the Pharmaceutical Industry

制药行业的议价与国际参考定价

Pierre Dubois, Ashvin Gandhi, Shoshana Vasserman

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 加拿大、美国

Abstract

The United States spends twice as much per person on pharmaceuticals as European countries, in large part because prices are much higher in the US. This fact has led policymakers to consider legislation for price controls. This paper assesses the effects of a US international reference pricing policy that would cap prices in US markets by those offered in reference countries. We estimate a structural model of demand and supply for pharmaceuticals in the US and reference countries like Canada where prices are set through a negotiation process between pharmaceutical companies and the government. We then simulate the counterfactual equilibrium under such international reference pricing rules, allowing firms to internalize the cross-country externalities introduced by these policies. We find that in general, these policies would result in much smaller price decreases in the US than price increases in reference countries. The magnitude of these effects depends on the number, size and market structure of references countries. We compare these policies with a direct bargaining on prices in the US.

中文摘要

美国在药品上的人均支出是欧洲国家的两倍，很大程度上是因为美国药品价格高得多。这一事实促使政策制定者考虑实施价格管制立法。本文评估了一项美国国际参考定价政策的效果，该政策将美国市场的价格上限设定为参考国家提供的价格。我们估计了美国和加拿大等参考国家（这些国家的价格通过制药公司与政府之间的谈判过程设定）药品需求与供给的结构模型。然后，我们在此类国际参考定价规则下模拟反事实均衡，允许企业内化这些政策引入的跨国外部性。我们发现，总体而言，这些政策将导致美国价格下降的幅度远小于参考国家价格上涨的幅度。这些影响的大小取决于参考国家的数量、规模和市场结构。我们将这些政策与在美国直接进行价格谈判进行了比较。

Skill Heterogeneity and Aggregate Labor Market Dynamics

技能异质性与总体劳动力市场动态

John R. Grigsby

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper studies aggregate labor market dynamics when workers have heterogeneous skills for tasks which are subject to non-uniform labor demand shocks. When workers have different skills, movements in aggregate wages partly reflect a reallocation of different workers across tasks and into employment. This ensures that there nearly always exists some combination of task-specific demand shocks that induce aggregate employment and wages to negatively comove even in a frictionless economy. Furthermore, such reallocations would be interpreted either as a labor wedge or as a shift in an aggregate labor supply curve in representative agent economies. Developing a method to estimate the multidimensional skill distribution, I show that a frictionless model with realistic heterogeneity can replicate the mean wage increase and employment collapse of the Great Recession. Reduced-form composition-adjustment methods recover positive co-movements between employment and wages in recent periods suggesting an increasing role for composition effects through time, which the model rationalizes through changes in the skill distribution and composition of sectoral shocks.

中文摘要

本文研究了当工人对面临非均匀劳动力需求冲击的任务具有异质性技能时，总体劳动力市场的动态变化。当工人拥有不同技能时，总体工资的变动部分反映了不同工人在不同任务之间以及进入就业状态的重新配置。这确保了即使在无摩擦经济中，也几乎总是存在某种任务特定需求冲击的组合，能够导致总体就业和工资呈负向联动。此外，在代表性代理人经济中，这种重新配置将被解释为劳动力楔子或总体劳动力供给曲线的移动。通过开发一种估计多维技能分布的方法，我表明一个具有现实异质性的无摩擦模型可以复制大衰退期间的平均工资增长和就业崩溃。近期数据中，简化形式的构成调整方法恢复了就业与工资之间的正联动，表明构成效应随时间推移的作用日益增强，而该模型通过技能分布和部门冲击构成的变化对此进行了合理化解释。

No. 8论文详情NBER w30051

The Demand for Money, Near-Money, and Treasury Bonds

对货币、准货币和国债的需求

Arvind Krishnamurthy, Wenhao Li

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Bank-created money, shadow-bank money, and Treasury bonds all satisfy investors' demand for a liquid transaction medium and safe store of value. We measure the quantity of these three forms of liquidity and their corresponding liquidity premium over a sample from 1934 to 2016. We empirically examine the links between these different assets, estimating the extent to which they are substitutes, and the amount of liquidity per unit delivered by each asset. Treasury bonds and bank deposits are imperfect substitutes, in contrast to the findings of perfect substitutes of Nagel (2016). This result is directly relevant to the monetary transmission mechanism running through shifts in asset supplies, such as quantitative easing policies. Our results on the imperfect substitutability of bank and shadow-bank money also inform analyses of the coexistence of the shadow-banking and regulated banking system. We construct a new broad monetary aggregate based on our estimates and show that it helps resolve the money-demand instability and missing-money puzzles of the monetary economics literature.

中文摘要

银行创造的货币、影子银行货币和国债都满足了投资者对流动性交易媒介和安全价值储存的需求。我们衡量了1934年至2016年样本期间这三种流动性形式的数量及其相应的流动性溢价。我们实证检验了这些不同资产之间的联系，估计了它们之间的替代程度以及每种资产每单位提供的流动性数量。国债和银行存款是不完全替代品，这与Nagel（2016）发现的完全替代品结论形成对比。这一结果与通过资产供给变动（如量化宽松政策）发挥作用的货币传导机制直接相关。我们关于银行和影子银行货币不完全替代性的结论也为分析影子银行与受监管银行体系共存提供了信息。我们基于估计结果构建了一个新的广义货币总量，并表明它有助于解决货币经济学文献中的货币需求不稳定性和“失踪货币”之谜。

Small Campaign Donors

小额竞选捐款人

Laurent Bouton, Julia Cagé, Edgard Dewitte, Vincent Pons

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

We assemble the first comprehensive dataset on small campaign donors in the U.S. from 2005 to 2020, leveraging the fact that small, previously unobservable contributions now largely flow through fundraising platforms required to report them. Although the number of small donors has surged, their aggregate contributions have not grown faster than large donors’. Small donors are more representative of the population but remain disproportionately white and affluent. Abstract

The classical approach to the problem requires using instrumental variables that must satisfy the stringent condition of exclusion restriction. A forefront of recent research is a new paradigm of handling endogenous regressors without using instrumental variables.
