How Hybrid Working From Home Works Out

混合居家办公的效果如何

Nicholas Bloom, Ruobing Han, James Liang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Hybrid working from home (hybrid), whereby employees work a mix of days at home and at work each week, has become common for graduate employees. This paper evaluates a randomized control trial of hybrid on 1612 graduate engineers, marketing and finance employees of a large technology firm. There are four key results. First, hybrid was highly valued by employees on average, reducing attrition by 33% and improving job-satisfaction measures. Second, hybrid reduced working hours on home days and increased them on office days and the weekend, altering the structure of the working week. Third, hybrid increased messaging and video calls, even when all employees were in the office, reflecting a move towards more electronic communication. Finally, there were large differences in the valuations of hybrid between managers and non-managers. Non-managers were more likely to volunteer into the hybrid experiment, to work from home on eligible days, to predict positive impacts on productivity, and to reduce their attrition under hybrid. In contrast, managers were less likely to volunteer, less likely to work from home on eligible days, predicted a negative average impact of hybrid on productivity, and saw increased attrition rates under hybrid.

中文摘要

混合居家办公（hybrid），即员工每周部分时间在家办公、部分时间在办公室办公，已成为毕业生员工的常见模式。本文评估了一项针对一家大型科技公司1612名毕业生工程师、市场营销和财务员工的混合办公随机对照试验。有四个关键结果。第一，混合办公平均而言受到员工高度重视，使离职率降低33%，并提高了工作满意度指标。第二，混合办公减少了在家工作日的工时，增加了在办公室日和周末的工时，改变了工作周的结构。第三，混合办公增加了消息和视频通话，即使所有员工都在办公室时也是如此，反映出向更多电子化沟通的转变。最后，管理者与非管理者对混合办公的估值存在巨大差异。非管理者更有可能自愿参与混合办公实验，在符合条件的日子居家办公，预测对生产率的积极影响，并在混合办公下降低离职率。相比之下，管理者不太可能自愿参与，不太可能在符合条件的日子居家办公，预测混合办公对生产率的平均负面影响，并在混合办公下看到离职率上升。

Simple Tests for Selection: Learning More from Instrumental Variables

选择性的简单检验：从工具变量中了解更多

Dan A. Black, Joonhwi Joo, Robert LaLonde, Jeffrey A. Smith, Evan J. Taylor

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We provide simple tests for selection on unobserved variables in the Vytlacil-Imbens-Angrist framework for Local Average Treatment Effects (LATEs). Our setup allows researchers not only to test for selection on either or both of the treated and untreated outcomes, but also to assess the magnitude of the selection effect. We show that it applies to the standard binary instrument case, as well as to experiments with imperfect compliance and fuzzy regression discontinuity designs, and we link it to broader discussions regarding instrumental variables. We illustrate the substantive value added by our framework with three empirical applications drawn from the literature.

中文摘要

我们在局部平均处理效应（LATE）的Vytlacil-Imbens-Angrist框架中，提供了针对未观测变量选择性的简单检验。我们的设置不仅允许研究者检验是否对处理结果或未处理结果存在选择性，还可以评估选择性效应的大小。我们表明，该方法适用于标准二元工具变量情形，以及存在不完全依从性的实验和模糊断点回归设计，并将其与关于工具变量的更广泛讨论联系起来。我们通过文献中的三个实证应用来说明我们框架的实质性附加值。

American Agriculture, Water Resources, and Climate Change

美国农业、水资源与气候变化

Gary D. Libecap, Ariel Dinar

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

This paper highlights the role of agriculture in the American economy and society over time and points to farmer historical and contemporary responses to varying climatic conditions. It indicates the importance of water as an input to agricultural production and identifies possible impacts of climate change on access to water. It then summarizes a set of eleven papers from an NBER research project on water, climate change, and the agricultural sector.

中文摘要

本文强调了农业在美国经济和社会中随时间演变的作用，并指出了农民历史上及当代对多变气候条件的应对方式。文章说明了水作为农业生产投入要素的重要性，并识别了气候变化对水资源获取可能产生的影响。随后，文章总结了一个NBER研究项目中关于水、气候变化和农业部门的十一篇论文。

Banking on Transparency for the Poor: Experimental Evidence from India

为穷人押注透明度：来自印度的实验证据

Erica M. Field ⓡ Natalia Rigol ⓡ Charity M. Troyer Moore ⓡ Rohini Pande ⓡ Simone G. Schaner

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 印度

Abstract

Do information frictions limit the benefits of financial inclusion drives for the rural poor? We evaluate an experimental intervention among recently banked poor Indian women receiving government cash transfers via direct deposit. Treated women were provided automated voice calls confirming details of transactions posted to their accounts. The intervention increased women's knowledge of account balances and trust in their local banking agent. Indicative of improved consumption-smoothing by income-constrained women, administrative data show that treated women accessed government transfers faster when the service was active, with treatment effects dissipating after the notifications were discontinued. On average, other aspects of account use remained unchanged. However, consistent with account information benefiting those with high transaction costs more, the intervention increased account use among women who lived more than an hour from the kiosk.

中文摘要

信息摩擦是否会限制金融包容性举措对农村贫困人口的益处？我们评估了一项针对近期获得银行账户的印度贫困女性的实验性干预措施，这些女性通过直接存款接收政府现金转移支付。接受干预的女性会收到自动语音电话，确认其账户交易详情。该干预措施提高了女性对账户余额的了解以及对当地银行代理的信任。行政数据显示，在服务活跃期间，接受干预的女性更快地获取了政府转移支付，这表明收入受限女性的消费平滑得到了改善，但在通知停止后，处理效应逐渐消失。平均而言，账户使用的其他方面保持不变。然而，与账户信息对交易成本高的人更有益一致的是，该干预措施增加了居住在距离服务亭超过一小时路程的女性的账户使用。

The Effect of Macroeconomic Uncertainty on Firm Decisions

宏观经济不确定性对企业决策的影响

Saten Kumar, Yuriy Gorodnichenko, Olivier Coibion

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 新西兰

Abstract

Using a new survey of firms in New Zealand, we document how exogenous variation in the macroeconomic uncertainty perceived by firms affects their economic decisions. We use randomized information treatments that provide different types of information about the first and/or second moments of future economic growth to generate exogenous changes in the perceived macroeconomic uncertainty of some firms. The effects on their decisions relative to their initial plans as well as relative to an untreated control group are measured in a follow-up survey six months later. We find that as firms become more uncertain, they reduce their prices, employment, and investment, their sales decline, and they become less likely to invest in new technologies or open new facilities. These ex-post effects of uncertainty are similar to how firms say they would respond to higher uncertainty when asked hypothetical questions.

中文摘要

利用一项针对新西兰企业的新调查，我们记录了企业感知到的宏观经济不确定性的外生变化如何影响其经济决策。我们采用随机信息处理方式，提供关于未来经济增长第一和/或第二矩的不同类型信息，以引起部分企业感知到的宏观经济不确定性的外生变化。在六个月后的后续调查中，我们测量了这些变化相对于企业初始计划以及相对于未接受处理的对照组对其决策的影响。我们发现，随着企业变得更加不确定，它们会降低价格、减少就业和投资，销售额下降，并且投资新技术或开设新设施的可能性降低。这些不确定性的事后效应与企业在被问及假设性问题时表示将如何应对更高不确定性的方式相似。

Are Menthol Smokers Different? An Economic Perspective

薄荷醇吸烟者有何不同？一个经济学视角

Yu-Chun Cheng, Donald S. Kenkel, Alan D. Mathios, Hua Wang

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Of the 45.7 million current smokers in the U.S. age 12 and over, more than 18.5 million usually smoke menthol cigarettes. The Food and Drug Administration recently proposed a tobacco product standard that would prohibit menthol as a characterizing flavor in cigarettes. The FDA concludes that the prohibition of menthol in cigarettes is appropriate for public health, meeting the criterion established by the 2009 Tobacco Control Act. In this paper we explore whether there are internality-based market failures that provide an applied welfare economics rationale to prohibit menthol. Our empirical approach provides descriptive evidence from the 2018-2019 Tobacco Use Supplement to the Current Population Survey on how menthol use is associated with smokers’ market demand along multiple intensive and extensive margins. We also use direct measures of smoking-related misinformation and internalities, and stated preference data, from a 2021 Cornell Online Survey. We acknowledge that the associations we document in observational data might reflect bias due to self-selection into menthol use. We discuss several lines of evidence about whether self-selection into menthol use might be mainly quasi-random. We leave it to reader whether there is convincing evidence that differential levels of internality-based market failures are a sufficient justification for the proposed prohibition of menthol cigarettes.

中文摘要

在美国12岁及以上的4570万当前吸烟者中，超过1850万人通常吸薄荷醇香烟。美国食品药品监督管理局最近提出了一项烟草产品标准，将禁止在香烟中使用薄荷醇作为特征风味。FDA得出结论，禁止香烟中的薄荷醇对公共健康是适当的，符合2009年《烟草控制法》确立的标准。在本文中，我们探讨是否存在基于内部性的市场失灵，为禁止薄荷醇提供应用福利经济学的理由。我们的实证方法利用2018-2019年当前人口调查的烟草使用补充数据，提供了关于薄荷醇使用如何与吸烟者在多个集约和广延边际上的市场需求相关联的描述性证据。我们还使用了2021年康奈尔在线调查中关于吸烟相关错误信息和内部性的直接测量，以及陈述偏好数据。我们承认，我们在观测数据中记录的相关性可能反映了因自我选择进入薄荷醇使用而产生的偏差。我们讨论了关于自我选择进入薄荷醇使用是否可能主要是准随机的几条证据线索。我们留给读者判断：是否存在令人信服的证据表明，基于内部性的市场失灵的不同水平足以成为拟议禁止薄荷醇香烟的充分理由。

Were Small Businesses More Likely to Permanently Close in the Pandemic?

小企业在疫情期间更可能永久关闭吗？

Robert W. Fairlie, Frank M. Fossen, Reid L. Johnsen, Gentian Droboniku

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Previous estimates indicate that COVID-19 led to a large drop in the number of operating businesses operating early in the pandemic, but surprisingly little is known on whether these shutdowns turned into permanent closures and whether small businesses were disproportionately hit. This paper provides the first analysis of permanent business closures using confidential administrative firm-level panel data covering the universe of businesses filing sales taxes from the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration. We find large increases in closures rates in the first two quarters of 2020, but a strong reversal of this trend in the third quarter of 2020. The increase in closures rates in the first two quarters of the pandemic was substantially larger for small businesses than large businesses, but the rebound in the third quarter was also larger. The disproportionate closing of small businesses led to a sharp concentration of market share among larger businesses as indicated by the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index with only a partial reversal after the initial increase. The findings highlight the fragility of small businesses during a large adverse shock and the consequences for the competitiveness of markets.

中文摘要

先前的估计表明，COVID-19导致疫情初期运营企业数量大幅下降，但令人惊讶的是，关于这些停业是否转变为永久关闭以及小企业是否受到不成比例冲击的信息很少。本文首次利用来自加州税费管理局的涵盖所有提交销售税企业的机密行政企业层面面板数据，对永久性企业关闭进行了分析。我们发现，2020年前两个季度的关闭率大幅上升，但在2020年第三季度出现了强劲逆转。在疫情的前两个季度，小企业的关闭率增长远大于大企业，但第三季度的反弹也更大。小企业不成比例的关闭导致市场份额急剧集中于大企业，如赫芬达尔-赫希曼指数所示，且初始增长后仅出现部分逆转。这些发现突显了小企业在巨大不利冲击下的脆弱性及其对市场竞争力的影响。

World War II Blues: The Long–lasting Mental Health Effect of Childhood Trauma

二战忧郁：童年创伤的长期心理健康影响

Mevlude Akbulut-Yuksel, Erdal Tekin, Belgi Turan

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 德国

Abstract

There has been a revival of warfare and threats of interstate war in recent years as the number of countries engaged in armed conflict surged dramatically, reaching levels unprecedented since the end of the Cold War. This is happening at a time when the global burden of mental health illness is also on the rise. We examine the causal impact of early life exposure to warfare on long–term mental health, using novel data on the amount of bombs dropped in German cities by Allied Air Forces during World War II (WWII) and the German Socio-economic Panel. Our identification strategy leverages a generalized difference-in-differences design, exploiting the plausibly exogenous variation in the bombing intensity experienced by the former West German cities during the war as a quasi-experiment. We find that cohorts younger than age five at the onset of WWII or those born during the war are in significantly worse mental health later in life when they are between the ages of the late 50s and 70s. Specifically, an increase of one standard deviation in the bombing intensity experienced during WWII is associated with about a 10 percent decline in an individual’s long–term standardized mental health score. This effect is equivalent to a 16.2 percent increase in the likelihood of being diagnosed with clinical depression. Our investigation suggests that mechanisms such as the destruction of healthcare infrastructure, the increased burden on the healthcare system, and wealth losses during WWII exacerbate the adverse impact of bombing exposure on long–term mental health. Conversely, war relief funds transferred to municipalities following the war have a mitigating impact. Our findings are robust to numerous empirical checks and specifications. With the mental health impact of childhood exposure to warfare persisting well into the late stages of life, the global burden of mental illness may be aggravated for many years to come. Our findings imply that prioritizing children and a long–term horizon in public health planning and response may be critical to mitigating the adverse mental health consequences of exposure to armed conflict.

中文摘要

近年来，随着参与武装冲突的国家数量急剧增加，达到冷战结束以来前所未有的水平，战争和国家间战争的威胁再度抬头。这发生在全球心理健康疾病负担也在上升的时期。我们利用盟军空军在第二次世界大战期间投放到德国城市的炸弹数量的新数据以及德国社会经济面板数据，考察了早年暴露于战争对长期心理健康的因果影响。我们的识别策略采用广义双重差分设计，利用前西德城市在战争期间经历的轰炸强度的外生变化作为准实验。我们发现，在二战开始时年龄小于五岁或在战争期间出生的群体，在晚年（50多岁到70多岁之间）的心理健康状况显著更差。具体来说，二战期间经历的轰炸强度每增加一个标准差，个体的长期标准化心理健康得分就会下降约10%。这一效应相当于被诊断患有临床抑郁症的可能性增加16.2%。我们的研究表明，二战期间医疗基础设施的破坏、医疗系统负担的增加以及财富损失等机制加剧了轰炸暴露对长期心理健康的不利影响。相反，战后转移给市政当局的战争救济基金则具有缓解作用。我们的发现对众多实证检验和设定均保持稳健。由于童年时期暴露于战争对心理健康的影响会持续到生命晚期，全球心理健康疾病负担可能会在未来许多年加剧。我们的发现意味着，在公共卫生规划和应对中优先考虑儿童并着眼长期，对于减轻暴露于武装冲突的不利心理健康后果可能至关重要。

Adverse Selection as a Policy Instrument: Unraveling Climate Change

Steve Cicala, David Hémous, Morten G. Olsen

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We propose a new policy instrument that leverages adverse selection when Pigouvian policies are infeasible or undesirable. Our policy gives firms the option to pay a tax on their voluntarily disclosed emissions, or an output tax based on the average emissions among undisclosed firms. We derive sufficient statistics formulas to calculate the welfare gains relative to an output tax, and an algorithm to implement the policy with minimal information. In an application to methane emissions from oil and gas fields, our policy generates significant welfare gains. Finally, we extend our analysis to the design of international carbon policy.

The Impact of Climate Change on Mortality in the United States: Benefits and Costs of Adaptation

气候变化对美国死亡率的影响：适应的收益与成本

Olivier Deschenes

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

This paper reviews and extends the recent empirical literature on the impact of climate change on mortality and adaptation in the United States. The analysis produces several new facts. First, the reductions in the impact of extreme heat on mortality risk previously documented up to 2004 have continued up to 2019, consistent with continued investments in health-protecting adaptations to high temperatures. The second part of the paper examines the private and external costs of electricity generation and consumption related to high temperatures, a commonly-used proxy for measuring the consumption of adaptation services. Extreme temperatures increase electricity demand in the residential sector (relative to moderate temperatures), but not in the commercial, industrial, and transportation end-use sectors. The additional electricity demand in response to high temperatures results in significant external costs due to the release of local and global pollutants caused by the combustion of fossil fuels in order to produce electricity. These external costs, documented for the first time in this paper, are one order of magnitude larger than the private cost of adaptation associated with electricity consumption.

Paul Bergin, Kyunghun Kim, Ju H. Pyun

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper finds that limited exchange rate flexibility in the form of “fear of appreciation” slows adjustment of current account imbalances, providing a novel perspective on Friedman’s conjecture regarding exchange-rate flexibility. We present evidence that countries classified as more flexible have faster convergence than peggers for current account deficits, but not so for surpluses. An implication is that current account surpluses are more persistent than deficits on average. Evidence indicates that this asymmetry is associated with a one-sided muting of exchange rate appreciations. We develop a multi-country DSGE model augmented with a “fear of appreciation” policy rule, solved as an occasionally binding constraint. It can explain greater persistence of current account surpluses compared to deficits in general equilibrium where surpluses and deficits must cancel in aggregate.

Abstract

Measuring Climate Change Impacts on Agriculture: An Equilibrium Perspective on Supply-Side Approaches

Christophe Gouel

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

A popular approach for estimating climate change impacts on agriculture is to rely on supply-side reduced-form regressions. These methods, which include the Ricardian approach, focus on how farmers and agricultural land market react to changes in climatic conditions, under the implicit assumption that crop prices stay constant. To test whether this assumption is innocuous, I use a quantitative trade model of global agricultural markets to emulate the findings of a supply-side approach as well as to calculate welfare changes accounting for price changes. The results show that both welfare measures are weakly correlated and can be of opposite signs, and that the supply-side approach tends to underestimate the cost of climate change. The main drivers of these differences are the neglects of the imperfect substitutability of crops in demand and of terms-of-trade changes. The supply-side approach provides a valid approximation of the welfare cost of climate change only if crops are almost perfectly substitutable in demand and trade costs are neglected, a situation in which it is reasonable to assume constant prices.

Abstract

Existing theories of media competition imply that advertisers will pay a lower price in equilibrium to reach consumers who multi-home across competing outlets. Abstract

Abstract

Consider an economy in which agents face income risk but interact in a stochastic financial network where the randomness is dictated by both chance and choice. We study the financial centrality of an agent defined as the ex-ante marginal social value of providing a small liquid asset to that agent. We show financially central agents are not only those who are linked often, but are more likely to be linked when (i) the realized network is fragmented, (ii) income risk is high, (iii) shocks are positively correlated, (iv) attitudes toward risk are more sensitive in the aggregate, and (v) there are tail risks. We apply our framework to models of financial markets with participation shocks, supply chains subject to disruptions, and village risk-sharing networks. We also study how the stochastic financial network structure influences bargaining, thereby endogenizing Pareto weights in the planner's problem. Evidence from Thai villages is consistent with these bargaining foundations, showing that agents who are more central indeed receive greater Pareto weight. We conclude by examining the welfare consequences of targeting larger liquid assets to key traders in markets, and to the most liquidity-sensitive links in supply chains.

中文摘要

考虑一个经济，其中代理人面临收入风险，但在一个由随机性和选择共同决定的随机金融网络中互动。我们研究代理人的金融中心性，定义为向该代理人提供少量流动性资产的先验边际社会价值。我们表明，金融中心代理人不仅是那些经常被连接的代理人，而且更有可能在以下情况下被连接：（i）实现的网络是碎片化的，（ii）收入风险高，（iii）冲击是正相关的，（iv）总体风险态度更敏感，以及（v）存在尾部风险。我们将我们的框架应用于具有参与冲击的金融市场模型、易受中断影响的供应链模型以及村庄风险分担网络模型。我们还研究了随机金融网络结构如何影响谈判，从而内生化规划者问题中的帕累托权重。来自泰国村庄的证据与这些谈判基础一致，表明更中心的代理人确实获得了更大的帕累托权重。最后，我们考察了将更大的流动性资产定向分配给市场中的关键交易者以及供应链中对流动性最敏感的环节的福利后果。

Heterogeneity in Disease Resistance and the Impact of Antibiotics in the US.

疾病抵抗力的异质性与美国抗生素的影响

C. Justin Cook, Jason Fletcher

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

We hypothesize that the impact of antibiotics is moderated by a population’s inherent (genetic) resistance to infectious disease. Using the introduction of sulfa drugs in 1937, we show that US states that are more genetically susceptible to infectious disease saw larger declines in their bacterial mortality rates following the introduction of sulfa drugs in 1937. This suggests area-level genetic endowments of disease resistance and the discovery of medical technologies have acted as substitutes in determining levels of health across the US. We also document immediate effects of sulfa drug exposure to the age of the workforce and cumulative effects on educational attainment for cohorts exposed to sulfa drugs in early life.

中文摘要

我们假设抗生素的影响受到人群对传染病固有（遗传）抵抗力的调节。利用1937年磺胺类药物的引入，我们发现，在遗传上更易感染传染病的美国各州，在1937年磺胺类药物引入后，其细菌性死亡率下降幅度更大。这表明，在决定美国各地健康水平方面，地区层面的疾病抵抗力遗传禀赋与医疗技术的发现起到了替代作用。我们还记录了磺胺类药物暴露对劳动力年龄的即时影响，以及对在生命早期暴露于磺胺类药物的队列在教育程度上的累积影响。

The Geography of Child Penalties and Gender Norms: A Pseudo-Event Study Approach

育儿惩罚与性别规范的地理分布：一种伪事件研究方法

Henrik Kleven

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

This paper develops a pseudo-event study approach for estimating child penalties using cross-sectional data. The approach produces estimates that align closely with those obtained from true event studies using panel data and is statistically more precise. This allows for providing more granular evidence and study mechanisms. The paper presents a detailed investigation of the variation in child penalties across time, geography, and demographic/cultural groups in the US. There is large variation in these dimensions. Using a variety of approaches—including epidemiological studies of movers and immigrants—the paper finds that gender norms have sizable effects on child penalties.

中文摘要

本文开发了一种利用横截面数据估计育儿惩罚的伪事件研究方法。该方法产生的估计值与使用面板数据的真实事件研究得出的估计值高度一致，并且在统计上更为精确。这使得能够提供更细粒度的证据并研究机制。本文详细调查了美国育儿惩罚在时间、地理以及人口/文化群体间的变异。在这些维度上存在巨大差异。利用多种方法——包括针对迁移者和移民的流行病学研究——本文发现性别规范对育儿惩罚有显著影响。
