No. 1论文详情NBER w30593

The Electric Ceiling: Limits and Costs of Full Electrification

电气化天花板：全面电气化的限制与成本

David S. Rapson, James B. Bushnell

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Electrification is a centerpiece of global decarbonization efforts. Yet there are reasons to be skeptical of the inevitability, or at least the optimal pace, of the transition. We discuss several under-appreciated costs of full, or even deep, electrification. Consumer preferences can operate in favor of and in opposition to electrification goals; and electrification is likely to encounter physical and economic obstacles when it reaches some as-yet-unknown level. While we readily acknowledge the external benefits of decarbonization, we also explore several under-appreciated external costs. The credibility and eventual success of decarbonization efforts is enhanced by foreseeing and ideally avoiding predictable but non-obvious costs of promising abatement pathways. Thus, even with all of its promise, the degree of electrification may ultimately reach a limit.

中文摘要

电气化是全球脱碳努力的核心。然而，有理由对转型的必然性，或者至少是最优速度持怀疑态度。我们讨论了全面甚至深度电气化的几个被低估的成本。消费者偏好既可能有利于也可能不利于电气化目标；当电气化达到某个未知水平时，它很可能会遇到物理和经济障碍。虽然我们欣然承认脱碳的外部效益，但我们也探讨了几个被低估的外部成本。通过预见并理想地避免有前景的减排路径中可预测但不明显的成本，可以提高脱碳努力的可信度和最终成功。因此，即使电气化前景广阔，其程度最终也可能达到一个极限。

No. 2论文详情NBER w30591

Housing Wealth and Consumption: The Role of Heterogeneous Credit Constraints

住房财富与消费：异质性信贷约束的作用

S. Borağan Aruoba, Ronel Elul, Ṣebnem Kalemli-Özcan

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We quantify the role of heterogeneity in households’ financial constraints in explaining the link between the decline in aggregate consumption and the decline in house values between 2006 and 2009 using individual-level data. Constraints that are triggered by a decline in house values, and a small fraction of consumers facing particularly severe constraints prior to the decline in house values explain 76 percent of the aggregate response. Local general equilibrium feedback and a decline in bank credit to consumers make up the remaining 24 percent. We find no contribution of a pure wealth effect in explaining the consumption decline.

中文摘要

我们利用个体层面数据，量化了家庭财务约束异质性在解释2006年至2009年间总消费大幅下降与房价下跌之间联系中的作用。由房价下跌引发的约束，以及在房价下跌前就面临特别严重约束的一小部分消费者，解释了总消费反应的76%。局部一般均衡反馈和银行对消费者信贷的下降构成了剩余的24%。我们发现纯财富效应对解释消费下降没有贡献。

Global Knowledge and Trade Flows: Theory and Measurement

全球知识与贸易流动：理论与测度

Nelson Lind, Natalia Ramondo

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易

Abstract

We study the global innovation and diffusion of ideas by introducing trade into the model in Eaton and Kortum (1999) (EK). This extension allows us to use international trade flows and country-level factor costs to estimate both the intensity of innovation within countries over time and diffusion rates across countries. We find significant specialization across the globe: some countries have high innovation rates, while other countries rely on diffusion. Although innovation is correlated with economic growth, there are many high income countries that primarily produce using diffused ideas. Additionally, these patterns shift over time — we estimate that a wave of innovation began in China during the early-2000’s, reducing its reliance on diffused technology.

中文摘要

我们通过将贸易引入Eaton和Kortum（1999）（EK）模型，研究了全球思想的创新与扩散。这一扩展使我们能够利用国际贸易流动和国家层面的要素成本，来估计各国随时间变化的创新强度以及跨国扩散率。我们发现全球存在显著的专业化分工：一些国家创新率很高，而另一些国家则依赖技术扩散。尽管创新与经济增长相关，但许多高收入国家主要依靠扩散的思想进行生产。此外，这些模式随时间推移而变化——我们估计，中国在21世纪初开始了一波创新浪潮，从而减少了对扩散技术的依赖。

The Effect of Low-Skill Immigration Restrictions on US Firms and Workers: Evidence from a Randomized Lottery

低技能移民限制对美国企业与工人的影响：来自随机抽签的证据

Michael A. Clemens, Ethan G. Lewis

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

U.S. firms face a binding quota on visas to employ foreign workers in low-skill occupations outside of agriculture. The government allocates this quota to firms in part through a randomized lottery. We evaluate the marginal impact of the quota on firms entering this lottery in 2021 and 2022, using a novel survey and pre-analysis plan. Firms exogenously authorized to employ more immigrants in low-skill jobs significantly increase production (elasticity 0.20–0.22), investment (1.5–2.1), and the rate of profit (0.15). Because the foreign-native elasticity of substitution in production is very low in the policy-relevant occupations (0.8–2.2), the effect on native employment is zero or positive overall, and positive in rural areas. Forensic analysis suggests similarly low substitutability of black-market labor.

中文摘要

美国企业在农业以外的低技能职业中雇用外国工人时，面临具有约束力的签证配额。政府部分通过随机抽签将这一配额分配给企业。我们利用一项新颖的调查和预分析计划，评估了2021年和2022年参与该抽签的企业所受配额的边际影响。被外生授权在低技能岗位雇用更多移民的企业，显著提高了产量（弹性0.20–0.22）、投资（1.5–2.1）和利润率（0.15）。由于在政策相关职业中，外国人与本地人之间的生产替代弹性非常低（0.8–2.2），因此对本地人就业的影响为零或总体为正，在农村地区则为正。法证分析表明，黑市劳动力的可替代性同样很低。

Can Social Media Rhetoric Incite Hate Incidents? Evidence from Trump's "Chinese Virus" Tweets

社交媒体言论能否煽动仇恨事件？来自特朗普“中国病毒”推文的证据

Andy Cao, Jason M. Lindo, Jiee Zhong

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题

Abstract

We investigate whether Donald Trump's "Chinese Virus" tweets contributed to the rise of anti-Asian incidents. We find that the number of incidents spiked following Trump’s initial “Chinese Virus” tweets and the subsequent dramatic rise in internet search activity for the phrase. Difference-in-differences and event-study analyses leveraging spatial variation indicate that this spike in anti-Asian incidents was significantly more pronounced in counties that supported Donald Trump in the 2016 presidential election relative to those that supported Hillary Clinton. We estimate that anti-Asian incidents spiked by 4000 percent in Trump-supporting counties, over and above the spike observed in Clinton-supporting counties.

中文摘要

我们调查了唐纳德·特朗普的“中国病毒”推文是否助长了反亚裔事件的增加。我们发现，在特朗普最初发布“中国病毒”推文以及随后该短语的互联网搜索活动急剧上升之后，反亚裔事件的数量激增。利用空间差异的双重差分和事件研究分析表明，与支持希拉里·克林顿的县相比，在2016年总统选举中支持唐纳德·特朗普的县，反亚裔事件的激增显著更为明显。我们估计，在特朗普支持县，反亚裔事件激增了4000%，这超过了克林顿支持县观察到的激增幅度。

Carbon Emissions Trading and Environmental Protection: International Evidence

碳排放交易与环境保护：国际证据

Jennie Bai, Hong Ru

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We study how the implementation of emissions trading systems (ETS) impacts emissions reductions and the usage of renewable energy using a panel sample of the largest 100 countries worldwide. Exploiting the cross-country variations in ETS implementations, we show that ETS adoption materially reduced greenhouse gas (carbon dioxide) emissions by 12.1% (18.1%). Moreover, ETSs reduced overall emissions by cutting fossil fuel usage, such as coal, by 23.70% while boosting the usage of renewable energy by 61.59%, on average. In contrast, the introduction of carbon taxes has a less effective impact on emissions reduction and fails to boost the usage of renewable energy.

中文摘要

我们利用全球最大100个国家的面板样本，研究了排放交易体系（ETS）的实施如何影响减排和可再生能源的使用。利用各国ETS实施情况的差异，我们表明ETS的采用显著减少了温室气体（二氧化碳）排放，分别减少12.1%（18.1%）。此外，ETS通过削减化石燃料（如煤炭）使用量平均23.70%来降低总体排放，同时将可再生能源的使用量平均提高61.59%。相比之下，碳税的引入对减排的效果较弱，且未能促进可再生能源的使用。

Labor-Market Power, Deadweight Loss, and Technology Adoption

劳动力市场势力、无谓损失与技术采用

Michael Rubens

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Buyer power can induce deadweight loss, but it can also incentivize buyers’ technology adoption by reducing investment holdup. In this paper, I construct a structural model that incorporates these two opposing forces, and use it to quantify the net welfare effects of employers’ market power over their workers. Applying the model to the late-19th-century Illinois coal mining industry, a textbook monopsony example that experienced a large technological shock due to the invention of mechanical cutting machines, I find that an increase in employer power would have induced substantially higher mechanization rates. Assuming exogenous capital investment leads to overestimating the consumer and labor welfare losses from employer power by 13% and 7%.

中文摘要

买方势力可能导致无谓损失，但它也可以通过减少投资套牢问题来激励买方的技术采用。在本文中，我构建了一个包含这两种相反力量的结构模型，并用其量化雇主对工人市场势力的净福利效应。将该模型应用于19世纪末伊利诺伊州的煤炭开采业——一个教科书式的买方垄断案例，该行业因机械切割机的发明而经历了巨大的技术冲击——我发现雇主势力的增强本会导致机械化率大幅提高。假设资本投资是外生的，会导致雇主势力造成的消费者和劳动力福利损失被高估13%和7%。

Information Aggregation and Transmission in Strategic Networks

战略网络中的信息聚合与传播

Fan-chin Kung, Ping Wang

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Observing the increasingly important roles played by the creation and transmission of information and tacit knowledge, we construct an information-network model incorporating both information transmitters and information aggregators. Given information-processing roles in aggregation or transmission, we establish various general properties concerning the existence of a network equilibrium, its optimality and the patterns of equilibrium and optimal configuration. We then allow for endogenous choice of the information-processing roles. We prove the existence and show that, with sufficiently small link maintenance costs, the monocentric network with one aggregator connecting to all other agents as transmitters on a tree graph is the unique configuration. In general, a rich array of equilibrium configurations may emerge, including core-star, star-with-satellites and cycles. We further characterize an information-processing chain network with all information aggregators and transmitters linked along a chain and compute numerically the ranges of transmission decays and link costs within which a network equilibrium arises.

中文摘要

观察到信息和隐性知识的创造与传播所扮演的日益重要的角色，我们构建了一个包含信息传播者和信息聚合者的信息网络模型。给定聚合或传播中的信息处理角色，我们建立了关于网络均衡存在性、其最优性以及均衡和最优配置模式的各种一般性质。然后，我们允许信息处理角色的内生选择。我们证明了存在性，并表明，在链接维护成本足够小的情况下，一个聚合者通过树状图连接到所有其他作为传播者的代理的单中心网络是唯一的配置。一般来说，可能会出现丰富的均衡配置，包括核心星型、带卫星的星型和环型。我们进一步刻画了一个信息处理链网络，其中所有信息聚合者和传播者沿着一条链连接，并数值计算了网络均衡出现的传播衰减和链接成本的范围。

A North-South Model of Structural Change and Growth

结构变迁与增长的南北模型

Maria Aristizabal-Ramirez, John V. Leahy, Linda Tesar

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政

Abstract

This paper is motivated by a set of cross-country observations on growth, structural transformation, and investment rates in a large sample of countries. We observe a hump-shaped relationship between a country's investment rate and its level of development, both within countries over time and across countries. Advanced economies reach their investment peak at a higher level of income and at an earlier point in time relative to emerging markets. We also observe the familiar patterns of structural change (a decline in the agricultural share and an increase in the services share, both relative to manufacturing). The pace of change observed in the 1930 to 1980 period in advanced economies is remarkably similar to that in emerging markets since 1960. We develop a two-region model of the world economy in which regions are isolated from each other up to the point of capital market liberalization in the early 1990s. At that point, capital flows from advanced economies to emerging markets and accelerates the process of structural change in emerging markets. The majority of gains from financial liberalization accrue to emerging economies. We consider the impact of a “second wave” of liberalization when China fully opens its economy to capital inflows.

中文摘要

本文的动机来自对大量国家样本中关于增长、结构转型和投资率的一系列跨国观察。我们观察到，无论是在国家内部随时间推移，还是在国家之间，一国的投资率与其发展水平之间都存在驼峰形关系。与新兴市场相比，发达经济体在更高的收入水平和更早的时间点达到投资峰值。我们还观察到结构变迁的常见模式（农业份额下降和服务业份额上升，两者均相对于制造业）。发达经济体在1930年至1980年期间观察到的变化速度，与新兴市场自1960年以来的变化速度惊人地相似。我们构建了一个世界经济的两区域模型，在该模型中，区域之间在20世纪90年代初资本市场自由化之前是相互隔离的。在自由化时，资本从发达经济体流向新兴市场，加速了新兴市场的结构变迁进程。金融自由化的大部分收益归于新兴经济体。我们考虑了中国完全开放资本流入时“第二波”自由化的影响。

U.S Electric Utility Adaptation to Natural Disasters Shocks and Green Power Mandates

美国电力公用事业对自然灾害冲击与绿色能源指令的适应

Robert Huang, Matthew E. Kahn

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

Access to electricity is a crucial determinant of quality of life and productivity. The United States has a highly reliable electricity grid, but it faces new resilience challenges posed by more intense natural disasters and ambitious green power requirements. Using a US electric utility panel dataset from 2013 to 2020, we document that natural disasters disrupt service, but utilities have made progress in adapting to such shocks. Over the last decade, utilities have faced a tradeoff between achieving local carbon mitigation goals and offering reliable power access. We discuss alternative approaches to attenuate this tradeoff.

中文摘要

电力供应是生活质量和生产力的关键决定因素。美国拥有高度可靠的电网，但面临着由更强烈的自然灾害和雄心勃勃的绿色能源要求所带来的新韧性挑战。利用2013年至2020年的美国电力公用事业面板数据集，我们记录了自然灾害会中断服务，但公用事业在适应此类冲击方面已取得进展。在过去十年中，公用事业在实现本地碳减排目标和提供可靠电力供应之间面临权衡。我们讨论了缓解这种权衡的替代方法。

Councils and Indirect Rule in British Africa

英属非洲的议会与间接统治

Jutta Bolt, Leigh Gardner, Jennifer Kohler, Jack Paine, James A. Robinson

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 英国

Abstract

Did Western colonial rule transform African political institutions? Despite extensive research on indirect rule, we have little systematic evidence about how colonizers’ aims interacted with the structure of indigenous institutions to shape local governance. We explain why colonizers faced incentives to delegate authority to traditionally legitimate institutions, even in historically decentralized areas. Empirically, we analyze originally compiled data on African political institutions in the precolonial and colonial periods for more than 450 subnational units across British Africa. We focus on councils as a form of executive constraints. Subnational councils were widespread, highly correlated with precolonial institutions and patterns of socioeconomic development, and exerted meaningful decision-making powers. These indirect-rule institutions reflected reforms to replace ineffective installed agents. Pressure from below prompted British officials to reintroduce systems of executive constraints based on precolonial models. Our findings contrast with the widespread claim that colonizers could unilaterally implement indirect-rule institutions while disregarding precolonial precedents.
