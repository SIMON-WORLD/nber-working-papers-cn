The Adoption-Substitution Gap: Administrative Evidence from Swedish E-Bike Subsidies

采纳-替代缺口：来自瑞典电动自行车补贴的行政证据

Anders Anderson, Harrison Hong, Eline Jacobs

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 瑞典

Abstract

Policymakers increasingly use green subsidies to advance Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) related to climate, mobility, and health. These programs assume a causal chain: subsidies drive technology adoption, which induces behavioral substitution that then generates sustainability impact. We test this mechanism in the context of a nationwide e-bike subsidy program in Sweden, focusing on the adoption-substitution gap—the friction between acquiring green technology and displacing high-carbon behaviors. Linking administrative data on all 90,000 recipients to vehicle registry and insurance records, we find that while the subsidy successfully doubled e-bike sales with complete price pass-through, behavioral substitution was minimal. Objective vehicle-kilometers traveled (VKT) changed marginally, leading to negligible emissions reductions and health gains. Notably, survey-based substitution estimates within the same population substantially overstate realized reductions, suggesting a reporting bias in stated-preference data. Our findings highlight the necessity of targeting green subsidies toward populations whose behavior is most elastic to ensure cost-effective climate and mobility outcomes.

中文摘要

政策制定者越来越多地使用绿色补贴来推进与气候、出行和健康相关的可持续发展目标。这些项目假设了一个因果链：补贴推动技术采纳，进而引发行为替代，最终产生可持续性影响。我们在瑞典全国性电动自行车补贴项目的背景下检验了这一机制，重点关注采纳-替代缺口——即获取绿色技术与替代高碳行为之间的摩擦。通过将涵盖所有9万名受助者的行政数据与车辆登记和保险记录相关联，我们发现，尽管补贴成功地将电动自行车销量翻了一番，且价格完全传导，但行为替代微乎其微。客观的车辆行驶里程变化很小，导致减排和健康收益可以忽略不计。值得注意的是，在同一人群中基于调查的替代估计值大大高估了实际减少量，这表明陈述偏好数据中存在报告偏差。我们的研究结果强调了将绿色补贴瞄准行为弹性最大的人群的必要性，以确保气候和出行目标的成本效益。

Dads and Daughters: Disentangling Altruism and Investment Motives for Spending on Children

父亲与女儿：区分子女支出的利他动机与投资动机

Rebecca Dizon-Ross, Seema Jayachandran

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 乌干达

Abstract

This paper tests whether mothers and fathers differ in their spending on their daughters relative to their sons. We compare mothers’ and fathers’ willingness to pay (WTP) for specific goods for their children, diverging from the previous literature’s approach of comparing the expenditure effects of mothers’ versus fathers’ income. Our method, which we apply in Uganda, allows us to estimate gender differences and explore mechanisms with greater precision. A second innovation is that we examine why spending patterns differ between mothers and fathers, e.g., altruism, personal returns to investing in children. We find that fathers have a lower WTP for their daughters’ human capital than their sons’ human capital, whereas mothers do not. We also find evidence that altruism plays a role in the mother-father differences: fathers’ WTP for goods that simply bring joy to their daughters is lower than their WTP for such goods for their sons, but mothers’ is not.

中文摘要

本文检验了母亲和父亲在女儿与儿子身上的支出是否存在差异。我们比较了母亲和父亲为子女购买特定商品的支付意愿，这与以往文献比较母亲收入与父亲收入对支出影响的方法不同。我们在乌干达应用了这种方法，从而能够更精确地估计性别差异并探索其机制。第二个创新之处在于，我们考察了母亲和父亲支出模式不同的原因，例如利他主义、投资子女的个人回报。我们发现，父亲对女儿人力资本的支付意愿低于对儿子人力资本的支付意愿，而母亲则没有这种差异。我们还发现证据表明，利他主义在母亲与父亲的差异中发挥了作用：父亲对仅为女儿带来快乐的商品的支付意愿低于对儿子此类商品的支付意愿，但母亲则不然。

Aggregating Partial Rankings from Neighbors: Methodology and Empirical Evidence

聚合来自邻居的偏序：方法与实证证据

Pascaline Dupas, Marcel Fafchamps, Deivy Houeix

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Many decisions require ordering alternatives: for example, the selection of top candidates for a competitive academic program or the selection of the poorest individuals for a cash transfer program. One common approach consists in aggregating orderings reported by different observers (e.g., committee or community members), but those orderings are typically partial: not all observers rank all applicants. We introduce a novel type of approach, based on pairwise rankings, to (i) aggregate partial orderings reported by multiple observers and (ii) construct confidence intervals for the resulting aggregate ordering. We identify, both theoretically and using simulations, the conditions under which a pairwise approach dominates rank averaging: when reporting error is low, reported orderings are partial, and observers rank alternatives that are close to each other in their true latent ordering. We introduce improvements to rank averaging and pairwise methods and illustrate them using several datasets. We find that, with partial reported orderings, Borda counts (i.e., simple rank averages) are dominated by the averaging of normalized ranks and should never be used in practice.

中文摘要

许多决策需要对备选方案进行排序：例如，为竞争性学术项目选拔顶尖候选人，或为现金转移项目选择最贫困的个人。一种常见的方法是聚合不同观察者（例如委员会或社区成员）报告的排序，但这些排序通常是偏序的：并非所有观察者都对所有申请者进行排序。我们引入了一种基于成对排序的新型方法，用于（i）聚合多个观察者报告的偏序，以及（ii）为由此产生的聚合排序构建置信区间。我们通过理论和模拟确定了成对方法优于排序平均法的条件：当报告误差较低、报告排序为偏序、且观察者对真实潜在排序中彼此接近的备选方案进行排序时。我们引入了对排序平均法和成对方法的改进，并使用多个数据集进行了说明。我们发现，在报告偏序的情况下，博达计数（即简单的排序平均值）被归一化排序的平均值所主导，在实践中不应使用。

A labor market view on the risks of a U.S. hard landing

从劳动力市场视角看美国硬着陆的风险

Alex Domash, Lawrence H. Summers

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

This paper uses historical labor market data to assess the plausibility that the Federal Reserve can engineer a soft landing for the economy. We first show that the labor market today is significantly tighter than implied by the unemployment rate: the vacancy and quit rates currently experienced in the United States correspond to a degree of labor market tightness previously associated with sub-2 percent unemployment rates. We highlight that the super-tight labor market coincides with current wage inflation of 6.5 percent – the highest level experienced in the past 40 years – and that firm-side slack measures predict further increases in wage inflation over the coming year. Finally, we show that high levels of wage inflation have historically been associated with a substantial risk of a recession over the next one to two years. We argue that periods that historically have been hailed as successful soft landings have little in common with the present moment. Our results suggest a very low likelihood that the Federal Reserve can reduce inflation without causing a significant slowdown in economic activity.

中文摘要

本文利用历史劳动力市场数据来评估美联储实现经济软着陆的可能性。我们首先表明，当前的劳动力市场比失业率所暗示的要紧张得多：美国目前经历的职位空缺率和离职率，对应于以往与低于2%的失业率相关的劳动力市场紧张程度。我们强调，这种超紧张的劳动力市场恰逢当前6.5%的工资通胀率——这是过去40年来的最高水平——并且企业侧的闲置指标预示着未来一年工资通胀将进一步上升。最后，我们表明，历史上高水平的工资通胀与未来一到两年内发生衰退的巨大风险相关。我们认为，历史上被誉为成功软着陆的时期与当前时刻几乎没有共同之处。我们的结果表明，美联储在不导致经济活动显著放缓的情况下降低通胀的可能性非常低。

Housing Market Expectations

住房市场预期

Theresa Kuchler, Monika Piazzesi, Johannes Stroebel

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We review the recent literature on the determinants and effects of housing market expectations. We begin by providing an overview of existing surveys that elicit housing market expectations, and discuss how those surveys may be expanded in the future. We then document a number of facts about time-series and cross-sectional patterns of housing market expectations in these survey data, before summarizing research that has studied how individuals form these expectations. Housing market expectations are strongly influenced by recently observed house price changes, by personally or locally observed house price changes, by house price changes observed in a person's social network, and by current home ownership status. Similarly, experienced house price volatility affects expectations uncertainty. We also summarize recent work that documents how differences in housing market expectations translate into differences in individuals’ housing market behaviors, including their home purchasing and mortgage financing decisions. Finally, we highlight research on how expectations affect aggregate outcomes in the housing market.

中文摘要

我们回顾了近期关于住房市场预期决定因素和影响的文献。我们首先概述了现有的调查，这些调查引出了住房市场预期，并讨论了未来如何扩展这些调查。然后，我们记录了这些调查数据中住房市场预期的时间序列和横截面模式的一系列事实，然后总结了研究个体如何形成这些预期的研究。住房市场预期受到近期观察到的房价变化、个人或本地观察到的房价变化、个人社交网络中观察到的房价变化以及当前住房所有权状况的强烈影响。同样，经历过的房价波动会影响预期的不确定性。我们还总结了近期的工作，这些工作记录了住房市场预期的差异如何转化为个体住房市场行为的差异，包括他们的购房和抵押贷款融资决策。最后，我们重点介绍了关于预期如何影响住房市场总体结果的研究。

Estimating General Equilibrium Spillovers of Large-Scale Shocks

估计大规模冲击的一般均衡溢出效应

Kilian Huber

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Large-scale shocks directly affect some firms and households and indirectly affect others through general equilibrium spillovers. In this paper, I describe how researchers can directly estimate spillovers using quasi-experimental or experimental variation. I then argue that spillover estimates suffer from distinct sources of mechanical bias that standard empirical tools cannot resolve. These biases are particularly relevant in finance and macroeconomics, where multiple spillover channels and nonlinear effects are common. I offer guidance on how to detect and overcome mechanical biases. An application and several examples highlight that the suggested methods are broadly relevant and can inform policy and multiplier calculations.

中文摘要

大规模冲击直接影响一些企业和家庭，并通过一般均衡溢出效应间接影响其他企业和家庭。在本文中，我描述了研究人员如何利用准实验或实验变异直接估计溢出效应。然后，我论证了溢出估计值会受到标准实证工具无法解决的独特机械性偏差的影响。这些偏差在金融和宏观经济学中尤其相关，因为在这些领域中，多重溢出渠道和非线性效应很常见。我提供了关于如何检测和克服机械性偏差的指导。一个应用和几个例子强调了所建议的方法具有广泛的相关性，并且可以为政策和乘数计算提供信息。

Transferable Skills? Founders as Venture Capitalists

可转移技能？创始人作为风险投资家

Paul A. Gompers, Vladimir Mukharlyamov

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In this paper we explore whether or not the experience as a founder of a venture capital-backed startup influences the performance of founders who become venture capitalists (VCs). We find that nearly 7% of VCs were previously founders of a venture-backed startup. Having a successful exit and being male and white increase the probability that a founder transitions into a venture capital career. Successful founder-VCs have investment success rates that are 6.5 percentage points higher than professional VCs while unsuccessful founder-VCs have investment success rates that are 4 percentage points lower than professional VCs. While successful founder-VCs do get higher quality deal flow than professional or unsuccessful founder-VCs, observably higher deal quality does not explain the entire difference in performance. Using an instrumental variables approach to separate unobservable deal quality from value-add, we find that the outperformance of successful founder-VCs is consistent with them adding more value post-investment.

中文摘要

在本文中，我们探讨了作为风险投资支持初创企业创始人的经历是否会影响那些成为风险投资家的创始人的业绩。我们发现，近7%的风险投资家曾是风险投资支持初创企业的创始人。成功退出以及身为男性和白人会增加创始人转型为风险投资家的概率。成功的创始人-风险投资家的投资成功率比职业风险投资家高6.5个百分点，而不成功的创始人-风险投资家的投资成功率比职业风险投资家低4个百分点。虽然成功的创始人-风险投资家确实比职业风险投资家或不成功的创始人-风险投资家获得了更高质量的交易流，但可观察到的更高交易质量并不能解释全部业绩差异。使用工具变量方法来分离不可观察的交易质量与增值，我们发现，成功的创始人-风险投资家的优异表现与其在投资后增加更多价值是一致的。

Economics of Foster Care

寄养经济学

Anthony Bald, Joseph J. Doyle Jr., Max Gross, Brian Jacob

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

Foster care provides substitute living arrangements to protect maltreated children. The practice is remarkably common: it is estimated that 5 percent of children in the United States are placed in foster care at some point during childhood. These children exhibit poor outcomes as children and adults, and economists have begun to estimate the causal relationship between foster care and life outcomes. This paper describes tradeoffs in child welfare policy and provides background on the latest trends in foster care practice to highlight areas most in need of rigorous evidence. These trends include efforts to prevent foster care on the demand side and to improve foster home recruitment on the supply side. With increasing data availability and a growing interest in evidence-based practices, there are opportunities for economic research to inform policies that protect vulnerable children.

中文摘要

寄养提供替代性居住安排以保护受虐待的儿童。这种做法非常普遍：据估计，美国有5%的儿童在童年时期的某个阶段被安置在寄养家庭中。这些儿童在儿童期和成年期的结果都很差，经济学家已经开始估计寄养与生活结果之间的因果关系。本文描述了儿童福利政策中的权衡，并提供了寄养实践最新趋势的背景，以突出最需要严格证据的领域。这些趋势包括在需求侧努力预防寄养，以及在供给侧努力改善寄养家庭的招募。随着数据可用性的提高和对循证实践兴趣的增长，经济研究有机会为保护弱势儿童的政策提供信息。

Estimation of a Life-Cycle Model with Human Capital, Labor Supply and Retirement

包含人力资本、劳动供给与退休的生命周期模型估计

Xiaodong Fan, Ananth Seshadri, Christopher R. Taber

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop and estimate a life-cycle model in which individuals make decisions about consumption, human capital investment, and labor supply and use it to analyze changes in Social Security rules. The most important aspect of our paper is human capital towards the end of the life cycle which responds to changes in the rules. Retirement arises endogenously as part of the labor supply decision. The model allows for both an endogenous wage process through human capital investment (which is typically assumed exogenous in the retirement literature), an endogenous retirement decision (which is typically assumed exogenous in the human capital literature), and accounts for the Social Security system. We estimate the model using indirect inference to match the life-cycle profiles of employment and measured wages from the SIPP data. The model replicates the main features of the data—in particular the large increase in measured wages and small increase in labor supply at the beginning of the life cycle as well as the small decrease in measured wages but large decrease in labor supply at the end of the life cycle. We use the model to estimate the effects of various changes to tax and Social Security policies and show that allowing for human capital accumulation is critical.

中文摘要

我们构建并估计了一个生命周期模型，其中个体对消费、人力资本投资和劳动供给做出决策，并利用该模型分析社会保障规则的变化。我们论文最重要的方面是生命周期末期的人力资本，它会对规则变化做出反应。退休作为劳动供给决策的一部分内生于模型。该模型同时允许通过人力资本投资形成的内生工资过程（这在退休文献中通常被假定为外生）、内生的退休决策（这在人力资本文献中通常被假定为外生），并考虑了社会保障体系。我们使用间接推断方法估计模型，以匹配来自SIPP数据的就业和测量工资的生命周期轮廓。该模型复制了数据的主要特征——特别是生命周期初期测量工资的大幅增长和劳动供给的小幅增长，以及生命周期末期测量工资的小幅下降但劳动供给的大幅下降。我们利用该模型估计税收和社会保障政策各种变化的影响，并表明考虑人力资本积累至关重要。

Zombie Lending: Theoretical, International and Historical Perspectives

僵尸贷款：理论、国际与历史视角

Viral V. Acharya, Matteo Crosignani, Tim Eisert, Sascha Steffen

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

This paper surveys the theory on zombie lending incentives and the consequences of zombie lending for the real economy. It also offers a historical perspective by reviewing the growing empirical evidence on zombie lending along three dimensions: (i) the role of under-capitalized banks, (ii) effects on zombie firms, and (iii) spillovers and distortions for non-zombie firms. We then provide an overview of how zombie lending can be attenuated. Finally, we use a sample of U.S. publicly listed firms to compare various measures proposed in the literature to classify firms as "zombies." Andreas Fagereng, Luigi Guiso, Luigi Pistaferri

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 挪威

Abstract

We use population data on capital income and wealth holdings for Norway to measure asset positions and wealth returns before individuals marry and after the household is formed. These data allow us to establish a number of novel facts. First, individuals sort on personal wealth rather than parents' wealth. Assortative mating on own wealth dominates, and in fact renders assortative mating on parental wealth statistically insignificant. Second, people match also on their personal returns to wealth and assortative mating on returns is as strong as that on wealth. Third, post-marriage returns on family wealth are largely explained by the return of the spouse with the highest pre-marriage return. This suggests that family wealth is largely managed by the spouse with the highest potential to grow it. This is particularly true for households at the top of the wealth distribution at marriage. We use a simple analytical example to illustrate how assortative mating on wealth and returns and wealth management task allocation between spouses affect wealth inequality.

Designing Stress Scenarios

设计压力情景

Cecilia Parlatore, Thomas Philippon

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop a tractable framework to study the optimal design of stress scenarios. A principal wants to manage the unknown risk exposures of a set of agents. She asks the agents to report their losses under hypothetical scenarios before mandating actions to mitigate the exposures. We show how to apply a Kalman filter to solve the learning problem and we characterize the scenario design as a function of the risk environment, the principal’s preferences, and the available remedial actions. We apply our results to banking stress tests. We show how the principal learns from estimated losses under different scenarios and across different banks. Optimal capital requirements are set to cover losses under an adverse scenario while targeted interventions depend on the covariance between residual exposure uncertainty and physical risks.

Abstract

Measured as yield spreads against AAA corporate bonds, the convenience premium for agency MBS averaged 47 basis points between 1995 and 2021, about half of the long-term-Treasury convenience premium. Both the MBS convenience premium and the issuance amount vary negatively with the mortgage rate, which is consistent with a prepayment-driven demand channel. This negative dependence contrasts strikingly with the positive dependence of the MBS-repo convenience premium on interest rates, as implied by the “opportunity cost of money” hypothesis. Abstract

Farmers have higher rates of disability and illness than the general population and more volatile incomes due to frequent crop and livestock losses from extreme weather events. Abstract

We explore a model of non-Bayesian information aggregation in networks. Agents non-cooperatively choose among Friedkin-Johnsen type aggregation rules to maximize payoffs. The DeGroot rule is chosen in equilibrium if and only if there is noiseless information transmission, leading to consensus. Inequality and Income Dynamics in Germany

德国的收入不平等与收入动态

Moritz Drechsel-Grau, Andreas Peichl, Johannes F. Schmieder, Kai D. Schmid, Hannes Walz, Stefanie Wolter

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 德国

Abstract

We provide a comprehensive analysis of income inequality and income dynamics for Germany over the last two decades. Combining personal income tax and social security data allows us – for the first time – to offer a complete picture of the distribution of annual earnings in Germany. We find that cross-sectional inequality rose until 2009 for men and women. After the Great Recession inequality continued to rise at a slower rate for men and fell slightly for women due to compression at the lower tail. We further document substantial gender differences in average earnings and inequality over the life-cycle. While for men earnings rise and inequality falls as they grow older, many women reduce working hours when starting a family such that average earnings fall and inequality increases. Men’s earnings changes are on average smaller than women’s but are substantially more affected by the business cycle. During the Great Recession, men’s earnings losses become magnified and gains are attenuated. Apart from recession years, earnings changes are significantly right-skewed reflecting the good overall state of the German labor market and increasing labor supply. In the second part of the paper, we study the distribution of total income including incomes of self-employed, business owners, and landlords. We find that total inequality increased significantly more than earnings inequality. Regarding income dynamics, entrepreneurs’ income changes are more dispersed, less skewed, less leptokurtic and less dependent on average past income than workers’ income changes. Finally, we find that top income earners have become less likely to fall out of the top 1 and 0.1 percent.
