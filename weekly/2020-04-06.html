Aggregate and Firm-Level Stock Returns During Pandemics, in Real Time

大流行期间的总量和公司层面股票回报：实时视角

Laura Alfaro, Anusha Chari, Andrew N. Greenland, Peter K. Schott

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We show that unexpected changes in the trajectory of COVID-19 infections predict US stock returns, in real time. Parameter estimates indicate that an unanticipated doubling (halving) of projected infections forecasts next-day decreases (increases) in aggregate US market value of 4 to 11 percent, indicating that equity markets may begin to rebound even as infections continue to rise, if the trajectory of the disease becomes less severe than initially anticipated. Using the same variation in unanticipated projected cases, we find that COVID-19-related losses in market value at the firm level rise with capital intensity and leverage, and are deeper in industries more conducive to disease transmission. These relationships provide important insight into current record job losses. Measuring US states' drops in market value as the employment weighted average declines of the industries they produce, we find that states with milder drops in market value exhibit larger initial jobless claims per worker. This initially counter-intuitive result suggests that investors value the relative ease with which labor versus capital costs can be shed as revenues decline.\n

中文摘要

我们表明，COVID-19感染轨迹的意外变化可以实时预测美国股票回报。参数估计表明，预期感染人数的意外翻倍（减半）预示着美国整体市场价值次日下降（上升）4%至11%，这表明即使感染人数继续上升，如果疾病轨迹变得比最初预期的温和，股票市场也可能开始反弹。利用相同的意外预期病例变化，我们发现公司层面因COVID-19导致的市场价值损失随着资本密集度和杠杆率而上升，并且在更有利于疾病传播的行业中更深。这些关系为当前创纪录的失业提供了重要见解。通过衡量美国各州市场价值下降（作为其生产行业的就业加权平均下降），我们发现市场价值下降较温和的州，每位工人的初始失业救济申请数量更大。这个最初反直觉的结果表明，投资者看重的是，当收入下降时，劳动力成本相对于资本成本更容易被削减。

How Does Household Spending Respond to an Epidemic? Consumption During the 2020 COVID-19 Pandemic

家庭支出如何应对流行病？2020年COVID-19大流行期间的消费

Scott R. Baker, R.A. Farrokhnia, Steffen Meyer, Michaela Pagel, Constantine Yannelis

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We explore how household consumption responds to epidemics, utilizing transaction-level household financial data to investigate the impact of the COVID-19 virus. As the number of cases grew, households began to radically alter their typical spending across a number of major categories. Initially spending increased sharply, particularly in retail, credit card spending and food items. This was followed by a sharp decrease in overall spending. Households responded most strongly in states with shelter-in-place orders in place by March 29th. We explore heterogeneity across partisan affiliation, demographics and income. Greater levels of social distancing are associated with drops in spending, particularly in restaurants and retail.

How Many Jobs Can be Done at Home?

有多少工作可以在家完成？

Jonathan I. Dingel, Brent Neiman

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Evaluating the economic impact of "social distancing" measures taken to arrest the spread of COVID-19 raises a fundamental question about the modern economy: how many jobs can be performed at home? We classify the feasibility of working at home for all occupations and merge this classification with occupational employment counts. We find that 37 percent of jobs in the United States can be performed entirely at home, with significant variation across cities and industries. These jobs typically pay more than jobs that cannot be done at home and account for 46 percent of all US wages. Applying our occupational classification to 85 other countries reveals that lower-income economies have a lower share of jobs that can be done at home.

评估为遏制COVID-19传播而采取的“社交距离”措施的经济影响，引发了一个关于现代经济的基本问题：有多少工作可以在家完成？我们对所有职业在家工作的可行性进行了分类，并将此分类与职业就业人数相结合。我们发现，美国37%的工作可以完全在家完成，不同城市和行业之间存在显著差异。这些工作的薪酬通常高于无法在家完成的工作，并占美国所有工资总额的46%。将我们的职业分类应用于其他85个国家，结果显示，低收入经济体中可在家完成的工作比例较低。

The Impact of COVID-19 on Gender Equality

COVID-19对性别平等的影响

Titan Alon, Matthias Doepke, Jane Olmstead-Rumsey, Michèle Tertilt

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The economic downturn caused by the current COVID-19 outbreak has substantial implications for gender equality, both during the downturn and the subsequent recovery. Compared to “regular” recessions, which affect men’s employment more severely than women’s employment, the employment drop related to social distancing measures has a large impact on sectors with high female employment shares. In addition, closures of schools and daycare centers have massively increased child care needs, which has a particularly large impact on working mothers. The effects of the crisis on working mothers are likely to be persistent, due to high returns to experience in the labor market. Beyond the immediate crisis, there are opposing forces which may ultimately promote gender equality in the labor market. First, businesses are rapidly adopting flexible work arrangements, which are likely to persist. Second, there are also many fathers who now have to take primary responsibility for child care, which may erode social norms that currently lead to a lopsided distribution of the division of labor in house work and child care.

中文摘要

当前COVID-19疫情引发的经济衰退，在衰退期间及随后的复苏过程中，都对性别平等产生了重大影响。与“常规”衰退（对男性就业的影响比女性更严重）相比，与社交距离措施相关的就业下降对女性就业份额高的行业影响巨大。此外，学校和日托中心的关闭极大地增加了儿童保育需求，这对在职母亲的影响尤为显著。由于劳动力市场经验的回报率很高，危机对在职母亲的影响可能持续存在。在危机之外，存在一些可能最终促进劳动力市场性别平等的相反力量。首先，企业正在迅速采用灵活的工作安排，这些安排可能会持续下去。其次，现在许多父亲不得不承担起儿童保育的主要责任，这可能会侵蚀当前导致家务劳动和儿童保育分工不平衡的社会规范。

The Unprecedented Stock Market Impact of COVID-19

COVID-19前所未有的股市影响

Scott R. Baker, Nicholas Bloom, Steven J. Davis, Kyle J. Kost, Marco C. Sammon, Tasaneeya Viratyosin

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 西班牙、美国

Abstract

No previous infectious disease outbreak, including the Spanish Flu, has impacted the stock market as forcefully as the COVID-19 pandemic. In fact, previous pandemics left only mild traces on the U.S. stock market. We use text-based methods to develop these points with respect to large daily stock market moves back to 1900 and with respect to overall stock market volatility back to 1985. We also evaluate potential explanations for the unprecedented stock market reaction to the COVID-19 pandemic. The evidence we amass suggests that government restrictions on commercial activity and voluntary social distancing, operating with powerful effects in a service-oriented economy, are the main reasons the U.S. stock market reacted so much more forcefully to COVID-19 than to previous pandemics in 1918-19, 1957-58 and 1968.

中文摘要

以往任何传染病疫情，包括西班牙流感，都没有像COVID-19大流行那样对股市产生如此强烈的影响。事实上，以往的疫情只在美国股市留下了轻微的痕迹。我们使用基于文本的方法，针对1900年以来股市的大幅日波动以及1985年以来的整体股市波动率，阐述了这些观点。我们还评估了对COVID-19大流行导致股市出现前所未有的反应的可能解释。我们收集的证据表明，政府对商业活动的限制和自愿的社交距离，在一个以服务为导向的经济中发挥着强大的作用，是美国股市对COVID-19的反应比以往1918-19年、1957-58年和1968年疫情强烈得多的主要原因。

Quitting in Protest: Presidential Policymaking and Civil Service Response

以辞职抗议：总统决策与公务员回应

Charles Cameron, John M. de Figueiredo

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We formally model the impact of presidential policymaking on the willingness of bureaucrats to exert effort and stay in the government. In the model, centralized policy initiative by the president demotivates policy-oriented bureaucrats and can impel them to quit rather than implicate themselves in presidentially imposed policies they dislike. Those most likely to quit are a range of moderate bureaucrats. More extreme bureaucrats may be willing to wait out an incumbent president in the hope of shaping future policy. As control of the White House alternates between ideologically opposed extreme presidents, policy-minded moderates depart from bureaucratic agencies leaving only policy extremists or poorly performing "slackers." The consequences for policy making are substantial. Despite these adverse consequences, presidents have strong incentives to engage in centralized policymaking.

中文摘要

我们正式建模分析了总统决策对公务员努力意愿和留任政府意愿的影响。在该模型中，总统的集中政策倡议会削弱以政策为导向的公务员的积极性，并可能促使他们辞职，而不是卷入他们不喜欢的总统强加的政策中。最有可能辞职的是一系列温和派公务员。更极端的公务员可能愿意等待现任总统下台，以期塑造未来的政策。当白宫控制权在意识形态对立的极端总统之间交替时，以政策为导向的温和派会离开官僚机构，只留下政策极端分子或表现不佳的“懒散者”。这对政策制定的影响是巨大的。尽管存在这些不利后果，总统仍有强烈的动机进行集中决策。

The Murder-Suicide of the Rentier: Population Aging and the Risk Premium

食利者的谋杀式自杀：人口老龄化与风险溢价

Joseph Kopecky, Alan M. Taylor

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Population aging has been linked to global declines in real interest rates. A similar trend is seen for equity risk premia, which are on the rise. An existing literature can explain part of the declining trend in safe rates using demographics, but has no mechanism to speak to trends in relative returns on different assets. We calibrate a heterogeneous agent life-cycle model with equity markets and aggregate risk, and we show that aging demographics can simultaneously account for both the majority of a downward trend in the risk free rate, while also increasing the return premium attached to risky assets. This is because the life-cycle savings dynamics that have been well documented exert less pressure on risky assets as older households shift away from risk. Under reasonable calibrations we find declines in the safe rate that are considerably larger than most existing estimates between the years 1990 and 2017. We are also able to account for most of the rise in the equity risk premium. Projecting forward to 2050 we show that persistent demographic forces will continue push the risk free rate further into negative territory, while the equity risk premium remains elevated.

中文摘要

人口老龄化与全球实际利率的下降有关。股票风险溢价也呈现出类似的趋势，但方向相反，即正在上升。现有文献可以用人口统计学解释安全利率下降趋势的一部分，但无法解释不同资产相对回报率的趋势。我们校准了一个包含股票市场和总体风险的异质性主体生命周期模型，并表明老龄化的人口结构可以同时解释无风险利率下降趋势的大部分，同时增加风险资产的回报溢价。这是因为，随着老年家庭远离风险，已有充分记录的生命周期储蓄动态对风险资产施加的压力较小。在合理的校准下，我们发现1990年至2017年间安全利率的下降幅度远大于大多数现有估计。我们还能够解释股票风险溢价上升的大部分原因。展望2050年，我们表明持续的人口力量将继续推动无风险利率进一步进入负值区域，而股票风险溢价仍将居高不下。

Is the Social Safety Net a Long-Term Investment? Large-Scale Evidence from the Food Stamps Program

社会安全网是长期投资吗？来自食品券计划的大规模证据

Martha J. Bailey, Hilary W. Hoynes, Maya Rossin-Slater, Reed Walker

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

We use novel, large-scale data on 43 million Americans from the 2000 Census and the 2001 to 2013 American Communities Survey linked to the Social Security Administration’s NUMIDENT to study how a policy-driven increase in economic resources for families affects children’s long-term outcomes. Using variation from the county-level roll-out of the Food Stamps program between 1961 and 1975, we find that children with access to greater economic resources before age five experience an increase of 6 percent of a standard deviation in their adult human capital, 3 percent of a standard deviation in their adult economic self-sufficiency, 8 percent of a standard deviation in the quality of their adult neighborhoods, 0.4 percentage-point increase in longevity, and a 0.5 percentage-point decrease in likelihood of being incarcerated. Based on these estimates, we conclude that Food Stamps’ transfer of resources to families is a highly cost-effective investment into young children, yielding a marginal value of public funds of approximately 56.

The Saving Glut of the Rich

富人的储蓄过剩

Atif R. Mian, Ludwig Straub, Amir Sufi

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper develops a new unveiling methodology to trace distributional household saving through the financial system and applies it to tax records from 1963 to 2019. Since the 1980s, saving by the top 1% has surged, creating a saving glut of the rich comparable in scale to the global saving glut and equally influential in driving the demand for safe assets. Unveiling the financial sector reveals that this glut financed much of the rise in middle-class borrowing before 2008 and the expansion of federal debt thereafter. 纽约市被恰当地描述为美国冠状病毒大流行的中心。在该市报告首例冠状病毒感染病例仅一个月后，出现COVID-19严重并发症的感染者负担已超过该市许多医院的承载能力。与大多数大流行一样，科学家和公职人员没有关于新感染路径的完整、准确、实时数据。尽管存在这些数据不足，但已有足够证据表明纽约市的曲线确实在趋平。本报告的目的是阐述支持——和反对——这一初步但可能重要结论的证据。在审查证据后，我们接着探究：如果曲线确实在趋平，我们是否知道是什么导致了它趋于平稳？
