The Children of HOPE VI Demolitions: National Evidence on Labor Market Outcomes

HOPE VI 拆除项目的儿童：关于劳动力市场结果的全国性证据

John C. Haltiwanger, Mark J. Kutzbach, Giordano E. Palloni, Henry Pollakowski, Matthew Staiger, Daniel Weinberg

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We combine national administrative data on earnings and participation in subsidized housing to study how the demolition of 160 public housing projects—funded by the HOPE VI program—affected the adult labor market outcomes for 18,500 children. Our empirical strategy compares children exposed to the program to children drawn from thousands of non-demolished projects, adjusting for observable differences using a flexible estimator that combines features of matching and regression. We find that children who resided in HOPE VI projects earn 14% more at age 26 relative to children in comparable non-HOPE VI projects. These earnings gains are strongest for demolitions in large cities, particularly in neighborhoods with higher pre-demolition poverty rates and lower pre-demolition job accessibility. There is no evidence that the labor market gains are driven by improvements in household or neighborhood environments that promote human capital development in children. Rather, subsequent improvements in job accessibility represent a likely pathway for the results.

中文摘要

我们结合了关于收入与补贴住房参与的全国行政数据，研究了由 HOPE VI 计划资助的 160 个公共住房项目的拆除如何影响 18,500 名儿童的成年劳动力市场结果。我们的实证策略将接触该计划的儿童与来自数千个未拆除项目的儿童进行比较，并使用一种结合匹配和回归特征的灵活估计器来调整可观测差异。我们发现，与居住在可比非 HOPE VI 项目中的儿童相比，居住在 HOPE VI 项目中的儿童在 26 岁时的收入高出 14%。这些收入增长在大型城市中最为显著，尤其是在拆除前贫困率较高且拆除前就业可达性较低的社区。没有证据表明劳动力市场的改善是由促进儿童人力资本发展的家庭或社区环境改善所驱动的。相反，随后就业可达性的改善是这些结果的一个可能途径。

ESG Preference, Institutional Trading, and Stock Return Patterns

ESG 偏好、机构交易与股票回报模式

Jie Cao, Sheridan Titman, Xintong Zhan, Weiming Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Socially responsible (SR) institutions tend to focus more on the ESG performance and less on quantitative signals of value. Consistent with this difference in focus, we find that SR institutions react less to quantitative mispricing signals. Our evidence suggests that the increased focus on ESG may have influenced stock return patterns. Specifically, abnormal returns associated with these mispricing signals are greater for stocks held more by SR institutions. The link between SR ownership and the efficacy of mispricing signals only emerges in recent years with the rise of ESG investing, and is significant only when there are arbitrage-related funding constraints.

中文摘要

社会责任型机构往往更关注 ESG 表现，而较少关注价值的量化信号。与这种关注差异一致，我们发现社会责任型机构对量化错误定价信号的反应较弱。我们的证据表明，对 ESG 日益增长的关注可能影响了股票回报模式。具体来说，与这些错误定价信号相关的异常回报在那些被社会责任型机构持有更多的股票中更大。社会责任型所有权与错误定价信号有效性之间的联系仅在近年来随着 ESG 投资的兴起才出现，并且仅在存在与套利相关的资金约束时才显著。

How Does Cost-Sharing Impact Spending Growth and Cost-Effective Treatments? Evidence from Deductibles

成本分摊如何影响支出增长和成本效益治疗？来自免赔额的证据

Claudio Lucarelli, Molly Frean, Aliza S. Gordon, Lynn M. Hua, Mark Pauly

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The growth of health care spending has been a longstanding policy concern. Over the years, several innovations have been proposed to lower levels of health care spending; however, their impact has been limited and not sustained over time. Costly new technology, while often an improvement to existing care, has been identified as a principal driver of health care spending growth. Recent literature has shown that high deductible health plans (HDHP) can have an immediate impact on levels of health care spending, but their medium- and long-run effects on spending growth remain unknown. In this paper, we use multiple-employer-group claims data from a large national insurer to (i) study whether HDHPs reduce the growth in spending over four years compared to lower deductible alternatives; and (ii) explore the mechanisms behind any reductions in growth by looking at whether HDHPs reduce the use of low- vs. high-value treatments. We find that HDHPs have a limited effect on spending growth, with a statistically significant reduction observed only for prescription drugs. HDHPs are not associated with significantly lower growth in spending on highly cost-effective medicines in a sample of drugs but do reduce spending growth for less cost-effective drugs.

中文摘要

医疗保健支出的增长一直是一个长期的政策关注点。多年来，提出了若干创新措施以降低医疗保健支出水平；然而，它们的影响有限且未能持续。昂贵的新技术，虽然通常是对现有护理的改进，但已被确定为医疗保健支出增长的主要驱动因素。近期文献表明，高免赔额健康计划可以对医疗保健支出水平产生即时影响，但其对支出增长的中长期影响仍然未知。在本文中，我们使用来自一家大型全国保险公司的多雇主群体索赔数据，来（i）研究高免赔额健康计划是否能在四年内降低支出增长，与较低免赔额的替代方案相比；以及（ii）通过考察高免赔额健康计划是否减少了对低价值与高价值治疗的使用，来探索任何增长减缓背后的机制。我们发现，高免赔额健康计划对支出增长的影响有限，仅在处方药方面观察到统计上显著的减少。在一个药物样本中，高免赔额健康计划与高成本效益药物的支出增长显著降低无关，但确实减少了成本效益较低药物的支出增长。

Black and White: Access to Capital among Minority-Owned Startups

黑与白：少数族裔初创企业的资本获取

Robert W. Fairlie, Alicia Robb, David T. Robinson

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use confidential and restricted-access data from the Kauffman Firm Survey and matched administrative data on credit scores to explore racial disparities in access to capital for new business ventures. The novel results on racial inequality in startup financing indicate that black-owned startups start smaller and stay smaller over the entire first eight years of their existence. Black startups face more difficulty in raising external capital, especially external debt. We find that disparities in credit-worthiness constrain black entreprenuers, but perceptions of treatment by banks also hold them back. Black entrepreneurs apply for loans less often than white entrepreneurs largely because they expect to be denied credit, even when they have a good credit history and in settings where strong local banks favor new business development.

中文摘要

我们使用来自考夫曼企业调查的保密和限制访问数据，以及匹配的信用评分行政数据，来探讨新创企业在资本获取方面的种族差异。关于初创企业融资中种族不平等的新颖结果表明，黑人拥有的初创企业起步规模较小，并且在成立后的整个前八年内保持较小规模。黑人初创企业在筹集外部资本方面面临更大困难，尤其是外部债务。我们发现，信用价值的差异限制了黑人企业家，但对银行对待方式的看法也阻碍了他们。黑人企业家申请贷款的频率低于白人企业家，很大程度上是因为他们预期会被拒绝信贷，即使他们拥有良好的信用记录，并且在当地强势银行有利于新业务发展的环境中也是如此。

Employer Neighborhoods and Racial Discrimination

雇主社区与种族歧视

Amanda Y. Agan, Sonja B. Starr

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using a large field experiment, we show that racial composition of employer neighborhoods predicts employment discrimination patterns in a direction suggesting in-group bias. Our data also show racial disparities in the geographic distribution of job postings. Simulations illustrate how these patterns combine to shape disparities. When jobs are located far from Black neighborhoods, Black applicants are doubly disadvantaged: discrimination patterns disfavor them, and they have fewer nearby opportunities. Finally, building on prior work on Ban-the-Box laws, we show that employers in less Black neighborhoods appear much likelier to stereotype Black applicants as potentially criminal when they lack criminal record information.

中文摘要

通过一项大规模的实地实验，我们表明雇主社区的种族构成预测了就业歧视模式，其方向表明存在内群体偏见。我们的数据还显示了招聘信息地理分布上的种族差异。模拟说明了这些模式如何共同形成差异。当工作地点远离黑人社区时，黑人申请者面临双重劣势：歧视模式对他们不利，而且他们附近的就业机会更少。最后，基于先前关于“禁止询问犯罪记录”法律的研究，我们表明，在黑人较少的社区，当雇主缺乏犯罪记录信息时，他们似乎更有可能将黑人申请者刻板地视为潜在的罪犯。

Lockdowns and Innovation: Evidence from the 1918 Flu Pandemic

封锁与创新：来自1918年流感大流行的证据

Enrico Berkes, Olivier Deschenes, Ruben Gaetani, Jeffrey Lin, Christopher Severen

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Does social distancing harm innovation? We estimate the effect of non-pharmaceutical interventions (NPIs)—policies that restrict interactions in an attempt to slow the spread of disease—on local invention. We construct a panel of issued patents and NPIs adopted by 50 large US cities during the 1918 flu pandemic. Difference-in-differences estimates show that cities adopting longer NPIs did not experience a decline in patenting during the pandemic relative to short-NPI cities, and recorded higher patenting afterward. Rather than reduce local invention by restricting localized knowledge spillovers, NPIs adopted during the pandemic may have better preserved other inventive factors.

中文摘要

社交距离会损害创新吗？我们估计了非药物干预措施——即旨在减缓疾病传播而限制互动的政策——对本地发明的影响。我们构建了一个包含1918年流感大流行期间美国50个大城市所采用的非药物干预措施和已授权专利的面板数据。双重差分估计表明，与采用较短非药物干预措施的城市相比，采用较长非药物干预措施的城市在大流行期间并未经历专利数量的下降，并且在之后记录了更高的专利数量。大流行期间采用的非药物干预措施可能并非通过限制本地化知识溢出而减少本地发明，而是更好地保留了其他发明因素。

Revenue Collapses and the Consumption of Small Business Owners in the Early Stages of the COVID-19 Pandemic

收入崩溃与COVID-19大流行早期阶段小企业主的消费

Olivia S. Kim, Jonathan A. Parker, Antoinette Schoar

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using financial account data linking small businesses to their owner households, we examine how business owners’ consumption responded to changes in business revenues during the COVID-19 crisis. In the first two months following the National Emergency, business revenues declined by 40 percent, largely driven by national factors rather than local infection rates or policies. However, the pass-through of revenue losses to owner consumption was limited: each dollar of revenue loss resulted in only a 1.6-cent decline in consumption. This muted pass-through persisted through 2021, even after the introduction of COVID-19 vaccines. Our findings suggest that federal subsidies and pandemic-induced reductions in spending opportunities explain the limited impact.

中文摘要

利用将小企业与其所有者家庭联系起来的金融账户数据，我们研究了在COVID-19危机期间，企业主的消费如何响应企业收入的变化。在国家紧急状态宣布后的头两个月，企业收入下降了40%，这主要是由全国性因素而非当地感染率或政策驱动的。然而，收入损失向所有者消费的传导是有限的：每损失1美元收入仅导致1.6美分的消费下降。这种微弱的传导持续到2021年，即使在COVID-19疫苗推出之后也是如此。我们的发现表明，联邦补贴以及大流行导致的支出机会减少解释了这种有限的影响。

Taxing Our Wealth

对我们的财富征税

Florian Scheuer, Joel Slemrod

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 瑞士、美国

Abstract

This paper evaluates proposals for an annual wealth tax. While a dozen OECD countries levied wealth taxes in the recent past, now only three retain them, with only Switzerland raising a comparable fraction of revenue as recent proposals for a US wealth tax. Studies of these taxes sometimes, but not always, find a substantial behavioral response, including of saving, portfolio change, avoidance, and evasion, and the impact depends crucially on design features, especially the broadness of the base and enforcement provisions. Because the US proposals are very different from any previous wealth tax, experience in other countries offers only broad lessons, but we can gain insights from closely related taxes, such as the property and the estate tax, and from optimal tax analysis of the role of wealth taxation.

中文摘要

本文评估了年度财富税的提案。尽管有十几个经合组织国家在近期征收过财富税，但现在只有三个国家保留它们，其中只有瑞士征收的财政收入比例与近期美国财富税提案相当。对这些税收的研究有时（但并非总是）发现显著的行为反应，包括储蓄、投资组合变化、避税和逃税，其影响关键取决于设计特征，尤其是税基的广泛性和执行条款。由于美国的提案与以往任何财富税都截然不同，其他国家的经验仅提供宽泛的教训，但我们可以从密切相关的税收（如财产税和遗产税）以及关于财富税作用的最优税收分析中获得见解。

Production Networks and International Fiscal Spillovers

生产网络与国际财政溢出效应

Michael B. Devereux, Karine Gente, Changhua Yu

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 德国

Abstract

This paper analyzes the impact of ﬁscal spending shocks in a dynamic, multi-country model with international production networks. We ﬁrst derive a decomposition of the eﬀects of a ﬁscal spending shock on the GDP of any country. This decomposition deﬁnes the response as the sum of a Direct, Income, and Price eﬀect. The Direct Eﬀect depends only on structural parameters and is independent of assumptions about monetary policy, wage setting, or capital mobility, while the Price Eﬀect is zero in the aggregate across countries. We apply this decomposition to an analysis of ﬁscal spillovers in the Eurozone, using the production network structure from the World Input Output Database (WIOD). We ﬁnd that ﬁscal spillovers from Germany and some other large Eurozone countries may be large, and within the range of empirical estimates. Without international production network linkages, spillovers would be only a third as large as predicted by the baseline model. Finally, we explore the diﬀusion of identiﬁed government spending shocks at the sectoral level, both within and across countries, using an empirical measure of the response, based on the theoretical decomposition. The empirical estimates are strongly consistent with the theoretical model.

中文摘要

本文在一个包含国际生产网络的动态多国模型中分析了财政支出冲击的影响。我们首先推导出财政支出冲击对任何国家GDP影响的分解。该分解将响应定义为直接效应、收入效应和价格效应之和。直接效应仅取决于结构参数，且独立于关于货币政策、工资设定或资本流动性的假设，而价格效应在各国加总后为零。我们将此分解应用于欧元区财政溢出效应的分析，使用了世界投入产出数据库（WIOD）中的生产网络结构。我们发现，来自德国及其他一些大型欧元区国家的财政溢出效应可能很大，且处于经验估计的范围内。如果没有国际生产网络联系，溢出效应将仅为基线模型预测的三分之一。最后，我们基于理论分解，使用响应的经验度量，在部门层面探索了已识别的政府支出冲击在国内和国家间的扩散。经验估计与理论模型高度一致。

All Aboard: The Effects of Port Development

全体登船：港口发展的影响

César Ducruet, Réka Juhász, Dávid Krisztián Nagy, Claudia Steinwender

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Seaports facilitate the fast flow of goods across space, but ports also entail local costs borne by host cities. We use the introduction of containerized shipping to explore the effects of port development. At the local level, we find that seaport development increases city population by making a city more attractive, but this market access effect is offset by costs which make the city less attractive. At the aggregate level, we find that the local costs associated with port development are heterogeneous across cities and reduce aggregate welfare gains, which however are still positive and substantial.

我们研究了17种行为偏差如何相互关联，以及它们与其他决策输入和决策输出的关系。在我们的全国代表性面板数据中，大多数消费者表现出多种偏差。即使在相似的人口统计/技能群体内，消费者之间也存在显著的异质性。偏差在个体内呈正相关，尤其是在调整测量误差后，并且与其他输入（风险厌恶、耐心、认知技能和人格特质）的相关性较低，但存在一些预期的例外。考虑到这种相关结构，我们将29个决策输入缩减为八个共同因子。七个共同因子至少负载于两种偏差，六个负载于理论上相关的偏差集群，两到三个明显是行为性的。除一个共同因子外，所有因子都与认知技能不同。因子得分与各领域的决策和结果在条件上高度相关。我们讨论了这种分类学对多种建模行为偏差对决策制定影响方法的若干潜在启示。

Mediating Conflict in the Lab

实验室中的冲突调解

Alessandra Casella, Evan Friedman, Manuel Perez Archila

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Mechanism design teaches us that a mediator can strictly improve the chances of peace between two opponents even when the mediator has no independent resources, is less informed than the two parties, and has no enforcement power. We test the theory in a lab experiment where two subjects negotiate how to share a resource; in case of conflict, the subjects' privately known strength determines their payoffs. The subjects send cheap talk messages about their strength to one another (in the treatment with direct communication) or to the mediator (in the mediation treatment), before making their demands or receiving the mediator's recommendations. We find that, in line with the theory, messages are significantly more sincere when sent to the mediator. However, contrary to the theory, peaceful resolution is not more frequent, even when the mediator is a computer implementing the optimal mediation program. While the theoretical result refers to the best (i.e. most peaceful) equilibrium under mediation, multiple equilibria exist, and the best equilibrium is particularly vulnerable to small deviations from full truthfulness. Subjects are not erratic and their deviations induce only small losses in payoffs, and yet they translate into significant increases in conflict.

中文摘要

机制设计理论告诉我们，即使调解人没有独立资源、信息少于双方且没有执行权力，调解人也能严格改善双方之间和平的机会。我们在实验室实验中检验了这一理论，其中两名受试者就如何分享资源进行谈判；在冲突情况下，受试者私下知晓的实力决定了他们的收益。受试者在提出要求或接受调解人建议之前，向对方（在直接沟通处理中）或调解人（在调解处理中）发送关于其实力的廉价谈话信息。我们发现，与理论一致，当信息发送给调解人时，信息显著更真诚。然而，与理论相反，和平解决并未更频繁地发生，即使调解人是实施最优调解程序的计算机。虽然理论结果指的是调解下最优（即最和平）的均衡，但存在多重均衡，且最优均衡特别容易受到完全诚实性的微小偏离的影响。受试者并非反复无常，他们的偏离仅导致收益的小幅损失，但这些损失却转化为冲突的显著增加。

Consumer Surplus of Alternative Payment Methods: Paying Uber with Cash

替代支付方式的消费者剩余：用现金支付优步

Fernando E. Alvarez, David O. Argente

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 墨西哥

Abstract

We estimate the private benefits for Uber riders from using alternative payment methods. We focus on Mexico where riders have the option to use cash or credit cards to pay for rides. We use three field experiments involving approximately 400,000 riders to estimate the loss of private benefits for riders if a ban on cash payments is implemented. We find that Uber riders, using cash as means of payment either sometimes or exclusively, suffer an average loss of approximately 50% of their expenditures on trips paid in cash before the ban.
