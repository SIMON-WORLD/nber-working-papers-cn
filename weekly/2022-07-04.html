No. 1论文详情NBER w30219

Screening Adaptive Cartels

筛选适应性卡特尔

Juan M. Ortner, Sylvain Chassang, Kei Kawai, Jun Nakabayashi

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose an equilibrium theory of data-driven antitrust oversight in which regulators launch investigations on the basis of suspicious bidding patterns and cartels can adapt to the statistical screens used by regulators. We emphasize the use of asymptotically safe tests, i.e. tests that are passed with probability approaching one by competitive firms, regardless of the underlying economic environment. Our main result establishes that screening for collusion with safe tests is a robust improvement over laissez-faire. Safe tests do not create new collusive equilibria, and do not hurt competitive industries. In addition, safe tests can have strict bite, including unraveling all collusive equilibria in some settings. We provide evidence that cartel adaptation to regulatory oversight is a real concern.

中文摘要

我们提出了一个数据驱动的反垄断监管的均衡理论，其中监管机构基于可疑的投标模式发起调查，而卡特尔可以适应监管机构使用的统计筛选方法。我们强调使用渐近安全检验，即无论潜在经济环境如何，竞争性企业以趋近于一的概率通过的检验。我们的主要结果表明，使用安全检验筛选合谋是对自由放任的稳健改进。安全检验不会创造新的合谋均衡，也不会损害竞争性行业。此外，安全检验可以产生严格效力，包括在某些情况下瓦解所有合谋均衡。我们提供证据表明，卡特尔对监管监督的适应性是一个真实的问题。

Using Divide-and-Conquer to Improve Tax Collection

利用分而治之策略改善税收征管

Samuel Kapon, Lucia Del Carpio, Sylvain Chassang

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 秘鲁

Abstract

Tax collection by capacity constrained governments may exhibit multiple equilibria: if delinquency is low, limited enforcement capacity is enough to discipline deviators; if delinquency is high, limited enforcement capacity is overstretched and no longer dissuasive. In principle, divide-and-conquer, a theoretically important but untested principle from mechanism design, can be used to unravel the undesirable high-delinquency equilibrium. We investigate the challenge of doing so in practice.\nOur preferred mechanism takes the form of Prioritized Iterative Enforcement (PIE). Tax-payers are assigned a rank trading-off expected collection and expected capacity use. Tax-payers are then iteratively threatened in small groups for which collection capacity is sufficient to induce compliance. After repayment occurs, unused collection capacity is released to issue the next round of threats.\nIn partnership with a district of Lima (Peru) we experimentally evaluate the impact of PIE on the collection of property taxes from 13432 tax-payers. Reduced-form evidence both validates and refines the theoretical benchmark. A structural model of tax-payer behavior suggests that, keeping the number of collection actions fixed, PIE would increase tax revenue by 11.3%.

中文摘要

能力受限的政府的税收征管可能存在多重均衡：如果拖欠率低，有限的执法能力足以惩戒违规者；如果拖欠率高，有限的执法能力不堪重负，不再具有威慑力。原则上，分而治之——一个理论上重要但未经检验的机制设计原则——可以用来瓦解不理想的高拖欠率均衡。我们研究了在实践中实现这一目标的挑战。 我们偏好的机制采取优先迭代执法的形式。纳税人被分配一个排名，该排名权衡了预期征收额和预期能力使用。然后，纳税人被迭代地以小组形式受到威胁，这些小组的征收能力足以确保合规。在还款发生后，未使用的征收能力被释放以进行下一轮威胁。 我们与秘鲁利马的一个区合作，通过实验评估了优先迭代执法对13432名纳税人财产税征收的影响。简化形式的证据既验证了理论基准，也对其进行了完善。一个纳税人行为的结构模型表明，在征收行动数量固定的情况下，优先迭代执法将使税收收入增加11.3%。

The Virtue of Complexity in Return Prediction

回报预测中复杂性的价值

Bryan T. Kelly, Semyon Malamud, Kangying Zhou

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 美国

Abstract

Much of the extant literature predicts market returns with “simple” models that use only a few parameters. Contrary to conventional wisdom, we theoretically prove that simple models severely understate return predictability compared to “complex” models in which the number of parameters exceeds the number of observations. We empirically document the virtue of complexity in US equity market return prediction. Our findings establish the rationale for modeling expected returns through machine learning.

中文摘要

现有文献大多使用仅包含少数参数的“简单”模型来预测市场回报。与传统观点相反，我们从理论上证明，与参数数量超过观测数量的“复杂”模型相比，简单模型会严重低估回报的可预测性。我们通过实证记录了美国股票市场回报预测中复杂性的价值。我们的发现为通过机器学习建模预期回报提供了理论依据。

No. 4论文详情NBER w30216

Information Spillovers and Sovereign Debt: Theory Meets the Eurozone Crisis

信息溢出与主权债务：理论与欧元区危机

Harold L. Cole, Guillermo Ordoñez, Daniel Neuhann

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We develop a theory of information spillovers in sovereign bond markets in which investors can acquire information about default risk before trading in primary and secondary markets. If primary markets are structured as multi-unit discriminatory-price auctions, an endogenous winner’s curse leads to strategic complementarities in information acquisition. As a result, shocks to default risk in one country may trigger crisis episodes with widespread information acquisition, sharp increases in the level and volatility of yields in risky countries, falling yields in safe countries, endogenous market segmentation, and arbitrage profits between primary and secondary markets. These predictions are consistent with the behavior of primary and secondary market yields, market segmentation, and measures of information acquisition during the Eurozone sovereign debt crisis.

中文摘要

我们发展了一个主权债券市场信息溢出理论，其中投资者可以在一级和二级市场交易前获取关于违约风险的信息。如果一级市场被构建为多单位歧视性价格拍卖，则内生的赢家诅咒会导致信息获取中的策略互补性。因此，一国违约风险的冲击可能引发危机事件，表现为广泛的信息获取、风险国家收益率水平和波动率的急剧上升、安全国家收益率的下降、内生市场分割以及一级和二级市场之间的套利利润。这些预测与欧元区主权债务危机期间一级和二级市场收益率、市场分割以及信息获取指标的行为一致。

The Privacy Elasticity of Behavior: Conceptualization and Application

行为的隐私弹性：概念化与应用

Inbal Dekel, Rachel Cummings, Ori Heffetz, Katrina Ligett

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose and initiate the study of privacy elasticity—the responsiveness of economic variables to small changes in the level of privacy given to participants in an economic system. Individuals rarely experience either full privacy or a complete lack of privacy; we use differential privacy—a computer-science theory increasingly adopted by industry and government—as a standardized means of quantifying continuous privacy changes. The resulting privacy measure implies a privacy-elasticity notion that is portable and comparable across contexts. We discuss applications, and demonstrate the feasibility of this approach by estimating the privacy elasticity of public-good contributions in a lab experiment.

中文摘要

我们提出并启动了隐私弹性的研究——即经济变量对经济系统中参与者所获隐私水平微小变化的响应程度。个体很少经历完全隐私或完全缺乏隐私；我们使用差分隐私——一种日益被行业和政府采用的计算机科学理论——作为量化连续隐私变化的标准化手段。由此产生的隐私度量隐含了一个可跨情境移植和比较的隐私弹性概念。我们讨论了应用，并通过在实验室实验中估计公共品贡献的隐私弹性来证明这种方法的可行性。

Polarization and Public Policy: Political Adverse Selection under Obamacare

极化与公共政策：奥巴马医改下的政治逆向选择

Leonardo Bursztyn, Jonathan T. Kolstad, Aakaash Rao, Pietro Tebaldi, Noam Yuchtman

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Politicizing policies designed to address market failures can diminish their effectiveness. We document a pattern of “political adverse selection” in the health insurance exchanges established under the Affordable Care Act (ACA; “Obamacare” in political debates): Republicans enrolled at lower rates than Democrats and independents, a gap driven by healthier Republicans. This selection raised public subsidy spending by approximately $124 per enrollee annually (2.4% of average cost). We field a survey to show this selection does not exist for other insurance products. Lower enrollment and higher costs are concentrated in more Republican areas, potentially contributing to polarized views of the policy.

中文摘要

将旨在解决市场失灵的政策政治化会削弱其有效性。我们在《平价医疗法案》（ACA；政治辩论中称为“奥巴马医改”）下建立的健康保险交易所中记录了一种“政治逆向选择”模式：共和党人的参保率低于民主党人和独立人士，这一差距由更健康的共和党人驱动。这种选择每年使每位参保人的公共补贴支出增加约124美元（占平均成本的2.4%）。我们进行了一项调查，表明这种选择在其他保险产品中并不存在。较低的参保率和较高的成本集中在共和党人较多的地区，可能加剧了对该政策的两极化看法。

No. 7论文详情NBER w30213

Can Fiscal Externalities Be Internalized?

财政外部性能否被内部化？

Erzo F.P. Luttmer

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Subsidies and in-kind transfers give rise to negative fiscal externalities. However, internalizing negative fiscal externalities through taxation would undo the subsidy or in-kind transfer that caused them. Similarly, positive fiscal externalities cannot be internalized though government subsidies. This paper describes a mechanism that transfers fiscal externalities from the government to private parties. Such transfers generate incentives within the private sector to reduce inefficiencies caused by fiscal externalities. Thus, the paper offers a straightforward, but powerful, insight: transferring fiscal externalities to third parties extends the reach of the Coase Theorem to inefficiencies stemming from fiscal externalities.

中文摘要

补贴和实物转移会产生负财政外部性。然而，通过税收来内部化负财政外部性会抵消导致这些外部性的补贴或实物转移。同样，正财政外部性也无法通过政府补贴来内部化。本文描述了一种将财政外部性从政府转移到私人部门的机制。这种转移在私人部门内部产生激励，以减少由财政外部性导致的低效率。因此，本文提供了一个直接但有力的见解：将财政外部性转移给第三方，将科斯定理的适用范围扩展到由财政外部性导致的低效率。

No. 8论文详情NBER w30212

Are Effective Teachers for Students with Disabilities Effective Teachers for All?

对残疾学生有效的教师对所有学生都有效吗？

W. Jesse Wood, Ijun Lai, Neil R. Filosa, Scott A. Imberman, Nathan D. Jones, Katharine O. Strunk

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The success of many students with disabilities (SWDs) depends on access to high-quality general education teachers. Yet, most measures of teacher value-added measures (VAM) fail to distinguish between a teacher’s effectiveness in educating students with and without disabilities. We create two VAM measures: one focusing on teachers’ effectiveness in improving outcomes for SWDs, and one for non-SWDs. We find top-performing teachers for non-SWDs often have relatively lower VAMs for SWDs, and that SWDs sort to teachers with lower scores in both VAMs. Overall, SWD-specific VAMs may be more suitable for identifying which teachers have a history of effectiveness with SWDs and could play a role in ensuring that students are being optimally assigned to these teachers.

中文摘要

许多残疾学生的成功取决于能否获得高质量的普通教育教师。然而，大多数教师增值衡量指标未能区分教师在教育残疾学生和非残疾学生方面的有效性。我们创建了两个增值衡量指标：一个关注教师改善残疾学生成果的有效性，另一个关注非残疾学生。我们发现，对非残疾学生表现最佳的教师，其对残疾学生的增值衡量指标通常相对较低，并且残疾学生会选择在两项增值衡量指标上得分都较低的教师。总体而言，针对残疾学生的增值衡量指标可能更适合识别哪些教师有对残疾学生有效的历史，并可能在确保学生被最优分配给这些教师方面发挥作用。

No. 9论文详情NBER w30211

u* = √uv

u* = √uv

Pascal Michaillat, Emmanuel Saez

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Most governments are mandated to maintain their economies at full employment. We propose that the best marker of full employment is the efficient unemployment rate, u*. We define u* as the unemployment rate that minimizes the nonproductive use of labor—both jobseeking and recruiting. The nonproductive use of labor is well measured by the number of jobseekers and vacancies, u + v. Through the Beveridge curve, the number of vacancies is inversely related to the number of jobseekers. With such symmetry, the labor market is efficient when there are as many jobseekers as vacancies (u = v), too tight when there are more vacancies than jobseekers (v > u), and too slack when there are more jobseekers than vacancies (u > v). Accordingly, the efficient unemployment rate is the geometric average of the unemployment and vacancy rates: u* = √uv. We compute u* for the United States between 1930 and 2022. We find for instance that the US labor market has been over full employment (u < u*) since May 2021.

中文摘要

大多数政府被授权维持其经济处于充分就业状态。我们提出，充分就业的最佳标志是有效失业率u*。我们将u*定义为最小化劳动力非生产性使用——包括求职和招聘——的失业率。劳动力的非生产性使用可以通过求职者和职位空缺的数量u+v很好地衡量。通过贝弗里奇曲线，职位空缺数量与求职者数量呈负相关。在这种对称性下，当求职者数量等于职位空缺数量（u=v）时，劳动力市场是有效的；当职位空缺多于求职者（v>u）时，市场过于紧张；当求职者多于职位空缺（u>v）时，市场过于松弛。因此，有效失业率是失业率和职位空缺率的几何平均值：u* = √uv。我们计算了1930年至2022年间美国的u*。例如，我们发现自2021年5月以来，美国劳动力市场一直处于过度充分就业状态（u < u*）。

No. 10论文详情NBER w30210

Fed Implied Market Prices and Risk Premia

美联储隐含的市场价格与风险溢价

Charles W. Calomiris, Joanna Harris, Harry Mamaysky, Cristina Tessari

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We introduce FDIF, a measure of Fed communication surprise based on the text of FOMC statements. FDIF measures the difference between text-implied and actual values of key market variables. Positive FDIF of countercyclical variables (e.g., credit spreads) is associated with negative macroeconomic forecast revisions; the opposite holds for procyclical variables. Industries that hedge bad FDIF news earn low returns on FOMC announcement days, but high returns on non-FOMC days. The opposite holds for FDIF-exposed industries, and the return differences are large. Controlling for FDIF exposure, rate-based policy surprise measures are not priced in the cross-section of industry returns.

中文摘要

我们引入了FDIF，这是一种基于联邦公开市场委员会声明文本的美联储沟通意外度量。FDIF衡量了文本隐含的关键市场变量值与实际值之间的差异。逆周期变量（如信用利差）的正FDIF与负的宏观经济预测修正相关；顺周期变量则相反。对冲FDIF坏消息的行业在联邦公开市场委员会公告日获得低回报，但在非公告日获得高回报。暴露于FDIF的行业则相反，且回报差异很大。在控制FDIF暴露后，基于利率的政策意外度量在行业回报的横截面上不被定价。

Officer-Involved: The Media Language of Police Killings

涉警事件：警察杀人案的媒体语言

Jonathan Moreno-Medina, Aurelie Ouss, Patrick Bayer, Bocar A. Ba

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

This paper examines language patterns in US television news coverage of police killings. Abstract

This paper quantifies and decomposes recent trends in U.S. PM2.5 disparities from the electricity sector using a high-resolution pollution transport model. Between 2000-2018, PM2.5 concentrations from electricity fell by 89% for the average individual, more than double the decline rate in overall U.S. ambient PM2.5 concentrations. Across racial/ethnic groups, we detect a dramatic convergence: since 2000, the Black-White PM2.5 disparity from electricity has narrowed by 95% and the Hispanic-White PM2.5 disparity has narrowed by 93%, though these disparities still exist in 2018. A decomposition reveals nearly all of these disparity trends can be attributed roughly equally to improvements in emissions intensities and compositional changes in electric generators, with small contributions from scale and residential location changes. This suggests both local air pollution policies and recent coal-to-natural gas fuel switching have played major roles in reducing U.S. racial/ethnic pollution disparities from electricity. While we detect similarly large PM2.5 improvements for the average low and high income individual, PM2.5 disparities by income are relatively small, with little change over time.

中文摘要

本文利用高分辨率污染传输模型，量化并分解了美国电力行业PM2.5差异的最新趋势。在2000年至2018年间，普通个体来自电力的PM2.5浓度下降了89%，是美国整体环境PM2.5浓度下降率的两倍多。在不同种族/民族群体中，我们发现了显著的趋同：自2000年以来，来自电力的黑人与白人之间的PM2.5差异缩小了95%，西班牙裔与白人之间的PM2.5差异缩小了93%，尽管这些差异在2018年仍然存在。分解分析显示，这些差异趋势几乎可以大致平均地归因于排放强度的改善和发电机组构成的变化，而规模和居住地变化的贡献较小。这表明，地方空气污染政策和近期从煤炭到天然气的燃料转换在减少美国种族/民族间来自电力的污染差异方面发挥了重要作用。虽然我们检测到普通低收入和高收入个体的PM2.5改善幅度同样巨大，但按收入划分的PM2.5差异相对较小，且随时间变化不大。

The Shift to Remote Work Lessens Wage-Growth Pressures

向远程工作的转变减轻了工资增长压力

Jose Maria Barrero, Nicholas Bloom, Steven J. Davis, Brent H. Meyer, Emil Mihaylov, David Altig

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

The recent shift to remote work raised the amenity value of employment. As compensation adjusts to share the amenity-value gains with employers, wage-growth pressures moderate. We find empirical support for this mechanism in the wage-setting behavior of U.S. employers, and we develop novel survey data to quantify its force. Our data imply a cumulative wage-growth moderation of 2.0 percentage points over two years. This moderation offsets more than half the real-wage catchup effect that Blanchard (2022) highlights in his analysis of near-term inflation pressures. The amenity-values gains associated with the recent rise of remote work also lower labor’s share of national income by 1.1 percentage points. In addition, the “unexpected compression” of wages since early 2020 (Autor and Dube, 2022) is partly explained by the same amenity-value effect, which operates differentially across the earnings distribution.

中文摘要

近期向远程工作的转变提高了就业的舒适度价值。随着薪酬调整以与雇主分享舒适度价值的收益，工资增长压力得以缓和。我们在美国雇主的工资设定行为中找到了支持这一机制的经验证据，并开发了新的调查数据来量化其力量。我们的数据表明，两年内累计工资增长放缓了2.0个百分点。这种放缓抵消了Blanchard（2022）在分析近期通胀压力时强调的实际工资追赶效应的一半以上。与近期远程工作兴起相关的舒适度价值收益也使国民收入中的劳动份额下降了1.1个百分点。此外，自2020年初以来的“意外工资压缩”（Autor和Dube，2022）部分可由相同的舒适度价值效应解释，该效应在收入分布中差异化地发挥作用。

Crash Narratives

崩盘叙事

William N. Goetzmann, Dasol Kim, Robert J. Shiller

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Narratives can serve as linguistic mechanisms for transmitting knowledge about important historical events. Shiller (2017) argues that narratives are potent mechanisms for the propagation of beliefs about the economy. We use a controlled context approach to test the effect of media narratives about historic stock market crashes on current investor beliefs and choices. Our methodology avoids the problem of large language model leakage that can lead to look-ahead biases. We find that crash narratives propagate broadly once they appear in news articles, and that they predict market volatility. We exploit investor heterogeneity using survey data to distinguish the effects of narrativity from response to fundamental factors. Finally, we develop a measure of pure narrativity to examine when the financial press is more likely to employ narratives.

中文摘要

叙事可以作为传递关于重要历史事件知识的语言机制。Shiller（2017）认为，叙事是传播关于经济信念的有力机制。我们采用受控情境方法来测试关于历史股市崩盘的媒体叙事对当前投资者信念和选择的影响。我们的方法避免了可能导致前瞻性偏差的大型语言模型泄漏问题。我们发现，崩盘叙事一旦出现在新闻文章中就会广泛传播，并且它们能预测市场波动。我们利用调查数据中的投资者异质性来区分叙事效应与对基本面因素的反应。最后，我们开发了一个纯叙事性衡量指标，以考察金融媒体何时更可能使用叙事。

An Evaluation of a National Program to Reduce Student Absenteeism in High School

一项减少高中生缺勤率的国家计划评估

Michael Baker, Nina Drange, Hege Marie Gjefsen

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 挪威

Abstract

Starting in the 2016/17 academic year, high school students in Norway who missed more than 10 percent of hours in a given course without a medical excuse could not receive a final grade. Across all students, the new policy reduced total absence by 21-28 percent, and chronic absence by 29-39 percent. This behavioral response was largely sufficient to avoid the academic penalty for absence over the 10 percent threshold. We also find the policy had a positive impact on teacher awarded GPA for groups with larger baseline absence and greater absolute changes in attendance.

中文摘要

从2016/17学年开始，挪威高中生在特定课程中无故缺勤超过总课时的10%将无法获得最终成绩。在所有学生中，新政策使总缺勤率降低了21-28%，长期缺勤率降低了29-39%。这种行为反应在很大程度上足以避免因缺勤超过10%门槛而受到的学业惩罚。我们还发现，对于基线缺勤率较高且出勤率绝对变化较大的群体，该政策对教师评定的平均绩点产生了积极影响。

Price Setting with Strategic Complementarities as a Mean Field Game

具有战略互补性的价格设定作为平均场博弈

Fernando E. Alvarez, Francesco Lippi, Takis Souganidis

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the propagation of monetary shocks in a sticky-price general-equilibrium economy where the firms’ pricing strategy feature a complementarity with the decisions of other firms. In a dynamic equilibrium the firm’s price-setting decisions depend on aggregates, which in turn depend on firms’ decisions. We cast this fixed-point problem as a Mean Field Game and establish several analytic results. We study existence and uniqueness of the equilibrium and characterize the impulse response function (IRF) of output following an aggregate “MIT” shock. We prove that strategic complementarities make the IRF larger at each horizon, in a convex fashion. We establish that complementarities may give rise to an IRF with a hump-shaped profile. As the complementarity becomes large enough the IRF diverges and at a critical point there is no equilibrium. Finally, we show that the amplification effect of the strategic interactions is similar across models. For instance, the Calvo model and the Golosov-Lucas model display a comparable amplification, in spite of the fact that the non-neutrality in Calvo is much larger.

中文摘要

我们研究在粘性价格一般均衡经济中货币冲击的传导机制，其中企业的定价策略与其他企业的决策存在互补性。在动态均衡中，企业的价格设定决策依赖于总量变量，而总量变量又取决于企业的决策。我们将这一不动点问题建模为平均场博弈，并建立了若干分析结果。我们研究了均衡的存在性和唯一性，并刻画了在总体“MIT”冲击后产出的脉冲响应函数。我们证明，战略互补性使每个时间跨度上的脉冲响应函数更大，且呈凸性特征。我们证实，互补性可能导致脉冲响应函数呈现驼峰形轮廓。当互补性足够强时，脉冲响应函数发散，并在临界点处不存在均衡。最后，我们表明，战略互动的放大效应在不同模型中相似。例如，卡尔沃模型和戈洛索夫-卢卡斯模型显示出可比的放大效应，尽管卡尔沃模型中的非中性效应要大得多。

Expanding Capacity for Vaccines Against Covid-19 and Future Pandemics: A Review of Economic Issues

扩大新冠疫苗及未来大流行病疫苗产能：经济问题综述

Susan Athey, Juan Camilo Castillo, Esha Chaudhuri, Michael Kremer, Alexandre Simoes Gomes, Christopher Snyder

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We review economic arguments for using public policy to accelerate vaccine supply during a pandemic. Rapidly vaccinating a large share of the global population helps avoid economic, mortality, and social losses, which in the case of Covid-19 mounted into trillions of dollars. However, pharmaceutical firms are unlikely to have private incentives to invest in vaccine capacity at the socially optimal scale and speed. The socially optimal level of public spending may cause some sticker shock but—as epitomized by the tagline “spending billions to save trillions”—is eclipsed by the benefits and can be restrained with the help of careful policy design and advance preparations. Capacity is so valuable during a pandemic that fractional dosing and other measures to stretch available capacity should be explored.

中文摘要

我们回顾了在大流行病期间利用公共政策加速疫苗供应的经济论据。快速为全球大部分人口接种疫苗有助于避免经济、死亡和社会损失，在新冠疫情期间这些损失高达数万亿美元。然而，制药公司不太可能有私人激励以社会最优的规模和速度投资于疫苗产能。社会最优的公共支出水平可能会引起一些价格冲击，但——正如“花费数十亿以节省数万亿”这一口号所体现的——其收益远大于成本，并且可以通过精心设计的政策和预先准备加以控制。在大流行病期间，产能如此宝贵，以至于应探索分剂量接种和其他扩大可用产能的措施。
