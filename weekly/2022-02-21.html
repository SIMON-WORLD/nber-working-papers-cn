The Unemployment-Inflation Trade-off Revisited: The Phillips Curve in COVID Times

失业与通胀权衡再审视：新冠疫情时期的菲利普斯曲线

Richard K. Crump, Stefano Eusepi, Marc Giannoni, Ayşegül Şahin

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using a New Keynesian Phillips curve, we document the rapid and persistent increase in the natural rate of unemployment, u*ₜ, in the aftermath of the pandemic and characterize its implications for inflation dynamics. While the bulk of the inflation surge is attributed to temporary supply factors, we also find an important role for current and expected negative unemployment gaps. Through the lens of the model, the 2022–2023 disinflation was driven by the expectation that the unemployment gap will close through a progressive decline in u*ₜ and a rise in the unemployment rate. This implies that convergence to long-run price stability depends critically on expectations about labor market tightness. Using a variety of cross-sectional data sources we provide corroborating evidence of unusually tight labor market conditions, consistent with our estimated rise in u*ₜ.

中文摘要

利用新凯恩斯菲利普斯曲线，我们记录了疫情后自然失业率u*ₜ的快速且持续上升，并刻画了其对通胀动态的影响。虽然通胀飙升主要归因于暂时性供给因素，但我们发现当前和预期的负失业缺口也发挥了重要作用。根据模型视角，2022-2023年的通胀下降是由以下预期驱动的：失业缺口将通过u*ₜ的逐步下降和失业率的上升而闭合。这意味着向长期价格稳定的收敛关键取决于对劳动力市场紧张程度的预期。利用多种横截面数据来源，我们提供了劳动力市场异常紧张的佐证证据，这与我们估计的u*ₜ上升相一致。

Has the Willingness to Work Fallen during the Covid Pandemic?

新冠疫情期间工作意愿是否下降？

R. Jason Faberman, Andreas I. Mueller, Ayşegül Şahin

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine the effect of the Covid pandemic on willingness to work along both the extensive and intensive margins of labor supply. Special survey questions in the Job Search Supplement of the Survey of Consumer Expectations (SCE) allow us to elicit information about individuals’ desired work hours for the 2013-2021 period. Using these questions, along with workers’ actual labor market participation, we construct a labor market underutilization measure, the Aggregate Hours Gap (AHG), following Faberman et al. (2020). The AHG captures changes in labor market underutilization for the full population along both the extensive and intensive margins using data on desired work hours as a measure of their potential labor supply. We find that the sharp increase in the AHG during the Covid pandemic essentially disappeared by the end of 2021. We also document a sharp decline in desired work hours during the pandemic that persists through the end of 2021 and is roughly double the drop in the labor force participation rate. Ignoring the decline in desired hours overstates the degree of underutilization by 2.5 percentage points (12.5%). Our findings suggest that, as of 2021Q4, the labor market is tighter than suggested by the unemployment rate and the adverse labor supply effect of the pandemic is more pronounced than implied by the labor force participation rate. These discrepancies underscore the importance of taking into account the intensive margin for both labor market underutilization and potential labor supply.

中文摘要

我们考察了新冠疫情对劳动力供给广延边际和集约边际上工作意愿的影响。消费者预期调查（SCE）求职补充问卷中的特殊问题使我们能够获取2013-2021年期间个人期望工作小时数的信息。利用这些问题以及工人的实际劳动力市场参与情况，我们按照Faberman等人（2020）的方法构建了一个劳动力市场利用不足指标——总工时缺口（AHG）。AHG利用期望工作小时数作为潜在劳动力供给的度量，捕捉了全体人口在广延边际和集约边际上劳动力市场利用不足的变化。我们发现，新冠疫情期间AHG的急剧上升到2021年底基本消失。我们还记录了疫情期间期望工作小时数的急剧下降，这种下降持续到2021年底，并且大约是劳动力参与率下降幅度的两倍。忽略期望工作小时数的下降会使利用不足程度被高估2.5个百分点（12.5%）。我们的研究结果表明，截至2021年第四季度，劳动力市场比失业率所暗示的更为紧张，而疫情对劳动力供给的不利影响比劳动力参与率所暗示的更为显著。这些差异凸显了在考虑劳动力市场利用不足和潜在劳动力供给时，纳入集约边际的重要性。

What Determines Effectiveness of Renewable Energy Standards? General Equilibrium Analytical Model and Empirical Analysis

什么决定了可再生能源标准的有效性？一般均衡分析模型与实证分析

Don Fullerton, Chi L. Ta

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 美国

Abstract

Our new analytical general equilibrium model is used to study effects of tightening state Renewable Portfolio Standards (RPS) on electricity price, CO2 emissions, fossil fuel electricity generation, and two kinds of renewable generation. We show how those outcomes depend on key state characteristics such as endowments of potential intermittent and non-intermittent (“dispatchable”) renewable sources and the degree of intermittency. Our three extensions investigate key assumptions. We prove theorems and derive empirical hypotheses about what state characteristics makes RPS programs more effective. Using U.S. state-level data from 1990 to 2015, we find the data are consistent with these hypotheses.

中文摘要

我们利用新的分析性一般均衡模型，研究各州收紧可再生能源组合标准（RPS）对电价、二氧化碳排放、化石燃料发电以及两种可再生能源发电的影响。我们展示了这些结果如何取决于关键州特征，例如潜在间歇性和非间歇性（“可调度”）可再生资源的禀赋以及间歇性程度。我们的三个扩展部分研究了关键假设。我们证明了定理并推导出关于哪些州特征使RPS计划更有效的实证假设。利用1990年至2015年美国州级数据，我们发现数据与这些假设一致。

Human Capital Reallocation Across Firms: Evidence from Idiosyncratic Shocks

跨企业的人力资本重新配置：来自特质性冲击的证据

Erik P. Gilje, Jérôme P. Taillard, Linghang Zeng

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study human capital reallocation following firm-specific idiosyncratic shocks. Theory offers diverging predictions as to whether human capital gets reallocated to its most productive use following these shocks. To empirically test these predictions, we focus on relegation battles in the English Premier League. This setting offers well identified idiosyncratic shocks as well as both individual-level and firm-level productivity metrics. We find that human capital exits firms after a negative idiosyncratic shock. Specifically, we find that more productive players move to more productive clubs and maintain their long-term productivity. They get replaced with lower productivity players. Overall, our results show that in a setting with highly transferable skills, idiosyncratic shocks lead to a reallocation of human capital that moves an industry towards a better overall match between individual-level and firm-level productivity.

中文摘要

我们研究了企业特定特质性冲击后的人力资本重新配置。关于人力资本在经历这些冲击后是否会重新配置到其最具生产力的用途，理论给出了不同的预测。为了实证检验这些预测，我们聚焦于英格兰足球超级联赛的保级战。这一设定提供了识别良好的特质性冲击，以及个人层面和企业层面的生产力指标。我们发现，在经历负面特质性冲击后，人力资本会离开企业。具体而言，我们发现生产力更高的球员会转会到生产力更高的俱乐部，并保持其长期生产力。他们被生产力较低的球员所取代。总体而言，我们的结果表明，在一个技能高度可转移的环境中，特质性冲击会导致人力资本重新配置，使行业朝着个人层面和企业层面生产力之间整体更优匹配的方向发展。

Integrating Refugees by Addressing Labor Shortages? A Policy Evaluation

通过解决劳动力短缺来融合难民？一项政策评估

Mette Foged, Janis Kreuder, Giovanni Peri

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 丹麦

Abstract

We evaluate the effect on newly arrived refugees' employment of a policy, introduced in Denmark in 2013, that matched refugees to occupations with local labor shortages after basic training for those jobs. Leveraging the staggered roll-out across municipalities, we find that the policy increased employment by 5-6 percentage points one year after arrival and 10 percentage points two years after. The policy was especially effective for male refugees and refugees with some secondary education. The findings suggest that this type of policy could alleviate long-term labor shortages and integrate low-skilled immigrants, while having minimal competition effects on natives.

中文摘要

我们评估了一项于2013年在丹麦引入的政策对新抵达难民就业的影响，该政策在难民接受基本职业培训后，将其与存在当地劳动力短缺的职业进行匹配。利用各市镇分阶段推广的情况，我们发现该政策在难民抵达一年后使就业率提高了5-6个百分点，两年后提高了10个百分点。该政策对男性难民和接受过一定中等教育的难民尤其有效。研究结果表明，这类政策可以缓解长期劳动力短缺并融合低技能移民，同时对本地人的竞争效应最小。

A Solomonic Solution to Ownership Disputes: An Application to Blockchain Front-Running

解决所有权争议的所罗门式方案：应用于区块链抢先交易

Joshua S. Gans, Richard T. Holden

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Blockchain front-running involves multiple agents, other than the legitimate agent, claiming a payment from performing a contract. It arises because of the public nature of blockchain transactions and potential network congestion. This paper notes that disputes over payments are similar to classic ownership disputes (such as King Solomon's dilemma). We propose a simultaneous report mechanism that resolves Solomon's dilemma (using only ordinal preference information) and also eliminates blockchain front-running. In each case, the mechanism relies on threats to remove ownership from all claimants and preferences from the legitimate claimant over allocations to other agents.

中文摘要

区块链抢先交易涉及除合法代理人之外的多个代理人，声称有权从执行合约中获得付款。它源于区块链交易的公开性质和潜在的网络拥堵。本文指出，付款争议类似于经典的所有权争议（如所罗门王的困境）。我们提出一个同时报告机制，该机制解决了所罗门困境（仅使用序数偏好信息），同时也消除了区块链抢先交易。在每种情况下，该机制依赖于威胁：剥夺所有索赔人的所有权，以及合法索赔人对其他代理人分配的偏好。

Gifted Children Programs’ Short and Long-Term Impact: Higher Education, Earnings, and the Knowledge Economy

天才儿童项目的短期与长期影响：高等教育、收入与知识经济

Yoav Goldstein, Victor Lavy, Genia Rachkovski

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper examines the short- and long-term consequences of participation in high school Gifted Children Programs (GCPs). Using administrative data that follow individuals from primary school to adulthood, we find that while these programs have negligible effects on high school academic achievement, they substantially influence university trajectories. Specifically, GCP participation shifts the choice of field of study, increases the incidence of double majors, and increases the probability of attaining advanced degrees. Interestingly, we find no effect on earnings or employment—either overall or within knowledge-based sectors—indicating that the high baseline productivity gifted children possess is not further enhanced by program participation. However, we find that GCP participation positively affects spouse “quality” and significantly increases the likelihood of working with fellow GCP peers, suggesting that the program has important long-run effects on the social connections of gifted students. We discuss potential mechanisms within the context of psychological theories of giftedness.

中文摘要

本文考察了参与高中天才儿童项目（GCP）的短期和长期后果。利用从小学到成年期追踪个体的行政数据，我们发现虽然这些项目对高中学业成绩的影响微乎其微，但它们显著影响了大学轨迹。具体而言，参与GCP改变了专业选择方向，增加了双专业的发生率，并提高了获得高等学位的概率。有趣的是，我们发现对收入或就业（无论是在知识型部门内还是总体上）没有影响，这表明天才儿童已有的高基线生产力并未因项目参与而进一步增强。然而，我们发现参与GCP对配偶“质量”有积极影响，并显著增加了与GCP同伴共事的可能性，这表明该项目对天才学生的社会关系具有重要的长期影响。我们在天才心理理论的背景下讨论了潜在机制。

Gaps Between Official and Excess Covid-19 Mortality Measures: The Effects of Institutional Quality and Vaccinations

官方与超额新冠死亡率指标之间的差距：制度质量与疫苗接种的影响

Joshua Aizenman, Alex Cukierman, Yothin Jinjarak, Sameer Nair-Desai, Weining Xin

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We evaluate quartile rankings of countries during the Covid-19 pandemic using both official (confirmed) and excess mortality data. By December 2021, the quartile rankings of three-fifths of the countries differ when ranked by excess vs. official mortality. Countries that are ‘doing substantially better’ in the excess mortality are characterized by higher urban population shares; higher GDP/Capita; and higher scores on institutional and policy variables. We perform two regressions in which the ratio of Cumulative Excess to Official Covid-19 mortalities (E/O ratio) is regressed on covariates. In a narrow study, controlling for GDP/Capita and vaccination rates, by December 2021 the E/O ratio was smaller in countries with higher vaccination rates. In a broad study, adding institutional and policy variables, the E/O ratio was smaller in countries with higher degree of voice and accountability. The arrival of vaccines in 2021 and voice and accountability had a discernible association on the E/O ratio.

中文摘要

我们利用官方（确诊）和超额死亡率数据评估了新冠疫情期间国家的四分位数排名。截至2021年12月，按超额死亡率与官方死亡率排名时，五分之三国家的四分位数排名存在差异。在超额死亡率方面“表现明显更好”的国家，其特点是城市人口比例更高；人均GDP更高；以及制度和政策变量得分更高。我们进行了两次回归，将累计超额死亡率与官方新冠死亡率的比率（E/O比率）对协变量进行回归。在一项狭义研究中，控制人均GDP和疫苗接种率后，截至2021年12月，疫苗接种率较高的国家E/O比率较小。在一项广义研究中，加入制度和政策变量后，发言权和问责制程度较高的国家E/O比率较小。2021年疫苗的到来以及发言权和问责制与E/O比率存在可辨别的关联。

Exorbitant Privilege? Quantitative Easing and the Bond Market Subsidy of Prospective Fallen Angels

过高特权？量化宽松与准堕落天使的债券市场补贴

Viral V. Acharya, Ryan Banerjee, Matteo Crosignani, Tim Eisert, Renée Spigt

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

We document capital misallocation in the U.S. investment-grade (IG) corporate bond market, driven by quantitative easing (QE). Prospective fallen angels—risky firms just above the IG cutoff—enjoyed subsidized bond financing in 2009-19. This effect is driven by prolonged cumulative Fed purchases of securities inducing long-duration IG-focused investors to rebalance their portfolios towards higher-yielding IG bonds. The benefiting firms (i) exploited the sluggish downward adjustment of credit ratings after M&A to finance risky acquisitions with bond issuances, (ii) increased market share affecting competitors' employment and investment, but (iii) suffered severe downgrades at the onset of the pandemic.

This effect is larger and more persistent in countries with lower institutional quality, and in sectors with greater financial constraints.

The Federal Funds Market, Pre- and Post-2008

联邦基金市场：2008年前后

Eric T. Swanson

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

This chapter provides an overview of the federal funds market and how the equilibrium federal fund rate is determined. I devote particular attention to comparing and contrasting the federal funds market before and after 2008, since there were several dramatic changes around that time that completely changed the market and the way in which the equilibrium federal funds rate is determined. The size of this structural break is arguably as large and important as the period of reserves targeting under Fed Chairman Paul Volcker from 1979–82. The Great Gatsby Curve

了不起的盖茨比曲线

Steven N. Durlauf, Andros Kourtellos, Chih Ming Tan

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper provides a synthesis of theoretical and empirical work on the Great Gatsby Curve, the positive empirical relationship between cross-section income inequality and persistence of income across generations. We present statistical models of income dynamics that mechanically give rise to the relationship between inequality and mobility. Five distinct classes of theories, including models on family investments, skills, social influences, political economy, and aspirations are developed, each providing a behavioral mechanism to explain the relationship. Intergenerational Mobility

代际流动性

Neil A. Cholli, Steven N. Durlauf

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This essay reviews the theory and empirics of intergenerational mobility. Our review draws on models and empirical analyses of classic and more recent work from both economics and sociology. We summarize models and the surrounding empirical evidence of two key sets of mechanisms: family factors (income, education, credit constraints, household composition, and genes) and social factors (schools, neighborhood sorting, racial segregation, and peer and role model effects). We then discuss and evaluate current methods used to measure intergenerational mobility, including linear regressions and Markov chains. Theoretical models imply nonlinear relationships between parent and child status that are often ignored in practice and offer potentially different interpretations of the evidence of heterogeneity in mobility across locations, groups, and time. We conclude that the next generation of studies would benefit from a closer integration of theory with empirics.

Group Corruption via Sequential Bargaining in a Hierarchical Organization

层级组织中的群体腐败：通过序贯谈判实现

Fan-chin Kung, Ping Wang, Quan Wen

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop a framework of group corruption via back-door negotiations between an outside initiator and an authority of decision-makers in a hierarchical organization. We examine the role played by the architecture of a multi-tier authority and determine under such a structure how bargaining proceeds, in what order, and when it breaks down. We verify that equilibrium bargaining sequence proceeds as a chain through decision-making agents, regardless of the hierarchy of the organization. We prove the existence of a compromised equilibrium, where the decision of the authority is compromised, and establish sufficient conditions under which the most natural bottom-up bargaining configuration arises in equilibrium where a proposer negotiates with an immediately higher ranked respondent, starting with the initiator bargaining with the lowest ranked decision-maker in the organization. We then show the circumstances under which a top-down or a non-monotonic equilibrium configuration may emerge, and those under which the deal may break down. This enables us to capture a rich array of group corruptive configurations as observed. We conclude by investigating the extension to multi-tier authorities with multiple agents of the same rank in each tier, such as in a tree hierarchy.

中文摘要

我们构建了一个群体腐败框架，该框架通过外部发起者与层级组织中的决策者权威之间的幕后谈判来实现。我们研究了多层权威结构所扮演的角色，并确定了在这种结构下谈判如何进行、以何种顺序进行以及何时破裂。我们验证了无论组织层级如何，均衡谈判序列都作为一条链条贯穿决策者。我们证明了妥协均衡的存在，其中权威的决策被妥协，并建立了充分条件，使得最自然的自下而上谈判配置在均衡中出现，即提议者与直接上级回应者谈判，从发起者与组织中最低级别的决策者谈判开始。然后，我们展示了自上而下或非单调均衡配置可能出现的情况，以及交易可能破裂的情况。这使我们能够捕捉到观察到的丰富多样的群体腐败配置。最后，我们研究了扩展到具有多层权威且每层有多个同级代理人的情况，例如树形层级结构。
