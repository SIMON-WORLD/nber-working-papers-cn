Land Quality

土地质量

J. Vernon Henderson, Adam Storeygard, David N. Weil

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We develop a new measure of land quality by estimating weights in a Poisson regression of grid-cell population on geographic characteristics and country fixed effects. Aggregating to countries, we construct average land quality (ALQ) and quality-adjusted population density (QAPD). We show: First, current income per capita is positively correlated with ALQ. Second, while income today is unrelated to conventional population density, it is strongly negatively related to QAPD. Third, this negative relationship was not present in 1820 and emerged because today’s lower income countries have experienced faster subsequent population growth. Fourth, countries with higher average land quality began sustained modern economic growth earlier, and this earlier takeoff largely explains the modern income-ALQ relationship. We posit a framework in which land quality induced denser populations in Malthusian equilibrium and, via agglomeration, earlier takeoffs. Less dense countries experienced larger population multipliers during their later demographic transitions due to imported health technologies.

中文摘要

我们通过估计一个将网格单元人口对地理特征和国家固定效应进行泊松回归的权重，开发了一种新的土地质量衡量指标。汇总到国家层面，我们构建了平均土地质量（ALQ）和质量调整后的人口密度（QAPD）。我们表明：第一，当前人均收入与ALQ正相关。第二，虽然当前收入与传统人口密度无关，但与QAPD强烈负相关。第三，这种负相关关系在1820年并不存在，而是因为当今收入较低的国家随后经历了更快的人口增长。第四，平均土地质量较高的国家更早开始持续的现代经济增长，而这种更早的起飞在很大程度上解释了现代收入与ALQ之间的关系。我们提出了一个框架，其中土地质量在马尔萨斯均衡中导致人口更密集，并通过集聚效应导致更早的经济起飞。人口较不密集的国家在随后的 demographic transition 中，由于引进了健康技术，经历了更大的人口乘数效应。

Corporate Governance, Business Group Governance and Economic Development Traps

公司治理、商业集团治理与经济发展陷阱

Luis Dau, Randall Morck, Bernard Yeung

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Every firm in a developed economy relies on the mere existence of countless other firms to keep prices competitive up and down all supply chains. Without this network externality, no firm forms; and without many firms, no network forms; locking in a low-income trap. Business group governance supersedes corporate governance in most developing economies and in the rapid catch-up development phases of most high-income economies by hierarchically coordinating firms in multiple industries, internalizing this network externality. High-income economies grow via creative destruction - creative firms imposing a negative externality upon firms they destroy or disrupt, but a larger positive innovation-related externality upon the whole economy. Business groups avoid creative self-destruction, innovation by one group firm that disrupts another. Corporate governance supersedes business group governance in high-income economies to facilitate productivity growth. If business group governance does not retreat, productivity growth is impaired and a middle-income trap can result.

中文摘要

在发达经济体中，每家企业都依赖于无数其他企业的存在，以保持所有供应链上下游的价格竞争力。没有这种网络外部性，企业就无法形成；而没有众多企业，网络也无法形成，从而陷入低收入陷阱。在大多数发展中经济体以及多数高收入经济体快速追赶的发展阶段，商业集团治理通过层级化协调多个行业的企业，将这种网络外部性内部化，从而取代了公司治理。高收入经济体通过创造性破坏实现增长——创造性企业对其摧毁或颠覆的企业施加负外部性，但对整个经济产生更大的与创新相关的正外部性。商业集团会避免创造性的自我毁灭，即一个集团企业的创新扰乱另一个集团企业。在高收入经济体中，公司治理取代商业集团治理以促进生产率增长。如果商业集团治理不退出，生产率增长将受到损害，并可能导致中等收入陷阱。

Effects of the COVID-19 Pandemic on Domestic Violence in Los Angeles

COVID-19疫情对洛杉矶家庭暴力的影响

Amalia R. Miller, Carmit Segal, Melissa K. Spencer

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Around the world, policymakers and news reports warned that the COVID-19 pandemic, and attendant restrictions on individual mobility and commercial activity, could lead to a surge in domestic violence (DV). Some prominent initial studies of the effects of the pandemic showed evidence of greater DV incidence, but findings were inconsistent across locations and DV measures. This paper focuses on a single major city, Los Angeles, to measure the impact of the initial pandemic shutdown on three DV measures based on police data (911 calls, crime incidents, and arrests) as well as measures based on two non-police administrative sources (the county DV hotline and hospital records). After confirming the prior finding of an increase in DV calls to police during the shutdown, we also show that calls to the hotline increased. However, we find that DV crimes recorded by police and hospital visits by female assault (and DV) victims both decreased significantly, suggesting that the increase in calls came from higher reporting rates, possibly for verbal disputes. Arrests per population for DV crimes were unaffected by the shutdown, but a higher share of DV crimes led to arrests. This points against a decrease in the intensity of policing of DV crimes driving the lower crime rate. We conclude that the initial LA shutdown increased the rates at which people contacted authorities about domestic-related concerns, but decreased the overall incidence of physical DV crimes in the population.

中文摘要

全球范围内，政策制定者和新闻报道警告称，COVID-19疫情及其伴随的个人流动性和商业活动限制可能导致家庭暴力激增。一些关于疫情影响的初步重要研究显示了家庭暴力发生率上升的证据，但不同地点和家庭暴力衡量指标之间的结果并不一致。本文聚焦于洛杉矶这一主要城市，基于警方数据（911报警电话、犯罪事件和逮捕）以及两个非警方行政来源（县家庭暴力热线和医院记录）的衡量指标，衡量疫情初期封锁对三种家庭暴力指标的影响。在确认了封锁期间警方接到的家庭暴力报警电话增加这一先前发现后，我们还显示热线电话也增加了。然而，我们发现警方记录的家庭暴力犯罪和女性攻击（及家庭暴力）受害者的医院就诊次数均显著下降，这表明报警电话的增加源于更高的报告率，可能涉及言语纠纷。人均家庭暴力犯罪逮捕率未受封锁影响，但更高比例的家庭暴力犯罪导致了逮捕。这反驳了家庭暴力犯罪执法强度下降导致犯罪率降低的观点。我们得出结论，洛杉矶初期的封锁提高了人们就家庭相关问题联系当局的频率，但降低了人群中身体家庭暴力犯罪的总体发生率。

Forward Guidance and Durable Goods Demand

前瞻指引与耐用品需求

Alisdair McKay, Johannes F. Wieland

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Durable goods attenuate the power of forward guidance. The extensive and intensive margins of durable goods demand are both more sensitive to the contemporaneous user cost than to future user costs. Changes in the contemporaneous real interest rate directly affect the contemporaneous user cost and durable demand, whereas promises of low future real interest rates have weaker effects through equilibrium price changes. Quantitatively, reducing the real interest rate one year from now increases output by only forty percent as much as reducing the real interest rate today. Our results are little affected by the maturity of financial assets that finance durable purchases.

中文摘要

耐用品削弱了前瞻指引的力量。耐用品需求的广延边际和集约边际对当期用户成本比未来用户成本更为敏感。当期实际利率的变化直接影响当期用户成本和耐用品需求，而承诺未来低实际利率则通过均衡价格变化产生较弱的影响。定量来看，将一年后的实际利率降低一个单位，其产出增加效果仅为降低当期实际利率的40%。我们的结果几乎不受为耐用品购买融资的金融资产期限的影响。

Stereotypes in Financial Literacy: Evidence from PISA

金融素养中的刻板印象：来自PISA的证据

Laura Bottazzi, Annamaria Lusardi

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 意大利

Abstract

We examine gender differences in financial literacy among high school students in Italy using data from the 2012 Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA). Gender differences in financial literacy are large among the young in Italy. They are present in all regions and are particularly severe in the South and the Islands. Combining the rich PISA data with a variety of other indicators, we provide a thorough analysis of the potential determinants of the gender gap in financial literacy. We find that parental background, in particular the role of mothers, matters for the financial knowledge of girls. Moreover, we show that the social and cultural environment in which girls and boys live plays a crucial role in explaining gender differences. We also show that history matters: Medieval commercial hubs and the nuclear family structure created conditions favorable to the transformation of the role of women in society, and shaped gender differences in financial literacy as well.

中文摘要

我们利用2012年国际学生评估项目的数据，研究了意大利高中生在金融素养方面的性别差异。在意大利年轻人中，金融素养的性别差异很大。这些差异存在于所有地区，在南部和岛屿地区尤为严重。通过将丰富的PISA数据与各种其他指标相结合，我们对金融素养性别差异的潜在决定因素进行了全面分析。我们发现，父母背景，特别是母亲的作用，对女孩的金融知识很重要。此外，我们表明女孩和男孩所处的社会和文化环境在解释性别差异方面起着关键作用。我们还表明历史很重要：中世纪的商业中心以及核心家庭结构为妇女在社会中角色的转变创造了有利条件，并同样塑造了金融素养方面的性别差异。

Choices and Outcomes in Assignment Mechanisms: The Allocation of Deceased Donor Kidneys

分配机制中的选择与结果：已故捐赠者肾脏的分配

Nikhil Agarwal, Charles Hodgson, Paulo Somaini

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

While the mechanism design paradigm emphasizes notions of efficiency based on agent preferences, policymakers often focus on alternative objectives. School districts emphasize educational achievement, and transplantation communities focus on patient survival. It is unclear whether choice-based mechanisms perform well when assessed based on these outcomes. This paper evaluates the assignment mechanism for allocating deceased donor kidneys on the basis of patient life-years from transplantation (LYFT).We examine the role of choice in increasing LYFT and compare realized assignments to benchmarks that remove choice. Our model combines choices and outcomes in order to study how selection affects LYFT. We show how to identify and estimate the model using instruments derived from the mechanism. The estimates suggest that the design in use selects patients with better post-transplant survival prospects and matches them well, resulting in an average LYFT of 9.29, which is 1.75 years more than a random assignment. However, the maximum aggregate LYFT is 14.08. Realizing the majority of the gains requires transplanting relatively healthy patients, who would have longer life expectancies even without a transplant. Therefore, a policymaker faces a dilemma between transplanting patients who are sicker and those for whom life will be extended the longest.

中文摘要

尽管机制设计范式强调基于代理人偏好的效率概念，但政策制定者通常关注替代目标。学区强调教育成就，移植界关注患者生存。当基于这些结果进行评估时，基于选择的机制是否表现良好尚不清楚。本文基于患者移植后生命年数这一指标，评估了已故捐赠者肾脏的分配机制。我们考察了选择在增加移植后生命年数中的作用，并将实际分配与去除选择的基准进行了比较。我们的模型结合了选择和结果，以研究选择如何影响移植后生命年数。我们展示了如何利用源自该机制的工具变量来识别和估计该模型。估计结果表明，当前使用的设计选择了移植后生存前景更好的患者，并进行了良好匹配，导致平均移植后生命年数为9.29年，比随机分配多1.75年。然而，最大总移植后生命年数为14.08年。实现大部分收益需要移植相对健康的患者，即使没有移植，这些患者也会有更长的预期寿命。因此，政策制定者面临着在移植病情更重的患者与移植寿命延长最长的患者之间的两难选择。

FinTech Adoption and Household Risk-Taking: From Digital Payments to Platform Investments

金融科技采纳与家庭风险承担：从数字支付到平台投资

Claire Yurong Hong, Xiaomeng Lu, Jun Pan

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study household finance in the age of FinTech, where consumption, payments, and investments take place via all-in-one super-apps. We hypothesize that FinTech adoption can improve household risk-taking by breaking down the traditional physical and psychological barriers and enhance financial inclusion. Taking advantage of an individual-level FinTech dataset, we find that higher FinTech adoption, both at the individual-level and the county-level instrumented by distance-from-Hangzhou, results in higher participation and more risk-taking in mutual-fund investments. Moreover, individuals who are otherwise more constrained, those with higher risk tolerance or living in under-banked counties, stand to benefit more from the advent of FinTech.

中文摘要

我们研究了金融科技时代的家庭金融，其中消费、支付和投资通过一体化超级应用进行。我们假设，金融科技的采纳可以通过打破传统的物理和心理障碍来改善家庭风险承担，并增强金融包容性。利用个人层面的金融科技数据集，我们发现，无论是个人层面还是以距杭州距离为工具变量的县级层面，更高的金融科技采纳都会导致更高的共同基金投资参与度和更多的风险承担。此外，原本受到更多约束的个人、风险承受能力较高的人或生活在银行服务不足县的人，从金融科技的出现中获益更多。

How Would Medicare for All Affect Health System Capacity? Evidence from Medicare for Some

全民医保将如何影响卫生系统能力？来自部分医保的证据

Jeffrey Clemens, Joshua D. Gottlieb, Jeffrey Hicks

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Proposals to create a national health care plan such as "Medicare for All" rely heavily on reducing the prices that insurers pay for health care. These changes affect physicians' short-run incentives for care provision and may also change health care providers' incentives to invest in capacity, thereby influencing the availability of care in the long term. We provide evidence on these responses using a major Medicare payment change combined with survey data on physicians' time use. We find evidence that physicians increase their time spent on capacity building when remuneration increases, and that they are subsequently more willing to accept new patients---especially those who may be the residual claimants on marginal capacity. These forces imply that short-run supply curves likely differ from long-run supply curves. Policymakers need to account for how major changes to payment incentives would influence the investments that determine health system capacity.

中文摘要

创建国家医疗计划（如“全民医保”）的提案在很大程度上依赖于降低保险公司为医疗保健支付的价格。这些变化会影响医生提供护理的短期激励，也可能改变医疗保健提供者投资于能力的激励，从而影响长期护理的可及性。我们利用一次重大的医疗保险支付变化，结合医生时间使用的调查数据，提供了关于这些反应的证据。我们发现，当报酬增加时，医生会增加用于能力建设的时间，随后他们更愿意接受新患者——尤其是那些可能是边际能力剩余索取者的患者。这些力量意味着短期供给曲线可能与长期供给曲线不同。政策制定者需要考虑支付激励的重大变化将如何影响决定卫生系统能力的投资。

Answering the Call of Automation: How the Labor Market Adjusted to Mechanizing Telephone Operation

响应自动化的召唤：劳动力市场如何适应电话接线机械化

James Feigenbaum, Daniel P. Gross

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

In the early 1900s, telephone operation was among the most common jobs for American women, and telephone operators were ubiquitous. Between 1920 and 1940, AT&T undertook one of the largest automation investments in modern history, replacing operators with mechanical switching technology in over half of the U.S. telephone network. Using variation across U.S. cities in the timing of adoption, we study how this wave of automation affected the labor market for young women. Although automation eliminated most of these jobs, it did not reduce future cohorts' overall employment: the decline in operators was counteracted by employment growth in middle-skill clerical jobs and lower- skill service jobs, including in new categories of work. Using a new genealogy-based census-linking method, we show that incumbent telephone operators were most impacted, and a decade later more likely to be in lower-paying occupations or no longer working.

On one hand, users of the acquiring platform benefited from the merger because of network effects. On the other hand, users of the acquired platform experienced worse outcomes. Our results highlight the importance of platform differentiation even when platforms enjoy network effects.

Comprehensive Approaches to Increasing Student Completion in Higher Education: A Survey of the Landscape

Rachel Fulcher Dawson, Melissa S. Kearney, James X. Sullivan

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We describe the challenge of college non-completion in the U.S. and a variety of explanations for the high rate of non-completion. We then provide an overview of the implementation of and evidence from eight specific college completion interventions designed to increase college completion rates through a comprehensive set of services. The eight interventions that we review have all been implemented in the past decade and have been evaluated through randomized controlled trials (RCT). A Generalizable and Accessible Approach to Machine Learning with Global Satellite Imagery

基于全球卫星影像的机器学习通用且可及的方法

Esther Rolf, Jonathan Proctor, Tamma Carleton, Ian Bolliger, Vaishaal Shankar, Miyabi Ishihara, Benjamin Recht, Solomon Hsiang

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Combining satellite imagery with machine learning (SIML) has the potential to address global challenges by remotely estimating socioeconomic and environmental conditions in data-poor regions, yet the resource requirements of SIML limit its accessibility and use. We show that a single encoding of satellite imagery can generalize across diverse prediction tasks (e.g. forest cover, house price, road length). Our method achieves accuracy competitive with deep neural networks at orders of magnitude lower computational cost, scales globally, delivers label super-resolution predictions, and facilitates characterizations of uncertainty. Since image encodings are shared across tasks, they can be centrally computed and distributed to unlimited researchers, who need only fit a linear regression to their own ground truth data in order to achieve state-of-the-art SIML performance.
