Bank Liquidity Provision Across the Firm Size Distribution

银行流动性供给与企业规模分布

Gabriel Chodorow-Reich, Olivier Darmouni, Stephan Luck, Matthew C. Plosser

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use supervisory loan-level data to document that small firms (SMEs) obtain shorter maturity credit lines than large firms; have less active maturity management; post more collateral; have higher utilization rates; and pay higher spreads. We rationalize these facts as the equilibrium outcome of a trade-off between lender commitment and discretion. Using the COVID recession, we test the prediction that SMEs are subject to greater lender discretion by examining credit line utilization. We show that SMEs do not drawdown in contrast to large firms despite SME demand, but that PPP loans helped alleviate the shortfall.

中文摘要

我们利用监管层面的贷款数据记录到，小企业（SMEs）获得的信贷额度期限比大企业短；其期限管理活跃度较低；抵押品要求更高；信贷额度利用率更高；且支付的利差更高。我们将这些事实合理化，视为贷款人承诺与自由裁量权之间权衡的均衡结果。利用新冠疫情衰退，我们通过考察信贷额度使用情况来检验小企业面临更大贷款人自由裁量权的预测。我们发现，尽管小企业有需求，但它们并未像大企业那样提取信贷额度，而薪资保护计划（PPP）贷款有助于缓解这一短缺。

Temptation and Commitment: A Model of Hand-to-Mouth Behavior

诱惑与承诺：一个“手到口”消费行为的模型

Orazio Attanasio, Agnes Kovacs, Patrick Moran

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper presents a model of consumption behavior that explains the presence of ‘wealthy hand-to-mouth’ consumers using a mechanism that differs from those analyzed previously. We show that a two-asset model with temptation preferences generates a demand for commitment and thus illiquidity, leading to hand-to-mouth behavior even when liquid assets deliver higher returns than illiquid assets. This preference for illiquidity has important implications for consumption behaviour and for fiscal stimulus policies. Our model matches the recent empirical evidence that MPCs remain high even for large income shocks, suggesting a larger response to targeted fiscal stimulus than previously believed.

中文摘要

本文提出了一个消费行为模型，该模型通过一种与以往分析不同的机制来解释“富裕的手到口”消费者的存在。我们证明，一个包含诱惑偏好的双资产模型会产生对承诺（从而对非流动性）的需求，即使流动性资产带来的回报高于非流动性资产，也会导致手到口行为。这种对非流动性的偏好对消费行为和财政刺激政策具有重要影响。我们的模型与近期实证证据相符，即即使面对大额收入冲击，边际消费倾向（MPC）仍然很高，这表明对定向财政刺激的反应比先前认为的更大。

Medical Research and Health Care Finance: Evidence from Academic Medical Centers

医学研究与医疗融资：来自学术医疗中心的证据

Pierre Azoulay, Misty L. Heggeness, Jennifer L. Kao

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Academic Medical Centers (AMCs)—comprising medical schools, teaching hospitals, and research laboratories—play an important role in US biomedical innovation. The Balanced Budget Act of 1997 changed reimbursements for Medicare inpatient claims and subsidies for medical residents. We compare AMCs' relative exposure to the reform and how these differences affect their researchers' ability to attract NIH grant funding, as well as the quantity, impact, and content of their publications. We find that in response to the reform, research activity increases by approximately 6%, with larger effects observed for “translational” and clinical research. We find little effect on clinical outcomes.

中文摘要

学术医疗中心（AMCs）——包括医学院、教学医院和研究实验室——在美国生物医学创新中扮演着重要角色。1997年的《平衡预算法案》改变了医疗保险住院索赔的报销方式以及对医学住院医师的补贴。我们比较了各学术医疗中心对该改革的相对暴露程度，以及这些差异如何影响其研究人员吸引美国国立卫生研究院（NIH）资助的能力，以及其出版物的数量、影响和内容。我们发现，作为对该改革的回应，研究活动增加了约6%，其中对“转化”研究和临床研究的影响更大。我们几乎没有发现对临床结果的影响。

Have Exchange-Listed Firms Become Less Important for the Economy?

交易所上市公司对经济的重要性是否已下降？

Frederik P. Schlingemann, René M. Stulz

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The firms listed on the stock market in aggregate contribute less to total non-farm employment and GDP now than in the 1970s. A major reason for this development is the decline of manufacturing and the growth of the service economy as firms providing services are less likely to be listed on exchanges. A firm’s stock market capitalization is much less instructive about its employment now than in earlier years. Listed stock market superstars account for less employment than they did in the 1970s. Market capitalizations have not become systematically less informative about firms’ contribution to GDP.

中文摘要

总体而言，股票市场上市的公司对非农就业总量和国内生产总值（GDP）的贡献现在低于20世纪70年代。这一发展的一个主要原因是制造业的衰落和服务经济的增长，因为提供服务的公司上市的可能性较低。现在，一家公司的股票市值对其就业情况的指示作用远不如早年。上市股市巨星所贡献的就业量低于20世纪70年代。市值对公司GDP贡献的信息含量并未系统性降低。

A Theory of Voluntary Testing and Self-isolation in an Ongoing Pandemic

持续大流行中自愿检测与自我隔离的理论

Thomas F. Hellmann, Veikko Thiele

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Thinking beyond Covid-19, there is a growing interest in what economic structures will be needed to face ongoing pandemics. In this paper we focus on the diagnostic problem and examine a new paradigm of voluntary self-testing by private individuals. People without symptoms face daily choices of either taking the risk of going out (to work and socialize), versus staying at home in self-isolation. Our theory shows that two types of people voluntary test themselves: those who otherwise would have self-isolated, and those who would have gone out indiscriminately. Our central insight is that the equilibrium infection risk falls when home-based testing becomes cheaper and easier to use, even if tests are not always accurate. Our results challenge the clinical mainstream view that diagnostic testing is a prerogative of the medical profession, and supports the notion that frequent self-testing is vital for an economy facing an ongoing pandemic.

中文摘要

超越新冠疫情，人们越来越关注需要什么样的经济结构来应对持续的大流行。在本文中，我们聚焦于诊断问题，并研究一种由私人个体进行自愿自我检测的新范式。没有症状的人每天都面临着选择：要么冒险外出（工作和社交），要么待在家中自我隔离。我们的理论表明，有两类人会自愿进行检测：一类是原本会自我隔离的人，另一类是原本会不加区分地外出的人。我们的核心见解是，当家庭检测变得更便宜、更易用时，均衡感染风险会下降，即使检测并非总是准确。我们的结果挑战了临床主流观点——即诊断检测是医疗行业的特权，并支持了以下观点：频繁的自我检测对于面临持续大流行的经济体至关重要。

Who Benefits From the Child Tax Credit?

谁从儿童税收抵免中受益？

Jacob Goldin, Katherine Michelmore

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 西班牙、美国

Abstract

In recent years, the Child Tax Credit (CTC) has provided a cash transfer of up to $2,000 per child under age 17 to millions of families in the United States. Using the Current Population Survey, we examine the aggregate effects and distributional implications of the rules governing the credit. We focus on the rules that were in place for years prior to 2021 and that are currently scheduled to apply for years beginning in 2022. While approximately 90% of children benefit to at least some degree from the CTC, we document striking disparities in eligibility and benefits by income and race. The vast majority of children living in households in the bottom decile of the national income distribution are completely ineligible for the CTC and the majority of filers in the bottom thirty percent are eligible only for a partial credit. In contrast, virtually all children living in households in the top half of the income distribution qualify for the full credit amount. Approximately three-quarters of white, non-Hispanic and Asian children are eligible for the full CTC, compared to only about half of Black and Hispanic children. We use our results to estimate the distributional effects of a range of reforms to the CTC eligibility rules. Our results suggest that reforming the credit to include a larger share of children would more evenly distribute the credit’s benefits across children of different races and incomes.

中文摘要

近年来，儿童税收抵免（CTC）为美国数百万家庭提供了每名17岁以下儿童最高2000美元的现金转移。利用当前人口调查数据，我们研究了管理该抵免规则的总体效应和分配影响。我们重点关注2021年之前实施且目前计划从2022年开始适用的规则。虽然约90%的儿童在某种程度上受益于CTC，但我们发现按收入和种族划分的资格和福利存在显著差异。绝大多数居住在全国收入分配底层十分位家庭的儿童完全不符合CTC资格，而底层30%收入群体中的多数申报者仅能获得部分抵免。相比之下，几乎所有居住在上半部分收入分配家庭的儿童都有资格获得全额抵免。约四分之三的非西班牙裔白人和亚裔儿童有资格获得全额CTC，而黑人和西班牙裔儿童中这一比例仅约一半。我们利用研究结果估算了多项CTC资格规则改革的分配效应。结果表明，改革抵免规则以覆盖更多儿童，将更均匀地在不同种族和收入的儿童之间分配抵免福利。

Corporate Taxation and the Distribution of Income

公司税与收入分配

James R. Hines Jr.

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

Higher corporate taxes reduce corporate business operations, replacing them with operations by noncorporate businesses that are risky and have undiversified ownership. This shift contributes to income dispersion, with effects so large that higher corporate taxes can increase income inequality even when the corporate tax burden falls entirely on capital owned disproportionately by the rich. Estimates suggest that the riskiness of U.S. noncorporate business increases by 12.3% the aggregate income of the top one percent, and that income dispersion created by a higher U.S. corporate tax rate offsets more than half of the distributional effects of reducing average returns to capital.

中文摘要

更高的公司税会减少公司业务运营，取而代之的是风险较高且所有权分散的非公司企业运营。这种转变加剧了收入分散，其影响如此之大，以至于即使公司税负完全由富人不成比例拥有的资本承担，更高的公司税也可能加剧收入不平等。估计表明，美国非公司企业的风险性使最顶层1%人群的总收入增加了12.3%，而更高的美国公司税率所产生的收入分散抵消了降低资本平均回报率分配效应的一半以上。

Highways and Globalization

高速公路与全球化

Taylor Jaworski, Carl Kitchens, Sergey Nigai

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

This paper quantifies the value of US highways. We develop a multisector general equilibrium model with many locations in the United States (i.e., counties) and many countries. In the model, producers choose shipping routes subject to domestic and international trade costs, endogenous congestion, and port efficiency at international transshipment points. Applying the model, we find that removing the Interstate Highway System reduces real GDP by $421-$578 billion. The results highlight the gains from intersectoral and international trade as well as the role of domestic transportation infrastructure in shaping regional comparative advantage.

中文摘要

本文量化了美国高速公路的价值。我们构建了一个包含美国多个地点（即县）和多个国家的多部门一般均衡模型。在该模型中，生产者在面临国内和国际贸易成本、内生拥堵以及国际转运点港口效率的情况下选择运输路线。应用该模型，我们发现拆除州际公路系统将使实际GDP减少4210亿至5780亿美元。结果突显了部门间和国际贸易的收益，以及国内交通基础设施在塑造区域比较优势中的作用。

Powering Work from Home

为居家办公提供电力

Steve Cicala

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

This paper documents an increase in residential electricity consumption while industrial and commercial consumption has fallen during the COVID-19 pandemic in the United States. Hourly smart meter data from Texas reveals how daily routines changed during the pandemic, with usage during weekdays closely resembling those of weekends. The 16% residential increase during work hours offsets the declines from commercial and industrial customers. Using monthly data from electric utilities nationwide, I find a 10% increase in residential consumption, and a 12% and 14% reduction in commercial and industrial usage, respectively, during the second quarter of 2020. This contrasts with the financial crisis of 2008, which also witnessed a rapid decline in industrial electricity consumption, but left residential usage unaffected. The increase in residential consumption is found to be positively associated with the share of the labor force that may work from home. Abstract

We examine the role of trade policy uncertainty in shaping the import decisions of firms. If the adoption of a new input requires a sunk cost investment, then the prospect of price increases in that input, e.g. due to trade barriers, reduces the adoption of that input (a substitution effect) and possibly other inputs (complementarity via lower profits).
