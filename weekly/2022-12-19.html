Health Care Spending Growth Has Slowed: Will the Bend in the Curve Continue?

医疗支出增长已放缓：曲线拐点会持续吗？

Sheila D. Smith, Joseph P. Newhouse, Gigi A. Cuckler

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Over 2009-2019 the seemingly inexorable rise in health care’s share of GDP markedly slowed, both in the US and elsewhere. To address whether this slowdown represents a reduced steady-state growth rate or just a temporary pause we specify and estimate a decomposition of health care spending growth. The post-2009 slowdown was importantly influenced by four factors. Population aging increased health care’s share of GDP, but three other factors more than offset the effect of aging: a temporary income effect stemming from the Great Recession; slowing relative medical price inflation; and a possibly longer lasting slowdown in the nature of technological change to increase the rate of cost-saving innovation. Looking forward, the post-2009 moderation in the role of technological change as a driver of growth, if sustained, implies a reduction of 0.8 percentage points in health care spending growth; a sizeable decline in the context of the 2.0 percentage point differential in growth between health care spending and GDP in the 1970 to 2019 period.

中文摘要

在2009年至2019年间，美国及其他地区医疗支出占GDP比重看似不可阻挡的上升趋势显著放缓。为判断这一放缓是稳态增长率的降低还是暂时性停顿，我们设定并估计了医疗支出增长的分解。2009年后的放缓主要受四个因素影响。人口老龄化增加了医疗支出占GDP的比重，但其他三个因素超过了对老龄化的抵消效应：大衰退带来的暂时性收入效应；相对医疗价格通胀的放缓；以及可能更持久的、技术变革性质向增加节约成本创新率的转变。展望未来，如果2009年后技术变革作为增长驱动因素的作用放缓得以持续，则意味着医疗支出增长率将降低0.8个百分点；在1970年至2019年期间医疗支出与GDP增长率2.0个百分点的差异背景下，这是一个相当大的降幅。

Do Ordeals Work for Selection Markets? Evidence from Health Insurance Auto-Enrollment

磨难在甄别市场中有效吗？来自健康保险自动注册的证据

Mark Shepard, Myles Wagner

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Are application hassles, or “ordeals,” an effective way to limit public program enrollment? We provide new evidence by studying (removal of) an auto-enrollment policy for health insurance, adding an extra step to enroll. This minor ordeal has a major impact, reducing enrollment by 33% and differentially excluding young, healthy, and economically disadvantaged people. Using a simple model, we show that adverse selection – a classic feature of insurance markets – undermines ordeals’ standard rationale of excluding low-value individuals, since they are also low-cost and may not be inefficient. Our analysis illustrates why ordeals targeting is unlikely to work well in selection markets.

中文摘要

申请障碍或“磨难”是限制公共项目注册的有效方式吗？我们通过研究健康保险自动注册政策的（取消）提供了新证据，该政策增加了注册的额外步骤。这一微小磨难产生了重大影响，使注册率降低了33%，并差异性地排除了年轻、健康和经济弱势群体。通过一个简单模型，我们表明逆向选择——保险市场的一个经典特征——削弱了磨难排除低价值个体的标准理由，因为他们同时也是低成本个体，可能并非低效。我们的分析说明了为什么在甄别市场中，磨难式定向筛选不太可能有效。

Tournament-Style Political Competition and Local Protectionism: Theory and Evidence from China

锦标赛式政治竞争与地方保护主义：来自中国的理论与证据

Hanming Fang, Ming Li, Zenan Wu

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法

Abstract

We argue that inter-jurisdictional competition in a regionally decentralized authoritarian regime distorts local politicians’ incentives in resource allocation among firms from their own city and a competing city. We develop a tournament model of project selection that captures the driving forces of local protectionism. The model robustly predicts that the joint presence of regional spillover and the incentive for political competition leads to biased resource allocations against the competing regions. Combining several unique data sets, we test our model predictions in the context of government procurement allocation and firms' equity investment across Chinese cities. We find that, first, when local politicians are in more intensive political competition, they allocate less government procurement contracts to firms in the competing city; second, local firms, especially local SOEs, internalize the local politicians’ career concerns and invest less in the competing cities. Our paper provides a political economy explanation for inefficient local protectionism in an autocracy incentivized by tournament-style political competition.

中文摘要

我们认为，在区域分权威权体制下，辖区间竞争扭曲了地方政治家在来自本城市和竞争城市的企业间资源配置的激励。我们开发了一个项目选择的锦标赛模型，捕捉了地方保护主义的驱动因素。该模型稳健地预测，区域溢出效应与政治竞争激励的共同存在会导致资源分配偏向于本地区而歧视竞争地区。结合多个独特数据集，我们在中国城市间的政府采购分配和企业股权投资背景下检验了模型预测。我们发现：首先，当地方政治家面临更激烈的政治竞争时，他们分配给竞争城市企业的政府采购合同更少；其次，本地企业，尤其是地方国有企业，内化了地方政治家的职业关切，对竞争城市的投资更少。我们的论文为威权体制下由锦标赛式政治竞争激励的低效地方保护主义提供了一种政治经济学解释。

The Economics of the Public Option: Evidence from Local Pharmaceutical Markets

公共选择的经济学：来自地方药品市场的证据

Juan Pablo Atal, José Ignacio Cuesta, Felipe González, Cristóbal Otero

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 智利

Abstract

We study the effects of competition by state-owned firms, leveraging the decentralized entry of public pharmacies to local markets in Chile. Public pharmacies sell the same drugs at a third of private pharmacy prices, because of stronger upstream bargaining and market power in the private sector, but are of lower quality. Public pharmacies induced market segmentation and price increases in the private sector, which benefited the switchers to the public option but harmed the stayers. The countrywide entry of public pharmacies would reduce yearly consumer drug expenditure by 1.5 percent.

中文摘要

我们研究了国有企业竞争的影响，利用了智利公共药店向地方市场的分散化进入。公共药店以私营药店三分之一的价格销售相同药品，原因是私营部门拥有更强的上游议价能力和市场势力，但公共药店质量较低。公共药店导致了市场分割和私营部门的价格上涨，这有利于转向公共选择的消费者，但损害了留在私营部门的消费者。全国范围内公共药店的进入将使消费者年度药品支出减少1.5%。

Identification and Estimation of Discrete Choice Demand Models when Observed and Unobserved Characteristics are Correlated

当观测和未观测特征相关时离散选择需求模型的识别与估计

Amil Petrin, Mark Ponder, Boyoung Seo

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The standard Berry, Levinsohn, and Pakes (1995) (BLP) approach to estimation of demand and supply parameters assumes that the product characteristic observed by consumers and producers but not the researcher is conditionally mean independent of observed characteristics. We extend BLP to allow all product characteristics to be endogenous, so the unobserved characteristic can be correlated with the observed characteristics. We derive moment conditions based on the assumption that firms choose product characteristics to maximize expected profits given their beliefs at that time about market conditions and that the “mistake” in the amount of the characteristic that is revealed once all products are on the market is conditionally mean independent of the firm’s information set. Using the original BLP dataset we find that observed and unobserved product characteristics are highly positively correlated, biasing demand elasticities upward, as average estimated price elasticities double in absolute value and average markups fall by 50%.

中文摘要

标准的Berry、Levinsohn和Pakes（1995）（BLP）方法在估计需求和供给参数时，假设消费者和生产者观察到但研究者未观测到的产品特征在条件均值上独立于观测特征。我们将BLP扩展为允许所有产品特征内生，因此未观测特征可以与观测特征相关。我们基于以下假设推导矩条件：企业根据其对市场状况的信念选择产品特征以最大化预期利润，并且一旦所有产品上市后揭示的特征数量中的“错误”在条件均值上独立于企业的信息集。使用原始BLP数据集，我们发现观测和未观测产品特征高度正相关，导致需求弹性向上偏误，因为平均估计价格弹性的绝对值翻倍，平均加成率下降50%。

Refining Public Policies with Machine Learning: The Case of Tax Auditing

用机器学习优化公共政策：以税务审计为例

Marco Battaglini, Luigi Guiso, Chiara Lacava, Douglas L. Miller, Eleonora Patacchini

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 意大利

Abstract

We study the extent to which ML techniques can be used to improve tax auditing efficiency using administrative data, without the need of randomized audits. Using Italy's population data on sole proprietorship tax returns, audits and their outcome, we develop a new approach to address the so called selective labels problem - the fact that a ML algorithm must necessarily be trained on endogenously selected data. We document the existence of substantial margins for raising revenue from audits by improving the selection of taxpayers to audit with ML. Replacing the 10% least productive audits with an equal number of taxpayers selected by our trained algorithm raises detected tax evasion by as much as 38%, and evasion that is actually payed back by 29%.

中文摘要

我们研究了机器学习技术能在多大程度上利用行政数据提高税务审计效率，而无需随机审计。利用意大利个体经营者纳税申报、审计及其结果的全人口数据，我们开发了一种新方法来解决所谓的“选择性标签”问题——即机器学习算法必须在内生选择的数据上进行训练的事实。我们记录了通过改进机器学习选择的纳税人进行审计来提高审计收入的巨大空间。用我们训练的算法选择的同等数量纳税人替换10%效率最低的审计，可使检测到的逃税增加高达38%，实际追回的逃税增加29%。

Lasting Scars: The Impact of Depression in Early Adulthood on Subsequent Labor Market Outcomes

持久的伤痕：成年早期抑郁对后续劳动力市场结果的影响

Buyi Wang, Richard G. Frank, Sherry A. Glied

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

A growing body of evidence indicates that poor health early in life can leave lasting scars on adult health and economic outcomes. While much of this literature focuses on childhood experiences, mechanisms generating these lasting effects – recurrence of illness and interruption of human capital accumulation – are not limited to childhood. In this study, we examine how an episode of depression experienced in early adulthood affects subsequent labor market outcomes. We find that, at age 50, people who had met diagnostic criteria for depression when surveyed at ages 27-35 earn 10% lower hourly wages (conditional on occupation) and work 120-180 fewer hours annually, together generating 24% lower annual wage incomes. A portion of this income penalty (21-39%) occurs because depression is often a chronic condition, recurring later in life. But a substantial share (25-55%) occurs because depression in early adulthood disrupts human capital accumulation, by reducing work experience and by influencing selection into occupations with skill distributions that offer lower potential for wage growth. These lingering effects of early depression reinforce the importance of early and multifaceted intervention to address depression and its follow-on effects in the workplace.

中文摘要

越来越多的证据表明，生命早期的健康不佳可能对成年后的健康和经济结果留下持久伤痕。虽然这一文献主要关注童年经历，但产生这些持久影响的机制——疾病复发和人力资本积累中断——并不仅限于童年。在本研究中，我们考察了成年早期经历的抑郁发作如何影响后续劳动力市场结果。我们发现，在50岁时，那些在27-35岁调查时符合抑郁症诊断标准的人，小时工资（按职业条件）低10%，年工作时间少120-180小时，共同导致年工资收入低24%。这部分收入惩罚（21-39%）是因为抑郁症通常是一种慢性疾病，会在以后的生活中复发。但很大一部分（25-55%）是因为成年早期的抑郁症通过减少工作经验以及影响进入技能分布提供较低工资增长潜力的职业选择，从而破坏了人力资本积累。早期抑郁的这些持久影响强化了早期和多方面干预以应对抑郁症及其在工作场所后续效应的重要性。

Search Frictions and Product Design in the Municipal Bond Market

市政债券市场中的搜索摩擦与产品设计

Giulia Brancaccio, Karam Kang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

This paper shows that product design shapes search frictions and that intermediaries leverage this channel to increase their rents in the context of the U.S. municipal bond market. The majority of bonds are designed via negotiations between a local government and its underwriter. They are then traded in a decentralized market, where the underwriter often also acts as an intermediary. Exploiting variations in state regulations that limit government officials’ conflicts of interest, we provide evidence that bond design from the government’s perspective involves a trade-off between flexibility and liquidity, but the underwriter benefits from designing and trading complex bonds. Motivated by these findings, we build and estimate a model of bond origination and trades to quantify market inefficiency driven by underwriters’ role in intermediating trades and discuss policy implications.

中文摘要

本文表明，产品设计塑造了搜索摩擦，而中介机构利用这一渠道在美国市政债券市场中增加其租金。大多数债券是通过地方政府与其承销商之间的谈判设计的。这些债券随后在分散化市场中进行交易，承销商通常也充当中介角色。利用限制政府官员利益冲突的州法规差异，我们提供了证据表明，从政府角度看，债券设计涉及灵活性与流动性之间的权衡，但承销商从设计和交易复杂债券中获益。受这些发现的启发，我们构建并估计了一个债券发行和交易模型，以量化由承销商在交易中介中的角色所驱动的市场低效率，并讨论政策含义。

Heterogeneous Downward Nominal Wage Rigidity: Foundations of a Nonlinear Phillips Curve

异质性向下名义工资刚性：非线性菲利普斯曲线的基础

Stephanie Schmitt-Grohé, Martín Uribe

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We propose a model with heterogeneous downward nominal wage rigidity for individual labor varieties. The model delivers a nonlinear wage Phillips curve linking current wage inflation with current unemployment that is relatively steep at high levels of inflation and flat at low levels of inflation. The predicted nonlinear Phillips curve matches well the pattern of wage inflation and unemployment observed in the United States over the past 40 years.
