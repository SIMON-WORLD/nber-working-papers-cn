Opioid Use, Health and Crime: Insights from a Rapid Reduction in Heroin Supply

阿片类药物使用、健康与犯罪：来自海洛因供应急剧减少的启示

Timothy J. Moore, Kevin T. Schnepel

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 澳大利亚

Abstract

In 2001, a large and sustained supply shock halted a heroin epidemic in Australia. We use drug offenses to identify individual opioid users and examine how the shock affected their mortality risks and criminal activity over the next eight years. Initially, gains from fewer overdoses are offset by drug substitution and more crime, including homicides. Most adverse effects dissipate over time, while persistent mortality reductions save the lives of around one in 48 individuals in our sample. Our results demonstrate that reducing the supply of illicit opioids can lead to meaningful longer-term improvements, even when the short-term effects are ambiguous.

中文摘要

2001年，一次大规模且持续的供应冲击遏制了澳大利亚的海洛因流行。我们利用毒品犯罪来识别个体阿片类药物使用者，并考察该冲击如何影响他们在接下来八年中的死亡风险和犯罪活动。最初，因过量服用减少带来的收益被药物替代和更多犯罪（包括凶杀）所抵消。大多数不利影响随时间消散，而持续的死亡率降低挽救了我们样本中大约每48人中有1人的生命。我们的结果表明，减少非法阿片类药物的供应可以带来有意义的长期改善，即使短期效果尚不明确。

Agglomeration Economies and Race Specific Spillovers

集聚经济与种族特定溢出效应

Elizabeth Ananat, Shihe Fu, Stephen Ross

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Racial social isolation within and across workplaces may reduce firm productivity. We provide descriptive evidence that African-Americans feel socially isolated from Whites. To test whether isolation affects productivity, we estimate models of Total Factor Productivity for manufacturing firms allowing returns to local area concentrations of economic activity and human capital spillovers to vary with the racial and ethnic composition of both the establishment and the local area employment. Higher own-race exposure for establishment workers to workers at surrounding establishments strengthens the relationship between productivity and both employment density and concentrations of college educated workers. Effects for human capital spillovers are largest for firms with more patents and more research and development spending. Looming demographic changes suggest that this drag on productivity may increase over time.

中文摘要

工作场所内部及跨工作场所的种族社会隔离可能降低企业生产率。我们提供了非裔美国人在白人群体中感到社会隔离的描述性证据。为了检验隔离是否影响生产率，我们估计了制造业企业的全要素生产率模型，允许经济活动本地集中度的回报和人力资本溢出效应随企业及本地就业的种族和民族构成而变化。企业员工与周边企业员工更高的同种族接触率，增强了生产率与就业密度及大学教育程度工人集中度之间的关系。人力资本溢出效应对于拥有更多专利和更多研发支出的企业影响最大。即将到来的人口结构变化表明，这种对生产率的拖累可能会随时间加剧。

More than an Ivory Tower: The Impact of Research Institutions on the Quantity and Quality of Entrepreneurship

不仅仅是象牙塔：研究机构对创业数量和质量的影响

Valentina Tartari, Scott Stern

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper provides systematic empirical evidence for the distinctive role of universities on local entrepreneurial ecosystems. Assessing the impact of research institutions on entrepreneurship is challenging, given that these institutions are often located in economic and innovation environments conducive to growth-oriented entrepreneurial activity, are themselves a source of local demand, and produce knowledge, which might serve as the foundation for new ventures. To overcome this inference challenge, we first combine comprehensive business registration records with a predictive analytics approach to measure both the quantity and quality-adjusted quantity of entrepreneurship at the zip-code level on an annual basis. We then link each location to the presence or absence of research-oriented universities or national laboratories. Finally, we exploit significant changes over time in Federal commitments to both universities and national laboratories. Our key finding is that changes in Federal research commitments to universities are uniquely linked to positively correlated changes in the quality-adjusted quantity of entrepreneurship. In contrast, increases in non-research funding to universities and funding to national laboratories is associated with either a neutral or negative impact on the quality-adjusted quantity of entrepreneurship. Research funding to universities seems to play a unique role in promoting the acceleration of local entrepreneurial ecosystems.

中文摘要

本文为大学在本地创业生态系统中的独特作用提供了系统的经验证据。评估研究机构对创业的影响具有挑战性，因为这些机构通常位于有利于增长导向型创业活动的经济和创新环境中，它们本身是本地需求的来源，并产生可能成为新企业基础的知识。为了克服这一推断挑战，我们首先将全面的商业注册记录与预测分析方法相结合，以年度为基础衡量邮政编码层面的创业数量和质量调整后的数量。然后，我们将每个地点与研究型大学或国家实验室的存在与否联系起来。最后，我们利用联邦对大学和国家实验室承诺的随时间显著变化。我们的关键发现是，联邦对大学研究承诺的变化与质量调整后创业数量的正相关变化独特地联系在一起。相比之下，对大学非研究经费的增加以及对国家实验室经费的增加，与质量调整后创业数量的中性或负面影响相关。对大学的研究经费似乎在促进本地创业生态系统加速方面发挥着独特作用。

Making Entrepreneurs: Returns to Training Youth in Hard Versus Soft Business Skills

培养创业者：对青年进行硬技能与软技能商业培训的回报

Laura Chioda, David Contreras-Loya, Paul Gertler, Dana Carney

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 乌干达

Abstract

We study the medium-term impacts of the Skills for Effective Entrepreneurship Development (SEED) program, an innovative in-residence 3-week mini-MBA program for high school students modeled after western business school curricula and adapted to the Ugandan context. The program featured two separate treatments: the hard-skills MBA features a mix of approximately 75% hard skills and 25% soft skills; the soft skills curriculum has the reverse mix. Using data on 4400 youth from a nationally representative sample in a 3-arm field experiment in Uganda, the 3.5 year follow-up demonstrated that training was effective in improving both hard and soft skills, but only soft skills were directly linked to improvements in self-efficacy, persuasion, and negotiation. The skill upgrade was rewarded in substantially higher earnings; 32.1% and 29.8% increases in earnings for those who attended hard- and soft-training, respectively, most of which, was generated through self-mployment. Furthermore, youth in both groups were more likely to start enterprises and more successful in ensuring their businesses' survival. The program led to significantly larger profits (24.2% and 27.2% for hard- and soft- treatment arms respectively) and larger business capital investments (38.4% and 32.6% for SEED hard and SEED soft, respectively). Both SEED curricula were very cost-effective; two months worth of the extra earnings caused by the training alone would exceed the cost of the program. These benefits abstract from the job- and business-creation benefits of the program, which were substantial: relative to the control group, SEED entrepreneurs created 985 additional jobs and 550 new businesses.

中文摘要

我们研究了有效创业发展技能项目的长期影响，这是一个创新的、为期三周的住宿式迷你MBA项目，面向高中生，模仿西方商学院课程并根据乌干达国情进行调整。该项目设有两个独立的处理组：硬技能MBA课程大约包含75%的硬技能和25%的软技能；软技能课程则相反。利用来自乌干达全国代表性样本中4400名青年的数据，在为期三年半的跟踪调查中，我们发现培训有效地提高了硬技能和软技能，但只有软技能与自我效能感、说服力和谈判能力的提升直接相关。技能提升带来了显著更高的收入；参加硬技能和软技能培训的人收入分别增长了32.1%和29.8%，其中大部分来自自雇就业。此外，两个组的青年都更有可能创办企业，并且在确保企业生存方面更为成功。该项目带来了显著更高的利润（硬技能和软技能处理组分别增长24.2%和27.2%）和更大的商业资本投资（硬技能和软技能处理组分别增长38.4%和32.6%）。两个课程都非常具有成本效益；仅由培训带来的额外收入中两个月的收入就超过了项目成本。这些收益还不包括项目带来的就业和创业创造效益，这些效益相当可观：与对照组相比，项目创业者创造了985个额外就业岗位和550家新企业。

Learning in the Household

家庭中的学习

John J. Conlon, Malavika Mani, Gautam Rao, Matthew W. Ridley, Frank Schilbach

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 印度

Abstract

We study social learning between spouses using an experiment in Chennai, India. We vary whether individuals discover information themselves or must instead learn what their spouse discovered via a discussion. Women treat their 'own' and their husband's information the same. In sharp contrast, men's beliefs respond less than half as much to information that was discovered by their wife. This is not due to a lack of communication: husbands put less weight on their wife's signals even when perfectly informed of them. In a second experiment, when paired with mixed- and same-gender strangers, both men and women heavily discount their teammate's information relative to their own. We conclude that people have a tendency to underweight others' information relative to their own. The marital context creates a countervailing force for women, resulting in a gender difference in learning (only) in the household.

中文摘要

我们通过印度金奈的一项实验研究了配偶之间的社会学习。我们改变了信息发现的方式：是个人自己发现信息，还是必须通过讨论了解配偶发现的信息。女性对待“自己的”信息和丈夫的信息方式相同。与此形成鲜明对比的是，男性对妻子发现的信息的反应程度不到其自身信息的一半。这并非由于缺乏沟通：即使丈夫完全知晓妻子的信号，他们也会对其赋予较低的权重。在第二个实验中，当与混合性别和同性别的陌生人配对时，男性和女性都严重低估队友的信息相对于自己信息的价值。我们得出结论，人们倾向于低估他人信息相对于自己信息的价值。婚姻背景为女性创造了一种反作用力，导致（仅在）家庭内部出现学习方面的性别差异。

How Resilient Is Mortgage Credit Supply? Evidence from the COVID-19 Pandemic

抵押贷款信贷供给的韧性有多强？来自COVID-19大流行的证据

Andreas Fuster, Aurel Hizmo, Lauren Lambie-Hanson, James Vickery, Paul S. Willen

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We study the resilience of US mortgage credit supply during the COVID-19 pandemic—the most significant shock since the financial crisis—and draw out broader lessons about the functioning of this important market. While mortgage lending boomed in 2020-21, we find that a sharp increase in intermediation markups limited the passthrough of low rates to households. We link this increase in markups to capacity constraints amplified by pandemic-related operational and labor market frictions. We also present new evidence that capacity constraints in the mortgage market are national in scope and have not yet been significantly mitigated by recent technological change. Nonbank lenders, often thought to be fragile, gained market share from banks but remain reliant on securitization. We also find evidence that government credit guarantees support the flow of credit to risky borrowers but are not always sufficient, and that quantitative easing boosts supply particularly for the types of loans being purchased.

中文摘要

我们研究了美国抵押贷款信贷供给在COVID-19大流行期间的韧性——这是自金融危机以来最重大的冲击——并从中提炼出关于这一重要市场运作的更广泛教训。尽管2020-21年抵押贷款发放量激增，但我们发现，中介加价的大幅增加限制了低利率向家庭的传导。我们将这种加价增加与产能限制联系起来，而疫情相关的运营和劳动力市场摩擦加剧了这些限制。我们还提出了新的证据，表明抵押贷款市场的产能限制是全国性的，并且尚未被近期的技术变革显著缓解。非银行放贷机构（通常被认为脆弱）从银行手中夺取了市场份额，但仍然依赖证券化。我们还发现，政府信贷担保支持了向高风险借款人的信贷流动，但并不总是足够的；量化宽松尤其增加了对所购买贷款类型的供给。

Sorting Out the Real Effects of Credit Supply

厘清信贷供给的真实效应

Briana Chang, Matthieu Gomez, Harrison Hong

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We document that banks which cut lending more during the Great Recession were lending to riskier firms. To explain this evidence, we build a competitive matching model of bank-firm relationships in which risky firms borrow from banks with low holding costs. Based on default probabilities and equilibrium loan rates, we use our sorting model to recover the latent bank holding cost distribution. The measure of banks with low holding costs dropped during the Great Recession. This credit supply shift conservatively accounted for around 50% of the decline in corporate loans over this period. Our attribution cannot be captured by panel regression estimates from the bank lending channel literature.

中文摘要

我们记录了在大衰退期间削减贷款更多的银行，其贷款对象是风险更高的企业。为了解释这一证据，我们构建了一个银行-企业关系的竞争性匹配模型，其中风险企业从持有成本较低的银行借款。基于违约概率和均衡贷款利率，我们利用排序模型来恢复潜在的银行持有成本分布。在大衰退期间，低持有成本银行的数量下降了。保守估计，这一信贷供给的转变解释了该时期企业贷款下降的约50%。我们的归因无法被银行贷款渠道文献中的面板回归估计所捕捉。

Suburbanization in the United States 1970-2010

美国郊区化：1970-2010

Stephen J. Redding

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

The second half of the twentieth century saw large-scale suburbanization in the United States, with the median share of residents who work in the same county where they live falling from 87 to 71 percent between 1970 and 2000. We introduce a new methodology for discriminating between the three leading explanations for this suburbanization (workplace attractiveness, residence attractiveness and bilateral commuting frictions). This methodology holds in the class of spatial models that are characterized by a structural gravity equation for commuting. We show that the increased openness of counties to commuting is mainly explained by reductions in bilateral commuting frictions, consistent with the expansion of the interstate highway network and the falling real cost of car ownership. We find that changes in workplace attractiveness and residence attractiveness are more important in explaining the observed shift in employment by workplace and employment by residence towards lower densities over time.

本文利用创业制图项目来自美国八个州的数据，为新冠疫情后创业性质与地理格局的变化提供了新见解。与其他数据来源一致，在经历了最初的下降之后，各州的企业注册总量不仅反弹，而且在所有八个州均有所增长。我们重点关注各州内部及跨州之间新企业形成动态模式的显著异质性。具体而言，不同种族和社会经济地位的社区在新企业注册者的动态变化上存在显著差异。在2019年至2020年间，黑人居民比例较高，特别是中等收入较高的黑人社区，其创业形成率增长更高。此外，这些动态变化也反映在主要联邦救助计划的通过中。尽管诸如《CARES法案》之类的立法并未直接支持新企业形成，但救助计划的通过和实施之后，创业形成率相对上升，尤其是在中等收入较高且黑人居民比例较高的社区。
