No. 1论文详情NBER w30072

A Structural Approach to High-Frequency Event Studies: The Fed and Markets as Case History

Francesco Bianchi, Sydney C. Ludvigson, Sai Ma

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

What do markets learn from newsworthy events? We develop a methodology for integrating a high-frequency event study into a macro-finance model and structural estimation, and apply it to Federal Reserve announcements. Beliefs about the economic state and about regime change in future policy jump in response to monetary news and move subjective risk premia through two main channels, macro volatility and a Fed put, with the Fed put accounting for most of the resulting movement in premia. Such jumps often generate positive comovement between short rates and the stock market, erroneously suggesting “Fed information shocks.”

Idiosyncrasy as a Leading Indicator

特质性作为领先指标

Randall Morck, Bernard Yeung, Lu Y. Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Disequilibrating macro shocks affect different firms' prospects differently, increasing idiosyncratic variation in forward-looking stock returns before affecting economic growth. Consistent with most such shocks from 1947 to 2020 enhancing productivity, increased idiosyncratic stock return variation forecasts next-quarter real GDP growth, industrial production growth, and consumption growth both in-sample and out-of-sample. These effects persist after controlling for other leading economic indicators.

中文摘要

破坏均衡的宏观冲击对不同企业的前景产生不同影响，在影响经济增长之前，增加了前瞻性股票回报的特质性变异。与1947年至2020年间大多数此类冲击提高生产率相一致，特质性股票回报变异的增加在样本内和样本外均预测了下一季度的实际GDP增长、工业生产增长和消费增长。这些效应在控制其他领先经济指标后仍然存在。

No. 3论文详情NBER w30070

The Crime Effect of Refugees

难民对犯罪的影响

Mevlude Akbulut-Yuksel, Naci H. Mocan, Semih Tumen, Belgi Turan

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 叙利亚、土耳其

Abstract

We analyze the impact on crime of 3.7 million refugees who entered and stayed in Turkey as a result of the civil war in Syria. Using a novel administrative data source on the flow of offense records to prosecutors’ offices in 81 provinces of the country each year, and utilizing the staggered movement of refugees across provinces over time, we estimate instrumental variables models that address potential endogeneity of the number of refugees and their location, and find that an increase in the number of refugees leads to more crime. We estimate that the influx of refugees between 2012 and 2016 generated additional 75,000 to 150,000 crimes per year, although it is not possible to identify the distribution of these crimes between refugees and natives. Additional analyses reveal that low-educated native population has a separate, but smaller, effect on crime. We also highlight the pitfalls of employing incorrect empirical procedures and using poor proxies of criminal activity which produce the wrong inference about the refugee-crime relationship. Our results underline the need to quickly strengthen the social safety systems, to take actions to dampen the impact on the labor market, and to provide support to the criminal justice system in order to mitigate the repercussions of massive influx of individuals into a country, and to counter the social and political backlash that typically emerges in the wake of such large-scale population movements.

中文摘要

我们分析了因叙利亚内战而进入并留在土耳其的370万难民对犯罪的影响。利用关于每年全国81个省检察官办公室犯罪记录流量的新颖行政数据源，并利用难民随时间跨省交错流动的情况，我们估计了工具变量模型，以解决难民数量及其所在地的潜在内生性问题，发现难民数量的增加导致更多犯罪。我们估计，2012年至2016年间难民的涌入每年额外产生了7.5万至15万起犯罪，尽管无法确定这些犯罪在难民和本地人之间的分布。进一步分析显示，低教育程度的本地人口对犯罪有独立但较小的影响。我们还强调了采用错误实证程序和使用不良犯罪活动代理变量所产生的陷阱，这些做法会导致关于难民与犯罪关系的错误推断。我们的结果强调了迅速加强社会安全体系、采取措施减轻对劳动力市场的影响、以及为刑事司法系统提供支持的必要性，以缓解大量人口涌入一个国家的后果，并应对通常在此类大规模人口流动之后出现的社会和政治反弹。

Agricultural Crises and Government Responses Between the World Wars in the Atlantic Trading Network

两次世界大战期间大西洋贸易网络中的农业危机与政府应对

Price V. Fishback

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

The paper summarizes research on the heterogeneous experiences of actors in agriculture in Europe and the Americas between the First and Second World Wars. Following a period of increasing globalization of agricultural markets, the First World War sharply limited farming in the main combatant nations, which led to sharp increases in agricultural prices and farm incomes in countries outside the combat zones. During the 1920s the combatants experienced a return to normalcy, while farmers that experienced booms during the war went through hard times. During the Great Depression that followed, farm prices for most goods fell sharply and farm regions were flooded with unemployed workers. During both decades, most countries responded by raising tariffs and setting quotas on farm imports in an attempt to protect farmers, most often large farmers, against the drops in prices. After experimenting with aiding farmers through price guarantees in the 1920s, nearly every government in the 1930s regulated agriculture in some new way: by providing subsidies, setting minimum prices, purchasing surpluses, or limiting output. Often the regulations led to problems that led to new regulatory fixes while setting the precedents for the domestic farm programs that continue to protect farmers in the modern era.

中文摘要

本文总结了关于第一次和第二次世界大战之间欧洲和美洲农业参与者异质性经验的研究。在农业市场日益全球化的时期之后，第一次世界大战严重限制了主要参战国的农业，导致非战区国家农产品价格和农业收入急剧上升。在1920年代，参战国恢复了正常状态，而战时经历繁荣的农民则经历了困难时期。在随后的大萧条期间，大多数农产品价格大幅下跌，农业地区充斥着失业工人。在这两个十年中，大多数国家通过提高关税和设定农产品进口配额来应对，试图保护农民（通常是大农场主）免受价格下跌的影响。在1920年代尝试通过价格保证援助农民之后，1930年代几乎所有政府都以某种新方式监管农业：提供补贴、设定最低价格、购买剩余产品或限制产量。这些监管常常导致问题，进而引发新的监管修补，同时为现代继续保护农民的国内农业计划开创了先例。

Urban Sprawl and Social Capital: Evidence from Indonesian Cities

城市蔓延与社会资本：来自印度尼西亚城市的证据

Andrea Civelli, Arya Gaduh, Alexander D. Rothenberg, Yao Wang

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 印度、印度尼西亚

Abstract

We use detailed data from Indonesian cities to study how variation in density within urban areas affects social capital. For identification, we instrument density with soil characteristics, and control for community averages of observed characteristics. Under plausible assumptions, these controls address sorting on observables and unobservables. We find that lower density increases trust in neighbors and community participation. We also find that lower density is associated with lower interethnic tolerance, but this relationship is explained by sorting. Heterogeneity analysis suggests that crime in dense areas undermines community trust and participation, intensifying the negative impact of density.

中文摘要

我们利用印度尼西亚城市的详细数据，研究城市区域内密度变化如何影响社会资本。为了识别，我们使用土壤特征作为密度的工具变量，并控制社区平均观测特征。在合理假设下，这些控制解决了基于可观测和不可观测特征的分类问题。我们发现，较低的密度增加了对邻居的信任和社区参与。我们还发现，较低的密度与较低的族裔间容忍度相关，但这种关系可由分类解释。异质性分析表明，密集地区的犯罪破坏了社区信任和参与，加剧了密度的负面影响。

No. 6论文详情NBER w30067

Safety Nets and Social Welfare Expenditures in World Economic History

世界经济史中的安全网与社会福利支出

Price V. Fishback

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 德国

Abstract

The safety nets in high-income countries before 1900 and in low-income countries today were based on savings and aid from extended family, friends, charities, churches, and small amounts from local governments. Mutual societies and eventually insurance companies offered insurance against lost earnings from sickness, injury, death, and old age. Germany led the way in mandating that employers provide benefits. Since 1900 higher income nations have sharply increased public and private social welfare expenditures to well over 20 percent relative to GDP. A large share of this rise has come in increases in aid to the elderly and health care expenses, often in the form of contributory social insurance financed by payroll taxes on workers and employers. Meanwhile, noncontributory transfer programs for the poor have risen relatively little. In most countries, the employer’s share of payroll taxes are higher than the worker’s share. There are some major countries who have followed a path of reliance on private programs, which are largely financed by employers. Probably the most striking feature of social welfare programs world-wide is the very large variation in expenditures relative to GDP, in the categories of spending, and in the mix of taxation, private programs, and government programs.

中文摘要

1900年前高收入国家和当今低收入国家的安全网基于大家庭、朋友、慈善机构、教会以及地方政府少量资金的储蓄和援助。互助协会以及后来的保险公司提供了针对疾病、伤害、死亡和老年收入损失的保险。德国率先强制雇主提供福利。自1900年以来，高收入国家大幅增加了公共和私人社会福利支出，占GDP比重超过20%。这一增长中很大一部分来自对老年人援助和医疗保健支出的增加，通常以工人和雇主工资税资助的缴费型社会保险形式出现。与此同时，针对贫困的非缴费型转移支付项目增长相对较少。在大多数国家，雇主承担的工资税份额高于工人。一些主要国家遵循依赖私人项目的路径，这些项目主要由雇主资助。全球社会福利项目最显著的特征可能是支出占GDP比重、支出类别以及税收、私人项目和政府项目组合的巨大差异。

No. 7论文详情NBER w30066

Markets, Governments, and the Institutional Structure of Social Welfare Expenditures in the United States and Sweden in the 20th Century

20世纪美国和瑞典的市场、政府与社会福利支出的制度结构

Price V. Fishback

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 瑞典、美国

Abstract

Social welfare spending on health, welfare, and insurance against adverse outcomes expanded a great deal in all of the developed countries during the 20th century. The institutional structure of the spending varies with respect to the extent that governments or market institutions provide the services. Sweden and the United States are on opposite ends of this spectrum. After discussing the problems with adverse selection and moral hazard that bedevil private and public social welfare organizations, I compare the development of the social welfare institutions in the U.S. and Sweden in the 20th century.

中文摘要

20世纪，所有发达国家在健康、福利和针对不利结果的保险方面的社会福利支出大幅扩张。支出的制度结构因政府或市场机构提供服务的程度而异。瑞典和美国处于这一光谱的两端。在讨论了困扰私人和公共社会福利组织的逆向选择和道德风险问题之后，我比较了20世纪美国和瑞典社会福利制度的发展。

Bayesian Persuasion with Lie Detection

带有谎言检测的贝叶斯说服

Florian Ederer, Weicheng Min

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

How does lie detection constrain the potential for one person to persuade another to change her action? We consider a model of Bayesian persuasion in which the Receiver can detect lies with positive probability. We show that the Sender lies more when the lie detection probability increases. As long as this probability is sufficiently small, the Sender’s and the Receiver’s equilibrium payoffs are unaffected by the presence of lie detection because the Sender simply compensates by lying more. However, when the lie detection probability is sufficiently high, the Sender’s equilibrium payoff decreases and the Receiver’s equilibrium payoff increases with the lie detection probability. We explore several extensions including partial commitment, general state and action spaces, and different detection technologies and show that our model’s main insights continue to hold.

中文摘要

谎言检测如何限制一个人说服另一个人改变其行为的潜力？我们考虑了一个贝叶斯说服模型，其中接收方能够以正概率检测到谎言。我们表明，当谎言检测概率增加时，发送方会更多地撒谎。只要这个概率足够小，发送方和接收方的均衡收益就不会受到谎言检测的影响，因为发送方只是通过更多地撒谎来补偿。然而，当谎言检测概率足够高时，发送方的均衡收益会随着谎言检测概率的增加而减少，而接收方的均衡收益则会增加。我们探讨了多种扩展情况，包括部分承诺、一般状态和行动空间以及不同的检测技术，并表明我们模型的主要见解仍然成立。

Reserve System Design for Allocation of Scarce Medical Resources in a Pandemic: Some Perspectives from the Field

大流行期间稀缺医疗资源分配的储备系统设计：来自实地的一些视角

Parag A. Pathak, Govind Persad, Tayfun Sönmez, M. Utku Ünver

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The Covid-19 pandemic has brought renewed attention to rationing guidelines for scarce medical resources. This paper describes the benefits of using a reserve system over a priority system in operationalizing compromises between certain ethical goals. In the last two years, more than a dozen states and local jurisdictions have adopted reserve systems in initial phases of vaccine distribution. We highlight several design issues arising in some of these implementations.

中文摘要

新冠疫情重新引起了人们对稀缺医疗资源配给指南的关注。本文描述了在实现某些伦理目标之间的折衷时，使用储备系统相对于优先级系统的优势。在过去两年中，超过十二个州和地方管辖区在疫苗分配的初始阶段采用了储备系统。我们强调了其中一些实施中出现的设计问题。

No. 10论文详情NBER w30063

Safeguarding Consumers Through Minimum Quality Standards: Milk Inspections and Urban Mortality, 1880-1910

通过最低质量标准保护消费者：牛奶检查与城市死亡率，1880-1910

D. Mark Anderson, Kerwin Kofi Charles, Michael McKelligott, Daniel I. Rees

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine the effect of enforcing minimum quality standards (MQSs) on consumer health. In the late 1800s, the urban milk supply was regularly skimmed and diluted with water, but consumers could not easily determine its quality because dyes, caramel, and salt were added. To protect consumers, milk inspectors were tasked with enforcing a well-defined MQS. Using city-level data for the period 1880-1910, we find that milk inspections reduced mortality from waterborne and foodborne diseases by 12-19 percent. Ours is the first study to provide evidence that MQSs can improve consumer health when directly applied to an experience or credence good.

中文摘要

我们研究了执行最低质量标准对消费者健康的影响。在19世纪末，城市牛奶供应经常被撇去脂肪并用水稀释，但由于添加了染料、焦糖和盐，消费者难以轻易判断其质量。为了保护消费者，牛奶检查员被指派执行明确的最低质量标准。利用1880-1910年间的城市层面数据，我们发现牛奶检查使水媒和食源性疾病死亡率降低了12-19%。我们的研究首次提供了证据，表明当最低质量标准直接应用于经验品或信任品时，可以改善消费者健康。

Gender Differences in Response to Competitive Organization? Differences Across Fields from a Product Development Platform Field Experiment

竞争性组织中的性别差异？来自产品开发平台实地实验的跨领域差异

Kevin Boudreau, Nilam Kaushik

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Prior research, primarily based on lab experiments, suggests that females might be more averse to competition than males and could be more inclined towards collaboration, instead. Were these findings to generalize to adults across the workforce, there could be profound implications for organizational design and personnel management. We report on a field experiment in which 97,678 adults from a wide range of fields and ages were invited to join a product development opportunity. Individuals were randomly assigned to treatments framing the opportunity as either involving competitive or collaborative interactions with other participants. Among those outside of science, technology, engineering, and math fields (STEM), we find significant gender differences in willingness to participate under competition. Among those in STEM fields, we detect no statistical gender differences. These results and broader patterns documented in the study are consistent with significant heterogeneity in competitiveness across both men and women, with field and career sorting resulting in differences (in gender differences) across fields.

中文摘要

先前的研究（主要基于实验室实验）表明，女性可能比男性更厌恶竞争，并且可能更倾向于合作。如果这些发现能推广到整个劳动力市场的成年人，那么可能对组织设计和人事管理产生深远影响。我们报告了一项实地实验，其中邀请了来自广泛领域和年龄段的97,678名成年人参与一个产品开发机会。个体被随机分配到不同的处理组，这些处理组将机会描述为涉及与其他参与者的竞争性或合作性互动。在科学、技术、工程和数学领域之外的人群中，我们发现参与竞争的意愿存在显著的性别差异。在科学、技术、工程和数学领域的人群中，我们没有检测到统计上的性别差异。这些结果以及研究中记录更广泛的模式，与男性和女性在竞争性方面存在显著异质性一致，而领域和职业筛选导致了跨领域的性别差异。

No. 12论文详情NBER w30061

Air Pollution and Student Performance in the U.S.

美国空气污染与学生表现

Michael Gilraine, Angela Zheng

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

We combine satellite-based pollution data and test scores from over 10,000 U.S. school districts to estimate the relationship between air pollution and test scores. To deal with potential endogeneity we instrument for air quality using (i) year-to-year coal production variation and (ii) a shift-share instrument that interacts fuel shares used for nearby power production with national growth rates. We find that each one-unit increase in particulate pollution reduces test scores by 0.02 standard deviations. Our findings indicate that declines in particulate pollution exposure raised test scores and reduced the black-white test score gap by 0.06 and 0.01 standard deviations, respectively.

中文摘要

我们结合基于卫星的污染数据和来自美国超过10,000个学区的考试成绩，来估计空气污染与考试成绩之间的关系。为了处理潜在的内生性问题，我们使用以下工具变量来衡量空气质量：(i) 年度煤炭产量变化，以及 (ii) 一个将附近电力生产的燃料份额与全国增长率相互作用的份额-转移工具变量。我们发现，颗粒物污染每增加一个单位，考试成绩就会降低0.02个标准差。我们的研究结果表明，颗粒物污染暴露的下降分别使考试成绩提高了0.06个标准差，并使黑人与白人之间的考试成绩差距缩小了0.01个标准差。

Real Effects of Supplying Safe Private Money

提供安全私人货币的真实效应

Chenzi Xu, He Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Privately issued money often bears devaluation risk that create monetary transaction frictions. We evaluate the real effects of supplying a new type of safe money in the historical context of the US in 1863. We instrument for the change in monetary frictions locally using regulatory capital requirements and measure the degree safe money access with a market access approach derived from general equilibrium trade theory. Lowering monetary transaction costs increased traded goods production and spurred structural transformation with more manufacturing output, employment, and urban population. The growth in manufacturing was driven by employment and inputs rather than capital investment.

中文摘要

私人发行的货币通常带有贬值风险，这会产生货币交易摩擦。我们以1863年美国的历史背景为背景，评估了提供一种新型安全货币的真实效应。我们使用监管资本要求作为工具变量来衡量当地货币摩擦的变化，并采用源自一般均衡贸易理论的市场准入方法来衡量安全货币的可及性程度。降低货币交易成本增加了贸易品的生产，并推动了结构性转型，带来了更多的制造业产出、就业和城市人口。制造业的增长是由就业和投入驱动的，而非资本投资。

Exorbitant Privilege Gained and Lost: Fiscal Implications

获得与失去的过高特权：财政影响

Zefeng Chen, Zhengyang Jiang, Hanno Lustig, Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh, Mindy Z. Xiaolan

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 英国

Abstract

We study three centuries of U.K. fiscal history. Before WW-I, when the U.K. dominated global bond markets, the U.K.’s government debt was not always fully backed by its future surpluses, even after accounting for the seigniorage revenue from convenience yields. As predicted by theories of safe asset determination, investors concentrate extra fiscal capacity in a single country, the global safe asset supplier, based on relative macro fundamentals, and its debt growth may temporarily outstrip what is warranted by its own macro fundamentals. After the relative deterioration in U.K. fundamentals, due to the run-up in debt during WW-I and WW-II, bond investors focused exclusively on the U.K’s own macro fundamentals. Since then the U.K. debt has been fully backed by surpluses.

中文摘要

我们研究了三个世纪的英国财政历史。在第一次世界大战之前，当英国主导全球债券市场时，英国政府债务并非总是完全由其未来盈余支持，即使在考虑了来自便利收益的铸币税收入之后也是如此。正如安全资产决定理论所预测的那样，投资者根据相对宏观基本面，将额外的财政能力集中在一个国家，即全球安全资产供应国，其债务增长可能暂时超过其自身宏观基本面所保证的水平。在第一次世界大战和第二次世界大战期间债务累积导致英国基本面相对恶化之后，债券投资者完全专注于英国自身的宏观基本面。自那时起，英国债务已完全由盈余支持。
