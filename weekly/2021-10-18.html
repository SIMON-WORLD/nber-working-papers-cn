Telemigration and Development: On the Offshorability of Teleworkable Jobs

远程迁移与发展：论可远程工作职位的可离岸性

Richard Baldwin, Jonathan I. Dingel

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

The Covid-19 pandemic has introduced huge numbers of employers and employees to remote work. How many of these newly remote jobs will go overseas? We offer a rough quantification based on two observations: 1) offshore work is trade in services, and 2) the number of telemigrants is the volume of this trade divided by the average wage. Combining these with gravity-model estimates, we can roughly predict the number of new telemigrants that would arise from lower barriers to trade in services. Telemigration seems unlikely to be transformative when it comes to the development paths of most emerging economies. The baseline service trade flows are modest, and the standard gravity model restricts modest changes to have modest impacts. There are no tipping points in structural gravity models. Finally, we propose a simple model of telemigration in which small changes can have large consequences. The key is to assume that latent comparative advantage takes a different shape than typically assumed in quantitative trade models. Given this, small changes in trade costs can generate large and asymmetric increases in the exports of service tasks from low-wage nations.

中文摘要

新冠疫情让大量雇主和员工接触了远程工作。这些新出现的远程工作中有多少会流向海外？我们基于两个观察提供粗略量化：1）离岸工作是服务贸易，2）远程移民数量等于该贸易量除以平均工资。结合引力模型估计，我们可以大致预测因服务贸易壁垒降低而产生的新远程移民数量。就大多数新兴经济体的发展路径而言，远程迁移似乎不太可能具有变革性。基准服务贸易流量不大，标准引力模型限制微小变化产生微小影响。结构引力模型中不存在临界点。最后，我们提出了一个简单的远程迁移模型，其中小变化可能产生大后果。关键在于假设潜在比较优势的形状与定量贸易模型中通常假设的不同。鉴于此，贸易成本的微小变化可能导致低收入国家服务任务出口的大幅且不对称的增长。

Immediate and Longer-Term Housing Market Effects of a Major U.S. Airport Closure

美国主要机场关闭对住房市场的即时与长期影响

Jeffrey P. Cohen, Cletus C. Coughlin, Jonas Crews, Stephen L. Ross

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

A busy airport’s closure has large effects on noise, real estate markets, and neighborhood demographics. Using a unique dataset, we examine the effects of closing Denver’s Stapleton Airport on nearby housing markets. We find evidence of immediate anticipatory price effects upon announcement, but no price changes at closing and little evidence of upward trending prices between announcement and closing. However, after the airport closure, more higher-income and fewer black households moved in, and developers built higher quality houses. Finally, post-closing, these demographic and housing stock changes had substantial housing price effects, even after restricting the sample to pre-existing housing sales.

中文摘要

繁忙机场的关闭会对噪音、房地产市场以及社区人口结构产生重大影响。我们利用一个独特的数据集，研究了丹佛斯台普顿机场关闭对附近住房市场的影响。我们发现，在关闭公告发布时，存在即时的预期价格效应，但在关闭时没有价格变化，且从公告到关闭期间几乎没有价格上升趋势的证据。然而，机场关闭后，更多高收入家庭迁入，黑人家庭减少，开发商建造了质量更高的房屋。最后，在关闭后，这些人口结构和住房存量的变化产生了显著的住房价格效应，即使将样本限制在已有的住房销售中也是如此。

School Food Policy Affects Everyone: Retail Responses to the National School Lunch Program

学校食品政策影响所有人：零售业对全国学校午餐计划的反应

Jessie Handbury, Sarah Moshary

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the private market response to the National School Lunch Program, documenting economically meaningful spillovers to non-recipients. We focus on the Community Eligibility Provision (CEP), an expansion of the lunch program under the 2010 Healthy, Hunger-Free Kids Act. Under the CEP, participating schools offer free lunch to all students. We leverage both the staggered roll-out and eligibility criterion for the CEP, which is limited to schools where at least 40% of students participate in other means-tested welfare programs. We find that local adoption of the CEP causes households with children to reduce their grocery purchases, leading to a 10% decline in grocery sales at large retail chains. Retailers respond with chain-level price adjustments: chains with the most exposure lower prices by 2.5% across all outlets in the years following adoption, so that the program's welfare benefits propagate spatially. Using a stylized model of grocery demand, we estimate that, by 2016, the indirect benefit had reduced grocery costs for the median household by approximately 4.5%.

中文摘要

我们研究了私人市场对全国学校午餐计划的反应，记录了该计划对非受益者产生的具有经济意义的溢出效应。我们重点关注社区资格规定（CEP），这是2010年《健康、无饥饿儿童法案》下午餐计划的一项扩展。根据CEP，参与学校向所有学生提供免费午餐。我们利用了CEP的分阶段推出和资格标准，该标准仅限于至少40%的学生参与其他经济状况调查福利项目的学校。我们发现，当地采用CEP导致有孩子的家庭减少食品杂货购买，导致大型零售连锁店的食品杂货销售额下降10%。零售商通过连锁层面的价格调整来应对：在采用后的几年里，受影响最大的连锁店将所有门店的价格降低了2.5%，因此该计划的福利效益在空间上得以传播。使用一个简化的食品杂货需求模型，我们估计，到2016年，间接效益使中等家庭的食品杂货成本降低了约4.5%。

Fleshing Out the Olive? On Income Polarization in China

充实橄榄？——论中国的收入极化

Martin Ravallion, Shaohua Chen

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政

Abstract

In a rare example of an explicit national goal for income distribution besides reducing poverty, China’s leadership has recently committed to expanding the middle-income share—moving to a less polarized “olive-shaped” distribution. Recognizing the potential trade-offs, the paper asks whether China’s experience indicates that income-polarization was a by-product of past economic progress, including poverty reduction. The paper does not find robust time-series evidence of polarizing effects alongside either economic growth or population urbanization (including among those below the national median). There was strong co-movement between polarization and inequality. Larger urban-rural gaps in mean incomes are strongly polarizing in China.

中文摘要

除了减少贫困之外，中国领导层最近承诺扩大中等收入群体份额——转向一个极化程度较低的“橄榄形”分配，这是一个罕见的明确国家收入分配目标。认识到潜在的权衡，本文探讨中国的经验是否表明收入极化是过去经济进步（包括减贫）的副产品。本文没有发现强有力的时间序列证据表明极化与经济增长或人口城市化（包括在国家中位数以下的人群中）同时存在。极化与不平等之间存在强烈的联动关系。在中国，城乡平均收入差距越大，极化效应越强。

A (Dynamic) Investigation of Stereotypes, Belief-Updating, and Behavior

（动态）研究刻板印象、信念更新与行为

Katherine B. Coffman, Paola Ugalde Araya, Basit Zafar

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Many decisions – such as what educational or career path to pursue – are dynamic in nature, with individuals receiving feedback at one point in time and making decisions later. Using a controlled experiment, with two sessions one week apart, we analyze the dynamic effects of feedback on beliefs about own performance and decision-making across two different domains (verbal skills and math). We find significant gender gaps in beliefs and choices before feedback: men are more optimistic about their performance and more willing to compete than women in both domains, but the gaps are significantly larger in math. Feedback significantly shifts individuals' beliefs and choices. Despite this, we see substantial persistence of gender gaps over time. This is particularly true among the set of individuals who receive negative feedback. We find that, holding fixed performance and decisions before feedback, women update their beliefs and choices more negatively than men do after bad news. Our results highlight the challenges involved in overcoming gender gaps in dynamic settings.

中文摘要

许多决策——例如选择何种教育或职业道路——本质上是动态的，个体在某个时间点收到反馈，然后在之后做出决策。通过一项控制实验（两次实验间隔一周），我们分析了反馈对两个不同领域（语言技能和数学）中关于自身表现和决策的信念的动态影响。我们发现，在反馈之前，信念和选择存在显著的性别差距：男性在两个领域都对自己的表现更乐观，也更愿意竞争，但在数学领域的差距显著更大。反馈显著改变了个体的信念和选择。尽管如此，我们观察到性别差距随时间持续存在。在收到负面反馈的个体群体中尤其如此。我们发现，在控制反馈前的表现和决策后，女性在收到坏消息后比男性更消极地更新自己的信念和选择。我们的结果凸显了在动态环境中克服性别差距所面临的挑战。

Occupational Choice and the Intergenerational Mobility of Welfare

职业选择与福利的代际流动性

Corina Boar, Danial Lashkari

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

How does parental income shape labor market outcomes? Standard measures of intergenerational mobility typically focus on earnings as the main outcome, but parental income may also influence children’s access to more fulfilling careers. Using U.S. survey data, we show that children from higher-income families are significantly more likely to select occupations offering greater non-monetary qualities such as autonomy, intellectual stimulation, and workplace respect. To explain this pattern, we develop and estimate a model where parental resources allow children to prioritize occupational quality over earnings. When we adjust earnings to compensate for the monetary equivalent of these desirable qualities, intergenerational mobility falls by 15–35%, revealing that traditional income-based measures overstate the equality of opportunity. Finally, we document recent labor market shifts toward higher-quality occupations that raise our compensated measure of intergenerational mobility, suggesting structural changes that may have led to more equal distributions of opportunity.

中文摘要

父母收入如何塑造劳动力市场结果？代际流动性的标准衡量通常以收入作为主要结果，但父母收入也可能影响子女获得更有成就感职业的机会。利用美国调查数据，我们表明，来自高收入家庭的子女更有可能选择提供更高非货币品质（如自主性、智力刺激和工作场所尊重）的职业。为了解释这一模式，我们构建并估计了一个模型，其中父母资源允许子女优先考虑职业品质而非收入。当我们调整收入以补偿这些理想品质的货币等价物时，代际流动性下降了15-35%，这表明传统的基于收入的衡量标准高估了机会均等。最后，我们记录了近期劳动力市场向更高质量职业的转变，这提高了我们经补偿后的代际流动性衡量指标，表明结构性变化可能导致了更平等的机会分配。

Exposures and Behavioral Responses to Wildfire Smoke

暴露与行为反应：野火烟雾的影响

Marshall Burke, Sam Heft-Neal, Jessica Li, Anne Driscoll, Patrick W. Baylis, Matthieu Stigler, Joakim Weill, Jennifer Burney, Jeff Wen, Marissa Childs, Carlos Gould

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

The impacts of environmental change on human outcomes often depend on local exposures and behavioral responses that are challenging to observe with traditional administrative or sensor data. We show how data from private pollution sensors, cell phones, social media posts, and internet search activity yield new insights on exposures and behavioral responses during large wildfire smoke events across the US, a rapidly-growing environmental stressor. Health-protective behavior, mobility, and sentiment all respond to increasing ambient wildfire smoke concentrations, but responses differ by income. Indoor pollution monitors provide starkly different estimates of likely personal exposure during smoke events than would be inferred from traditional ambient outdoor sensors, with similar outdoor pollution levels generating >20x differences in average indoor PM2.5 concentrations. Our results suggest that the current policy reliance on self protection to mitigate health risks in the face of rising smoke exposure will result in modest and unequal benefits.

中文摘要

环境变化对人类结果的影响通常取决于局部暴露和难以通过传统行政或传感器数据观察到的行为反应。我们展示了来自私人污染传感器、手机、社交媒体帖子和互联网搜索活动的数据如何为美国大规模野火烟雾事件（一种快速增长的环境压力源）期间的暴露和行为反应提供新见解。健康保护行为、流动性和情绪都对环境野火烟雾浓度的增加做出反应，但反应因收入而异。室内污染监测器提供的烟雾事件期间个人可能暴露的估计值与从传统环境室外传感器推断出的估计值截然不同，相似的室外污染水平导致室内PM2.5平均浓度差异超过20倍。我们的结果表明，当前政策依赖自我保护来减轻因烟雾暴露增加而带来的健康风险，将产生有限且不平等的效益。

Predicting the Oil Market

预测石油市场

Charles W. Calomiris, Nida Çakır Melek, Harry Mamaysky

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the performance of many traditional and novel, text-based variables for in-sample and out-of-sample forecasting of oil spot, futures, and energy company stock returns, and changes in oil volatility, production, and inventories. After controlling for small-sample biases, we find evidence of in-sample predictability. Our text measures, derived using energy news articles, hold their own against traditional variables. While we cannot identify ex-ante rules for selecting successful out-of-sample forecasters, an analysis of all possible two-variable models reveals out-of-sample performance above that expected under random variation. Our findings provide new directions for identifying robust forecasting models for oil markets, and beyond.

中文摘要

我们研究了许多传统和基于文本的新变量在样本内和样本外预测石油现货、期货和能源公司股票回报，以及石油波动率、产量和库存变化方面的表现。在控制小样本偏差后，我们发现了样本内可预测性的证据。我们的文本指标（源自能源新闻文章）与传统变量相比毫不逊色。虽然我们无法确定选择成功样本外预测者的先验规则，但对所有可能的双变量模型的分析显示，其样本外表现高于随机变化下的预期。我们的发现为识别石油市场及其他领域的稳健预测模型提供了新方向。

Data Engineering for Cognitive Economics

认知经济学的数据工程

Andrew Caplin

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Cognitive economics studies imperfect information and decision-making mistakes. A central scientific challenge is that these can’t be identified in standard choice data. Overcoming this challenge calls for data engineering, in which new data forms are introduced to separately identify preferences, beliefs, and other model constructs. I present applications to traditional areas of economic research, such as wealth accumulation, earnings, and consumer spending. I also present less traditional applications to assessment of decision-making skills, and to human-AI interactions. Methods apply both to individual and to collective decisions. I make the case for broader application of data engineering beyond cognitive economics. It allows symbiotic advances in modeling and measurement. It cuts across existing boundaries between disciplines and styles of research.

中文摘要

认知经济学研究不完全信息和决策错误。一个核心的科学挑战是，这些无法在标准选择数据中被识别。克服这一挑战需要数据工程，即引入新的数据形式来分别识别偏好、信念和其他模型构念。我展示了在传统经济研究领域（如财富积累、收入和消费者支出）的应用。我还展示了在评估决策技能以及人机交互方面的非传统应用。这些方法既适用于个人决策，也适用于集体决策。我主张在认知经济学之外更广泛地应用数据工程。它允许建模和测量共生进步。它跨越了学科和研究风格之间的现有界限。

Pay, Productivity and Management

薪酬、生产率与管理

Nicholas Bloom, Scott W. Ohlmacher, Cristina J. Tello-Trillo, Melanie Wallskog

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using confidential Census matched employer-employee earnings data we find that employees at more productive firms, and firms with more structured management practices, have substantially higher pay, both on average and across every percentile of the pay distribution. This pay-performance relationship is particularly strong amongst higher paid employees, with a doubling of firm productivity associated with 11% more pay for the highest-paid employee (likely the CEO) compared to 4.7% for the median worker. This pay-performance link holds in public and private firms, although it is almost twice as strong in public firms for the highest-paid employees. Top pay volatility is also strongly related to productivity and structured management, suggesting this performance-pay relationship arises from more aggressive monitoring and incentive practices for top earners.

中文摘要

Abstract
