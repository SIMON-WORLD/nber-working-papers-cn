The Effect of Principal Reduction on Household Distress: Evidence from Mortgage Cramdown

本金减免对家庭困境的影响：来自抵押贷款减记的证据

Jacelly C. Cespedes, Carlos R. Parra, Clemens Sialm

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Mortgage cramdown enabled bankruptcy judges to discharge the underwater portion of a mortgage during Chapter 13 bankruptcy before the Supreme Court disallowed this practice in 1993. We exploit the random assignment of cases to judges to quantify the ex-post effects of Chapter 13 bankruptcy over the period from 1989 to 1993. We find that a successful Chapter 13 filing in a cramdown court substantially decreases the five-year foreclosure rate, the propensity to move, and the crime rate. Our results suggest that principal write-down considerably reduces homeowner’s distress.

中文摘要

抵押贷款减记允许破产法官在第十三章破产程序中免除抵押贷款的资不抵债部分，直到最高法院在1993年禁止这种做法。我们利用案件随机分配给法官的情况，量化了1989年至1993年期间第十三章破产的事后效应。我们发现，在减记法院成功提交第十三章破产申请显著降低了五年内的止赎率、搬家倾向和犯罪率。我们的结果表明，本金减记大大减少了房主的困境。

Sustainability in a Risky World

风险世界中的可持续性

John Y. Campbell, Ian Martin

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

How much consumption is sustainable, if \\sustainability" requires that welfare should not be expected to decline over time? We impose a sustainability constraint on a standard consumption/portfolio choice problem. The constraint does not distort port- folio choice, but it imposes an upper bound on the sustainable consumption-wealth ratio, which must lie between the riskless interest rate and the expected return on wealth (and if risky capital evolves according to a geometric Brownian motion, it lies exactly halfway between the two). Sustainability requires an upward drift in wealth and consumption to compensate future generations for the increased risk they face.

中文摘要

如果“可持续性”要求福利不应预期随时间下降，那么多少消费是可持续的？我们在标准的消费/投资组合选择问题上施加了可持续性约束。该约束不会扭曲投资组合选择，但它对可持续的消费-财富比率施加了一个上限，该比率必须介于无风险利率和财富预期回报率之间（如果风险资本遵循几何布朗运动，则恰好位于两者中间）。可持续性要求财富和消费向上漂移，以补偿后代面临的更高风险。

Modeling Behavior during a Pandemic: Using HIV as an Historical Analogy

大流行期间的行为建模：以艾滋病作为历史类比

Nicholas W. Papageorge

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Many models from epidemiology are not designed to capture tradeoffs between health and economic well-being and few are equipped to predict how these tradeoffs interact with individuals’ preferences to influence behavior. Policies based on such models may not reflect societal preferences or capture how preferences affect behavior and disease spread. Models with epidemiological processes that do incorporate behavior change or health-wealth tradeoffs (mostly from economics) drastically limit individual-level heterogeneity to remain tractable. This makes it difficult to understand variation in behavioral responses and distributional consequences of the pandemic, which should be central to any evaluation of pandemic-related policy. Without the proper tools, we are unable to fully address even basic policy questions, such as whether the long-run effects of social-distancing justify the costs - and if so, for whom and under what circumstances. After reviewing modeling approaches from epidemiology and economics (among other fields), I propose a framework adapted from earlier work on the HIV/AIDS epidemic. Analysis of individual behavior is based on the idea that health is a form of human capital in which individuals invest through their choices. The model integrates individual level variation that other frameworks omit, but does so at the cost of drastically limiting aggregate economic and epidemiological processes. I conclude that what is needed is a multi-disciplinary effort that helps to bridge gaps across disciplines, which would amount to constructing models that capture key features of different approaches. To be useful in evaluating pandemic-related policy, such a model should have rich heterogeneity and capture how individual choices are linked to the broad contours of disease spread and aggregate economic output. Whether such an effort would be feasible and fruitful remains to be seen.

中文摘要

许多流行病学模型并非旨在捕捉健康与经济福祉之间的权衡，也很少有模型能够预测这些权衡如何与个人偏好相互作用以影响行为。基于此类模型的政策可能无法反映社会偏好，也无法捕捉偏好如何影响行为和疾病传播。那些确实包含行为变化或健康-财富权衡的流行病学过程模型（主要来自经济学）为了保持可处理性而大幅限制了个人层面的异质性。这使得难以理解行为反应的变异性和大流行的分配后果，而这应成为任何大流行相关政策评估的核心。没有适当的工具，我们甚至无法充分解决基本的政策问题，例如社交距离的长期效应是否证明其成本是合理的——如果是，对谁以及在什么情况下合理。在回顾了流行病学和经济学（以及其他领域）的建模方法后，我提出了一个改编自早期关于艾滋病毒/艾滋病流行病工作的框架。对个体行为的分析基于健康是一种人力资本形式的理念，个人通过其选择进行投资。该模型整合了其他框架忽略的个人层面变异，但代价是大幅限制了总体经济和流行病学过程。我的结论是，需要多学科努力来弥合学科间的差距，这相当于构建能够捕捉不同方法关键特征的模型。为了在评估大流行相关政策时有用，这样的模型应具有丰富的异质性，并捕捉个人选择如何与疾病传播的广泛轮廓和总体经济产出相联系。这种努力是否可行且富有成效仍有待观察。

Delay the Pension Age or Adjust the Pension Benefit? Implications for Labor Supply and Individual Welfare in China

延迟退休年龄还是调整养老金待遇？对中国劳动供给和个人福利的影响

Yuanyuan Deng, Hanming Fang, Katja Hanewald, Shang Wu

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收

Abstract

We develop and calibrate a life-cycle model of labor supply and consumption to quantify the implications of alternative pension reforms on labor supply, individual welfare, and government budget for China’s basic old-age insurance program. We focus on urban males and distinguish low-skilled and high-skilled individuals, who differ in their preferences, health and labor income dynamics, and medical expense processes. We use the calibrated model to evaluate three potential pension reforms: (i) increasing the pension eligibility age from 60 to 65, but keeping the current pension benefit rule unchanged; (ii) keeping the pension eligibility age at 60, but proportionally lowering pension benefits so that the pension program’s budget is the same as under Reform (i); and (iii) increasing the pension eligibility age to 65 and simultaneously increasing the pension benefits so that individuals of both skill types attain the same individual welfare levels as in the status quo. We find that relative to the baseline, both Reforms (i) and (ii) can substantially improve the budgets of the pension system, but at the cost of substantial individual welfare loss for both skill types. In contrast, we find that Reform (iii) can modestly improve the budget of the pension system while ensuring that both skill types are as well off as in the status quo. We find that Reforms (i) and (ii) slightly increases, but Reform (iii) slightly decreases, the overall labor supply.

中文摘要

我们开发并校准了一个劳动供给和消费的生命周期模型，以量化替代性养老金改革对中国基本养老保险计划的劳动供给、个人福利和政府预算的影响。我们关注城市男性，并区分低技能和高技能个体，他们在偏好、健康和劳动收入动态以及医疗支出过程上存在差异。我们使用校准后的模型评估三种潜在的养老金改革：（i）将养老金领取年龄从60岁提高到65岁，但保持当前养老金待遇规则不变；（ii）保持养老金领取年龄为60岁，但按比例降低养老金待遇，使养老金计划的预算与改革（i）相同；（iii）将养老金领取年龄提高到65岁，同时提高养老金待遇，使两种技能类型的个人都达到与现状相同的个人福利水平。我们发现，相对于基准，改革（i）和（ii）都能显著改善养老金系统的预算，但代价是两种技能类型的个人福利大幅损失。相比之下，我们发现改革（iii）可以适度改善养老金系统的预算，同时确保两种技能类型的个人福利与现状相同。我们发现，改革（i）和（ii）略微增加了总体劳动供给，而改革（iii）则略微减少了总体劳动供给。

Labor Dynamics and Supply Chain Disruption in Food Manufacturing

食品制造业中的劳动力动态与供应链中断

A. Ford Ramsey, Barry Goodwin, Mildred Haley

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Food manufacturing and processing is an important link between agricultural producers and consumers in the agricultural supply chain. The food manufacturing sector in the United States is both increasingly mechanized and increasingly concentrated. Consequently, labor risks in food manufacturing have changed over time with changes in industry structure. Labor risks were highlighted by the COVID-19 pandemic - particularly in the animal slaughtering and processing industry - where labor-driven disruptions resulted in temporary plant closures. We use county-level data on employment in food manufacturing and dynamic panel models estimated via generalized method of moments to examine employment and wage dynamics in the food manufacturing sector and animal processing industry. We then compare forecasts from the estimated models with changes in food manufacturing and animal processing employment and wages during the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic. Our results provide insight into the role of operational and disruption risks in food manufacturing. We find significant delays in adjustment to employment and quicker adjustment in wages. Although there is an unanticipated drop in employment in food manufacturing and animal processing in April of 2020, employment returned to predicted levels within two to three months. The response of wages and employment to the COVID-19 pandemic suggest a degree of resilience in food supply chains.

中文摘要

食品制造和加工是农业供应链中连接农业生产者和消费者的重要环节。美国的食品制造业既日益机械化，也日益集中。因此，食品制造业中的劳动力风险随着行业结构的变化而随时间改变。COVID-19大流行凸显了劳动力风险——特别是在动物屠宰和加工行业——其中劳动力驱动的中断导致工厂暂时关闭。我们使用县级食品制造业就业数据以及通过广义矩估计法估计的动态面板模型，来考察食品制造业和动物加工业的就业与工资动态。然后，我们将估计模型的预测与COVID-19大流行初期食品制造业和动物加工业就业与工资的变化进行比较。我们的结果提供了对食品制造业运营风险和中断风险作用的见解。我们发现就业调整存在显著延迟，而工资调整更快。尽管2020年4月食品制造业和动物加工业的就业出现了意外下降，但就业在两到三个月内恢复到了预测水平。工资和就业对COVID-19大流行的反应表明食品供应链具有一定程度的韧性。

Learning about Housing Cost: Survey Evidence from the German House Price Boom

学习住房成本：来自德国房价繁荣期的调查证据

Fabian Kindermann, Julia Le Blanc, Monika Piazzesi, Martin Schneider

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 德国

Abstract

This paper uses new household survey data to study expectation formation during the recent housing boom in Germany. The cross section of forecasts depends on only two household characteristics: location and tenure. The average household in a region responds to local conditions but underpredicts local price growth. Renters make on average higher and hence more accurate forecasts than owners, although their forecasts are more dispersed and their mean squared forecast errors are higher. A quantitative model of learning about housing cost can match these facts. It emphasizes the unique information structure of housing among asset markets: renters who do not own the asset are relatively well informed about its cash flow, since they pay for housing services that owners simply consume. Renters then make more accurate forecasts in a boom driven by an increase in rents and recovery from a financial crisis.

中文摘要

本文利用新的家庭调查数据，研究德国近期住房繁荣期间的预期形成。预测的横截面仅取决于两个家庭特征：地理位置和住房所有权。一个地区的普通家庭会对当地状况做出反应，但会低估当地房价增长。租房者平均而言比房主做出更高、因而更准确的预测，尽管他们的预测更为分散，且均方预测误差更高。一个关于住房成本学习的定量模型可以匹配这些事实。该模型强调了住房在资产市场中独特的信息结构：不拥有该资产的租房者对其现金流相对更了解，因为他们为房主直接消费的住房服务付费。因此，在由租金上涨和金融危机复苏驱动的繁荣中，租房者能做出更准确的预测。

Judging Under Public Pressure

在公众压力下进行裁决

Alma Cohen, Zvika Neeman, Florian Auferoth

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Individuals who engage in “judging” – that is, rendering a determination in a dispute or contest between two parties – might be influenced by public pressure to favor one of the parties. Many rules and arrangements seek to insulate such individuals from public pressure or to address the effects of such pressure. We study this subject empirically, investigating the circumstances in which public pressure is more and less likely to affect judging.

中文摘要

从事“裁决”（即在双方之间的争端或竞赛中做出决定）的个人可能会受到公众压力的影响，从而偏袒其中一方。许多规则和安排旨在使此类个人免受公众压力影响，或应对这种压力的后果。我们对此主题进行实证研究，探究公众压力更可能或更不可能影响裁决的具体情境。

Politics and Gender in the Executive Suite

高管层的政治倾向与性别

Alma Cohen, Moshe Hazan, David Weiss

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Are the political preferences of CEOs associated with the representation and compensation of women in the executive suite? We find that Democratic CEOs (those who contribute more to Democratic candidates) are associated with higher representation of women in the executive suite. To explore causality, we use an event study approach and show that replacing a Republican with a Democratic CEO is associated with 20%-60% in more women in the executive suite. Finally, we show that Democratic CEOs associated with a significant reduction (or even disappearance) of the gender gap in the level and performance-sensitivity of executive pay.

中文摘要

CEO的政治偏好是否与高管层中女性的代表性和薪酬相关？我们发现，民主党CEO（那些向民主党候选人捐款更多的人）与高管层中女性比例更高相关。为探究因果关系，我们采用事件研究法，表明将共和党CEO替换为民主党CEO后，高管层中女性比例增加20%-60%。最后，我们证明民主党CEO与高管薪酬水平和绩效敏感度方面的性别差距显著缩小（甚至消失）相关。

Global Banks and Systemic Debt Crises

全球银行与系统性债务危机

Juan M. Morelli, Pablo Ottonello, Diego J. Perez

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We study the role of global financial intermediaries in international lending. We construct a model of the world economy, in which heterogeneous borrowers issue risky securities purchased by financial intermediaries. Aggregate shocks transmit internationally through financial intermediaries' net worth. The strength of this transmission is governed by the degree of frictions intermediaries face in financing their risky investments. We provide direct empirical evidence on this mechanism showing that around Lehman Brothers' collapse, emerging-market bonds held by more distressed global banks experienced larger price contractions. A quantitative analysis of the model shows that global financial intermediaries play a relevant role in driving borrowing-cost and consumption fluctuations in emerging-market economies, during both debt crises and regular business cycles. The portfolio of financial intermediaries and the distribution of bond holdings in the world economy are key to determine aggregate dynamics.

中文摘要

我们研究全球金融中介在国际借贷中的作用。我们构建了一个世界经济模型，其中异质性借款人发行由金融中介购买的风险证券。总冲击通过金融中介的净值在国际间传导。这种传导的强度取决于中介在为其风险投资融资时所面临的摩擦程度。我们提供了关于这一机制的直接经验证据，表明在雷曼兄弟倒闭前后，由陷入困境的全球银行持有的新兴市场债券经历了更大的价格下跌。模型的定量分析表明，在债务危机和常规商业周期中，全球金融中介在推动新兴市场经济体的借贷成本波动和消费波动方面发挥着重要作用。金融中介的投资组合以及世界经济中债券持有量的分布是决定总体动态的关键因素。

On the Green Interest Rate.

论绿色利率

Nicholas Z. Muller

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

This paper demonstrates how a central bank might operationalize an expanded role inclusive of managing risks from environmental pollution. The analysis introduces the green interest rate (rg) which depends on temporal changes in the pollution intensity of output. This policy instrument reallocates consumption from periods when output is pollution intensive to when output is cleaner. In economies on a cleaning-up path, rg exceeds r*. For those growing more polluted, rg is less than r*. In the U.S. economy from 1957 to 2016, rg exceeded r* by 50 basis points. Federal environmental policy reversed the orientation between rg and r*.

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济 · 美国

Abstract

Digital platforms are not only match-making intermediaries but also establish internal rules that govern all users in their ecosystems. To better understand the governing role of platforms, we study two Airbnb pro-guest rules that pertain to guest and host cancellations, using data on Airbnb and VRBO listings in 10 US cities. We demonstrate that such pro-guest rules can drive demand and supply to and from the platform, as a function of the local platform competition between Airbnb and VRBO. Our results suggest that platform competition sometimes dampens a platform wide pro-guest rule and sometimes reinforces it, often with heterogeneous effects on different hosts. This implies that platform competition does not necessarily mitigate a platform's incentive to treat the two sides asymmetrically, and any public policy in platform competition must consider its implication on all sides.

中文摘要

数字平台不仅是撮合交易的中介，还建立内部规则来管理其生态系统中的所有用户。为更深入理解平台的治理角色，我们利用美国10个城市中爱彼迎和VRBO房源数据，研究了爱彼迎两项针对客人的规则，分别涉及客人和房东的取消行为。我们证明，此类亲客规则能够根据爱彼迎与VRBO之间的本地平台竞争情况，驱动需求与供给进出平台。结果表明，平台竞争有时会削弱平台层面的亲客规则，有时则会强化它，且对不同房东往往产生异质性影响。这意味着平台竞争未必能缓解平台对双边用户区别对待的动机，任何针对平台竞争的公共政策都必须考虑其对所有各方的影响。

Employed in a SNAP? The Impact of Work Requirements on Program Participation and Labor Supply

在SNAP中就业？工作要求对项目参与和劳动供给的影响

Colin Gray, Adam Leive, Elena Prager, Kelsey B. Pukelis, Mary Zaki

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Work requirements are common in U.S. safety net programs. Evidence remains limited, however, on the extent to which work requirements increase economic self-sufficiency or screen out vulnerable individuals. Using linked administrative data on food stamps (SNAP) and earnings with a regression discontinuity design, we find robust evidence that work requirements increase program exits by 23 percentage points (64 percent) among incumbent participants after 18 months. There is a 53 percent overall reduction in program participation among adults who are subject to work requirements. Homeless adults are disproportionately screened out. We find no effects on employment, and suggestive evidence of increased earnings in some specifications. Our findings indicate that, per dollar of public expenditure, eliminating work requirements would likely transfer more resources to low-income adults than other programs targeting the same population.

中文摘要

工作要求在美国安全网项目中十分普遍。然而，关于工作要求在多大程度上能提高经济自给自足或筛选出弱势个体的证据仍然有限。利用食品券（SNAP）与收入的关联行政数据，结合断点回归设计，我们发现强有力的证据表明，在现有参与者中，工作要求在18个月后使项目退出率提高了23个百分点（64%）。在受工作要求约束的成年人中，项目参与总体减少了53%。无家可归的成年人被不成比例地筛选出去。我们未发现对就业的影响，但在某些设定中，有提示性证据表明收入有所增加。我们的研究结果表明，按每美元公共支出计算，取消工作要求可能比针对同一人群的其他项目将更多资源转移给低收入成年人。
