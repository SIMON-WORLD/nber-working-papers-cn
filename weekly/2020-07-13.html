Private Precaution and Public Restrictions: What Drives Social Distancing and Industry Foot Traffic in the COVID-19 Era?

私人预防与公共限制：COVID-19时代什么驱动了社交距离和行业客流量？

Christopher J. Cronin, William N. Evans

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine the role of state and local policies to encourage social distancing, including stay at home orders, public school closures, and restrictions on restaurants, entertainment, and large social gatherings. Outcomes come from cell phone records and include foot traffic in six industries (essential and nonessential retail, entertainment, hotel, restaurant, and business services) plus the fraction of cell phones that are home all day. Structural break models show mobility series at the national and state levels start to change dramatically in a short window from March 8-14, well before state or local restrictions of note are in place. In difference-in-difference models, declarations of state of emergency reduce foot traffic and increase social distancing. Stay at home restrictions explain a modest fraction of the change in behavior across outcomes. Industry-specific restrictions have large impacts. For example, restrictions on dining in restaurants reduce traffic in restaurants, hotels, and nonessential retail. Private, self-regulating behavior explains more than three-quarters of the decline in foot traffic in most industries. Restrictive regulation explains half the decline in foot traffic in essential retail and 75 percent of the increase in the fraction home all day. In this latter result, public school closings have a substantial effect.

中文摘要

我们考察了州和地方鼓励社交距离的政策作用，包括居家令、公立学校关闭以及对餐馆、娱乐和大型社交聚会的限制。结果来自手机记录，包括六个行业（必需品和非必需品零售、娱乐、酒店、餐饮和商业服务）的客流量，以及全天在家手机的比例。结构断点模型显示，国家和州层面的流动性序列在3月8日至14日的短窗口内开始发生显著变化，远在重要的州或地方限制实施之前。在双重差分模型中，宣布紧急状态减少了客流量并增加了社交距离。居家限制解释了行为变化中适度的一部分。行业特定限制有较大影响。例如，限制在餐馆就餐减少了餐馆、酒店和非必需品零售的客流量。私人自我调节行为解释了大多数行业客流量下降的四分之三以上。限制性法规解释了必需品零售客流量下降的一半，以及全天在家比例增加的75%。在后一个结果中，公立学校关闭有显著影响。

The Impact of the WWI Agricultural Boom and Bust on Female Opportunity Cost and Fertility

一战农业繁荣与萧条对女性机会成本和生育率的影响

Carl T. Kitchens, Luke P. Rodgers

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using variation in crop prices induced by large swings in demand surrounding World War I, we examine the fertility response to crop revenue increases from 1910 to 1930. Our estimates from samples utilizing complete count decennial census microdata and newly collected county-level data from state health reports indicate that agricultural price increases reduced fertility, explaining about 9 percent of the overall decline in fertility over the period. The effect persists years after the collapse of the war boom. Importantly, we show that fertility declines were concentrated in women living on the farm and that fertility declined along both the intensive and extensive margins. Combined, the pattern of estimates is consistent with agricultural women experiencing an increase in the opportunity cost of their time.

中文摘要

利用第一次世界大战前后需求大幅波动引起的作物价格变化，我们考察了1910年至1930年间作物收入增加对生育率的反应。我们的估计基于使用完整计数十年一次人口普查微观数据和新收集的县级卫生报告数据，表明农产品价格上涨降低了生育率，解释了该时期生育率总体下降的约9%。这种影响在战争繁荣崩溃后持续多年。重要的是，我们表明生育率下降集中在生活在农场的女性中，并且生育率在集约边际和广延边际上均有所下降。综合来看，估计模式与农业女性时间机会成本增加的解释一致。

The Real Effects of Modern Information Technologies: Evidence from the EDGAR Implementation

现代信息技术的真实效应：来自EDGAR实施的证据

Itay Goldstein, Shijie Yang, Luo Zuo

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using the implementation of the Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval (EDGAR) system from 1993 to 1996 as a shock to information dissemination technologies, we examine how a significant reduction in disclosure processing costs affects the real economy. We find that the EDGAR implementation leads to an increase in corporate investment and that this effect is concentrated in value firms. We provide evidence that improved equity financing and enhanced managerial incentives are likely the underlying mechanisms. Specifically, the EDGAR implementation leads to an increase in a firm’s stock liquidity, a decrease in the cost of equity capital, and an increase in the level of equity financing. Consistent with the monitoring effect of broad information dissemination, the EDGAR implementation leads to an increase in a firm’s operating performance. Our findings suggest that it is important to consider information dissemination beyond information production when examining the real effects of corporate disclosures.

中文摘要

利用1993年至1996年电子数据收集、分析和检索系统的实施作为信息传播技术的冲击，我们考察了披露处理成本显著降低如何影响实体经济。我们发现EDGAR实施导致企业投资增加，且这种效应集中在价值型公司。我们提供证据表明，改善股权融资和增强管理层激励可能是潜在机制。具体来说，EDGAR实施导致公司股票流动性增加、股权资本成本降低以及股权融资水平提高。与广泛信息传播的监督效应一致，EDGAR实施导致公司经营业绩提升。我们的发现表明，在考察公司披露的真实效应时，除了信息生产外，考虑信息传播也很重要。

Identification and Estimation of Undetected COVID-19 Cases Using Testing Data from Iceland

利用冰岛检测数据识别和估计未检测到的COVID-19病例

Karl M. Aspelund, Michael C. Droste, James H. Stock, Christopher D. Walker

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 冰岛

Abstract

In the early stages of the COVID-19 pandemic, international testing efforts tended to target individuals whose symptoms and/or jobs placed them at a high presumed risk of infection. Testing regimes of this sort potentially result in a high proportion of cases going undetected. Quantifying this parameter, which we refer to as the undetected rate, is an important contribution to the analysis of the early spread of the SARS-CoV-2 virus. We show that partial identification techniques can credibly deal with the data problems that common COVID-19 testing programs induce (i.e. excluding quarantined individuals from testing and low participation in random screening programs). We use public data from two Icelandic testing regimes during the first month of the outbreak and estimate an identified interval for the undetected rate. Our main approach estimates that the undetected rate was between 89% and 93% before the medical system broadened its eligibility criteria and between 80% and 90% after.

中文摘要

在COVID-19大流行早期阶段，国际检测工作往往针对症状和/或工作使其处于高假定感染风险的个体。这种检测制度可能导致大量病例未被检测到。量化这一参数（我们称之为未检测率）是对SARS-CoV-2病毒早期传播分析的重要贡献。我们表明，部分识别技术可以可信地处理常见COVID-19检测项目引起的数据问题（即排除隔离个体进行检测以及随机筛查项目参与率低）。我们使用冰岛两种检测制度在疫情爆发第一个月的公开数据，估计了未检测率的识别区间。我们的主要方法估计，在医疗系统扩大其资格标准之前，未检测率在89%至93%之间，之后在80%至90%之间。

A Theory of Social Impact Bonds

社会影响债券的理论

Daniel L. Tortorice, David E. Bloom, Paige Kirby, John Regan

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Social impact bonds (SIBs) are an innovative financing mechanism for public goods. In a SIB, an investor provides capital to a service provider for a social intervention. The investor receives a return based on the outcome of the intervention relative to a predetermined benchmark. We describe the basic structure of a SIB and provide some descriptive statistics for these financial instruments. We then consider a formal model of SIBs and examine their ability to finance positive net present value projects that traditional debt finance cannot. We find that SIBs expand the set of implementable projects if governments are pessimistic (relative to the private sector) about the probability an intervention would succeed or if the government is particularly averse to paying costs associated with a project that does not generate offsetting benefits. As both these features are present in various public programs, we conclude that SIBs are a real innovation in public finance and should be considered for projects when traditional debt finance has been rejected.

中文摘要

社会影响债券（SIBs）是一种用于公共产品的创新融资机制。在SIB中，投资者为社会干预项目向服务提供者提供资本。投资者根据干预结果相对于预定基准的表现获得回报。我们描述了SIB的基本结构，并提供了这些金融工具的一些描述性统计。然后，我们考虑了一个SIB的形式化模型，并检验了它们为传统债务融资无法实现的正净现值项目提供资金的能力。我们发现，如果政府（相对于私营部门）对干预成功的概率持悲观态度，或者政府特别厌恶为那些无法产生抵消收益的项目支付成本，那么SIB会扩大可实施项目的范围。由于这两个特征在各种公共项目中都存在，我们得出结论，SIB是公共财政领域的一项真正创新，在传统债务融资被拒绝的项目中应考虑使用SIB。

Why Does Globalization Fuel Populism? Economics, Culture, and the Rise of Right-wing Populism

全球化为何助长民粹主义？经济学、文化与右翼民粹主义的兴起

Dani Rodrik

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

There is compelling evidence that globalization shocks, often working through culture and identity, have played an important role in driving up support for populist movements, particularly of the right-wing kind. I start with an empirical analysis of the 2016 presidential election in the U.S. to show globalization-related attitudinal variables were important correlates of the switch to Trump. I then provide a conceptual framework that identifies four distinct channels through which globalization can stimulate populism, two each on the demand and supply sides of politics, respectively. I evaluate the empirical literature with the help of this framework, discussing trade, financial globalization, and immigration separately. I conclude the paper by discussing some apparently anomalous cases where populists have been against, rather than in favor of trade protection.

中文摘要

有令人信服的证据表明，全球化冲击——通常通过文化和身份认同发挥作用——在推动民粹主义运动（尤其是右翼民粹主义）的支持率上升方面发挥了重要作用。我首先对美国2016年总统选举进行实证分析，以表明与全球化相关的态度变量是转向支持特朗普的重要关联因素。然后，我提供了一个概念框架，识别出全球化刺激民粹主义的四个不同渠道，其中两个分别位于政治的需求侧和供给侧。我借助这一框架评估了实证文献，分别讨论了贸易、金融全球化和移民。最后，我通过讨论一些明显的反常案例来结束本文，在这些案例中，民粹主义者反对而非支持贸易保护。

Measuring Employer-to-Employer Reallocation

衡量雇主到雇主的重新配置

Shigeru Fujita, Giuseppe Moscarini, Fabien Postel-Vinay

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We revisit measurement of Employer-to-Employer (EE) transitions, the main engine of labor market competition and employment reallocation, in the monthly Current Population Survey (CPS). We follow Fallick and Fleischman (2004) and exploit a key survey question introduced with the 1994 CPS redesign. We detect a sudden and sharp increase in the incidence of missing answers to this question starting in 2007, when the U.S. Census Bureau introduced a change in survey methodology, the Respondent Identification Policy (RIP). We show evidence of selection into answering the EE question by both observable and unobservable worker characteristics that correlate with EE mobility. We propose a selection model and a procedure to impute missing answers to the key survey question, thus EE transitions, after the introduction of the RIP. Our imputed aggregate EE series restores a close congruence with the business cycle, especially with the onset of the Great Recession, exhibits a much less dramatic drop in 2008-2009 and a full recovery by 2016, and eliminates the spurious appearance of declining EE dynamism in the US labor market after 2000. Abstract

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 日本、韩国、台灣

Abstract

The paper demonstrates voter-based mechanisms underlying policy emulation across countries. These heterogeneous responses arise due to citizens’ divergent predispositions about a foreign country being their peer. Our findings caution the optimism about policy convergence across countries as technology lowers the cost of acquiring information.

Rational Sentiments and Economic Cycles

Maryam Farboodi, Péter Kondor

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose a rational model of endogenous cycles generated by the two-way interaction between credit market sentiments and real outcomes. Sentiments are high when most lenders optimally choose lax lending standards. This leads to low interest rates and high output growth, but also to the deterioration of future credit application quality. When the quality is sufficiently low, lenders endogenously switch to tight standards, i.e. sentiments become low. This implies high credit spreads and low output, but a gradual improvement in the quality of applications, which eventually triggers a shift back to lax lending standards and the cycle continues. The equilibrium cycle might feature a long boom, a lengthy recovery, or a double-dip recession. It is generically different from the optimal cycle as atomistic lenders ignore their effect on the composition of the pool of borrowers. Carefully chosen macro-prudential or countercyclical monetary policy often improves the decentralized equilibrium cycle.
