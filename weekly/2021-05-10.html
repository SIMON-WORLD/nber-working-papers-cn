Air Pollution and Adult Cognition: Evidence from Brain Training

空气污染与成人认知：来自大脑训练的证据

Andrea La Nauze, Edson R. Severnini

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We exploit novel data from brain-training games to examine the impacts of air pollution on a comprehensive set of cognitive skills in adults. We find that exposure to particulate matter (PM2.5) impairs adult cognitive function, and that these effects are largest for those in prime working age. These results confirm a hypothesized mechanism for the impacts of air pollution on workforce productivity. We also find that the cognitive effects are largest for new tasks and for those with low ability, suggesting that air pollution increases inequality in productivity.

中文摘要

我们利用大脑训练游戏的新颖数据，研究了空气污染对成年人一系列综合认知技能的影响。我们发现，暴露于细颗粒物（PM2.5）会损害成年人的认知功能，且这种影响对处于主要工作年龄的人群最为显著。这些结果证实了空气污染影响劳动力生产率的一个假设机制。我们还发现，认知影响对于新任务和能力较低的人群最为显著，这表明空气污染加剧了生产率的不平等。

Temporal Instability of Risk Preference among the Poor: Evidence from Payday Cycles

贫困人口风险偏好的时间不稳定性：来自发薪日周期的证据

Mika Akesaka, Peter Eibich, Chie Hanaoka, Hitoshi Shigeoka

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 日本、美国

Abstract

The poor live paycheck to paycheck and are repeatedly exposed to strong cyclical income fluctuations. We investigate whether such income fluctuations affect risk preference among the poor. If risk preference temporarily changes around payday, optimal decisions made before payday may no longer be optimal afterward, which could reinforce poverty. By exploiting Social Security payday cycles in the US, we find that risk preference among the poor relying heavily on Social Security changes around payday. Rather than cognitive decline before payday, the deterioration of mental health and relative deprivation may play a role. We find similar evidence among the Japanese elderly.

中文摘要

贫困人口靠薪水度日，反复面临强烈的周期性收入波动。我们研究了这种收入波动是否会影响贫困人口的风险偏好。如果风险偏好在发薪日前后暂时发生变化，那么发薪日前做出的最优决策在发薪日后可能不再最优，这可能会加剧贫困。通过利用美国社会保障发薪日周期，我们发现严重依赖社会保障的贫困人口的风险偏好在发薪日前后发生变化。心理健康恶化和相对剥夺感可能在其中起作用，而非发薪日前的认知衰退。我们在日本老年人中也发现了类似的证据。

Time is of the Essence: Climate Adaptation Induced by Existing Institutions

时间至关重要：现有制度引发的气候适应

Antonio Bento, Noah S. Miller, Mehreen Mookerjee, Edson R. Severnini

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

This study conceptualizes and demonstrates empirically that existing non-climate policies can induce climate adaptation. Adaptation involves adjusting to or coping with climatic change with the goal of reducing our vulnerability to its harmful effects. We examine the impact of temperature on ambient ozone concentration in the United States from 1980-2013. Ozone is formed under warm temperatures, but regulated by the Clean Air Act. These air quality standards may act as a buffer against extreme increases in ambient ozone concentration. Indeed, adaptation in counties out of attainment with the standards is 107 percent larger than under attainment, implying substantial regulation-induced adaptation.

中文摘要

本研究概念化并实证证明了现有的非气候政策可以引发气候适应。适应涉及调整或应对气候变化，目标是减少我们对其有害影响的脆弱性。我们考察了1980年至2013年间温度对美国环境臭氧浓度的影响。臭氧在温暖温度下形成，但受到《清洁空气法》的监管。这些空气质量标准可能起到缓冲作用，防止环境臭氧浓度极端升高。事实上，未达标县的适应效应比达标县高出107%，这表明存在显著的监管引发的适应。

The Long-Run Spillover Effects of Pollution: How Exposure to Lead Affects Everyone in the Classroom

污染的长期溢出效应：铅暴露如何影响课堂中的每个人

Ludovica Gazze, Claudia Persico, Sandra Spirovska

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Children exposed to pollutants like lead are more disruptive and have lower achievement. However, little is known about whether lead-exposed children affect the long-run outcomes of their peers. We estimate these spillover effects using new data on preschool blood lead levels (BLLs) matched to education data for all students in North Carolina public schools. We compare siblings whose school-grade cohorts differ in the proportion of children with elevated BLLs, holding constant school and peers’ demographics. Having more lead-exposed peers is associated with lower high-school graduation and SAT-taking rates and increased suspensions and absences. Peer effects are larger for same-gendered students.

中文摘要

接触铅等污染物的儿童更具破坏性，学业成绩也更低。然而，关于铅暴露儿童是否会影响其同龄人的长期结果，我们知之甚少。我们利用北卡罗来纳州公立学校所有学生的学龄前血铅水平新数据，并将其与教育数据匹配，来估计这些溢出效应。我们比较了兄弟姐妹中，其学校年级队列中血铅水平升高儿童比例不同的情况，同时控制了学校和同龄人的人口统计学特征。拥有更多铅暴露同龄人的学生，其高中毕业率和SAT参加率较低，而停学和缺勤率较高。同龄人效应在同性别学生中更大。

Mortgage Lenders and the Geographic Concentration of Foreclosures

抵押贷款机构与止赎的地理集中

Stephen L. Ross, Yuan Wang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use HMDA rate spread loans to identify lenders involved in riskier lending prior to the foreclosure crisis. We develop a shift-share measure of changes in high rate spread share lender representation in housing submarkets across origination years. While half the cross-sectional correlation between foreclosure and high rate spread lender share is explained by borrower observables, we find robust and stable estimates of the within housing submarket relationship between foreclosure and predicted changes in market share. Estimates are not explained by local housing price variation, rather evidence suggests servicer behavior in response to rising local foreclosure rates as a mechanism.

中文摘要

我们利用HMDA利差贷款数据来识别在止赎危机前参与高风险贷款的机构。我们开发了一个基于份额转移的衡量指标，用于衡量在贷款发放年份中，住房子市场中高利差份额贷款机构代表性的变化。虽然止赎率与高利差贷款机构份额之间的横截面相关性有一半可由借款人可观测特征解释，但我们发现，在住房子市场内部，止赎率与预测的市场份额变化之间的关系具有稳健且稳定的估计值。这些估计值无法由当地房价变动解释，相反，证据表明，服务商对当地止赎率上升的反应是一种作用机制。

Augmenting State Capacity for Child Development: Experimental Evidence from India

增强儿童发展中的国家能力：来自印度的实验证据

Alejandro J. Ganimian, Karthik Muralidharan, Christopher R. Walters

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 印度

Abstract

We use a large-scale randomized experiment to study the impact of augmenting staffing in the world’s largest public early childhood program: India’s Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS). Adding a half-time worker doubled net preschool instructional time and led to 0.29σ and 0.46σ increases in math and language test scores after 18 months for children who remained enrolled in the program. Rates of stunting and severe malnutrition were also lower in the treatment group. A cost-benefit analysis suggests that the benefits of augmenting ICDS staffing are likely to significantly exceed its costs even under conservative assumptions.

中文摘要

我们利用一项大规模随机实验，研究了在全球最大的公共儿童早期项目——印度综合儿童发展服务（ICDS）中增加人员配置的影响。增加一名半职工作人员使学前教学净时间翻倍，并在18个月后使继续参与该项目的儿童的数学和语言测试成绩分别提高了0.29个标准差和0.46个标准差。治疗组的发育迟缓和严重营养不良率也较低。成本效益分析表明，即使在保守假设下，增强ICDS人员配置的收益也可能显著超过其成本。

Should There Be Vertical Choice in Health Insurance Markets?

健康保险市场是否应存在垂直选择？

Victoria R. Marone, Adrienne Sabety

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the welfare effects of offering choice over coverage levels—“vertical choice”—in regulated health insurance markets. We emphasize that heterogeneity in the efficient level of coverage is not sufficient to motivate choice. When premiums do not reflect individuals' costs, it may not be in consumers' best interest to select their efficient coverage level. We show that vertical choice is efficient only if consumers with higher willingness to pay for insurance have a higher efficient level of coverage. We investigate this condition empirically and find that as long as a minimum coverage level can be enforced, the welfare gains from vertical choice are either zero or economically small.

中文摘要

我们研究了在受监管的健康保险市场中提供覆盖水平选择（即“垂直选择”）的福利效应。我们强调，有效覆盖水平的异质性并不足以成为提供选择的理由。当保费不反映个人成本时，消费者选择其有效覆盖水平可能并不符合自身最佳利益。我们证明，只有当对保险支付意愿较高的消费者具有较高的有效覆盖水平时，垂直选择才是有效的。我们通过实证检验了这一条件，并发现只要能够强制执行最低覆盖水平，垂直选择带来的福利收益要么为零，要么在经济上微不足道。

Do Firms with Specialized M&A Staff Make Better Acquisitions?

拥有专业并购人员的公司能做出更好的收购吗？

Sinan Gokkaya, Xi Liu, René M. Stulz

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We open the black box of the M&A decision process by constructing a comprehensive sample of US firms with specialized M&A staff. We investigate whether specialized M&A staff improves acquisition performance or facilitates managerial empire building instead. We find that firms with specialized M&A staff make better acquisitions when acquisition performance is measured by stock price reactions to announcements, long-run stock returns, operating performance, divestitures, and analyst earnings forecasts. This effect does not hold when the CEO is powerful, overconfident, or entrenched. Acquisitions by firms without specialized staff do not create value, on average. We provide evidence on mechanisms through which specialized M&A staff improves acquisition performance. For identification, we use the staggered recognition of inevitable disclosure doctrine as a source of exogenous variation in the employment of specialized M&A staff.

中文摘要

我们通过构建一个拥有专业并购人员的美国公司综合样本，打开了并购决策过程的黑箱。我们研究了专业并购人员是提高了收购绩效，还是反而助长了管理层的帝国构建。我们发现，当收购绩效通过公告股价反应、长期股票回报、经营业绩、资产剥离和分析师盈利预测来衡量时，拥有专业并购人员的公司能做出更好的收购。当CEO权力过大、过度自信或固守职位时，这种效应不成立。平均而言，没有专业人员的公司进行的收购并未创造价值。我们提供了专业并购人员改善收购绩效的机制证据。为了进行识别，我们利用了不可避免披露原则的分阶段认可作为专业并购人员雇佣的外生变化来源。

Abstract

The covered interest rate parity (CIP) condition is a fundamental arbitrage relationship in international finance. Household Debt Overhang Did Hardly Cause a Larger Spending Fall during the Financial Crisis in Australia

澳大利亚金融危机期间，家庭债务积压几乎未导致更大的支出下降

Lars E.O. Svensson

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 澳大利亚

Abstract

The “debt-overhang hypothesis” – that households cut back more on their spending in a crisis when they have higher levels of outstanding mortgage debt (Dynan, 2012) – seems to be taken for granted by macroprudential authorities in several countries in their policy decisions, as well as by the international organizations that evaluate and comment on countries’ macroprudential policy. New results for Australian microdata are presented that reject the debt-overhang hypothesis. The results instead support the “spending-normalization hypothesis” of Andersen, Duus, and Jensen (2016), what can also be called the “debt-financed overspending” hypothesis – that the correlation between high pre-crisis household indebtedness and subsequent spending falls during the crisis reflects high debt-financed spending pre-crisis and a return to normal spending during the crisis. As discussed in Svensson (2019, 2020), this is consistent with the above correlation reflecting debt-financed overspending through what Muellbauer (2012) calls the “housing-collateral household demand” channel and Mian and Sufi (2018) the “credit-driven household demand” channel.

We exploit family-based random genetic variation and find no evidence that these higher returns were driven by genetically-influenced individual characteristics such as innate ability or skills.

中文摘要

本文研究教育是否削弱了早期生活劣势与后期社会经济地位之间的关系。我们使用三个优势代理变量，并证明它们与中年时期的社会经济地位独立相关。除了早期有利的家庭和邻里条件外，我们认为儿童继承的基因也代表了一种优势来源。利用断点回归设计和超过11万人的数据，我们研究了英国一项义务教育改革，该改革产生了学校教育的外生变化。虽然改革成功减少了教育差距，但它并未削弱早期生活劣势与工资之间的关系。这意味着优势儿童的教育回报更高。我们利用基于家庭的随机遗传变异，没有发现证据表明这些更高的回报是由遗传影响的个体特征（如先天能力或技能）驱动的。
