Execution Risk and Arbitrage Opportunities in the Foreign Exchange Markets

外汇市场中的执行风险与套利机会

Takatoshi Ito, Kenta Yamada, Misako Takayasu, Hideki Takayasu

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

With the high-frequency data of firm quotes in the transaction platform of foreign exchanges, arbitrage profit opportunities—in the forms of a negative bid-ask spread of a currency pair and triangular transactions involving three currency pairs—can be detected to emerge and disappear in the matter of seconds. The frequency and duration of such arbitrage opportunities have declined over time, most likely due to the emergence of algorithmic trading. When a human trader detects such an arbitrage opportunity and places orders for multiple transactions—two in negative spreads and three in triangular arbitrage—there is no guarantee all of those orders are fulfilled in a fraction of one second. Thus, the arbitrageur has to consider execution risk, when he/she/it detects the emergence of such an opportunity. The novelty of this paper is to show that those arbitrage opportunities were exploitable and executable, before the mid-2000s, even considering the transactions costs and execution risk. After many algorithmic computers were allowed to be connected directly to the EBS transaction platform in the mid-2000s, the frequency of free lunch cases has declined and probabilities of successful executions of all legs for arbitrage declined. We calculate the change in the expected profit of an attempt to execute necessary transactions to reap benefits from arbitrage opportunity.

中文摘要

利用外汇交易平台中公司报价的高频数据，可以检测到套利利润机会——表现为货币对负买卖价差和涉及三个货币对的三角交易——在几秒钟内出现和消失。此类套利机会的频率和持续时间随着时间的推移而下降，很可能是由于算法交易的出现。当人类交易者检测到这样的套利机会并下达多个交易订单时——两个在负价差中，三个在三角套利中——并不能保证所有这些订单在不到一秒的时间内都能成交。因此，套利者在检测到这种机会出现时必须考虑执行风险。本文的新颖之处在于表明，在2000年代中期之前，即使考虑了交易成本和执行风险，这些套利机会也是可利用和可执行的。在2000年代中期，许多算法计算机被允许直接连接到EBS交易平台后，免费午餐案例的频率下降，套利所有环节成功执行的概率也下降了。我们计算了尝试执行必要交易以从套利机会中获利的预期利润的变化。

Extending the Race between Education and Technology

扩展教育与技术之间的竞赛

David Autor, Claudia Goldin, Lawrence F. Katz

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

The race between education and technology provides a canonical framework that does an excellent job of explaining U.S. wage structure changes across the twentieth century. The framework involves secular increases in the demand for more-educated workers from skill-biased technological change, combined with variations in the supply of skills from changes in educational access. We expand the analysis backwards and forwards. The framework helps explain rising skill differentials in the nineteenth and twenty-first centuries, but needs to be augmented to illuminate the recent convexification of education returns and implied slowdown in the growth of the relative demand for college workers. Increased educational wage differentials explain 75 percent of the rise of U.S. wage inequality from 1980 to 2000 as compared to 38 percent for 2000 to 2017.

中文摘要

教育与技术之间的竞赛提供了一个经典框架，该框架出色地解释了整个二十世纪美国工资结构的变化。该框架涉及技能偏向型技术变革对高学历工人需求的长期增长，以及教育机会变化带来的技能供给变化。我们将分析向前和向后扩展。该框架有助于解释十九世纪和二十一世纪技能差异的扩大，但需要加以扩展以阐明近期教育回报的凸化以及大学工人相对需求增长的放缓。教育工资差异的增加解释了1980年至2000年美国工资不平等上升的75%，而2000年至2017年这一比例为38%。

Dynamic Taxation

动态税收

Stefanie Stantcheva

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper reviews recent advances in the study of dynamic taxation, considering three main approaches: the dynamic Mirrlees, the parametric Ramsey, and the sufficient statistics approaches. In the first approach, agents' heterogeneous abilities to earn income are private information and evolve stochastically over time. Dynamic taxes are not ex ante restricted and are set for redistribution and insurance considerations. Capital is taxed only in order to improve incentives to work. Human capital is optimally subsidized if it reduces post-tax inequality and risk on balance. The Ramsey approach specifies ex ante restricted tax instruments and adopts quantitative methods, which allows it to consider more complex and realistic economies. Capital taxes are optimal when age-dependent labor income taxes are not possible. The newer and tractable sufficient statistics approach derives robust tax formulas that depend on estimable elasticities and features of the income distributions. It simplifies the transitional dynamics thanks to a newly defined criterion, the “utility-based steady state approach” that prevents the government from exploiting sluggish responses in the short-run. Capital taxes are here based on the standard equity-efficiency trade-off.

中文摘要

本文回顾了动态税收研究的最新进展，考虑了三种主要方法：动态Mirrlees方法、参数化Ramsey方法和充分统计量方法。在第一种方法中，代理人赚取收入的异质性能力是私人信息，并随时间随机演变。动态税收在事前不受限制，并出于再分配和保险考虑而设定。资本征税仅仅是为了改善工作激励。如果人力资本在平衡状态下减少税后不平等和风险，则对其进行最优补贴。Ramsey方法指定了事前受限的税收工具并采用定量方法，这使其能够考虑更复杂和现实的经济体。当无法实施与年龄相关的劳动所得税时，资本税是最优的。较新且易于处理的充分统计量方法推导出稳健的税收公式，这些公式依赖于可估计的弹性和收入分布特征。它通过一个新定义的标准——“基于效用的稳态方法”——简化了过渡动态，该方法防止政府在短期内利用缓慢的反应。这里的资本税基于标准的公平-效率权衡。

Ask and You Shall Receive? Gender Differences in Regrades in College

有求必应？大学改分中的性别差异

Cher Hsuehhsiang Li, Basit Zafar

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using administrative data from a large 4-year public university, we show that male students are 18.6 percent more likely than female students to receive favorable grade changes initiated by instructors. These gender differences cannot be explained by observable characteristics of the students, instructors, and the classes. To understand the mechanisms underlying these gendered outcomes, we conduct surveys of students and instructors, which reveal that regrade requests are prevalent, and that male students are more likely than female students to ask for regrades on the intensive margin. Finally, we corroborate the gender differences in regrade requests in an incentivized controlled experiment where participants receive noisy signals of their performance, and where they can ask for regrades: we find that males have a higher willingness to pay (WTP) to ask for regrades. Because students' payoff depends on their final grade and the cost of regrades, male students' higher propensity to ask for regrades makes them financially better off only when the cost is low. Males are more likely than females to become financially worse off when the regrade cost is high. Almost half of the gender difference in the WTP is due to gender differences in confidence, uncertainty in beliefs about ability, and the Big Five personality traits.

中文摘要

利用一所大型四年制公立大学的行政数据，我们表明，男性学生获得由教师发起的有利成绩更改的可能性比女性学生高18.6%。这些性别差异无法用学生、教师和班级的可观测特征来解释。为了理解这些性别化结果背后的机制，我们对学生和教师进行了调查，结果显示改分请求很普遍，并且男性学生在集约边际上比女性学生更有可能提出改分请求。最后，我们在一个有激励的对照实验中证实了改分请求中的性别差异，在该实验中，参与者收到其表现的有噪声信号，并且他们可以请求改分：我们发现男性有更高的支付意愿（WTP）来请求改分。由于学生的报酬取决于他们的最终成绩和改分成本，男性学生更高的改分请求倾向只有在成本较低时才会使他们的财务状况更好。当改分成本较高时，男性比女性更有可能在财务上变得更糟。WTP中近一半的性别差异是由于性别在信心、能力信念的不确定性以及大五人格特质方面的差异。

The Past and Future of Econ 101: The John R. Commons Award Lecture

经济学101的过去与未来：约翰·R·康芒斯奖演讲

N. Gregory Mankiw

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The introductory economics course, often called Econ 101, is where most economists get their start and where many students receive their only exposure to the field. This essay discusses the course’s evolution. It first looks back at how economics was taught at Harvard in the 19th century, based on a textbook by Professor Francis Bowen. It then looks ahead at how the introductory course may change as pedagogical tools improve, as society confronts new challenges, and as the field accumulates new knowledge.

中文摘要

入门经济学课程（常被称为经济学101）是大多数经济学家起步的地方，也是许多学生唯一接触该领域的途径。本文讨论了这门课程的演变。首先回顾了19世纪哈佛大学如何教授经济学，依据是弗朗西斯·鲍恩教授的教科书。然后展望了入门课程可能如何随着教学工具的改进、社会面临新挑战以及该领域积累新知识而发生变化。

No. 6论文详情NBER w26701

Leverage and Asset Prices: An Experiment.

杠杆与资产价格：一项实验

Marco Cipriani, Ana Fostel, Daniel Houser

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop a model of leverage that is amenable to laboratory implementation and gather experimental data. We compare two identical economies: in one economy, agents cannot borrow; in the other, they can leverage a risky asset to issue debt. Leverage increases asset prices in the laboratory. This increase is significant and quantitatively close to what theory predicts. Moreover, also as theory suggests, leverage allows gains from trade to be realized in the laboratory. Finally, the mechanism generating the price increase in the lab is due to the asset role as collateral, and different from what we would observe with a simple credit line or bigger cash endowments.

中文摘要

我们开发了一个适用于实验室实施的杠杆模型，并收集了实验数据。我们比较了两个相同的经济体：在一个经济体中，代理人不能借贷；在另一个经济体中，他们可以用风险资产作为杠杆来发行债务。杠杆在实验室中提高了资产价格。这种提高是显著的，并且在数量上接近理论预测。此外，正如理论所暗示的，杠杆使得在实验室中实现贸易收益成为可能。最后，实验室中价格上升的机制源于资产作为抵押品的作用，这与我们通过简单信贷额度或更大现金 endowment 所观察到的情况不同。

Should the Government be Paying Investment Fees on $3 Trillion of Tax-Deferred Retirement Assets?

政府是否应为3万亿美元税收递延退休资产支付投资费用？

Mattia Landoni, Stephen P. Zeldes

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Under standard assumptions, both individuals and the government are indifferent between traditional tax-deferred retirement accounts and “front-loaded” (Roth) accounts. When we add investment fees to this benchmark, individuals are still indifferent but the government is not. We estimate that tax deferral increases demand for asset management services by $3 trillion, causing the government to pay $20.7 billion in corresponding annual fees. In a general equilibrium model with asset management services as differentiated products, we examine the incidence and welfare implications of the added demand. Tax deferral in our model produces a larger asset management industry, higher taxes, and lower social welfare.

中文摘要

在标准假设下，个人和政府对于传统的税收递延退休账户和“前端加载”（罗斯）账户都无差异。当我们将投资费用加入这一基准时，个人仍然无差异，但政府则不然。我们估计，税收递延使资产管理服务需求增加了3万亿美元，导致政府支付相应的年度费用207亿美元。在一个将资产管理服务视为差异化产品的一般均衡模型中，我们考察了额外需求的归宿和福利影响。在我们的模型中，税收递延导致了更大的资产管理行业、更高的税收和更低的社会福利。

Linguistic Traits and Human Capital Formation

语言特征与人力资本形成

Oded Galor, Ömer Özak, Assaf Sarid

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This research establishes the influence of linguistic traits on human behavior. Exploiting variations in the languages spoken by children of migrants with identical ancestral countries of origin, the analysis indicates that the presence of periphrastic future tense, and its association with long-term orientation has a significant positive impact on educational attainment, whereas the presence of sex-based grammatical gender, and its association with gender bias, has a significant adverse impact on female educational attainment.

中文摘要

本研究确立了语言特征对人类行为的影响。利用来自相同原籍国的移民子女所使用语言的差异，分析表明，迂回将来时的存在及其与长期导向的关联，对教育成就具有显著的正向影响；而基于性别的语法性别的存在及其与性别偏见的关联，对女性教育成就具有显著的负面影响。

Factor Market Failures and the Adoption of Irrigation in Rwanda

要素市场失灵与卢旺达灌溉技术的采用

Maria Jones, Florence Kondylis, John Loeser, Jeremy Magruder

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 卢旺达

Abstract

We examine constraints to adoption of new technologies in the context of hillside irrigation schemes in Rwanda. We leverage a plot-level spatial regression discontinuity design to produce 3 key results. First, irrigation enables dry season horticultural production, which boosts on-farm cash profits by 70%. Second, adoption is constrained: access to irrigation causes farmers to substitute labor and inputs away from their other plots. Eliminating this substitution would increase adoption by at least 21%. Third, this substitution is largest for smaller households and wealthier households. This result can be explained by labor market failures in a standard agricultural household model.

中文摘要

我们考察了卢旺达山坡灌溉计划背景下采用新技术的制约因素。我们利用地块层面的空间断点回归设计得出了三个关键结果。首先，灌溉使得旱季园艺生产成为可能，从而将农场现金利润提高了70%。其次，采用受到制约：获得灌溉导致农民从其他地块转移劳动力和投入。消除这种替代将使采用率至少提高21%。第三，这种替代在较小的家庭和较富裕的家庭中最为显著。这一结果可以用标准农业家庭模型中的劳动力市场失灵来解释。

Do Elections Make You Sick?

选举会让你生病吗？

Hung-Hao Chang, Chad Meyerhoefer

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 台灣

Abstract

Anecdotal reports and small-scale studies suggest that elections are stressful, and might lead to a deterioration in voters’ mental well-being. Nonetheless, researchers have yet to establish whether elections actually make people sick, and if so, why. By applying a regression discontinuity design to administrative health care claims from Taiwan, we determine that elections increased health care use and expense only during legally specified campaign periods by as much as 19%. Overall, the treatment cost of illness caused by elections exceeded publicly reported levels of campaign expenditure, and accounted for 2% of total national health care costs during the campaign period.

中文摘要

轶事报道和小规模研究表明，选举是有压力的，并可能导致选民心理健康状况恶化。尽管如此，研究人员尚未确定选举是否真的让人们生病，如果是，原因何在。通过将断点回归设计应用于台湾的行政医疗保健索赔数据，我们确定选举仅在法律规定的竞选期间内使医疗保健使用和费用增加了高达19%。总体而言，由选举引起的疾病治疗成本超过了公开报告的竞选支出水平，并在竞选期间占全国医疗保健总成本的2%。

The Employment Effects of the Social Security Earnings Test

社会保障收入测试的就业效应

Alexander M. Gelber, Damon Jones, Daniel W. Sacks, Jae Song

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

We investigate the impact of the Social Security Annual Earnings Test (AET) on the employment decisions of older Americans. The AET reduces Social Security benefits by one dollar for every two dollars earned above the exempt amount. Using a differences-in-differences design, we find that the employment rate of those predicted to become subject to the AET decreases substantially relative to those not predicted to become subject to it. The point estimates suggest that the AET reduces the employment rate of Americans aged 63-64 by at least 1.2 percentage points.

中文摘要

我们研究了社会保障年度收入测试（AET）对美国老年人就业决策的影响。AET规定，收入超过免税额每两美元，社会保障福利减少一美元。使用双重差分设计，我们发现，相对于预计不受AET约束的人群，预计受AET约束的人群的就业率大幅下降。点估计表明，AET使63-64岁美国人的就业率至少降低了1.2个百分点。

Regional Monetary Policies and the Great Depression

区域货币政策与大萧条

Pooyan Amir-Ahmadi, Gustavo S. Cortes, Marc D. Weidenmier

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We compile a new monthly database for each Federal Reserve district between 1923-33 to analyze the national and regional nature of the monetary transmission mechanism around the Great Depression. We employ sign-identified structural VARs and narrative sign restrictions informed by uncontroversial theory and the historical record. Our findings demonstrate that there was significant heterogeneity in regional monetary policy as well as its dynamic effects on real economic activity. Prices in the 12 Fed districts were generally more responsive to contractionary regional monetary policy than national monetary policy shocks. The district reserve banks played a key role in the great contraction.

中文摘要

Abstract

Abstract

How important are social norms, information gaps and family constraints in explaining the low rates of female labor force participation (FLFP) in conservative societies? To answer this question, we conducted a field experiment embedded in a survey of female university students at a large public university in Saudi Arabia. We randomly provided one subset of individuals with information on the labor market and aspirations of their female peers (T1), while another subset was provided with this information along with a prime that made their parents more salient (T2). Abstract

We provide the first quasi-experimental evidence on the relationship between cigarette taxes and sexual minority adult smoking by studying individuals in same-sex households (a large share of whom are in same-sex romantic relationships) from the 1996-2018 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System. Abstract

We modify the Diamond and Dybvig (1983) model so that, besides offering liquidity services to depositors, banks also raise equity funding, make loans that are risky, and can invest in safe, liquid assets. The bank and its borrowers are subject to limited liability. When profitable, banks monitor borrowers to ensure that they repay loans. Depositors may choose to run based on conjectures about the available resources for people withdrawing early and beliefs about banks’ monitoring. We model the run decision by solving a novel global game. We find that banks opt for a more deposit-intensive capital structure than a social planner would choose.
