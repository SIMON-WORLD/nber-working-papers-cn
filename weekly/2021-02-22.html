No. 1论文详情NBER w28492

Preparing for a Pandemic: Accelerating Vaccine Availability

为疫情做准备：加速疫苗供应

Amrita Ahuja, Susan Athey, Arthur Baker, Eric Budish, Juan Camilo Castillo, Rachel Glennerster, Scott Duke Kominers, Michael Kremer, Jean Nahrae Lee, Canice Prendergast, Christopher M. Snyder, Alex Tabarrok, Brandon Joel Tan, Witold Więcek

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Vaccinating the world’s population quickly in a pandemic has enormous health and economic benefits. We analyze the problem faced by governments in determining the scale and structure of procurement for vaccines. We analyze alternative approaches to procurement. We find that if the goal is to accelerate the vaccine delivery timetable, buyers should directly fund manufacturing capacity and shoulder most of the risk of failure, while maintaining some direct incentives for speed. We analyzed the optimal portfolio of vaccine investments for countries with different characteristics as well as the implications for international cooperation. Our analysis, considered in light of the experience of 2020, suggests lessons for future pandemics.

中文摘要

在大流行中快速为全球人口接种疫苗具有巨大的健康和经济利益。我们分析了政府在确定疫苗采购规模和结构时所面临的问题。我们分析了替代采购方法。我们发现，如果目标是加速疫苗交付时间表，买家应直接资助制造能力并承担大部分失败风险，同时保持一些直接的速度激励。我们分析了针对不同特征国家的最优疫苗投资组合，以及对国际合作的影响。结合2020年的经验，我们的分析为未来大流行提供了教训。

Contractual Rigidity and Political Contestability: Revisiting Public Contract Renegotiations

合同刚性与政治竞争性：重新审视公共合同重新谈判

Jean Beuve, Marian W. Moszoro, Pablo T. Spiller

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We present a model of public procurement in which both contractual flexibility and political tolerance for contractual deviations determine renegotiations. In the model, contractual flexibility allows for adaptation without formal renegotiation while political tolerance for deviations decreases with political competition. We then compare renegotiation rates of procurement contracts in which the procurer is either a public administration or a private corporation. We find robust evidence consistent with the model predictions: public-to-private contracts are renegotiated more often than comparable private-to-private contracts, and that this pattern is more salient in politically contestable jurisdictions. The frequent renegotiation of public contracts results from their inherent rigidity and provides a relational quality of adaptability to contingencies in politically contestable environments.

中文摘要

我们提出了一个公共采购模型，其中合同灵活性和对合同偏离的政治容忍度共同决定了重新谈判。在该模型中，合同灵活性允许无需正式重新谈判即可进行调整，而对偏离的政治容忍度随着政治竞争加剧而降低。然后，我们比较了采购方是公共行政部门或私营公司的采购合同的重新谈判率。我们发现了与模型预测一致的稳健证据：公共对私人合同比可比的私人对私人合同更常被重新谈判，并且这种模式在政治竞争激烈的司法管辖区更为显著。公共合同的频繁重新谈判源于其固有的刚性，并在政治竞争环境中提供了对偶发事件的适应性关系质量。

Do Required Minimum Distribution 401(k) Rules Matter, and For Whom? Insights from a Lifecycle Model

最低提款401(k)规则是否重要，对谁重要？来自生命周期模型的见解

Vanya Horneff, Raimond Maurer, Olivia S. Mitchell

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 美国

Abstract

Tax-qualified vehicles helped U.S. private-sector workers accumulate $25Tr in retirement assets. An often-overlooked important institutional feature shaping decumulations from these retirement plans is the “Required Minimum Distribution” (RMD) regulation, requiring retirees to withdraw a minimum fraction from their retirement accounts or pay excise taxes on withdrawal shortfalls. Our calibrated lifecycle model measures the impact of RMD rules on financial behavior of heterogeneous households during their worklives and retirement. We show that proposed reforms to delay or eliminate the RMD rules should have little effects on consumption profiles but more impact on withdrawals and tax payments for households with bequest motives.

中文摘要

税收合格工具帮助美国私营部门工人积累了25万亿美元的退休资产。一个常被忽视但重要的制度特征塑造了这些退休计划的资产提取，即“最低提款”规定，要求退休人员从其退休账户中提取最低比例，否则需对提款不足部分缴纳消费税。我们校准的生命周期模型衡量了最低提款规则对异质性家庭在工作生涯和退休期间财务行为的影响。我们表明，提议的延迟或取消最低提款规则的改革对消费概况影响很小，但对有遗赠动机的家庭的提款和税收支付影响更大。

The Two Faces of Information

信息的双面性

Gaetano Gaballo, Guillermo Ordoñez

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In absence of insurance contracts to share risk, public information is a double-edged sword. On the one hand, it empowers self-insurance as agents better react to shocks, reducing risk. On the other hand, it weakens market-insurance as common knowledge of shocks restricts trading risk. We embody these two faces of information in a single general-equilibrium model. We characterize the conditions under which market-insurance is superior, and then public information – even though costless and precise – is socially undesirable. In the absence of information, however, market-insurance is still underprovided as individuals fail to internalize its general equilibrium benefits.

中文摘要

在缺乏分担风险的保险合约的情况下，公共信息是一把双刃剑。一方面，它增强了自我保险，因为代理人能更好地应对冲击，从而降低风险。另一方面，它削弱了市场保险，因为冲击的公共知识限制了风险交易。我们将信息的这两面性融入一个单一的一般均衡模型中。我们刻画了市场保险更优的条件，此时公共信息——即使免费且精确——在社会上也是不可取的。然而，在缺乏信息的情况下，市场保险仍然供给不足，因为个体未能内化其一般均衡收益。

Culture, Institutions & the Long Divergence

文化、制度与长期分化

Alberto Bisin, Jared Rubin, Avner Seror, Thierry Verdier

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Recent theories of the Long Divergence between Middle Eastern and Western European economies focus on Middle Eastern (over-)reliance on religious legitimacy, use of slave soldiers, and persistence of restrictive proscriptions of religious (Islamic) law. These theories take as exogenous the cultural values that complement the prevailing institutions. As a result, they miss the role of cultural values in either supporting the persistence of or inducing change in the economic and institutional environment. In this paper, we address these issues by modeling the joint evolution of institutions and culture. In doing so, we place the various hypotheses of economic divergence into one, unifying framework. We highlight the role that cultural transmission plays in reinforcing institutional evolution toward either theocratic or secular states. We extend the model to shed light on political decentralization and technological change in the two regions.

中文摘要

关于中东与西欧经济体之间长期分化的近期理论，聚焦于中东（过度）依赖宗教合法性、使用奴隶士兵以及宗教（伊斯兰）法限制性禁令的持续存在。这些理论将补充现行制度的文化价值观视为外生给定。因此，它们忽略了文化价值观在支持经济与制度环境持续存在或引发变革中的作用。在本文中，我们通过模拟制度与文化的共同演化来解决这些问题。在此过程中，我们将经济分化的各种假说纳入一个统一框架。我们强调文化传播在强化制度演化走向神权或世俗国家中的作用。我们扩展了模型，以阐明这两个地区的政治分权和技术变革。

Kinks as Goals: Accelerating Commissions and the Performance of Sales Teams

拐点作为目标：加速佣金与销售团队绩效

Peter J. Kuhn, Lizi Yu

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the performance of small retail sales teams facing an incentive scheme that includes both a lump sum bonus and multiple accelerators (kinks where the commission rate jumps upward). Consistent with standard labor supply models, we find that the presence of an attainable bonus or kink on a work-day raises mean sales, and that sales are highly bunched at the bonus; inconsistent with those models we find that teams bunch at the kinks instead of avoiding them. Combining simple theoretical models, institutional evidence, and heterogeneity analyses, we argue that that this unexpected bunching results from a previously unrecognized motivational benefit of piecewise linear reward schemes in team environments: Teams use the convenient, salient, kink-points as shared goals, which yield symbolic utility to their members when the points are attained.

中文摘要

我们研究了小型零售销售团队在面对包含一次性奖金和多个加速器（佣金率向上跳跃的拐点）的激励方案时的绩效。与标准劳动供给模型一致，我们发现工作日存在可达的奖金或拐点会提高平均销售额，且销售额高度集中在奖金点；但与这些模型不一致的是，我们发现团队集中在拐点处而非避开它们。结合简单理论模型、制度证据和异质性分析，我们认为这种意外的集中源于团队环境中分段线性奖励方案先前未被认识到的激励益处：团队将便捷、显著的拐点用作共享目标，当这些点达到时，为其成员提供象征性效用。

Aggregate Implications of Barriers to Female Entrepreneurship

女性创业障碍的总体经济影响

Gaurav Chiplunkar, Pinelopi K. Goldberg

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 印度

Abstract

We develop a framework for quantifying barriers to labor force participation (LFP) and entrepreneurship faced by women in India. We find substantial barriers to LFP, and higher costs of expanding businesses through the hiring of workers for women entrepreneurs. However, there is one area in which female entrepreneurs have an advantage: the hiring of female workers. We show that this is not driven by the sectoral composition of female employment. Consistent with this pattern, policies promoting female entrepreneurship can significantly increase female LFP even without explicitly targeting female LFP. Counterfactual simulations indicate that removing all excess barriers faced by women entrepreneurs would substantially increase the fraction of female-owned firms, female LFP, earnings, and generate substantial gains for the economy. These gains are due to higher LFP, higher real wages and profits, and reallocation: low productivity male-owned firms previously sheltered from female competition are replaced by higher productivity female-owned firms previously excluded from the economy.

中文摘要

我们构建了一个量化框架，用于评估印度女性在劳动力参与和创业方面面临的障碍。研究发现，女性在劳动力参与方面存在显著障碍，且女性企业家通过雇佣工人来扩大业务的成本更高。然而，女性企业家在一个领域具有优势：雇佣女性工人。我们表明，这一现象并非由女性就业的部门构成所驱动。与此模式一致，促进女性创业的政策即使没有明确针对女性劳动力参与，也能显著提高女性劳动力参与率。反事实模拟表明，消除女性企业家面临的所有额外障碍将大幅增加女性所有企业的比例、提高女性劳动力参与率和收入，并为经济带来显著收益。这些收益源于更高的劳动力参与率、更高的实际工资和利润，以及资源重新配置：此前受女性竞争保护的低生产率男性所有企业，将被此前被排除在经济之外的高生产率女性所有企业所取代。

Fiscal Policy and Households’ Inflation Expectations: Evidence from a Randomized Control Trial

财政政策与家庭通胀预期：来自随机对照试验的证据

Olivier Coibion, Yuriy Gorodnichenko, Michael Weber

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Rising government debt levels around the world are raising the specter that authorities might seek to inflate away the debt. In theoretical settings where fiscal policy “dominates” monetary policy, higher debt without offsetting changes in primary surpluses should lead households to anticipate this higher inflation. Are household inflation expectations sensitive to fiscal considerations in practice? We field a large randomized control trial on U.S. households to address this question by providing randomly chosen subsets of households with information treatments about the fiscal outlook and then observing how they revise their expectations about future inflation as well as taxes and government spending. We find that information about the current debt or deficit levels has little impact on inflation expectations but that news about future debt leads them to anticipate higher inflation, both in the short run and long run. News about rising debt also induces households to anticipate rising spending and a higher rate of interest for government debt.

中文摘要

全球政府债务水平的上升引发了当局可能试图通过通胀来稀释债务的担忧。在财政政策“主导”货币政策的理论设定中，更高的债务若没有基本盈余的相应变化，应导致家庭预期到更高的通胀。在实践中，家庭通胀预期是否对财政因素敏感？我们针对美国家庭开展了一项大规模随机对照试验，通过向随机选定的家庭子集提供关于财政前景的信息处理，然后观察他们如何修正对未来通胀、税收和政府支出的预期，来回答这一问题。我们发现，关于当前债务或赤字水平的信息对通胀预期影响甚微，但关于未来债务的消息会导致他们预期短期和长期通胀上升。关于债务上升的消息还促使家庭预期支出增加以及政府债务利率上升。

Biases in Information Selection and Processing: Survey Evidence from the Pandemic

信息选择与处理中的偏差：来自大流行调查的证据

Ester Faia, Andreas Fuster, Vincenzo Pezone, Basit Zafar

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

How people form beliefs is crucial for understanding decision-making under uncertainty. This is particularly true in a situation such as a pandemic, where beliefs will affect behaviors that impact public health as well as the aggregate economy. We conduct two survey experiments to shed light on potential biases in belief formation, focusing in particular on the tone of information people choose to consume and how they incorporate this information into their beliefs. In the first experiment, people express their preferences over pandemic-related articles with optimistic and pessimistic headlines, and are then randomly shown one of the articles. We find that respondents with more pessimistic prior beliefs about the pandemic are substantially more likely to prefer pessimistic articles, which we interpret as evidence of confirmation bias. In line with this, respondents assigned to the less preferred article rate it as less reliable and informative (relative to those who prefer it); they also discount information from the article when it is less preferred. We further find that these motivated beliefs end up impacting incentivized behavior. In a second experiment, we study how partisan views interact with information selection and processing. We find strong evidence of source dependence: revealing the news source further distorts information acquisition and processing, eliminating the role of prior beliefs in article choice.

中文摘要

人们如何形成信念对于理解不确定性下的决策至关重要。在大流行等情况下尤其如此，因为信念会影响影响公共卫生和整体经济的行为。我们进行了两项调查实验，以揭示信念形成中的潜在偏差，特别关注人们选择消费的信息基调以及他们如何将这些信息纳入信念。在第一个实验中，人们表达了对带有乐观和悲观标题的大流行相关文章的偏好，然后被随机展示其中一篇文章。我们发现，对大流行持有更悲观先验信念的受访者明显更倾向于选择悲观文章，我们将其解释为确认偏差的证据。与此一致，被分配到不太喜欢的文章的受访者（相对于那些喜欢它的人）认为该文章可靠性更低、信息量更少；当文章不太受欢迎时，他们也会忽略其中的信息。我们进一步发现，这些动机性信念最终会影响激励行为。在第二个实验中，我们研究了党派观点如何与信息选择和处理相互作用。我们发现了来源依赖性的有力证据：揭示新闻来源进一步扭曲了信息获取和处理，消除了先验信念在文章选择中的作用。

No. 10论文详情NBER w28483

Housing Consumption and the Cost of Remote Work

住房消费与远程工作的成本

Christopher T. Stanton, Pratyush Tiwari

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper estimates housing choice differences between households with and without remote workers. Prior to the pandemic, the expenditure share on housing was more than seven percent higher for remote households compared to similar non-remote households in the same commuting zone. Remote households’ higher housing expenditures arise from larger dwellings (more rooms) and a higher price per room. Pre-COVID, households with remote workers were actually located in areas with above-average housing costs, and sorting within-commuting zone to suburban or rural areas was not economically meaningful. Using the pre-COVID distribution of locations, we estimate how much additional pre-tax income would be necessary to compensate non-remote households for extra housing expenses arising from remote work in the absence of geographic mobility, and we compare this compensation to commercial office rents in major metro areas.

中文摘要

本文估计了有远程工作者和无远程工作者的家庭之间的住房选择差异。在大流行之前，与同一通勤区内相似的无远程工作者家庭相比，远程工作家庭的住房支出份额高出7%以上。远程工作家庭更高的住房支出源于更大的居住空间（更多房间）和更高的每间房价格。在新冠疫情前，有远程工作者的家庭实际上位于住房成本高于平均水平的地区，而在通勤区内向郊区或农村地区的分类在经济上并不显著。利用新冠疫情前的居住地分布，我们估计了在缺乏地理流动性的情况下，需要多少额外的税前收入才能补偿无远程工作者家庭因远程工作而产生的额外住房支出，并将这一补偿与主要大都市区的商业办公租金进行了比较。

Competition Policy in a Simple General Equilibrium Model

简单一般均衡模型中的竞争政策

Louis Kaplow

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The flow of resources across sectors to their best use, with concomitant entry and exit, is central to the functioning and welfare properties of a market economy. Nevertheless, most industrial organization research, including applications to competition policy, undertakes partial equilibrium analysis in a single sector, often with a fixed number of firms. This article examines competition policy in a simple, multi-sector, general equilibrium model with free entry and exit. Even partial equilibrium analysis yields some lessons, such as that accounting for free entry often makes strengthening competition policy more rather than less attractive. Cross-sectoral Externalities Related to Natural Resources and Ecosystem Services

Abstract

Standard approaches to environmental and natural resource use externalities generally focus on single-sector resources and user groups. Remedies include Pigouvian-style government constraints, small group controls following Elinor Ostrom, or less frequently, bargaining across users as outlined by Ronald Coase. However, many difficult natural resource management problems involve competing uses of the same resource or multiple interdependent resources, across multiple, heterogeneous sectors. Cross-sectoral externalities are generated and impede attainment of conservation objectives. The multiplicity of resources and stakeholders, who may have different property rights, hold different use or non-use values, have different traditions, or fall under different regulatory regimes, increases the likelihood of multi-jurisdictional conflicts. We provide an institutional analysis following Oliver Williamson’s four-levels of institutions (social embeddedness, institutional environment, governance, resource allocation) to illustrate the sources of potential conflict, the costs of addressing them, and the potentials for exchange. In comparing the costs of alternative approaches, we include transaction costs associated with property rights; the costs of lobbying, implementing, and enforcing government regulation; and the costs of scaling up from small-group controls when resource problems involve multiple sectors and heterogeneous populations. In our illustrative case examples, instruments that are not formal property rights are exchanged at lower transaction costs. We close by discussing how Coasean, Pareto-improving voluntary exchange agreements may be lower cost, more effective, and more durable solutions than alternative management regimes to mitigate cross-sectoral externalities.

Abstract

In this study, we quantify the effects of receiving stocks from certain brands on spending in the brand's stores. We use data from a new FinTech company called Bumped that opens brokerage accounts for its users and rewards them with stocks when they shop at previously elected stores. For identification, we use 1) the staggered distribution of brokerage accounts over time after individuals sign up for a waitlist and 2) randomly distributed stock grants. We find that individuals spend 40% more per week at elected brands and stores after being allocated an account. We thus provide micro evidence for the idea that stock ownership drives brand loyalty, which is an intangible asset that leads to lower firm cash flow volatility.

Abstract

This paper reviews the research on the impacts of charter school attendance on students’ academic and other outcomes, the mechanisms behind those effects, and the influence of charter schools on nearby traditional public schools, almost three decades after the first charter school was established. Abstract

Wage inequality does not fully capture differences in job quality. Jobs also differ along other key dimensions, including the prevalence of labor rights violations.
