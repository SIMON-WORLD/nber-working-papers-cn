No. 1论文详情NBER w28923

Five Facts about the UIP Premium

关于未抵补利率平价溢价的五个事实

Ṣebnem Kalemli-Özcan, Liliana Varela

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We introduce a new measure of currency risk for emerging markets, the UIP premium, and highlight five key insights into its cross-sectional and time-series properties. We derive the UIP premium from survey data on exchange rate expectations and show that it captures a substantial local risk factor, as evidenced by: (1) The UIP premium for emerging markets is consistently positive, higher, and more volatile than the UIP premium for advanced economies; (2) A significant portion of cross-sectional and time-series variation in the UIP premium is driven by local risk factors; (3) The interest rate differential component of the UIP premium is more volatile and strongly correlated with local risk factors; (4) Local and global risk factors influence exchange rate expectations, which closely align with actual exchange rate movements; (5) The local risk factor is associated with country-specific policy shocks, where such policy uncertainty can predict persistent expectations of depreciations.

中文摘要

我们引入了一种衡量新兴市场货币风险的新指标——未抵补利率平价溢价，并强调了其横截面和时间序列特性的五个关键见解。我们从汇率预期的调查数据中推导出未抵补利率平价溢价，并表明它捕捉了一个重要的本地风险因子，证据如下：（1）新兴市场的未抵补利率平价溢价持续为正，且比发达经济体的未抵补利率平价溢价更高、更波动；（2）未抵补利率平价溢价的横截面和时间序列变动的很大一部分由本地风险因子驱动；（3）未抵补利率平价溢价的利差组成部分更波动，且与本地风险因子强相关；（4）本地和全球风险因子影响汇率预期，而汇率预期与实际汇率变动紧密一致；（5）本地风险因子与国家特定的政策冲击相关，这种政策不确定性可以预测持续的贬值预期。

The Dynamics of Inattention in the (Baseball) Field

（棒球）场上的注意力动态

James E. Archsmith, Anthony Heyes, Matthew J. Neidell, Bhaven N. Sampat

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

Recent theoretical and empirical work characterizes attention as a limited resource that decision-makers strategically allocate. There has been less research on the dynamic interdependence of attention: how paying attention now may affect performance later. In this paper, we exploit high-frequency data on decision-making by Major League Baseball umpires to examine this. We find that umpires not only apply greater effort to higher-stakes decisions, but also that effort applied to earlier decisions increases errors later. These findings are consistent with the umpire having a depletable ‘budget’ of attention. There is no such dynamic interdependence after breaks during the game (at the end of each inning) suggesting that even short rest periods can replenish attention budgets. We also find that an expectation of higher stakes future decisions leads to reduced attention to current decisions, consistent with forward-looking behavior by umpires aware of attention scarcity.

中文摘要

近期的理论和实证工作将注意力描述为一种决策者战略性分配的有限资源。关于注意力的动态相互依赖性——即现在如何分配注意力可能影响后续表现——的研究较少。在本文中，我们利用美国职业棒球大联盟裁判决策的高频数据来检验这一点。我们发现，裁判不仅对更高风险的决策投入更多努力，而且对早期决策投入的努力会增加后续的错误。这些发现与裁判拥有一个可耗尽的注意力“预算”是一致的。在比赛期间（每局结束后）的休息之后，不存在这种动态相互依赖性，这表明即使是短暂的休息也能补充注意力预算。我们还发现，对未来更高风险决策的预期会导致对当前决策的注意力减少，这与裁判意识到注意力稀缺并具有前瞻性行为是一致的。

What Does Codetermination Do?

共同决定制度有何作用？

Simon Jäger, Shakked Noy, Benjamin Schoefer

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We provide a comprehensive overview of codetermination, i.e., worker representation in firms’ governance and management. The available micro evidence points to zero or small positive effects of codetermination on worker and firm outcomes, and leaves room for moderate positive effects on productivity, wages, and job stability. Similarly, we present new country-level, general-equilibrium event studies of codetermination reforms between the 1960s and 2010s, finding no effects on aggregate economic outcomes or the quality of industrial relations. We offer three explanations of the institution’s limited impact. First, existing codetermination laws convey little authority to workers. Second, countries with codetermination laws have high baseline levels of informal worker voice. Third, codetermination laws may interact with other labor market institutions, such as union representation and collective bargaining. We close by discussing implications for recent codetermination proposals in the United States.

中文摘要

我们提供了关于共同决定制度（即工人参与公司治理和管理）的全面概述。现有的微观证据表明，共同决定制度对工人和企业结果的影响为零或微小的正向效应，并且为生产率、工资和工作稳定性的适度正向效应留有余地。同样，我们提出了关于1960年代至2010年代共同决定制度改革的新国家层面一般均衡事件研究，发现其对总体经济结果或劳资关系质量没有影响。我们对该制度有限影响提供了三种解释。首先，现有的共同决定法律赋予工人的权力很小。其次，有共同决定法律的国家具有较高水平的非正式工人发言权。第三，共同决定法律可能与其他劳动力市场制度（如工会代表和集体谈判）相互作用。最后，我们讨论了近期美国共同决定提案的启示。

Tasks, Automation, and the Rise in US Wage Inequality

任务、自动化与美国工资不平等加剧

Daron Acemoglu, Pascual Restrepo

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We document that between 50% and 70% of changes in the US wage structure over the last four decades are accounted for by the relative wage declines of worker groups specialized in routine tasks in industries experiencing rapid automation. We develop a conceptual framework where tasks across a number of industries are allocated to different types of labor and capital. Automation technologies expand the set of tasks performed by capital, displacing certain worker groups from employment opportunities for which they have comparative advantage. This framework yields a simple equation linking wage changes of a demographic group to the task displacement it experiences. We report robust evidence in favor of this relationship and show that regression models incorporating task displacement explain much of the changes in education differentials between 1980 and 2016. Our task displacement variable captures the effects of automation technologies (and to a lesser degree offshoring) rather than those of rising market power, markups or deunionization, which themselves do not appear to play a major role in US wage inequality. We also propose a methodology for evaluating the full general equilibrium effects of task displacement (which include induced changes in industry composition and ripple effects as tasks are reallocated across different groups). Our quantitative evaluation based on this methodology explains how major changes in wage inequality can go hand-in-hand with modest productivity gains.

中文摘要

我们证明，在过去四十年中，美国工资结构变化的50%至70%可由那些在经历快速自动化的行业中专门从事常规任务的工人群体的相对工资下降来解释。我们开发了一个概念框架，其中多个行业的任务被分配给不同类型的劳动力和资本。自动化技术扩展了资本执行的任务集，取代了某些工人群体，使其失去具有比较优势的就业机会。该框架产生了一个简单的方程，将人口群体的工资变化与其经历的任务替代联系起来。我们报告了支持这一关系的稳健证据，并表明包含任务替代变量的回归模型解释了1980年至2016年间教育差异的大部分变化。我们的任务替代变量捕捉了自动化技术（以及在较小程度上离岸外包）的影响，而非市场力量、加成率或去工会化的影响，后者本身似乎并未在美国工资不平等中发挥主要作用。我们还提出了一种评估任务替代完全一般均衡效应的方法（其中包括行业构成的诱导变化以及任务在不同群体间重新分配时的涟漪效应）。基于该方法的定量评估解释了工资不平等的重大变化如何能与适度的生产率增长并存。

The Effect of Changes in Social Security's Delayed Retirement Credit: Evidence from Administrative Data

社会保障延迟退休积分变化的影响：来自行政数据的证据

Mark Duggan, Irena Dushi, Sookyo Jeong, Gina Li

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 民主刚果

Abstract

The delayed retirement credit (DRC) increases monthly OASI (Old Age and Survivors Insurance) benefits for primary beneficiaries who claim after their full retirement age (FRA). For many years, the DRC was set at 3.0 percent per year (0.25 percent monthly). The 1983 amendments to Social Security more than doubled this actuarial adjustment to 8.0 percent per year. These changes were phased in gradually, so that those born in 1924 or earlier retained a 3.0 percent DRC while those born in 1943 or later had an 8.0 percent DRC. In this paper, we use administrative data from the Social Security Administration (SSA) to estimate the effect of this policy change on individual claiming behavior. We focus on the first half of the DRC increase (from 3.0 to 5.5 percent) given changes in other SSA policies that coincided with the later increases. Our findings demonstrate that the increase in the DRC led to a significant increase in delayed claiming of social security benefits and strongly suggest that the effects were larger for those with higher lifetime incomes, who would have a greater financial incentive to delay given their longer life expectancies.

中文摘要

延迟退休积分（DRC）会提高在完全退休年龄（FRA）之后申领的主要受益人的月度老年和遗属保险（OASI）福利。多年来，DRC设定为每年3.0%（每月0.25%）。1983年社会保障修正案将这一精算调整幅度提高了一倍以上，达到每年8.0%。这些变化是逐步实施的，因此1924年或之前出生的人保留3.0%的DRC，而1943年或之后出生的人则享有8.0%的DRC。在本文中，我们使用社会保障管理局（SSA）的行政数据来估计这一政策变化对个人申领行为的影响。鉴于其他SSA政策的变化与后来的增长同时发生，我们重点关注DRC增长的前半部分（从3.0%到5.5%）。我们的研究结果表明，DRC的提高导致社会保障福利延迟申领显著增加，并强烈表明，对于终身收入较高的人，其影响更大，因为他们的预期寿命更长，延迟申领的经济激励也更大。

Culture and the Family

文化与家庭

Natalie Bau, Raquel Fernández

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This handbook chapter focuses on interactions between the family and culture that have significant economic implications. We start by documenting the wide range of global variation in family institutions and discussing some of the profound changes in family structure in the modern era. We next discuss the family’s role in generating economic outcomes given that it is the main transmitter of culture, focusing on female labor force participation, fertility, and human capital investment. We review the persistent effects of different traditional family institutions and describe how cultural practices related to the family, such as son preference, co-residence traditions, polygyny, and marriage payments, affect decision-making within the family and interact with policy. Lastly we examine how diverse forces, including new policies and technologies, have led family institutions and culture to change. We conclude that studying the family in a vacuum, without accounting for the role of culture, may lead to misleading conclusions regarding the effects of diverse shocks and policies, including technological change.

中文摘要

本手册章节聚焦于家庭与文化之间具有重要经济影响的相互作用。我们首先记录了全球范围内家庭制度的广泛差异，并讨论了现代时期家庭结构的一些深刻变化。接着，我们探讨了家庭在产生经济结果中的作用，因为它是文化的主要传递者，重点关注女性劳动力参与、生育率和人力资本投资。我们回顾了不同传统家庭制度的持续影响，并描述了与家庭相关的文化实践，如重男轻女、同居传统、一夫多妻制和婚姻支付，如何影响家庭内部的决策并与政策相互作用。最后，我们考察了包括新政策和技术在内的多种力量如何导致家庭制度和文化发生变化。我们得出结论，如果忽视文化的作用而孤立地研究家庭，可能会导致关于各种冲击和政策（包括技术变革）影响的误导性结论。

Can Competitiveness predict Education and Labor Market Outcomes? Evidence from Incentivized Choice and Survey Measures

竞争力能否预测教育和劳动力市场结果？来自激励性选择和调查测量的证据

Thomas Buser, Muriel Niederle, Hessel Oosterbeek

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We assess the predictive power of two measures of competitiveness for education and labor market outcomes using a large, representative survey panel. The first is incentivized and is an online adaptation of the laboratory-based Niederle-Vesterlund measure. The second is an unincentivized survey question eliciting general competitiveness on an 11-point scale. Both measures are strong and consistent predictors of income, occupation, completed level of education and field of study. The predictive power of the new unincentivized measure for these outcomes is robust to controlling for other traits, including risk attitudes, confidence and the Big Five personality traits. For most outcomes, the predictive power of competitiveness exceeds that of the other traits. Gender differences in competitiveness can explain 5-10 percent of the observed gender differences in education and labor market outcomes.

中文摘要

我们使用一个大型、具有代表性的调查面板，评估了两种竞争力测量对教育和劳动力市场结果的预测能力。第一种是激励性的，是基于实验室的Niederle-Vesterlund测量的在线改编版。第二种是非激励性的调查问题，在11分量表上引出一般竞争力。这两种测量都是收入、职业、完成的教育水平和研究领域的强有力且一致的预测指标。新的非激励性测量对这些结果的预测能力在控制其他特质（包括风险态度、信心和大五人格特质）后依然稳健。对于大多数结果，竞争力的预测能力超过了其他特质。竞争力方面的性别差异可以解释观察到的教育和劳动力市场结果中性别差异的5%至10%。

Crisis Innovation Policy from World War II to COVID-19

从二战到新冠疫情的危机创新政策

Daniel P. Gross, Bhaven N. Sampat

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

Innovation policy can be a crucial component of governments' responses to crises. Because speed is a paramount objective, crisis innovation may also require different policy tools than innovation policy in non-crisis times, raising distinct questions and tradeoffs. In this paper, we survey the U.S. policy response to two crises where innovation was crucial to a resolution: World War II and the COVID-19 pandemic. After providing an overview of the main elements of each of these efforts, we discuss how they compare, and to what degree their differences reflect the nature of the central innovation policy problems and the maturity of the U.S. innovation system. We then explore four key tradeoffs for crisis innovation policy—top-down vs. bottom-up priority setting, concentrated vs. distributed funding, patent policy, and managing disruptions to the innovation system—and provide a logic for policy choices. Finally, we describe the longer-run impacts of the World War II effort and use these lessons to speculate on the potential long-run effects of the COVID-19 crisis on innovation policy and the innovation system.

中文摘要

创新政策可以成为政府应对危机的关键组成部分。由于速度是首要目标，危机创新可能还需要与非危机时期的创新政策不同的政策工具，从而引发独特的问题和权衡。在本文中，我们调查了美国在两次危机中的政策响应，在这两次危机中，创新对解决问题至关重要：第二次世界大战和新冠疫情。在概述了这些努力的主要要素之后，我们讨论了它们之间的比较，以及它们的差异在多大程度上反映了核心创新政策问题的性质和美国创新体系的成熟度。然后，我们探讨了危机创新政策的四个关键权衡——自上而下与自下而上的优先级设定、集中与分散的资金、专利政策以及管理对创新体系的干扰——并为政策选择提供了逻辑依据。最后，我们描述了二战努力的长期影响，并利用这些经验教训来推测新冠疫情危机对创新政策和创新体系的潜在长期影响。

Shocks, Institutions and Secular Changes in Employment of Older Individuals

冲击、制度与老年人就业的长期变化

Richard Rogerson, Johanna Wallenius

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Employment rates of males aged 55-64 have changed dramatically in the OECD over the last 5 decades. The average employment rate decreased by more than 15 percentage points between the mid-1970s and the mid-1990s, only to increase by roughly the same amount subsequently. One proposed explanation in the literature is that spousal non-working times are complements and that older males are working longer as a result of secular increases in labor supply of older females. In the first part of this paper we present evidence against this explanation. We then offer a new narrative to understand the employment rate changes for older individuals. We argue that the dramatic U-shaped pattern for older male employment rates should be understood as reflecting a mean reverting low frequency shock to labor market opportunities for all workers in combination with temporary country specific policy responses that incentivized older individuals to withdraw from market work.

中文摘要

个人破产与影子债务的积累

Bronson Argyle, Benjamin Iverson, Taylor D. Nadauld, Christopher Palmer

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Compiling new liability-level data from the balance sheets of personal bankruptcy filers, we document that a sizable share of reported liabilities are “shadow debt,” debt not reported to credit bureaus that often arises from the non-payment of goods and services. We use this new data to evaluate how debtor cash flows affect when consumers file for bankruptcy and how much debt they have at bankruptcy. We find that filers respond to a quasi-exogenous $100 increase in monthly cash flows by delaying filing by an average of one month and by increasing unsecured indebtedness by $4,000 in the months preceding filing. A large share of the additional debt incurred by delaying filers is shadow debt, and our effects are concentrated among filers without employment, health, or marriage shocks.

中文摘要

通过汇编个人破产申请者资产负债表上新的负债层面数据，我们记录了报告负债中有相当大一部分是“影子债务”，即未向信用机构报告且通常源于商品和服务未付款的债务。我们利用这些新数据来评估债务人现金流如何影响消费者何时申请破产以及破产时的债务规模。我们发现，申请者对每月现金流准外生增加100美元的反应是，平均延迟申请一个月，并在申请前几个月内增加4000美元的无担保债务。延迟申请者所承担的额外债务中很大一部分是影子债务，并且我们的效应集中在没有就业、健康或婚姻冲击的申请者中。
