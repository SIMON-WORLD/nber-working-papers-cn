Who Will Fill China's Shoes? The Global Evolution of Labor-Intensive Manufacturing

谁将接替中国的位置？劳动密集型制造业的全球演变

Gordon H. Hanson

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

In this paper, I review evidence on changing global specialization in labor-intensive exporting. Production of apparel, footwear, furniture, and related products are how many low-income countries first enter export manufacturing. Just as China's rise as a powerhouse in these goods supplanted a role previously occupied by the East Asian Tigers, the world may again be on the cusp of significant change in where labor-intensive goods are produced. China's prowess in these sectors peaked in the early 2010s; its share in their global exports, while still substantial, is now in decline. Mechanisms through which the global economy may adjust to China's graduation into more technologically sophisticated activities include expanded labor-intensive export production in other emerging economies and labor-saving technological change in products currently heavily reliant on less-educated labor. Available evidence suggests that the first mechanism is operating slowly and the second hardly at all. As a third mechanism, China may in part replace itself by moving labor-heavy factories out of densely populated and expensive coastal cities and into the country's interior. Such a transition, though still in its infancy, would mirror the decentralization of manufacturing production in the U.S. and Europe, which occurred after World War II.

中文摘要

本文回顾了全球劳动密集型出口专业化变化的证据。服装、鞋类、家具及相关产品的生产是许多低收入国家首次进入出口制造业的途径。正如中国在这些商品领域的崛起取代了此前由东亚四小龙占据的角色一样，世界可能再次处于劳动密集型产品生产地重大变革的前沿。中国在这些领域的实力在2010年代初达到顶峰；其在这些产品全球出口中的份额虽然仍然可观，但目前已呈下降趋势。全球经济可能通过两种机制适应中国向更技术密集型活动的升级：其他新兴经济体扩大劳动密集型出口生产，以及当前严重依赖低教育水平劳动力的产品领域发生节约劳动的技术变革。现有证据表明，第一种机制进展缓慢，第二种机制几乎未发挥作用。作为第三种机制，中国可能部分通过将劳动密集型工厂从人口密集且昂贵的沿海城市迁往内陆地区来实现自我替代。这种转型虽仍处于初期阶段，但将反映二战后美国和欧洲制造业生产分散化的趋势。

A Grant to Every Citizen: Survey Evidence of the Impact of a Direct Government Payment in Israel

给每位公民的补助：以色列直接政府支付影响的调查证据

Naomi Feldman, Ori Heffetz

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 以色列

Abstract

In early August, 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, Israel disbursed one-time, universal grants to its citizens, of $220 per adult and $150 per child. Using survey data, we estimate that 25–45 percent either had already mostly spent or were planning to spend the money by year’s end and 36–52 percent mostly paid down debts. Interestingly, about as many people reported mostly donating the grant or using it to help family or friends as reported saving it (10–18 percent), with donations mostly originating from higher-income respondents. This voluntary rerouting of governmental assistance may help alleviate the trade-off between targeting and simplicity/speed of disbursement.

中文摘要

2020年8月初，在新冠疫情期间，以色列向其公民发放了一次性全民补助，每位成人220美元，每位儿童150美元。利用调查数据，我们估计25%至45%的人要么已经基本花完，要么计划在年底前花掉这笔钱；36%至52%的人主要用于偿还债务。有趣的是，报告主要将补助用于捐赠或帮助家人朋友的人数与报告储蓄的人数大致相当（10%至18%），其中捐赠主要来自高收入受访者。这种对政府援助的自愿转拨可能有助于缓解精准发放与发放简便/快速之间的权衡。

Trauma at School: The Impacts of Shootings on Students' Human Capital and Economic Outcomes

校园创伤：枪击事件对学生人力资本和经济结果的影响

Marika Cabral, Bokyung Kim, Maya Rossin-Slater, Molly Schnell, Hannes Schwandt

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We examine how shootings at schools—an increasingly common form of gun violence in the United States—impact the educational and economic trajectories of students. Using linked schooling and labor market data in Texas from 1992 to 2018, we compare within-student and across-cohort changes in outcomes following a shooting to those experienced by students at matched control schools. We find that school shootings increase absenteeism and grade repetition; reduce high school graduation, college enrollment, and college completion; and reduce employment and earnings at ages 24–26. We further find school-level increases in the number of leadership staff and reductions in retention among teachers and teaching support staff in the years following a shooting. The adverse impacts of shootings span student characteristics, suggesting that the economic costs of school shootings are universal.

中文摘要

我们研究了校园枪击事件——美国日益常见的一种枪支暴力形式——如何影响学生的教育和经济轨迹。利用1992年至2018年德克萨斯州的关联学校与劳动力市场数据，我们比较了枪击事件后学生个体内部及不同队列之间的结果变化，与匹配控制学校学生的经历进行对比。我们发现，校园枪击事件增加了缺勤率和留级率；降低了高中毕业率、大学入学率和大学完成率；并降低了24至26岁年龄段的就业率和收入。我们还发现，在枪击事件后的几年里，学校层面的领导人员数量增加，而教师和教学支持人员的留任率下降。枪击事件的不利影响跨越了学生特征，表明校园枪击事件的经济成本具有普遍性。

Measuring Unemployment in Crisis: Effects of COVID-19 on Potential Biases in the CPS

危机中的失业测量：COVID-19对当前人口调查潜在偏差的影响

Ori Heffetz, Daniel Reeves

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

From February to April 2020, as COVID-19 hit the U.S. economy, the official unemployment rate (UR) climbed from 3.5 percent—the lowest in more than 50 years—to 14.7—the highest since current measurement began in January 1948. This unprecedented, speedy quadrupling of UR coincided with major disruptions in survey-data-collection procedures and a dramatic, differential drop in response rates. To what extent did measurement issues contribute to this quadrupling? We revisit two recently studied potential biases in the Current Population Survey: rotation group bias (Krueger, Mas and Niu, 2017) and difficulty-of-reaching bias (Heffetz and Reeves, 2019). We extend the original analyses to the years prior to the crisis and focus on the six months of peak UR, from April to September 2020. Our ballpark estimates suggest that the peak official UR figure could be biased by up to ∼1.5 percentage points in either direction.

中文摘要

从2020年2月到4月，随着COVID-19冲击美国经济，官方失业率从3.5%（50多年来的最低点）攀升至14.7%（自1948年1月开始当前测量以来的最高点）。这种前所未有的、迅速的失业率四倍增长，恰逢调查数据收集程序的中断以及回应率的急剧且差异化的下降。测量问题在多大程度上导致了这种四倍增长？我们重新审视了当前人口调查中两个近期研究的潜在偏差：轮换组偏差（Krueger, Mas and Niu, 2017）和难以接触偏差（Heffetz and Reeves, 2019）。我们将原始分析扩展到危机前的年份，并重点关注失业率高峰期的六个月，即2020年4月至9月。我们的粗略估计表明，官方失业率峰值数字可能在任何方向上存在高达约1.5个百分点的偏差。

Cities, Productivity, and Trade

城市、生产率与贸易

Alvaro Garcia Marin, Andrei V. Potlogea, Nico Voigtländer, Yang Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易

Abstract

We document a novel stylized fact: Using data for several countries, we show that export activity is disproportionately concentrated in larger cities – even more so than overall economic activity. We account for this fact by marrying elements of international trade and economic geography. We build a model with agglomeration economies where firms with heterogeneous productivity sort across city sizes and select into exporting. The model allows us to study the geographic implications of trade policy, as well as the international trade effects of urban policies. We show that (i) lifting restrictions on housing supply raises not only the aggregate productivity of the economy but also its aggregate export intensity, by allowing more firms to locate in larger cities and profit from agglomeration effects; (ii) conversely, while opening up to trade has complex overall economic geography implications, within sectors it tends to shift employment towards larger cities. We structurally estimate the model using data for the universe of Chinese manufacturing firms and study the general equilibrium effects of trade liberalization and of urban policies. We find that the effects of these policies are quantitatively different from those predicted by trade models that ignore economic geography, and by economic geography models that omit international trade (both of which are nested in our framework).

中文摘要

我们记录了一个新颖的典型事实：利用多个国家的数据，我们表明出口活动不成比例地集中在更大的城市——甚至比整体经济活动更为集中。我们通过融合国际贸易和经济地理学的要素来解释这一事实。我们构建了一个具有集聚经济的模型，其中生产率异质的企业在不同城市规模中进行分类并选择出口。该模型使我们能够研究贸易政策的地理影响，以及城市政策的国际贸易效应。我们表明：（i）取消住房供应限制不仅提高了经济的总体生产率，还通过允许更多企业落户大城市并从集聚效应中获益，提高了经济的总体出口强度；（ii）相反，虽然开放贸易具有复杂的总体经济地理影响，但在部门内部，它倾向于将就业转向更大的城市。我们利用中国全部制造业企业的数据对模型进行了结构估计，并研究了贸易自由化和城市政策的一般均衡效应。我们发现，这些政策的影响在数量上不同于忽略经济地理的贸易模型以及忽略国际贸易的经济地理模型（两者都嵌套在我们的框架中）所预测的结果。

Self-image Bias and Lost Talent

自我形象偏见与人才流失

Marciano Siniscalchi, Pietro Veronesi

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose an overlapping-generation model wherein researchers belong to two groups, M or F, and established researchers evaluate new researchers. Group imbalance obtains even with group-neutral evaluations and identical productivity distributions. Evaluators’ self-image bias and mild between-group heterogeneity in equally productive research characteristics lead the initially dominant group, say M, to promote scholars with characteristics similar to theirs. Promoted Fresearchers are few and similar to M-researchers, perpetuating imbalance. Candidates’ career concerns and institutions’ hiring practices exacerbate talent loss. Mentorship reduces group imbalance, but increases F-group talent loss. Affirmative action reduces both. Our mechanism explains existing evidence and suggests different policies.

中文摘要

我们提出了一个世代交叠模型，其中研究人员属于两个群体M或F，且资深研究人员评估新研究人员。即使评估是群体中性的且生产率分布相同，也会出现群体失衡。评估者的自我形象偏见以及同等生产率研究特征中轻微的群体间异质性，导致最初占主导地位的群体（例如M）提拔具有与其相似特征的学者。被提拔的F群体研究人员数量很少且与M群体研究人员相似，从而延续了失衡。候选人的职业关切和机构的招聘实践加剧了人才流失。导师制减少了群体失衡，但增加了F群体的人才流失。平权行动则同时减少了这两者。我们的机制解释了现有证据并提出了不同的政策建议。

Exposure to a School Shooting and Subsequent Well-Being

校园枪击事件暴露与后续福祉

Phillip B. Levine, Robin McKnight

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

This paper examines the impact of school shootings on the educational performance and long-term health consequences of students who survive them, highlighting the impact of indiscriminate, high-fatality incidents. Initially, we focus on test scores in the years following a shooting. We also examine whether exposure to a shooting affects chronic absenteeism, which may play a role in explaining any such effect, and school expenditures, which may counteract it. We analyze national, school-district level data and additional school-level data from Connecticut in this part of the analysis. In terms of effects on health status, we focus on its most extreme measure, mortality in the years following a shooting. In this part of the analysis, we analyze county-level data on mortality by cause. In all analyses, we treat the timing of these events as random, enabling us to identify causal effects. Our results indicate that indiscriminate, high-fatality school shootings, such as those that occurred at Sandy Hook and Columbine, have considerable adverse effects on students exposed to them. We cannot rule out substantive effects of other types of shootings with fewer or no fatalities.

中文摘要

本文研究了校园枪击事件对幸存学生的学业表现和长期健康后果的影响，重点关注无差别、高死亡率的枪击事件。最初，我们关注枪击事件后几年的考试成绩。我们还考察了暴露于枪击事件是否会影响长期缺勤（这可能解释任何此类效应）以及学校支出（这可能抵消该效应）。在这部分分析中，我们使用了全国学区层面的数据以及康涅狄格州的额外学校层面数据。在健康状态影响方面，我们关注其最极端的衡量指标，即枪击事件后几年的死亡率。在这部分分析中，我们分析了按死因分类的县级死亡率数据。在所有分析中，我们将这些事件的发生时间视为随机的，从而能够识别因果效应。我们的结果表明，无差别、高死亡率的校园枪击事件，例如桑迪胡克和哥伦拜恩事件，对暴露其中的学生产生了相当大的不利影响。我们不能排除其他类型枪击事件（死亡人数较少或无死亡）的实质性影响。

Event-day Options

事件日期权

Jonathan H. Wright

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper considers new options on Treasury and stock futures than expire each Wednesday and Friday. I examine the volatilities implied by these options as of the night before expiration, and compare the volatilies just before FOMC days and employment report days with the volatilities on other Tuesdays or Thursdays, respectively. This can be used to measure the risk neutral uncertainty associated with FOMC announcements and employment reports. I can also compare the average physical and risk neutral uncertainty: the difference between them is the average variance risk premium. Average variance risk premia are large and significantly positive, especially for FOMC days. Lastly, I construct options-implied densities on the eve of FOMC and employment report days.

中文摘要

本文考虑了每周三和周五到期的新国债和股票期货期权。我研究了这些期权在到期前夜隐含的波动率，并将联邦公开市场委员会（FOMC）日和就业报告日之前的波动率与其他周二或周四的波动率分别进行比较。这可用于衡量与FOMC公告和就业报告相关的风险中性不确定性。我还可以比较平均实际波动率和风险中性波动率：两者之差即为平均方差风险溢价。平均方差风险溢价很大且显著为正，尤其是在FOMC日。最后，我在FOMC日和就业报告日前夜构建了期权隐含的密度函数。

Vertical Integration and Cream Skimming of Profitable Referrals: The Case of Hospital-Owned Skilled Nursing Facilities

纵向整合与盈利性转诊的撇脂行为：以医院所有的专业护理机构为例

David M. Cutler, Leemore Dafny, David C. Grabowski, Steven Lee, Christopher Ody

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine whether vertical integration of hospitals and skilled nursing facilities (SNFs) could lessen competition by foreclosing rival SNFs’ access to lucrative referrals. We find that it could: among integrated providers, a one percent increase in SNF reimbursement for a given patient discharged from the upstream hospital increases the self-referral rate to the hospital’s downstream SNF(s) by 1.8 percent. We find no evidence of offsetting benefits for patients and payers: these increased self-referrals have an imprecisely estimated zero effect on patient outcomes and Medicare spending.

Sovereign Debt Standstills

主权债务暂停

Juan C. Hatchondo, Leonardo Martinez, César Sosa-Padilla

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 全球/多国

Abstract

As a response to the economic difficulties triggered by the COVID-19 pandemic, the G20 implemented the Debt Service Suspension Initiative (DSSI), a standstill or suspension of official bilateral sovereign debt payments for some of the poorest countries. The G20 and others also called on private creditors to offer comparable terms but this did not materialize. We first show that a standard default model can account for the decline in sovereign spreads triggered by the DSSI. The model also accounts for the private creditors’ reluctance to participate in a debt standstill: A private-creditor standstill implies sizable capital losses for debt holders. Furthermore, while sovereign debt standstill proposals emphasize debt payment suspensions without write-offs on the face value of debt obligations, we find that complementing private-creditor standstills with write-offs could reduce debt holders’ losses and simultaneously increase sovereign welfare gains.

Scarred but Wiser: World War 2’s COVID Legacy

伤痕累累但更明智：第二次世界大战的COVID遗产

Michael Lokshin, Vladimir Kolchin, Martin Ravallion

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

The paper formalizes and tests the hypothesis that greater exposure to big shocks induces stronger societal responses for adaptation and protection from future big shocks. We find support for this hypothesis in various strands of the literature and in new empirical tests using cross-country data on deaths due to COVID-19 and deaths during World War 2. Countries with higher death rates in the war saw lower death rates in the first wave of the COVID pandemic, though the effect faded in the pandemic’s second wave. Our tests are robust to a wide range of model specifications and alternative assumptions.

Advanced Technologies Adoption and Use by U.S. Firms: Evidence from the Annual Business Survey

美国企业先进技术的采用与使用：来自年度商业调查的证据

Nikolas Zolas, Zachary Kroff, Erik Brynjolfsson, Kristina McElheran, David N. Beede, Cathy Buffington, Nathan Goldschlag, Lucia Foster, Emin Dinlersoz

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济 · 美国

Abstract

We introduce a new survey module intended to complement and expand research on the causes and consequences of advanced technology adoption. The 2018 Annual Business Survey (ABS), conducted by the Census Bureau in partnership with the National Center for Science and Engineering Statistics (NCSES), provides comprehensive and timely information on the diffusion among U.S. firms of advanced technologies including artificial intelligence (AI), cloud computing, robotics, and the digitization of business information. The 2018 ABS is a large, nationally representative sample of over 850,000 firms covering all private, nonfarm sectors of the economy. We describe the motivation for and development of the technology module in the ABS, as well as provide a first look at technology adoption and use patterns across firms and sectors. We find that digitization is quite widespread, as is some use of cloud computing. In contrast, advanced technology adoption is rare and generally skewed towards larger and older firms. Adoption patterns are consistent with a hierarchy of increasing technological sophistication, in which most firms that adopt AI or other advanced business technologies also use the other, more widely diffused technologies. Finally, while few firms are at the technology frontier, they tend to be large so technology exposure of the average worker is significantly higher. This new data will be available to qualified researchers on approved projects in the Federal Statistical Research Data Center network.

Public and Private Options in Practice: The Military Health System

实践中的公共与私人选择：军事医疗体系

Michael D. Frakes, Jonathan Gruber, Timothy Justicz

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Recent debates over health care reform, including in the context of the Military Health System (MHS) and Veterans Health Administration, highlight the dispute between public and private provision of health care services. Using novel data on childbirth claims from the MHS and drawing on the combination of plausibly exogenous patient moves and heterogeneity across bases in the availability of base hospitals, we identify the impact of receiving obstetrical care on versus off military bases. We find evidence that off-base care is associated with slightly greater resource intensity, but also notably better outcomes, suggesting marginal efficiency gains from care privatization.
