Keep your Enemies Closer: Strategic Platform Adjustments during U.S. and French Elections

亲近你的敌人：美国与法国选举中的战略平台调整

Rafael Di Tella, Randy Kotti, Caroline Le Pennec, Vincent Pons

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 法国、美国

Abstract

We study changes in political discourse during campaigns, using a novel dataset of candidate websites for U.S. House elections, 2002-2016, and manifestos for French parliamentary and local elections, 1958-2022. We find that candidates move to the center in ideology and rhetorical complexity between the first round (or primary) and the second round (or general election). This convergence reflects candidates’ strategic adjustment to their competitor, as predicted by the median voter theorem: Using an RDD, we show that candidates converge to the platform of opponents who narrowly qualified for the last round, as opposed to those who narrowly failed to qualify.

中文摘要

我们利用2002年至2016年美国众议院选举候选人网站的新数据集，以及1958年至2022年法国议会和地方选举的宣言，研究了竞选期间政治话语的变化。我们发现，候选人在第一轮（或初选）和第二轮（或大选）之间在意识形态和修辞复杂性上向中间立场移动。这种趋同反映了候选人根据竞争对手进行的战略调整，正如中位选民定理所预测的那样：利用断点回归设计，我们表明候选人会向那些勉强有资格进入最后一轮的对手的平台趋同，而不是那些勉强未能有资格的对手。

Abstract

We survey the nascent literature on machine learning in the study of financial markets. Social Security Claiming Intentions: Psychological Ownership, Loss Aversion, and Information Displays

社会保障申领意向：心理所有权、损失厌恶与信息展示

Suzanne Shu, John W. Payne

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

For many Americans the question of when to claim Social Security benefits is one of the most consequential financial decisions they will ever face. While acknowledging that individuals differ in terms of optimal timing for starting Social Security benefits, many economists argue that an average person would benefit from delaying claiming as long as they could. Yet this is not what average Americans do. Many more Americans claim as soon as possible, at age 62, rather than as late as possible, at age 70. Why? This paper focuses on individual differences in beliefs and values that influence Social Security claiming intentions. As expected from economic theory, individual differences in life expectations and degree of patience for later larger payouts relate to claiming intentions. In addition, however, we also find that individual differences in psychological ownership of one’s Social Security benefits and individual differences in degree of loss aversion are both significant predictors of Social Security claiming intentions. Further, we find that an “enriched” information display manipulation (nudge) that emphasizes longer-term consequences of late claiming leads to earlier, not later, claiming intentions, and that the size of this effect is related to individual differences in the degree of loss aversion.

中文摘要

对许多美国人而言，何时申领社会保障福利是他们面临的最重要的财务决策之一。尽管承认个人在开始领取社会保障福利的最佳时机上存在差异，但许多经济学家认为，普通人会从尽可能延迟申领中受益。然而，这并非普通美国人的实际行为。更多美国人选择尽早申领（62岁），而非尽可能晚地申领（70岁）。原因何在？本文聚焦于影响社会保障申领意向的个体信念和价值观差异。正如经济理论所预期，个体在预期寿命和对后期更高收益的耐心程度上的差异与申领意向相关。但此外，我们还发现，个体对自身社会保障福利的心理所有权差异以及损失厌恶程度差异，都是社会保障申领意向的重要预测因素。进一步地，我们发现，一种强调延迟申领长期后果的“丰富化”信息展示干预（助推）会导致更早而非更晚的申领意向，且该效应的大小与个体损失厌恶程度的差异相关。

Selection-Neglect in the NFT Bubble

NFT泡沫中的选择忽视

Dong Huang, William N. Goetzmann

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using transaction data from a large non-fungible token (NFT) trading platform, this paper examines how the behavioral bias of selection-neglect interacts with extrapolative beliefs, accelerating the boom and delaying the crash in the recent NFT bubble. We show that the price-volume relationship is consistent with extrapolative beliefs about increasing prices which were plausibly triggered by a macroeconomic shock. We test the hypothesis that agents prone to selection-neglect formed even more optimistic beliefs and traded more aggressively than their counterparts during the boom. When liquidity for NFTs declined, observed NFT prices were subject to severe selection bias due in part to seller loss aversion delaying the onset of the crash. Finally, we show that market participants with sophisticated bidding behavior were less subject to selection bias and performed better.

中文摘要

本文利用来自大型非同质化代币（NFT）交易平台的交易数据，研究了选择忽视这一行为偏差如何与外推信念相互作用，从而在近期的NFT泡沫中加速繁荣并延迟崩溃。我们表明，价格-数量关系与对价格上涨的外推信念一致，而这些信念可能由宏观经济冲击触发。我们检验了以下假设：在繁荣期，倾向于选择忽视的个体比其对应方形成了更乐观的信念，并进行了更激进的交易。当NFT流动性下降时，观察到的NFT价格受到严重的选择偏差影响，部分原因是卖方的损失厌恶延迟了崩溃的启动。最后，我们表明，具有复杂竞价行为的市场参与者受选择偏差影响较小，且表现更好。

Were COVID and the Great Recession Well-being Reducing?

大衰退和新冠疫情是否降低了幸福感？

David G. Blanchflower, Alex Bryson

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 俄罗斯、乌克兰、美国

Abstract

We show individuals’ reports of subjective well being in Europe did decline in the Great Recession and during the Covid pandemic on most measures and on four bordering countries to Ukraine after the Russian invasion in 2022. However, the movements are not large and are not apparent everywhere. We also used data from the European Commission's Business and Consumer Surveys on people’s expectations of life in general, their financial situation and the economic and employment situation in the country, all of which dropped markedly in the Great Recession and during Covid, but bounced back quickly, as did firms’ expectations of the economy and the labor market. Neither the UN’s Human Development Index (HDI) nor data used in the World Happiness Report from the Gallup World Poll shifted much in response to negative shocks. The HDI has been rising in the last decade or so reflecting overall improvements in economic and social wellbeing, captured in part by real earnings growth, although it fell slightly after 2020 as life expectancy dipped. This secular improvement is mirrored in life satisfaction which has been rising in the last decade. However, so too have negative affect in Europe and despair in the USA

中文摘要

我们表明，在大衰退和新冠疫情期间，欧洲个体报告的主观幸福感在大多数指标上确实有所下降，并且在2022年俄罗斯入侵后，乌克兰四个邻国也是如此。然而，这些变化幅度不大，且并非在所有地方都明显。我们还使用了欧盟委员会商业与消费者调查中关于人们对总体生活、个人财务状况以及国家经济和就业状况预期的数据，这些数据在大衰退和新冠疫情期间均显著下降，但迅速反弹，企业对经济和劳动力市场的预期也是如此。无论是联合国人类发展指数（HDI），还是盖洛普世界民意调查中用于《世界幸福报告》的数据，对负面冲击的反应都不大。HDI在过去十年左右持续上升，反映了经济和社会福祉的总体改善，部分体现在实际收入增长上，尽管在2020年后因预期寿命下降而略有下滑。这种长期改善也反映在生活满意度上，后者在过去十年中持续上升。然而，欧洲的负面情绪和美国的绝望感也在上升。

Pandemic, War, Inflation: Oil Markets at a Crossroads?

疫情、战争、通胀：石油市场处于十字路口？

Christiane Baumeister

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 俄罗斯、乌克兰

Abstract

The COVID-19 pandemic as well as the Russian invasion of Ukraine have had profound effects on the global energy landscape, with some of the longer-lasting effects still unfolding. This paper discusses how these events have reshaped the supply side of the global oil market by focusing on structural changes in each of the three main oil-producing countries. The demand side has responded to geopolitical developments by devising a set of policy tools to stabilize oil markets and counter inflationary pressures. In particular, the price cap policy was introduced to supplement the EU embargo on seaborne Russian oil exports, and record volumes of oil were released from government-controlled emergency stockpiles. The sources of oil price fluctuations associated with these events are also discussed, as is their role in the recent surge of inflation, with a particular focus on the heterogeneity in the pass-through of oil supply shocks within the Euro area.

中文摘要

新冠疫情以及俄罗斯入侵乌克兰对全球能源格局产生了深远影响，其中一些较长期的影响仍在显现。本文通过聚焦三个主要产油国各自的结构性变化，讨论了这些事件如何重塑了全球石油市场的供给侧。需求侧则通过制定一系列政策工具来稳定石油市场并应对通胀压力，以回应地缘政治发展。特别是，引入了价格上限政策以补充欧盟对俄罗斯海运石油出口的禁运，并从政府控制的紧急储备中释放了创纪录数量的石油。本文还讨论了与这些事件相关的石油价格波动的来源，以及它们在近期通胀飙升中的作用，特别关注了欧元区内石油供给冲击传导的异质性。

Conservation Priorities and Environmental Offsets: Markets for Florida Wetlands

保护优先事项与环境补偿：佛罗里达湿地市场

Daniel Aronoff, Will Rafey

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We introduce an empirical framework for valuing markets in environmental offsets. Using newly-collected data on wetland conservation and offsets, we apply this framework to evaluate a set of decentralized markets in Florida, where land developers purchase offsets from long-lived producers who restore wetlands over time. We find that offsets led to substantial private gains from trade, creating $2.4 billion of net surplus from 1995–2020 relative to direct conservation. Offset trading also generated new hydrological externalities. A locally differentiated Pigouvian tax would have prevented $1.6 billion of new flood damage while preserving more than two-thirds of the private gains from trade.

中文摘要

我们引入了一个评估环境补偿市场价值的实证框架。利用新收集的湿地保护和补偿数据，我们将该框架应用于评估佛罗里达州的一组分散化市场，在该市场中，土地开发商从长期运营的湿地恢复生产者那里购买补偿。我们发现，补偿带来了可观的私人交易收益，在1995年至2020年间相对于直接保护创造了24亿美元的净剩余。补偿交易也产生了新的水文外部性。一项地区差异化的庇古税本可以防止16亿美元的新增洪水损失，同时保留超过三分之二的私人交易收益。

No. 10论文详情NBER w31494

Remote Work and City Structure

远程工作与城市结构

Ferdinando Monte, Charly Porcher, Esteban Rossi-Hansberg

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

Relative to remote work, working downtown facilitates valuable interactions with other in-office workers, but entails commuting costs. The resulting coordination mechanism can lead to multiple stationary equilibria with varying levels of remote work. Temporary reductions in commuters, as in the COVID-19 pandemic, can then lead to persistently large fractions of remote workers. Cell-phone-based mobility data for the U.S. shows that commuting trips in the largest cities, which are more likely to exhibit multiplicity, have stabilized at only 60% of pre-pandemic levels, while they are fully back in smaller cities. Cities with permanently low commuting experience average welfare losses of 2.3%.

中文摘要

与远程工作相比，在市中心工作有利于与其他办公室员工进行有价值的互动，但需要承担通勤成本。由此产生的协调机制可能导致多个具有不同远程工作水平的稳定均衡。通勤者的暂时减少，例如在COVID-19大流行期间，可能导致远程工作者的比例持续居高不下。基于手机移动数据的美国数据显示，最有可能出现多重均衡的大城市的通勤出行量已稳定在大流行前水平的60%，而小城市的通勤出行量已完全恢复。通勤量永久性较低的城市平均福利损失为2.3%。

To What Extent are Trends in Teen Mental Health Driven by Changes in Reporting? The Example of Suicide-Related Hospital Visits

青少年心理健康趋势在多大程度上由报告变化驱动？以自杀相关医院就诊为例

Adriana Corredor-Waldron, Janet Currie

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 泽西岛

Abstract

Rising reports of suicidal behaviors in children and adolescents have led to the recognition of a youth mental health crisis. However, reported rates can be influenced by access to screening and changes in reporting conventions, as well as by changes in social stigma. Using data on all hospital visits in New Jersey from 2008-2019, we investigate two inflection points in adolescent suicide-related visits and show that a rise in 2012 followed changes in screening recommendations, while a sharp rise in 2016-2017 followed changes in the coding of suicidal ideation. Rates of other suicidal behaviors including self-harm, attempted suicides, and completed suicides were essentially flat over this period. These results suggest that underlying suicide-related behaviors among children, while alarmingly high, may not have risen as sharply as reported rates suggest. Hence, researchers should approach reported trends cautiously.

中文摘要

儿童和青少年自杀行为报告的增加导致人们认识到青少年心理健康危机。然而，报告率可能受到筛查可及性、报告惯例变化以及社会污名变化的影响。利用2008年至2019年新泽西州所有医院就诊数据，我们调查了青少年自杀相关就诊的两个拐点，并表明2012年的上升是筛查建议变化的结果，而2016-2017年的急剧上升则是自杀意念编码变化的结果。在此期间，包括自残、自杀未遂和自杀完成在内的其他自杀行为率基本持平。这些结果表明，儿童中潜在的自杀相关行为虽然高得惊人，但可能并未像报告率所显示的那样急剧上升。因此，研究人员应谨慎对待报告的趋势。

No. 12论文详情NBER w31492

Diversifying Society’s Leaders? The Determinants and Causal Effects of Admission to Highly Selective Private Colleges

使社会领导者多元化？进入高选择性私立大学的决定因素与因果效应

Raj Chetty, David J. Deming, John N. Friedman

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use anonymized admissions data from several colleges linked to income tax records and SAT and ACT test scores to study the determinants and causal effects of attending Ivy-Plus colleges (Ivy League, Stanford, MIT, Duke, and Chicago). Children from families in the top 1% are more than twice as likely to attend an Ivy-Plus college as those from middle-class families with comparable SAT/ACT scores. Dollar Rivals

美元的竞争对手

Jeffrey A. Frankel

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Written on the 50th anniversary of floating exchange rates, this paper deals with possible alternatives to a unipolar dollar-based system. It considers (1) measures of international currency use; (2) potential challengers to the dollar; (3) network externalities; and (4) the plausibility of gold and digital currencies, as alternatives to regular currencies. On the one hand, network externalities operate in favor of the status quo: the dollar as the single leading international currency. On the other hand, the danger of abuse of exorbitant privilege – for example, by debasing the currency or repeated use of sanctions – operates in favor of challengers. A good guess is that the dollar will continue to lose market share slowly to others, but will remain in the lead.

Sparse Modeling Under Grouped Heterogeneity with an Application to Asset Pricing

分组异质性下的稀疏建模及其在资产定价中的应用

Lin William Cong, Guanhao Feng, Jingyu He, Junye Li

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

Sparse models, though long preferred and pursued by social scientists, can be ineffective or unstable relative to large models, for example, in economic predictions (Giannone et al., 2021). To achieve sparsity for economic interpretation while exploiting big data for superior empirical performance, we introduce a general framework that jointly clusters observations (via new decision trees) and locally selects variables (with Bayesian priors) for modeling panel data with potential grouped heterogeneity. We derive analytical marginal likelihoods as global split criteria in our Bayesian Clustering Model (BCM), to incorporate economic guidance, address parameter and model uncertainties, and prevent overfitting. We apply BCM to asset pricing and estimate uncommon-factor models for data-driven asset clusters and macroeconomic regimes. We find (i) cross-sectional heterogeneity linked to (non-linear interactions of) return volatility, size, and value, (ii) structural changes in factor relevance predicted by market volatility and valuation, and (iii) MKTRF and SMB as common factors and multiple uncommon factors across characteristics-managed-market-timed clusters. BCM helps explain volatility- or size-related anomalies, exploit within-group tests, and mitigate the “factor zoo” problem. Overall, BCM outperforms benchmark common-factor models in pricing and investments in U.S. equities, e.g., attaining out-of-sample cross-sectional R2s exceeding 25% for multiple clusters and Sharpe ratio of tangency portfolios tripling built from ME-B/M 5 × 5 portfolios.

中文摘要

When Your Bootstraps Are Not Enough: How Demand and Supply Interact to Generate Learning in Settings of Extreme Poverty

当你的努力不够时：需求与供给如何相互作用在极端贫困环境中产生学习成果

Alex Eble, Maya Escueta

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 冈比亚

Abstract

In settings of extreme poverty, how do demand and supply combine to produce child learning? In rural Gambia, caregivers with high aspirations for their children's future, measured before children start school, invest substantially more than others in children’s education. Despite this, essentially no children are literate or numerate three years later. When villages receive a highly impactful, teacher-focused supply-side intervention, however, children of high-aspirations caregivers are 25 percent more likely to achieve literacy and numeracy than others in the same village. We estimate patterns of substitutability and complementarity between demand and supply in generating learning that change with skill difficulty.
