No. 1论文详情NBER w29861

Pretrial Juvenile Detention

审前青少年拘留

E. Jason Baron, Brian Jacob, Joseph P. Ryan

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Roughly one in four juveniles arrested in the U.S. spend time in a detention center prior to their court date. To study the consequences of this practice for youth, we link the universe of individual public school records in Michigan to juvenile and adult criminal justice records. Using a combination of exact matching and inverse probability weighting, we estimate that juvenile detention leads to a 31% decline in the likelihood of graduating high school and a 25% increase in the likelihood of being arrested as an adult. Falsification tests suggest the results are not driven by unobserved heterogeneity.

中文摘要

在美国，大约每四名被捕青少年中就有一人在开庭前被关押在拘留中心。为了研究这种做法对青少年的影响，我们将密歇根州所有公立学校个人记录与青少年和成人刑事司法记录相关联。结合精确匹配和逆概率加权方法，我们估计青少年拘留导致高中毕业可能性下降31%，成年后被逮捕可能性增加25%。虚假检验表明，结果并非由未观测到的异质性驱动。

In-group Favoritism and Peer Effects in Wrongful Acquittals: NBA Referees as Judges

群体内偏袒与错误无罪判决中的同伴效应：以NBA裁判为例

Naci H. Mocan, Eric Osborne-Christenson

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We provide the first analysis of racial in-group bias in Type-I and Type-II errors. Using player-referee matched data from NBA games we show that there is no overall racial bias or in-group bias in foul calls made by referees. Similarly, there is no racial bias or in-group bias in Type-I errors (incorrect foul calls). On the other hand, there is significant in-group favoritism in Type-II errors. These are wrongful acquittals where the referee did not blow the whistle although a foul was committed. We also analyze peer effects and find that black referees’ proclivity to make Type-II errors in favor of black players exists as long black referees have at least one black peer referee on the court, and that the bias disappears only if black referees have two white peers. In case of white referees, in-group favoritism in Type-II errors emerges if white referees have two black peers with them on the court. We provide evidence showing that the results are not attributable to skill differences between referees. We also show that a higher Type-I error rate during the season lowers referees’ probability to be selected to officiate a game in the playoffs, whereas variations in the rate of Type-II errors have no impact on the likelihood of a playoff assignment. These results indicate that in-group favoritism takes place in a domain which is not costly (making Type-II errors), and that bias is eliminated when it is costly to the decisionmaker.

中文摘要

我们首次分析了第一类错误和第二类错误中的种族内群体偏见。利用NBA比赛中球员与裁判的匹配数据，我们发现裁判在犯规判罚中不存在整体种族偏见或内群体偏见。同样，在第一类错误（错误犯规判罚）中也不存在种族偏见或内群体偏见。另一方面，在第二类错误中存在显著的内群体偏袒。这些是错误的无罪判决，即裁判在犯规发生时未吹哨。我们还分析了同伴效应，发现黑人裁判对黑人球员做出第二类错误的倾向仅在至少有一名黑人同伴裁判在场时存在，只有当黑人裁判有两名白人同伴时偏见才会消失。对于白人裁判，只有当白人裁判有两名黑人同伴在场时，第二类错误中的内群体偏袒才会出现。我们提供的证据表明，这些结果并非归因于裁判之间的技能差异。我们还表明，赛季中较高的第一类错误率会降低裁判被选入季后赛执法的概率，而第二类错误率的变化对季后赛指派可能性没有影响。这些结果表明，内群体偏袒发生在无成本的领域（做出第二类错误），而当对决策者而言成本高昂时，偏见会消失。

Syndicated Lending, Competition and Relative Performance Evaluation

银团贷款、竞争与相对绩效评估

Thomas Schneider, Philip Strahan, Jun Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Relative performance evaluation (RPE) intensifies competitive pressure by tying executive compensation to the profits of rivals. We show that these contracts make loan syndication harder by reducing banks’ willingness to participate in loans underwritten by banks named in their RPE contracts. Lead arranger banks which are more frequently named in RPE hold larger shares of the loans they syndicate, and their borrowers face higher spreads. These banks, in turn, lose market share to banks less likely to be named in RPE. Our results highlight the tension between the normal benefits of competition versus the need for cooperation in loan syndication.

中文摘要

相对绩效评估通过将高管薪酬与竞争对手利润挂钩，加剧了竞争压力。我们表明，这些合同通过降低银行参与其相对绩效评估合同中提及银行承销贷款的意愿，使贷款银团化更加困难。更频繁出现在相对绩效评估中的牵头安排行在其银团贷款中持有更大份额，其借款人面临更高的利差。这些银行反过来将市场份额输给不太可能出现在相对绩效评估中的银行。我们的结果凸显了竞争的正常收益与贷款银团化中合作需求之间的张力。

Black Americans’ Landholdings and Economic Mobility after Emancipation: New Evidence on the Significance of 40 Acres

解放后黑人的土地持有与经济流动性：关于“四十英亩”意义的新证据

William J. Collins, Nicholas C. Holtkamp, Marianne H. Wanamaker

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

The US Civil War ended in 1865 without the distribution of land or compensation to those formerly enslaved—a decision often seen as a cornerstone of racial inequality. We build a dataset to observe Black households’ landholdings in 1880, a key component of their wealth, alongside a sample of White households. We then link their sons to the 1900 census records to observe economic and human capital outcomes. We show that Black landowners (and skilled workers) were able to transmit substantial intergenerational advantages to their sons. But such advantages were small relative to the overall racial gaps in economic status.

中文摘要

美国内战于1865年结束，未向被奴役者分配土地或提供补偿——这一决定常被视为种族不平等的基石。我们构建了一个数据集，观察1880年黑人家庭的土地持有情况（其财富的关键组成部分），同时包含白人家庭样本。然后，我们将他们的儿子与1900年人口普查记录关联，以观察经济和人力资本结果。我们表明，黑人土地所有者（以及熟练工人）能够将显著的代际优势传递给他们的儿子。但这些优势相对于经济地位的整体种族差距而言较小。

Improving the Availability of Unrelated Stem Cell Donors: Evidence from a Major Donor Registry

提高无关干细胞捐赠者的可用性：来自主要捐赠者登记处的证据

Michael Haylock, Patrick Kampkötter, Mario Macis, Jürgen Sauter, Susanne Seitz, Robert Slonim, Daniel Wiesen, Alexander H. Schmidt

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 德国

Abstract

The unavailability of potential stem cell donors poses a critical challenge for donor registries worldwide. This study investigates the impact of initiatives of a stem cell donor registry to enhance donors' availability for confirmatory typing. Initiatives ask donors to provide a sample for genetic analysis and/or information on their temporal unavailability. We analyzed 91,479 confirmatory typing requests from DKMS Germany, a large stem cell donor registry, exploiting a quasi-random initiative assignment based on observable characteristics. We find that, first, invitation to the initiatives increases donors' availability. Intention-to-treat estimates yield effects ranging from 2.5 to 3.2 percentage points, and local average treatment effects estimates range from 3.8 to 8.2 percentage points (baseline: 77.1%). Second, the difference in availability between participants and non-participants is over 10 percentage points. The initiatives yield a direct positive effect on donor availability and a selection effect through which participation signals a higher commitment.

中文摘要

潜在干细胞捐赠者的不可用性对全球捐赠者登记处构成关键挑战。本研究调查了干细胞捐赠者登记处为增强捐赠者用于确认分型的可用性而采取的举措的影响。这些举措要求捐赠者提供基因分析样本和/或关于其暂时不可用性的信息。我们分析了来自德国大型干细胞捐赠者登记处DKMS的91,479份确认分型请求，利用基于可观测特征的准随机举措分配。我们发现，首先，受邀参与举措提高了捐赠者的可用性。意向治疗估计显示效应范围为2.5至3.2个百分点，局部平均处理效应估计范围为3.8至8.2个百分点（基线：77.1%）。其次，参与者和非参与者之间的可用性差异超过10个百分点。这些举措对捐赠者可用性产生直接正向效应，并通过参与表明更高承诺产生选择效应。

Fertility and Savings: The Effect of China’s Two-Child Policy on Household Savings

生育与储蓄：中国二孩政策对家庭储蓄的影响

Scott R. Baker, Efraim Benmelech, Zhishu Yang, Qi Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 蒙古

Abstract

China’s high household savings rate has attracted great academic interest but remains a puzzle. Potential explanations include demographic, policy, and financial causes. Yet a lack of reliable microlevel data on household finances makes it difficult to assess the relative importance of each factor. This paper uses individual income and spending transactions linked to demographic characteristics and financial information on loan applications and credit availability from a large Chinese bank in Inner Mongolia. We match a large subset of bank customers to administrative records covering marriage and births and obtain a unique view into consumption and saving patterns around important life events. Our results point toward identifying income growth, financial instability, and credit access, rather than such directives as the one-child policy, as the primary causes of high levels of savings among Chinese households.

中文摘要

中国的高家庭储蓄率引起了学术界的极大兴趣，但仍是一个谜。潜在解释包括人口、政策和金融原因。然而，缺乏可靠的家庭财务微观数据使得评估各因素的相对重要性变得困难。本文利用来自内蒙古一家大型中国银行的个人收入和支出交易数据，这些数据与人口特征以及贷款申请和信贷可用性的财务信息相关联。我们将大量银行客户与涵盖婚姻和生育的行政记录进行匹配，从而获得对重要生活事件前后消费和储蓄模式的独特视角。我们的结果表明，收入增长、金融不稳定和信贷获取（而非独生子女政策等指令）是中国家庭高储蓄率的主要原因。

Public School Funding, School Quality, and Adult Crime

公立学校资助、学校质量与成人犯罪

E. Jason Baron, Joshua M. Hyman, Brittany N. Vasquez

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

This paper asks whether increasing public school funding can be an effective long-run crime-prevention strategy in the U.S. Specifically, we examine the effect of increases in funding early in children’s lives on the likelihood that they are arrested as adults. We exploit quasi-experimental variation in public school funding, leveraging two natural experiments in Michigan and a novel administrative dataset linking the universe of Michigan public school students to adult criminal justice records. The first research design exploits variation in operating expenditures due to Michigan’s 1994 school finance reform, Proposal A. The second design exploits variation in capital spending by leveraging close school district capital bond elections in a regression discontinuity framework. In both cases, we find that students exposed to additional funding during elementary school were substantially less likely to be arrested in adulthood. We show that the social benefits of increasing school funding are greater than the costs, even when considering only the crime-reducing benefits.

中文摘要

本文探讨增加公立学校资助能否成为美国有效的长期犯罪预防策略。具体而言，我们研究了儿童早期获得的资助增加对其成年后被逮捕可能性的影响。我们利用公立学校资助的准实验性变化，借助密歇根州的两次自然实验以及一个将密歇根州公立学校全体学生与成人刑事司法记录关联起来的新型行政数据集。第一个研究设计利用了密歇根州1994年学校财政改革（提案A）导致的运营支出变化。第二个设计则利用学区资本债券选举的断点回归框架，通过资本支出的变化进行分析。在这两种情况下，我们都发现，在小学阶段获得额外资助的学生成年后被逮捕的可能性显著降低。我们表明，即使仅考虑减少犯罪的效益，增加学校资助的社会收益也大于成本。

The Benefits and Costs of a U.S. Child Allowance

美国儿童津贴的收益与成本

Irwin Garfinkel, Laurel Sariscsany, Elizabeth Ananat, Sophie M. Collyer, Robert Paul Hartley, Buyi Wang, Christopher Wimer

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

We conduct a benefit-cost analysis of a U.S. child allowance, based on a systematic literature review of the highest quality available causal evidence on the short- and long-term effects of cash and near-cash transfers. In contrast to the previous studies we synthesize, which tend to measure a subset of benefits and costs available in a particular dataset, we establish a comprehensive accounting of potential effects and secure estimates of each. We produce core estimates of the benefits and costs per child and per adult of increasing household income by $1,000 in one year; these can be applied to value any cash or near-cash program that increases household income. Using microsimulation, we then apply these estimates to determine net aggregate benefits of three child allowance policies, including the expanded Child Tax Credit as enacted for the year 2021 in the American Rescue Plan (ARP). Our estimates indicate that making that expansion permanent would cost $97 billion per year and generate social benefits with net present value of $982 billion per year. Sensitivity analyses indicate that our estimates are robust to alternative assumptions and that all three child allowance policies we evaluate produce very high net returns for the U.S. population.

中文摘要

我们基于对现金及近似现金转移支付短期和长期影响最高质量因果证据的系统性文献回顾，对美国儿童津贴进行了成本效益分析。与我们综合的以往研究（这些研究往往衡量特定数据集中可获得的收益和成本子集）不同，我们建立了潜在影响的全面核算，并获得了每项影响的可靠估计。我们得出了在一年内将家庭收入增加1000美元时，每个儿童和每个成年人的收益和成本的核心估计值；这些估计值可用于评估任何增加家庭收入的现金或近似现金项目。然后，我们利用微观模拟，将这些估计值应用于确定三项儿童津贴政策的净总收益，包括《美国救援计划》中为2021年制定的扩大的儿童税收抵免。我们的估计表明，将该扩大政策永久化每年将花费970亿美元，并产生每年净现值为9820亿美元的社会收益。敏感性分析表明，我们的估计对替代假设是稳健的，并且我们评估的所有三项儿童津贴政策都为美国人口带来了非常高的净回报。

Frontier Knowledge in Higher Education

Barbara Biasi, Song Ma

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper studies the dissemination of frontier knowledge through higher education. Using natural language processing on 1.7 million university course syllabi and 20 million academic articles, we construct frontier knowledge proximity, a measure of how closely a syllabus is connected to recent research in its field. We document three facts. First, courses differ in frontier knowledge proximity, even within field, course level, and year. Second, instructors shape course content: proximity rises when instructors change and is higher for research-active instructors, especially when their research matches the course topic. Third, access is unequal: advantaged and faculty-intensive schools offer more frontier-oriented courses.

Strengthening State Capacity: Postal Reform and Innovation during the Gilded Age

加强国家能力：镀金时代的邮政改革与创新

Abhay Aneja, Guo Xu

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

We use newly digitized records from the U.S. Post Office to study how strengthening state capacity affects public service delivery and innovation in over 2,700 cities between 1875–1905. Exploiting the gradual expansion of a major civil service reform, cities with a reformed postal office experience fewer errors in delivery, lower unit costs, and an increase in mail handled per worker. This improvement goes with greater information flow, as measured by increased volumes of mail and newspapers. We use personnel data to show that reformed offices see a decline in turnover and an increase in merit-based retention, consistent with a reduction in political interference. We observe more joint patenting involving inventors and businesses from different cities, suggesting that a more effective postal service contributed to innovation and growth during the Gilded Age.

中文摘要

我们利用美国邮政局新近数字化的记录，研究了1875年至1905年间加强国家能力如何影响超过2700个城市的公共服务提供和创新。利用一项重大公务员制度改革的逐步推广，经过改革的邮政局所在城市在投递错误、单位成本方面有所减少，并且每位工人处理的邮件量有所增加。这种改善伴随着更大的信息流动，以邮件和报纸数量的增加来衡量。我们利用人事数据表明，改革后的邮政局人员流动率下降，基于功绩的留任率上升，这与政治干预的减少相一致。我们观察到更多涉及来自不同城市的发明者和企业的联合专利申请，这表明更有效的邮政服务为镀金时代的创新和增长做出了贡献。

Evidence on Retrieved Context: How History Matters

关于检索背景的证据：历史如何重要

William N. Goetzmann, Akiko Watanabe, Masahiro Watanabe

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper tests the retrieved context model of Wachter and Kahana (2019) using a long-term panel of economic forecasts by participants in the Livingston Survey. Events in historical time contribute additional explanatory power to a relative time series model. Historical precedents for current macroeconomic conditions appear to be more relevant for extreme quantile forecasts. The results are consistent with the use of the retrieved context mechanism for formulating expectations about asset prices. They also suggest that historical events, not just lagged variables in relative time, matter in economic forecasting.

中文摘要

This is Air: The "Non-Health" Effects of Air Pollution

这就是空气：空气污染的“非健康”影响

Sandra Aguilar-Gomez, Holt Dwyer, Joshua S. Graff Zivin, Matthew J. Neidell

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

A robust body of evidence shows that air pollution exposure is detrimental to health outcomes, often measured as deaths and hospitalizations. This literature has focused less on subclinical channels that nonetheless impact behavior, performance, and skills. This article reviews the economic research investigating the causal effects of pollution on "non-health" endpoints, including labor productivity, cognitive performance, and multiple forms of decision making. Subclinical effects of pollution can be more challenging to observe than formal health care encounters but may be more pervasive if they affect otherwise healthy people. The wide variety of possible impacts of pollution should be informed by plausible mechanisms and require appropriate hypothesis testing to limit false discovery. Finally, any detected effects of pollution, both in the short and long run, may be dampened by costly efforts to avoid exposure ex-ante and remediate its impacts ex-post; these costs must be considered for a full welfare analysis

Do Startups Benefit from Their Investors’ Reputation? Evidence from a Randomized Field Experiment

初创企业能否从投资者的声誉中受益？来自随机实地实验的证据

Shai Bernstein, Kunal Mehta, Richard R. Townsend, Ting Xu

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We analyze a field experiment conducted on AngelList Talent, a large online search platform for startup jobs. In the experiment, AngelList randomly informed job seekers of whether a startup was funded by a top-tier investor and/or was funded recently. We find that the same startup receives significantly more interest when information about top-tier investors is provided. Information about recent funding has no effect. The effect of top-tier investors is not driven by low-quality candidates and is stronger for earlier-stage startups. The results show that venture capitalists can add value passively, simply by attaching their names to startups.

中文摘要

我们分析了在AngelList Talent（一个大型初创企业职位在线搜索平台）上进行的一项实地实验。实验中，AngelList随机告知求职者某家初创企业是否由顶级投资者资助，以及/或者是否近期获得融资。我们发现，当提供关于顶级投资者的信息时，同一家初创企业会获得显著更多的关注。关于近期融资的信息则没有影响。顶级投资者的效应并非由低质量候选人驱动，并且对于更早期的初创企业更为显著。结果表明，风险资本家可以通过简单地将自己的名字与初创企业关联，被动地增加价值。
