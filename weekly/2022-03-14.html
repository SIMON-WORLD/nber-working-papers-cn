Allocating Scarce Information

稀缺信息的分配

Richard T. Holden, Anup Malani, Chris Teh

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Sender conveys scarce information to a number of receivers to maximize the sum of receiver payoffs. Each receiver’s payoff depends on the state of the world and an action she takes. The optimal action is state contingent. Under mild regularity conditions, we show that the payoff of each receiver is convex in the amount of information she receives. Thus, it is optimal for Sender to target information to a single receiver. We then study four extensions in which interior information allocations are optimal.

中文摘要

发送者向多个接收者传递稀缺信息，以最大化接收者收益的总和。每个接收者的收益取决于世界状态和她采取的行动。最优行动是依状态而定的。在温和的正则条件下，我们证明每个接收者的收益是她所接收信息量的凸函数。因此，发送者将信息集中传递给单个接收者是最优的。随后，我们研究了四种扩展情形，在这些情形中，内部信息分配是最优的。

Economic Geography and the Efficiency of Environmental Regulation

经济地理与环境规制的效率

Alex Hollingsworth, Taylor Jaworski, Carl Kitchens, Ivan J. Rudik

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

We develop a quantitative economic geography model with endogenous emissions, amenities, trade, and labor reallocation to evaluate the spatial impact of the leading air quality regulation in the United States: the National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS). We find that the NAAQS generate $40 billion in annual welfare gains. The gains are spatially concentrated in a small set of cities targeted by the NAAQS, and the improved amenities attract large numbers of nonmanufacturing workers into these areas. We use our model to analyze counterfactual policies and find that using first-best emissions pricing increases welfare by an additional $70 billion per year. Atmospheric transport of emissions, labor reallocation, and trade are first-order factors for quantifying the level and distribution of the costs and benefits of the NAAQS.

中文摘要

我们构建了一个包含内生排放、宜居性、贸易和劳动力重新配置的定量经济地理模型，以评估美国主要空气质量法规——国家环境空气质量标准（NAAQS）的空间影响。我们发现，NAAQS每年带来400亿美元的福利收益。这些收益在空间上集中于NAAQS所针对的一小部分城市，并且改善后的宜居性吸引了大量非制造业工人进入这些地区。我们利用模型分析反事实政策，发现采用最优排放定价每年可额外增加700亿美元的福利。大气污染物传输、劳动力重新配置和贸易是量化NAAQS成本与收益水平及分布的一阶因素。

The Influence of Cousin Order and Cousin Group Size on Educational Outcomes

堂表亲出生顺序与堂表亲群体规模对教育成果的影响

Kieron Barclay, Dalton Conley

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 瑞典

Abstract

Despite growing interest in the potential influence of grandparents on grandchild status attainment, research has not addressed whether the ordinal position or number of grandchildren affects outcomes. We apply sibling- and cousin-fixed effects analyses to Swedish population data to examine how cousin order and cousin group size influence grade point average (GPA) percentile rank at the end of compulsory school. We study cohorts born 1972-2003 (N=1,591,979). In cousin fixed effects analyses, second-born, fifth-born, and tenth or later born maternal cousins achieve GPA ranked scores 1.04, 2.17, and 4.97 percentile points lower than first-born cousins, respectively. Amongst paternal cousins the differences relative to the first-born cousin are 0.02, 0.46, and 1.86 percentile points low-er, respectively—suggesting the greater influence of the mother’s extended family. In further analyses we examine whether an arguably exogenous shock to cousin group size, a twin birth to an aunt or uncle, has any impact on GPA percentile rank. Instrumental variable analyses indicate that an increase in maternal cousin group size has a statistically significant negative effect on GPA rank, lowering GPA rank in percentile points by 0.27, but an increase in paternal cousin group size does not negatively affect GPA rank.

中文摘要

尽管对祖父母可能影响孙辈地位获得的兴趣日益增长，但研究尚未探讨孙辈的出生顺序或数量是否会影响结果。我们运用兄弟姐妹固定效应和堂表亲固定效应分析，基于瑞典人口数据，考察堂表亲出生顺序和堂表亲群体规模如何影响义务教育结束时的平均绩点（GPA）百分位排名。我们研究了1972-2003年出生的队列（N=1,591,979）。在堂表亲固定效应分析中，与头胎出生的母系堂表亲相比，第二胎、第五胎以及第十胎及以后出生的母系堂表亲的GPA百分位排名分别低1.04、2.17和4.97个百分点。在父系堂表亲中，与头胎出生的堂表亲相比，这些差异分别低0.02、0.46和1.86个百分点——这表明母亲一方的大家庭影响更大。在进一步分析中，我们检验了堂表亲群体规模的一个可视为外生的冲击——即姑姨或叔舅的双胞胎出生——是否对GPA百分位排名有任何影响。工具变量分析表明，母系堂表亲群体规模的增加对GPA排名有统计上显著的负向影响，使GPA百分位排名降低0.27，但父系堂表亲群体规模的增加对GPA排名没有负向影响。

Highly Powered Analysis Plans

高功效的分析计划

Michael L. Anderson, Jeremy Magruder

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Formal analysis plans limit false discoveries by registering and multiplicity adjusting statistical tests. As each registered test reduces power on other tests, researchers prune hypotheses based on prior knowledge, often by combining related indicators into evenly-weighted indices. We propose two improvements to maximize learning within these types of analysis plans. First, we develop data-driven optimized indices that can yield more powerful tests than evenly-weighted indices. Second, we discuss organizing the logical structure of an analysis plan into a gated tree that directs type I error towards these high-powered tests. In simulations we show that researchers may prefer these "optimus gates" across a wide range of data-generating processes. We then assess our strategy using the community-driven development (CDD) application from Casey et al. (2012) and the Oregon Health Insurance Experiment from Finkelstein et al. (2012). We find substantial power gains in both applications, meaningfully changing the conclusions of Casey et al. (2012).

中文摘要

正式的分析计划通过注册统计检验并进行多重性调整来限制虚假发现。由于每个注册的检验会降低其他检验的功效，研究者会基于先验知识精简假设，通常通过将相关指标合并为等权指数来实现。我们提出两项改进，以在这类分析计划中最大化学习效果。首先，我们开发了数据驱动的优化指数，其能产生比等权指数更有效的检验。其次，我们讨论了将分析计划的逻辑结构组织成一个门控树，该树将第一类错误导向这些高功效检验。在模拟中，我们表明研究者可能在广泛的数据生成过程中偏好这些“最优门控”。随后，我们使用Casey等人（2012）的社区驱动发展（CDD）应用和Finkelstein等人（2012）的俄勒冈健康保险实验来评估我们的策略。我们发现这两个应用都有显著的功效提升，从而有意义地改变了Casey等人（2012）的结论。

Energy Prices and Electric Vehicle Adoption

能源价格与电动汽车普及

James B. Bushnell, Erich Muehlegger, David S. Rapson

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper presents evidence that gasoline prices have a larger effect on demand for electric vehicles (EVs) than electricity prices in California. We match a spatially-disaggregated panel dataset of monthly EV registration records to detailed records of gasoline and electricity prices in California from 2014-2017, and use these to estimate the effect of energy prices on EV demand. Two distinct empirical approaches (panel fixed-effects and a utility-border discontinuity) yield remarkably similar results: a given change in gasoline prices has roughly four to six times the effect on EV demand as a similar percentage change in electricity prices. We explore the implications for optimal EV subsidies, which promote externality reduction benefits and correct for consumer misoptimization stemming from the undervaluation of future electricity costs.

中文摘要

本文提供的证据表明，在加利福尼亚州，汽油价格对电动汽车（EV）需求的影响大于电价。我们将2014-2017年加利福尼亚州月度电动汽车注册记录的空间细分面板数据集与汽油和电价的详细记录进行匹配，并利用这些数据估计能源价格对电动汽车需求的影响。两种不同的实证方法（面板固定效应和公用事业边界断点回归）得出了显著相似的结果：汽油价格的给定变化对电动汽车需求的影响，大约是相同百分比电价变化的四到六倍。我们探讨了对最优电动汽车补贴的启示，这些补贴旨在促进外部性减少效益，并纠正因低估未来电力成本而导致的消费者错误优化。

Presidential Address: Corporate Finance and Reality

主席致辞：公司金融与现实

John Graham

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper conducts surveys that document CFO perspectives on corporate planning, corporate investment, capital structure, payout, and the goal of the firm. Current policy choices are compared to CFO survey data from two decades prior, which allows me to identify decision-making themes that are common across policies and through time. These common elements of real-world corporate finance indicate that companies make decisions based on internal forecasts that are miscalibrated and thought to be reliable only two years ahead; use decision rules that are conservative, sticky, simple, and that attempt to market time; and, emphasize corporate objectives that increasingly focus on stakeholders and revenues. These themes can guide and discipline academic models and tests, with the aim of better explaining outcomes. A model of satisficing decision-making aligns with many of these practice-of-finance characteristics: optimization is difficult in a complex fast-changing world, so managers use simple rules to make incremental improvements and they stick with rules that have worked well enough in the past. Non-behavioral models with costly biases can also account for some of the themes. Implications and avenues for future research are discussed

中文摘要

本文通过调查记录了首席财务官（CFO）在企业规划、企业投资、资本结构、派息以及公司目标方面的观点。将当前的决策选择与二十年前的CFO调查数据进行比较，这使我能够识别出跨政策和跨时间共通的决策制定主题。这些现实世界公司金融的共同要素表明，公司基于内部预测做出决策，这些预测存在校准偏差，且被认为仅在未来两年内可靠；公司使用保守、粘性、简单且试图把握市场时机的决策规则；并且，公司强调日益关注利益相关者和收入的企业目标。这些主题可以指导和约束学术模型与检验，旨在更好地解释结果。一个满意型决策模型与许多这些金融实践特征相符：在复杂多变的世界中优化是困难的，因此管理者使用简单规则进行渐进式改进，并坚持使用过去效果足够好的规则。具有成本偏差的非行为模型也可以解释部分主题。本文讨论了未来研究的启示和方向。

Invisible Primes: Fintech Lending with Alternative Data

隐形优质借款人：使用替代数据的金融科技贷款

Marco Di Maggio, Dimuthu Ratnadiwakara, Don Carmichael

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We exploit anonymized administrative data provided by a major fintech platform to investigate whether using alternative data to assess borrowers' creditworthiness results in broader credit access. Comparing actual outcomes of the fintech platform’s model to counterfactual outcomes based on a “traditional model” used for regulatory reporting purposes, we find that the latter would result in a 70% higher probability of being rejected and higher interest rates for those approved. The borrowers most positively affected are the “invisible primes”--borrowers with low credit scores and short credit histories, but also a low propensity to default. We show that funding loans to these borrowers leads to better economic outcomes for the borrowers and higher returns for the fintech platform.

中文摘要

我们利用一家主要金融科技平台提供的匿名行政数据，调查使用替代数据评估借款人信用状况是否会扩大信贷获取范围。将金融科技平台模型的实际结果与基于用于监管报告的“传统模型”的反事实结果进行比较，我们发现后者会导致被拒绝的概率高出70%，并且对获批者收取更高的利率。受正面影响最大的借款人是“隐形优质借款人”——即信用评分低、信用记录短，但违约倾向也低的借款人。我们表明，向这些借款人提供贷款会为借款人带来更好的经济结果，并为金融科技平台带来更高的回报。

Reducing Frictions in Healthcare Access: The ActionHealth NYC Experiment for Undocumented Immigrants

减少医疗获取中的摩擦：面向无证移民的ActionHealth NYC实验

Jonathan Gruber, Adrienne Sabety, Rishi Sood, Jin Yung Bae

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

In 2016, New York City designed and implemented an intervention reducing frictions in accessing safety-net care: randomly making initial primary care appointments for 2,428 undocumented immigrants. We leverage a novel survey-administrative data linkage to show that the program resulted in a more efficient allocation of care. The program increased self-reported access to primary care, leading to a 21% fall in emergency department (ED) use. This effect was driven by high-risk individuals whose ED visits fell by 42% on average. Among those visiting sponsored clinics, chronic condition diagnoses and preventive screens increased, positively affecting long-run health.

中文摘要

2016年，纽约市设计并实施了一项干预措施，旨在减少获取安全网医疗的摩擦：随机为2428名无证移民安排首次初级保健预约。我们利用一项新颖的调查-行政数据链接，表明该计划带来了更高效的医疗资源配置。该计划提高了自我报告的初级保健可及性，导致急诊科使用率下降21%。这一效应由高风险个体驱动，其急诊就诊次数平均减少了42%。在前往赞助诊所就诊的人群中，慢性病诊断和预防性筛查有所增加，对长期健康产生了积极影响。

Stock Volatility and the War Puzzle: The Military Demand Channel

股票波动性与战争之谜：军事需求渠道

Gustavo S. Cortes, Angela Vossmeyer, Marc D. Weidenmier

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 伊拉克、美国

Abstract

U.S. stock volatility is 25 percent lower during wartime and periods of conflict, including World War II. Schwert (1989) identified the “war puzzle” as a surprising fact from two centuries of realized stock volatility data. We hypothesize that stable demand from defense spending makes corporate America’s cash flows easier to forecast during wartime. Using new hand-collected data on 100 years of military spending, we document that defense outlays reduce aggregate, sector- and state-level stock volatility. Firm-level event studies of recent U.S. military conflicts demonstrate that equity analysts’ earnings forecasts of procurement-intensive companies became significantly less dispersed in the aftermath of 9/11 and the invasion of Iraq.

中文摘要

美国股票波动性在战争和冲突时期（包括第二次世界大战）降低了25%。Schwert（1989）根据两个世纪的实际股票波动性数据，将“战争之谜”识别为一个令人惊讶的事实。我们假设，国防支出的稳定需求使得美国企业的现金流在战争期间更易于预测。利用新手工收集的100年军事支出数据，我们记录了国防支出降低了总体、行业和州层面的股票波动性。对近期美国军事冲突的公司层面事件研究表明，在9/11事件和入侵伊拉克之后，股权分析师对采购密集型公司的盈利预测分散度显著降低。

The Effects of Legal Representation on Tenant Outcomes in Housing Court: Evidence from New York City's Universal Access Program

法律代理对住房法庭中租户结果的影响：来自纽约市全民法律援助计划的证据

Michael T. Cassidy, Janet Currie

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Housing is one of the areas where it may be most critical for poor people to have access to legal representation in civil cases. We use the roll-out of New York City’s Universal Access to Counsel program (UA) to assess the effects of legal representation on tenant outcomes, using detailed address-level housing court data from 2016 to 2019. The program offers free legal representation in housing court to tenants with income at or below 200 percent of the federal poverty guideline. We find that tenants who gain lawyers are less likely to be subject to possessory judgments, face smaller monetary judgments, are less likely to have eviction warrants issued against them, and are less likely to be evicted. Lawyers have larger effects in poorer places and in those with larger shares of noncitizens. Our results support the idea that legal representation in civil procedures can have important positive impacts on the lives of poor people.

中文摘要

住房是穷人在民事案件中可能最需要获得法律代理的领域之一。我们利用纽约市全民法律援助计划的逐步推广，使用2016年至2019年详细的地址级住房法庭数据，评估法律代理对租户结果的影响。该计划为收入等于或低于联邦贫困线200%的租户提供住房法庭的免费法律代理。我们发现，获得律师的租户更少受到占有判决的影响，面临的金钱判决金额更小，针对他们发出的驱逐令更少，并且被驱逐的可能性更低。律师在更贫困的地区以及非公民比例更高的地区效果更大。我们的结果支持这样一种观点，即民事诉讼中的法律代理可以对穷人的生活产生重要的积极影响。

Consumption Heterogeneity and Monetary Policy in an Open Economy

开放经济中的消费异质性与货币政策

Sihao Chen, Michael B. Devereux, Kang Shi, Jenny Xu

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We explore how consumption heterogeneity affects the international transmission mechanism of monetary shocks and the choice of optimal monetary policy in an open economy. Incorporating two types of agents (Ricardian versus Keynesian) into a standard open economy macro model, we find that there are sizeable ranges of household heterogeneity in which monetary policy become ineffective, but this depends sensitively on the interaction of aggregate demand and relative price effects. We derive the global optimal monetary policy with household heterogeneity under alternative pricing regimes. PPI targeting is still the optimal monetary policy under PCP and can restore the economy to the efficient equilibrium. Under LCP, however, the presence of consumption heterogeneity and currency misalignment implies that CPI inflation targeting is no longer optimal in most cases. Finally, we show that when fiscal instruments such as an import tax and export subsidy are introduced, both currency misalignment and consumption heterogeneity can be eliminated, and even under LCP, PPI targeting is the optimal monetary rule.

中文摘要

我们探讨了消费异质性如何影响货币冲击的国际传导机制以及开放经济中最优货币政策的选择。将两类代理人（李嘉图型与凯恩斯型）纳入标准开放经济宏观模型，我们发现存在相当大的家庭异质性范围，在此范围内货币政策变得无效，但这敏感地依赖于总需求与相对价格效应的相互作用。我们在不同定价机制下推导了具有家庭异质性的全球最优货币政策。在生产者货币定价下，生产者价格指数目标仍然是最优货币政策，并能将经济恢复到有效均衡。然而，在当地货币定价下，消费异质性和货币错配的存在意味着，在大多数情况下，消费者价格指数通胀目标不再是最优的。最后，我们表明，当引入进口税和出口补贴等财政工具时，货币错配和消费异质性都可以被消除，即使在当地货币定价下，生产者价格指数目标也是最优的货币规则。

New Evidence on Sectoral Labor Productivity: Implications for Industrialization and Development

关于部门劳动生产率的新证据：对工业化与发展的启示

Berthold Herrendorf, Richard Rogerson, Ákos Valentinyi

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Moving labor from agriculture to manufacturing – “industrialization” – is often viewed as essential for the development of poor countries. We present new evidence on the channels through which industrialization can help poor countries close the productivity gap with rich countries. To achieve this, we leverage recent data releases by the Groningen Growth and Development Centre and build a new dataset of comparable labor productivity levels in agriculture and manufacturing for 64 mostly poor countries during 1990–2018. We find two key results: (i) cross-country labor productivity gaps in manufacturing are larger than in the aggregate and (ii) there is no tendency for manufacturing labor productivity to converge. While these results challenge the notion that expanding manufacturing employment is essential for the development of today’s poor countries, we also find that higher labor productivity growth in manufacturing is associated with higher labor productivity growth in the aggregate and in several key sectors.

中文摘要

将劳动力从农业转移到制造业——即“工业化”——通常被视为贫穷国家发展的关键。我们提供了关于工业化如何帮助贫穷国家缩小与富裕国家生产率差距的新证据。为此，我们利用格罗宁根增长与发展中心最近发布的数据，构建了一个涵盖1990-2018年期间64个主要是贫穷国家的农业和制造业可比劳动生产率水平的新数据集。我们发现了两个关键结果：（i）制造业的跨国劳动生产率差距大于总体差距；（ii）制造业劳动生产率没有趋同的趋势。虽然这些结果挑战了扩大制造业就业对当今贫穷国家发展至关重要的观点，但我们也发现，制造业更高的劳动生产率增长与总体以及几个关键部门更高的劳动生产率增长相关联。

High-Dimensional Factor Models with an Application to Mutual Fund Characteristics

高维因子模型及其在共同基金特征中的应用

Martin Lettau

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper considers extensions of two-dimensional factor models to higher-dimensional data represented as tensors. I describe decompositions of tensors that generalize the standard matrix singular value decomposition and principal component analysis to higher dimensions. I estimate the model using a three-dimensional data set consisting of 25 characteristics of 1,342 mutual funds observed over 34 quarters. The tensor factor models reduce the data dimensionality by 97% while capturing 93% of the variation of the data. I relate higher-dimensional tensor models to standard two-dimensional models and show that the components of the model have clear economic interpretations.

Personal Increasing Returns: Analytics and Applications

个人递增回报：分析与应用

Casey B. Mulligan

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The human capital investment model with endogenous labor supply is generalized to consumer and health behaviors while retaining the tractability of comparative-static analysis of a single first-order condition. Accounting for the endogenous specialization responses is essential to properly distinguish supply and demand factors and to understand how the magnitude of their effects vary across time and circumstances. Even signing effects of policy interventions can hinge on the existence and extent of personal increasing returns. Applications include the gender gap in earnings, the dynamics of substance abuse, effects of taxes on human capital, the tradeoff between product quality and quantity, and unintended consequences of energy regulation. Metrics are provided for assessing the extent of personal increasing returns.

Fueling Alternatives: Gas Station Choice and the Implications for Electric Charging

替代燃料的选择：加油站选择及其对电动汽车充电的启示

Jackson Dorsey, Ashley Langer, Shaun McRae

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper quantifies the value of electric vehicle (EV) charging networks and the marginal value of network speed and density. We estimate a model of gasoline drivers’ refueling preferences and simulate how these potential future EV drivers value refueling time under counterfactual charging networks. Drivers value refueling time at $19.73/hour. EV adopters with home charging receive $675 per vehicle in benefits from avoiding travel to gas stations, whereas refueling travel and waiting time costs $7,763 for drivers using public charging. Increasing network charging speed yields three times greater time savings than a proportional increase in station density.

Export Expansion and Investment in Children’s Human Capital: Evidence from the U.S.-Vietnam Bilateral Trade Agreement

出口扩张与儿童人力资本投资：来自美越双边贸易协定的证据

Brian McCaig, Minh Nguyen, Robert Kaestner

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 越南

Abstract

We examine how export expansion induced by the U.S-Vietnam Bilateral Trade Agreement (BTA) affected migration, school enrollment, work and healthcare use of young children and adolescents in Vietnam. To do so, we exploit variation in tariff reductions across industries associated with the BTA and differences in industry employment shares across Vietnamese provinces prior to the policy change. We find that the BTA led to migration to the most affected provinces, particularly by adolescents (15 to 18) and young adults (19 to 29). The BTA also increased household expenditures, slightly decreased employment among nonmigrant adults and increased employment among migrant adults. Among adolescents, enrolment increased among non-migrants, but fell among migrants, with the opposite pattern for working. For children (7 to 14), enrolment did not change among non-migrants, but fell for migrants, who typically moved with their family. Conditional on being enrolled, education expenditures increased for both children and adolescents. We find evidence that healthcare utilization decreased for both children and adolescents.

中文摘要

我们研究了由美越双边贸易协定（BTA）引发的出口扩张如何影响越南儿童和青少年的迁移、入学、工作及医疗保健使用情况。为此，我们利用了BTA相关行业关税削减的差异，以及政策变化前越南各省份行业就业份额的差异。我们发现，BTA导致人口向受影响最严重的省份迁移，尤其是青少年（15至18岁）和年轻成年人（19至29岁）。BTA还增加了家庭支出，略微减少了非移民成年人的就业，并增加了移民成年人的就业。在青少年中，非移民的入学率上升，但移民的入学率下降，而工作模式则相反。对于儿童（7至14岁），非移民的入学率没有变化，但移民的入学率下降，这些移民通常随家庭迁移。在已入学的情况下，儿童和青少年的教育支出均有所增加。我们发现有证据表明，儿童和青少年的医疗保健利用率均有所下降。

Progressivity of Pricing at US Public Universities

美国公立大学定价的累进性

Emily E. Cook, Sarah Turner

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Substantial increases in public university tuition often raise concerns about college affordability. But assessment of the impacts on low- and moderate-income families requires consideration of whether net tuition—tuition less grant aid—has increased commensurately. This paper describes recent shifts in net tuition by family income and institution type and assesses the role of changes in state funding in generating these shifts. Using data reported by universities on net tuition paid by students from different family income levels, we find that public research universities have increasingly shifted to high-tuition, high-aid pricing. From 2012 to 2018, net tuition fell by far more than would have been predicted by the growth in state appropriations, while tuition levels continued to rise, albeit at a slower rate than in the prior years. The increased progressivity in pricing, particularly among research universities, cannot be explained by changes in state appropriations.

中文摘要

Loss Aversion or Lack of Trust: Why Does Loss Framing Work to Encourage Preventative Health Behaviors?

损失厌恶还是缺乏信任：为什么损失框架能有效鼓励预防性健康行为？

Emily A. Beam, Yusufcan Masatlioglu, Tara Watson, Dean Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We implemented a field experiment designed to increase participants’ willingness to visit a health clinic. We find differential responses to a $50 incentive framed as a loss versus framed as a gain. We find little support for the notion that loss aversion is responsible for the effectiveness of loss framing. Instead, it appears that loss framing promotes take-up by raising the perceived probability that the incentive will be provided as promised. The results suggest trust is an alternative pathway through which loss framing may affect behavior, and trust may be an important way to promote desirable health behaviors.

Stock Market Stimulus

股市刺激

Robin Greenwood, Toomas Laarits, Jeffrey Wurgler

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 香港、美国

Abstract

We study the stock market effects of the arrival of the three rounds of “stimulus checks” to U.S. taxpayers and the single round of direct payments to Hong Kong citizens. The first two rounds of U.S. checks appear to have increased retail buying and share prices of retail-dominated portfolios. The Hong Kong payments increased overall turnover and share prices on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. We cannot rule out that these price effects were permanent. The findings raise novel questions about the role of fiscal stimulus in the stock market.

Consumption and Saving after Retirement

退休后的消费与储蓄

Bent Jesper Christensen, Malene Kallestrup-Lamb, John Kennan

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 丹麦、美国

Abstract

The paper analyzes consumption decisions of retired workers, using Danish register data. A major puzzle, which motivates much of the analysis below, is that wealth actually increases for a large fraction of the people in our data. One would expect that wealth accumulated before retirement would be used to augment consumption in later life, with the implication that wealth should decline over time. The risk of large out-of-pocket medical expenditures is negligible in Denmark, so although explanations associated with such expenditures might explain similar patterns in U.S. data, these explanations are not plausible for Denmark (and therefore also questionable for the U.S.). Our analysis instead attempts to explain wealth paths using a model that emphasizes fluctuations in the marginal utility of consumption. The results show that a latent state variable extension of the standard life-cycle consumption model is quite successful in explaining the curious observed wealth patterns after retirement for singles.

中文摘要

What Do the Data Tell Us About Inflation Expectations?

数据告诉我们关于通胀预期的什么？

Francesco D’Acunto, Ulrike Malmendier, Michael Weber

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Inflation expectations are central to economics because they affect the effectiveness of fiscal and monetary policy as well as realized inflation. We survey the recent literature with a focus on the inflation expectations of households. We first review standard data sources and discuss their advantages and disadvantages. We then document that household inflation expectations are biased upwards, dispersed across individuals, and volatile in the time series. We also provide evidence of systematic differences by gender, income, education, and race. Turning to the underlying expectations formation process, we highlight the role of individuals' exposure to price signals in their daily lives, such as price changes in groceries, the role of lifetime experiences, and the role of cognition. We then discuss the literature that links inflation expectations to economic decisions at the individual level, including consumption-savings and financial decisions. We conclude with an outlook for future research.

中文摘要

通胀预期对经济学至关重要，因为它们影响财政和货币政策的有效性以及实际通胀。我们调查了近期文献，重点关注家庭通胀预期。我们首先回顾了标准数据来源，并讨论了它们的优缺点。然后，我们记录了家庭通胀预期存在向上偏差、个体间分散且时间序列波动大的特点。我们还提供了按性别、收入、教育和种族存在系统性差异的证据。转向潜在预期形成过程，我们强调了个人在日常生活中接触价格信号（如杂货价格变化）、终身经历以及认知的作用。接着，我们讨论了将通胀预期与个人层面经济决策（包括消费储蓄和金融决策）联系起来的文献。最后，我们对未来研究进行了展望。
