No. 1论文详情NBER w30314

Social Capital II: Determinants of Economic Connectedness

社会资本 II：经济关联性的决定因素

Raj Chetty, Matthew O. Jackson, Theresa Kuchler, Johannes Stroebel, Nathaniel Hendren, Robert B. Fluegge, Sara Gong, Federico Gonzalez, Armelle Grondin, Matthew Jacob, Drew Johnston, Martin Koenen, Eduardo Laguna-Muggenburg, Florian Mudekereza, Tom Rutter, Nicolaj Thor, Wilbur Townsend, Ruby Zhang, Mike Bailey, Pablo Barberá, Monica Bhole, Nils Wernerfelt

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Low levels of social interaction across class lines have generated widespread concern and are associated with worse outcomes, such as lower rates of upward income mobility. Here, we analyze the determinants of cross-class interaction using data from Facebook, building upon the analysis in the first paper in this series. We show that about half of the social disconnection across socioeconomic lines—measured as the difference in the share of high-socioeconomic status (SES) friends between low- and high-SES people—is explained by differences in exposure to high- SES people in groups such as schools and religious organizations. The other half is explained by friending bias—the tendency for low-SES people to befriend high-SES people at lower rates even conditional on exposure. Friending bias is shaped by the structure of the groups in which people interact. For example, friending bias is higher in larger and more diverse groups and lower in religious organizations than in schools and workplaces. Distinguishing exposure from friending bias is helpful for identifying interventions to increase cross-SES friendships (economic connectedness). Using fluctuations in the share of high-SES students across high school cohorts, we show that increases in high-SES exposure lead low-SES people to form more friendships with high-SES people in schools that exhibit low levels of friending bias. Hence, socioeconomic integration can increase economic connectedness in communities where friending bias is low. In contrast, when friending bias is high, increasing cross-SES interaction among existing members may be necessary to increase economic connectedness. To support such efforts, we release privacy-protected statistics on economic connectedness, exposure, and friending bias for each ZIP code, high school, and college in the U.S. at www.socialcapital.org.

中文摘要

跨阶层社会互动水平低下引发了广泛担忧，并与收入向上流动性降低等更差的结果相关。在此，我们利用Facebook的数据，基于本系列第一篇论文的分析，研究了跨阶层互动的决定因素。我们发现，约一半的社会经济断层（以低社会经济地位人群与高社会经济地位人群之间，拥有高社会经济地位朋友比例的差异来衡量）可归因于在学校和宗教组织等群体中接触高社会经济地位人群的差异。另一半则归因于交友偏差——即低社会经济地位人群即使在接触条件相同的情况下，与高社会经济地位人群交友的倾向也较低。交友偏差受到人们互动群体结构的影响。例如，在规模更大、更多元化的群体中，交友偏差更高；而在宗教组织中，交友偏差低于学校和工作场所。区分接触与交友偏差有助于识别增加跨社会经济地位友谊（经济关联性）的干预措施。利用高中年级队列中高社会经济地位学生比例的波动，我们发现在交友偏差水平较低的学校，高社会经济地位接触的增加会导致低社会经济地位人群与高社会经济地位人群建立更多友谊。因此，在交友偏差较低的社区，社会经济融合可以增加经济关联性。相反，当交友偏差较高时，可能需要增加现有成员之间的跨社会经济地位互动，以提高经济关联性。为支持此类努力，我们在www.socialcapital.org上发布了美国每个邮政编码区、高中和大学的经济关联性、接触和交友偏差的隐私保护统计数据。

No. 2论文详情NBER w30313

Social Capital I: Measurement and Associations with Economic Mobility

社会资本 I：测量及其与经济流动性的关联

Raj Chetty, Matthew O. Jackson, Theresa Kuchler, Johannes Stroebel, Nathaniel Hendren, Robert B. Fluegge, Sara Gong, Federico González, Armelle Grondin, Matthew Jacob, Drew Johnston, Martin Koenen, Eduardo Laguna-Muggenburg, Florian Mudekereza, Tom Rutter, Nicolaj Thor, Wilbur Townsend, Ruby Zhang, Mike Bailey, Pablo Barberá, Monica Bhole, Nils Wernerfelt

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

In this paper—the first in a series of two papers that use data on 21 billion friendships from Facebook to study social capital—we measure and analyze three types of social capital by ZIP code in the United States: (i) connectedness between different types of people, such as those with low vs. high socioeconomic status (SES); (ii) social cohesion, such as the extent of cliques in friendship networks; and (iii) civic engagement, such as rates of volunteering. These measures vary substantially across areas, but are not highly correlated with each other. We demonstrate the importance of distinguishing these forms of social capital by analyzing their associations with economic mobility across areas. The fraction of high-SES friends among low-SES individuals—which we term economic connectedness—is among the strongest predictors of upward income mobility identified to date, whereas other social capital measures are not strongly associated with economic mobility. If children with low-SES parents were to grow up in counties with economic connectedness comparable to that of the average child with high-SES parents, their incomes in adulthood would increase by 20% on average. Differences in economic connectedness can explain well-known relationships between upward income mobility and racial segregation, poverty rates, and inequality. To support further research and policy interventions, we publicly release privacy-protected statistics on social capital by ZIP code at www.socialcapital.org.

中文摘要

在本文——本系列两篇论文中的第一篇，利用Facebook上210亿个友谊数据研究社会资本——中，我们测量并分析了美国按邮政编码区划分的三种社会资本：(i) 不同类型人群（如低社会经济地位与高社会经济地位人群）之间的关联性；(ii) 社会凝聚力，例如友谊网络中派系的程度；(iii) 公民参与，例如志愿服务率。这些指标在不同地区差异显著，但彼此之间相关性不高。我们通过分析它们与地区间经济流动性的关联，证明了区分这些社会资本形式的重要性。低社会经济地位个体中高社会经济地位朋友的比例——我们称之为经济关联性——是迄今为止已识别的向上收入流动性最强的预测因子之一，而其他社会资本指标与经济流动性的关联并不强。如果父母为低社会经济地位的儿童，在具有与普通高社会经济地位父母儿童相当的经济关联性的县成长，他们成年后的收入平均将增加20%。经济关联性的差异可以解释向上收入流动性与种族隔离、贫困率和不平等之间已知的关系。为支持进一步研究和政策干预，我们在www.socialcapital.org上公开发布了按邮政编码区划分的社会资本隐私保护统计数据。

The Impact of the Clean Air Act on Particulate Matter in the 1970s.

1970年代《清洁空气法》对颗粒物的影响

Maureen L. Cropper, Nicholas Z. Muller, Yongjoon Park, Victoria Perez-Zetune

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine whether counties designated as out of attainment with the National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS) under the 1970 CAA experienced larger reductions in Total Suspended Particulates (TSP) during the 1970s than attainment counties. We answer this question using the official designation of nonattainment status which, between 1972 and 1978, was by Air Quality Control Region (AQCR). Data from balanced panels of TSP monitors in operation from 1969-78 and from 1971-78 are used to examine the impact of nonattainment status on TSP. We also examine the impact of nonattainment on TSP using the definition in the literature, which designates a county as out of attainment if any of its monitors violated the NAAQS. Using the official (AQCR) nonattainment designation, TSP, on average, fell by 10.2 μg/m3 using the 1969 panel and 9.1 μg/m3 using the 1971 panel. Using the definition of nonattainment in the literature yields smaller reductions: 6.0 μg/m3 using the 1969-78 panel and 7.7 μg/m3 using the 1971-78 panel. Using the correct definition of nonattainment in difference-in-differences (DID) models calls into question whether these results can be interpreted as causal. When counties are characterized using the official nonattainment designation, the parallel trends assumption, crucial to causal inference in the DID context, is violated.

中文摘要

我们考察了根据1970年《清洁空气法》被指定为未达到国家环境空气质量标准（NAAQS）的县，在1970年代是否比达标县经历了更大的总悬浮颗粒物（TSP）减少。我们使用1972年至1978年间按空气质量控制区（AQCR）进行的官方未达标状态指定来回答这个问题。我们利用1969-1978年和1971-1978年期间运行的TSP监测仪平衡面板数据，检验未达标状态对TSP的影响。我们还使用文献中的定义（即如果某个县的任何监测仪违反了NAAQS，则将该县指定为未达标）来检验未达标对TSP的影响。使用官方（AQCR）未达标指定，使用1969年面板，TSP平均下降了10.2 μg/m³；使用1971年面板，平均下降了9.1 μg/m³。使用文献中的未达标定义，下降幅度较小：使用1969-1978年面板为6.0 μg/m³，使用1971-1978年面板为7.7 μg/m³。在双重差分（DID）模型中使用正确的未达标定义，使这些结果是否可被解释为因果关系受到质疑。当使用官方未达标指定对县进行特征描述时，对DID背景下因果推断至关重要的平行趋势假设被违反了。

No. 4论文详情NBER w30311

The Race Between Education, Technology, and Institutions

教育、技术与制度之间的竞赛

Jonathan Vogel

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

I generalize the canonical model--in which relative supply and demand for worker skills shape the skill premium--incorporating monopsony power, minimum wages, and unemployment. I estimate the extended canonical model using national data and, separately, state-level data. I show that incorporating the minimum wage improves the out-of-sample fit of the traditional canonical model. I document that minimum wages--together with supply and demand--play a central role in shaping the evolution of the U.S. college premium and the differential evolution of state-level college premia. Lending credibility to these conclusions, the state and national estimates are not only qualitatively, but also quantitatively consistent.

中文摘要

我将经典模型——其中工人技能的相对供给和需求塑造了技能溢价——进行了推广，纳入了买方垄断势力、最低工资和失业。我分别使用全国数据和州级数据估计了扩展后的经典模型。我表明，纳入最低工资改善了传统经典模型的样本外拟合度。我记录了最低工资——与供给和需求一起——在塑造美国大学溢价的演变以及州级大学溢价的差异化演变中发挥着核心作用。为这些结论提供可信度的是，州级和全国估计不仅在定性上一致，而且在定量上也一致。

Screening with Multitasking: Theory and Empirical Evidence from Teacher Tenure Reform

多任务下的筛选：来自教师任期改革的理论与实证证据

Michael Dinerstein, Isaac M. Opper

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 美国

Abstract

What happens when employers screen their employees but only observe a subset of output? We specify a model with heterogeneous employees and show that their response to the screening affects output in both the probationary period and the post-probationary period. The post-probationary impact is due to their heterogeneous responses affecting which individuals are retained and hence the screening efficiency. We show that the impact of the endogenous response on both the unobserved outcome and screening efficiency depends on whether increased effort on one task increases or decreases the marginal cost of effort on the other task. If the response decreases unobserved output in the probationary period then it increases the screening efficiency, and vice versa. We then assess these predictions empirically by studying a change to teacher tenure policy in New York City, which increased the role that a single measure—test score value-added—played in tenure decisions. We show that in response to the policy teachers increased test score value-added and decreased output that did not enter the tenure decision. The increase in test score value-added was largest for the teachers with more ability to improve students' untargeted outcomes, increasing their likelihood of getting tenure. We estimate that the endogenous response to the policy announcement reduced the screening efficiency gap—defined as the reduction of screening efficiency stemming from the partial observability of output—by 28%, effectively shifting some of the cost of partial observability from the post-tenure period to the pre-tenure period.

中文摘要

当雇主筛选其员工但仅观察到部分产出时会发生什么？我们构建了一个包含异质性员工的模型，并表明他们对筛选的反应会影响试用期和试用期后的产出。试用期后的影响源于他们的异质性反应影响了哪些个体被保留，从而影响了筛选效率。我们表明，内生反应对未观测结果和筛选效率的影响取决于在一项任务上增加努力是否会增加或减少另一项任务上的边际努力成本。如果反应降低了试用期的未观测产出，那么它会提高筛选效率，反之亦然。然后，我们通过研究纽约市教师任期政策的变化来实证评估这些预测，该政策增加了单一指标——考试成绩增值——在任期决策中的作用。我们表明，作为对该政策的回应，教师提高了考试成绩增值，并减少了未进入任期决策的产出。考试成绩增值的增加在那些更有能力提高学生非目标结果的教师中最大，从而增加了他们获得任期的可能性。我们估计，对政策公告的内生反应将筛选效率差距——定义为由产出的部分可观测性导致的筛选效率降低——减少了28%，有效地将部分部分可观测性的成本从任期后时期转移到了任期前时期。

Social Protection and Social Distancing During the Pandemic: Mobile Money Transfers in Ghana

疫情期间的社会保护与社会距离：加纳的移动货币转账

Dean Karlan, Matt Lowe, Robert Darko Osei, Isaac Osei-Akoto, Benjamin N. Roth, Christopher R. Udry

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 加纳

Abstract

We randomized mobile money transfers to a sample of low-income Ghanaians during the COVID-19 pandemic. Treated households received eight transfers that sum to roughly one month’s income, while control households only received one transfer. The mere announcement of upcoming transfers has null effects. Once disbursed, transfers increase contemporaneous food expenditure by 8% and income by 20%, but do not affect psychological well-being. Over 40% of the transfers are spent on food. We find suggestive evidence that transfers increased social distancing. The positive effect on income does not persist to two years after the last transfer, and surprisingly, two-year effects on consumption and psychological well-being are negative. Together, we learn that pandemic-era cash transfers can support households economically without diminishing adherence to public health protocols, though with null or negative long-term effects.

中文摘要

在COVID-19疫情期间，我们向加纳低收入人群样本随机发放了移动货币转账。受助家庭收到了八笔总计约一个月收入的转账，而对照家庭仅收到一笔转账。仅宣布即将到来的转账没有效果。一旦发放，转账使同期食品支出增加了8%，收入增加了20%，但不影响心理健康。超过40%的转账用于食品。我们发现了转账增加了社会距离的提示性证据。对收入的积极影响并未持续到最后一笔转账后的两年，而且令人惊讶的是，两年后对消费和心理健康的影响是负面的。总之，我们了解到，疫情时期的现金转移可以在不削弱遵守公共卫生协议的情况下为家庭提供经济支持，尽管长期影响为零或负面。

Still Worth the Trip? School Busing Effects in Boston and New York

仍然值得一程？波士顿和纽约的校车接送效果

Joshua Angrist, Guthrie Gray-Lobe, Clemence M. Idoux, Parag A. Pathak

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

School assignment in Boston and New York City came to national attention in the 1970s as courts across the country tried to integrate schools. Today, district-wide choice allows Boston and New York students to enroll far from home, perhaps enhancing integration. Urban school transportation is increasingly costly, however, and has unclear integration and education consequences. We estimate the causal effects of non-neighborhood school enrollment and school travel on integration, achievement, and college enrollment using an identification strategy that exploits partly-random assignment in the Boston and New York school matches. Instrumental variables estimates suggest distance and travel boost integration for those who choose to travel, but have little or no effect on test scores and college attendance. We argue that small effects on educational outcomes reflect modest effects of distance and travel on school quality as measured by value-added.

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The last two centuries witnessed the rise and fall of empires. We construct a model which rationalises this in terms of the changing trade gains from empires. In the model, empires are arrangements that reduce trade cost between an industrial metropole and the agricultural periphery. During early industrialisation, the value of such bilateral trade increases, and so does the value of empires. As industrialisation diffuses, and as manufactures become more differentiated, trade becomes more multilateral and intra-industry, reducing the value of empires. Our results are consistent with long-term changes in income distribution and trade patterns, and with previous historical arguments.

中文摘要

过去两个世纪见证了帝国的兴衰。我们构建了一个模型，从帝国带来的贸易收益变化角度对此进行解释。在该模型中，帝国是降低工业大都市与农业边缘地区之间贸易成本的安排。在早期工业化时期，这种双边贸易的价值增加，帝国的价值也随之增加。随着工业化的扩散以及制成品变得更加差异化，贸易变得更加多边化和产业内化，从而降低了帝国的价值。我们的结果与收入分配和贸易模式的长期变化以及以往的历史论点是一致的。

The Distributional Impact of the Minimum Wage in the Short and Long Run

最低工资在短期和长期的分配效应

Erik Hurst, Patrick J. Kehoe, Elena Pastorino, Thomas Winberry

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop a framework with rich worker heterogeneity, firm monopsony power, and putty-clay technology to study the distributional impact of the minimum wage in the short and long run. Our production technology is disciplined to be consistent with the small estimated employment effects of the minimum wage in the short run and the large estimated elasticities of substitution across inputs in the long run. We find that in the short run, a large increase in the minimum wage has a small effect on employment and therefore increases the labor income of the workers who were earning less than the new minimum wage. In the long run, however, the minimum wage has perverse distributional implications in that it reduces the employment, income, and welfare of precisely the low-income workers it is meant to help. Nonetheless, these long-run effects take time to fully materialize because firms slowly adjust their mix of inputs. Existing transfer programs, such as the earned income tax credit (EITC), are more effective at improving long-run outcomes for workers at the low end of the wage distribution. But combining existing programs with a modest increase in the minimum wage generates even larger welfare gains for low-earning workers.

中文摘要

我们构建了一个包含丰富工人异质性、企业买方垄断力量和油灰-粘土技术的框架，以研究最低工资在短期和长期的分配效应。我们的生产技术受到约束，使其与短期最低工资就业效应较小以及长期投入替代弹性较大的估计结果一致。我们发现，在短期内，大幅提高最低工资对就业影响很小，因此增加了收入低于新最低工资的工人的劳动收入。然而，在长期中，最低工资会产生反常的分配效应，因为它恰恰减少了本应受其帮助的低收入工人的就业、收入和福利。尽管如此，这些长期效应需要时间才能完全显现，因为企业会缓慢调整其投入组合。现有的转移支付项目，如劳动所得税抵免（EITC），在改善工资分布低端工人的长期结果方面更为有效。但将现有项目与适度提高最低工资相结合，将为低收入工人带来更大的福利收益。

First Foot Forward: A Two-Step Econometric Method for Parsing and Estimating the Impacts of Multiple Identities

迈出第一步：解析和估计多重身份影响的二步计量经济学方法

Andrew S. Hanks, Kevin M. Kniffin, Xuechao Qian, Bo Wang, Bruce A. Weinberg

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Marketing and strategy researchers have often studied how organizations navigate multiple identities in relation to category spanning but extant literature pays less attention to understanding how individuals do so. Moreover, current econometric approaches only scratch the surface with respect to addressing the impact of multiple identities in professional settings. As a model domain to study labor market returns when individuals have more than one identity, we focus on interdisciplinary dissertators in the United States since evidence shows clear uptrends in dissertators engaging multiple professional identities and unclear trends in their outcomes. Our novel estimation method leverages a two-step process to characterize salaries of interdisciplinary dissertators as functions of the identities (academic fields) they acquire as graduate students. We estimate a first-stage regression of log earnings for monodisciplinarians on field dummies and respondent characteristics. After capturing the estimated field coefficients, we then regress log earnings for interdisciplinarians on linear and non-linear functions of these coefficients. Our estimates robustly reject the hypothesis that interdisciplinarians receive a salary premium. We also find evidence that the academic market, but not other employment sectors, particularly compensates researchers based on their primary discipline, an outcome that challenges emphases on interdisciplinarity. While our findings for interdisciplinarians point to the primary identity holding predominant importance for doctoral graduates in the United States, our two-step method provides a framework for parsing and estimating the varied impacts of multiple identities across a wide range of contexts.

市场营销和战略研究者经常研究组织如何在跨类别背景下处理多重身份，但现有文献较少关注个体如何做到这一点。此外，当前的计量经济学方法在处理专业环境中多重身份的影响方面仅触及表面。作为研究个体拥有多重身份时劳动力市场回报的模型领域，我们聚焦于美国的跨学科博士论文作者，因为有证据显示，从事多重专业身份的博士论文作者呈明显上升趋势，而他们的结果趋势却不明确。我们的新颖估计方法利用一个两步过程，将跨学科博士论文作者的薪资表征为他们作为研究生所获得的身份（学科领域）的函数。我们首先对单一学科者的对数收入进行第一阶段回归，自变量包括学科虚拟变量和受访者特征。在获得估计的学科系数后，我们再将跨学科者的对数收入对这些系数的线性和非线性函数进行回归。我们的估计稳健地拒绝了跨学科者获得薪资溢价的假设。我们还发现证据表明，学术市场（而非其他就业部门）特别根据研究者的主要学科来补偿他们，这一结果挑战了对跨学科性的强调。虽然我们对跨学科者的发现表明，对于美国的博士毕业生而言，主要身份占据主导重要性，但我们的两步方法提供了一个框架，用于在广泛情境中解析和估计多重身份的不同影响。

Help Really Wanted? The Impact of Age Stereotypes in Job Ads on Applications from Older Workers

真的需要帮助吗？招聘广告中的年龄刻板印象对老年工作者申请的影响

Ian Burn, Daniel Firoozi, Daniel Ladd, David Neumark

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Correspondence studies have found evidence of age discrimination in callback rates for older workers, but less is known about whether job advertisements can themselves shape the age composition of the applicant pool. We construct job ads for administrative assistant, retail, and security guard jobs, using language from real job ads collected in a prior large-scale correspondence study (Neumark et al., 2019a). We modify the job-ad language to randomly vary whether or not the job ad includes ageist language regarding age-related stereotypes. Our main analysis relies on machine learning methods to design job ads based on the semantic similarity between phrases in job ads and age-related stereotypes. In contrast to a correspondence study in which job searchers are artificial and researchers study the responses of real employers, in our research the job ads are artificial and we study the responses of real job searchers. \nWe find that job-ad language related to ageist stereotypes, even when the language is not blatantly or specifically age-related, deters older workers from applying for jobs. The change in the age distribution of applicants is large, with significant declines in the average and median age, the 75th percentile of the age distribution, and the share of applicants over 40. Based on these estimates and those from the correspondence study, and the fact that we use real-world ageist job-ad language, we conclude that job-ad language that deters older workers from applying for jobs can have roughly as large an impact on hiring of older workers as direct age discrimination in hiring.

中文摘要

通信研究已发现老年工作者在回电率方面存在年龄歧视的证据，但关于招聘广告本身是否能塑造申请者的年龄构成，我们知之甚少。我们为行政助理、零售和保安职位构建了招聘广告，使用了先前一项大规模通信研究（Neumark et al., 2019a）中收集的真实招聘广告的语言。我们修改了招聘广告的语言，随机变化广告是否包含与年龄相关刻板印象有关的年龄歧视性语言。我们的主要分析依赖于机器学习方法，根据招聘广告中的短语与年龄相关刻板印象之间的语义相似性来设计招聘广告。与通信研究中求职者是虚构的、研究人员研究真实雇主的反应不同，在我们的研究中，招聘广告是虚构的，我们研究真实求职者的反应。我们发现，与年龄歧视性刻板印象相关的招聘广告语言，即使这些语言并非明显或专门与年龄相关，也会阻止老年工作者申请工作。申请者年龄分布的变化很大，平均年龄和中位年龄、年龄分布的第75百分位数以及40岁以上申请者的比例均显著下降。基于这些估计以及通信研究的估计，并且考虑到我们使用了现实世界中的年龄歧视性招聘广告语言，我们得出结论：阻止老年工作者申请工作的招聘广告语言对老年工作者招聘的影响，大致与招聘中的直接年龄歧视一样大。
