No. 1论文详情NBER w30191

On the Role of Learning, Human Capital, and Performance Incentives for Wages

论学习、人力资本与绩效激励对工资的作用

Braz Camargo, Fabian Lange, Elena Pastorino

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Although performance pay is a key source of wage dispersion, little is known about why it typically accounts for only a small portion of pay or how it shapes wages over the life cycle. As we document, standard models of performance incentives fail to explain its declining importance later in the life cycle. We fill this gap and resolve this puzzle by proposing a new framework that integrates well-known models of dynamic moral hazard, uncertainty about worker productivity, and human capital acquisition, and proving it is identified based on its equilibrium characterization. Once we parameterize the model using well-known firm-level data, we find that performance pay is central to life-cycle wages. Strikingly, its level and life-cycle profile are not driven by the canonical risk-incentive tradeoff of moral-hazard models. Instead, they reflect workers’ desire to insure against the productivity risk arising from uncertainty about their productivity and to acquire human capital.

中文摘要

尽管绩效薪酬是工资离散的关键来源，但关于它为何通常仅占薪酬的一小部分，以及它如何塑造整个生命周期的工资，人们知之甚少。正如我们所记录的，标准的绩效激励模型无法解释其在生命周期后期重要性下降的现象。我们通过提出一个新框架来填补这一空白并解决这一谜题，该框架整合了关于动态道德风险、工人生产率不确定性以及人力资本获取的知名模型，并证明基于其均衡特征可以识别该模型。一旦我们使用知名的企业层面数据对模型进行参数化，我们发现绩效薪酬对生命周期工资至关重要。引人注目的是，其水平和生命周期轨迹并非由道德风险模型中经典的风险-激励权衡所驱动。相反，它们反映了工人希望为因自身生产率不确定性而产生的生产率风险提供保险，以及获取人力资本的愿望。

Why Does Right-to-Carry Cause Violent Crime to Increase?

为何“携带权”会导致暴力犯罪增加？

John J. Donohue, Samuel V. Cai, Matthew V. Bondy, Philip J. Cook

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

While the recent state panel data literature has broadly concluded that “right-to-carry” (RTC) concealed handgun regimes increase violent crime, there is little empirical evidence on the precise mechanisms that drive this increase. Using data from 217 US cities, we find that the effect of RTC on violent crime is concentrated to large urban centers. In cities with an average population of over 250,000 between 1979 and 2019, we find that the introduction of RTC increases violent crime by 20 percent. We then present novel estimates that RTC increases gun theft by 50 percent and lowers violent crime clearance rates by 9 percent in these large cities. Leveraging city-level heterogeneity in RTC-induced violent crime effects, we demonstrate that these two mechanisms explain a substantial portion of the RTC-induced increase in violent crime.

中文摘要

尽管近期州面板数据文献普遍认为“携带权”（RTC）隐蔽持枪制度会增加暴力犯罪，但关于驱动这一增长的具体机制，实证证据很少。利用美国217个城市的数据，我们发现RTC对暴力犯罪的影响集中在大城市中心。在1979年至2019年间平均人口超过25万的城市中，我们发现引入RTC会使暴力犯罪增加20%。随后，我们提出了新的估计，表明在这些大城市中，RTC使枪支盗窃增加50%，并使暴力犯罪破案率降低9%。利用城市层面RTC引发的暴力犯罪效应的异质性，我们证明这两种机制解释了RTC导致的暴力犯罪增长的很大一部分。

Mechanism Design Approaches to Blockchain Consensus

区块链共识的机制设计方法

Joshua S. Gans, Richard T. Holden

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Blockchain consensus is a state whereby each node in a network agrees on the current state of the blockchain. Existing protocols achieve consensus via a contest or voting procedure to select one node as a dictator to propose new blocks. However, this procedure can still lead to potential attacks that make consensus harder to achieve or lead to coordination issues if multiple, competing chains (i.e., forks) are created with the potential that an untruthful fork might be selected. We explore the potential for mechanisms to be used to achieve consensus that are triggered when there is a dispute impeding consensus. Using the feature that nodes stake tokens in proof of stake (POS) protocols, we construct revelation mechanisms in which the unique (subgame perfect) equilibrium involves validating nodes propose truthful blocks using only the information that exists amongst all nodes. We construct operationally and computationally simple mechanisms under both Byzantine Fault Tolerance and a Longest Chain Rule, and discuss their robustness to attacks. Our perspective is that the use of simple mechanisms is an unexplored area of blockchain consensus and has the potential to mitigate known trade-offs and enhance scalability.

中文摘要

区块链共识是指网络中每个节点就区块链的当前状态达成一致的状态。现有协议通过竞赛或投票程序选择单个节点作为独裁者来提议新区块，从而实现共识。然而，这一程序仍可能导致潜在攻击，使共识更难达成，或者如果创建了多个竞争链（即分叉），且可能选择不诚实的分叉，则会导致协调问题。我们探索了在出现阻碍共识的争议时，使用机制来实现共识的潜力。利用权益证明（POS）协议中节点质押代币的特性，我们构建了揭示机制，在该机制中，唯一的（子博弈完美）均衡涉及验证节点仅使用所有节点之间存在的现有信息来提议诚实的区块。我们在拜占庭容错和最长链规则下构建了操作和计算上简单的机制，并讨论了它们对攻击的稳健性。我们的观点是，使用简单机制是区块链共识中一个尚未被探索的领域，并且有可能缓解已知的权衡并增强可扩展性。

Globalization, Trade Imbalances and Inequality

全球化、贸易失衡与不平等

Rafael Dix-Carneiro, Sharon Traiberman

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We investigate the role of trade imbalances for the distributional consequences of globalization. We do so through the lens of a quantitative, general equilibrium, multi-country, multi-sector model of trade with four key ingredients: (a) workers with different levels of skills are organized into separate representative households; (b) endogenous trade imbalances arise from households' consumption and saving decisions; (c) production exhibits capital-skill complementarity; (d) labor market frictions across sectors and non-employment. We conduct a series of counterfactual experiments that illustrate the quantitative importance of both trade imbalances and capital-skill complementarity for the dynamics of the skill premium. We show that modelling trade imbalances can lead to stark differences between short- and long-run consequences of globalization shocks for the skill premium.

中文摘要

我们研究了贸易失衡在全球化分配后果中的作用。我们通过一个定量的一般均衡、多国、多部门贸易模型来审视这一问题，该模型包含四个关键要素：(a) 不同技能水平的工人被组织成独立的代表性家庭；(b) 内生贸易失衡源于家庭的消费和储蓄决策；(c) 生产表现出资本-技能互补性；(d) 跨部门的劳动力市场摩擦和非就业。我们进行了一系列反事实实验，这些实验说明了贸易失衡和资本-技能互补性对技能溢价动态的定量重要性。我们表明，对贸易失衡进行建模可能导致全球化冲击对技能溢价的短期和长期后果之间存在显著差异。

Aggregate Lapsation Risk

总体退保风险

Ralph S. J. Koijen, Hae Kang Lee, Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study aggregate lapsation risk in the life insurance sector. We construct two lapsation risk factors that explain a large fraction of the common variation in lapse rates of the 30 largest life insurance companies. The first is a cyclical factor that is positively correlated with credit spreads and unemployment, while the second factor is a trend factor that correlates with the level of interest rates. Using a novel policy-level database from a large life insurer, we examine the heterogeneity in risk factor exposures based on policy and policyholder characteristics. Young policyholders with higher health risk in low-income areas are more likely to lapse their policies during economic downturns. We explore the implications for hedging and valuation of life insurance contracts. Ignoring aggregate lapsation risk results in mispricing of life insurance policies. The calibrated model points to overpricing on average. In the cross-section, young, low-income, and high-health risk households face higher effective mark-ups than the old, high-income, and healthy.

中文摘要

我们研究了寿险行业的总体退保风险。我们构建了两个退保风险因子，它们解释了30家最大寿险公司退保率中大部分共同变动。第一个是周期性因子，与信用利差和失业率正相关，而第二个是趋势因子，与利率水平相关。利用一家大型寿险公司新颖的保单层面数据库，我们根据保单和保单持有人特征考察了风险因子暴露的异质性。在经济衰退期间，低收入地区健康风险较高的年轻保单持有人更有可能退保。我们探讨了对寿险合同对冲和估值的启示。忽略总体退保风险会导致寿险保单定价错误。校准后的模型表明平均而言存在定价过高。在横截面上，与年长、高收入和健康的家庭相比，年轻、低收入和高健康风险的家庭面临更高的有效加价。

The Effect of Labor Market Liberalization on Political Behavior and Free Market Norms

劳动力市场自由化对政治行为与自由市场规范的影响

Ran Abramitzky, Netanel Ben-Porath, Shahar Lahad, Victor Lavy, Michal Palgi

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 以色列

Abstract

We study the effects of labor market liberalization on political behavior and attitudes towards free-market capitalism and socialism, exploiting a reform whereby the Israeli socialist communities called kibbutzim shifted from equal sharing to market-based wages. Our identification strategy relies on this reform's sharp and staggered implementation in different kibbutzim. We first examine changes in behavior associated with this labor market liberalization and document that the reform led to a shift in electoral voting patterns, resulting in decreased support for left-wing political parties and increased support for the center and right parties in national elections. Using annual survey data on attitudes over 25 years, we show that the reform led to increased support for free-market policies such as full privatization and differential wages. Moreover, it decreased support for socialist policies such as the joint ownership of production means. Yet, the reform increased support for the safety net to support weak members through mutual guarantee. These effects appear to be driven by an increase in living standards and work ethics that resulted from the reform. We conclude that introducing market-based wages led to a shift in attitudes towards a market economy with compassion, revealing a change in members’ support from their traditional democratic socialist model to a social democratic model.

中文摘要

我们研究了劳动力市场自由化对政治行为以及对自由市场资本主义和社会主义态度的影响，利用了一项改革，即以色列的社会主义社区——基布兹——从平均分配转向基于市场的工资。我们的识别策略依赖于这项改革在不同基布兹中突然且交错实施的特点。我们首先考察了与此次劳动力市场自由化相关的行为变化，并记录到该改革导致了选举投票模式的转变，表现为在全国选举中对左翼政党的支持减少，对中间和右翼政党的支持增加。利用25年间的年度态度调查数据，我们表明该改革导致了对自由市场政策（如完全私有化和差异化工资）的支持增加。此外，它减少了对社会主义政策（如生产资料共同所有制）的支持。然而，该改革增加了对通过互助保障支持弱势成员的安全网的支持。这些效应似乎是由改革带来的生活水平提高和职业道德增强所驱动的。我们得出结论，引入基于市场的工资导致态度向带有同情心的市场经济转变，揭示了成员对其传统民主社会主义模式的支持转向社会民主模式。

Excess Savings and Twin Deficits: The Transmission of Fiscal Stimulus in Open Economies

超额储蓄与双赤字：开放经济中财政刺激的传导

Rishabh Aggarwal, Adrien Auclert, Matthew Rognlie, Ludwig Straub

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the effects of debt-financed fiscal transfers in a general equilibrium, heterogeneous-agent model of the world economy. In the long run, increases in government debt anywhere raise the world interest rate and increase private wealth everywhere. In the short run, a country with a larger-than-average fiscal deficit experiences both a large increase in private savings (“excess savings”) and a small but persistent current account deficit (a slow-motion “twin deficit”). These patterns are consistent with the evolution of the world’s balance of payments since the beginning of the Covid pandemic.

中文摘要

我们在一个世界经济的异质性代理人一般均衡模型中研究了债务融资型财政转移支付的影响。长期来看，任何地方政府债务的增加都会提高世界利率并增加所有地方的私人财富。短期内，一个财政赤字高于平均水平的国家会同时经历私人储蓄的大幅增加（“超额储蓄”）和一个小而持续的经常账户赤字（一个缓慢的“双赤字”）。这些模式与自新冠疫情开始以来世界国际收支的演变是一致的。

No. 8论文详情NBER w30184

Labor Supply and the Pension-Contribution Link

劳动力供给与养老金缴费关联

Eric French, Attila S. Lindner, Cormac O'Dea, Tom A. Zawisza

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 波兰

Abstract

We estimate the impact of public pension systems on labor supply far from the normal retirement age by exploiting Poland's switch from a Defined Benefit to a Notional Defined Contribution scheme for men born after 1948. Using the universe of taxpayers and this sharp cohort-based discontinuity in the link between current contributions and future benefits, we estimate an employment elasticity with respect to the return to work of 0.44 for ages 51-54. We estimate a lifecycle model that matches these results. The model implies that the change in the contribution-benefit link from the reform increases employment among those in their 30s but decreases it at older ages, reducing overall labor supply across the lifecycle by 2 months.

中文摘要

我们利用波兰对1948年后出生的男性从固定收益制转向名义固定缴费制的改革，估计了公共养老金制度对远未达到正常退休年龄的劳动力供给的影响。利用全体纳税人数据和这种基于出生队列的、当前缴费与未来福利之间关联的尖锐断点，我们估计了51-54岁年龄段劳动力供给对工作回报的弹性为0.44。我们估计了一个与这些结果相匹配的生命周期模型。该模型表明，改革带来的缴费-福利关联变化增加了30多岁人群的就业，但减少了老年人群的就业，从而在整个生命周期内将总体劳动力供给减少了2个月。

No. 9论文详情NBER w30183

The Political Polarization of Corporate America

美国企业的政治极化

Vyacheslav Fos, Elisabeth Kempf, Margarita Tsoutsoura

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

U.S. executives are increasingly segregating by political party. Using voter registration records for top executives of S&P 1500 firms between 2008 and 2022, we document that while the executive pool shifted toward Democrats, partisan segregation across firms increased sharply. Intensifying segregation offsets 74% of the decline in team-level homogeneity implied by changing party composition. Turnover is the driving force: politically misaligned executives are more likely to depart, and executives who join top teams increasingly match the team’s partisan majority. The trend accelerates after the 2016 presidential election, marking an inflection point in the political polarization of corporate America.

中文摘要

美国高管正日益按政党划分。利用2008年至2022年间标普1500强企业高管的选民登记记录，我们记录了以下现象：虽然高管群体整体转向民主党，但企业间的党派隔离急剧加剧。这种加剧的隔离抵消了因政党构成变化所隐含的团队层面同质性下降的74%。人员流动是主要驱动力：政治立场不一致的高管更可能离职，而加入高层团队的高管越来越倾向于与团队的党派多数保持一致。这一趋势在2016年总统大选后加速，标志着美国企业政治极化的一个转折点。

No. 10论文详情NBER w30182

Politics At Work

职场中的政治

Emanuele Colonnelli, Valdemar Pinho Neto, Edoardo Teso

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 巴西

Abstract

We study how individual political views shape firm behavior and labor market outcomes using new micro-data from Brazil. We first show that business owners are considerably more likely to employ copartisan workers. This phenomenon is in part driven by the overlapping of political and social networks. Multiple tests—a survey, an event study, analyses of wage premia and promotions within the firm, and a field experiment—further highlight how business owners’ political preferences directly influence firms’ employment decisions. A channel of political discrimination appears more relevant than one of political quid-pro-quo between firms and politicians.

中文摘要

我们利用来自巴西的新微观数据，研究个人政治观点如何塑造企业行为和劳动力市场结果。我们首先发现，企业主雇佣同党派工人的可能性显著更高。这一现象部分由政治与社会网络的重叠驱动。多项检验——一项调查、一项事件研究、对企业内部工资溢价和晋升的分析，以及一项实地实验——进一步凸显了企业主的政治偏好如何直接影响企业的雇佣决策。政治歧视的渠道似乎比企业与政客之间的政治利益交换渠道更为相关。

Uncharted Waters: Effects of Maritime Emission Regulation

未知水域：海上排放法规的影响

Jamie Hansen-Lewis, Michelle M. Marcus

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

Maritime shipping emits as much fine particulate matter as half of global road traffic. Abstract

Abstract

Abstract

Preparing for the (Non-Existent?) Future of Work

为（不存在的？）未来工作做准备

Anton Korinek, Megan Juelfs

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper considers the labor market and distributional implications of a scenario of ever-more-intelligent autonomous machines that substitute for human labor and drive down wages. We lay out three concerns arising from such a scenario and evaluate recent predictions and objections to these concerns. Then we analyze how a utilitarian social planner would allocate work and income if these concerns start to materialize. As the income produced by autonomous machines rises and the value of labor declines, a utilitarian planner finds it optimal to phase out work, beginning with workers who have low labor productivity and job satisfaction, since they have comparative advantage in enjoying leisure. This is in stark contrast to welfare systems that force individuals with low labor productivity to work. If there are significant wage declines, avoiding mass misery will require other ways of distributing income than labor markets, whether via sufficiently well-distributed capital ownership or via benefits. Recipients could still engage in work for its own sake if they enjoy work amenities such as structure, purpose and meaning. If work gives rise to positive externalities such as social connections or political stability, or if individuals undervalue the benefits of work because of internalities, then a social planner would incentivize work. However, in the long run, the planner might be able to achieve a higher level of social welfare by adopting alternative ways of providing these benefits.
