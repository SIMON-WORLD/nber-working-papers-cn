Repurchase Options in the Market for Lemons

柠檬市场中的回购期权

Saki Bigio, Liyan Shi

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study repurchase options (repo contracts) in a competitive asset market with asymmetric information. Gains from trade emerge from a liquidity need, but private information about asset quality prevents the full realization of trade. We obtain a unique equilibrium, which features a pooling repo contract and full participation among borrowers. The equilibrium repo contract resolves adverse selection: the embedded repurchase option prevents the market unraveling that occurs in asset-sale markets. However, the contract is inefficient due to cream skimming. Competition to attract high-quality borrowers through the terms of the repurchase option inefficiently lowers liquidity. The equilibrium contract has a closed form and is portable to many applications.

中文摘要

我们在一个存在信息不对称的竞争性资产市场中研究回购期权（回购合约）。交易收益源于流动性需求，但关于资产质量的私人信息阻碍了交易的完全实现。我们得到了一个唯一均衡，其特征是存在一个混合回购合约以及借款人的完全参与。均衡回购合约解决了逆向选择问题：嵌入的回购期权防止了资产销售市场中出现的市场崩溃。然而，由于撇脂行为，该合约是低效的。通过回购期权条款吸引高质量借款人的竞争会低效地降低流动性。均衡合约具有封闭形式，并可移植到许多应用中。

The Stable Transformation Path

稳定转型路径

Francisco J. Buera, Joseph P. Kaboski, Martí Mestieri, Daniel G. O'Connor

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Dynamic models of structural transformation are often non-stationary, precluding balanced growth path (BGP) analysis. We develop a generalization of the BGP concept that we call a Stable Transformation Path (STraP). The STraP characterizes the medium-term dynamics of the economy in a turnpike sense; it is the non-stationary path toward which the economy (quickly) con- verges from an arbitrary initial capital stock. Calibrated simulations demonstrate that these medium-term dynamics have important quantitative implications for structural transformation, investment, and growth, including slow convergence. Medium-term dynamics alone account for the observed 40% secular decline in average growth rates across stages of development.

中文摘要

结构转型的动态模型通常是非平稳的，这阻碍了平衡增长路径分析。我们发展了一个平衡增长路径概念的推广，称之为稳定转型路径。稳定转型路径在高速公路意义上刻画了经济的中期动态；它是经济从任意初始资本存量（快速）收敛到的非平稳路径。校准后的模拟表明，这些中期动态对结构转型、投资和增长具有重要的定量含义，包括缓慢的收敛。仅中期动态就解释了观察到的各发展阶段平均增长率40%的长期下降。

Coping with H-1B Shortages: Firm Performance and Mitigation Strategies

应对H-1B短缺：企业绩效与缓解策略

Anna Maria Mayda, Francesc Ortega, Giovanni Peri, Kevin Y. Shih, Chad Sparber

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

The United States' H-1B visa program, which allows private firms to hire highly skilled foreign workers, was so severely over-subscribed in the years since 2014 that H-1B status was distributed by lotteries to a subset of applicants. Using data on H-1B applications and on a range of outcomes for publicly traded companies, we find that employers using the H-1B program experienced reduced employment, sales and profits, compared to non-users in the years since 2014. We also find that some employers anticipated the rationing of H-1Bs and retained a larger share of H-1B workers, mitigating the damaging effects of H-1B rationing on their performance.

中文摘要

美国的H-1B签证计划允许私营企业雇佣高技能外籍工人，自2014年以来该计划严重超额认购，H-1B身份通过抽签方式分配给部分申请人。利用H-1B申请数据以及上市公司的一系列结果数据，我们发现，与未使用该计划的企业相比，自2014年以来使用H-1B计划的企业在就业、销售额和利润方面均出现下降。我们还发现，一些雇主预见到了H-1B的配给限制，并保留了更大比例的H-1B工人，从而减轻了H-1B配给对其绩效的破坏性影响。

Income and Poverty in the COVID-19 Pandemic

COVID-19疫情中的收入与贫困

Jeehoon Han, Bruce D. Meyer, James X. Sullivan

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

This paper addresses the economic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic by providing timely and accurate information on the impact of the current pandemic on income and poverty to inform the targeting of resources to those most affected and assess the success of current efforts. We construct new measures of the income distribution and poverty with a lag of only a few weeks using high frequency data from the Basic Monthly Current Population Survey (CPS), which collects income information for a large, representative sample of U.S. families. Because the family income data for this project are rarely used, we validate this timely measure of income by comparing historical estimates that rely on these data to estimates from data on income and consumption that have been used much more broadly. Our results indicate that at the start of the pandemic, government policy effectively countered its effects on incomes, leading poverty to fall and low percentiles of income to rise across a range of demographic groups and geographies. Simulations that rely on the detailed CPS data and that closely match total government payments made show that the entire decline in poverty that we find can be accounted for by the rise in government assistance, including unemployment insurance benefits and the Economic Impact Payments. Our simulations further indicate that of those losing employment the vast majority received unemployment insurance, though this was less true early on in the pandemic and receipt was uneven across the states, with some states not reaching a large share of their out of work residents.\nUpdated information on the summary measures presented in this paper, using the latest data available, may be found at povertymeasurement.org.

中文摘要

本文通过提供关于当前疫情对收入和贫困影响的及时准确信息，来评估COVID-19疫情的经济影响，以便将资源精准投向受影响最严重的人群，并评估当前努力的成功程度。我们利用基本月度当前人口调查（CPS）的高频数据，构建了滞后仅数周的收入分配和贫困新指标，该调查收集了大量具有代表性的美国家庭样本的收入信息。由于本项目使用的家庭收入数据很少被采用，我们通过将依赖这些数据的历史估计与更广泛使用的收入和消费数据估计进行比较，验证了这一及时收入指标的有效性。我们的结果表明，在疫情初期，政府政策有效抵消了其对收入的影响，导致贫困率下降，并且在一系列人口群体和地区中，低收入百分位的收入有所上升。基于详细的CPS数据并与政府实际支付总额高度匹配的模拟显示，我们发现的贫困率下降完全可以由政府援助的增加来解释，包括失业保险福利和经济影响支付。我们的模拟进一步表明，在失业人群中，绝大多数人获得了失业保险，尽管这在疫情早期并不完全如此，且各州的领取情况不均衡，一些州未能覆盖其大部分失业居民。关于本文所呈现的汇总指标的最新信息（使用最新可用数据）可在povertymeasurement.org找到。

Heterogeneous Effects Of Health Insurance On Birth Related Outcomes: Unpacking Compositional Vs. Direct Changes

健康保险对生育相关结果的异质性影响：分解构成变化与直接变化

Jie Ma, Kosali I. Simon

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

When women of childbearing age gain health insurance, we expect their birth outcomes to improve, but comparing births that occur before and after policy changes may confound two separate impacts of coverage. For one, health insurance could affect who gives birth, through reduced costs of contraception. Health insurance could also directly improve maternal and child health among those who give birth, through additional prenatal resources. We address this question using the Affordable Care Act young adult provision, comparing birth related outcomes for those aged 24-25 years after the law, to outcomes among older young adults. We show that since the law subsidized contraceptives mainly among higher socioeconomic groups, the composition of mothers shifted towards less advantaged groups. Accounting for this shift, we find evidence of direct improvements in prenatal care and pregnancy-related health (reduced gestational diabetes and hypertension).

中文摘要

当育龄女性获得健康保险时，我们预期她们的生育结果会改善，但比较政策变化前后发生的生育可能会混淆保险覆盖的两种不同影响。一方面，健康保险可能通过降低避孕成本来影响谁选择生育。另一方面，健康保险也可能通过增加产前资源，直接改善已生育女性的母婴健康。我们利用《平价医疗法案》的年轻成人条款来解决这个问题，比较该法案实施后24-25岁人群与较年长年轻成人的生育相关结果。我们表明，由于该法案主要补贴了较高社会经济群体的避孕措施，母亲构成转向了更弱势的群体。在考虑这一构成变化后，我们发现了产前护理和妊娠相关健康（妊娠期糖尿病和高血压减少）得到直接改善的证据。

Dynamic Trade-offs and Labor Supply Under the CARES Act

《CARES法案》下的动态权衡与劳动力供给

Corina Boar, Simon Mongey

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The CARES Act resulted in many unemployed workers receiving benefits that exceeded wages at their previous job. Given this, would an unemployed worker reject an offer to return to their former job at the same wage? Qualitatively, we provide a very simple dynamic model that incorporates four reasons the answer could be ‘no’: (i) the temporary nature of the CARES Act, (ii) uncertainty that their return-to-work offer might expire, (iii) search frictions, and (iv) wage losses out of unemployment in a recession. Quantitatively, when evaluated under empirically relevant parameters, we find it unlikely a worker would reject an offer to return to work at the same wage. We show special cases where this is not true and relate these to anecdotal evidence.

中文摘要

《CARES法案》导致许多失业工人获得的福利超过了他们之前工作的工资。鉴于此，失业工人是否会拒绝以相同工资重返原工作岗位的提议？从定性角度，我们提供了一个非常简单的动态模型，其中包含了答案可能是“否”的四个原因：（i）《CARES法案》的临时性，（ii）重返工作岗位的提议可能失效的不确定性，（iii）搜索摩擦，以及（iv）经济衰退期间失业导致的工资损失。从定量角度，当在经验相关参数下进行评估时，我们发现工人不太可能拒绝以相同工资重返工作岗位的提议。我们展示了这不成立的特殊情况，并将其与轶事证据联系起来。

Increasing Hours Worked: Moonlighting Responses to a Large Tax Reform

增加工作时间：对大规模税收改革的兼职反应

Alisa Tazhitdinova

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 德国

Abstract

Moonlighting is increasingly popular in OECD countries, with 5 to 10% of workers holding two or more jobs. However, little is known about the responsiveness of moonlighting to financial incentives due to the lack of identifying variation. This paper studies a unique reform in Germany that allowed workers to hold small secondary jobs tax-free, decreasing the marginal tax rate by between 19.5 to 66pp. I show that the reform resulted in a dramatic increase in moonlighting that was not offset by reductions in primary earnings, and that hours constraints is the key determinant of moonlighting.

中文摘要

在OECD国家，兼职现象日益普遍，5%至10%的工人拥有两份或更多工作。然而，由于缺乏识别性变化，关于兼职对财务激励的反应程度知之甚少。本文研究了德国一项独特的改革，该改革允许工人免税从事小型副业，将边际税率降低了19.5至66个百分点。我表明，这项改革导致了兼职活动的急剧增加，且并未被主要收入的减少所抵消，而工作时间限制是决定兼职的关键因素。

Gender and Culture

性别与文化

Paola Giuliano

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper reviews the literature on gender and culture. Gender gaps in various outcomes (competitiveness, labor force participation, and performance in mathematics, amongst many others) show remarkable differences across countries and tend to persist over time. The economics literature initially explained these differences by looking at standard economic variables such as the level of development, women’s education, the expansion of the service sector, and discrimination. More recent literature has argued that gender differences in a variety of outcomes could reflect underlying cultural values and beliefs. This article reviews the literature on the relevance of culture in the determination of different forms of gender gap. I examine how differences in historical situations could have been relevant in generating gender differences and the conditions under which gender norms tend to be stable or to change over time, emphasizing the role of social learning. Finally, I review the role of different forms of cultural transmission in shaping gender differences, distinguishing between channels of vertical transmission (the role of the family), horizontal transmission (the role of peers), and oblique transmission (the role of teachers or role models).

中文摘要

本文回顾了关于性别与文化的文献。各种结果（如竞争力、劳动力参与率和数学表现等）中的性别差距在不同国家之间表现出显著差异，并且往往随时间持续存在。经济学文献最初通过考察标准经济变量（如发展水平、女性教育、服务业的扩张和歧视）来解释这些差异。最近的文献则认为，各种结果中的性别差异可能反映了潜在的文化价值观和信念。本文回顾了关于文化在决定不同形式性别差距中相关性的文献。我考察了历史情境的差异如何可能在产生性别差异方面具有相关性，以及性别规范在何种条件下趋于稳定或随时间变化，强调了社会学习的作用。最后，我回顾了不同形式的文化传播在塑造性别差异中的作用，区分了垂直传播（家庭的作用）、水平传播（同伴的作用）和斜向传播（教师或榜样的作用）渠道。

On Socializing and Social Distancing in Markets: Implications for Retail Prices, Store-level Consumer Density, and Disease Transmission

论市场中的社交与社交距离：对零售价格、门店层面消费者密度和疾病传播的影响

Ricardo Lagos

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

I generalize the "noisy search" model of Burdett and Judd (1983) to settings where individual buyers have preferences over the number of other buyers who visit the same seller as them. I consider a version in which buyers have a preference for social distancing derived from the risk of contracting a disease from other buyers, and use it to study the two-way equilibrium interaction between supply-side considerations (such as the distribution of prices posted by sellers) and individual buyers' behavioral responses to the risk of contagion. I find that the price response to the buyers' shift toward social distancing can be an important determinant of the degree to which buyers' individual behavioral responses to the risk of contagion can mitigate the spread of the disease.

中文摘要

我将Burdett和Judd（1983）的“噪声搜索”模型推广到个体买家对访问同一卖家的其他买家数量有偏好的情境。我考虑了一个版本，其中买家因从其他买家那里感染疾病的风险而产生对社交距离的偏好，并利用它来研究供给侧因素（如卖家发布的价格分布）与个体买家对传染风险的行为反应之间的双向均衡互动。我发现，价格对买家转向社交距离行为的反应，可能是决定买家个体对传染风险的行为反应能在多大程度上缓解疾病传播的重要因素。

Data-intensive Innovation and the State: Evidence from AI Firms in China

数据密集型创新与国家：来自中国人工智能企业的证据

Martin Beraja, David Y. Yang, Noam Yuchtman

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济

Abstract

Developing AI technology requires data. In many domains, government data far exceeds in magnitude and scope data collected by the private sector, and AI firms often gain access to such data when providing services to the state. We argue that such access can stimulate commercial AI innovation in part because data and trained algorithms are shareable across government and commercial uses. We gather comprehensive information on firms and public security procurement contracts in China’s facial recognition AI industry. We quantify the data accessible through contracts by measuring public security agencies’ capacity to collect surveillance video. Using a triple-differences strategy, we find that data-rich contracts, compared to data-scarce ones, lead recipient firms to develop significantly and substantially more commercial AI software. Our analysis indicates a contribution of government data to the rise of China’s facial recognition AI firms, and suggests that states’ data collection and provision policies could shape AI innovation.

跨学科证据表明，对于轻中度抑郁和焦虑，谈话疗法比抗抑郁药更具治愈效果。然而，很少有患者使用它。我们开发了一个动态选择模型来分析患者对抑郁和焦虑治疗的需求。该模型纳入了众多可能阻碍治疗使用的因素，以及心理健康改善与收入之间的联系。估计模型显示，心理健康改善是有价值的，既直接通过效用，也间接通过收入。然而，患者对使用疗法的抵触情绪几乎不受合理的反事实政策（例如降低价格或消除其他成本）的影响。患者行为可能反映了污名化、对疗法有效性的信念偏差，或不愿与陌生人讨论个人或痛苦问题。更广泛地说，随机试验中估计的疗法益处只讲述了故事的一半。如果患者在实验环境之外不使用治疗——而我们又不理解原因或如何促使他们使用——那么估计的治疗效果就无法被利用。

Exit vs. Voice

退出 vs. 发声

Eleonora Broccardo, Oliver D. Hart, Luigi Zingales

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the relative effectiveness of exit (divestment and boycott) and voice (engagement) strategies in promoting socially desirable outcomes in companies. We show that in a competitive world exit is less effective than voice in pushing firms to act in a socially responsible manner. Furthermore, we demonstrate that individual incentives to join an exit strategy are not necessarily aligned with social incentives, whereas they are when well-diversified investors are allowed to express their voice. We discuss what social and legal considerations might sometimes make exit preferable to voice.

中文摘要

我们研究了退出（撤资和抵制）与发声（参与）策略在促进公司追求社会合意结果方面的相对有效性。我们表明，在竞争性世界中，退出在推动企业以社会责任方式行事方面不如发声有效。此外，我们证明，个人加入退出策略的激励不一定与社会激励一致，而当充分多元化的投资者被允许表达其发声时，两者则是一致的。我们讨论了哪些社会和法律考量有时可能使退出比发声更可取。

Permanent Income Shocks, Target Wealth, and the Wealth Gap

永久性收入冲击、目标财富与财富差距

Tullio Jappelli, Luigi Pistaferri

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 意大利

Abstract

We test the key implication of the buffer stock model, namely that any revision in permanent income leads to a proportionate revision in target wealth. We use panel data on the amount of wealth held for precautionary purposes available in the 2002-2016 SHIW. Using an instrumental variable approach to overcome measurement error issues and direct estimates of the permanent component of income, we find that households indeed revise approximately one-for-one their target wealth in response to permanent income shocks. We explore heterogeneity of the response across the cash-on-hand distribution, for positive and negative shocks, and for shocks of different size. We also find that the change in the ratio of cash-on-hand to permanent income is negatively correlated with the “wealth gap”, particularly for individuals whose wealth is substantially above target.

中文摘要

我们检验了缓冲库存模型的关键含义，即永久性收入的任何修正都会导致目标财富的成比例修正。我们使用了2002-2016年意大利家庭收入和财富调查（SHIW）中关于为预防目的而持有的财富金额的面板数据。采用工具变量方法克服测量误差问题，并直接估计收入的永久性成分，我们发现，家庭确实会根据永久性收入冲击大致一对一地修正其目标财富。我们探讨了这种反应在现金持有量分布、正向与负向冲击以及不同规模冲击下的异质性。我们还发现，现金持有量与永久性收入之比的变化与“财富差距”呈负相关，特别是对于那些财富大幅高于目标的个体。

No. 25论文详情NBER w27708

The Baker Hypothesis

贝克假说

Anusha Chari, Peter Blair Henry, Hector Reyes

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

In 1985, James A. Baker III's “Program for Sustained Growth” proposed a set of economic policy reforms including, inflation stabilization, trade liberalization, greater openness to foreign investment, and privatization, that he believed would lead to faster growth in countries then known as the Third World, but now categorized as emerging and developing economies (EMDEs). A country-specific, time-series assessment of the reform process reveals three clear facts. First, in the 10-year-period after stabilizing high inflation, the average growth rate of real GDP in EMDEs is 2.2 percentage points higher than in the prior ten-year period. Second, the corresponding growth increase for trade liberalization episodes is 2.66 percentage points. Third, in the decade after opening their capital markets to foreign equity investment, the spread between EMDEs average cost of equity capital and that of the US declines by 240 basis points. The impact of privatization is less straightforward to assess, but taken together, the three central facts of reform provide empirical support for the Baker Hypothesis and suggest a simple neoclassical interpretation of the unprecedented increase in growth that has taken place in EMDEs since 1995.

中文摘要

1985年，詹姆斯·A·贝克三世提出的“持续增长计划”建议了一套经济政策改革，包括稳定通胀、贸易自由化、对外国投资更加开放以及私有化。他认为这些改革将促进当时被称为第三世界、现在归类为新兴和发展中经济体（EMDEs）的国家实现更快增长。对改革进程进行的国别时间序列评估揭示了三个明确的事实。首先，在稳定高通胀后的十年内，EMDEs的实际GDP平均增长率比前十年高出2.2个百分点。其次，贸易自由化事件对应的增长提升为2.66个百分点。第三，在向外国股权投资开放资本市场后的十年内，EMDEs的平均股权资本成本与美国之间的利差下降了240个基点。私有化的影响较难评估，但综合来看，改革的这三个核心事实为贝克假说提供了实证支持，并暗示了自1995年以来EMDEs所经历的前所未有增长的一种简单新古典解释。
