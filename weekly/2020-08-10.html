The $100 Million Nudge: Increasing Tax Compliance of Businesses and the Self-Employed using a Natural Field Experiment

一亿美元的助推：通过自然实地实验提升企业和自雇人士的税收合规性

Justin E. Holz, John A. List, Alejandro Zentner, Marvin Cardoza, Joaquin Zentner

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 多米尼加、多明尼加

Abstract

This paper uses a natural field experiment to examine the effectiveness of specific nudges on tax compliance amongst firms and the self-employed in the Dominican Republic. In collaboration with the Dominican Republic’s tax authority, we designed messages for more than 28,000 self-employed workers and over 56,000 firms. Leveraging administrative tax data, we find evidence that our nudges (increasing the salience of prison sentences or public disclosure of tax evaders) have large effects on increasing tax compliance, primarily working through the channel of decreasing claimed tax exemptions. Interestingly, we find that firms are more impacted than the self-employed, and that firm size is critically linked to nudge effectiveness: larger firms are considerably more influenced by nudges than smaller firms. We find this latter result noteworthy given the paucity of evidence showing significant behavioral impacts of nudges amongst the largest players in a market. Overall, our messages increased tax revenue by $193 million (roughly 0.23% of the Dominican Republic’s GDP in 2018), with over $100 million constituting income that the government would not have received without our field experimental nudges.

本文利用自然实地实验，研究了特定助推措施对多米尼加共和国企业和自雇人士税收合规性的有效性。我们与多米尼加共和国税务机关合作，为超过28,000名自雇工人和56,000多家企业设计了信息提示。借助行政税收数据，我们发现这些助推措施（提高监禁刑罚或公开曝光逃税者的显著性）对提升税收合规性产生了显著效果，主要通过减少申报的税收豁免这一渠道发挥作用。有趣的是，我们发现企业比自雇人士受到的影响更大，并且企业规模与助推效果密切相关：大型企业比小型企业更显著地受到助推的影响。鉴于缺乏证据表明助推对市场中最大参与者有显著行为影响，我们认为后一个结果值得关注。总体而言，我们的信息提示增加了1.93亿美元的税收收入（约占2018年多米尼加共和国GDP的0.23%），其中超过1亿美元是如果没有我们的实地实验助推，政府本不会收到的收入。

Valuing Long-Term Property Rights with Anticipated Political Regime Shifts

在预期政治体制转变下评估长期产权价值

Zhiguo He, Maggie Rong Hu, Zhenping Wang, Vincent Yao

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 香港

Abstract

We identify exposure to political risk by exploiting a unique variation around land lease extension protection after 2047 in Hong Kong's housing market due to historical arrangements under the “One Country, Two Systems” design. Relative to properties that have been promised an extension protection, those with legally unprotected leases granted by the current government are sold at a substantial discount of around 8%, and those with colonial leases suffer an additional discount of about 8%. Incorporating estimated structural parameters that suggest an additional 20% ground rent after 2047, our model matches these empirical discounts well across long-term lease horizons. We find that the discount increases as time approaches 2047 and is higher in areas where residents feel more pessimistic about the city's future.

Reconciling Trends in U.S. Male Earnings Volatility: Results from a Four Data Set Project

调和美国男性收入波动趋势：来自四个数据集项目的结果

Robert A. Moffitt

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

There is a large literature on earnings and income volatility in labor economics, household finance, and macroeconomics. One strand of that literature has studied whether individual earnings volatility has risen or fallen in the U.S. over the last several decades. There are strong disagreements in the empirical literature on this important question, with some studies showing upward trends, some downward trends, and some flat trends. Some studies have suggested that the differences are the result of using flawed survey data instead of more accurate administrative data. This paper provides an overview of a project attempting to reconcile these findings with four different data sets and six different data series--three survey and three administrative data series, including two which match survey respondent data to their administrative data. Using common specifications, measures of volatility, and other treatments of the data, the papers show almost uniformly a lack of any significant long-term trend in male earnings volatility over the last 30 years. Moreover, the survey and the administrative data almost entirely agree on that long-term stability when the comparison is done properly. Several possible explanations for the differing finds in past work are suggested by the papers. The stability of earnings volatility raises many questions for future research on trends in the U.S. labor market.

中文摘要

在劳动经济学、家庭金融和宏观经济学中，关于收入和收入波动性的文献众多。其中一支文献研究了美国过去几十年中个人收入波动性是上升还是下降。在这个重要问题上，实证文献存在严重分歧，一些研究显示上升趋势，一些显示下降趋势，还有一些显示平稳趋势。一些研究认为，这些差异源于使用有缺陷的调查数据而非更准确的行政数据。本文概述了一个项目，试图用四个不同的数据集和六个不同的数据序列（三个调查数据序列和三个行政数据序列，包括两个将调查受访者数据与其行政数据匹配的序列）来调和这些发现。使用共同的规格、波动性度量和其他数据处理方式，这些论文几乎一致显示，在过去30年中，男性收入波动性缺乏任何显著的长期趋势。此外，当进行适当比较时，调查数据和行政数据在长期稳定性上几乎完全一致。这些论文提出了过去研究中不同发现的几种可能解释。收入波动性的稳定性为未来关于美国劳动力市场趋势的研究提出了许多问题。

The Missing Inflation Puzzle: The Role of the Wage-Price Pass-Through

缺失的通胀之谜：工资-价格传导的作用

Sebastian Heise, Fatih Karahan, Ayşegül Şahin

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Price inflation in the U.S. has been sluggish and slow to pick up in the last two decades. We show that this missing inflation can be traced to a growing disconnect between unemployment and core goods inflation. We exploit rich industry-level data to show that weakening pass-through from wages to prices in the goods-producing sector is an important source of the slow inflation pick-up in the last two decades. We set up a theoretical framework where markups and pass-through are a function of firms' market shares and show that increased import competition and rising market concentration reduce pass-through from wages to prices. We then use industry-level data and find strong support for these two channels consistent with the implications of our model.

中文摘要

美国的价格通胀在过去二十年中一直低迷且难以回升。我们表明，这种缺失的通胀可以追溯到失业与核心商品通胀之间日益脱节的关系。我们利用丰富的行业层面数据来证明，商品生产部门中工资向价格传导的减弱是过去二十年通胀缓慢回升的重要来源。我们建立了一个理论框架，其中加价和传导是企业市场份额的函数，并表明进口竞争加剧和市场集中度上升会降低工资向价格的传导。然后，我们使用行业层面数据，发现了支持这两个渠道的有力证据，这与我们模型的含义一致。

Informational Barriers to Market Access: Experimental Evidence from Liberian Firms

市场准入的信息障碍：来自利比里亚企业的实验证据

Jonas Hjort, Golvine de Rochambeau, Vinayak Iyer, Fei Ao

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 利比里亚

Abstract

Evidence suggests that many firms in lower-income countries stagnate because they cannot access growth-conducive markets. We hypothesize that overlooked informational barriers distort market access, excluding productive but “information-poor” suppliers. To investigate, we gave a random subset of medium-sized Liberian firms vouchers for a week-long program targeting equal-opportunity access to the input purchases of government, companies, and other organizations—a market that makes up upwards of 80 percent of global GDP. The program exclusively teaches “sellership”: how to navigate large buyers’ complex, formal sourcing procedures. Firms that participate win three times as many formal contracts a year later. The impact is heterogeneous: informational sales barriers bind for about a quarter of Liberian firms. Three years post-training, these firms continue to win desirable contracts, are more likely to operate, and employ more workers. We use a simple model of managers’ time-constraints to illustrate a possible explanation for why informational market access barriers can persist and generate poverty-trap-like dynamics among firms, even absent credit constraints. Our results help rationalize common demand-side policies in public procurement that nonetheless appear to scratch at the surface of a bigger distortion.

中文摘要

有证据表明，许多低收入国家的企业因无法进入促进增长的市场而停滞不前。我们假设，被忽视的信息障碍扭曲了市场准入，排除了有生产力但“信息贫乏”的供应商。为了调查这一点，我们向随机抽取的利比里亚中型企业提供了为期一周的培训券，该培训旨在促进平等机会地进入政府、公司和其他组织的采购市场——这个市场占全球GDP的80%以上。该培训专门教授“销售技巧”：如何应对大型买家复杂、正式的采购程序。参与的企业一年后赢得的正式合同数量是原来的三倍。影响是异质性的：信息销售障碍制约了约四分之一的利比里亚企业。培训三年后，这些企业继续赢得理想的合同，更有可能持续运营，并雇佣更多工人。我们使用一个简单的管理者时间约束模型，来说明为什么信息市场准入障碍可能持续存在并在企业中产生类似贫困陷阱的动态，即使没有信贷约束。我们的结果有助于解释公共采购中常见的需求侧政策，这些政策似乎只是触及了更大扭曲的表面。

The Effect of Mentoring on School Attendance and Academic Outcomes: A Randomized Evaluation of the Check & Connect Program

辅导对学生出勤和学业成果的影响：Check & Connect项目的随机评估

Jonathan Guryan, Sandra Christenson, Ashley Cureton, Ijun Lai, Jens Ludwig, Catherine Schwarz, Emma Shirey, Mary Clair Turner

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In response to budget problems, many urban school systems reduced resources for getting students to come to school, like truancy officers. Chicago, for instance, went from 150 truancy officers down to, in 1991, a total of zero. Is that a good idea? We explore here the effects of increased support by a pro-social adult, or “social capital,” delivered through a structured student monitoring and mentoring program called Check & Connect (C&C). We carried out a large-scale randomized controlled trial with C&C in partnership with the Chicago Public Schools (CPS) to students in grades 1-8. Program participation decreased absences in grades 5-7 by 4.2 days, or 22.9 percent, but with no detectable effects on students in grades 1-4. We also did not find statistically significant effects on learning outcomes such as test scores or GPA, or any detectable spillovers to other students within the schools where the program was administered. The modest impacts per dollar spent, compared to previous evidence on either low-cost "nudges" or relatively intensive, higher-cost interventions, raise the possibility that, for very disadvantaged students, there may be decreasing but then increasing returns to program intensity for the problem of student disengagement.

中文摘要

为了应对预算问题，许多城市学校系统减少了用于让学生上学的资源，例如逃学官员。例如，芝加哥从150名逃学官员减少到1991年的零。这是个好主意吗？我们在此探讨通过一个名为Check & Connect（C&C）的结构化学生监测和辅导项目，由亲社会成年人提供的增强支持（或“社会资本”）的效果。我们与芝加哥公立学校（CPS）合作，对1-8年级的学生进行了一项大规模的C&C随机对照试验。项目参与使5-7年级学生的缺勤减少了4.2天，即22.9%，但对1-4年级学生没有检测到显著影响。我们也没有发现对学习成绩（如考试成绩或GPA）有统计上显著的影响，或者对项目所在学校内其他学生有任何可检测的溢出效应。与先前关于低成本“助推”或相对密集、高成本干预的证据相比，每美元支出的影响适中，这提出了一个可能性：对于非常弱势的学生，针对学生脱离问题，项目强度可能存在递减但随后递增的回报。

This Time It's Different: The Role of Women's Employment in a Pandemic Recession

这次不同：女性就业在大流行衰退中的作用

Titan Alon, Matthias Doepke, Jane Olmstead-Rumsey, Michèle Tertilt

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

In recent US recessions, employment losses have been much larger for men than for women. Yet, in the current recession caused by the Covid-19 pandemic, the opposite is true: unemployment is higher among women. In this paper, we analyze the causes and consequences of this phenomenon. We argue that women have experienced sharp employment losses both because their employment is concentrated in heavily affected sectors such as restaurants, and due to increased childcare needs caused by school and daycare closures, preventing many women from working. We analyze the repercussions of this trend using a quantitative macroeconomic model featuring heterogeneity in gender, marital status, childcare needs, and human capital. Our quantitative analysis suggests that a pandemic recession will i) feature a strong transmission from employment to aggregate demand due to diminished within-household insurance; ii) result in a widening of the gender wage gap throughout the recovery; and iii) contribute to a weakening of the gender norms that currently produce a lopsided distribution of the division of labor in home work and childcare.

金融研究中增长最快的领域之一是对管理者偏见及其对公司结果影响的研究。自2000年代中期以来，这一行为公司金融分支提供了关于偏见在公司领域影响的理论和实证证据，例如过度自信、经验效应和沉没成本谬误。该领域一直是瓦解传统经济机制——选择、学习和市场纪律——足以维持理性管理者范式这一论点的主导力量。相反，证据表明，行为力量在首席执行官（CEO）职业生涯的每个阶段都发挥着显著影响。首先，在任命阶段，选择并不会阻碍有行为偏见的经理人的晋升。相反，竞争环境常常促进他们的晋升，即使在价值最大化的选择机制下也是如此。其次，在执掌公司期间，学习机会有限，因为许多管理决策发生频率低，其因果效应被自我归因偏差所模糊，并且难以与同时发生的事件的影响区分开来。第三，在解雇阶段，市场纪律并不能确保有偏见的决策者被解雇，因为董事会成员自身在评估CEO时也会受到偏见的影响。 通过记录偏见如何影响甚至是最有学识和最有影响力的决策者（如CEO），该领域对偏见的“硬接线”产生了重要见解。偏见不仅仅源于缺乏教育，也不仅限于低能力的代理人。相反，偏见是组织最高层人类决策的重要组成部分。 未来研究的一个重要问题是如何在CEO职业生涯的每个阶段限制管理偏见的负面影响。潜在的方法包括完善选择机制、设计和实施公司修复措施，以及重塑公司治理，不仅要考虑激励错位，还要考虑有偏见的决策。

Abstract

Central banks around the world are exploring and in some cases even piloting Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs). CBDCs promise to realize a broad range of new capabilities, including direct government disbursements to citizens, frictionless consumer payment and money-transfer systems, and a range of new financial instruments and monetary policy levers.
