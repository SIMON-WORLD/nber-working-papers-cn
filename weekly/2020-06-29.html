Political Power, Elite Control, and Long-Run Development: Evidence from Brazil

政治权力、精英控制与长期发展：来自巴西的证据

Claudio Ferraz, Frederico Finan, Monica Martinez-Bravo

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 巴西

Abstract

This paper analyzes how changes in the concentration of political power affect long-run development. We study Brazil’s military dictatorship whose rise to power dramatically altered the distribution of power of local political elites. We document that municipalities that were more politically concentrated prior to the dictatorship in the 1960s are relatively richer in 2000, despite being poorer initially. Our evidence suggests that this reversal of fortune was the result of the military’s policies aimed at undermining the power of traditional elites. These policies increased political competition locally, which ultimately led to better governance, more public goods, and higher income levels.

中文摘要

本文分析了政治权力集中度的变化如何影响长期发展。我们研究了巴西的军事独裁政权，该政权的崛起极大地改变了地方政治精英的权力分配。我们记录到，在1960年代独裁统治之前政治集中度更高的市镇，尽管最初较为贫困，但在2000年却相对更富裕。我们的证据表明，这种命运逆转是军方旨在削弱传统精英权力的政策的结果。这些政策增加了地方政治竞争，最终带来了更好的治理、更多的公共品和更高的收入水平。

Income-driven Labor Market Polarization

收入驱动的劳动力市场极化

Diego A. Comin, Ana Danieli, Martí Mestieri

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We propose a mechanism for labor-market polarization based on the nonhomotheticity of demand that we call the income-driven channel. Our mechanism builds on a novel empirical fact: expenditure elasticities and production intensities in low- and high-skill occupations are positively correlated across sectors. Thus, as income grows, demand shifts towards expenditure-elastic sectors, and the relative demand for low- and high-skill occupations increases, causing labor-market polarization. A calibrated general-equilibrium model suggests this mechanism accounts for 90% and 35% of the increase in the wage-bill share of low- and high-skill occupations observed in the US during 1980-2016, and for 64% and 28% of the rise in the employment shares of low- and high-skill occupations. This mechanism is similarly important for the polarization of labor markets in Western Europe during 1980-2016, as well as in the US during earlier decades and, possibly, the near future.

中文摘要

我们提出了一种基于需求非位似性的劳动力市场极化机制，称之为收入驱动渠道。该机制建立在一个新颖的实证事实之上：跨部门来看，低技能和高技能职业的支出弹性与生产密集度呈正相关。因此，随着收入增长，需求转向支出弹性高的部门，低技能和高技能职业的相对需求增加，导致劳动力市场极化。一个校准的一般均衡模型表明，这一机制解释了1980-2016年间美国低技能和高技能职业工资份额增长的90%和35%，以及就业份额增长的64%和28%。该机制对于1980-2016年西欧劳动力市场的极化，以及美国更早时期乃至可能近期的极化同样重要。

The Performance of Hedge Fund Performance Fees

对冲基金业绩报酬的表现

Itzhak Ben-David, Justin Birru, Andrea Rossi

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the long-run outcomes associated with hedge funds' compensation structure. Over a 22-year period, the aggregate effective incentive fee rate is 2.5 times the average contractual rate (i.e., around 50% instead of 20%). Overall, investors collected 36 cents for every dollar earned on their invested capital (over a risk-free hurdle rate and before adjusting for any risk). In the cross-section of funds, there is a substantial disconnect between lifetime performance and incentive fees earned. These poor outcomes stem from the asymmetry of the performance contract, investors' return-chasing behavior, and underwater fund closures.

中文摘要

我们研究了对冲基金薪酬结构相关的长期结果。在22年的时间里，总有效激励费率是平均合同费率的2.5倍（即约50%而非20%）。总体而言，投资者每从投资资本中赚取一美元（超过无风险门槛利率且未进行任何风险调整），仅能收回36美分。在基金的横截面上，终身业绩与赚取的激励费之间存在显著脱节。这些糟糕的结果源于业绩合同的不对称性、投资者的追逐回报行为以及水下基金的关闭。

Towards a Dynamic Disequilibrium Theory with Randomness

迈向带有随机性的动态非均衡理论

Martin M. Guzman, Joseph E. Stiglitz

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Most macroeconomic crises, such as the 2008 Global Financial Crisis, are associated with endogenous large changes in beliefs and understandings about the workings of the economy. Such downturns and crises are not consistent with the standard paradigm of a well-functioning competitive economy, and macroeconomic equilibrium models based on that paradigm have failed to predict the possibility of those downturns, to explain them, or even to design appropriate policy responses. The framework assumes there are no macroeconomic inconsistencies—all plans are realized, all budget constraints honored. \nIn this paper, we present a dynamic disequilibrium theory with randomness that is based on the premise that a better way to understand deep downturns is to think of the economy experiencing a constant evolution, marked by uncertainty, in which there is continual learning about the economic system. Our framework explains why macroeconomic inconsistencies may arise and investigates their consequences. We explain why decentralized market forces may be disequilibrating. We identify the crucial departures from the Arrow-Debreu assumptions and those underlying DSGE models, emphasizing the limitations in the assumption of equilibrium and the absence of a coherent theory of how it is attained, the incompleteness of markets and the non-stationarity of the stochastic processes describing the economy. We analyze the policy implications of this alternative theory, which typically differ markedly from those of the standard model: In particular, the consequences for the effectiveness of different monetary and fiscal policies, and the eventual need of debt restructuring policies to restore macroeconomic consistency.

中文摘要

大多数宏观经济危机，例如2008年全球金融危机，都与关于经济运作方式的信念和理解的內生性巨大变化相关。这种衰退和危机与运行良好的竞争性经济的标准范式不一致，基于该范式的宏观经济均衡模型未能预测这些衰退的可能性、解释它们，甚至未能设计出适当的政策应对。该框架假设不存在宏观经济不一致性——所有计划都得以实现，所有预算约束都得到遵守。在本文中，我们提出了一个带有随机性的动态非均衡理论，其前提是理解深度衰退的更好方式是将经济视为经历着以不确定性为标志的持续演化，其中存在对经济体系的持续学习。我们的框架解释了宏观经济不一致性为何可能出现，并研究了其后果。我们解释了为何分散化的市场力量可能具有非均衡性。我们识别了与阿罗-德布鲁假设以及DSGE模型基础假设的关键偏离，强调了均衡假设的局限性以及缺乏关于如何达到均衡的连贯理论、市场的不完全性以及描述经济的随机过程的非平稳性。我们分析了这一替代理论的政策含义，这些含义通常与标准模型显著不同：特别是对不同货币和财政政策有效性的影响，以及最终需要债务重组政策来恢复宏观经济一致性。

Personal Wealth and Self-Employment

个人财富与自雇就业

Aymeric Bellon, J. Anthony Cookson, Erik P. Gilje, Rawley Z. Heimer

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We examine how wealth windfalls affect self-employment decisions using data on cash payments from claims on Texas shale drilling to people throughout the United States. Individuals who receive large wealth shocks (greater than $50,000) have 51% higher self-employment rates. The increase in self-employment rates is driven by individuals who lengthen existing self-employment spells, and not by individuals who leave regular employment for self-employment. Moreover, the effect of wealth reverts for individuals whose payments run out. Rather than alleviating a financial constraint, our evidence suggests that unrestricted cash windfalls affect self-employment decisions primarily through self-employment’s non-pecuniary benefits.

中文摘要

我们利用来自德克萨斯州页岩钻探索赔的现金支付数据（覆盖全美各地个人），研究了财富意外收益如何影响自雇就业决策。获得大额财富冲击（超过5万美元）的个人，其自雇就业率高出51%。自雇就业率的上升是由延长现有自雇就业期的个人推动的，而非由离开正规就业转向自雇就业的个人推动。此外，对于支付到期的个人，财富效应会逆转。我们的证据表明，无限制的现金意外收益并非通过缓解财务约束，而主要是通过自雇就业的非金钱收益来影响自雇就业决策。

Pandemic Shocks and Fiscal-Monetary Policies in the Eurozone: COVID-19 Dominance During January - June 2020

欧元区的疫情冲击与财政货币政策：2020年1月至6月的COVID-19主导地位

Yothin Jinjarak, Rashad Ahmed, Sameer Nair-Desai, Weining Xin, Joshua Aizenman

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We compare the importance of market factors against that of COVID-19 dynamics and policy responses in explaining Eurozone sovereign spreads. First, we estimate a multifactor model for changes in credit default swap (CDS) spreads over January 2014 - June 2019. Then, we apply a synthetic control-type procedure to extrapolate model-implied changes in the CDS. The factor model does very well over the rest of 2019 but breaks down during the pandemic, especially during March 2020 when there is a large divergence between the actual and model-implied CDS changes. We find that the March 2020 divergence is well accounted for by COVID-specific risks and associated policies, mortality outcomes, and policy announcements, rather than traditional determinants. Daily CDS widening ceased almost immediately after the ECB announced the PEPP, but the divergence between actual and model-implied changes persisted. This points to COVID-19 Dominance: widening spreads during the pandemic has led to unconventional monetary policies that primarily aim to mitigate short-run fears, temporarily pushing away concerns over fiscal risk.

中文摘要

我们比较了市场因素与COVID-19动态及政策应对在解释欧元区主权利差方面的重要性。首先，我们估计了2014年1月至2019年6月期间信用违约互换（CDS）利差变化的多因子模型。然后，我们应用一种合成控制类型的程序来外推模型隐含的CDS变化。该因子模型在2019年剩余时间内表现良好，但在疫情期间崩溃，尤其是在2020年3月，实际CDS变化与模型隐含变化之间出现巨大分歧。我们发现，2020年3月的分歧很好地被COVID-19特定风险及相关政策、死亡率结果和政策公告所解释，而非传统决定因素。在欧洲央行宣布PEPP后，每日CDS利差扩大几乎立即停止，但实际变化与模型隐含变化之间的分歧持续存在。这指向了COVID-19主导地位：疫情期间利差扩大导致了非常规货币政策，这些政策主要旨在缓解短期恐慌，暂时推开了对财政风险的担忧。

Why Retirement, Social Security, and Age Discrimination Policies Need to Consider the Intersectional Experiences of Older Women

为何退休、社会保障和年龄歧视政策需要考虑老年女性的交叉性经历

Ian Burn, Patrick Button, Theodore F. Figinski, Joanne Song McLaughlin

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We provide an overview of research that indicates that older women face unique challenges and opportunities with respect to work, retirement, Social Security, and age discrimination law. We present estimates of poverty by age and sex, showing that poverty increases with age for women due to older women often outliving their spouses and becoming widowed. We discuss research that shows that women benefit more than men from working longer. We then note that older women face intersectional discrimination that can unfortunately be a barrier to older women working longer. We detail how older women often “fall between the cracks” of the Age Discrimination in Employment Act and Title VII of the Civil Rights Act and are thus not well protected against this intersectional discrimination. As a final example of how women face different circumstances, we summarize research on how older women were differentially negatively impacted by the elimination of Social Security’s Retirement Earnings.

中文摘要

我们概述了表明老年女性在工作、退休、社会保障和年龄歧视法律方面面临独特挑战和机遇的研究。我们按年龄和性别呈现了贫困率估计，显示由于老年女性通常比配偶活得更久并成为寡妇，女性的贫困率随年龄增长而上升。我们讨论了表明女性比男性更能从延长工作中受益的研究。然后我们指出，老年女性面临交叉性歧视，这不幸地可能成为她们延长工作的障碍。我们详细说明了老年女性如何常常“落入”《就业年龄歧视法》和《民权法案》第七章的“缝隙”中，因此未能得到针对这种交叉性歧视的良好保护。作为女性面临不同情况的最后一个例子，我们总结了关于老年女性如何因取消社会保障的退休收入测试而受到不同负面影响的的研究。

Social Norms as a Barrier to Women's Employment in Developing Countries

社会规范作为发展中国家女性就业的障碍

Seema Jayachandran

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This article discusses cultural barriers to women's participation and success in the labor market in developing countries. I begin by describing how gender norms influence the relationship between economic development and female employment, as well as how gender norms vary substantially across societies at the same level of economic development. I then examine several specific gender-related social norms and how they constrain women's employment. Abstract

Treatment effect estimates in regression discontinuity (RD) designs are often sensitive to the choice of bandwidth and polynomial order, the two important ingredients of widely used local regression methods. While Imbens and Kalyanaraman (2012) and Calonico, Cattaneo and Titiunik (2014) provide guidance on bandwidth, the sensitivity to polynomial order still poses a conundrum to RD practitioners. It is understood in the econometric literature that applying the argument of bias reduction does not help resolve this conundrum, since it would always lead to preferring higher orders. We therefore extend the frameworks of Imbens and Kalyanaraman (2012) and Calonico, Cattaneo and Titiunik (2014) and use the asymptotic mean squared error of the local regression RD estimator as the criterion to guide polynomial order selection. We show in Monte Carlo simulations that the proposed order selection procedure performs well, particularly in large sample sizes typically found in empirical RD applications. This procedure extends easily to fuzzy regression discontinuity and regression kink designs.

Abstract

The threat of COVID-19 has increased the health risks of going to an office or factory, leading more workers to do their jobs remotely. In this paper, we provide results from firm surveys on both small and large businesses on the prevalence and productivity of remote work, and expectations about the persistence of remote work once the COVID-19 crisis ends. We present four main findings. First, while overall levels of remote work are high, there is considerable variation across industries. Abstract

At the aggregate level, the evidence that deviations from purchasing power parity (PPP) are too persistent to be explained solely by nominal rigidities has long been a puzzle (Rogoff, 1996). Another puzzle from the micro price evidence of the law of one price (LOP), which is the basic building block of PPP, is that LOP deviations are less persistent than PPP deviations. To reconcile the empirical evidence, we adapt the model of behavioral inattention in Gabaix (2014, 2020) to a simple two-country sticky-price model. Second, the LOP deviations decrease to less than two-thirds of the PPP deviations in the degree of persistence.
