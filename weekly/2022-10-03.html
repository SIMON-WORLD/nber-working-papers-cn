Automation and Polarization

自动化与极化

Daron Acemoglu, Jonas Loebbing

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop an assignment model of automation. Each of a continuum of tasks of variable complexity is assigned to either capital or one of a continuum of labor skills. We characterize conditions for interior automation, whereby tasks of intermediate complexity are assigned to capital. Interior automation arises when the most skilled workers have a comparative advantage in the most complex tasks relative to capital, and because the wages of the least skilled workers are sufficiently low relative to their productivity and the effective cost of capital in low-complexity tasks. Minimum wages and other sources of higher wages at the bottom make interior automation less likely. Starting with interior automation, a reduction in the cost of capital (or an increase in capital productivity) causes employment and wage polarization. Specifically, further automation pushes workers into tasks at the lower and upper ends of the task distribution. It also monotonically increases the skill premium above a skill threshold and reduces the skill premium below this threshold. Moreover, automation tends to reduce the real wage of workers with comparative advantage profiles close to that of capital. We show that large enough increases in capital productivity ultimately induce a transition to low-skill automation and qualitatively alter the effects of automation - thereafter inducing monotone increases in skill premia rather than wage polarization.

中文摘要

我们构建了一个自动化的任务分配模型。每个具有不同复杂度的连续任务被分配给资本或一系列连续劳动技能中的一种。我们刻画了内部自动化的条件，即中等复杂度的任务被分配给资本。当技能最高的工人相对于资本在最复杂任务中具有比较优势，并且技能最低的工人的工资相对于其生产率以及在低复杂度任务中的有效资本成本足够低时，内部自动化就会出现。最低工资和底部其他导致更高工资的来源使得内部自动化不太可能发生。从内部自动化开始，资本成本的降低（或资本生产率的提高）会导致就业和工资极化。具体来说，进一步的自动化将工人推向任务分布的低端和高端。它还会单调地提高高于某个技能门槛的技能溢价，并降低低于该门槛的技能溢价。此外，自动化往往会降低那些比较优势特征与资本相近的工人的实际工资。我们表明，足够大的资本生产率增长最终会引发向低技能自动化的转变，并从根本上改变自动化的影响——此后会导致技能溢价的单调增长，而非工资极化。

How to Run Surveys: A Guide to Creating Your Own Identifying Variation and Revealing the Invisible

如何开展调查：创建自身识别变异与揭示隐性因素的指南

Stefanie Stantcheva

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Surveys are an essential approach for eliciting otherwise invisible factors such as perceptions, knowledge and beliefs, attitudes, and reasoning. These factors are critical determinants of social, economic, and political outcomes. Surveys are not merely a research tool. They are also not only a way of collecting data. Instead, they involve creating the process that will generate the data. This allows the researcher to create their own identifying and controlled variation. Thanks to the rise of mobile technologies and platforms, surveys offer valuable opportunities to study either broadly representative samples or focus on specific groups. This paper offers guidance on the complete survey process, from the design of the questions and experiments to the recruitment of respondents and the collection of data to the analysis of survey responses. It covers issues related to the sampling process, selection and attrition, attention and carelessness, survey question design and measurement, response biases, and survey experiments.

中文摘要

调查是揭示感知、知识和信念、态度以及推理等隐性因素的基本方法。这些因素是决定社会、经济和政治结果的关键因素。调查不仅仅是一种研究工具，也不仅仅是收集数据的一种方式。相反，它们涉及创建将生成数据的过程。这使得研究者能够创建自身的识别变异和受控变异。得益于移动技术和平台的兴起，调查为研究广泛代表性样本或关注特定群体提供了宝贵的机会。本文提供了关于完整调查过程的指导，从问题和实验的设计，到受访者的招募和数据的收集，再到调查响应的分析。它涵盖了与抽样过程、选择和损耗、注意力和粗心、调查问题设计和测量、响应偏差以及调查实验相关的问题。

Work From Home and the Office Real Estate Apocalypse

远程办公与写字楼房地产末日

Arpit Gupta, Vrinda Mittal, Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

We study the impact of remote work on the commercial office sector. We document large shifts in lease revenues, office occupancy, lease renewal rates, lease durations, and market rents as firms shifted to remote work in the wake of the Covid-19 pandemic. We show that the pandemic has had large effects on both current and expected future cash flows for office buildings. Remote work also changes the risk premium on office real estate. We revalue the stock of New York City commercial office buildings taking into account pandemic-induced cash flow and discount rate effects. We find a 45% decline in office values in 2020 and 39% in the longer-run, the latter representing a $453 billion value destruction. Higher quality office buildings were somewhat buffered against these trends due to a flight to quality, while lower quality office buildings see much more dramatic swings. These valuation changes have repercussions for local public finances and financial sector stability.

中文摘要

我们研究了远程工作对商业办公领域的影响。我们记录了在新冠疫情后企业转向远程工作时，租赁收入、写字楼入住率、续租率、租赁期限和市场租金的巨大变化。我们表明，疫情对写字楼当前的现金流和预期的未来现金流都产生了巨大影响。远程工作还改变了写字楼房地产的风险溢价。我们重新评估了纽约市商业写字楼存量，考虑了疫情引发的现金流和贴现率效应。我们发现，2020年写字楼价值下降了45%，长期下降39%，后者代表着4530亿美元的价值毁灭。由于向优质资产的转移，较高质量的写字楼在一定程度上缓冲了这些趋势，而较低质量的写字楼则经历了更为剧烈的波动。这些估值变化对地方公共财政和金融部门稳定产生了影响。

Horses for Courses: Measuring Foreign Supply Chain Exposure

各取所需：衡量外国供应链暴露度

Richard Baldwin, Rebecca Freeman, Angelos Theodorakopoulos

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Perceptions of global supply chains (GSCs) have shifted in recent years from a positive to a more cautious view. Standard GSC measures have mostly not adapted to this change as they focus on participation in, rather than exposure to, foreign supply chains. This paper presents the tools necessary to track foreign GSC exposure, and introduces a systematic shocks approach to GSC indicator design. We use this to develop indicators that gauge the impact of a variety of foreign shocks. We argue that different indicators are appropriate to different questions and show that they can provide qualitatively different answers to the same foreign exposure question.

中文摘要

近年来，对全球供应链的看法已从积极转向更为谨慎。标准的全球供应链衡量指标大多未能适应这一变化，因为它们关注的是参与外国供应链，而非暴露于外国供应链。本文提出了追踪外国供应链暴露度所需的工具，并介绍了一种系统性的冲击方法来设计全球供应链指标。我们利用这一点来开发衡量各种外国冲击影响的指标。我们认为，不同的指标适用于不同的问题，并表明它们可能对相同的外国暴露问题给出性质不同的答案。

Global Trends in Income Inequality and Income Dynamics: New Insights from GRID

全球收入不平等与收入动态趋势：来自GRID的新见解

Fatih Guvenen, Luigi Pistaferri, Giovanni L. Violante

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 全球/多国

Abstract

The Global Repository of Income Dynamics (GRID) is a new open-access, cross- country database that contains a wide range of micro statistics on income inequality, dynamics, and mobility. It has four key characteristics: it is built on micro panel data drawn from administrative records; it fully exploits the longitudinal dimension of the underlying datasets; it offers granular descriptions of income inequality and income dynamics for finely defined subpopulations; and it is designed from the ground up with the goals of harmonization and cross-country comparability. This paper introduces the database and presents a set of global trends in income inequality and income dynamics across the 13 countries that are currently in GRID. Our results are based on the statistics created for GRID by the 13 country teams who also contributed to this special issue with individual articles.

中文摘要

全球收入动态数据库是一个新的开放获取、跨国数据库，包含关于收入不平等、动态和流动性的广泛微观统计数据。它有四个关键特征：它建立在来自行政记录的微观面板数据之上；它充分利用了基础数据集的纵向维度；它为精细定义的子群体提供了收入不平等和收入动态的详细描述；并且它从一开始就以协调和跨国可比性为目标进行设计。本文介绍了该数据库，并展示了目前GRID中13个国家在收入不平等和收入动态方面的一系列全球趋势。我们的结果基于13个国家团队为GRID创建的统计数据，这些团队也以各自文章的形式为本特刊做出了贡献。

Are Immigrants more Left leaning than Natives?

移民是否比本地人更左倾？

Simone Moriconi, Giovanni Peri, Riccardo Turati

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We analyze whether second-generation immigrants have different political preferences relative to observationally identical children of citizens in the host countries. Using data on individual voting behavior in 22 European countries between 2001 and 2017, we characterize each vote on a left-right scale based on the ideological and policy positions of the party receiving the vote. In the first part of the paper, we characterize the size of the "left-wing bias" in the vote of second-generation immigrants after controlling for a large set of individual characteristics and origin and destination country fixed effects. We find a significant left-wing bias of second-generation immigrants, comparable in magnitude to the left-wing bias associated with living in urban (rather than rural) areas. We then show that this left-wing bias is associated with stronger preferences for inequality-reducing government intervention, internationalism and multiculturalism. We do not find that second-generation immigrants are biased towards or away from populist political agendas.

中文摘要

我们分析了第二代移民是否与东道国中观察特征相同的公民子女具有不同的政治偏好。利用2001年至2017年间22个欧洲国家的个人投票行为数据，我们根据接收投票的政党的意识形态和政策立场，将每次投票置于左-右光谱上。在论文的第一部分，我们在控制了大量个人特征以及来源国和目的地国固定效应后，刻画了第二代移民投票中“左翼偏向”的大小。我们发现第二代移民存在显著的左翼偏向，其幅度与居住在城市（而非农村）地区相关的左翼偏向相当。然后我们表明，这种左翼偏向与对减少不平等的政府干预、国际主义和文化多元主义的更强偏好相关。我们没有发现第二代移民倾向于或远离民粹主义政治议程。

War, Influenza, and U.S. Carbon Intensity.

战争、流感与美国碳强度

Nicholas Z. Muller

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 西班牙、美国

Abstract

Carbon intensity from fossil fuel use in the United States economy peaked in 1917. World War I ended, and the Spanish Flu pandemic broke out one year later in 1918. This paper contends that these events, coupled with associated turmoil in the domestic coal industry, were largely responsible for the turning point in carbon intensity. It is instructive to consider that geopolitics, labor markets, and public health at the time of peak carbon intensity bear relevance to the global economy from 2019 to the present. Interventions in markets intended to mitigate detrimental consequences of pandemic and war may induce ancillary impacts for long-run climate change and environmental quality.

中文摘要

美国经济中化石燃料使用的碳强度在1917年达到峰值。第一次世界大战结束，西班牙流感大流行于一年后的1918年爆发。本文认为，这些事件，加上国内煤炭行业的相关动荡，在很大程度上是碳强度出现转折点的原因。值得思考的是，碳强度达到峰值时的地缘政治、劳动力市场和公共卫生状况，与2019年至今的全球经济具有相关性。旨在减轻疫情和战争不利后果的市场干预措施，可能会对长期气候变化和环境质量产生附带影响。

Conflicts of Interest in Banks: Evidence from Proprietary Trading

Rainer Haselmann, Christian Leuz, Sebastian Schreiber

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 德国

Abstract

We analyze the conflict of interest that arises when universal banks engage in proprietary trading of borrower stocks, which has been a prominent concern in the regulatory debates for a long time. We combine trade-by-trade supervisory data with credit registry information in Germany. Our findings reveal that lending relationships inform banks' prop trading. To separate bank expertise and private information from lending as explanations, we study prop trading around corporate events. We show that banks execute purchases (sales) in borrower stocks in the weeks before positive (negative) news events, even when these events are unscheduled and surprising to the market. We link this trading pattern to situations when banks possess private borrower information, and rule out that it is explained by specialized expertise. We also find evidence consistent with information flows through banks' centralized risk management, that banks alter trading patterns once they acquire private information, consistent with shrouding of informed trading, and that OTC counter-parties price-protect against relationship banks. Our evidence highlights the difficulty of avoiding conflicts in universal banking.

Assessing School District Decision-Making: Evidence from the COVID-19 Pandemic

评估学区决策：来自COVID-19大流行的证据

Alvin Christian, Brian Jacob, John D. Singleton

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

The COVID-19 pandemic drew new attention to the role of school boards in the U.S. In this paper, we examine school districts’ choices of learning modality—whether and when to offer in-person, virtual, or hybrid instruction—over the course of the 2020-21 pandemic school year. The analysis takes advantage of granular weekly data on learning mode and COVID-19 cases for Ohio school districts. We show that districts respond on the margin to health risks: all else equal, a marginal increase in new cases reduces the probability that a district offers in-person instruction the next week. Moreover, this negative response is magnified when the district was in-person the prior week and attenuates in magnitude over the school year. These findings are consistent with districts learning from experience about the effect of in-person learning on disease transmission in schools. We also find evidence that districts are influenced by the decisions of their peers.

中文摘要

COVID-19大流行重新引起了人们对美国学校董事会角色的关注。在本文中，我们研究了学区在2020-21学年期间对教学模式的选择——即是否以及何时提供面对面、虚拟或混合教学。该分析利用了俄亥俄州学区关于教学模式和COVID-19病例的详细周度数据。我们发现，学区会在边际上对健康风险做出反应：在其他条件相同的情况下，新增病例的边际增加会降低学区下周提供面对面教学的概率。此外，当学区前一周处于面对面教学状态时，这种负面反应会加剧，并且其幅度在整个学年中逐渐减弱。这些发现与学区从经验中学习面对面教学对学校内疾病传播影响的情况一致。我们还发现，学区会受到同行决策的影响。

A Tale of Tier 3 Cities

三线城市的故事

Kenneth S. Rogoff, Yuanchen Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价

Abstract

This paper provides new estimates of the housing stock, construction rates and price developments by city tier in China in order to understand where excess supply might be concentrated, and the implications of any significant contraction. We also update estimates of the size of China’s rapidly evolving real estate sector through 2021, allowing one to look at the initial impact of COVID-19, as well as extending the analysis to incorporate urban-expansion related infrastructure construction. We argue that China overall faces imbalances between supply and demand for housing stock, but the problem is significantly deeper in the generally smaller and lower income tier 3 cities, which nevertheless account for more than 60% of both China’s GDP and its housing stock.

中文摘要

本文提供了按城市层级划分的中国住房存量、建设率和价格发展的新估计，以了解过剩供应可能集中在何处，以及任何显著收缩的影响。我们还更新了截至2021年中国快速发展的房地产行业规模的估计，从而可以考察COVID-19的初步影响，并将分析扩展到包括与城市扩张相关的基础设施建设。我们认为，中国整体上面临住房存量供需失衡的问题，但在通常规模较小且收入较低的三线城市，问题更为严重，然而这些城市占中国GDP和住房存量的比例均超过60%。

No. 11论文详情NBER w30518

Neoclassical Growth with Limited Commitment

有限承诺下的新古典增长

Dirk Krueger, Harald Uhlig

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We characterize the stationary equilibrium of a continuous-time neoclassical production economy with capital accumulation in which agents can insure against idiosyncratic income risk by trading state-contingent assets, subject to limited commitment constraints that rule out short-selling. For an N-state Poisson labor productivity process we provide the household consumption-asset allocation, stationary asset distribution and aggregate capital supply. When production is Cobb-Douglas, when productivity takes two values, of which one is zero, and when agents have log-utility, we calculate the equilibrium interest rate, capital stock and consumption distribution in closed form, including its comparative statics change with respect to idiosyncratic income risk.

This paper describes the challenges of globalization in terms of the logic underpinning four distinct policy constraints or “trilemmas” and their interrelationship; in particular the disturbances that arise from capital flows and the difficulties of adjusting monetary policies to a global monetary environment. These trilemmas intersect and interlock. The trilemmas are: 1. The traditional Macroeconomic trilemma between capital mobility, fixed exchange rates and monetary autonomy; 2. The International relations trilemma between capital mobility, sovereignty and international order; 3. The Political economy trilemma between capital mobility, democracy and sovereignty; 4. The Financial stability trilemma between capital mobility, financial stability and independent national policies. The four trilemmas offer a way to analyze how domestic monetary, financial, economic and political systems are interconnected within the international system that opens up vulnerabilities. They can be described as the impossible policy choices at the heart of globalization.

中文摘要

本文根据支撑四种不同政策约束或“三难困境”的逻辑及其相互关系，描述了全球化的挑战；特别是资本流动引起的扰动以及调整货币政策以适应全球货币环境的困难。这些三难困境相互交叉和锁定。这些三难困境是：1. 传统的宏观经济三难困境：资本流动性、固定汇率和货币自主权之间；2. 国际关系三难困境：资本流动性、主权和国际秩序之间；3. 政治经济三难困境：资本流动性、民主和主权之间；4. 金融稳定三难困境：资本流动性、金融稳定和独立国家政策之间。这四个三难困境提供了一种分析国内货币、金融、经济和政治体系如何在国际体系内相互关联的方式，这种关联打开了脆弱性。它们可以被描述为全球化核心中不可能的政策选择。

Firming up Price Inflation

夯实价格通胀

Philip Bunn, Lena S. Anayi, Nicholas Bloom, Paul Mizen, Gregory Thwaites, Ivan Yotzov

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 英国

Abstract

We use data from a large panel survey of UK firms to analyze the economic drivers of price setting since the start of the Covid pandemic. Inflation responded asymmetrically to movements in demand. This helps to explain why inflation did not fall much during the negative initial pandemic demand shock. Energy prices and shortages of labor and materials account for most of the rise during the rebound. Inflation rates across firms have become more dispersed and skewed since the start of the pandemic. We find that average price inflation is positively correlated with the dispersion and skewness of the distribution. Finally, we also introduce a novel measure of subjective inflation uncertainty within firms and show how this has increased during the pandemic, continuing to rise in 2022 even as sales uncertainty dropped back.

中文摘要

我们利用英国企业大型面板调查的数据，分析了自新冠疫情开始以来价格设定的经济驱动因素。通胀对需求变动的反应是不对称的。这有助于解释为什么在疫情初期负面的需求冲击期间通胀没有大幅下降。能源价格以及劳动力和材料短缺解释了反弹期间通胀上升的大部分原因。自疫情开始以来，企业间的通胀率变得更加分散和偏斜。我们发现平均价格通胀与分布的离散度和偏度呈正相关。最后，我们还引入了一种衡量企业内部主观通胀不确定性的新方法，并展示了这种不确定性在疫情期间如何增加，即使在2022年销售不确定性回落时仍继续上升。
