No. 1论文详情NBER w29338

Bayesian Learning

贝叶斯学习

Isaac Baley, Laura Veldkamp

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We survey work using Bayesian learning in macroeconomics, highlighting common themes and new directions. First, we present many of the common types of learning problems agents face---signal extraction problems---and trace out their effects on macro aggregates, in different strategic settings. Then we review different perspectives on how agents get their information. Models differ in their motives for information acquisition and the cost of information, or learning technology. Finally, we survey the growing literature on the data economy, where economic activity generates data and the information in data feeds back to affect economic activity.

中文摘要

我们综述了宏观经济学中使用贝叶斯学习的研究，强调了共同主题和新方向。首先，我们介绍了代理人面临的许多常见学习问题类型——信号提取问题——并追溯了它们在不同战略环境中对宏观总量的影响。然后，我们回顾了关于代理人如何获取信息的不同观点。模型在信息获取动机、信息成本或学习技术方面存在差异。最后，我们综述了关于数据经济日益增长的文献，其中经济活动产生数据，而数据中的信息反馈回来影响经济活动。

Decrypting New Age International Capital Flows

解密新时代国际资本流动

Clemens Graf von Luckner, Carmen M. Reinhart, Kenneth S. Rogoff

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper employs high frequency transactions data on the world’s oldest and most extensive centralized peer-to-peer Bitcoin market, which enables trade in the currencies of more than 135 countries. We develop an algorithm that allows, with high probability, the detection of “crypto vehicle transactions” in which crypto currency is used to move capital across borders or facilitate domestic transactions. In contrast to previous work which has used “on-chain” data, our approach enables one to investigate parts of the vastly larger pool of “off-chain” transactions. We find that, as a conservative lower bound, over 7 percent of the 45 million trades on the exchange we explore represent crypto vehicle transactions in which Bitcoin is used to make payment in fiat currency. Roughly 20 percent of these represent international capital flight/flows/remittances. Although our work cannot be used to put a price on cryptocurrencies, it provides the first systematic quantitative evidence that the transactional use of cryptocurrencies constitutes a fundamental component of their value, at least under the current regulatory regime.

中文摘要

本文利用世界上最古老、最广泛的中心化点对点比特币市场的高频交易数据，该市场支持超过135个国家的货币交易。我们开发了一种算法，能够以高概率检测“加密车辆交易”，即使用加密货币跨境转移资本或促进国内交易。与之前使用“链上”数据的研究不同，我们的方法能够调查规模大得多的“链下”交易池的一部分。我们发现，作为保守的下限，在我们研究的交易所的4500万笔交易中，超过7%是加密车辆交易，其中比特币被用于以法定货币进行支付。其中大约20%代表国际资本外逃/流动/汇款。尽管我们的工作不能用于为加密货币定价，但它提供了第一个系统的定量证据，表明加密货币的交易用途构成了其价值的基本组成部分，至少在当前的监管制度下是如此。

Exposure, Experience, and Expertise: Why Personal Histories Matter in Economics

暴露、经历与专长：为何个人经历在经济学中重要

Ulrike Malmendier

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Personal experiences of economic outcomes, from global financial crises to individual-level job losses, can shape individual beliefs, risk attitudes, and choices for years to come. A growing literature on experience effects shows that individuals act as if past outcomes that they experienced were overly likely to occur again, even if they are fully informed about the actual likelihood. This reaction to past experiences is long-lasting though it decays over time as individuals accumulate new experiences. Modern brain science helps understand these processes. Evidence on neuroplasticity reveals that personal experiences and learning alter the strength of neural connections and fine-tune the brain structure to those past experiences ("use-dependent brain"). I show that experience effects help understand belief formation and decision-making in a wide area of economic applications, including inflation, home purchases, mortgage choices, and consumption expenditures. I argue that experience-based learning is broadly applicable to economic decision-making and discuss topics for future research in education, health, race, and gender economics.

中文摘要

个人对经济结果的经历，从全球金融危机到个人层面的失业，可能会在多年后塑造个人的信念、风险态度和选择。关于经历效应的日益增长的文献表明，即使个人完全了解实际可能性，他们也会表现得好像过去经历过的结果极有可能再次发生。这种对过去经历的反应是持久的，尽管随着个人积累新经历而随时间衰减。现代脑科学有助于理解这些过程。关于神经可塑性的证据表明，个人经历和学习会改变神经连接的强度，并使大脑结构适应这些过去的经历（“使用依赖性大脑”）。我表明，经历效应有助于理解广泛经济应用中的信念形成和决策，包括通货膨胀、购房、抵押贷款选择和消费支出。我认为基于经历的学习广泛适用于经济决策，并讨论了教育、健康、种族和性别经济学领域的未来研究主题。

Tracing Value Added in the Presence of Foreign Direct Investment

存在外国直接投资情况下的增加值追踪

Zhi Wang, Shang-Jin Wei, Xinding Yu, Kunfu Zhu

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We develop a unified framework to trace value added along global supply chains in the presence of foreign direct investment by decomposing either GDP based on forward linkages or final production based on backward linkages. The new framework accounts for the presence of foreign invested enterprises (FIEs), their interactions with local firms in the host countries as well as their activities in international trade. The size of the GVC activities identified with this framework roughly doubles that in the previous literature that treats FIEs the same as local firms. The “missing GVC activities” are more serious for high-tech sectors than for those with a lower R&D intensity, and more serious for high-income economies than for middle-income economies.

中文摘要

我们开发了一个统一框架，用于在存在外国直接投资的情况下追踪全球供应链中的增加值，该框架通过基于前向关联分解国内生产总值或基于后向关联分解最终生产来实现。新框架考虑了外资企业的存在、它们与东道国当地企业的互动以及它们在国际贸易中的活动。使用该框架识别的全球价值链活动规模大约是先前将外资企业与当地企业同等对待的文献中的两倍。对于高研发强度的高科技行业，这种“缺失的全球价值链活动”比低研发强度行业更为严重，对于高收入经济体比中等收入经济体更为严重。

Minimum Wages and Teenage Childbearing: New Estimates Using a Dynamic Difference-in-Differences Approach

最低工资与青少年生育：基于动态双重差分法的新估计

Daniel I. Rees, Joseph J. Sabia, Rebecca Margolit

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The minimum wage is increasingly viewed as an important tool for improving public health outcomes, including reducing childbearing among teenagers. Taken at face value, recently reported estimates suggest that raising the federal minimum wage to $15 per hour could reduce the number of teenage births by 35,000 per year. Using an event study framework that accounts for dynamic and heterogeneous treatment effects, we find little evidence that minimum wages are causally related to teenage childbearing. Moreover, the estimated effects of minimum wages on teenage sexual behaviors, including contraception use, abstinence, and number of partners are consistently small and statistically insignificant.

中文摘要

最低工资正日益被视为改善公共卫生结果的重要工具，包括减少青少年生育。从表面上看，近期报告的估计表明，将联邦最低工资提高到每小时15美元，每年可减少3.5万例青少年生育。我们采用一个考虑动态和异质性处理效应的事件研究框架，发现几乎没有证据表明最低工资与青少年生育存在因果关系。此外，最低工资对青少年性行为（包括避孕使用、禁欲和伴侣数量）的估计效应始终很小且统计上不显著。

Creative Destruction, Distance to Frontier, and Economic Development

创造性破坏、与前沿的距离与经济发展

Michael Peters, Fabrizio Zilibotti

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 印度、美国

Abstract

We construct a model of creative destruction with endogenous firm dynamics. We integrate the theory into a general equilibrium multi-country model of technological convergence where countries interact via international spillovers. We derive implications for both firm dynamics and aggregate productivity dynamics. In richer economies, firms are on average larger and the best firms grow larger over time. In poorer economies, there is little creative destruction, low selection, and firms remain small. We estimate the parameters of the model using firm-level data for India and the United States. We study the effect of counterfactual policy reforms. Industrial policy that selectively targets the more productive firms can be beneficial in poor countries while being harmful in countries close to the economic frontier. The findings echo Acemoglu et al. (2006).

中文摘要

我们构建了一个具有内生企业动态的创造性破坏模型。我们将该理论整合到一个技术收敛的一般均衡多国模型中，各国通过国际溢出效应相互影响。我们推导出对企业动态和总体生产率动态的影响。在较富裕的经济体中，企业平均规模更大，且最佳企业随时间推移增长更大。在较贫穷的经济体中，创造性破坏很少，选择效应低，企业保持小规模。我们利用印度和美国的企业层面数据估计了模型参数。我们研究了反事实政策改革的效果。针对生产率较高企业的选择性产业政策在贫穷国家可能有益，而在接近经济前沿的国家则可能有害。这一发现与Acemoglu等人（2006）的研究相呼应。

The US Pretrial System: Balancing Individual Rights and Public Interests

美国审前制度：平衡个人权利与公共利益

Will S. Dobbie, Crystal Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 西班牙、美国

Abstract

In this article, we review a growing empirical literature on the effectiveness and fairness of the US pretrial system and discuss its policy implications. Despite the importance of this stage of the criminal legal process, researchers have only recently begun to explore how the pretrial system balances individual rights and public interests. We describe the empirical challenges that have prevented progress in this area and how recent work has made use of new data sources and quasi-experimental approaches to credibly estimate both the individual harms (such as loss of employment or government assistance) and public benefits (such as preventing non-appearance at court and new crimes) of cash bail and pretrial detention. These new data and approaches show that the current pretrial system imposes substantial short- and long-term economic harms on detained defendants in terms of lost earnings and government assistance, while providing little in the way of decreased criminal activity for the public interest. Non-appearances at court do significantly decrease for detained defendants, but the magnitudes cannot justify the economic harms to individuals observed in the data. A second set of studies shows that these costs of cash bail and pretrial detention are disproportionately borne by Black and Hispanic individuals, giving rise to large and unfair racial differences in cash bail and detention that cannot be explained by underlying differences in pretrial misconduct risk. We then turn to policy implications and describe areas of future work that would enable a deeper understanding of what drives these undesirable outcomes.

中文摘要

在本文中，我们回顾了关于美国审前制度有效性和公平性的日益增长的实证文献，并讨论了其政策含义。尽管这一刑事法律程序阶段至关重要，但研究人员直到最近才开始探索审前制度如何平衡个人权利与公共利益。我们描述了阻碍该领域进展的实证挑战，以及近期研究如何利用新数据源和准实验方法，可靠地估计现金保释和审前拘留对个人的损害（如失业或政府援助损失）和公共效益（如防止不出庭和新的犯罪）。这些新数据和方法表明，当前的审前制度在收入损失和政府援助方面对被拘留被告造成了显著的短期和长期经济损害，而为了公共利益减少犯罪活动方面的效果甚微。被拘留被告的不出庭率确实显著降低，但其幅度无法证明数据中观察到的个人经济损害是合理的。第二组研究表明，现金保释和审前拘留的这些成本不成比例地由黑人和西班牙裔个人承担，导致现金保释和拘留中存在巨大且不公平的种族差异，这无法用潜在的审前不当行为风险差异来解释。然后，我们转向政策含义，并描述了未来工作的领域，这些工作将有助于更深入地理解导致这些不良结果的原因。

Charitable Giving Responses to Education Budgets

对教育预算的慈善捐赠反应

Jonathan Meer, Hedieh Tajali

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Do changes in government spending affect voluntary contributions to those recipients? We examine how changes in K-12 education budgets impact donations to teachers using data from DonorsChoose.org, an online crowdfunding platform for public school teachers to raise money. We find a positive correlation between budgets and voluntary contributions when not accounting for their endogeneous relationship. With instrumental variables, we find evidence for crowd-out of private giving, though the magnitudes are small relative to spending and do not meaningfully offset budget changes. These results are driven entirely by teachers’ posting of requests.

中文摘要

政府支出的变化是否会影响对这些受助者的自愿捐款？我们利用DonorsChoose.org（一个为公立学校教师筹集资金的在线众筹平台）的数据，研究了K-12教育预算变化如何影响对教师的捐赠。我们发现，在不考虑其内生关系时，预算与自愿捐款之间存在正相关。使用工具变量，我们找到了私人捐赠被挤出的证据，尽管其规模相对于支出而言较小，且并未显著抵消预算变化。这些结果完全由教师发布请求所驱动。

No. 9论文详情NBER w29330

Supply Chain Resilience: Should Policy Promote Diversification or Reshoring?

供应链韧性：政策应促进多元化还是回流？

Gene M. Grossman, Elhanan Helpman, Hugo Lhuillier

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Supply chain disruptions, which have become commonplace, are often associated with globalization and trade. Little is known about optimal policy in the face of insecure supply chains. Should governments promote resilience by subsidizing backup sources of input supply? Should they encourage firms to source from closer and presumably safer domestic suppliers? We address these questions in a very simple model of production with a single critical input and with exogenous risks of relationship-specific and country-wide supply disturbances. We follow Matsuyama and Ushchev (2020) in positing a class of preferences that are homothetic with a single aggregator and that obey Marshall's Second Law of Demand. The familiar case of CES preferences is a member of the class, but it imposes restrictions that are important for policy conclusions.We find that, in the CES case, a subsidy for diversification achieves the constrained social optimum and dominates a policy that promotes reshoring or offshoring. When the demand elasticity rises with price, two policy instruments generally are needed to achieve efficient supply chains, private investments in resilience may be socially excessive, and policy that alter incentives to invest at home versus abroad may achieve greater welfare than ones that encourage or discourage diversification.

中文摘要

供应链中断已变得司空见惯，且通常与全球化和贸易相关。关于面对不安全的供应链时最优政策应如何，我们知之甚少。政府是否应通过补贴投入供应的备用来源来促进韧性？是否应鼓励企业从更近且可能更安全的国内供应商采购？我们在一个非常简单的生产模型中探讨这些问题，该模型包含单一关键投入以及关系特定和国家范围供应中断的外生风险。我们遵循Matsuyama和Ushchev（2020）的方法，假设一类具有单一加总器且服从马歇尔第二需求定律的位似偏好。熟悉的CES偏好是该类的一个成员，但它施加了对政策结论至关重要的限制。我们发现，在CES情形下，多元化补贴实现了受约束的社会最优，并且优于促进回流或离岸外包的政策。当需求弹性随价格上升时，通常需要两种政策工具来实现高效供应链，私人对韧性的投资可能社会过度，而改变国内外投资激励的政策可能比鼓励或抑制多元化的政策带来更高的福利。

No. 10论文详情NBER w29329

Market Size and Spatial Growth - Evidence from Germany’s Post-War Population Expulsions

市场规模与空间增长——来自德国战后人口驱逐的证据

Michael Peters

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 德国

Abstract

Virtually all theories of economic growth predict a positive relationship between population size and productivity. In this paper I study a particular historical episode to provide direct evidence for the empirical relevance of such scale effects. In the aftermath of the Second World War about 8m ethnic Germans were expelled from their domiciles in Eastern Europe and transferred to West Germany. This inflow increased the German population by almost 20%. Using variation across counties I show that the settlement of refugees had a large and persistent effect on the size of the local population, manufacturing employment and income per capita. I show that these findings are quantitatively consistent with an idea-based model of spatial growth if population mobility is subject to frictions and productivity spillovers occur locally. The model implies that the refugee settlement increased aggregate income per capita by about 12% after 25 years and that the historical settlement rule triggered persistent industrialization of rural areas.
