Crimes Against Morality: Unintended Consequences of Criminalizing Sex Work

违反道德罪：将性工作刑事化的意外后果

Lisa Cameron, Jennifer Seager, Manisha Shah

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 印度、印度尼西亚

Abstract

We examine the impact of criminalizing sex work, exploiting an event in which local officials unexpectedly criminalized sex work in one district in East Java, Indonesia, but not in neighboring districts. We collect data from female sex workers and their clients before and after the change. We find that criminalization increases sexually transmitted infections among female sex workers by 58 percent, measured by biological tests. This is driven by decreased condom access and use. We also find evidence that criminalization decreases earnings among women who left sex work due to criminalization, and decreases their ability to meet their children's school expenses while increasing the likelihood that children begin working to supplement household income. While criminalization has the potential to improve population STI outcomes if the market shrinks permanently, we show that five years post-criminalization the market has rebounded and the probability of STI transmission within the general population is likely to have increased.

中文摘要

我们考察了将性工作刑事化的影响，利用印度尼西亚东爪哇省一个地区的地方官员意外将性工作刑事化而邻近地区没有的事件。我们在政策变化前后收集了女性性工作者及其客户的数据。我们发现，刑事化使女性性工作者的性传播感染（通过生物学检测衡量）增加了58%。这是由于安全套获取和使用减少所致。我们还发现证据表明，刑事化降低了因刑事化而离开性工作的女性的收入，降低了她们支付子女教育费用的能力，同时增加了子女开始工作以补充家庭收入的可能性。虽然如果市场永久萎缩，刑事化有可能改善人群的性传播感染结果，但我们表明，在刑事化五年后，市场已经反弹，并且性传播感染在一般人群中传播的可能性可能已经增加。

Non-Random Exposure to Exogenous Shocks: Theory and Applications

对外生冲击的非随机暴露：理论与应用

Kirill Borusyak, Peter Hull

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 美国

Abstract

We develop new tools for estimating the causal effects of treatments or instruments that combine multiple sources of variation according to a known formula. Examples include treatments capturing spillovers in social and transportation networks, simulated instruments for policy eligibility, and shift-share instruments. We show how exogenous shocks to some, but not all, determinants of such variables can be leveraged while avoiding omitted variables bias. Our solution involves specifying counterfactual shocks that may as well have been realized and adjusting for a summary measure of non-randomness in shock exposure: the average treatment (or instrument) across such counterfactuals. We further show how to use shock counterfactuals for valid finite-sample inference, and characterize the valid instruments that are asymptotically efficient. We apply this framework to address bias when estimating employment effects of market access growth from Chinese high-speed rail construction, and to boost power when estimating coverage effects of expanded Medicaid eligibility.

中文摘要

我们开发了新的工具，用于估计那些根据已知公式组合多个变异来源的处理或工具的因果效应。例子包括捕捉社会和交通网络中溢出效应的处理、政策资格模拟工具以及份额转移工具。我们展示了如何利用此类变量的某些（而非全部）决定因素的外生冲击，同时避免遗漏变量偏差。我们的解决方案涉及指定可能已经实现的反事实冲击，并针对冲击暴露非随机性的一个汇总度量进行调整：这些反事实下的平均处理（或工具）。我们进一步展示了如何使用反事实冲击进行有效的有限样本推断，并刻画了渐近有效的工具。我们将此框架应用于估计中国高铁建设带来的市场准入增长的就业效应时的偏差校正，以及提高扩大医疗补助资格覆盖效应估计的统计功效。

Uncertainty, Long-Run, and Monetary Policy Risks in a Two-Country Macro Model

两国宏观模型中的不确定性、长期风险与货币政策风险

Kimberly A. Berg, Nelson C. Mark

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 日本、美国

Abstract

We study international currency risk in a two-country dynamic stochastic general equilibrium model under incomplete markets. The underlying sources of risk are direct shocks to productivity growth, shocks to a long-run risk component of productivity growth, shocks to a stochastic volatility component of productivity growth, and shocks to monetary policy. The long-run risk and stochastic volatility shocks have the interpretation of aggregate demand shocks. Cross-country heterogeneity in the model arises from three sources: differences in the long-run risk and stochastic volatility process parameters that we estimate using United States and Japanese total factor productivity data, differences in monetary policy parameters, and differences in export pricing. The driving force behind currency risk is heterogeneity in precautionary saving. Differences in monetary policy can generate moderate currency risk, but structural differences in productivity growth are more important. Export pricing conventions are not important sources of currency risk. Stochastic volatility shocks are key to generating volatility in the currency risk premium, but they do not help at all in explaining the forward premium bias/anomaly.

中文摘要

我们在不完全市场条件下，研究了两国动态随机一般均衡模型中的国际货币风险。风险的潜在来源包括：对生产率增长的直接冲击、对生产率增长长期风险成分的冲击、对生产率增长随机波动成分的冲击，以及对货币政策的冲击。长期风险冲击和随机波动冲击可被解释为总需求冲击。模型中的跨国异质性源于三个方面：我们利用美国和日本的全要素生产率数据估计的长期风险与随机波动过程参数的差异、货币政策参数的差异，以及出口定价的差异。货币风险的驱动力是预防性储蓄的异质性。货币政策差异可以产生适度的货币风险，但生产率增长的结构性差异更为重要。出口定价惯例并非货币风险的重要来源。随机波动冲击对于产生货币风险溢价的波动性至关重要，但它们完全无助于解释远期溢价偏差/异常现象。

Man vs. Machine Learning: The Term Structure of Earnings Expectations and Conditional Biases

人类与机器学习：盈利预期的期限结构与条件性偏差

Jules H. van Binsbergen, Xiao Han, Alejandro Lopez-Lira

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We introduce a real-time measure of conditional biases in firms' earnings forecasts. The measure is defined as the difference between analysts' expectations and a statistically optimal unbiased machine-learning benchmark. Analysts' conditional expectations are, on average, biased upwards, and the bias increases in the forecast horizon. These biases are associated with negative cross-sectional return predictability, and the short legs of many anomalies contain firms with excessively optimistic earnings. Further, managers of companies with the greatest upward-biased earnings forecasts are more likely to issue stocks. Commonly-used linear earnings models do not work out-of-sample and are inferior to those provided by analysts.

中文摘要

我们引入了一种实时衡量企业盈利预测条件性偏差的指标。该指标定义为分析师预期与统计上最优的无偏机器学习基准之间的差异。分析师的预期平均而言存在向上偏差，且偏差随预测期限增加而增大。这些偏差与负面的横截面收益可预测性相关，许多异常现象的短端都包含盈利预期过度乐观的公司。此外，盈利预测向上偏差最大的公司管理者更有可能发行股票。常用的线性盈利模型在样本外表现不佳，且劣于分析师提供的模型。

Choice in Insurance Markets: A Pigouvian Approach to Social Insurance Design

保险市场中的选择：社会保险设计的庇古方法

Nathaniel Hendren, Camille Landais, Johannes Spinnewijn

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Should choice be offered in social insurance programs? The paper presents a conceptual framework that identifies the key forces determining the value of offering choice, reviews some existing evidence on these forces, and aims to guide further empirical research in different in- surance domains. The value of offering choice is higher the larger the variation in individual valuations, but gets reduced by both selection on risk and selection on moral hazard. The imple- mentation of choice-based policies is further challenged by the presence of adverse selection and choice frictions or the obligation to offer basic uncompensated care. These inefficiencies can be seen as externalities, which do not rationalize the absence of providing choice per se, but point to the need for regulatory policies and the potential value of corrective pricing à la Pigou.

中文摘要

社会保险项目中是否应提供选择？本文提出了一个概念框架，识别决定提供选择价值的关键力量，回顾了关于这些力量的一些现有证据，并旨在指导不同保险领域的进一步实证研究。提供选择的价值越大，个体估值差异越大，但会因风险选择（逆向选择）和道德风险选择而降低。基于选择的政策的实施还面临逆向选择和选择摩擦的存在，或提供基本无偿护理义务的挑战。这些低效率可被视为外部性，它们本身并不证明不提供选择的合理性，而是指出了监管政策的必要性以及庇古式矫正定价的潜在价值。

Pandemics Through the Lens of Occupations

通过职业视角看大流行病

Anand Chopra, Michael B. Devereux, Amartya Lahiri

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 哥伦比亚、英国

Abstract

We outline a macro-pandemic model where individuals can select into working from home or in the market. Market work increases the risk of infection. Occupations differ in the ease of substitution between market and home work, and in the risk of infection. We examine the evo- lution of a pandemic in the model as well as its macroeconomic and distributional consequences. The model is calibrated to British Columbian data to examine the implications of shutting down different industries by linking industries to occupations. We find that endogenous choice to self- isolate is key: it reduces the peak infection rate by 2 percentage points but reduces the trough consumption level by 4 percentage points, even without policy mandated lockdowns. The model also produces widening consumption inequality, a fact that has characterized COVID-19.

中文摘要

我们勾勒了一个宏观大流行病模型，其中个体可以选择在家工作或在市场工作。市场工作会增加感染风险。不同职业在家庭工作与市场工作之间的替代难易程度以及感染风险上存在差异。我们研究了模型中大流行病的演变及其宏观经济和分配后果。该模型根据不列颠哥伦比亚省的数据进行校准，通过将行业与职业联系起来，考察关闭不同行业的影响。我们发现，即使没有政策强制封锁，内生选择自我隔离也是关键：它将感染峰值率降低了2个百分点，但将消费低谷水平降低了4个百分点。该模型还产生了消费不平等加剧的现象，这是COVID-19的一个特征。

Globalization and Pandemics

全球化与大流行病

Pol Antràs, Stephen J. Redding, Esteban Rossi-Hansberg

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We propose a theory of the relationship between globalization and pandemics. We start by documenting the importance of international trade for the diffusion of infections in several pandemics throughout history and by showing that trade and travel are closely intertwined. Motivated by this evidence, we build a framework in which business travel facilitates trade according to a constant elasticity gravity equation mediated by mobility frictions. In turn, travel leads to human interactions that transmit disease, as in the Susceptible-Infected-Recovered (SIR) model. We highlight three novel interactions between these two mechanisms. First, trade-motivated travel generates an epidemiological externality across countries. Therefore, reductions in international frictions affect the evolution of the epidemic in each country, and the condition for a pandemic to occur. Second, if infections lead to deaths, or reduce individual labor supply, we establish a general equilibrium social distancing effect, whereby increases in relative prices in unhealthy countries reduce travel to those countries. Third, if agents internalize the threat of infection, we show that their behavioral responses lead to a reduction in travel that is larger for higher-trade-cost locations, and hence leads to an initial fall in the ratio of trade to GDP in the early stages of the epidemic, before a subsequent recovery.

中文摘要

我们提出了一个关于全球化与大流行病之间关系的理论。我们首先通过历史数据记录了国际贸易在多次大流行病中对于感染扩散的重要性，并表明贸易与旅行紧密相连。受此证据启发，我们构建了一个框架，其中商务旅行根据由流动性摩擦调节的恒定弹性引力方程促进贸易。反过来，旅行导致人际互动，从而传播疾病，如同易感-感染-康复（SIR）模型所述。我们强调了这两种机制之间的三种新颖互动。第一，贸易驱动的旅行产生了跨国流行病学外部性。因此，国际摩擦的减少会影响每个国家流行病的演变以及大流行病发生的条件。第二，如果感染导致死亡或减少个体劳动力供给，我们建立了一个一般均衡的社会距离效应，即不健康国家相对价格的上升会减少前往这些国家的旅行。第三，如果个体内化感染威胁，我们表明他们的行为反应会导致旅行减少，且对于贸易成本较高的地点，减少幅度更大，因此在流行病早期阶段导致贸易与GDP之比最初下降，随后才恢复。

Measuring Property Rights Institutions

衡量产权制度

Simeon Djankov, Edward L. Glaeser, Valeria Perotti, Andrei Shleifer

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 全球/多国

Abstract

How do the different elements in the standard bundle of property rights – such as the right of possession or the right of transfer – differentially impact outcomes, such as urban development? This paper incorporates insecure property rights into a standard model of urban land prices and density, and makes predictions about investment in land and property, informality, and the efficiency of land use. Our empirical analysis links data on institutions related to land titling and transfer with multiple urban outcomes, from 190 countries. The evidence is generally consistent with the model’s predictions, and more broadly with the Demsetz’s (1967) approach to property rights institutions. Indeed, we document world-wide improvements in the quality of institutions facilitating property transfer over time.

中文摘要

标准产权束中的不同要素——例如占有权或转让权——如何对城市发展等结果产生差异化影响？本文将不安全的产权纳入城市土地价格和密度的标准模型，并对土地和财产投资、非正规性以及土地利用效率做出预测。我们的实证分析将190个国家与土地确权和转让相关的制度数据与多个城市结果联系起来。证据总体上与模型的预测一致，并且更广泛地与德姆塞茨（1967）关于产权制度的方法论相符。事实上，我们记录了全球范围内促进财产转让的制度质量随时间推移而改善。

Child Skill Production: Accounting for Parental and Market-Based Time and Goods Investments

儿童技能生产：考虑父母与市场的时间及商品投资

Elizabeth M. Caucutt, Lance Lochner, Joseph Mullins, Youngmin Park

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Families invest parental time, home goods/services, and market-based child care in their children. We study these investments, focusing on two issues: the role of parental human capital and the substitutability of inputs in the skill production process. We develop a relative demand estimation strategy that uses intratemporal optimality to estimate input substitutability, as well as the relative productivity of inputs and the role played by parental education. This approach requires only a weak separability assumption on the dynamics of skills, no data on skills, and easily addresses measurement error in inputs. We show how relative demand restrictions can simplify estimation of the dynamics of skill production using (noisy) measures of skills when there are multiple investment inputs that are imperfectly measured. Finally, we show how moments related to relative demand can be combined with moments related to skill dynamics to determine whether beliefs about skill production align with the true technology. Using data from the Child Development Supplement of the PSID, we estimate the skill production technology for children ages 12 and younger, finding moderately strong complementarity between inputs. We estimate little effect of parental education on the child production technology: more-educated parents invest more, because they have higher incomes and stronger preference for children’s skills. Counterfactual simulations show that the degree of input complementarity we estimate has important implications for policies that subsidize specific inputs or provide free child care.

A calibration of the model consistent with empirically estimated volume semi-elasticities to tax changes and that features a 30% share of non-fundamental trading volume is associated with a 37bps optimal tax.

Dissertators with Distantly Related Foci Face Divergent Near-Term Outcomes

研究焦点相距甚远的博士毕业生面临分化的短期结果

Kevin M. Kniffin, Andrew S. Hanks, Xuechao Qian, Bo Wang, Bruce A. Weinberg

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

Institutional leaders have long championed interdisciplinary research; however, researchers have paid relatively little attention to the people responding to such calls and their subsequent career outcomes. With the benefit of two large datasets spanning from 1986 through 2016, we show that interdisciplinary dissertations have become consistently more common in recent years as institutional leaders have highlighted the value of boundary-spanning research for solving important and emergent problems. With the benefit of survey data from a near-complete population of all dissertators in the US starting in 2001 through 2016, we observe a consistent upward trend in interdisciplinary dissertations. Unfortunately, we show that these interdisciplinary dissertators have experienced a comparably persistent penalty when considering salaries for their first year after earning the PhD. We also show that among interdisciplinary dissertators, individuals in lower-paying fields tend to earn more when choosing distantly related topic-combinations whereas researchers in higher-paying fields tend to be most rewarded for staying within relatively narrow disciplinary silos.

Venture Capitalists and COVID-19

风险投资家与COVID-19

Paul Gompers, Will Gornall, Steven N. Kaplan, Ilya A. Strebulaev

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We survey over 1,000 institutional and corporate venture capitalists (VCs) at more than 900 different firms to learn how their decisions and investments have been affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. We compare their survey answers to those provided by a large sample of VCs in early 2016 and analyzed in Gompers, Gornall, Kaplan, and Strebulaev (2020). VCs have slowed their investment pace (71% of normal) and expect to invest at 81% of their normal pace over the coming year. Not surprisingly, they have devoted more time to guiding the portfolio companies through the pandemic. VCs report that 52% of their portfolio companies are positively affected or unaffected by the pandemic; 38% are negatively affected; and 10% are severely negatively affected. Overall, they expect the pandemic to have a small negative effect on their fund IRRs (-1.6%) and MOICs (-0.07). Surprisingly, we find little change in the allocation of their time to helping portfolio companies relative to looking for new investments. In general, we find only modest differences between institutional and corporate VCs.

Intergenerational Mobility in Socio-emotional Skills

社会情感技能的代际流动性

Orazio Attanasio, Áureo de Paula, Alessandro Toppeta

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 英国

Abstract

This paper investigates the intergenerational transmission of socio-emotional skills during childhood, using data from the 1970 British Cohort Study (BCS70) in the United Kingdom. This dataset enables us to measure two dimensions of socio-emotional development: internalising and externalising skills. More importantly, we can use multiple measures of parents’ skills collected during both their childhood and their adulthood. Whereas parent-child skills are strongly related when both are measured contemporaneously, they remain correlated when both are measured in childhood, with a stronger transmission observed from mothers to their children. Additionally, by leveraging the BCS70 data on socio-emotional skills for three generations, we estimate multi-generational persistence. Notably, we find a correlation between the grandmother’s internalising skill and the grandchildren’s skills, even after accounting for parental skills.
