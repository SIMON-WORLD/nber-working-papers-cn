Global Supply Chain Pressures, International Trade, and Inflation

全球供应链压力、国际贸易与通货膨胀

Julian di Giovanni, Ṣebnem Kalemli-Özcan, Alvaro Silva, Muhammed A. Yildirim

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

We study the impact of the Covid-19 pandemic on Euro Area inflation and how it compares to the experiences of other countries, such as the United States, over the two-year period 2020-21. Our model-based calibration exercises deliver four key results: 1) Compositional effects – the switch from services to goods consumption – are amplified through global input-output linkages, affecting both trade and inflation. 2) Inflation can be higher under sector-specific labor shortages relative to a scenario with no such supply shocks. 3) Foreign shocks and global supply chain bottlenecks played an outsized role relative to domestic aggregate demand shocks in explaining Euro Area inflation over 2020-21. 4) International trade did not respond to changes in GDP as strongly as it did during the 2008-09 crisis despite strong demand for goods. These lower trade elasticities in part reflect supply chain bottlenecks. These four results imply that policies aimed at stimulating aggregate demand would not have produced as high an inflation as the one observed in the data without the negative sectoral supply shocks.

中文摘要

我们研究了新冠疫情对欧元区通胀的影响，并将其与2020-21两年期间美国等其他国家的经历进行了比较。基于模型的校准分析得出了四个关键结果：1) 结构性效应——从服务消费转向商品消费——通过全球投入产出联系被放大，同时影响了贸易和通胀。2) 与没有此类供给冲击的情景相比，特定行业的劳动力短缺可能导致更高的通胀。3) 在解释2020-21年欧元区通胀方面，外国冲击和全球供应链瓶颈相对于国内总需求冲击发挥了更大的作用。4) 尽管对商品的需求强劲，但国际贸易对国内生产总值变化的反应不如2008-09年危机期间那样强烈。这些较低的贸易弹性部分反映了供应链瓶颈。这四个结果意味着，如果没有负面的行业供给冲击，旨在刺激总需求的政策不会产生数据中观察到的那么高的通胀。

Minimum Wages and Labor Markets in the Twin Cities

双城的最低工资与劳动力市场

Loukas Karabarbounis, Jeremy Lise, Anusha Nath

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We present new evidence on the labor market effects of large and permanent minimum wage increases by examining the policy changes implemented by Minneapolis and announced by Saint Paul in 2018. Beginning with synthetic difference-in-differences methods, we find that the increase in the minimum wage substantially decreased employment in restaurants, retail, and health, even after accounting for potential confounding effects from the pandemic and civil unrest. Next, using variation in exposure to the minimum wage across establishments and workers within zip codes and industries of the Twin Cities, we find employment effects that are about half as large as those from the time series. We quantify an industry equilibrium model to rationalize our estimates and differentiate among competing economic mechanisms that determine how the minimum wage affects the labor market. Our model accounts quantitatively for the importance of reduced entry in generating a larger employment decline than implied by the cross-sectional estimates; for the employment decline from the announcement of a future minimum wage increase; and for the more negative employment effects of the minimum wage over time, relative to the size of the increase, and when the economy is in a recession.

中文摘要

我们通过考察明尼阿波利斯市实施以及圣保罗市于2018年宣布的政策变化，提供了关于大规模、永久性最低工资上涨对劳动力市场影响的新证据。首先使用合成双重差分法，我们发现，即使在考虑了疫情和社会动荡的潜在混杂效应后，最低工资的提高也显著减少了餐饮、零售和医疗行业的就业。接下来，利用双城邮政编码和行业内各企业及工人对最低工资暴露程度的差异，我们发现的就业效应大约是时间序列估计结果的一半。我们量化了一个行业均衡模型，以合理化我们的估计，并区分决定最低工资如何影响劳动力市场的相互竞争的经济机制。我们的模型在数量上解释了：相对于横截面估计所暗示的就业下降，进入减少在产生更大就业下降中的重要性；未来最低工资上涨公告导致的就业下降；以及随着时间的推移、相对于涨幅而言以及当经济处于衰退时，最低工资带来的更负面的就业效应。

Will the Developing World’s Growing Middle Class Support Low Carbon Policies?

发展中国家日益壮大的中产阶级会支持低碳政策吗？

Matthew E. Kahn, Somik Lall

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

As billions of people in the developing world seek to increase their living standards, their aspirations pose a challenge to global efforts to cut greenhouse gas emissions. The emerging middle class are buying and operating energy intensive durables ranging from vehicles to air conditioners to computers. Owners of these durables represent an interest group with a stake in opposing carbon pricing. The political economy of encouraging middle class support for carbon pricing hinges on offsetting its perceived negative income effects. Rising environmentalism in the developing world could also increase support for credible GHG reduction policy. We quantify these effects as we estimate Engel curves of durables ownership, compare the grid’s carbon intensity by nation and study the demographic correlates of support for prioritizing environmental protection.

中文摘要

随着发展中国家数十亿人寻求提高生活水平，他们的愿望对全球减少温室气体排放的努力构成了挑战。新兴中产阶级正在购买和使用从汽车到空调再到电脑等高耗能耐用品。这些耐用品的所有者代表了一个利益集团，他们有理由反对碳定价。鼓励中产阶级支持碳定价的政治经济学关键在于抵消其感知到的负面收入效应。发展中国家日益增长的环保主义也可能增加对可信的温室气体减排政策的支持。我们在估计耐用品所有权的恩格尔曲线、比较各国电网的碳强度以及研究支持优先保护环境的人口统计学相关因素时，量化了这些效应。

Intergenerational Transmission of Health at Birth: Fathers Matter Too!

出生健康水平的代际传递：父亲也很重要！

Osea Giuntella, Giulia La Mattina, Climent Quintana-Domeque

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use a unique data set of linked birth records from Florida to analyze the intergenerational transmission of health at birth by parental gender. We show that both paternal and maternal birth weights signiﬁcantly predict the child’s birth weight, even after accounting for all genetic and environmental factors that are common and time-invariant within a family. Our estimates reveal that a one standard deviation increase in mother’s birth weight (535 grams) translates into a 0.13-0.24 standard deviations increase in child’s birth weight (70-128 grams), accounting or not for maternal grandmother ﬁxed eﬀects. On the father’s side, we ﬁnd that a one standard deviation increase in father’s birth weight (563 grams) translates into a 0.10-0.15 standard deviations increase in child’s birth weight (56-78 grams), accounting or not for paternal grandmother ﬁxed eﬀects. The signiﬁcant role of both maternal and paternal health at birth in explaining oﬀspring health at birth is conﬁrmed when using alternative metrics: intrauterine growth, being small for gestational age or being too heavy (i.e., macrosomic).

中文摘要

我们利用佛罗里达州关联出生记录的独特数据集，按父母性别分析了出生健康水平的代际传递。我们表明，即使在考虑了家庭内部所有共同且随时间不变的遗传和环境因素后，父亲和母亲的出生体重都能显著预测孩子的出生体重。我们的估计显示，母亲出生体重每增加一个标准差（535克），孩子的出生体重就会增加0.13-0.24个标准差（70-128克），无论是否考虑外祖母的固定效应。在父亲方面，我们发现父亲出生体重每增加一个标准差（563克），孩子的出生体重就会增加0.10-0.15个标准差（56-78克），无论是否考虑祖母的固定效应。当使用替代指标时，母亲和父亲出生健康水平在解释后代出生健康方面的显著作用得到了证实：这些指标包括宫内生长情况、小于胎龄儿或体重过重（即巨大儿）。

When Is Discrimination Unfair?

何时歧视是不公平的？

Peter J. Kuhn, Trevor T. Osaki

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 土耳其

Abstract

Using a vignette-based survey experiment on Amazon’s Mechanical Turk, we measure how people’s assessments of the fairness of race-based hiring decisions vary with the motivation and circumstances surrounding the discriminatory act and the races of the parties involved. Regardless of their political leaning, our subjects react in very similar ways to the employer’s motivations for the action, such as the quality of information on which statistical discrimination is based. Compared to conservatives, moderates and liberals are much less accepting of discriminatory actions, and consider the discriminatee’s race when making their fairness assessments. We describe four pre-registered models of fairness – (simple) utilitarianism, race-blind rules (RBRs), racial in-group bias, and belief-based utilitarianism (BBU) – and show that the latter two are inconsistent with major aggregate patterns in our data. Instead, we argue that a two-group framework, in which one group (mostly self-described conservatives) values employers’ decision rights and the remaining respondents value utilitarian concerns, explains our main findings well. In this model, both groups also value applying a consistent set of fairness rules in a race-blind manner.

中文摘要

通过在亚马逊土耳其机器人平台上进行的一项基于情景的调查实验，我们衡量了人们对基于种族的招聘决策公平性的评估如何随着歧视行为的动机和情境以及所涉种族的种族而变化。无论其政治倾向如何，我们的受试者对雇主行为动机（例如统计歧视所依据的信息质量）的反应方式非常相似。与保守派相比，温和派和自由派更不接受歧视性行为，并且在做出公平性评估时会考虑被歧视者的种族。我们描述了四个预先注册的公平模型——（简单）功利主义、种族盲规则、种族内群体偏好和基于信念的功利主义——并表明后两者与我们数据中的主要总体模式不一致。相反，我们认为一个双群体框架可以很好地解释我们的主要发现，在该框架中，一个群体（主要是自认为是保守派的人）重视雇主的决策权，而其余受访者则重视功利主义关切。在这个模型中，两个群体也都重视以种族盲目的方式应用一套一致的公平规则。

Data and Welfare in Credit Markets

信贷市场中的数据与福利

Mark Jansen, Fabian Nagel, Constantine Yannelis, Anthony Lee Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We show how to measure the welfare effects arising from increased data availability. When lenders have more data on prospective borrower costs, they can charge prices that are more aligned with these costs. This increases total social welfare and transfers surplus from borrowers to lenders. We show that the magnitudes of the welfare changes can be estimated using only quantity data and variation in prices. We apply the methodology on bankruptcy flag removals and find that removing prior bankruptcy information substantially increases the social surplus of previously bankrupt consumers, at the cost of a modest decrease in total social welfare. This suggests that flag removals have low efficiency costs for redistributing surplus to previously bankrupt borrowers. We show how the framework can be extended to incorporate adverse selection and imperfect competition.

中文摘要

我们展示了如何衡量数据可用性增加带来的福利效应。当贷款人拥有更多关于潜在借款人成本的数据时，他们可以设定与这些成本更一致的定价。这增加了总社会福利，并将剩余从借款人转移给贷款人。我们表明，福利变化的幅度可以仅使用数量数据和价格变化来估计。我们将该方法应用于破产标记移除，并发现移除先前的破产信息显著增加了先前破产消费者的社会剩余，但代价是总社会福利的适度下降。这表明，标记移除在将剩余重新分配给先前破产借款人方面具有较低效率成本。我们展示了如何将该框架扩展到包含逆向选择和不完全竞争。

Common Fund Flows: Flow Hedging and Factor Pricing

共同基金流量：流量对冲与因子定价

Leonid Kogan, Winston Wei Dou, Wei Wu

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Active equity funds care about fund size, affected by fund flows that obey a strong factor structure with the common component responding to macroeconomic shocks. Funds hedge against common flows by tilting their portfolios toward low-flow-beta stocks, while household/retail and index investors overweight high-flow-beta stocks in equilibrium. Consequently, common flows earn a risk premium, leading to a multi-factor asset-pricing model resembling the ICAPM, even with myopic agents and unsophisticated fund clients. Exploiting quasi-experiments induced by the local-natural-disaster occurrences and the unexpected trade-war announcements, we find that an increased outflow risk faced by funds leads to more aggressive flow-hedging portfolio tilts.

中文摘要

主动型股票基金关注基金规模，而基金规模受基金流量影响，这些流量遵循强因子结构，其共同成分对宏观经济冲击做出反应。基金通过将投资组合向低流量贝塔股票倾斜来对冲共同流量，而家庭/散户和指数投资者在均衡中则超配高流量贝塔股票。因此，共同流量获得风险溢价，导致一个类似于跨期资本资产定价模型的多因子资产定价模型，即使存在短视的代理人和不成熟的基金客户也是如此。利用由局部自然灾害发生和意外贸易战公告引发的准实验，我们发现基金面临的流出风险增加会导致更激进的对冲流量投资组合倾斜。

Combating Cross-Border Externalities

应对跨境外部性

Shiyi Chen, Joshua S. Graff Zivin, Huanhuan Wang, Jiaxin Xiong

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候

Abstract

This paper investigates the impact of a pioneering pollution reduction program, the Ecological Compensation Initiative (ECI) in China, which establishes side payments between upstream and downstream provinces along the same river. The program includes both Coasian and pay-for-performance elements. Instructed by a theoretical model, we employ a difference-in-differences empirical design and find strong evidence that the ECI mitigates the spillover effect of water pollution at the province boundary and brings about sharp reductions in water pollutant emissions from upstream firms, especially those in heavily polluting industries. This initiative also reduces upstream firms’ output and pollution intensity relative to downstream firms. The impact is stronger for upstream firms closer to the river and the point at which it enters the downstream province. Further evidence shows a significant increase in the rate of firms’ entry into neighboring prefectures, but no impact on firms’ exit from that region due to the initiative. Evidence from similar programs, later established in other river systems, suggests that cross-jurisdictional negotiations can effectively mitigate cross-border pollution externalities.

中文摘要

本文研究了一项开创性的污染减排计划——中国的生态补偿倡议的影响，该计划在同一条河流的上游和下游省份之间建立了横向转移支付。该计划既包含科斯式要素，也包含按绩效付费要素。在理论模型的指导下，我们采用双重差分实证设计，发现了强有力的证据表明生态补偿倡议减轻了省界处水污染的溢出效应，并导致上游企业（尤其是重污染行业的企业）的水污染物排放大幅减少。该倡议还降低了上游企业相对于下游企业的产出和污染强度。对于更靠近河流以及河流进入下游省份点的上游企业，其影响更强。进一步证据显示，该倡议显著提高了企业进入邻近地级市的比率，但对企业的退出没有影响。来自后来在其他河流系统建立的类似项目的证据表明，跨辖区谈判可以有效缓解跨境污染外部性。

Attrition from Administrative Data: Problems and Solutions with an Application to Postsecondary Education

行政数据中的样本流失：问题与解决方案——以高等教育为例

Andrew Foote, Kevin M. Stange

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper examines the bias arising from individuals’ migration from administrative outcome data, with a focus on the labor market consequences of postsecondary education. We find that out-of-state migration is particularly problematic for high-earners, flagship graduates, and certain majors. Consequently, the effect of graduating from a flagship university is 10% higher than one would estimate using in-state earnings exclusively, though the extent of bias differs substantially across contexts. The impact of obtaining a 2-year CTE credential is also understated, as are earnings differences across majors. Approaches to testing for and bounding this bias are considered.

中文摘要

本文考察了因个体从行政结果数据中迁移而产生的偏差，重点关注高等教育的劳动力市场后果。我们发现，跨州迁移对于高收入者、旗舰大学毕业生以及某些专业尤其成问题。因此，从旗舰大学毕业的影响比仅使用州内收入估计的结果高出10%，尽管偏差程度在不同情境下差异很大。获得两年制职业技术教育证书的影响也被低估，不同专业之间的收入差异同样如此。本文考虑了检验和界定这种偏差的方法。

The Value of Pharmacy Benefit Management

药品福利管理的价值

Casey B. Mulligan

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

In theory, equilibrium profits for drug patent holders would not involve significant restraints on production and patient utilization if the market had a mechanism for two-part pricing (Oi 1971) or quantity commitments (Murphy, Snyder, and Topel 2014). In fact, patent expiration has little effect on drug utilization especially when those drugs are delivered through insurance plans. This paper provides a quantitative model consistent with the theory and evidence in which pharmacy benefit management on behalf of insurance plans serves these and other purposes in both monopoly and oligopoly provider settings. Calibrating the model to the U.S. market, I conclude that pharmacy benefit management is worth at least $145 billion annually beyond its resource costs. PBM services add at least $192 billion annually in value to society compared to a manufacturer price-control regime. NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

Import tariffs tend to be higher for final goods than for inputs, a phenomenon commonly referred to as tariff escalation. Yet neoclassical trade theory – and modern Ricardian trade models, in particular – predict that welfare-maximizing tariffs are uniform across sectors. We show that tariff escalation can be rationalized on efficiency grounds in the presence of scale economies. When both downstream and upstream sectors produce under increasing returns to scale, a unilateral tariff in either sector boosts the size and productivity of that sector, raising welfare. While these forces are reinforced up the chain for final-good tariffs, input tariffs may drive final-good producers to relocate abroad, mitigating their potential productivity benefits. The welfare benefits of final-good tariffs thus tend to be larger, with the optimal degree of tariff escalation increasing in the extent of downstream returns to scale. A quantitative evaluation of the US-China trade war demonstrates that any welfare gains from the increase in US tariffs are overwhelmingly driven by final-good tariffs.
