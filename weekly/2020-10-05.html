Who Should Work from Home during a Pandemic? The Wage-Infection Trade-off

疫情期间谁应居家办公？工资与感染的权衡

Sangmin Aum, Sang Yoon (Tim) Lee, Yongseok Shin

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Shutting down the workplace is an effective means of reducing contagion, but can incur large economic losses. We construct an exposure index, which measures infection risks across occupations, and a work-from-home index, which gauges the ease with which a job can be performed remotely across both industries and occupations. Because the two indices are negatively correlated but distinct, the economic costs of containing a pandemic can be minimized by only sending home those jobs that are highly exposed but easy to perform from home. Compared to a lockdown of all non-essential jobs, the optimal policy attains the same reduction in aggregate exposure (32 percent) with one-third fewer workers sent home (24 vs. 36 percent) and with only half the loss in aggregate wages (15 vs. 30 percent). A move from the lockdown to the optimal policy reduces the exposure of low-wage workers the most and the wage loss of the high-wage workers the most, although everyone's wage losses become smaller. A constrained optimal policy under which health workers cannot be sent home still achieves the same exposure reduction with a one-third smaller loss in aggregate wages (19 vs. 30 percent).

中文摘要

关闭工作场所是减少传染的有效手段，但可能造成巨大的经济损失。我们构建了一个暴露指数，用于衡量不同职业的感染风险；同时构建了一个居家办公指数，用于评估不同行业和职业中工作可远程完成的难易程度。由于这两个指数呈负相关但彼此不同，因此，仅让那些暴露风险高但易于居家完成的工作者回家，可以最小化控制疫情的经济成本。与封锁所有非必要工作岗位相比，最优政策在实现相同的总体暴露减少（32%）的情况下，让回家的工人减少了三分之一（24%对36%），且总体工资损失仅为一半（15%对30%）。从封锁政策转向最优政策，对低工资工人的暴露减少最多，对高工资工人的工资损失减少最多，尽管每个人的工资损失都变得更小。即使在卫生工作者不能回家的约束条件下，最优政策仍能实现相同的暴露减少，且总体工资损失减少三分之一（19%对30%）。

Flight to Safety: How Economic Downturns Affect Talent Flows to Startups

避险行为：经济衰退如何影响人才流向初创企业

Shai Bernstein, Richard R. Townsend, Ting Xu

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Using proprietary data from AngelList Talent, we study how individuals’ job search and application behavior changed during the COVID downturn. We find that job seekers shifted their searches toward more established firms and away from early-stage startups, even within the same individual over time. Simultaneously, they broadened their other search parameters. Relative to more established firms, early-stage startups experienced a decline in applications, primarily driven by higher quality candidates. These declines hold within a firm or job posting over time. Our findings uncover a flight to safety channel in the labor market, which may amplify the pro-cyclical nature of entrepreneurial activities.

中文摘要

利用AngelList Talent的专有数据，我们研究了在COVID-19经济衰退期间，个人的求职和申请行为如何变化。我们发现，求职者将搜索方向转向更成熟的企业，远离早期初创企业，即使在同一人随时间变化的行为中也是如此。同时，他们拓宽了其他搜索参数。与更成熟的企业相比，早期初创企业的申请数量下降，这主要是由更高质量的候选人减少所致。这些下降在同一企业或职位发布随时间变化的情况下依然成立。我们的发现揭示了劳动力市场中的一种“避险”渠道，这可能会放大创业活动的顺周期性。

A Helicopter Tour of Some Underlying Issues In Empirical Industrial Organization

实证产业组织若干基础问题的概览

Ariel Pakes

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The paper considers conceptual issues underlying empirical work on markets. It has three parts. The first reviews the analysis of demand and equilibrium in retail markets and then considers recent advances in the analysis of markets which require different assumptions; markets where adverse selection and moral hazard may be important, vertical markets with bargaining, and markets wherein a centralized allocation mechanism replaces prices. The second part considers the analysis of cost and production. It reviews the simultaneity and selection issues in production function estimation and then considers; the distinction between revenue and quantity generating functions and its implications for the analysis of markups, and the empirical analysis of fixed costs and its implications for the analysis of product repositioning. The paper concludes by considering issues that arise due to the complexity of empirical analysis of market dynamics and appropriate ways of dealing with them.

中文摘要

本文探讨了市场实证研究背后的概念性问题。全文分为三部分。第一部分回顾了零售市场中需求与均衡的分析，然后考虑了需要不同假设的市场分析的最新进展：逆向选择和道德风险可能重要的市场、存在讨价还价的垂直市场，以及集中化分配机制取代价格的市场。第二部分考虑了成本与生产的分析。它回顾了生产函数估计中的联立性和选择性问题，然后探讨了：收入生成函数与数量生成函数之间的区别及其对加成率分析的影响，以及固定成本的实证分析及其对产品重新定位分析的影响。本文最后考虑了由于市场动态实证分析的复杂性而产生的问题，以及处理这些问题的适当方法。

Trade with Nominal Rigidities: Understanding the Unemployment and Welfare Effects of the China Shock

名义刚性下的贸易：理解中国冲击的失业与福利效应

Andrés Rodríguez-Clare, Mauricio Ulate, José P. Vásquez

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

We present a dynamic quantitative trade and migration model that incorporates downward nominal wage rigidities and show how this framework can generate changes in unemployment and labor participation that match those uncovered by the empirical literature studying the “China shock.” We find that the China shock leads to average welfare increases in most U.S. states, including many that experience unemployment during the transition. However, nominal rigidities reduce the overall U.S. gains by around two thirds. In addition, there are 18 states that experience welfare losses in the presence of downward nominal wage rigidity that would have experienced gains without it.

中文摘要

我们提出了一个包含向下名义工资刚性的动态定量贸易与移民模型，并展示了该框架如何产生与实证研究“中国冲击”文献所揭示的失业和劳动参与率变化相匹配的结果。我们发现，中国冲击导致美国大多数州的平均福利增加，包括许多在转型期间经历失业的州。然而，名义刚性使美国的总体收益减少了约三分之二。此外，在存在向下名义工资刚性的情况下，有18个州经历了福利损失，而如果没有这种刚性，这些州本应获得收益。

Emerging Markets Sovereign CDS Spreads During COVID-19: Economics versus Epidemiology News

COVID-19期间新兴市场主权CDS利差：经济新闻与流行病学新闻

Timo Daehler, Joshua Aizenman, Yothin Jinjarak

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Can bad news about COVID-19 induce negative expectations on sovereign credit risks? We investigate the factors driving credit default swap (CDS) spreads of emerging market sovereigns around the outbreak of COVID-19. Using 2014-2019 data, we estimate a two-factor model of global and regional risks and then extrapolate the model-implied spreads for the period July 2019–June 2020. Intriguingly, the model initially predicts the realized spreads well but loses predictive accuracy during the COVID-19 pandemic. Fiscal space and oil-revenue dependence primarily drive the differences between the realized and predicted sovereign spreads. Our augmented-factor model indicates that the cumulative COVID-19 mortality rate growth is positively associated with the CDS spreads. The evidence suggests that the epidemiological deterioration can lower confidence in the sovereign credit markets due to the prospects of prolonged lockdowns and a slower GDP growth recovery. Our results also hold for a single regression of daily spread changes during 2014-2020.

中文摘要

关于COVID-19的坏消息能否引发对主权信用风险的负面预期？我们研究了COVID-19爆发前后新兴市场主权信用违约互换（CDS）利差的驱动因素。利用2014-2019年的数据，我们估计了一个包含全球和区域风险的双因子模型，然后外推了2019年7月至2020年6月期间模型隐含的利差。有趣的是，该模型最初能很好地预测实际利差，但在COVID-19大流行期间失去了预测准确性。财政空间和石油收入依赖性是实际与预测主权利差差异的主要驱动因素。我们的扩展因子模型表明，累计COVID-19死亡率增长与CDS利差正相关。证据表明，由于长期封锁和GDP增长复苏放缓的前景，流行病学状况恶化可能降低主权信贷市场的信心。我们的结果在2014-2020年每日利差变化的单一回归中也成立。

Public Policy and Participation in Political Interest Groups: An Analysis of Minimum Wages, Labor Unions, and Effective Advocacy

公共政策与政治利益集团参与：最低工资、工会与有效倡导的分析

Jeffrey Clemens, Michael R. Strain

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Why do individuals join interest groups? Through what channels do interest groups and public policy affect one another? We study these questions by analyzing the interplay among labor unions, minimum wages, news coverage, and public opinion. Over the past decade, labor unions have played a significant role in advocating for state and federal minimum wage increases. Over this period, we find that each dollar in minimum wage increase predicts a 5 percent increase (0.3 pp) in the union membership rate among individuals age 16–40. We document four additional facts that shed light on the mechanisms that may underlie this finding. First, while we find increases overall in union membership, we find declines among the minimum wage’s most direct beneficiaries. This is consistent with a classic “free-riding” hypothesis. Second, we find increases in union membership among much broader groups that are not directly affected by the minimum wage. Third, we find that minimum wage increases predict increases in unions’ favorability ratings among the public. Fourth, we find that events in the legislative histories of minimum wage increases predict increases in counts of newspaper articles that simultaneously discuss the minimum wage and key players in the labor movement. Overall coverage of organized labor shifts towards articles that discuss the minimum wage. These facts are consistent with models in which a desire to affiliate with “effective advocacy” is an important driver of the decision to participate in unions and other politically oriented groups.

中文摘要

个人为何加入利益集团？利益集团与公共政策如何相互影响？我们通过分析工会、最低工资、新闻报道和公众舆论之间的相互作用来研究这些问题。在过去十年中，工会在倡导州和联邦最低工资上涨方面发挥了重要作用。在此期间，我们发现，每增加一美元的最低工资，预测16-40岁人群的工会会员率将增加5%（0.3个百分点）。我们记录了四个额外的事实，揭示了可能支撑这一发现的机制。首先，虽然我们发现工会会员总体增加，但在最低工资最直接的受益者中却出现了下降。这与经典的“搭便车”假说一致。其次，我们发现，在不受最低工资直接影响的更广泛群体中，工会会员有所增加。第三，我们发现最低工资上涨预示着公众对工会的好感度上升。第四，我们发现最低工资上涨立法过程中的事件，预示着同时讨论最低工资和劳工运动关键人物的报纸文章数量增加。对工会组织的整体报道转向讨论最低工资的文章。这些事实与以下模型一致：即对“有效倡导”的归属感是决定加入工会和其他政治导向团体的重要驱动因素。

Global Financial Cycle and Liquidity Management

全球金融周期与流动性管理

Olivier Jeanne, Damiano Sandri

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We use a tractable model to show that emerging markets can protect themselves from the global financial cycle by expanding (rather than restricting) capital flows. This involves accumulating reserves when global liquidity is high to buy back domestic assets at a discount when global financial conditions tighten. Since the private sector does not internalize how this buffering mechanism reduces international borrowing costs, a social planner increases the size of capital flows beyond the laissez-faire equilibrium. The model also provides a role for foreign exchange intervention in less financially developed countries. The main predictions of the model are consistent with the data.

中文摘要

我们使用一个易于处理的模型表明，新兴市场可以通过扩大（而非限制）资本流动来保护自己免受全球金融周期的影响。这包括在全球流动性高时积累储备，以便在全球金融状况收紧时以折扣价回购国内资产。由于私人部门没有内化这种缓冲机制降低国际借贷成本的作用，社会规划者会将资本流动规模扩大到自由放任均衡之上。该模型也为金融欠发达国家的外汇干预提供了作用依据。该模型的主要预测与数据一致。

Deterrence Effects of Antifraud and Abuse Enforcement in Health Care

医疗保健领域反欺诈与滥用执法的威慑效应

David H. Howard, Ian McCarthy

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Estimates of the benefits of antifraud enforcement in health care typically focus on direct monetary damages. Deterrence effects are acknowledged but unquantified. We evaluate the impact of a Department of Justice investigation of hospitals accused of billing Medicare for unnecessary implantable cardiac defibrillator (ICD) procedures on their use. Using 100% inpatient and outpatient procedure data from Florida, we estimate that the investigation caused a 22% decline in unnecessary ICD implantations. The present value of savings nationally over a 10 year period is $2.7 billion, nearly 10 times larger than the $280 million in settlements the Department of Justice recovered from hospitals. The investigation had a large and long-lasting effect on physician behavior, indicating the utility of antifraud enforcement as a tool for reducing wasteful medical care.

中文摘要

医疗保健领域反欺诈执法的收益估算通常侧重于直接货币损害赔偿。威慑效应虽被承认但未量化。我们评估了美国司法部对涉嫌向医疗保险收取不必要植入式心脏复律除颤器（ICD）手术费用的医院进行调查的影响。利用佛罗里达州100%的住院和门诊手术数据，我们估计该调查导致不必要的ICD植入量下降了22%。全国范围内10年期间的储蓄现值为27亿美元，几乎是司法部从医院追回的2.8亿美元和解金的10倍。该调查对医生行为产生了巨大且持久的影响，表明反欺诈执法作为减少浪费性医疗保健工具的有效性。

Wealth Transfers and Net Wealth at Death: Evidence from the Italian Inheritance Tax Records 1995–2016

财富转移与死亡时净财富：来自意大利1995-2016年遗产税记录的证据

Paolo Acciari, Salvatore Morelli

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 意大利

Abstract

In this paper we describe a novel source of data on the full record of inheritance tax files in Italy, covering up to 63% of total deceased. The work documents a substantial rise in the total value of inheritance and gifts as a share of national income, from 8.4% in 1995 to 15.1% in 2016. Consistent with the increasing role of total personal net wealth in the economy, the weight of inheritance and gifts in Italy appears relatively high by international standards. Over the same period, total wealth left at death has also become increasingly concentrated.
