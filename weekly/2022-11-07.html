Give Me a Pass: Flexible Credit for Entrepreneurs in Colombia

给我一个通行证：哥伦比亚企业家的灵活信贷

Lasse Brune, Xavier Giné, Dean Karlan

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 哥伦比亚

Abstract

Although microcredit has reached millions, recent randomized evaluations find limited average business impacts. Contract rigidity, specifically the fixed and frequent installments, may restrict moral hazard but limit productive risk-taking, thus limiting the impact. With a Colombian lender, we experimentally compared, for a sample of new borrowers, a standard rigid loan to a more flexible loan that included three “passes” with which borrowers could delay repayments. The flexible loan led to some shifts in investment behavior but had no average impact on revenue, profit level, or profit variance, and led to higher default.

中文摘要

尽管小额信贷已覆盖数百万人，但最近的随机评估发现其平均商业影响有限。合同刚性，特别是固定且频繁的分期付款，可能限制了道德风险，但也限制了生产性风险承担，从而限制了影响。我们与一家哥伦比亚贷款机构合作，针对新借款人样本，实验性地比较了标准刚性贷款与一种更灵活的贷款，后者包含三个“通行证”，借款人可以用其延迟还款。灵活贷款导致了一些投资行为的转变，但对收入、利润水平或利润方差没有平均影响，并导致了更高的违约率。

Smiles in Profiles: Improving Fairness and Efficiency Using Estimates of User Preferences in Online Marketplaces

资料中的微笑：利用在线市场中用户偏好的估计提高公平性和效率

Susan Athey, Dean Karlan, Emil Palikot, Yuan Yuan

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Online platforms often have conflicting goals: they face tradeoffs between increasing efficiency and reducing disparities, where the latter may relate to objectives such as the longer-term health of the marketplace or the organization’s mission. We examine how participants’ profile pictures shape this trade-off in the context of a peer-to-peer lending platform. We develop and apply an approach to estimate marketplace participants’ preferences for different profile features, distinguishing between (i) "type" (e.g., gender, age) and (ii) "style" (e.g., smiling in the photo). Relative to type, style features are easier to change, and platforms may be more willing to encourage such changes. Our approach starts by using causal inference methods together with computer vision algorithms applied to observational data to identify type and style features of profiles that appear to affect demand for transactions. We further decompose type-based disparities into a component driven by demand for certain types and a component that arises because different types have different distributions of style features; we find that style differences exacerbate type-based disparities. To improve internal validity, we then carry out two randomized survey experiments using generative models to create multiple versions of profile images that differ in one feature at a time. We then evaluate counterfactual platform policies based on the changeable profile features and identify approaches that can ameliorate the disparity-efficiency tension. We identify marketplace feedback effects, where encouraging certain style choices attracts participants who value these choices.

中文摘要

在线平台通常面临相互冲突的目标：它们在提高效率和减少差异之间面临权衡，后者可能与市场长期健康或组织使命等目标相关。我们研究了在点对点借贷平台背景下，参与者的个人资料照片如何塑造这种权衡。我们开发并应用了一种方法来估计市场参与者对不同资料特征的偏好，区分（i）“类型”（例如，性别、年龄）和（ii）“风格”（例如，照片中微笑）。与类型相比，风格特征更容易改变，平台可能更愿意鼓励这种改变。我们的方法首先使用因果推断方法以及应用于观测数据的计算机视觉算法，来识别似乎影响交易需求的资料类型和风格特征。我们进一步将基于类型的差异分解为由对某些类型的需求驱动的部分，以及由于不同类型具有不同风格特征分布而产生的部分；我们发现风格差异加剧了基于类型的差异。为了提高内部有效性，我们随后进行了两项随机调查实验，使用生成模型创建每次仅在一个特征上有所不同的多个版本的个人资料图像。然后，我们基于可改变的资料特征评估反事实平台政策，并确定可以缓解差异-效率紧张关系的方法。我们识别了市场反馈效应，即鼓励某些风格选择会吸引重视这些选择的参与者。

Effects of the Earned Income Tax Credit for Childless Adults: A Regression Discontinuity Approach

无子女成年人劳动所得税抵免的效果：断点回归方法

Jonathan Meer, Joshua Witter

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

Most antipoverty policy in the United States focuses on families with children, but efforts to assist childless adults have gained traction in recent years. We examine the impact of the Earned Income Tax Credit on the labor force outcomes of childless adults using the age-25 eligibility discontinuity. We find no impacts on labor force participation and employment outcomes, which may be due to lack of information about the credit, lack of behavioral response due to its small size, or that childless adults already have very high labor force participation rates.

中文摘要

美国大多数反贫困政策针对有子女的家庭，但近年来帮助无子女成年人的努力逐渐获得关注。我们利用25岁年龄资格断点，研究劳动所得税抵免对无子女成年人劳动力市场结果的影响。我们发现该政策对劳动力参与和就业结果没有显著影响，这可能是因为缺乏对该抵免的了解、其规模较小导致行为反应不足，或者无子女成年人的劳动力参与率已经很高。

Legal Activism, State Policy, and Racial Inequality in Teacher Salaries and Educational Attainment in the Mid-Century American South

法律行动、州政策与种族不平等：20世纪中期美国南部的教师工资与教育成就

Elizabeth U. Cascio, Ethan G. Lewis

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

In the late 1930s, the NAACP launched a campaign to equalize Black and white teacher salaries in the de jure segregated schools of the American South. Using newly collected county panel data spanning three decades, this paper first documents heterogeneous within-state impacts of the campaign on teacher salaries. In states that reinforced successful NAACP litigation by introducing universal minimum salary schedules based on objective criteria, the relatively large wage penalty historically suffered by Black teachers in districts with higher Black enrollment shares disappeared by the mid-1950s. In states that resisted by adopting salary schedules using the National Teacher Examination as a measure of teaching efficacy, that penalty remained. In the second part of the paper, we estimate the effect of teacher pay on educational attainment exploiting variation in Black salary gains over time across counties with different Black enrollment shares, and across states by whether subsequent state policy reinforced or resisted court rulings favorable to the NAACP. We find that Black teacher salary gains contributed to the large reductions in racial inequality in school enrollment and grade progression in the South at mid-century.

中文摘要

在20世纪30年代末，全国有色人种协进会发起了一场运动，旨在使美国南部法律上实行种族隔离的学校中黑人与白人教师工资平等。利用新收集的跨越三十年的县级面板数据，本文首先记录了该运动对教师工资在州内产生的异质性影响。在那些通过引入基于客观标准的统一最低工资表来强化全国有色人种协进会成功诉讼的州，历史上黑人教师在高黑人入学率地区遭受的相对较大的工资惩罚到20世纪50年代中期消失了。在那些通过采用以国家教师考试作为教学效能衡量标准的工资表来抵制的州，这种惩罚依然存在。在论文的第二部分，我们利用不同黑人入学率县之间黑人工资增长随时间的变化，以及各州后续政策是强化还是抵制有利于全国有色人种协进会的法院裁决，来估计教师工资对教育成就的影响。我们发现，黑人教师工资的增长促进了20世纪中期美国南部在入学率和年级晋升方面种族不平等的大幅减少。

Disaggregated Economic Accounts

分项经济账户

Asger L. Andersen, Kilian Huber, Niels Johannesen, Ludwig Straub, Emil Toft Vestergaard

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 丹麦

Abstract

We develop a system of disaggregated economic accounts. The system breaks down national accounting positions into bilateral flows between consistently defined groups of consumers (“consumer cells”), groups of producers (“producer cells”), the government, and the rest of the world. We disaggregate the full circular flow of money, including consumer spending, labor compensation, firm profits, trade in intermediates, foreign trade, and government transactions, while satisfying all national accounting identities. We implement the disaggregated system for small region-by-industry cells in Denmark and present stylized facts, including variation in domestic spending shares, local and urban bias in consumer spending, and a pattern of “triangular flows” across regions. Cell-level measures of “spending intensity” capture how much spending by a cell contributes to the income of cells experiencing unemployment after a shock. Using a general equilibrium model, we show that fiscal transfers are more effective in stimulating aggregate GDP when they target cells with high spending intensity on unemployed cells. Knowledge of the disaggregated economic accounts helps governments select more effective policies.

中文摘要

我们开发了一套分项经济账户体系。该体系将国民核算项目分解为一致定义的消费者群体（“消费者单元”）、生产者群体（“生产者单元”）、政府和世界其他地区之间的双边流量。我们对货币的完整循环流动进行分项，包括消费者支出、劳动报酬、企业利润、中间品贸易、对外贸易和政府交易，同时满足所有国民核算恒等式。我们在丹麦的小区域-行业单元层面实施了这套分项体系，并呈现了一些典型事实，包括国内支出份额的差异、消费者支出中的本地和城市偏向，以及跨区域的“三角流动”模式。单元层面的“支出强度”衡量了一个单元的支出在多大程度上贡献于遭受冲击后经历失业的单元的收入。利用一个一般均衡模型，我们表明，当财政转移支付针对那些对失业单元具有高支出强度的单元时，在刺激总GDP方面更为有效。了解分项经济账户有助于政府选择更有效的政策。

How Substitutable Are Workers? Evidence from Worker Deaths

工人的可替代性有多高？来自工人死亡的证据

Simon Jäger, Jörg Heining

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 德国

Abstract

We estimate how exogenous worker exits affect firms’ demand for incumbent workers and new hires. Drawing on administrative data from Germany, we analyze 34,000 unexpected worker deaths, which, on average, raise the remaining workers’ wages and retention probabilities. The average effect masks substantial heterogeneity: Coworkers in the same occupation as the deceased see positive wage effects; coworkers in other occupations experience wage decreases when a high-skilled or specialized worker dies. Our findings imply substantial replacement costs, which are larger in thin markets and when skills are specialized.

中文摘要

我们估计了外生的工人离职如何影响企业对在职员工和新招聘员工的需求。利用德国的行政数据，我们分析了34,000例意外的工人死亡事件。平均而言，这些事件提高了剩余工人的工资和留任概率。平均效应掩盖了显著的异质性：与逝者同一职业的同事看到了积极的工资效应；而当高技能或专业化工人去世时，其他职业的同事则经历了工资下降。我们的发现意味着可观的替代成本，这些成本在劳动力市场较薄和技能专业化程度较高时更大。

The NBER Orange Book Dataset: A User’s Guide

NBER橙皮书数据集：用户指南

Maya Durvasula, C. Scott Hemphill, Lisa Larrimore Ouellette, Bhaven N. Sampat, Heidi L. Williams

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

This paper introduces a newly digitized, open-access version of the Food and Drug Administration’s “Orange Book”—a linkage between approved small-molecule drugs and the patents that protect them. The Orange Book also reports any applicable regulatory exclusivity that prevents competitive entry. We summarize the Orange Book’s coverage and discuss the opportunities and challenges associated with using these data for research. Empirical validations against various administrative datasets suggest that Orange Book records are, largely, complete and accurate. We conclude with a specific use case—calculating legal exclusivity periods for drugs—to highlight the types of choices that researchers must make when using this resource.

中文摘要

本文介绍了美国食品药品监督管理局“橙皮书”的一个新近数字化、开放获取的版本——该文件将已批准的小分子药物与保护它们的专利联系起来。橙皮书还报告了任何可能阻止竞争性进入的适用监管排他期。我们总结了橙皮书的覆盖范围，并讨论了使用这些数据进行研究的机会和挑战。针对各种行政数据集的实证验证表明，橙皮书记录在很大程度上是完整和准确的。我们以一个具体的使用案例——计算药物的法定排他期——作为结尾，以强调研究人员在使用这一资源时必须做出的选择类型。

Heterogeneity in School Value-Added and the Private Premium

学校增值的异质性与私立学校溢价

Tahir Andrabi, Natalie Bau, Jishnu Das, Asim Ijaz Khwaja

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 巴基斯坦

Abstract

Using rich panel data from Pakistan, we compute test score based measures of quality (School Value-Addeds or SVAs) for more than 800 schools across 112 villages and verify that they are valid and unbiased. With the SVA measures, we then document three striking features of the schooling environment. First, there is substantial within-village variation in quality. The annualized difference in learning between the best and worst performing school in the same village is 0.4 sd; compounded over 5 years of primary schooling, this difference is similar in size to the test score gap between low- and high-income countries. Second, students learn more in private schools (0.15 sd per year on average), but substantial within-sector variation in quality means that the effects of reallocating students from public to private schools can range from -0.35sd to +0.65sd. Thus, there is a range of possible causal estimates of the private premium, a feature of the environment we illustrate using three different identification approaches. Finally, parents appear to recognize and reward SVA in the private sector, but the link between parental demand and SVA is weaker in the public sector. These results have implications for both the measurement of the private premium and how we design and evaluate policies that reallocate children across schools, such as school closures and vouchers.

中文摘要

利用来自巴基斯坦的丰富面板数据，我们为横跨112个村庄的800多所学校计算了基于考试成绩的质量衡量指标（学校增值），并验证了这些指标是有效且无偏的。利用学校增值指标，我们随后记录了学校环境的三个显著特征。首先，同一村庄内的学校质量存在显著差异。同一村庄中表现最好和最差学校之间的年化学习差异为0.4个标准差；在五年的小学教育中累积，这一差异与低收入和高收入国家之间的考试成绩差距规模相似。其次，学生在私立学校学习更多（平均每年0.15个标准差），但部门内质量的显著差异意味着将学生从公立学校重新分配到私立学校的效果范围可以从-0.35个标准差到+0.65个标准差。因此，私立学校溢价存在一系列可能的因果估计值，这是我们利用三种不同识别方法说明的环境特征。最后，父母似乎能够识别并奖励私立部门的学校增值，但在公立部门，父母需求与学校增值之间的联系较弱。这些结果对私立学校溢价的衡量以及我们如何设计和评估将儿童在学校间重新分配的政策（如学校关闭和代金券）都有启示。

Air Quality and Suicide

空气质量与自杀

Claudia Persico, Dave E. Marcotte

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

We conduct the first-ever large-scale study of the relationship between air pollution and suicide using detailed cause of death data from all death certificates in the U.S. between 2003 and 2010. Using wind direction as an instrument for daily pollution exposure, we find that a 1 μg/m3 increase in daily PM2.5 is associated with a 0.49% increase in daily suicides and 0.171 more suicide-related hospitalizations (a 50% increase). Estimates using 2SLS are larger and more robust, suggesting a bias towards zero arising from measurement error. Event study estimates further illustrate that contemporaneous pollution exposure matters more than exposure to pollution in previous weeks.

中文摘要

我们利用2003年至2010年间美国所有死亡证明的详细死因数据，进行了关于空气污染与自杀之间关系的首次大规模研究。使用风向作为日常污染暴露的工具变量，我们发现每日PM2.5每增加1 μg/m³，与每日自杀人数增加0.49%以及因自杀相关住院人数增加0.171例（增加50%）相关。使用两阶段最小二乘法的估计值更大且更稳健，表明测量误差导致了向零的偏误。事件研究估计进一步说明，当期的污染暴露比前几周的污染暴露更为重要。

Marriage Matching over Five Centuries in China

中国五个世纪以来的婚姻匹配

Carol H. Shiue, Wolfgang Keller

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法

Abstract

In the marriage market, families make investments on behalf of their young so that they are able to form a household with their preferred partner. Abstract
