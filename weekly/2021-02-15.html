The Economics of Immense Risk, Urgent Action and Radical Change: Towards New Approaches to the Economics of Climate Change

巨大风险、紧急行动与根本变革的经济学：迈向气候变化经济学的新方法

Nicholas Stern, Joseph E. Stiglitz, Charlotte Taylor

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Designing policy for climate change requires analyses which integrate the interrelationship between the economy and the environment. We argue that, despite their dominance in the economics literature and influence in public discussion and policymaking, the methodology employed by Integrated Assessment Models (IAMs) rests on flawed foundations, which become particularly relevant in relation to the realities of the immense risks and challenges of climate change, and the radical changes in our economies that a sound and effective response require. We identify a set of critical methodological problems with the IAMs which limit their usefulness and discuss the analytic foundations of an alternative approach that is more capable of providing insights into how best to manage the transition to net-zero emissions.

中文摘要

设计气候变化政策需要整合经济与环境相互关系的分析。我们认为，尽管综合评估模型在经济学文献中占据主导地位，并在公共讨论和政策制定中具有影响力，但其方法论建立在有缺陷的基础上，这在面对气候变化巨大风险和挑战的现实，以及有效应对所需的根本性经济变革时尤为突出。我们指出了综合评估模型的一系列关键方法论问题，这些限制了其有用性，并讨论了一种替代方法的分析基础，该方法更能为如何最好地管理向净零排放转型提供见解。

Does Marijuana Legalization Affect Work Capacity? Evidence from Workers’ Compensation Benefits

大麻合法化会影响工作能力吗？来自工人赔偿福利的证据

Rahi Abouk, Keshar M. Ghimire, Johanna Catherine Maclean, David Powell

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the effect of state recreational marijuana laws (RMLs) on workers’ compensation (WC) benefit receipt among adults 40-62 years. We find that WC receipt declines in response to RML adoption both in terms of the propensity to receive benefits and benefit amount. We estimate complementary declines in non-traumatic workplace injury rates and the incidence of work-limiting disabilities. We offer evidence that the primary driver of these reductions is an improvement in work capacity, likely due to access to an additional form of pain management therapy.

中文摘要

我们研究了州级娱乐用大麻法对40-62岁成年人工人赔偿福利领取的影响。我们发现，在采用娱乐用大麻法后，工人赔偿福利的领取在领取倾向和福利金额两方面均有所下降。我们估计，非创伤性工伤事故率和工作限制性残疾的发生率也出现了互补性的下降。我们提供的证据表明，这些减少的主要驱动因素是工作能力的改善，这很可能归因于获得了一种额外的疼痛管理疗法。

Aggregate Employment Effects of Unemployment Benefits During Deep Downturns: Evidence from the Expiration of the Federal Pandemic Unemployment Compensation

深度衰退期间失业救济金的总体就业效应：来自联邦大流行失业补偿计划到期的证据

Arindrajit Dube

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The expiration of the temporary $600 boost to weekly UI benefits under the Federal Pandemic Unemployment Compensation (FPUC) led to a sharp, unprecedented, 98 percentage point reduction (on average) in the replacement rate during a time when employment was recovering during the Covid recession. Leveraging the considerable variation in this drop across states, I use a difference-in-differences event study design to estimate the macro employment effects. I find little impact of job gains from the benefit reduction, especially when I focus on groups (non-college graduates, and those from non-high-income households) that comprise of most UI recipients. The estimates rule out job gains implied by much of the micro UI duration elasticities from the existing literature.

中文摘要

联邦大流行失业补偿计划下每周600美元失业保险金临时增加的到期，导致替代率平均急剧下降了98个百分点，而此时正值新冠衰退期间就业复苏之际。利用各州之间这种下降的巨大差异，我采用双重差分事件研究设计来估计宏观就业效应。我发现，福利减少带来的就业增长影响很小，尤其是当我关注构成大多数失业保险金领取者的群体（非大学毕业生和非高收入家庭人群）时。这些估计排除了现有文献中许多微观失业保险持续时间弹性所隐含的就业增长。

Auto-Enrollment Retirement Plans for the People: Choices and Outcomes in OregonSaves

面向大众的自动注册退休计划：俄勒冈储蓄计划中的选择与结果

John Chalmers, Olivia S. Mitchell, Jonathan Reuter, Mingli Zhong

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Oregon recently launched an automatic-enrollment retirement savings program for private sector workers lacking access to other workplace retirement plans. We analyze participation choices, account balances, and inflow/outflow data using administrative records between August 2018 and April 2020. Within the small to mid-sized firms served by OregonSaves, estimated average after-tax earnings are low ($2,365 per month) and turnover rates are high (38.2% per year). Younger employees and employees in larger firms are less likely to opt out, but participation rates fall over time. The most common reason given for opting out is “I can’t afford to save at this time,” but the second most common is “I have my own retirement plan.” As of April 2020, 67,731 accounts had positive account balances, holding $51.1 million in total assets. The average balance is $754, but with considerable dispersion; younger workers accumulating the fewest assets due to higher job turnover. Overall, we conclude that OregonSaves has meaningfully increased employee savings by reducing search costs. The 34.3% of workers with positive account balances in April 2020 is comparable to the marginal increase in participation at larger firms in the private sector. Employees opting out of OregonSaves are often doing so for rational reasons.

中文摘要

俄勒冈州近期推出了一项自动注册退休储蓄计划，面向无法参与其他工作场所退休计划的私营部门员工。我们利用2018年8月至2020年4月间的行政记录，分析了参与选择、账户余额以及资金流入/流出数据。在俄勒冈储蓄计划服务的中小型企业中，估计的平均税后收入较低（每月2365美元），而离职率较高（每年38.2%）。年轻员工和大型企业员工更不容易选择退出，但参与率随时间推移而下降。选择退出的最常见理由是“目前我负担不起储蓄”，但第二常见的理由是“我有自己的退休计划”。截至2020年4月，共有67731个账户拥有正余额，持有总资产5110万美元。平均余额为754美元，但分布相当分散；年轻工人因工作更替频繁而积累的资产最少。总体而言，我们得出结论，俄勒冈储蓄计划通过降低搜索成本，显著提高了员工储蓄。2020年4月拥有正账户余额的工人比例为34.3%，这与私营部门大型企业参与率的边际增长相当。选择退出俄勒冈储蓄计划的员工通常出于理性原因。

Reparations and Persistent Racial Wealth Gaps

赔偿与持续存在的种族财富差距

Job Boerma, Loukas Karabarbounis

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We analyze the magnitude and persistence of the racial wealth gap using a long-run model of heterogeneous dynasties with an occupational choice and bequests. Our innovation is to introduce endogenous beliefs about risky returns, reflecting differences in dynasties' investment experiences over time. Feeding the exclusion of Black dynasties from labor and capital markets into the model as the only driving force, we find that the model quantitatively reproduces current and historical racial gaps in wealth, income, entrepreneurship, mobility, and beliefs about risky returns. We explore how the future trajectory of the racial wealth gap might change in response to various policies. Wealth transfers to all Black dynasties that eliminate the average wealth gap today do not lead to long-run wealth convergence. The logic is that centuries-long exclusions lead Black dynasties to hold pessimistic beliefs about risky returns and to forgo investment opportunities after the wealth transfer. Investment subsidies toward Black entrepreneurs are more effective than wealth transfers in permanently eliminating the racial wealth gap.

中文摘要

我们利用一个包含职业选择和遗产的长期异质性王朝模型，分析了种族财富差距的规模和持续性。我们的创新之处在于引入了关于风险回报的内生信念，反映了不同王朝随时间推移的投资经验差异。将黑人王朝被排除在劳动力和资本市场之外作为模型中唯一的驱动因素，我们发现该模型定量地再现了当前和历史在财富、收入、创业、流动性以及关于风险回报信念方面的种族差距。我们探讨了种族财富差距的未来轨迹可能如何因各种政策而改变。向所有黑人王朝进行财富转移以消除当前的平均财富差距，并不会导致长期的财富趋同。其逻辑在于，长达几个世纪的排斥导致黑人王朝对风险回报持有悲观信念，并在财富转移后放弃投资机会。与财富转移相比，针对黑人企业家的投资补贴在永久消除种族财富差距方面更为有效。

China’s War on Pollution: Evidence from the First Five Years

中国的污染防治战：前五年的证据

Michael Greenstone, Guojun He, Shanjun Li, Eric Zou

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候

Abstract

The decade from 2010 to 2019 marked a significant turning point in China’s history of environmental regulation and pollution. This article describes the recent trends in air and water quality, with a focus on the five years since China declared a “war on pollution” in 2014. It summarizes the emerging literature that has taken advantage of accompanying improvements in data availability and accuracy to document sharp improvements in environmental quality, especially local air pollution, and understand their social, economic, and health consequences.

中文摘要

2010年至2019年的十年标志着中国环境监管和污染历史上的一个重要转折点。本文描述了近期空气和水质的变化趋势，重点关注自2014年中国宣布“污染防治战”以来的五年。文章总结了利用伴随而来的数据可用性和准确性改善的新兴文献，这些文献记录了环境质量，特别是局部空气污染的显著改善，并理解了其社会、经济和健康后果。

The Economic Geography of Global Warming

全球变暖的经济地理学

José-Luis Cruz, Esteban Rossi-Hansberg

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 加拿大

Abstract

Global warming is a worldwide and protracted phenomenon with heterogeneous local economic effects. In order to evaluate the aggregate and local economic consequences of higher temperatures, we propose a dynamic economic assessment model of the world economy with high spatial resolution. Our model features a number of mechanisms through which individuals can adapt to global warming, including costly trade and migration, and local technological innovations and natality rates. We quantify the model at a 1° × 1° resolution and estimate damage functions that determine the impact of temperature changes on a region’s fundamental productivity and amenities depending on local temperatures. Our baseline results show welfare losses as large as 15% in parts of Africa and Latin America but also high heterogeneity across locations, with northern regions in Siberia, Canada, and Alaska experiencing gains. Our results indicate large uncertainty about average welfare effects and point to migration and, to a lesser extent, innovation as important adaptation mechanisms. We use the model to assess the impact of carbon taxes, abatement technologies, and clean energy subsidies. Carbon taxes delay consumption of fossil fuels and help flatten the temperature curve but are much more effective when an abatement technology is forthcoming.

中文摘要

全球变暖是一种全球性且长期的现象，具有异质性的地方经济影响。为了评估气温升高的总体和地方经济后果，我们提出了一个具有高空间分辨率的世界经济动态经济评估模型。我们的模型包含了一系列个人可以适应全球变暖的机制，包括有成本的贸易和移民，以及地方技术创新和出生率。我们以1°×1°的分辨率量化了该模型，并估计了损害函数，该函数根据当地温度确定温度变化对地区基本生产力和宜居性的影响。我们的基线结果显示，非洲和拉丁美洲部分地区福利损失高达15%，但不同地点之间存在高度异质性，西伯利亚、加拿大和阿拉斯加的北部地区则出现收益。我们的结果表明，平均福利效应存在很大的不确定性，并指出移民以及较小程度上的创新是重要的适应机制。我们利用该模型评估了碳税、减排技术和清洁能源补贴的影响。碳税延迟了化石燃料的消费，并有助于平缓温度曲线，但当减排技术即将出现时，其效果要大得多。

Learning from Shared News: When Abundant Information Leads to Belief Polarization

从共享新闻中学习：当信息丰富导致信念极化时

Renee Bowen, Danil Dmitriev, Simone Galperti

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

We study learning via shared news. Each period agents receive the same quantity and quality of first-hand information and can share it with friends. Some friends (possibly few) share selectively, generating heterogeneous news diets across agents akin to echo chambers. Agents are aware of selective sharing and update beliefs by Bayes' rule. Contrary to standard learning results, we show that beliefs can diverge in this environment leading to polarization. This requires that (i) agents hold misperceptions (even minor) about friends' sharing and (ii) information quality is sufficiently low. Polarization can worsen when agents' social connections expand. When the quantity of first-hand information becomes large, agents can hold opposite extreme beliefs resulting in severe polarization. We find that news aggregators can curb polarization caused by news sharing. Our results hold without media bias or fake news, so eliminating these is not sufficient to reduce polarization. When fake news is included, it can lead to polarization but only through misperceived selective sharing. We apply our theory to shed light on the evolution of public opinions about climate change in the US.

中文摘要

我们研究通过共享新闻进行学习。每个时期，代理人获得相同数量和质量的第一手信息，并可以与朋友分享。一些朋友（可能很少）有选择性地分享，导致代理人之间产生类似于回音室的异质性新闻食谱。代理人意识到选择性分享，并通过贝叶斯规则更新信念。与标准学习结果相反，我们表明在这种环境下信念可能分化，导致极化。这需要满足以下条件：（i）代理人对朋友的分享存在误解（即使是轻微的），以及（ii）信息质量足够低。当代理人的社交联系扩大时，极化可能会恶化。当第一手信息的数量变得很大时，代理人可能持有相反的极端信念，导致严重的极化。我们发现新闻聚合器可以抑制由新闻分享引起的极化。我们的结果在没有媒体偏见或假新闻的情况下成立，因此消除这些因素并不足以减少极化。当包含假新闻时，它可能导致极化，但只能通过被误解的选择性分享来实现。我们应用我们的理论来揭示美国关于气候变化公众舆论的演变。

No. 9论文详情NBER w28464

The Analytic Theory of a Monetary Shock

货币冲击的分析理论

Fernando E. Alvarez, Francesco Lippi

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose an analytical method to analyze the propagation of a once-and-for-all shock in a broad class of sticky price models. The method is based on the eigenvalue- eigenfunction representation of the dynamics of the cross-sectional distribution of firms’ desired adjustments. A key novelty is that, under assumptions that are appropriate for low-inflation economies, we can approximate the whole profile of the impulse response for any moment of interest in response to an aggregate shock (any displacement of the invariant distribution). We present several applications and discuss extensions.

中文摘要

我们提出了一种分析方法，用于分析一次性冲击在一大类粘性价格模型中的传播。该方法基于企业期望调整的横截面分布动态的特征值-特征函数表示。一个关键的新颖之处在于，在适用于低通胀经济的假设下，我们可以近似地得到任何感兴趣的矩在应对总冲击（对不变分布的任何位移）时的整个脉冲响应曲线。我们提出了几个应用并讨论了扩展。

No. 10论文详情NBER w28463

Gender Quotas and Support for Women in Board Elections

性别配额与对董事会选举中女性的支持

Marina Gertsberg, Johanna Mollerstrom, Michaela Pagel

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study shareholder support for corporate board nominees before and after the 2018 California gender quota. Pre-quota, new female nominees received greater support than new male nominees, consistent with women being held to a higher standard. Post-quota, as the number of women increased, support for new (mandated) female nominees decreased to the same level of, but not lower than, the support that new male nominees enjoy. Still, share prices reacted negatively to the quota. We show that this reaction was concentrated in firms that did not turn over their least-supported male directors when adding women to comply with the quota.
