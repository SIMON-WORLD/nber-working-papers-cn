How Well Does Bargaining Work in Consumer Markets? A Robust Bounds Approach

讨价还价在消费者市场中效果如何？一种稳健边界方法

Joachim Freyberger, Bradley Larsen

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This study provides a structural analysis of detailed, alternating-offer bargaining data from eBay, deriving bounds on buyers and sellers private value distributions using a range of assumptions on behavior. These assumptions range from very weak (assuming only that acceptance and rejection decisions are rational) to less weak (e.g., assuming that bargaining offers are weakly increasing in players' private values). We estimate the bounds and show what they imply for consumer negotiation behavior in theory and practice. For the median product, bargaining ends in impasses in 43% of negotiations even when the buyer values the good more than the seller.

中文摘要

本研究对来自eBay的详细交替报价讨价还价数据进行了结构性分析，通过一系列行为假设推导出买方和卖方私人价值分布的边界。这些假设从非常弱（仅假设接受和拒绝决策是理性的）到不那么弱（例如，假设讨价还价报价随参与者私人价值弱递增）。我们估计了这些边界，并展示了它们在理论和实践中对消费者谈判行为的含义。对于中位数产品，即使在买方对商品的估值高于卖方的情况下，讨价还价在43%的谈判中以僵局告终。

The Financial Channel of Wage Rigidity

工资刚性的金融渠道

Benjamin Schoefer

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

I propose a financial channel of wage rigidity. In recessions, rather than propping up marginal (new hires’) costs of labor, rigid average wages squeeze cash flows, forcing firms to cut hiring due to financial constraints. Indeed, empirical cash flows and profits would turn acyclical if wages were only moderately more procyclical. I study this channel in a search and matching model with financial constraints and rigid wages among incumbent workers, while new hires’ wages are flexible. Individually, each feature generates no amplification. By contrast, their interaction can account for much of the empirical labor market fluctuations—breaking the neutrality of incumbents’ wages for hiring, and showing that financial amplification of business cycles requires wage rigidity.

中文摘要

我提出了工资刚性的金融渠道。在经济衰退中，刚性的平均工资不是支撑边际（新雇佣）劳动力成本，而是挤压现金流，迫使企业因金融约束而削减招聘。事实上，如果工资只是适度地更顺周期，经验性的现金流和利润将变得非周期性。我在一个具有金融约束和在职员工工资刚性的搜索与匹配模型中研究这一渠道，而新员工的工资是灵活的。单独来看，每个特征都不会产生放大效应。相比之下，它们的相互作用可以解释大部分经验性的劳动力市场波动——打破了在职员工工资对招聘的中性影响，并表明商业周期的金融放大需要工资刚性。

The Long-Run Impacts of Mexican-American School Desegregation

墨西哥裔美国人学校废除种族隔离的长期影响

Francisca M. Antman, Kalena Cortes

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 墨西哥、西班牙、美国

Abstract

We present the first quantitative analysis of the impact of ending de jure segregation of Mexican-American school children in the United States by examining the effects of the 1947 Mendez v. Westminster court decision on long-run educational attainment for Hispanics and non-Hispanic whites in California. Our identification strategy relies on comparing individuals across California counties that vary in their likelihood of segregating and across birth cohorts that vary in their exposure to the Mendez court ruling based on school start age. Results point to a significant increase in educational attainment for Hispanics who were fully exposed to school desegregation.

中文摘要

我们首次定量分析了美国结束对墨西哥裔学童法律上种族隔离的影响，通过考察1947年门德斯诉威斯敏斯特案对加利福尼亚州西班牙裔和非西班牙裔白人长期教育成就的影响。我们的识别策略依赖于比较加利福尼亚州各县（这些县在隔离可能性上存在差异）以及不同出生队列（这些队列根据入学年龄对门德斯案裁决的暴露程度不同）。结果表明，完全暴露于学校废除种族隔离的西班牙裔教育成就显著提高。

Is that Really a Kuznets Curve? Turning Points for Income Inequality in China

那真的是库兹涅茨曲线吗？中国收入不平等的转折点

Martin Ravallion, Shaohua Chen

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政

Abstract

The path of income inequality in post-reform China has been widely interpreted as “China’s Kuznets curve.” We show that the Kuznets growth model of structural transformation in a dual economy, alongside population urbanization, has little explanatory power for our new series of inequality measures back to 1981. Our simulations tracking the partial “Kuznets derivative” of inequality with respect to urban population share yield virtually no Kuznets curve. More plausible explanations for the inequality turning points relate to determinants of the gap between urban and rural mean incomes, including multiple agrarian policy reforms. Our findings warn against any presumption that the Kuznets process will assure that China has passed its time of rising inequality. More generally, our findings cast doubt on past arguments that economic growth through structural transformation in poor countries is necessarily inequality increasing, or that a turning point will eventually be reached after which that growth will be inequality decreasing.

中文摘要

改革后中国收入不平等的路径被广泛解释为“中国的库兹涅茨曲线”。我们表明，二元经济中结构转型的库兹涅茨增长模型，连同人口城市化，对我们追溯到1981年的新不平等序列几乎没有解释力。我们的模拟追踪了不平等相对于城市人口份额的偏“库兹涅茨导数”，几乎没有产生库兹涅茨曲线。对不平等转折点更合理的解释与城乡平均收入差距的决定因素有关，包括多项农业政策改革。我们的发现警告不要假设库兹涅茨过程将确保中国已经度过了收入不平等上升的时期。更一般地说，我们的发现对过去的论点提出了质疑，即贫困国家通过结构转型的经济增长必然增加不平等，或者最终会达到一个转折点，之后这种增长将减少不平等。

No. 5论文详情NBER w29198

Income-Contingent Loans As an Unemployment Benefit

作为失业救济金的收入或有贷款

Haaris Mateen, Joseph E. Stiglitz, Jungyoll Yun

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Imperfections in risk and capital markets imply that individuals who lose jobs suffer from imperfect smoothing of consumption across states and times. Compared to the first best, there will be too little search. Optimal unemployment programs, which balance the marginal benefit of consumption smoothing vs. the marginal cost of the insurance externality, increase welfare and may even increase GDP. Our analytical results suggest that welfare is higher if the unemployment benefits program includes income-contingent unemployment loans (ICL), where the amount repaid depends on the individual’s future income. Such loans can be financed by a risk premium imposed on the unemployed who avail themselves of the loans, and partially substitute for unemployment insurance (UI) benefits. Optimal unemployment benefits programs (UB) with ICL do a better job of smoothing consumption across states and time, and in particular total benefits when unemployed increase. We analyze how changes in key parameters, such as the degree of risk aversion and the nature of post-employment work, affect the design of the optimal UB program and the magnitude of the incremental benefits from including income-contingent loans.

中文摘要

风险市场和资本市场的不完善意味着失业者无法在状态和时间上完美平滑消费。与最优情况相比，将导致搜索不足。最优失业计划平衡消费平滑的边际收益与保险外部性的边际成本，能提高福利甚至可能增加GDP。我们的分析结果表明，如果失业救济金计划包括收入或有失业贷款（ICL），其中偿还金额取决于个人未来收入，则福利更高。此类贷款可以通过向使用贷款的失业者征收风险溢价来融资，并部分替代失业保险（UI）福利。包含ICL的最优失业救济金计划（UB）能更好地在状态和时间上平滑消费，特别是失业时的总福利增加。我们分析了关键参数的变化，如风险厌恶程度和就业后工作的性质，如何影响最优UB计划的设计以及包含收入或有贷款的增量福利大小。

Sovereign Spreads and the Political Leaning of Nations

主权利差与国家的政治倾向

Ionut Cotoc, Alok Johri, César Sosa-Padilla

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Nations with a higher propensity to elect left governments tend to pay higher and more volatile sovereign spreads. We build a sovereign default model with elections between left and right policymakers. Reelection probabilities increase with government spending, with the left having a small advantage (consistent with the data). We use variation in “election efficiency” to create model economies that elect the left more (left-leaning) or less frequently (right-leaning) in equilibrium. The left-leaning economy has a higher reluctance for fiscal austerity than the right-leaning economy, chooses higher government spending, and faces higher spreads, resulting in lower welfare.

中文摘要

更倾向于选举左翼政府的国家往往支付更高且更波动的主权利差。我们构建了一个包含左翼和右翼政策制定者选举的主权违约模型。连任概率随政府支出增加而增加，左翼具有微小优势（与数据一致）。我们利用“选举效率”的变化来创建在均衡中更频繁选举左翼（左倾）或更不频繁选举右翼（右倾）的模型经济体。左倾经济体比右倾经济体更不愿实施财政紧缩，选择更高的政府支出，面临更高的利差，从而导致更低的福利。

Opposing firm-level Responses to the China Shock: Horizontal Competition Versus Vertical Relationships?

对中国冲击的相反企业层面反应：横向竞争还是纵向关系？

Philippe Aghion, Antonin Bergeaud, Matthieu Lequien, Marc Melitz, Thomas Zuber

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 法国

Abstract

We decompose the “China shock” into two components that induce different adjustments for firms exposed to Chinese exports: an output shock affecting firms selling goods that compete with similar imported Chinese goods, and an input supply shock affecting firms using inputs similar to the imported Chinese goods. Combining French accounting, customs, and patent information at the firm-level, we show that the output shock is detrimental to firms’ sales, employment and innovation. Moreover, this negative impact is concentrated on low-productivity firms. By contrast, we find a positive effect - although often not significant - of the input supply shock on firms’ sales, employment and innovation.

中文摘要

我们将“中国冲击”分解为两个组成部分，它们对面临中国出口的企业引发不同的调整：一个产出冲击，影响销售与类似进口中国商品竞争的商品的企业；一个投入供给冲击，影响使用与进口中国商品类似投入的企业。结合法国企业层面的会计、海关和专利信息，我们表明产出冲击对企业的销售、就业和创新是有害的。此外，这种负面影响集中在低生产率企业上。相比之下，我们发现投入供给冲击对企业销售、就业和创新有积极影响——尽管通常不显著。

When Uncertainty and Volatility Are Disconnected: Implications for Asset Pricing and Portfolio Performance

当不确定性与波动性脱节时：对资产定价和投资组合绩效的影响

Yacine Aït-Sahalia, Felix Matthys, Emilio Osambela, Ronnie Sircar

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We analyze an environment where the uncertainty in the equity market return and its volatility are both stochastic, and may be potentially disconnected. We solve a representative investor's optimal asset allocation and derive the resulting conditional equity premium and risk-free rate in equilibrium. Our empirical analysis shows that the equity premium appears to be earned for facing uncertainty, especially high uncertainty that is disconnected from lower volatility, rather than for facing volatility as traditionally assumed. Incorporating the possibility of a disconnect between volatility and uncertainty significantly improves portfolio performance, over and above the performance obtained by conditioning on volatility only.

中文摘要

我们分析了一个环境，其中股票市场回报的不确定性及其波动性都是随机的，并且可能脱节。我们求解了代表性投资者的最优资产配置，并推导出均衡中的条件性股权溢价和无风险利率。我们的实证分析表明，股权溢价似乎是为了面对不确定性而获得的，特别是与较低波动性脱节的高不确定性，而不是像传统假设的那样为了面对波动性。纳入波动性与不确定性之间脱节的可能性显著改善了投资组合绩效，超过了仅以波动性为条件的绩效。

No. 9论文详情NBER w29194

Global Agricultural Value Chains and Structural Transformation

全球农业价值链与结构转型

Sunghun Lim

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Since the mid-1900s, agricultural global value chains (AGVCs) have grown rapidly and transformed the nature of agri-food production around the world. Little is known, however, about how participation in AGVCs changes the structure of participating economies. Using a constructed panel dataset from 155 countries for the period 1991-2015, I find that, in response to high AGVC participation, both GDP and employment shares in the agricultural and services sectors increase, and that both factors decrease in the manufacturing sector. Counter to conventional wisdom about structural transformation, I uncover evidence that modern agrarian economies are leapfrogging the manufacturing sector to directly develop their agriculture and services sectors through their participation in AGVCs.

中文摘要

自20世纪中期以来，农业全球价值链（AGVCs）迅速发展，并改变了全球农业食品生产的性质。然而，关于参与AGVCs如何改变参与经济体的结构，人们知之甚少。利用1991-2015年期间155个国家的面板数据集，我发现，面对较高的AGVC参与度，农业和服务业的GDP和就业份额均有所增加，而制造业的这两个因素均有所下降。与传统结构转型观点相反，我发现了证据表明，现代农业经济体正在跳过制造业部门，通过参与AGVCs直接发展其农业和服务业部门。

No. 10论文详情NBER w29193

Interest Rate Cuts vs. Stimulus Payments: An Equivalence Result

降息与刺激支付：一个等价性结果

Christian K. Wolf

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In a textbook New Keynesian model extended to allow for uninsurable household income risk, any path of inflation and output implementable via interest rate policy is similarly implementable through uniform lump-sum transfers ("stimulus checks"). A dual-mandate policymaker can thus use checks to perfectly substitute for conventional monetary policy when rates are constrained by a lower bound. In a quantitative heterogeneous-agent (HANK) model, the stimulus check policy that implements a given monetary allocation is well-characterized by a small number of measurable sufficient statistics. In the household cross-section, the transfer policy is associated with lower consumption inequality than the equivalent rate cut.

中文摘要

在一个扩展到包含不可保险家庭收入风险的教科书式新凯恩斯模型中，任何可通过利率政策实现的通胀和产出路径，同样可以通过统一的总额转移支付（“刺激支票”）来实现。因此，当利率受到下限约束时，双重使命的政策制定者可以使用刺激支票完美替代传统货币政策。在一个量化的异质性主体（HANK）模型中，实施给定货币配置的刺激支票政策，可以通过少量可测量的充分统计量很好地刻画。在家庭横截面中，转移支付政策与等效的降息相比，与更低的消费不平等相关。

Using Household Rosters from Survey Data to Estimate All-cause Mortality during COVID in India

利用调查数据中的家庭名册估算印度新冠期间的全因死亡率

Anup Malani, Sabareesh Ramachandran

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 印度

Abstract

Official statistics on deaths due to COVID undercount deaths due to lack of testing. In developed countries, death registries have been used to measure total excess death during the pandemic. However, very few developing countries have even partial death registries or the capacity to register deaths during a pandemic. In this paper we estimate excess deaths in India using the member roster of a large and representative household panel survey. We estimate roughly 6.3 million excess deaths during the pandemic through August 2021. We cannot demonstrate causality between COVID and deaths but the timing and age structure of deaths is consistent with the COVID pandemic and excess deaths are positively correlated with reported infections. Finally, we find that excess deaths were higher among higher-income persons and were negatively associated with mobility. The methods in this paper can be used in countries with a household panel to measure health-related demographic indicators.

Our contribution to the literature is two-fold. First, the model helps explain why food waste and shortages may occur with dramatic shifts in consumer demand. Second, this paper provides a new angle on evaluating the various mitigation strategies and policy responses to disruptions beyond Covid-19.

中文摘要

在新冠疫情期间，食品供应链经历了来自需求和供给两方面的重大中断。虽然一些后果（如食物浪费）直接由供应链低效导致的中断引起，但其他后果则间接由消费者偏好的变化引起。因此，评估食品供应链韧性是一项艰巨的任务。为了理解需求对食品供应链的影响，我们基于爱达荷州土豆供应链案例开发了一个基于主体的模型。结果表明，不仅需求冲击的幅度，而且其时机都会对供应链的不同利益相关者产生不同影响。我们对文献的贡献有两方面。首先，该模型有助于解释为什么消费者需求的剧烈变化可能导致食物浪费和短缺。其次，本文为评估超越新冠疫情的中断的各种缓解策略和政策应对提供了新视角。

Should Workplace Programs be Voluntary or Mandatory? Evidence from a Field Experiment on Mentorship

工作场所项目应该是自愿的还是强制性的？来自导师制田野实验的证据

Jason Sandvik, Richard Saouma, Nathan Seegert, Christopher T. Stanton

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In a field experiment, we find large differences in productivity treatment effects between voluntary and mandatory workplace mentorship programs. A significant portion of this difference is due to the best employees opting into the program when it is voluntary and these employees having the smallest treatment effects. Our findings suggest that pilot programs run on a voluntary group may obfuscate large potential gains. In our setting, the firm cannot rely on self-selection to help with program allocation because employees that benefit the most from the program are the least likely to participate. Our findings have implications for program evaluation, experimental design, productivity dispersion, and inequality.

中文摘要

在一项田野实验中，我们发现自愿性和强制性工作场所导师制项目在生产率处理效应上存在巨大差异。这种差异的很大一部分是由于当项目是自愿时，最优秀的员工会选择参与，而这些员工的处理效应最小。我们的研究结果表明，在自愿群体中进行的试点项目可能会掩盖巨大的潜在收益。在我们的设定中，企业不能依赖自我选择来帮助项目分配，因为从项目中获益最多的员工最不可能参与。我们的发现对项目评估、实验设计、生产率差异和不平等具有启示意义。
