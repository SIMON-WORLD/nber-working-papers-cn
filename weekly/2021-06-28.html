What Drives Demand for State-Run Lotteries? Evidence and Welfare Implications

州营彩票需求驱动因素：证据与福利影响

Benjamin Lockwood, Hunt Allcott, Dmitry Taubinsky, Afras Y. Sial

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use natural experiments embedded in state-run lotteries and a new nationally representative survey to provide reduced-form and structural estimates of risk preferences and behavioral biases in lottery demand. We ﬁnd that sales respond more to the expected value of the jackpot than to price, but are unresponsive to variation in the second prize—a pattern that implies probability weighting but is inconsistent with standard parameterizations. In the survey, we ﬁnd that lottery spending decreases modestly with income and is strongly associated with measures of innumeracy, poor statistical reasoning, and other proxies for behavioral bias. These bias proxies decline with income and statistically account for 43 percent of lottery purchases, suggesting that at least some of lottery demand is due to behavioral bias, not just anticipatory utility or entertainment value. We use these empirical moments to estimate a model of socially optimal lottery design. In the model, current multi-state lottery designs increase welfare but may harm heavy spenders.

中文摘要

我们利用州营彩票中的自然实验以及一项新的全国代表性调查，对彩票需求中的风险偏好和行为偏差提供了简化形式和结构性估计。研究发现，销售额对头奖预期价值的反应大于对价格的敏感度，但对二等奖的变化不敏感——这一模式意味着概率加权，但与标准参数化不一致。在调查中，我们发现彩票支出随收入增加而适度下降，并与计算能力不足、统计推理能力差以及其他行为偏差指标密切相关。这些偏差指标随收入增加而下降，并在统计上解释了43%的彩票购买行为，这表明至少部分彩票需求源于行为偏差，而不仅仅是预期效用或娱乐价值。我们利用这些实证矩来估计一个社会最优彩票设计模型。在该模型中，当前的多州彩票设计提高了福利，但可能损害高支出者利益。

Risk Perceptions, Board Networks, and Directors’ Monitoring

风险感知、董事会网络与董事监督

Wenzhi Ding, Chen Lin, Thomas Schmid, Michael S. Weisbach

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价

Abstract

What makes independent directors perform their monitoring duty? One possible reason is that they are worried about being sanctioned by regulators if they do not monitor sufficiently well. Using unique features of the Chinese financial market, we estimate the extent to which independent directors’ perceptions of the likelihood of receiving a regulatory penalty affect their monitoring. Our results suggest that they are more likely to vote against management after observing how another director in their board network received a regulatory penalty related to negligence. This effect is long-lasting and stronger if the observing and penalized directors share the same professional background or gender and if the observing director is at a firm that is more likely to be penalized. These results provide direct evidence suggesting that the possibility of receiving penalties is an important factor motivating directors.

中文摘要

是什么促使独立董事履行其监督职责？一个可能的原因是，他们担心如果监督不力会受到监管机构的处罚。利用中国金融市场的独特特征，我们估计了独立董事对受到监管处罚可能性的感知对其监督行为的影响程度。结果表明，在观察到董事会网络中另一位董事因疏忽而受到监管处罚后，他们更有可能投票反对管理层。这种影响是持久的，并且当观察董事与受罚董事具有相同职业背景或性别时，以及当观察董事所在公司更有可能受到处罚时，这种影响更强。这些结果提供了直接证据，表明受到处罚的可能性是激励董事的重要因素。

Does It Matter How Central Banks Accumulate Reserves? Evidence from Sovereign Spreads

央行积累储备的方式重要吗？来自主权利差的证据

César Sosa-Padilla, Federico Sturzenegger

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

There has been substantial research on the benefits of accumulating foreign reserves, but less on the relative merits of how these reserves are accumulated. In this paper we explore whether the form of accumulation affects country risk. We first present a model of endogenous sovereign debt defaults, where we show that reserve accumulation through the issuance of debt contingent on local output reduces spreads in a way that reserve accumulation with foreign borrowing does not. We confirm this model prediction when taking the theory to the data. These results suggest that attention should be placed on the way reserves are accumulated, a distinction that has important practical implications. In particular, our results call into question the benefits of programs of reserves strengthening through external debt such as those typically implemented by multilateral organizations.

中文摘要

关于积累外汇储备的好处已有大量研究，但关于这些储备积累方式的相对优劣却研究较少。本文探讨了积累形式是否会影响国家风险。我们首先提出了一个内生主权债务违约模型，在该模型中，我们表明通过发行依赖于本地产出的债务来积累储备，会以外国借款积累储备所没有的方式降低利差。在将理论应用于数据时，我们证实了这一模型预测。这些结果表明，应关注储备积累的方式，这一区别具有重要的实际意义。特别是，我们的结果对通过外部债务加强储备的计划（例如多边组织通常实施的那些计划）的好处提出了质疑。

Women Left Behind: Gender Disparities in Utilization of Government Health Insurance in India

被遗忘的女性：印度政府健康保险利用中的性别差异

Pascaline Dupas, Radhika Jain

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 印度

Abstract

We document large gender disparities within a government program that entitles 46 million poor individuals to free hospital care. We show that care is not free in practice and higher costs are associated with larger disparities. Lowering care costs increases female utilization but does not reduce gender disparities because marginal beneficiaries are as likely to be male as inframarginals. Long-term exposure to local female leaders reduces disparities by addressing factors lowering female care. In the presence of gender bias, subsidizing social services may fail to address gender inequalities without actions that specifically target females.

中文摘要

我们记录了一个政府项目内存在的巨大性别差异，该项目为4600万贫困人口提供免费住院治疗。我们表明，在实践中护理并非免费，更高的成本与更大的差异相关。降低护理成本会增加女性的利用率，但不会减少性别差异，因为边际受益者与边际内受益者一样可能是男性。长期接触地方女性领导者通过降低影响女性护理的因素来减少差异。在存在性别偏见的情况下，如果没有专门针对女性的行动，补贴社会服务可能无法解决性别不平等问题。

Delayed Creative Destruction: How Uncertainty Shapes Corporate Assets

延迟的创造性破坏：不确定性如何塑造企业资产

Murillo Campello, Gaurav Kankanhalli, Hyunseob Kim

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 马里、索马里

Abstract

We show how uncertainty shapes corporate asset allocation, composition, and productivity using data from the shipping industry. Firms curtail both ship acquisitions and disposals when uncertainty increases, primarily through cuts in new ship orders and ship demolitions—decisions that are costlier to reverse vis-à-vis secondary market transactions. Uncertainty also prompts firms to concentrate their fleets into narrower, less productive portfolios. We corroborate our findings using the 2009-2011 spike in Somali pirate attacks as an uncertainty shock to shipping activity. Uncertainty hampers “creative destruction,” slowing both the adoption of innovation embodied in new capital and the disposal of old capital.

中文摘要

我们利用航运业的数据展示了不确定性如何塑造企业资产配置、构成和生产率。当不确定性增加时，企业会同时减少船舶购置和处置，主要通过削减新船订单和船舶拆解——这些决策相对于二级市场交易而言逆转成本更高。不确定性还促使企业将其船队集中到更狭窄、生产率更低的组合中。我们利用2009-2011年索马里海盗袭击事件作为航运活动的不确定性冲击，证实了我们的发现。不确定性阻碍了“创造性破坏”，既减缓了体现在新资本中的创新的采用，也减缓了旧资本的处置。

Leverage and Cash Dynamics

杠杆与现金动态

Harry DeAngelo, Andrei S. Gonçalves, René M. Stulz

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper documents new and empirically important interactions between cash-balance and leverage dynamics. Cash ratios typically vary widely over extended horizons, with dynamics remarkably similar to (and complementary with) those of capital structure. Leverage and cash dynamics interact approximately as predicted by the internal-versus-external funding regimes in Myers and Majluf (1984). Leverage is quite volatile when cash ratios are stable and vice-versa, while net-debt ratios are almost always volatile. Most firms increase leverage sharply as cash balances (internal funds) become scarce. Capital structure models that extend Hennessy and Whited (2005) to include cash-balance dynamics explain some, but not all, aspects of the observed relation between cash squeezes and leverage increases.

中文摘要

本文记录了现金余额与杠杆动态之间新的且实证上重要的相互作用。现金比率通常在较长时间范围内变化很大，其动态与资本结构的动态非常相似（且互补）。杠杆和现金动态的相互作用大致如Myers和Majluf（1984）中内部与外部融资机制所预测的那样。当现金比率稳定时，杠杆波动很大，反之亦然，而净债务比率几乎总是波动很大。当现金余额（内部资金）变得稀缺时，大多数公司会急剧增加杠杆。将Hennessy和Whited（2005）扩展到包括现金余额动态的资本结构模型，解释了观察到的现金紧缩与杠杆增加之间关系的一些方面，但并非全部。

Size, Trade, Technology and the Division of Labor

规模、贸易、技术与劳动分工

Nuno Limão, Yang Xu

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The division of labor is limited by the extent of the market — but the extent of the market also grows with the division of labor (Young, 1928). We formalize this feedback in a model with input-output linkages where heterogeneous firms deepen their specialization by adopting intermediate-intensive technologies. We document that intermediate intensity for U.S. manufacturing firms varies within industries and over time, and is increasing in firm size, which is the heterogeneity the model generates. Market size expansion increases specialization and (i) real income, through both firm selection and an endogenous aggregate input-output multiplier; (ii) the aggregate intermediate-to-labor cost share; and (iii) profit concentration. Calibrating to U.S. manufacturing over 1987–2007, we find that reduced trade costs expand market size and generate a quantitatively important adoption mechanism even when labor and intermediates substitute directly through CES production. The under-adoption inefficiency in this model is ameliorated by industrial subsidies, which act as substitutes to trade liberalization in increasing specialization.

中文摘要

我们对古典思想进行了建模，即更大的市场允许更精细的劳动分工，而这种分工又反过来促进更大的市场规模。市场规模通过企业层面的规模报酬递增影响专业化，这种规模报酬递增源于采用中间密集型技术的固定成本。在一般均衡中，这些影响通过一个内生乘数（由于循环结构中的投入产出联系）和一个由于异质性基础生产率和进入成本而产生的选择效应而被放大。

Evaluating the Role of Insurance in Managing Risk of Future Pandemics

评估保险在管理未来大流行风险中的作用

Howard Kunreuther, Jason Schupp

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

COVID-19 has demonstrated the challenges that policymakers, insurers, businesses, and employees face when disaster assistance programs are developed after the pandemic has already started. There is now an opportunity to design and implement effective and efficient solutions to manage the financial risks of a future pandemic. This paper suggests a practical framework, informed by the recent experience with COVID-19, for defining a meaningful role for insurance in managing business interruption (BI) and other risks from future pandemics. Policymakers, regulators, businesses, and other stakeholders interacting with representatives from the insurance industry can assist in defining its role in providing protection against the financial consequences of future pandemics. This framework, while designed for dealing with future pandemics, may be applied to other catastrophic and systemic risks.

中文摘要

COVID-19已经展示了当灾难援助计划在大流行已经开始后才制定时，政策制定者、保险公司、企业和员工所面临的挑战。现在有机会设计和实施有效且高效的解决方案，以管理未来大流行的财务风险。本文基于近期COVID-19的经验，提出了一个实用框架，用于定义保险在管理未来大流行的业务中断（BI）和其他风险中的有意义角色。政策制定者、监管机构、企业以及其他与保险业代表互动的利益相关者，可以帮助定义其在提供针对未来大流行财务后果的保护方面的作用。该框架虽然是为应对未来大流行而设计的，但也可应用于其他灾难性和系统性风险。

In Search of the Origins of Financial Fluctuations: The Inelastic Markets Hypothesis

探寻金融波动的根源：无弹性市场假说

Xavier Gabaix, Ralph S. J. Koijen

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop a framework to theoretically and empirically analyze the fluctuations of the aggregate stock market. Households allocate capital to institutions, which are fairly constrained, for example operating with a mandate to maintain a fixed equity share or with moderate scope for variation in response to changing market conditions. As a result, the price elasticity of demand of the aggregate stock market is small, and flows in and out of the stock market have large impacts on prices.

中文摘要

我们构建了一个理论框架，用于从理论和实证角度分析整体股票市场的波动。家庭将资本配置给机构，这些机构受到相当严格的约束，例如，它们可能被要求维持固定的股权份额，或者根据市场条件变化进行调整的余地有限。因此，整体股票市场的需求价格弹性较小，资金流入和流出股票市场会对价格产生重大影响。

Who Paid Los Angeles' Minimum Wage? A Side-by-Side Minimum Wage Experiment in Los Angeles County

谁为洛杉矶的最低工资买单？洛杉矶县的一项并行最低工资实验

Christopher Esposito, Edward E. Leamer, Jerry Nickelsburg

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In the restaurant industry, the incidence of an increase in the minimum wage may fall on restaurant owners, customers, landlords, and/or employees. We analyze the first two in this study, with implications for the incidence borne by landlords and employees. We exploit a geographical discontinuity in Los Angeles County, where in 2015 the City of Los Angeles passed a minimum wage law and in 2016 the State of California passed a different minimum wage law. This created two minimum wage schedules in the county that remained unequal for over five years. Using a novel data set from a multi-year price survey, our analysis shows that the incidence of Los Angeles City’s higher minimum wage fell on customers in high-income neighborhoods, and on landlords and restaurant owners in low-income neighborhoods. We further show that the mix of responses at restaurants subject to the LA City minimum wage, including price increases, menu changes, and restaurant closures, was affected by proximity to restaurants subject to the lower California State minimum wage. The effect of neighborhood income levels and distance to lower-wage competition has important implications for designing minimum wage policies.

Abstract

Mussa Puzzle Redux

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

The Mussa (1986) puzzle is the observation of a sharp and simultaneous increase in the volatility of both nominal and real exchange rates following the end of the Bretton Woods System of pegged exchange rates in 1973. It is commonly viewed as a central piece of evidence in favor of monetary non-neutrality because it is an instance in which a change in the monetary regime caused a dramatic change in the equilibrium behavior of a real variable (the real exchange rate) and is often further interpreted as direct evidence in favor of models with nominal rigidities in price setting. This paper shows that the data do not support this latter conclusion because there was no simultaneous change in the properties of the other macro variables, nominal or real. We show that an extended set of Mussa facts equally falsifies both conventional flexible-price RBC models and sticky-price New Keynesian models as explanations for the Mussa puzzle. We present a resolution to the broader Mussa puzzle based on a model of segmented financial market — a particular type of financial friction by which the bulk of the nominal exchange rate risk is held by financial intermediaries and is not shared smoothly throughout the economy. We argue that rather than discriminating between models with sticky versus flexible prices, or monetary versus productivity shocks, the Mussa puzzle provides sharp evidence in favor of models with monetary non-neutrality arising in the financial market, suggesting the importance of monetary transmission via the risk premium channel.
