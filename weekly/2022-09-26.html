Zoning for Profits: How Public Finance Shapes Land Supply in China

为利润而分区：公共财政如何塑造中国的土地供应

Zhiguo He, Scott T. Nelson, Yang Su, Anthony Lee Zhang, Fudong Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收

Abstract

Public finance and real estate are uniquely intertwined in China, where local governments also serve as monopolist sellers of land. We shed new light on how land sale decisions and land prices depend on local governments’ financing objectives. First, we document the large (ten-fold) price premium paid for residential-zoned relative to industrial-zoned land and show this price premium can be explained by the greater future tax revenues generated by industrial land; the choice to sell land as industrial rather than residential generates an IRR of 7.70%, which is comparable to local governments’ cost of capital in bond markets. Second, local governments are sensitive to financing constraints: industrial land supply decreases with governments’ bond yields. Third, local governments’ land sales are sensitive to the intergovernmental tax sharing, such that industrial land sales increase with the share of taxes captured by local governments. Thus, shocks to local public finances can be expected to affect the Chinese real estate market and vice versa.

中文摘要

在中国，公共财政与房地产有着独特的交织关系，地方政府同时也是土地的垄断卖方。我们揭示了土地出售决策和土地价格如何依赖于地方政府的融资目标。首先，我们记录了住宅用地相对于工业用地所支付的大额（十倍）价格溢价，并表明这一价格溢价可以用工业用地产生的未来税收收入更高来解释；选择将土地作为工业用地而非住宅用地出售，会产生7.70%的内部收益率，这与地方政府在债券市场上的资本成本相当。其次，地方政府对融资约束很敏感：工业用地供应随着政府债券收益率的上升而减少。第三，地方政府的土地销售对政府间税收分成敏感，因此工业用地销售随着地方政府截留的税收份额增加而增加。因此，地方公共财政的冲击预计会影响中国的房地产市场，反之亦然。

Macroeconomic and Asset Pricing Effects of Supply Chain Disasters

供应链灾难的宏观经济与资产定价效应

Vladimir Smirnyagin, Aleh Tsyvinski

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We build a general equilibrium production-based asset pricing model with heterogeneous firms that jointly accounts for firm-level and aggregate facts emphasized by the recent macroeconomic literature, and for important asset pricing moments. Using administrative firm-level data, we establish empirical properties of large negative idiosyncratic shocks and their evolution. We then demonstrate that these shocks play an important role for delivering both macroeconomic and asset pricing predictions. Finally, we combine our model with data on the universe of U.S. seaborne import since 2007, and establish the importance of supply chain disasters for the cross-section of asset prices.

中文摘要

我们构建了一个包含异质性企业的生产型一般均衡资产定价模型，该模型同时解释了近期宏观经济文献所强调的企业层面和总体事实，以及重要的资产定价矩。利用行政企业层面数据，我们建立了大型负面异质性冲击及其演变的经验特征。然后，我们证明这些冲击在提供宏观经济和资产定价预测方面都发挥着重要作用。最后，我们将模型与2007年以来美国所有海运进口数据相结合，确立了供应链灾难对资产价格横截面差异的重要性。

The U.S. Manufacturing Sector’s Response to Higher Electricity Prices: Evidence from State-Level Renewable Portfolio Standards

美国制造业对更高电价的反应：来自州级可再生能源组合标准的证据

Ann Wolverton, Ronald Shadbegian, Wayne B. Gray

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

While several papers examine the effects of renewable portfolio standards (RPS) on electricity prices, they mainly rely on state-level data and there has been little research on how RPS policies affect manufacturing activity via their effect on electricity prices. Using plant-level data for the entire U.S. manufacturing sector and all electric utilities from 1992 – 2015, we jointly estimate the effect of RPS adoption and stringency on plant-level electricity prices and production decisions. To ensure that our results are not sensitive to possible pre-existing differences across manufacturing plants in RPS and non-RPS states, we implement coarsened exact covariate matching. Our results suggest that electricity prices for plants in RPS states averaged about 2% higher than in non-RPS states, notably lower than prior estimates based on state-level data. In response to these higher electricity prices, we estimate that plant electricity usage declined by 1.2% for all plants and 1.8% for energy-intensive plants, broadly consistent with published estimates of the elasticity of electricity demand for industrial users. We find smaller declines in output, employment, and hours worked (relative to the decline in electricity use). Finally, several key RPS policy design features that vary substantially from state-to-state produce heterogeneous effects on plant-level electricity prices.

中文摘要

虽然有几篇论文研究了可再生能源组合标准（RPS）对电价的影响，但它们主要依赖州级数据，并且关于RPS政策如何通过影响电价进而影响制造业活动的研究很少。利用1992年至2015年整个美国制造业和所有电力公用事业的企业层面数据，我们联合估计了RPS采纳及其严格程度对企业层面电价和生产决策的影响。为了确保我们的结果不受RPS州和非RPS州制造业企业之间可能存在的预先存在差异的影响，我们实施了粗化精确匹配。我们的结果表明，RPS州企业的电价平均比非RPS州高出约2%，显著低于先前基于州级数据的估计。作为对这些更高电价的回应，我们估计所有企业的用电量下降了1.2%，能源密集型企业的用电量下降了1.8%，这与已发表的工业用户电力需求弹性估计值大致一致。我们发现产出、就业和工作时间的下降幅度（相对于用电量的下降）较小。最后，各州之间差异很大的几个关键RPS政策设计特征对企业层面的电价产生了异质性影响。

Managing Public Portfolios

管理公共投资组合

Leo R. Aparisi de Lannoy, Anmol Bhandari, David Evans, Mikhail Golosov, Thomas J. Sargent

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We develop a unified framework for optimally managing public portfolios for a class of macro-finance models that include widely-used specifications for households' risk and liquidity preferences, market structures for financial assets, and trading frictions. An optimal portfolio hedges fluctuations in interest rates, primary surpluses, liquidities and inequalities. It recognizes liquidity benefits that government debts provide and internalizes equilibrium effects of public policies on financial asset prices. We express an optimal portfolio in terms of statistics that are functions only of macro and financial market data. An application to the U.S. shows that hedging interest rate risk plays a dominant role in shaping an optimal maturity structure of government debt.

中文摘要

我们为一个宏观金融模型类别开发了一个最优管理公共投资组合的统一框架，该模型类别包括家庭风险和流动性偏好、金融资产市场结构以及交易摩擦的广泛使用的设定。一个最优投资组合对冲利率、初级盈余、流动性和不平等的波动。它认识到政府债务提供的流动性收益，并将公共政策对金融资产价格的均衡效应内部化。我们用仅依赖于宏观和金融市场数据的统计量来表达一个最优投资组合。一个应用于美国的例子表明，对冲利率风险在塑造政府债务的最优期限结构中起着主导作用。

Racial Diversity in Private Capital Fundraising

私募资本募资中的种族多样性

Johan Cassel, Josh Lerner, Emmanuel Yimfor

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 西班牙

Abstract

Black- and Hispanic-owned funds control a very modest share of assets in the private capital industry. We find that the sensitivity of follow-on fundraising to fund performance is greater for minority-owned groups, particularly for underperforming groups. We find little support for a number of explanations for these patterns: that minority fund valuations are overstated, that minority funds encounter difficulties in hiring personnel, or that deploying capital is more difficult for these funds. We do find that the ability of minority groups to raise capital increases during periods of high racial awareness and when the chief investment officer of local public pension plans and endowments are minorities. Together, the results support the hypothesis that the modest representation of Black and Hispanic-owned firms in private capital stems at least partially from the nature of investor demand, rather than the supply of fund managers.

中文摘要

黑人和西班牙裔拥有的基金在私募资本行业中控制的资产份额非常有限。我们发现，后续募资对基金业绩的敏感性在少数族裔拥有的集团中更大，特别是对于业绩不佳的集团。我们几乎没有发现支持这些模式的一些解释的证据：少数族裔基金的估值被夸大，少数族裔基金在招聘人员方面遇到困难，或者这些基金部署资本更加困难。我们确实发现，在种族意识高涨时期，以及当地方公共养老金计划和捐赠基金的首席投资官是少数族裔时，少数族裔群体筹集资金的能力会提高。总之，这些结果支持了以下假设：黑人和西班牙裔拥有的公司在私募资本中的代表性不足至少部分源于投资者需求的性质，而不是基金经理的供给。

No. 6论文详情NBER w30499

Family Bargaining with Altruism

具有利他主义的家庭谈判

Robert A. Pollak

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The theoretical literature on bargaining ignores altruism and assumes that everyone is an egoist. Because the importance of altruism in the family is widely recognized, the egoism assumption is especially problematic in the economics of the family. This paper shows that incorporating altruism into cooperative bargaining models shrinks the set potential cooperative bargaining solutions. The analysis depends on the implications of altruism for Pareto efficiency and the implications of Pareto efficiency for potential cooperative bargaining solutions. The analysis here applies not only to Nash bargaining but to all cooperative bargaining models. For noncooperative bargaining, the analysis implies that any solution that lies outside the shrunken set of potential cooperative bargaining solutions is not Pareto efficient.

中文摘要

关于谈判的理论文献忽略了利他主义，并假设每个人都是利己主义者。由于利他主义在家庭中的重要性被广泛认可，利己主义假设在家庭经济学中尤其成问题。本文表明，将利他主义纳入合作谈判模型会缩小潜在合作谈判解决方案的集合。该分析取决于利他主义对帕累托效率的影响以及帕累托效率对潜在合作谈判解决方案的影响。这里的分析不仅适用于纳什谈判，而且适用于所有合作谈判模型。对于非合作谈判，该分析意味着任何位于缩小后的潜在合作谈判解决方案集合之外的解决方案都不是帕累托有效的。

Demand Stimulus as Social Policy

作为社会政策的需求刺激

Alan J. Auerbach, Yuriy Gorodnichenko, Daniel Murphy

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We exploit a panel of city-level data with rich demographic information to estimate the distributional effects of Department of Defense spending and its effects on a range of social outcomes. The income generated by defense spending accrues predominantly to households without a bachelor’s degree. These households as well as Black households tend to disproportionately benefit from this spending. Defense spending also promotes a range of beneficial social outcomes that are often targeted by government programs, including reductions in poverty, divorce rates, disability rates, and mortality rates, as well as increases in homeownership, health insurance rates, and occupational prestige. We compare the effects of defense spending with the effects of general demand shocks and explore reasons for the differential effects of the shocks.

中文摘要

我们利用一个包含丰富人口统计信息的城市级数据面板，来估计国防部支出的分配效应及其对一系列社会结果的影响。国防支出产生的收入主要归属于没有学士学位的家庭。这些家庭以及黑人家庭往往不成比例地从这项支出中受益。国防支出还促进了一系列有益的社会结果，这些结果通常是政府项目的目标，包括减少贫困、离婚率、残疾率和死亡率，以及提高住房拥有率、医疗保险覆盖率和职业声望。我们将国防支出的影响与一般需求冲击的影响进行了比较，并探讨了这些冲击产生不同效应的原因。

Trade-ins and Transaction Costs in the Market for Used Business Jets

二手公务机市场中的以旧换新与交易成本

Charles Hodgson

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Manufacturers of durable goods can encourage consumers facing transaction costs to upgrade by accepting used units as trade-ins. These “buyback schemes” increase demand for new units, but increase the supply of used units if trade-ins are resold. In this paper, I investigate the equilibrium effects of buyback schemes in the market for business jets. I find that buyback increases demand for new units by 37% at fixed prices. However, in equilibrium this increase in sales is diminished by 38% due to substitution away from new jets among first time buyers. Because of this cannibalization, offering buyback is a dominant strategy for only 3 of the 6 major firms, with 3 firms offering buyback as a best response to other firms’ policies. I show that equilibrium buyback policies can change under counterfactual market structures: a simulated merger leads to a reduction in consumer welfare, 70% of which is due to a change in buyback policy.

中文摘要

耐用品制造商可以通过接受旧单位作为以旧换新，来鼓励面临交易成本的消费者进行升级。这些“回购计划”增加了对新单位的需求，但如果以旧换新的产品被转售，则会增加旧单位的供应。在本文中，我研究了公务机市场中回购计划的均衡效应。我发现，在固定价格下，回购使对新单位的需求增加了37%。然而，在均衡状态下，由于首次购买者对新飞机的替代，这种销售增长被削弱了38%。由于这种蚕食效应，提供回购仅在6家主要公司中的3家是占优策略，而另外3家公司则将提供回购作为对其他公司政策的最佳回应。我表明，在反事实的市场结构下，均衡回购政策可能会发生变化：模拟的合并导致消费者福利减少，其中70%是由于回购政策的变化。

No. 9论文详情NBER w30496

Sparse Production Networks

稀疏生产网络

Andrew B. Bernard, Yuan Zi

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Firm-to-firm connections in domestic and international production networks play a fundamental role in economic outcomes. Firm heterogeneity and the sparse nature of firm-to-firm connections implicitly discipline network structure. We find that a large group of well-established statistical relationships are not useful in improving our understanding of production networks. We propose an ``elementary" model for production networks based on random matching and firm heterogeneity and characterize the families of statistics and data generating processes that may raise underidentification concerns in more complex models. The elementary model is a useful benchmark in developing ``instructive" statistics and informing model construction and selection.

中文摘要

国内和国际生产网络中企业间的联系在经济结果中发挥着基础性作用。企业异质性和企业间联系的稀疏性隐含地约束了网络结构。我们发现，大量已确立的统计关系无助于增进我们对生产网络的理解。我们提出了一个基于随机匹配和企业异质性的生产网络“基础”模型，并刻画了在更复杂模型中可能引发欠识别问题的统计量族和数据生成过程。该基础模型是开发“指导性”统计量以及指导模型构建与选择的有用基准。

No. 10论文详情NBER w30495

Land Rental Markets: Experimental Evidence from Kenya

土地租赁市场：来自肯尼亚的实验证据

Michelle Acampora, Lorenzo Casaburi, Jack Willis

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 肯尼亚

Abstract

Do land market frictions cause misallocation in agriculture? In a field experiment in Western Kenya, we randomly subsidize owners to rent out land. Transferring cultivation rights to renters increases output and value added on the plots, consistent with imperfect land markets and misallocation, and induced rentals persist after the subsidy ends. Additional analysis provides insights on the magnitude and nature of land frictions—which include search, risks, and learning—and on the sources of gains from trade—which include differences between owners and renters in crop choices, productivity, and financial market constraints, but not in labor constraints.

中文摘要

土地市场摩擦是否导致农业中的资源错配？在肯尼亚西部的一项实地实验中，我们随机补贴土地所有者以出租土地。将耕作权转移给租户增加了地块的产出和增加值，这与不完全土地市场和资源错配相一致，并且补贴结束后诱导的租赁行为得以持续。进一步分析提供了关于土地摩擦（包括搜索、风险和学习）的规模和性质，以及贸易收益来源（包括所有者和租户在作物选择、生产率和金融市场约束方面的差异，但不包括劳动力约束）的见解。

Multinationals and Structural Transformation

跨国公司与结构转型

Vanessa I. Alviarez, Cheng Chen, Nitya Pandalai-Nayar, Liliana Varela, Kei-Mu Yi, Hongyong Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 日本

Abstract

We study how multinational corporations (MNCs) shape firm-level and aggregate structural transformation. Using confidential microdata from Japan and exploiting a quasi-exogenous reform that expanded foreign investment opportunities in China, we assess empirically how this reform affected employment at firms in both the host country (China) and the home country (Japan). In liberalized industries, Japanese manufacturing affiliates in China expanded employment, while parent firms in Japan shifted out of manufacturing and into higher-value service activities, including R&D. To assess the broader relevance of this mechanism, we use microdata from several advanced and middle-income economies, and show that MNCs account for the majority of the middle-income countries’ reallocation to manufacturing.

中文摘要

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

We develop a quantitative theory of spatial structural change in which economic growth, sectoral reallocation, and regional development are jointly determined in equilibrium. Abstract

We analyze the offering, asking, and granting of help or other benefits as a three-stage game with bilateral private information between a person in need of help and a potential help-giver.
