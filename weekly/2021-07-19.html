A Signal to End Child Marriage: Theory and Experimental Evidence from Bangladesh

终结童婚的信号：来自孟加拉国的理论与实验证据

Nina Buchmann, Erica M. Field, Rachel Glennerster, Shahana Nazneen, Xiao Yu Wang

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 孟加拉国

Abstract

Child marriage remains common even where female schooling and employment opportunities have grown. We introduce a signaling model in which bride type is imperfectly observed but preferred types have lower returns to delaying marriage. We show that in this environment the market might pool on early marriage even when everyone would benefit from delay. In this setting, offering a small incentive can delay marriage of all treated types and untreated non-preferred types, while programs that act directly on norms can unintentionally encourage early marriage. We test these theoretical predictions by experimentally evaluating a financial incentive to delay marriage alongside a girls’ empowerment program designed to shift norms. As predicted, girls eligible for the incentive are 19% less likely to marry underage, as are nonpreferred type women ineligible for the incentive. Meanwhile, the empowerment program was successful in promoting more progressive gender norms but failed to decrease adolescent marriage and increased dowry payments.

中文摘要

即使在女性教育和就业机会增加的地方，童婚仍然普遍存在。我们引入了一个信号模型，其中新娘类型无法被完美观测，但偏好类型从延迟婚姻中获得的回报较低。我们证明，在这种环境下，即使所有人都能从延迟中受益，市场也可能聚集于早婚。在这种情况下，提供小额激励可以延迟所有受处理类型和未受处理的非偏好类型的婚姻，而直接作用于规范的项目则可能无意中鼓励早婚。我们通过实验评估了一项旨在延迟婚姻的财务激励措施，以及一个旨在转变规范的女孩赋权项目，来检验这些理论预测。正如预测的那样，有资格获得激励的女孩早婚的可能性降低了19%，没有资格获得激励的非偏好类型女性也是如此。与此同时，赋权项目成功地促进了更进步的性别规范，但未能减少青少年婚姻，反而增加了嫁妆支付。

Trade Policy

贸易政策

Lorenzo Caliendo, Fernando Parro

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

This chapter reviews a recent body of theoretical and empirical work that studies the normative and positive aspects of trade policy. We start by presenting reduced-form evidence of the eﬀects of trade policy in the presence of supply-chain linkages, on the short-run and persistent eﬀects of trade policy across local labor markets, and on the eﬀects of trade policy uncertainty on employment and ﬁrms. We describe the shift-share method for trade policy analysis, discuss the interpretation of the estimated eﬀects, and provide a theoretical foundation. We then describe new quantitative frameworks, methods, and data used to study the aggregate and distributional eﬀects of trade policy in general equilibrium. We discuss how to take into account supply-chain linkages, local labor markets, and diﬀerent sources of dynamics. As an illustration, we quantify the aggregate and distributional eﬀects of the 2018 trade war between the United States and its trading partners. Finally, we present recent theoretical insights on optimal unilateral trade policy with ﬁrm and product heterogeneity in the context of large and small open economies with perfectly and imperfectly competitive product markets. We also discuss how optimal trade policy is shaped by the presence of multiple sectors, intermediate goods, and supply-chain linkages. We close the chapter by discussing the scope of future research.

中文摘要

本章回顾了近期关于贸易政策规范性与实证性方面的理论与实证研究。我们首先展示了在供应链关联存在的情况下，贸易政策对短期及持续性影响的简化形式证据，这些影响涉及地方劳动力市场以及贸易政策不确定性对就业和企业的作用。我们描述了用于贸易政策分析的份额转移方法，讨论了估计效应的解释，并提供了理论基础。随后，我们介绍了用于研究一般均衡中贸易政策总体与分配效应的新量化框架、方法和数据。我们探讨了如何考虑供应链关联、地方劳动力市场以及不同的动态来源。作为示例，我们量化了2018年美国与其贸易伙伴之间贸易战的总体与分配效应。最后，我们提出了在大型和小型开放经济体中，面对完全竞争与不完全竞争产品市场，考虑企业与产品异质性时的最优单边贸易政策的最新理论见解。我们还讨论了多部门、中间品和供应链关联如何影响最优贸易政策。本章结尾探讨了未来研究的范围。

Macroeconomic Misery by Levels of Income in America

美国不同收入水平的宏观经济痛苦指数

Martin Ravallion

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Thirty years of distributional data are used to study the short-term impacts of popular macroeconomic indicators on real household incomes from the poorest to the richest Americans. The appropriate weights on unemployment versus inflation vary across the distribution. The unemployment rate matters at all levels, but especially so for the poorest. Inflation rates matter at middle incomes, though Okun’s famous Misery Index only performs well for the top income groups. GDP growth matters at all levels and proportionately more for the poorest, though only via the unemployment rate. Recessions are poverty-increasing, and skewness-decreasing, but with ambiguous effects on overall inequality.

中文摘要

利用三十年的分配数据，研究主流宏观经济指标对从最贫困到最富裕美国居民实际家庭收入的短期影响。失业率与通胀率的适当权重在收入分布中有所不同。失业率对所有收入水平都有影响，但对最贫困群体影响尤为显著。通胀率对中等收入群体有影响，尽管奥肯著名的痛苦指数仅对高收入群体表现良好。GDP增长对所有收入水平都有影响，且对最贫困群体影响比例更大，但这仅通过失业率渠道实现。经济衰退会增加贫困并降低偏度，但对总体不平等的影响不明确。

The Damages and Distortions from Discrimination in the Rental Housing Market

租赁住房市场歧视造成的损害与扭曲

Peter Christensen, Christopher Timmins

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 西班牙、美国

Abstract

By constraining an individual's choice during a search, housing discrimination distorts sorting decisions away from true preferences and results in a ceteris paribus reduction in welfare. This study combines a large-scale field experiment with a residential sorting model to derive utility-theoretic measures of renter welfare loss associated with the constraints imposed by discrimination in the rental housing market. Results from experiments conducted in five cities show that key neighborhood amenities are associated with higher levels of discrimination. Counterfactual simulations based on the sorting model suggest that discrimination imposes damages equivalent to 4.4% and 3.5% of the annual incomes for African American and Hispanic/LatinX renters, respectively. Damages are increasing in income for African American renters, such that impacts become stronger for economically mobile households. Renters of color must make substantial investments in additional search to mitigate the costs of these constraints. We find that a naive model ignoring discrimination constraints yields biased estimates of willingness to pay for key neighborhood amenities.

中文摘要

住房歧视在搜索过程中限制了个人的选择，从而扭曲了基于真实偏好的分类决策，并在其他条件不变的情况下导致福利减少。本研究将大规模实地实验与居住分类模型相结合，推导出与租赁住房市场歧视所施加约束相关的租房者福利损失的效用理论度量。在五个城市进行的实验结果表明，关键社区便利设施与更高水平的歧视相关。基于分类模型的反事实模拟表明，歧视对非裔美国人和西班牙裔/拉丁裔租房者造成的损害分别相当于其年收入的4.4%和3.5%。对于非裔美国人租房者，损害随收入增加而增加，因此对经济流动性较强的家庭影响更大。有色人种租房者必须进行大量额外的搜索投资，以减轻这些约束的成本。我们发现，忽略歧视约束的朴素模型会得出对关键社区便利设施支付意愿的有偏估计。

Persuading Investors: A Video-Based Study

说服投资者：一项基于视频的研究

Allen Hu, Song Ma

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Persuasive communication functions not only through content but also delivery, e.g., facial expression, tone of voice, and diction. This paper examines the persuasiveness of delivery in start-up pitches. Using machine learning (ML) algorithms to process full pitch videos, we quantify persuasion in visual, vocal, and verbal dimensions. Positive (i.e., passionate, warm) pitches increase funding probability. Yet conditional on funding, high-positivity startups underperform. Women are more heavily judged on delivery when evaluating single-gender teams, but they are neglected when co-pitching with men in mixed-gender teams. Using an experiment, we show persuasion delivery works mainly through leading investors to form inaccurate beliefs.

中文摘要

有说服力的沟通不仅通过内容发挥作用，还通过表达方式，例如面部表情、语调以及措辞。本文研究了创业推介中表达方式的说服力。利用机器学习算法处理完整的推介视频，我们在视觉、声音和语言维度上量化了说服力。积极（即充满激情、温暖）的推介会增加融资概率。然而，在获得融资的条件下，高积极性的初创企业表现不佳。在评估单一性别团队时，女性在表达方式上受到更严格的评判，但在混合性别团队中与男性共同推介时，她们则被忽视。通过一项实验，我们表明说服性表达主要通过引导投资者形成不准确的信念来发挥作用。

Do Urgent Care Centers Reduce Medicare Spending?

紧急护理中心能否减少医疗保险支出？

Janet Currie, Anastasia Karpova, Dan Zeltzer

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine the impact of the opening of a new urgent care center (UCC) on health care costs and the utilization of care among nearby Medicare beneficiaries. We focus on 2006–2016, a period of rapid UCC expansion. We find that total Medicare spending rises when residents of a zip code are first served by a UCC, relative to spending in yet-to-be-served zip codes, while mortality remains flat. We explore mechanisms by looking at categories of spending and by examining utilization. Increases in inpatient visits are the largest contributor to the overall increase in spending, rising by 6.65 percent within six years after UCC entry. The number of emergency room visits that result in a hospital admission also increases by 3.7 percent. In contrast, there is no change in the number of ER visits that do not result in admission to hospital, in visits to physicians outside a UCC, or in imaging and tests. Overall, these results provide little evidence that UCCs replace costly ER visits or that they crowd out visits to patients' regular doctors. Instead, the evidence is consistent with the possibility that UCCs—which are increasingly owned by or contract with hospital systems—induce greater spending on hospital care.

中文摘要

我们研究了新开设的紧急护理中心对附近医疗保险受益人的医疗成本和服务利用的影响。我们重点关注2006年至2016年这段紧急护理中心快速扩张的时期。我们发现，当一个邮政编码区的居民首次获得紧急护理中心服务时，相对于尚未获得服务的邮政编码区，医疗保险总支出上升，而死亡率保持不变。我们通过考察支出类别和利用情况来探索其机制。住院就诊次数的增加是总支出增加的最大贡献因素，在紧急护理中心进入后的六年内增长了6.65%。导致入院的急诊就诊次数也增加了3.7%。相比之下，未导致入院的急诊就诊次数、紧急护理中心以外的医生就诊次数以及影像和检查次数没有变化。总体而言，这些结果几乎没有证据表明紧急护理中心取代了昂贵的急诊就诊，或者挤出了患者对常规医生的就诊。相反，证据与以下可能性一致：紧急护理中心——越来越多地被医院系统拥有或与之签约——导致了更多的住院护理支出。

Growing Pains? A Comment on “Converging to Convergence”

成长的烦恼？对“趋同于收敛”的评论

Rohini Pande, Nils T. Enevoldsen

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Early tests of cross-country convergence found evidence only for conditional convergence. In contrast, with more recent data, Kremer, Willis, and You (2021) find evidence that since the mid-1980s there has been a trend towards unconditional convergence culminating in absolute convergence since 2000. Additionally, they find suggestive evidence that one of the major drivers of this trend is an underlying convergence towards development-favored policies. We discuss the implications of this result through the lens of individual welfare and poverty, concluding that the news is not as welcome as it may seem for the world’s poor. We point out that absolute convergence has happened contemporaneously with rising within-country inequality, resulting in more of the world’s poor living in middle-income countries. Next, we argue that domestic redistribution is essential to spread the benefits from industrialization, since the labor share of manufacturing isn’t reaching the heights it did in industrialized countries. Finally, we argue that the democratic institutions that can facilitate this redistribution themselves face headwinds. Democratic backsliding, the Covid-19 pandemic, and a bleak climate outlook all present obstacles to transforming economic growth into economic justice for the poor.

中文摘要

早期的跨国收敛检验仅发现了条件收敛的证据。相比之下，利用更新的数据，Kremer、Willis和You（2021）发现证据表明自20世纪80年代中期以来，存在向无条件收敛的趋势，并最终在2000年后达到绝对收敛。此外，他们发现暗示性证据表明，这一趋势的主要驱动因素之一是向有利于发展的政策的潜在收敛。我们通过个人福利和贫困的视角讨论了这一结果的含义，得出的结论是，对世界贫困人口而言，这一消息并不像看起来那么受欢迎。我们指出，绝对收敛与国内不平等加剧同时发生，导致更多世界贫困人口生活在中等收入国家。其次，我们认为国内再分配对于传播工业化的收益至关重要，因为制造业的劳动力份额并未达到工业化国家曾经的高度。最后，我们认为能够促进这种再分配的民主制度本身也面临阻力。民主倒退、新冠疫情以及黯淡的气候前景，都为将经济增长转化为贫困人口的经济正义设置了障碍。

Place-Based Redistribution in Location Choice Models

区位选择模型中的基于地点的再分配

Morris Davis, Jesse M. Gregory

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

In recent location choice models, households randomly vary with respect to their utility of living in a location. We demonstrate the distribution generating this randomness is not identifiable from location choice data and the optimal allocation chosen by a social planner is not identified. We propose an algorithm for setting the distribution generating the random utility across locations that implies a planner will optimally choose no redistribution in the absence of externalities or equity motives between different types of people. 我们分析了意大利电视新闻广播的逐分钟、个体层面收视率数据（来自AUDITEL™），并与详细的内容数据（来自帕维亚观察站）进行了匹配。我们关注观众的行为，特别是他们根据当前观看的新闻故事类型而切换离开新闻节目的决定。有点令人惊讶的是，我们发现“软”新闻系统性地促使观众切换离开，甚至比“硬”新闻更甚。另一方面，关于犯罪、事故和灾难的耸人听闻的故事与较少的切换行为相关。我们还发现，这种切换行为在性别、年龄和所观看的具体电视频道方面存在显著差异。例如，与老年人相比，年轻人相对更可能从硬新闻切换离开，而不是从软新闻切换离开。
