Representation is Not Sufficient for Selecting Gender Diversity

代表性不足以选择性别多样性

Justus A. Baron, Bernhard Ganglmair, Nicola Persico, Timothy Simcoe, Emanuele Tarantino

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Representation of women and minorities in a “selectorate”—the group that chooses an organization's leaders—is a key mechanism for promoting diversity. We show that representation, on its own, is not sufficient for selecting gender diversity: a supportive organizational culture is also required. In the case of the Internet Engineering Task Force, a random increase in female representation in its selection committee caused an increase in female appointments only after cultural norms supporting diversity and inclusion became more salient.

中文摘要

在“选拔者群体”（即选择组织领导者的群体）中，女性和少数群体的代表性是促进多样性的关键机制。我们表明，仅凭代表性本身不足以选择性别多样性：还需要支持性的组织文化。以互联网工程任务组为例，其选拔委员会中女性代表性的随机增加，只有在支持多样性和包容性的文化规范变得更加突出时，才导致女性任命的增加。

Mammograms and Mortality: How Has the Evidence Evolved?

乳房X光检查与死亡率：证据如何演变？

Amanda E. Kowalski

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Decades of evidence reveal a complicated relationship between mammograms and mortality. Mammograms may detect deadly cancers early, but they may also lead to the diagnosis and potentially fatal treatment of cancers that would never progress to cause symptoms. I provide a brief history of the evidence on mammograms and mortality, focusing on evidence from clinical trials, and I discuss how this evidence informs mammography guidelines. I then explore the evolution of all-cause mortality relative to breast cancer mortality within an influential clinical trial. I conclude with some responses to the evolving evidence.

The Public Health Effects of Legalizing Marijuana

大麻合法化的公共卫生影响

D. Mark Anderson, Daniel I. Rees

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Thirty-six states have legalized medical marijuana and 14 states have legalized the use of marijuana for recreational purposes. In this paper, we review the literature on the public health consequences of legalizing marijuana, focusing on studies that have appeared in economics journals as well as leading public policy, public health, and medical journals. Among the outcomes considered are: youth marijuana use, alcohol consumption, the abuse of prescription opioids, traffic fatalities, and crime. For some of these outcomes, there is a near consensus in the literature regarding the effects of medical marijuana laws (MMLs). As an example, leveraging geographic and temporal variation in MMLs, researchers have produced little credible evidence to suggest that legalization promotes marijuana use among teenagers. Likewise, there is convincing evidence that young adults consume less alcohol when medical marijuana is legalized. For other public health outcomes such as mortality involving prescription opioids, the effect of legalizing medical marijuana has proven more difficult to gauge and, as a consequence, we are less comfortable drawing firm conclusions. Finally, it is not yet clear how legalizing marijuana for recreational purposes will affect these and other important public health outcomes. We will be able to draw stronger conclusions when more post-treatment data are collected in states that have recently legalized recreational marijuana.

中文摘要

36个州已将医用大麻合法化，14个州已将娱乐用大麻合法化。在本文中，我们回顾了关于大麻合法化公共卫生后果的文献，重点关注发表在经济学期刊以及领先的公共政策、公共卫生和医学期刊上的研究。考虑的结果包括：青少年大麻使用、酒精消费、处方阿片类药物滥用、交通死亡和犯罪。对于其中一些结果，文献中关于医用大麻法律影响的共识近乎一致。例如，利用医用大麻法律的地理和时间变化，研究人员几乎没有产生可信的证据表明合法化会促进青少年使用大麻。同样，有令人信服的证据表明，当医用大麻合法化时，年轻人饮酒减少。对于其他公共卫生结果，如涉及处方阿片类药物的死亡率，衡量医用大麻合法化的影响已被证明更加困难，因此我们不太愿意得出明确结论。最后，尚不清楚娱乐用大麻合法化将如何影响这些及其他重要的公共卫生结果。当在最近将娱乐用大麻合法化的州收集到更多处理后数据时，我们将能够得出更可靠的结论。

Policy Implications of Non-Linear Effects of Tax Changes on Output

税收变化对产出的非线性效应的政策含义

Samara R. Gunter, Daniel Riera-Crichton, Carlos A. Vegh, Guillermo Vuletin

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 全球/多国

Abstract

In an earlier paper, titled "Non-linear effects of tax changes on output: The role of the initial level of taxation," we estimated tax multipliers using (i) a novel dataset on value-added taxes for 51 countries (21 industrial and 30 developing) for the period 1970-2014, and (ii) the so-called narrative approach developed by Romer and Romer (2010) to properly identify exogenous tax changes. The main finding is that, in line with existing theoretical distortionary and disincentive-based arguments, the effect of tax changes on output is highly non-linear. The tax multiplier is essentially zero under relatively low/moderate initial tax rate levels and more negative as the initial tax rate and the size of the change in the tax rate increase. This companion paper first shows that these findings have important policy implications, given that the initial level of taxes varies greatly across countries and thus so will the potential output effect of changing tax rates. The paper then turns to some specific policy applications. It focuses on the relevance of the arguments for revenue mobilization in countries with low levels of provision of public goods and social and infrastructure gaps, as well as in commodity-dependent countries. The paper then considers some practical implications for the standard debt sustainability analysis. Lastly, it evaluates the implications of the findings for the Laffer curve.

中文摘要

在之前一篇题为“税收变化对产出的非线性效应：初始税收水平的作用”的论文中，我们使用（i）一个涵盖1970-2014年51个国家（21个工业国和30个发展中国家）增值税的新数据集，以及（ii）由Romer和Romer（2010）开发的所谓叙事方法来正确识别外生税收变化，估计了税收乘数。主要发现是，与现有的基于扭曲和抑制激励的理论论点一致，税收变化对产出的影响高度非线性。在初始税率水平相对较低/中等时，税收乘数基本为零，随着初始税率和税率变化幅度的增加，乘数变得更负。这篇配套论文首先表明，这些发现具有重要的政策含义，因为各国的初始税收水平差异很大，因此改变税率对产出的潜在影响也会不同。然后，论文转向一些具体的政策应用。它重点关注这些论点对公共产品提供水平低、社会和基础设施存在差距的国家以及依赖商品的国家进行收入动员的相关性。然后，论文考虑了标准债务可持续性分析的一些实际含义。最后，它评估了这些发现对拉弗曲线的影响。

Back to School: The Effect of School Visits During COVID-19 on COVID-19 Transmission

重返校园：COVID-19期间学校访问对COVID-19传播的影响

Dena Bravata, Jonathan H. Cantor, Neeraj Sood, Christopher M. Whaley

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Schools across the United States and the world have been closed in an effort to mitigate the spread of COVID-19. However, the effect of school closure on COVID-19 transmission remains unclear. We estimate the causal effect of changes in the number of weekly visits to schools on COVID-19 transmission using a triple difference approach. In particular, we measure the effect of changes in county-level visits to schools on changes in COVID-19 diagnoses for households with school-age children relative to changes in COVID-19 diagnoses for households without school-age children. We use a data set from the first 46 weeks of 2020 with 130 million household-week level observations that includes COVID-19 diagnoses merged to school visit tracking data from millions of mobile phones. We find that increases in county-level in-person visits to schools lead to an increase in COVID-19 diagnoses among households with children relative to households without school-age children. However, the effects are small in magnitude. A move from the 25th to the 75th percentile of county-level school visits translates to a 0.3 per 10,000 household increase in COVID-19 diagnoses. This change translates to a 3.2 percent relative increase. We find larger differences in low-income counties, in counties with higher COVID-19 prevalence, and at later stages of the COVID-19 pandemic.

中文摘要

美国和世界各地的学校已关闭以减缓COVID-19的传播。然而，学校关闭对COVID-19传播的影响仍不清楚。我们使用三重差分法估计每周学校访问次数变化对COVID-19传播的因果效应。具体来说，我们衡量县级学校访问变化对有学龄儿童家庭COVID-19诊断变化的影响，相对于无学龄儿童家庭COVID-19诊断的变化。我们使用2020年前46周的数据集，包含1.3亿个家庭-周级别的观测值，其中包括COVID-19诊断数据，并与来自数百万部手机的学校访问追踪数据合并。我们发现，县级学校面对面访问的增加导致有儿童家庭相对于无学龄儿童家庭的COVID-19诊断增加。然而，影响幅度很小。从县级学校访问的第25百分位移动到第75百分位，相当于每万户家庭COVID-19诊断增加0.3例。这一变化相当于相对增加3.2%。我们在低收入县、COVID-19患病率较高的县以及COVID-19大流行后期发现了更大的差异。

Guaranteed Markets and Corporate Scientific Research

有保障的市场与企业科学研究

Sharon Belenzon, Larisa C. Cioaca

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

The U.S. government incentivizes firms to develop innovative technologies by awarding R&D contracts that often carry an implicit promise of “guaranteed demand.” Firms that demonstrate strong technological capabilities are rewarded with noncompetitive production contracts for the resulting products and services. Using newly assembled data on $4.2 trillion in government procurement contracts from all federal agencies, matched to U.S. publicly traded firms, we document a “crowding-in” effect, where government R&D contracts lead to increased investment in corporate scientific research. Firms co-invest with the government at the R&D stage to enhance their chances of securing these lucrative production contracts. We develop a theoretical framework to explain when it is optimal for the government to bundle R&D and production contracts. Our analysis shows that guaranteed demand can produce higher quality at a lower total cost for upstream R&D projects when the R&D firms have production capabilities. Our empirical results confirm these predictions. Additionally, we find that the crowding-in effect has weakened over time as the government has increasingly separated R&D contracts from production contracts. We discuss the potential implications of this decoupling.

中文摘要

美国政府通过授予研发合同来激励企业开发创新技术，这些合同通常隐含着“有保障的需求”的承诺。展现出强大技术能力的企业会获得针对其最终产品和服务的不具竞争性的生产合同。利用新整理的、涵盖所有联邦机构4.2万亿美元政府采购合同的数据，并与美国上市公司相匹配，我们记录了一种“挤入”效应：政府的研发合同导致企业科学研究投资增加。企业在研发阶段与政府共同投资，以提高获得这些利润丰厚的生产合同的机会。我们构建了一个理论框架来解释政府何时将研发合同与生产合同捆绑是最优的。我们的分析表明，当研发企业具备生产能力时，有保障的需求能够以更低的总成本为上游研发项目带来更高质量。我们的实证结果证实了这些预测。此外，我们发现，随着政府越来越多地将研发合同与生产合同分离，这种挤入效应随着时间的推移而减弱。我们讨论了这种脱钩的潜在影响。

Labor Rationing

劳动力配给

Emily Breza, Supreet Kaur, Yogita Shamdasani

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 印度

Abstract

This paper measures excess labor supply in equilibrium. We examine hiring shocks—which employ 24% of the labor force in external month-long jobs—in Indian local labor markets. In peak months, wages increase instantaneously and local aggregate employment declines. In lean months, consistent with severe labor rationing, wages and aggregate employment are unchanged, with positive employment spillovers on remaining workers—indicating that over a quarter of labor supply is rationed. At least 24% of lean self-employment among casual workers occurs because they cannot find jobs. Consequently, traditional survey approaches mismeasure labor market slack. Rationing has broad implications for labor market analysis.

中文摘要

本文衡量了均衡状态下的超额劳动力供给。我们考察了印度地方劳动力市场中的雇佣冲击——这些冲击在外部为期一个月的岗位中雇佣了24%的劳动力。在高峰月份，工资立即上涨，而地方总就业量下降。在低谷月份，与严重的劳动力配给一致，工资和总就业量保持不变，但对剩余工人产生了正向的就业溢出效应——这表明超过四分之一的劳动力供给被配给了。在临时工中，至少有24%的低谷月份自雇就业是因为他们找不到工作。因此，传统的调查方法错误地衡量了劳动力市场的松弛程度。配给对劳动力市场分析具有广泛的影响。

Surviving a Mass Shooting

大规模枪击事件的幸存者

Prashant Bharadwaj, Manudeep Bhuller, Katrine V. Løken, Mirjam Wentzel

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 挪威

Abstract

We use data on all middle and high school-aged children who survived a mass shooting incident on July 22, 2011 in Utøya, Norway, to understand how such events affect survivors, their families, and their peers. Using a difference-in-differences design to compare survivors to a matched control group, we find that in the short run children who survive have substantially lower GPA (nearly 0.5 SD), increased health visits, and more mental health diagnoses (nearly 400% increase). In the medium run, survivors have fewer years of schooling completed and lower labor force participation. Parents and siblings of survivors are also impacted, experiencing substantial increases in doctor visits and mental health diagnoses. However, there appear to be limited impacts on school-aged peers of survivors. While this event affected the entire country, we show that survivors and their families bear significant costs despite robust social safety nets and universal access to healthcare.

中文摘要

我们利用2011年7月22日挪威于特岛大规模枪击事件中所有幸存的初中和高中年龄段儿童的数据，来理解此类事件如何影响幸存者、他们的家人以及同龄人。采用双重差分设计，将幸存者与匹配的对照组进行比较，我们发现，在短期内，幸存儿童的GPA显著降低（近0.5个标准差），健康就诊次数增加，心理健康诊断数量增加（增幅近400%）。在中期，幸存者完成的受教育年限更少，劳动力参与率也更低。幸存者的父母和兄弟姐妹也受到影响，他们的就诊次数和心理健康诊断数量大幅增加。然而，对幸存者的学龄同龄人的影响似乎有限。尽管这一事件影响了整个国家，但我们表明，尽管拥有健全的社会安全网和全民医疗保健，幸存者及其家人仍承担了巨大的成本。

Food vs. Food Stamps: Evidence from an At-Scale Experiment in Indonesia

食品与食品券：来自印度尼西亚大规模实验的证据

Abhijit Banerjee, Rema Hanna, Benjamin A. Olken, Elan Satriawan, Sudarno Sumarto

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 印度、印度尼西亚

Abstract

Governments seeking to provide food assistance have a choice between providing in-kind food directly to beneficiaries, or providing vouchers that can be used to purchase food on the market. To understand the differences between these policies, the Government of Indonesia randomly phased in the transition from in-kind delivery of subsidized rice to approximately equivalent vouchers usable to buy rice and eggs across 105 districts comprising over 3.4 million beneficiary households. We find the transition led to substantial changes in the allocation of aid in practice. The vouchers provided concentrated assistance to targeted households, who received 45 percent more assistance in voucher areas than in in-kind districts. As a result, for households in the bottom 15 percent at baseline, poverty fell by 20 percent. Vouchers also allowed households to purchase higher-quality rice, and led to increased consumption of egg-based proteins. We find vouchers have little effect on aggregate rice prices, although we observe modest price increases in remote villages. Overall leakage from the program was not affected, but the administrative costs of benefits delivery substantially fell. In short, the results suggest that the change from in-kind food aid to vouchers led to substantial impacts on poverty through the way it changed how programs were implemented on the ground.

本文仅基于报告的感染病例数（允许漏报）估计了全球COVID-19的时变再生数。估计基于一个矩条件，该条件可以从基于主体的COVID-19传播随机网络模型中推导得出。截至2020年底，再生数和人均病例轨迹的结果非常多样化。再生数取决于传播率和易感人群比例，即群体免疫效应。传播率的变化取决于病毒行为的变化（反映突变和疫苗接种）以及人们行为的变化（反映自愿或政府强制隔离）。在我们的样本期内，突变和疫苗接种都不是主要因素，因此可以将传播率的变化归因于行为的变化。基于解释九个欧洲国家传播率的面板数据模型的证据表明，结果的多样性源于强制遏制措施、自愿预防性隔离以及政府为支持隔离提供的经济激励的非线性相互作用。这些效应估计精确且对各种假设稳健。因此，看似采取不同社交距离政策的国家在再生数方面取得了相当相似的结果。这些结果表明，忽略社交距离的自愿成分可能会高估封锁和支持政策对传播率的影响。完整的估计结果和复制包可在作者网站上获取。

Welfare Consequences of Sustainable Finance

可持续金融的福利后果

Harrison Hong, Neng Wang, Jinqiang Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We model the welfare consequences of portfolio mandates that restrict investors to hold firms with net-zero carbon emissions. To qualify for these mandates, value-maximizing firms have to accumulate decarbonization capital. Qualification lowers a firm’s required rate of return by its decarbonization investments divided by Tobin’s q, i.e., the dividend yield shareholders forgo to address the global-warming externality. The welfare-maximizing mandate approximates the first-best solution, yielding welfare gains compared to laissez faire by mitigating the weather disaster risks resulting from carbon emissions. Our model generates transitions to steady-state decarbonization-to-productive capital ratios that we use to evaluate the optimality of proposed net-zero targets.

中文摘要

我们构建了投资组合指令的福利后果模型，这些指令限制投资者只能持有净零碳排放企业。为了符合这些指令要求，追求价值最大化的企业必须积累脱碳资本。符合资格会降低企业的必要回报率，降低幅度等于其脱碳投资除以托宾q，即股东为应对全球变暖外部性而放弃的股息收益率。福利最大化的指令近似于最优解，通过减轻碳排放导致的天气灾害风险，与自由放任相比带来福利改善。我们的模型生成了向稳态脱碳-生产性资本比率的过渡，我们利用这一比率来评估所提议的净零目标的最优性。
