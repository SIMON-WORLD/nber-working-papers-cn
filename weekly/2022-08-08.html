Bank Liquid Assets, the Portfolio Motive, and Capital Requirements

银行流动资产、投资组合动机与资本要求

René M. Stulz, Alvaro G. Taboada, Mathijs A. van Dijk

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Banks hold large amounts of liquid assets. These amounts grow sharply for the largest banks after the global financial crisis (GFC). The standard transaction and precautionary motives for holding liquid assets cannot explain the size and evolution of bank liquid asset holdings. Motivated by the deposit view of banks, we introduce a portfolio motive such that banks hold more liquid assets when they have poorer lending opportunities, making loans and liquid assets substitutes. We find support for the portfolio motive and for its prediction that capital requirement increases help explain the post-GFC growth in the largest banks’ liquid asset holdings.

中文摘要

银行持有大量流动资产。全球金融危机后，最大银行的这些资产规模急剧增长。持有流动资产的常规交易动机和预防性动机无法解释银行流动资产持有量的规模和演变。受银行存款观点的启发，我们引入了一个投资组合动机，即当银行的贷款机会较差时，它们会持有更多流动资产，使贷款和流动资产成为替代品。我们找到了支持投资组合动机的证据，也支持其预测，即资本要求的提高有助于解释全球金融危机后最大银行流动资产持有量的增长。

The Benefits and Costs of U.S. Employer COVID-19 Vaccine Mandates

美国雇主COVID-19疫苗强制令的收益与成本

Maddalena Ferranna, Lisa A. Robinson, Daniel Cadarette, Michael Eber, David E. Bloom

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

In 2021, the Biden Administration issued mandates requiring COVID-19 vaccinations for U.S. federal employees and contractors and for some healthcare and private sector workers. Although these mandates have been subject to legal challenges and some have been halted or delayed, rigorous appraisal of their benefits and costs accompanied neither the decision to implement them nor the efforts to terminate them. This paper aims to help fill that gap. We estimate the direct costs and health-related benefits that would have accrued if these vaccination requirements had been implemented as intended. Compared with the vaccination rates observed in January 2022, we find that the mandates could have led to 15 million additional vaccinated individuals, increasing the overall proportion of the fully vaccinated U.S. population to 68%. The associated net benefits depend on the evolution of the pandemic from the time of mandate enactment—information unavailable ex ante to analysts or policymakers. In scenarios involving the emergence of a novel, more transmissible variant, against which vaccination and previous infection offer moderate protection, the estimated net benefits reach more than $16,000 per additional vaccinated individual, with more than 20,000 total deaths averted in total. In scenarios involving a fading pandemic, existing vaccination-acquired or infection-acquired immunity provides sufficient protection, and the mandates’ benefits are unlikely to exceed their costs. Thus, mandates may be most useful when the consequences of inaction are catastrophic. However, we do not compare the effects of mandates with alternative policies for increasing vaccination rates or promoting other protective measures, which may receive stronger public support and be less likely to be overturned by litigation.

中文摘要

2021年，拜登政府发布强制令，要求美国联邦雇员和承包商以及部分医疗保健和私营部门工人接种COVID-19疫苗。尽管这些强制令面临法律挑战，其中一些已被暂停或推迟，但在实施或终止这些强制令时，并未伴随对其收益和成本的严格评估。本文旨在帮助填补这一空白。我们估算了如果这些疫苗接种要求按计划实施本应产生的直接成本和健康相关收益。与2022年1月观察到的疫苗接种率相比，我们发现这些强制令本可使额外1500万人接种疫苗，将美国完全接种疫苗人口的整体比例提高到68%。相关的净收益取决于强制令颁布时大流行的演变——分析师或政策制定者事先无法获得这些信息。在出现一种新的、传染性更强的变种，且疫苗接种和既往感染提供中等保护的情景下，估计的净收益达到每位额外接种者超过16,000美元，总共避免超过20,000人死亡。在大流行消退的情景下，现有的疫苗获得性或感染获得性免疫提供了足够保护，强制令的收益不太可能超过其成本。因此，当不作为的后果是灾难性时，强制令可能最为有用。然而，我们并未将强制令的效果与提高疫苗接种率或推广其他保护措施的替代政策进行比较，这些替代政策可能获得更强的公众支持，且不太可能因诉讼而被推翻。

No. 3论文详情NBER w30338

Climate Change Around the World

全球气候变化

Per Krusell, Anthony A. Smith Jr.

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 全球/多国

Abstract

The economic effects of climate change vary across both time and space. To study these effects, this paper builds a global economy-climate model featuring a high degree of geographic resolution. Carbon emissions from the use of energy in production increase the Earth's (average) temperature and local, or regional, temperatures respond more or less sensitively to this increase. Each of the approximately 19,000 regions makes optimal consumption-savings and energy-use decisions as its climate (or regional temperature) and, consequently, its productivity change over time. The relationship between regional temperature and regional productivity has an inverted U-shape, calibrated so that the high-resolution model replicates estimates of aggregate global damages from global warming. At the global level, then, the high-resolution model nests standard one-region economy-climate models, while at the same time it features realistic spatial variation in climate and economic activity. The central result is that the effects of climate change vary dramatically across space---with many regions gaining while others lose---and the global average effects, while negative, are dwarfed quantitatively by the differences across space. A tax on carbon increases average (global) welfare, but there is a large disparity of views on it across regions, with both winners and losers. Climate change also leads to large increases in global inequality, across both regions and countries. These findings vary little as capital markets range from closed (autarky) to open (free capital mobility).

中文摘要

气候变化的经济效应在时间和空间上均存在差异。为研究这些效应，本文构建了一个具有高地理分辨率的全球经济-气候模型。生产中能源使用产生的碳排放会提高地球（平均）温度，而局部或区域温度对此升高的敏感程度各不相同。随着气候（或区域温度）及其生产率随时间变化，约19,000个区域中的每一个都会做出最优的消费-储蓄和能源使用决策。区域温度与区域生产率之间的关系呈倒U形，经过校准后，该高分辨率模型能够复制全球变暖对全球总体损害的估计值。因此，在全球层面上，高分辨率模型嵌套了标准的单区域经济-气候模型，同时体现了气候和经济活动在空间上的现实异质性。核心结果是，气候变化的影响在空间上差异巨大——许多地区受益而其他地区受损——全球平均效应虽为负值，但在数量上远小于空间差异。征收碳税会提高平均（全球）福利，但各地区对此存在巨大分歧，既有受益者也有受损者。气候变化还导致全球不平等程度大幅上升，无论是在区域之间还是国家之间。这些发现几乎不因资本市场从封闭（自给自足）到开放（资本自由流动）的状态变化而改变。

The Macroeconomic Consequences of Natural Rate Shocks: An Empirical Investigation

自然利率冲击的宏观经济后果：一项实证研究

Stephanie Schmitt-Grohé, Martín Uribe

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Much of the empirical literature on the natural rate of interest has focused on estimating its path. This paper studies the effects of natural rate shocks on output and inflation. It estimates a semi-structural model inspired by the DSGE literature. A decline in the natural rate is found to have a large negative effect on the trend of output and to be contractionary and deflationary in the short run. When the economy is constrained by the zero lower bound (ZLB), these results are consistent with the secular stagnation hypothesis. However, negative natural rate shocks are found to depress the trend of output even outside of the ZLB, calling for a more general theory of secular stagnation.

中文摘要

关于自然利率的实证文献大多侧重于估计其路径。本文研究自然利率冲击对产出和通胀的影响。它估计了一个受DSGE文献启发的半结构模型。研究发现，自然利率下降对产出趋势有较大的负向影响，并在短期内具有收缩性和通缩性。当经济受到零下限约束时，这些结果与长期停滞假说一致。然而，即使在零下限之外，负向自然利率冲击也会抑制产出趋势，这需要一种更一般的长期停滞理论来解释。

Internationalizing Like China

像中国一样国际化

Christopher Clayton, Amanda Dos Santos, Matteo Maggiori, Jesse Schreger

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价

Abstract

We empirically characterize how China is internationalizing its bond market by staggering the entry of different types of foreign investors into its domestic market and propose a dynamic reputation model to explain this strategy. Our framework rationalizes China’s strategy as trying to build credibility as a safe issuer while reducing the cost of capital flight. We use our framework to shed light on China’s response to episodes of capital outflows.

中文摘要

我们通过实证刻画了中国如何通过分阶段允许不同类型的外国投资者进入其国内市场来实现债券市场国际化，并提出了一个动态声誉模型来解释这一策略。我们的框架将中国的策略合理化，认为其旨在建立作为安全发行者的可信度，同时降低资本外逃的成本。我们利用该框架来阐明中国对资本外流事件的应对措施。

No. 6论文详情NBER w30335中国相关

Lessons from U.S.-China Trade Relations

美中贸易关系的教训

Lorenzo Caliendo, Fernando Parro

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

We review theoretical and empirical work on the economic effects of the United States and China trade relations during the last decades. We first discuss the origins of the China shock, its measurement, and present methods used to study its economic effects on different outcomes. We then focus on the recent U.S.-China trade war. We discuss methods used to evaluate its effects, describe its economic effects, and analyze if this increase in trade protectionism reverted the effects of the China shock. The main lessons learned in this review are: (i) the aggregate gains from U.S.-China trade created winners and losers; (ii) China's trade expansion seems not to be the main cause of the decline in U.S. manufacturing employment during the same period; and (iii) the recent trade war generated welfare losses, had small employment effects, and was ineffective in reversing the distributional effects due to the China shock.

中文摘要

我们回顾了过去几十年关于美中贸易关系经济效应的理论和实证研究。我们首先讨论了中国冲击的起源、其衡量方法，以及用于研究其对不同结果经济效应的现有方法。然后，我们聚焦于近期的美中贸易战。我们讨论了评估其影响的方法，描述了其经济效应，并分析了这种贸易保护主义的加剧是否逆转了中国冲击的影响。本综述的主要教训是：(i) 美中贸易带来的总体收益创造了赢家和输家；(ii) 中国贸易扩张似乎并非同期美国制造业就业下降的主要原因；(iii) 近期的贸易战造成了福利损失，对就业影响甚微，并且未能有效逆转中国冲击带来的分配效应。

Superiority-Seeking and the Preference for Exclusion

优越感追求与排斥偏好

Alex Imas, Kristóf Madarász

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose that a person’s desire to consume an object or possess an attribute increases in how much others want but cannot have it. We term this motive superiority-seeking, and show that it generates preferences for exclusion that help explain a host of market anomalies and make novel predictions in a variety of domains. In bilateral exchange, there is a reluctance to trade and people exhibit a ‘social endowment effect.’ People’s value of consuming a good increases in its scarcity, which generates a motive for firms and organizations to cater to such preferences by engaging in exclusionary policies. Randomly barring potential consumers from the opportunity to acquire a product increases the seller’s profits both in standard monopoly and auction settings. Such non-price-based exclusion leads to higher revenues than the classic optimal sales mechanisms. A series of experiments provides direct support for these predictions. In basic exchange, a person’s willingness to pay for a good increases as more people are explicitly barred from the opportunity to acquire it. In auctions, randomly excluding people from the opportunity to bid substantially increases bids amongst those who retain this option. Exclusion leads to bigger gains in expected revenue than increasing competition through inclusion. Our model of superiority-seeking generates ‘Veblen effects,’ rationalizes attitudes against redistribution, and provides a novel motive for social stratification and discrimination.

中文摘要

我们提出，一个人消费某物或拥有某种属性的欲望，会随着其他人想要但无法拥有该物的程度而增加。我们将这种动机称为优越感追求，并表明它会产生排斥偏好，这有助于解释一系列市场异象，并在多个领域做出新颖的预测。在双边交换中，存在交易惰性，人们表现出一种“社会禀赋效应”。人们对消费某物的价值会因其稀缺性而增加，这为企业或组织通过实施排斥性政策来迎合此类偏好提供了动机。随机阻止潜在消费者获得产品的机会，无论是在标准垄断还是拍卖环境中，都能提高卖方的利润。这种非价格排斥机制比经典的最优销售机制带来更高的收入。一系列实验为这些预测提供了直接支持。在基本交换中，随着更多人被明确禁止获得某物的机会，一个人对该物的支付意愿会增加。在拍卖中，随机排除人们出价的机会，会显著提高那些保留出价机会者的出价。排斥带来的预期收入增长大于通过包容来增加竞争。我们的优越感追求模型产生了“凡勃伦效应”，使反对再分配的态度合理化，并为社会分层和歧视提供了新的动机。

No. 8论文详情NBER w30333

Noisy Foresight

噪声预见

Anujit Chakraborty, Chad W. Kendall

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Rational agents must perform backwards induction by thinking contingently about future states and actions, but failures of backwards induction and contingent reasoning are ubiquitous. How do boundedly-rational agents make decisions when they fail to correctly forecast actions in the future? We construct an individual decision-making experiment to collect a rich dataset in which subjects must reason only about their own future actions. We demonstrate substantial mistakes relative to the rational benchmark, and use the rich dataset to estimate several possible models of boundedly-rational foresight. We find that a model in which subjects expect to make more mistakes when the payoff consequences of their future actions are more similar best explains behavior.

中文摘要

理性主体必须通过条件性地思考未来状态和行动来进行逆向归纳，但逆向归纳和条件性推理的失败普遍存在。当有限理性主体无法正确预测未来的行动时，他们如何做出决策？我们构建了一个个体决策实验，收集了一个丰富的数据集，其中受试者只需推理自己未来的行动。我们证明了相对于理性基准存在大量错误，并利用该丰富数据集估计了几种可能的有限理性预见模型。我们发现，一个假设受试者预期在未来行动的收益后果更相似时会犯更多错误的模型，最能解释其行为。

No. 9论文详情NBER w30332

Green Energy Jobs in the US: What Are They, and Where Are They?

美国的绿色能源就业：它们是什么，在哪里？

E. Mark Curtis, Ioana Marinescu

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Does the growth of renewable energy benefit US workers, and which workers stand to benefit the most? Until now, evidence on green energy jobs has been limited due to measurement issues. We use data on nearly all jobs posted online in the US, as collected by Burning Glass Technology, and we create a new measure of green jobs, defined here as solar and wind jobs. We use job titles and task requirements to define green jobs. We find that both solar and wind job postings have more than tripled since 2010, with solar jobs seeing especially strong growth that precedes the growth of new installed solar capacity. In 2019, we identify approximately 52,500 solar job openings and 13,500 wind job openings. Solar jobs are mostly (33%) in sales occupations, and in the utilities industry (16%). Wind jobs are most represented among installation and maintenance occupations (37%), and in the manufacturing industry (29%). Green jobs are created in occupations that are about 21% higher paying than average. The pay premium is even higher for jobs with a low educational requirement. Finally, green jobs tend to locate in counties with high shares of employment in fossil fuel extraction. Overall, our results suggest that the growth of renewable energy leads to the creation of relatively high paying jobs, which are more often than not located in areas that stand to lose from a decline in fossil fuel extraction jobs.

Origins of International Factor Structures

国际因子结构的起源

Zhengyang Jiang, Robert J. Richmond

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We show that exchange rate correlations tend to be explained by the global trade network while consumption correlations tend to be explained by productivity correlations. Sharing common trade linkages with other countries increases exchange rate correlations beyond bilateral linkages. We explain these findings using a model of the global trade network with market segmentation. Interdependent global production generates international comovements, while market segmentation disconnects the drivers of exchange rate correlations from the drivers of consumption correlations. Moreover, we show that the trade network generates common factors found in exchange rates. Our findings offer a trade-based account of the origins of international comovements and shed light on important frictions in international markets.

Non-Tariff Barriers in the U.S.-China Trade War

中美贸易战中的非关税壁垒

Tuo Chen, Chang-Tai Hsieh, Zheng Michael Song

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

We use Chinese customs data to show that unofficial non-tariff barriers were responsible for 50\\% of the overall reduction in Chinese imports from the U.S. during the height of the U.S.-China trade war in 2018 and 2019. We infer non-tariff barriers from the change in imports of U.S. products relative to imports from other countries of the same HS-6 product, after controlling for the change in the relative price of U.S. imports to the same product sold by other countries. These barriers were imposed on a small number of agricultural products, did not apply to state-owned importers, and were larger for products where the share of state importers in total imports of the U.S. product was large. Non-tariff barriers were responsible for more than 90\\% of the welfare cost to Chinese consumers of the U.S.-China trade war. The welfare loss to China of a given reduction in imports from the U.S. from non-tariff barriers is about six times larger than an equivalent import decline due to higher tariffs. Non-tariff barriers are more costly compared to tariffs because they applied to some importers and not others, which results in misallocation, and because non-tariff barriers do not generate revenues.

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Globalisation affects the functioning of the macroeconomy. The macroeconomy’s functioning, in turn, conditions the conduct and impact of monetary policy. This is why globalisation matters for central banks. It is also why central bankers should pay attention to the evolution of globalisation. And evolve it has. Barriers are radically higher and falling radically faster for services versus goods, and, unlike farm and factory goods, there is no capacity constraint when it comes to the export of intermediate services from emerging markets. Fertility and Labor Market Responses to Reductions in Mortality

Sonia R. Bhalotra, Atheendar Venkataramani, Selma Walther

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We investigate women’s fertility, labor and marriage market responses to a health innovation that led to reductions in mortality from treatable causes, and especially large declines in child mortality. We find delayed childbearing, with lower intensive and extensive margin fertility, a decline in the chances of ever having married, increased labor force participation and an improvement in occupational status. Our results provide the first evidence that improvements in child survival allow women to start fertility later and invest more in the labor market. We present a new theory of fertility that incorporates dynamic choices and reconciles our findings with existing models of behavior.

Producing Health: Measuring Value Added of Nursing Homes

生产健康：衡量养老院的增加值

Liran Einav, Amy Finkelstein, Neale Mahoney

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop a stylized model that allows us to estimate a value-added measure for nursing homes (“SNFs”) which accounts for patient selection both into and out of a SNF. We use the model, together with detailed data on the physical and mental health of about 6 million Medicare SNF patients between 2011 and 2016, to estimate the value added for about 14,000 distinct SNFs. We document substantial heterogeneity in value added. Nationwide, compared to a 10th percentile SNF, a 90th percentile SNF is able to discharge a patient at the same health level almost a week sooner, or one quarter of the median length of stay. Heterogeneity in value added within a market is almost as large as it is nationwide. Our results point to the potential for substantial gains through policies that encourage reallocation of patients to higher-quality SNFs within their market.
