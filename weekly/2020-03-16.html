Fiscal Federalism and the Budget Impacts of the Affordable Care Act's Medicaid Expansion

财政联邦主义与《平价医疗法案》医疗补助扩展的预算影响

Jonathan Gruber, Benjamin D. Sommers

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Medicaid’s federal-state matching system of financing is the nation’s largest example of fiscal federalism. Using generous federal subsidies, the Affordable Care Act incentivized states to expand Medicaid, which became a state option in the aftermath of a 2012 Supreme Court ruling. As of early 2020, 14 states had not yet expanded, with concerns over state budgetary effects described as a key barrier. We use an event-study approach to analyze state budget data from 2010-2018 and assess the effects of state Medicaid expansion decisions. We find that Medicaid expansion increased total spending in expansion states by 6% to 9%, compared to non-expansion states. By source of funds, federal spending via the states increased by 10% in the first year of Medicaid expansion, rising to 27% in 2018. Changes in spending from state funding were modest and non-significant, with less than a 1% change from baseline annually in the most recent years, 2017 and 2018. Meanwhile, we find no evidence that increased Medicaid spending from expansion produced any reductions in spending on education, corrections, transportation, or public assistance. Changes in Medicaid spending tracked closely with the baseline pre-ACA (2013) uninsured rate in each states, with expansion leading to roughly $2680 in added annual spending per uninsured adult. As a result, we estimate states that didn’t expand Medicaid passed up $43 billion in federally-subsidized program funds in 2018. Finally, state projections in the aggregate were reasonably accurate, with expansion states projecting average Medicaid spending from 2014-2018 within 2 percent of the actual amounts, and in fact overestimating Medicaid spending in most years.

中文摘要

医疗补助的联邦-州匹配融资体系是美国财政联邦主义的最大范例。通过慷慨的联邦补贴，《平价医疗法案》激励各州扩大医疗补助范围，但在2012年最高法院裁决后，这成为各州的选择。截至2020年初，仍有14个州未进行扩展，其中对州预算影响的担忧被描述为一个关键障碍。我们采用事件研究方法分析2010年至2018年的州预算数据，评估各州医疗补助扩展决策的影响。我们发现，与非扩展州相比，医疗补助扩展使扩展州的总支出增加了6%至9%。按资金来源划分，在医疗补助扩展的第一年，通过各州支出的联邦资金增加了10%，到2018年上升至27%。来自州资金的支出变化幅度较小且不显著，在最近的2017年和2018年，每年相对于基线的变化不到1%。同时，我们没有发现证据表明，因扩展而增加的医疗补助支出导致了教育、矫正、交通或公共援助支出的减少。医疗补助支出的变化与各州在ACA实施前（2013年）的基线未参保率密切相关，扩展导致每位未参保成年人每年增加约2680美元的支出。因此，我们估计，未扩展医疗补助的州在2018年放弃了430亿美元的联邦补贴项目资金。最后，各州的总体预测相当准确，扩展州预测的2014年至2018年平均医疗补助支出与实际金额相差在2%以内，并且在大多数年份实际上高估了医疗补助支出。

Reasonable Doubt: Experimental Detection of Job-Level Employment Discrimination

合理怀疑：职位层面就业歧视的实验检测

Patrick M. Kline, Christopher R. Walters

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper develops methods for detecting discrimination by individual employers using correspondence experiments that send fictitious resumes to real job openings. We establish identification of higher moments of the distribution of job-level callback rates as a function of the number of resumes sent to each job and propose shape-constrained estimators of these moments. Applying our methods to three experimental datasets, we find striking job-level heterogeneity in the extent to which callback probabilities differ by race or sex. Estimates of higher moments reveal that while most jobs barely discriminate, a few discriminate heavily. These moment estimates are then used to bound the share of jobs that discriminate and the posterior probability that each individual job is engaged in discrimination. In a recent experiment manipulating racially distinctive names, we find that at least 85% of jobs that contact both of two white applications and neither of two black applications are engaged in discrimination. To assess the potential value of our methods for regulators, we consider the accuracy of decision rules for investigating suspicious callback behavior in various experimental designs under a simple two-type model that rationalizes the experimental data. Though we estimate that only 17% of employers discriminate on the basis of race, we find that an experiment sending 10 applications to each job would enable detection of 7-10% of discriminatory jobs while yielding Type I error rates below 0.2%. A minimax decision rule acknowledging partial identification of the distribution of callback rates yields only slightly fewer investigations than a Bayes decision rule based on the two-type model. These findings suggest illegal labor market discrimination can be reliably monitored with relatively small modifications to existing correspondence designs.

中文摘要

本文开发了通过向真实职位空缺发送虚构简历的通信实验来检测单个雇主歧视的方法。我们建立了职位层面回调率分布高阶矩的识别条件，并提出了这些矩的形状约束估计量。将我们的方法应用于三个实验数据集，我们发现，在种族或性别方面回调概率差异的程度存在显著的职位层面异质性。高阶矩的估计显示，虽然大多数职位几乎不存在歧视，但少数职位存在严重歧视。这些矩估计随后被用于界定存在歧视的职位比例，以及每个单个职位参与歧视的后验概率。在最近一个操纵种族特征姓名的实验中，我们发现，在联系了两名白人申请者但未联系任何一名黑人申请者的职位中，至少有85%存在歧视。为了评估我们的方法对监管机构的潜在价值，我们考虑在一个能够合理化实验数据的简单双类型模型下，各种实验设计中用于调查可疑回调行为的决策规则的准确性。尽管我们估计只有17%的雇主存在基于种族的歧视，但我们发现，一个向每个职位发送10份申请的实验，能够检测出7%至10%的歧视性职位，同时将第一类错误率控制在0.2%以下。一个承认回调率分布部分识别的极小化极大决策规则，其调查数量仅略少于基于双类型模型的贝叶斯决策规则。这些发现表明，通过对现有通信设计进行相对较小的修改，可以可靠地监测非法的劳动力市场歧视。

Immigration and Worker-Firm Matching

移民与工人-企业匹配

Gianluca Orefice, Giovanni Peri

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 法国

Abstract

The process of matching between firms and workers is an important mechanism in determining the distribution of wages. In a labor market characterised by large dispersion of workers' productivity and worker-firm complementarity, high quality firms have strong incentives to screen for the quality of workers. This process will increase the positive quality association of firm-worker matches known as positive assortative matching (PAM). Immigration in a local labor market, by increasing the variance of workers abilities, may drive stronger PAM between firms and workers. Using French matched employer-employee (DADS) data over the period 1995-2005 we document that positive supply-driven changes of immigrant workers in a district increased the strength of PAM. We then show that this association is consistent with causality, is quantitatively significant, and is associated with higher average productivity and firm profits, but also with higher wage dispersion. We also show that the increased degree of positive assortative matching is mainly reached by high-productive firms "losing" lower quality workers and "attracting" higher quality workers.

中文摘要

企业与工人之间的匹配过程是决定工资分布的重要机制。在工人生产力高度分散且存在工人-企业互补性的劳动力市场中，高质量企业有强烈动机筛选工人质量。这一过程将增强企业与工人之间正向的质量关联，即正向分类匹配（PAM）。本地劳动力市场的移民通过增加工人能力的方差，可能推动企业与工人之间更强的PAM。利用1995-2005年法国匹配的雇主-雇员（DADS）数据，我们记录了某地区移民工人的正向供给驱动变化增强了PAM的强度。随后，我们表明这种关联与因果关系一致，在数量上显著，并与更高的平均生产率和企业利润相关，但也与更高的工资分散度相关。我们还表明，正向分类匹配程度的提高主要通过高生产率企业“失去”低质量工人并“吸引”高质量工人来实现。

Contagion of Fear

恐惧的传染

Kris James Mitchener, Gary Richardson

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The Great Depression is infamous for banking panics, which were a symptomatic of a phenomenon that scholars have labeled a contagion of fear. Using geocoded, microdata on bank distress, we develop metrics that illuminate the incidence of these events and how banks that remained in operation after panics responded. We show that between 1929-32 banking panics reduced lending by 13%, relative to its 1929 value, and the money multiplier and money supply by 36%. The banking panics, in other words, caused about 41% of the decline in bank lending and about nine-tenths of the decline in the money multiplier during the Great Depression.

中文摘要

大萧条因银行恐慌而臭名昭著，这些恐慌是学者们称为“恐惧传染”现象的症状。利用地理编码的银行困境微观数据，我们开发了衡量这些事件发生率以及恐慌后仍在运营的银行如何应对的指标。我们表明，在1929年至1932年间，银行恐慌使贷款相对于1929年水平减少了13%，并使货币乘数和货币供应量减少了36%。换句话说，银行恐慌导致了大萧条期间银行贷款下降的约41%以及货币乘数下降的约十分之九。

Wandering Astray: Teenagers' Choices of Schooling and Crime

误入歧途：青少年的上学与犯罪选择

Chao Fu, Nicolás Grau, Jorge Rivera

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 智利

Abstract

We build and estimate a dynamic model of teenagers' choices of schooling and crime, incorporating four factors that may contribute to the different routes taken by different teenagers: heterogeneous endowments, unequal opportunities, uncertainties about one's own ability, and contemporaneous shocks. We estimate the model using administrative panel data from Chile that link school records with juvenile criminal records. Counterfactual policy experiments suggest that, for teenagers with disadvantaged backgrounds, interventions that combine mild improvement in their schooling opportunities with free tuition (by adding 22 USD per enrollee-year to the existing voucher) would lead to an 11% decrease in the fraction of those ever arrested by age 18 and a 17% increase in the fraction of those consistently enrolled throughout primary and secondary education.

中文摘要

我们构建并估计了一个关于青少年上学与犯罪选择的动态模型，纳入了可能导致不同青少年走上不同道路的四个因素：异质性禀赋、不平等机会、对自身能力的不确定性以及同期冲击。我们利用智利的行政面板数据（将学校记录与青少年犯罪记录相关联）来估计该模型。反事实政策实验表明，对于背景不利的青少年，将适度改善其上学机会与免费学费相结合的干预措施（在现有代金券基础上为每位入学学生每年增加22美元），将使18岁前曾被逮捕的青少年比例降低11%，并使在小学和中学教育期间持续入学的比例提高17%。

Popular Economic Narratives Advancing the Longest U.S. Economic Expansion 2009-2019

推动2009-2019年美国最长经济扩张的流行经济叙事

Robert J. Shiller

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

The U.S. economic expansion since 2009 is the longest on record since 1854, according to the National Bureau of Economic Research Business Cycle Dating Committee. This paper seeks to understand this phenomenon better by looking at the time paths of popular narratives over this interval, of stories that people have been telling that offer clues into their economic behavior. Six constellations of narratives are studied, identified by keywords “Great Depression,” “secular stagnation,” “sustainability,” “housing bubble,” “strong economy,” and “save more.”

中文摘要

根据美国国家经济研究局商业周期测定委员会的数据，自2009年以来的美国经济扩张是自1854年以来有记录以来最长的一次。本文试图通过考察这一时期流行叙事的时间路径来更好地理解这一现象，这些叙事是人们讲述的故事，为他们的经济行为提供了线索。研究了六组叙事群，分别由关键词“大萧条”、“长期停滞”、“可持续性”、“房地产泡沫”、“强劲经济”和“增加储蓄”来识别。

Measuring the Cost of Regulation: A Text-Based Approach

衡量监管成本：一种基于文本的方法

Charles W. Calomiris, Harry Mamaysky, Ruoke Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We derive a measure of firm-level regulatory exposure from the text of corporate earnings calls. We use this measure to study the effect of regulation on companies’ growth, leverage, profitability, and equity returns. Higher regulatory exposure results in slower sales and asset growth, lower leverage, reduced profitability, but higher post-call equity returns. These effects are mitigated for larger firms. Our findings suggest that both compliance risk and physical operational cost are consequences of increased regulation, but the magnitude of the effects of compliance risk are larger. The topical context of regulation is important for future firm-level outcomes.

中文摘要

我们从企业财报电话会议的文本中推导出一个衡量企业层面监管暴露度的指标。我们使用该指标来研究监管对公司增长、杠杆率、盈利能力和股票回报的影响。更高的监管暴露度导致销售和资产增长放缓、杠杆率降低、盈利能力下降，但电话会议后的股票回报更高。这些影响对于大公司而言有所缓解。我们的发现表明，合规风险和实际运营成本都是监管加强的后果，但合规风险的影响幅度更大。监管的主题背景对于未来的企业层面结果很重要。

Redrawing the Map of Global Capital Flows: The Role of Cross-Border Financing and Tax Havens

重绘全球资本流动地图：跨境融资与避税天堂的作用

Antonio Coppola, Matteo Maggiori, Brent Neiman, Jesse Schreger

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

Global firms finance themselves through foreign subsidiaries, often shell companies in tax havens, which obscures their true economic location in official statistics. We associate the universe of traded securities issued by firms in tax havens with their issuer's ultimate parent and restate bilateral investment positions to better reflect the financial linkages connecting countries around the world. Portfolio investment from developed countries to firms in large emerging markets is dramatically larger than previously thought. The national accounts of the United States, for example, understate the U.S. position in Chinese firms by nearly 600 billion dollars, while China's official net creditor position to the rest of the world is overstated by about 50 percent. Further, we demonstrate how offshore issuance in tax havens affects our understanding of the currency composition of external portfolio liabilities and the nature of foreign direct investment. Finally, we provide additional restatements of bilateral investment positions, including one based the geographic distribution of sales.

中文摘要

全球企业通过外国子公司（通常是避税天堂的空壳公司）进行融资，这掩盖了它们在官方统计中的真实经济所在地。我们将避税天堂企业发行的所有交易证券与其发行人的最终母公司关联起来，并重新表述双边投资头寸，以更好地反映连接世界各国的金融联系。从发达国家到大型新兴市场企业的组合投资规模比之前认为的要大得多。例如，美国的国民账户将美国在中国企业的头寸低估了近6000亿美元，而中国对世界其他地区的官方净债权头寸则被高估了约50%。此外，我们展示了避税天堂的离岸发行如何影响我们对外部组合负债的货币构成以及外国直接投资性质的理解。最后，我们提供了双边投资头寸的额外重新表述，包括基于销售地理分布的表述。

Heterogeneous Globalization: Offshoring and Reorganization

异质性全球化：离岸外包与重组

Andrew B. Bernard, Teresa C. Fort, Valerie Smeets, Frederic Warzynski

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper exploits a unique offshoring survey to show that firms continue domestic production of the same goods they offshore to low-wage countries. This shift towards “produced-good imports” coincides with a reallocation of labor from physical production to innovation and technology occupations, and an increase in domestically-produced varieties' unit values. These responses suggest an additional, firm-level benefit of trade liberalization: the opportunity to offshore production of low-quality varieties, thereby freeing up domestic resources for the development, production, and marketing of higher-quality varieties. Firms’ reactions also motivate a new offshoring measure – produced- good imports – that is readily observed in most firm-level datasets.

中文摘要

本文利用一项独特的离岸外包调查表明，企业继续在国内生产它们离岸外包到低工资国家的相同商品。这种向“生产商品进口”的转变与劳动力从体力生产向创新和技术职业的重新分配，以及国内生产品种单位价值的增加同时发生。这些反应表明了贸易自由化的另一个企业层面收益：将低质量品种的生产离岸外包的机会，从而释放国内资源用于开发、生产和营销更高质量的品种。企业的反应也激发了一个新的离岸外包衡量指标——生产商品进口——这在大多数企业层面数据集中很容易观察到。

Do Consumers Distinguish Fixed Cost from Variable Cost? “Schmeduling" in Two-Part Tariffs in Energy

消费者能区分固定成本与可变成本吗？能源两部制电价中的“Schmeduling”

Koichiro Ito, Shuang Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易

Abstract

A central assumption in economics is that consumers properly distinguish fixed cost from variable cost. This assumption is fundamental to various economic theories, including optimal taxation, redistribution, and price discrimination. Using a quasi-experiment in heating price reform in China, we find empirical evidence that is inconsistent with this conventional assumption and more consistent with the “schmeduling” model in Liebman and Zeckhauser (2004). Abstract

Abstract

Has the occurrence of “extreme capital flow movements”—episodes of sudden surges, stops, flight and retrenchment—changed since the Global Financial Crisis (GFC)? This paper addresses this question by updating and building on the dataset and methodology introduced in Forbes and Warnock (2012) to calculate the occurrence of sharp capital flow movements by foreigners and domestics into and out of individual countries. Abstract

The classic Working-Leser household Engel curve is unpacked to reveal individual budget allocations across commodities as a function of both individual and household total spending. Two main findings emerge on calibrating our model to an unusual sub-household dataset for Senegal. First, for all except education spending, our results are consistent with the separable structures found in two-stage bargaining and collective models of the household. Second, there are large biases in standard household Engel-curve estimates when compared to consistently aggregated sub-household estimates, though in differing degrees and directions depending on the type of commodity. NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We state a sufficient condition under which choice data alone suffices to identify consumer preferences when choices are not fully informed. Suppose that: (i) the data generating process is a search model in which the attribute hidden to consumers is observed by the econometrician; (ii) if a consumer searches good j, she also searches goods which are better than j in terms of the non-hidden component of utility; and (iii) consumers choose the good that maximizes overall utility among searched goods. Canonical models will be biased: the value of the hidden attribute will be understated because consumers will be unresponsive to variation in the attribute for goods that they do not search. Under the conditions above and additional mild restrictions, an alternative method of recovering preferences using cross derivatives of choice probabilities succeeds regardless of the search protocol and is thus robust to whether consumers are informed. The approach nests several standard models, including full information. Our methods suggest natural tests for full information and can be used to forecast how consumers will respond to additional information. We verify in a lab experiment that our approach succeeds in recovering preferences when consumers engage in costly search.

Abstract

Development outcomes come in ‘clusters’ that seem difficult to exit. Using original data from Colombia, we present evidence of the interconnection between two critical political components: state weakness and clientelism. State weakness creates the right environment for clientelism to flourish. Clientelism sets in place a structure of incentives for politicians and citizens that is detrimental to building state capacity. NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Innovation is important for firm performance and broader economic growth. But breakthrough innovations necessarily require greater risk-taking than more incremental approaches. To understand how managers respond to uncertainty when making research and development decisions, we conducted three experiments with master’s degree students in a program focused on the intersection of business and technology. Study participants were asked to choose whether to fund hypothetical research projects using a process that mirrors real-world research and development funding decisions. The experiments provided financial rewards that disproportionately encouraged the choice of higher-risk projects. Despite these incentives, most participants chose lower-risk projects at the expense of projects more likely to generate a large payoff. Abstract

Abstract

We study the spending response of first-time borrowers to an overdraft facility and elicit their preferences, beliefs, and motives through a FinTech application. Users increase their spending permanently, lower their savings rate, and reallocate spending from non-discretionary to discretionary goods. Interestingly, liquid users react more than others but do not tap into negative deposits. The credit line acts as a form of insurance.
