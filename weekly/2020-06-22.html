Economic Uncertainty Before and During the COVID-19 Pandemic

COVID-19大流行之前和期间的经济不确定性

David Altig, Scott R. Baker, Jose Maria Barrero, Nicholas Bloom, Philip Bunn, Scarlet Chen, Steven J. Davis, Julia Leather, Brent H. Meyer, Emil Mihaylov, Paul Mizen, Nicholas B. Parker, Thomas Renault, Pawel Smietanka, Greg Thwaites

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 英国、美国

Abstract

We consider several economic uncertainty indicators for the US and UK before and during the COVID-19 pandemic: implied stock market volatility, newspaper-based economic policy uncertainty, twitter chatter about economic uncertainty, subjective uncertainty about future business growth, and disagreement among professional forecasters about future GDP growth. Three results emerge. First, all indicators show huge uncertainty jumps in reaction to the pandemic and its economic fallout. Indeed, most indicators reach their highest values on record. Second, peak amplitudes differ greatly – from a rise of around 100% (relative to January 2020) in two-year implied volatility on the S&P 500 and subjective uncertainty around year-ahead sales for UK firms to a 20-fold rise in forecaster disagreement about UK growth. Third, time paths also differ: Implied volatility rose rapidly from late February, peaked in mid-March, and fell back by late March as stock prices began to recover. In contrast, broader measures of uncertainty peaked later and then plateaued, as job losses mounted, highlighting the difference in uncertainty measures between Wall Street and Main Street.

中文摘要

我们考察了COVID-19大流行之前和期间美国和英国的几个经济不确定性指标：隐含股票市场波动率、基于报纸的经济政策不确定性、关于经济不确定性的推特讨论、对未来业务增长的主观不确定性，以及专业预测者对未来GDP增长的分歧。出现了三个结果。首先，所有指标都显示出对疫情及其经济影响反应的巨大不确定性跳跃。事实上，大多数指标达到了有记录以来的最高值。其次，峰值幅度差异很大——从标普500指数两年期隐含波动率和英国企业对未来一年销售的主观不确定性上升约100%（相对于2020年1月），到预测者对英国增长的分歧上升20倍。第三，时间路径也不同：隐含波动率从2月下旬迅速上升，3月中旬达到峰值，然后在3月下旬随着股价开始回升而回落。相比之下，更广泛的不确定性指标峰值出现较晚，然后趋于平稳，因为失业人数增加，这凸显了华尔街和主街之间不确定性指标的差异。

Misinformation During a Pandemic

大流行期间的错误信息

Leonardo Bursztyn, Aakaash Rao, Christopher P. Roth, David H. Yanagizawa-Drott

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Media outlets often present diverging, even conﬂicting, perspectives on reality — not only informing, but potentially misinforming audiences. We study the extent to which misinformation broadcast on mass media at the early stages of the coronavirus pandemic inﬂuenced health outcomes. We ﬁrst document large diﬀerences in content between the two most popular cable news shows in the US, both on the same network, and in the adoption of preventative behaviors among viewers of these shows. Through both a selection-on-observables strategy and an instrumental variable approach, we ﬁnd that areas with greater exposure to the show downplaying the threat of COVID-19 experienced a greater number of cases and deaths. We assess magnitudes through an epidemiological model highlighting the role of externalities and provide evidence that contemporaneous information exposure is a key underlying mechanism.

中文摘要

媒体机构经常呈现对现实的分歧甚至冲突的观点——不仅提供信息，还可能误导受众。我们研究了在冠状病毒大流行早期阶段，大众媒体传播的错误信息在多大程度上影响了健康结果。我们首先记录了美国同一网络的两个最受欢迎的有线新闻节目在内容上的巨大差异，以及这些节目观众在采取预防行为方面的差异。通过基于可观测变量的选择策略和工具变量方法，我们发现，对淡化COVID-19威胁的节目曝光度更高的地区，经历了更多的病例和死亡。我们通过一个强调外部性作用的流行病学模型评估了影响程度，并提供了证据表明同期信息暴露是一个关键的基础机制。

Treasury Inconvenience Yields during the COVID-19 Crisis

新冠危机期间的国债不便收益率

Zhiguo He, Stefan Nagel, Zhaogang Song

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

In sharp contrast to most previous crisis episodes, the Treasury market experienced severe stress and illiquidity during the COVID-19 crisis, raising concerns that the safe-haven status of U.S. Treasuries may be eroding. We document large shifts in Treasury ownership and temporary accumulation of Treasury and reverse repo positions on dealer balance sheets during this period. We build a dynamic equilibrium asset pricing model in which dealers subject to regulatory balance sheet constraints intermediate demand/supply shocks from habitat agents and provide repo financing to levered investors. The model predicts that Treasury inconvenience yields, measured as the spread between Treasuries and overnight-index swap rates (OIS), as well as spreads between dealers’ reverse repo and repo rates, should be highly positive during the COVID-19 crisis, which are confirmed in the data. The same model framework, adapted to the institutional setting in 2007-2009, also helps explain the negative Treasury-OIS spread observed during the Great Recession.

中文摘要

与以往大多数危机事件形成鲜明对比的是，美国国债市场在新冠危机期间经历了严重的压力和流动性不足，引发了对美国国债避险地位可能正在削弱的担忧。我们记录了这一时期国债所有权的巨大转变，以及交易商资产负债表上国债和逆回购头寸的暂时积累。我们构建了一个动态均衡资产定价模型，在该模型中，受监管资产负债表约束的交易商为来自栖息地代理人的需求/供给冲击提供中介，并向杠杆投资者提供回购融资。该模型预测，在新冠危机期间，国债的不便收益率（以国债与隔夜指数互换利率（OIS）之间的利差衡量）以及交易商逆回购与回购利率之间的利差应高度为正，这些预测在数据中得到了证实。同样的模型框架，经过调整以适应2007-2009年的制度环境，也有助于解释在大衰退期间观察到的负国债-OIS利差。

Conservation Co-Benefits from Air Pollution Regulation: Evidence from Birds

空气污染监管的保护协同效益：来自鸟类的证据

Yuanning Liang, Ivan J. Rudik, Eric Zou, Alison Johnston, Amanda D. Rodewald, Catherine L. Kling

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

Massive wildlife losses over the past 50 years have brought new urgency to identifying both the drivers of population decline and potential solutions. We provide the first large-scale evidence that air pollution, specifically ozone, is associated with declines in bird abundance in the United States. We show that an air pollution regulation limiting ozone precursors emissions has delivered substantial benefits to bird conservation. Our estimates imply that air quality improvements over the past four decades have stemmed the decline in bird populations, averting the loss of 1.5 billion birds, approximately 20 percent of current totals. Our results highlight that in addition to protecting human health, air pollution regulations have previously unrecognized and unquantified conservation co-benefits.

中文摘要

过去50年间大规模的野生动物损失，为识别种群下降的驱动因素和潜在解决方案带来了新的紧迫性。我们首次提供了大规模证据，表明空气污染（特别是臭氧）与美国鸟类数量的下降有关。我们证明，一项限制臭氧前体物排放的空气污染法规为鸟类保护带来了显著效益。我们的估算表明，过去四十年间的空气质量改善遏制了鸟类种群的下降，避免了15亿只鸟类的损失，约占当前总数的20%。我们的结果强调，除了保护人类健康外，空气污染法规还具有此前未被认识和量化的保护协同效益。

Food, Fuel and the Domesday Economy

食物、燃料与末日审判书经济

Juan Moreno-Cruz, M. Scott Taylor

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper develops a theory where access to food and fuel energy is critical to the location, number, and size of human settlements. By combining our theory with a simple Malthusian mechanism, we generate predictions for the distribution of economic activity and population across geographic space. We evaluate the model using data drawn from the very first census undertaken in the English language - the Domesday census - commissioned by William the Conqueror in 1086 A.D. Using G.I.S. data and techniques we find strong evidence that Malthusian forces determined the population size and the number of settlements in Domesday England.

中文摘要

本文发展了一个理论，其中获取食物和燃料能源对于人类定居点的位置、数量和规模至关重要。通过将我们的理论与一个简单的马尔萨斯机制相结合，我们生成了关于经济活动和人口在地理空间上分布的预测。我们使用来自英语世界首次人口普查——即征服者威廉于公元1086年下令进行的《末日审判书》普查——的数据来评估该模型。利用地理信息系统（G.I.S.）数据和技术，我们发现了强有力的证据，表明马尔萨斯力量决定了末日审判书时期英格兰的人口规模和定居点数量。

Nondogmatic Climate Policy

非教条式气候政策

Niko Jaakkola, Antony Millner

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Disagreements about normative aspects of social time preferences have led to estimates of the Social Cost of Carbon (SCC) that differ by orders of magnitude. We investigate how disagreements about the SCC change if planners are non-dogmatic, i.e., they admit the possibility of a change in their normative views, and internalise the preferences of future selves. Although non-dogmatic planners may disagree about all the contentious aspects of social time preferences, disagreements about the SCC reduce dramatically. Admitting the possibility of a change in views once every 40 years results in a 4.6-fold reduction in the range of recommended SCCs.

中文摘要

关于社会时间偏好规范性方面的分歧，导致了对碳社会成本（SCC）的估算相差数个数量级。我们研究了如果规划者是非教条式的，即他们承认其规范性观点可能发生改变，并内化未来自我的偏好，那么关于SCC的分歧将如何变化。尽管非教条式规划者可能在社会时间偏好的所有有争议方面存在分歧，但关于SCC的分歧会显著减少。承认每40年观点可能发生一次改变，会导致建议的SCC范围缩小4.6倍。

Betting on the House: Subjective Expectations and Market Choices

押注房产：主观预期与市场选择

Nicolas L. Bottan, Ricardo Perez-Truglia

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

Home price expectations play a central role in macroeconomics and finance. However, there is little direct evidence on how home price expectations affect market choices. We provide the first causal evidence based on a large-scale, high-stakes, and naturally occurring field experiment in the United States. We mailed letters with information on trends in home prices to 57,910 homeowners who had listed their homes on the market. Collectively, these homes were worth $34 billion. We randomized the information contained in the mailing to create non-deceptive, exogenous variation in the subjects’ home price expectations. We then used rich administrative data to measure the effects of these information shocks on the subjects’ market choices. We found that, consistent with economic theory, higher home price expectations caused a reduction in the probability of selling the home. These effects were highly statistically significant, economically large in magnitude, and robust to a number of sharp checks. Our results indicated that market choices were highly elastic to expectations: a 1 percentage point increase in home price expectations caused a 2.63 percentage point reduction in the probability of selling the property within 12 weeks.

中文摘要

房价预期在宏观经济学和金融学中扮演着核心角色。然而，关于房价预期如何影响市场选择的直接证据很少。我们基于美国一项大规模、高 stakes 且自然发生的实地实验，提供了首个因果证据。我们向57,910名已在市场上挂牌出售房屋的房主邮寄了包含房价趋势信息的信件。这些房屋的总价值为340亿美元。我们对邮件中包含的信息进行了随机化处理，以在受试者的房价预期中创造非欺骗性的外生变异。然后，我们使用丰富的行政数据来衡量这些信息冲击对受试者市场选择的影响。我们发现，与经济理论一致，更高的房价预期导致出售房屋的概率降低。这些影响在统计上高度显著，经济规模巨大，并且对一系列严格的检验具有稳健性。我们的结果表明，市场选择对预期具有高度弹性：房价预期每增加1个百分点，在12周内出售房产的概率就会降低2.63个百分点。

Financial Vulnerability and Risks to Growth in Emerging Markets

新兴市场的金融脆弱性与增长风险

Viral V. Acharya, Soumya Bhadury, Jay Surti

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper introduces a new financial vulnerability index for emerging market economies by exploiting key differences in their business cycles relative to those of advanced economies. Information on the domestic price of risk, cost of dollar hedging and market-based measures of bank vulnerability combine to generate indexes significantly more effective in capturing macro-financial vulnerability and stress compared to those based on information in trade and global factors.
