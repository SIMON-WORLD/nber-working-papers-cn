No. 1论文详情NBER w26726

Professional Interactions and Hiring Decisions: Evidence from the Federal Judiciary

专业互动与招聘决策：来自联邦司法系统的证据

Marco Battaglini, Jorgen M. Harris, Eleonora Patacchini

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine the effect of hearing cases alongside female judicial colleagues on the probability that a federal judge hires a female law clerk. Federal judges are assigned to cases and to judicial panels at random and have few limitations on their choices of law clerks: these two features make the federal court system a unique environment in which to study the effect of professional interactions and beliefs in organizations. We constructed a unique dataset by aggregating federal case records from 2007-2017 to collect information on federal judicial panels, and by merging this data with judicial hiring information from the Judicial Yellow Book, a directory of federal judges and clerks. We find that a one standard deviation increase in the fraction of co-panelists who are female increases a judge’s likelihood of hiring a female clerk by 4 percentage points. This finding suggests that increases in the diversity of the upper rungs of a profession can shift attitudes in a way that creates opportunities at the entry level of a profession.

中文摘要

我们研究了与女性司法同事共同审理案件对联邦法官雇佣女性法律助理概率的影响。联邦法官被随机分配案件和司法小组，且在选择法律助理方面几乎没有限制：这两个特点使联邦法院系统成为研究组织中专业互动和信念影响的独特环境。我们通过汇总2007-2017年的联邦案件记录构建了一个独特数据集，以收集联邦司法小组的信息，并将这些数据与《司法黄页》（一份联邦法官和助理名录）中的司法招聘信息合并。我们发现，合议庭中女性比例每增加一个标准差，法官雇佣女性助理的可能性就会提高4个百分点。这一发现表明，职业高层多样性的增加可以改变态度，从而在职业入门层面创造机会。

Is the Cure Worse than the Disease? Unintended Effects of Payment Reform in a Quantity-based Transfer Program

治疗比疾病更糟糕吗？基于数量转移支付项目中支付改革的意外效应

Katherine Meckel

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Quantity vouchers are used in redistributive programs to shield participants from price variation and alter their consumption patterns. However, because participants are insensitive to prices, vendors of program goods are incentivized to price discriminate between program and non-program customers. I study these trade-offs in the context of a reform to reduce price discrimination in the Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC), which provides a quantity voucher for nutritious foods to low-income mothers and children. The reform caused vendors to drop out, reducing program take-up. In addition, smaller vendors increased prices charged to non-WIC shoppers by 6.4%.

中文摘要

数量券被用于再分配项目，以保护参与者免受价格波动影响并改变其消费模式。然而，由于参与者对价格不敏感，项目商品的供应商有动机在项目客户和非项目客户之间进行价格歧视。我在妇女、婴儿和儿童补充营养计划（WIC）的背景下研究了这些权衡，该计划为低收入母亲和儿童提供营养食品的数量券，而改革旨在减少价格歧视。改革导致供应商退出，降低了项目参与率。此外，较小的供应商对非WIC购物者的价格提高了6.4%。

No. 4论文详情NBER w26722

Hours and Wages

工时与工资

Alexander Bick, Adam Blandin, Richard Rogerson

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We document two robust features of the cross-sectional distribution of usual weekly hours and hourly wages. First, usual weekly hours are heavily concentrated around 40 hours, while at the same time a substantial share of total hours come from individuals who work more than 50 hours. Second, mean hourly wages are non-monotonic across the usual hours distribution, with a peak at 50 hours. We develop and estimate a model of labor supply to account for these features. The novel feature of our model is that earnings are non-linear in hours, with the extent of nonlinearity varying over the hours distribution. Our estimates imply significant wage penalties for individuals that deviate from 40 hours in either direction, leading to a large mass of individuals that work 40 hours and are not very responsive to shocks. This has important implications for the role of labor supply as a mechanism for self-insurance in a standard heterogeneous agent-incomplete markets model and for empirical strategies designed to estimate labor supply parameters.

中文摘要

我们记录了通常每周工时和小时工资的横截面分布的两个稳健特征。首先，通常每周工时高度集中在40小时左右，同时总工时的很大一部分来自工作超过50小时的个体。其次，平均小时工资在通常工时分布上呈现非单调性，在50小时处达到峰值。我们开发并估计了一个劳动供给模型来解释这些特征。我们模型的新颖之处在于，收入在工时上是非线性的，且非线性程度随工时分布而变化。我们的估计表明，偏离40小时（无论方向如何）的个体会面临显著的工资惩罚，导致大量个体工作40小时且对冲击反应不敏感。这对劳动供给作为标准异质性代理人-不完全市场模型中自我保险机制的作用，以及用于估计劳动供给参数的实证策略具有重要意义。

Risk Premium Shocks Can Create Inefficient Recessions

风险溢价冲击可能导致低效衰退

Sebastian Di Tella, Robert E. Hall

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop an equilibrium theory of business cycles driven by spikes in risk premiums that depress business demand for capital and labor. Aggregate shocks increase firms’ uninsurable idiosyncratic risk and raise risk premiums. We show that risk shocks can create quantitatively realistic recessions, with contractions in employment, consumption, and investment. Business cycles are inefficient—output and employment fall too much during recessions, compared to the constrained-efficient allocation, and consumption should rise. Optimal policy involves stimulating employment and consumption during recessions.

中文摘要

我们发展了一个由风险溢价飙升驱动的商业周期均衡理论，这种飙升抑制了企业对资本和劳动力的需求。总体冲击增加了企业不可分散的异质性风险，并提高了风险溢价。我们表明，风险冲击可以产生数量上逼真的衰退，伴随就业、消费和投资的收缩。商业周期是低效的——与受约束的有效配置相比，衰退期间产出和就业下降过多，而消费本应上升。最优政策涉及在衰退期间刺激就业和消费。

Structural Reforms and Elections: Evidence from a World-Wide New Dataset

结构改革与选举：来自全球新数据集的证据

Alberto F. Alesina, Davide Furceri, Jonathan D. Ostry, Chris Papageorgiou, Dennis P. Quinn

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We assemble two unique databases. One is on reforms in domestic finance, external finance, trade, product markets and labor markets, which covers 90 advanced and developing economies from 1973 to 2014. The other is on electoral results and timing of elections. In the 66 democracies considered in the paper, we show that liberalizing reforms engender benefits for the economy, but they materialize only gradually over time. Partly because of this delayed effect, and possibly because voters are impatient or do not anticipate future benefits, liberalizing reforms are costly to incumbents when implemented close to elections. We also find that the electoral effects depend on the state of the economy at the time of reform: reforms are penalized during contractions; liberalizing reforms undertaken in expansions are often rewarded. Voters seem to attribute current economic conditions to the reforms without fully internalizing the delay that it takes for reforms to bear fruit.

中文摘要

我们整理了两个独特数据库。一个涉及国内金融、外部金融、贸易、产品市场和劳动力市场的改革，覆盖1973年至2014年的90个发达和发展中经济体。另一个涉及选举结果和选举时间。在本文考虑的66个民主国家中，我们表明自由化改革为经济带来好处，但这些好处是随时间逐渐实现的。部分由于这种延迟效应，并且可能因为选民缺乏耐心或未预期到未来收益，当改革在临近选举时实施，对现任者来说代价高昂。我们还发现，选举效应取决于改革时的经济状况：在经济收缩期间，改革会受到惩罚；而在扩张期间进行的自由化改革通常会得到奖励。选民似乎将当前经济状况归因于改革，而没有充分内化改革产生效果所需的延迟。

No. 7论文详情NBER w26719

The Wage Penalty of Regional Accents

地区口音的工资惩罚

Jeffrey Grogger, Andreas Steinmayr, Joachim Winter

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 德国

Abstract

Previous work has documented that speaking one’s native language with an accent distinct from the mainstream is associated with lower wages. In this study, we seek to estimate the causal effect of speaking with a distinctive regional accent, disentangling the effect of the accent from that of omitted variables. We collected data on workers’ speech in Germany, a country with wide variation in regional dialects. We use a variety of strategies in estimation, including an instrumental variables strategy in which the instruments are based on research findings from the linguistics of accent acquisition. All of our estimators show that speaking with a distinctive regional accent reduces wages by an amount that is comparable to the gender wage gap. We also find that workers with distinctive regional accents tend to sort away from occupations that demand high levels of face-to-face contact, consistent with various occupational sorting models.

中文摘要

先前的研究已表明，用与主流口音不同的口音说母语与较低的工资相关。在本研究中，我们试图估计说独特地区口音的因果效应，将口音的影响与遗漏变量的影响区分开来。我们收集了德国工人的语音数据，该国地区方言差异很大。我们在估计中使用了多种策略，包括一种工具变量策略，其中工具基于口音习得语言学的研究发现。我们所有的估计量都表明，说独特的地区口音会使工资降低，其幅度与性别工资差距相当。我们还发现，具有独特地区口音的工人倾向于远离需要高频率面对面接触的职业，这与各种职业分类模型一致。

Efficiency Versus Equity in the Provision of In-Kind Benefits: Evidence from Cost Containment in the California WIC Program

实物福利供给中的效率与公平：来自加州WIC项目成本控制的证据

Katherine Meckel, Maya Rossin-Slater, Lindsey M. Uniat

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

In many U.S. public programs, the government contracts with private firms to deliver in-kind benefits to recipients. These public-private partnerships generate agency problems that could drive up costs and lower program efficiency, but cost containment regulations may discourage firm participation and reduce access among eligible households. We examine these trade-offs in the context of California’s Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC), which provides vouchers to low-income pregnant women and children under five to obtain free food packages from private vendors, and has complex rules about eligible products. We use variation from a 2012 cost containment reform, which resulted in a 55 percent drop in the number of small vendors, and examine how local access to small vendors affects WIC take-up among pregnant women. We find that within-ZIP-code access to small vendors raises the likelihood of WIC take-up among first-time mothers, and that this effect is stronger for foreign-born than U.S.-born women and exists even for mothers who also have access to a larger WIC vendor. Our findings suggest that small vendors are uniquely effective at lowering barriers to take-up among subgroups of women with high program learning costs, and that cost containment reforms, which frequently target these vendors, may have unintended consequences of inequitably reducing program access.

中文摘要

在美国许多公共项目中，政府与私营企业签订合同，向受益人提供实物福利。这些公私合作伙伴关系会产生代理问题，可能推高成本并降低项目效率，但成本控制法规可能会抑制企业参与，并减少符合条件的家庭的获取机会。我们以加州妇女、婴儿和儿童特殊补充营养计划（WIC）为背景研究这些权衡。该计划向低收入孕妇和五岁以下儿童提供代金券，以便从私营供应商处获得免费食品包，并对合格产品有复杂的规定。我们利用2012年成本控制改革带来的变化（该改革导致小型供应商数量下降55%），考察当地小型供应商的可及性如何影响孕妇的WIC参与率。我们发现，在同一邮政编码区域内，小型供应商的可及性提高了初产妇参与WIC的可能性，并且这种效应在外籍出生的女性中比在美国出生的女性中更强，即使母亲也能接触到更大的WIC供应商，这种效应依然存在。我们的研究结果表明，小型供应商在降低具有高项目学习成本的女性亚群体的参与障碍方面具有独特效果，而经常针对这些供应商的成本控制改革可能会产生意想不到的后果，即不公平地减少项目获取机会。

Do Cash Windfalls Affect Wages? Evidence from R&D Grants to Small Firms

现金意外之财会影响工资吗？来自小企业研发资助的证据

Sabrina T. Howell, J. David Brown

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

This paper examines how employee earnings at small firms respond to a cash flow shock in the form of a government R&D grant. We use ranking data on applicant firms, which we link to IRS W2 earnings and other U.S. Census Bureau datasets. In a regression discontinuity design, we find that the grant increases average earnings with a rent-sharing elasticity of 0.07 (0.21) at the employee (firm) level. The beneficiaries are incumbent employees who were present at the firm before the award. Among incumbent employees, the effect increases with worker tenure. The grant also leads to higher employment and revenue, but productivity growth cannot fully explain the immediate effect on earnings. Instead, the data and a grantee survey are consistent with a backloaded wage contract channel, in which employees of financially constrained firms initially accept relatively low wages and are paid more when cash is available.

中文摘要

本文研究了小企业员工的收入如何响应以政府研发资助形式出现的现金流冲击。我们使用申请企业的排名数据，并将其与美国国税局W2收入数据及其他美国人口普查局数据集相关联。在断点回归设计中，我们发现，资助提高了平均收入，员工层面的租金分享弹性为0.07，企业层面为0.21。受益者是资助前已在企业任职的在职员工。在职员工中，效应随工作年限增加而增强。资助还导致就业和收入增加，但生产率增长无法完全解释对收入的即时影响。相反，数据和受资助者调查与一种后置工资合同渠道一致，即财务受限企业的员工最初接受相对较低的工资，并在现金可用时获得更多报酬。

Forecasting the Results of Experiments: Piloting an Elicitation Strategy

预测实验结果：试点一种启发策略

Stefano DellaVigna, Nicholas Otis, Eva Vivalt

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Forecasts of experimental results can clarify the interpretation of research results, mitigate publication bias, and improve experimental designs. We collect forecasts of the results of three Registered Reports preliminarily accepted to the Journal of Development Economics, randomly varying four features: (1) small versus large reference values; (2) whether predictions are in raw units or standard deviations; (3) text-entry versus slider responses; and (4) small versus large slider bounds. Forecasts are generally robust to elicitation features, though wider slider bounds are associated with higher forecasts throughout the forecast distribution. We make preliminary recommendations on how many forecasts should be gathered.

中文摘要

对实验结果的预测可以澄清研究结果的解释，减轻发表偏倚，并改进实验设计。我们收集了初步被《发展经济学杂志》接受的三份注册报告的预测结果，随机变化四个特征：（1）小参考值与大参考值；（2）预测以原始单位还是标准差表示；（3）文本输入与滑块响应；（4）小滑块边界与大滑块边界。预测通常对启发特征具有稳健性，尽管更宽的滑块边界与整个预测分布中更高的预测值相关。我们就应收集多少预测提出了初步建议。

No. 11论文详情NBER w26715

Technology and Big Data Are Changing Economics: Mining Text to Track Methods

技术与大数据正在改变经济学：通过文本挖掘追踪方法

Janet Currie, Henrik Kleven, Esmée Zwiers

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The last 40 years have seen huge innovations in computing technology and data availability. Data derived from millions of administrative records or by using (as we do) new methods of data generation such as text mining are now common. New data often requires new methods, which in turn can inspire new data collection. If history is any guide, some methods will stick and others will prove to be a flash in the pan. However, the larger trends towards demanding greater credibility and transparency from researchers in applied economics and a “collage” approach to assembling evidence will likely continue.

中文摘要

过去40年，计算技术和数据可用性经历了巨大创新。源自数百万行政记录的数据，或通过使用（如我们所做的）文本挖掘等新数据生成方法获得的数据，现已普遍。新数据通常需要新方法，而新方法反过来又能激发新数据收集。如果以史为鉴，一些方法将留存，而另一些则会被证明是昙花一现。然而，要求应用经济学研究人员提高可信度和透明度，以及采用“拼贴”方法汇集证据的大趋势，很可能会持续下去。

Abstract

Abstract

The ratio of revenue to inputs differs greatly across plants within countries such as the U.S. and India. Such gaps may reflect misallocation which hinders aggregate productivity. But differences in measured average products need not reflect differences in true marginal products. We propose a way to estimate the gaps in true marginal products in the presence of measurement error. Christoffer Koch, Gary Richardson, Patrick Van Horn

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Countercyclical capital buffers (CCyBs) are an old idea recently resurrected. CCyBs compel banks at the core of financial systems to accumulate capital during expansions so that they are better able to sustain operations during downturns. To gauge the potential impact of modern CCyBs, we compare the behavior of large and highly-connected commercial banks during booms before the Great Depression and Great Recession. Before the former, core banks did not expect bailouts and were subject to regulations that incentivized capital accumulation during booms. Before the later, core banks expected bailouts and kept capital levels close to regulatory minima. Our analysis indicates that the pre-Depression regulatory regime induced money-center banks to build capital buffers between 3% and 5% of total assets during economic expansions, which is up to double the maximum modern CCyB. These buffers enabled those banks to continue operations without government assistance during severe crises. This historical analogy indicates that modern countercyclical buffers may achieve their immediate goals of protecting core banks during crises but raises questions about whether they will contribute to overall financial stability.

Abstract

A seminal study of persistence in mutual fund performance is Carhart (1997), who found that U.S. equity mutual funds’ past-year returns positively predict their raw excess return and one-factor alpha over the next year. Based on these results, an investor may believe that she can earn higher returns by buying mutual funds with high past-year returns. We are able to replicate Carhart’s results in his 1963-1993 sample period, but we find that significant performance persistence does not exist in the 1994-2018 period.
