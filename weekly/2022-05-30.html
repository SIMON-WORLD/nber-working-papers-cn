No. 1论文详情NBER w30097

The Effects of Alcohol Excise Tax Increases by Drinking Level and by Income Level

按饮酒水平和收入水平划分的酒精消费税上调效应

Henry Saffer, Markus Gehrsitz, Michael Grossman

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The alcohol industry argues that alcohol excise taxes do not reduce heavy drinking because of substitutions to lower-cost products and that these taxes disproportionately burden low-income drinkers. Alternatively, some economists have argued that increases in alcohol excise taxes reduce heavy alcohol consumption. Using data from the Nielsen Homescan we investigate the effects of a large excise tax increase that raised alcohol prices. The results show that heavy drinkers reduce purchases, and this reduction is no different than the reductions by other drinkers. The results also show that only low-income drinkers pay more for ethanol after the tax increase.

中文摘要

酒精行业认为，由于消费者会转向低价产品，酒精消费税并不能减少重度饮酒，而且这些税种对低收入饮酒者造成了不成比例的负担。另一些经济学家则认为，提高酒精消费税能减少重度酒精消费。利用尼尔森家庭扫描数据，我们研究了一次大幅提高酒精价格的消费税上调的影响。结果显示，重度饮酒者减少了购买量，且减少幅度与其他饮酒者并无差异。结果还表明，只有低收入饮酒者在增税后为乙醇支付了更多费用。

No. 2论文详情NBER w30096

Inflation Past, Present and Future: Fiscal Shocks, Fed Response, and Fiscal Limits

通胀的过去、现在与未来：财政冲击、美联储应对与财政极限

John H. Cochrane

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Our current inflation stemmed from a fiscal shock. The Fed is slow to react. Why? Will the Fed's slow reaction spur more inflation? I write a simple model that encompasses the Fed's mild projections and its slow reaction, and traditional views that inflation will surge without swift rate rises. The key question is whether expectations are forward looking or backward looking. If expectations are forward looking, the Fed is right, and inflation will eventually fade without a period of high real interest rates. Price stickiness means inflation will persist past an initial shock. To reduce inflation, fiscal and monetary policy must be coordinated. Without fiscal contraction, an unpleasant arithmetic holds: The Fed can reduce inflation now, but only by increasing inflation later. If the Fed wishes to lower inflation durably via interest rate rises, those must come with fiscal support to pay higher costs on the debt and a windfall to bondholders. Coordinated fiscal, monetary and microeconomic reforms can, and have, swiftly eliminated inflation without the major recession of the early 1980s. Nonetheless, in the very long run, the central bank controls the price level.

中文摘要

我们当前的通货膨胀源于一次财政冲击。美联储反应迟缓。原因何在？美联储的缓慢反应是否会引发更多通胀？我构建了一个简单模型，该模型涵盖了美联储温和的预测及其缓慢的反应，以及认为若不迅速加息通胀将飙升的传统观点。关键问题在于预期是前瞻性的还是后顾性的。如果预期是前瞻性的，那么美联储就是正确的，通胀最终会消退，而无需经历一段高实际利率的时期。价格粘性意味着通胀会在初始冲击后持续存在。要降低通胀，财政政策和货币政策必须协调一致。如果没有财政紧缩，一种令人不快的算术就会成立：美联储现在可以降低通胀，但代价是未来通胀上升。如果美联储希望通过加息来持久地降低通胀，那么这些加息必须伴随财政支持，以支付更高的债务成本，并给债券持有人带来意外收益。协调一致的财政、货币和微观经济改革能够且已经迅速消除了通胀，而无需像1980年代初那样经历严重衰退。尽管如此，从长远来看，中央银行控制着价格水平。

No. 3论文详情NBER w30095

Individual Earnings and Family Income: Dynamics and Distribution

个人收入与家庭收入：动态与分布

Joseph G. Altonji, Disa M. Hynsjö, Ivan Vidangos

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We review research on the dynamics and distribution of individual earnings and family income. We start with univariate earnings models, which dominate the literature and are often used as the exogenous component of family income in structural models of saving. We present a version of the linear model that nests most of the specifications that have been used in the literature, and then discuss recent papers that stress nonnormal shocks, nonlinear and age-dependent processes, and heterogeneous model parameters. The recent work provides a much richer description of the nature of earnings volatility than the basic model. We then turn to models of individual earnings that are based on wages, employment, job mobility, and hours. These multivariate models permit measuring the sources of permanent differences in earnings and distinguishing among shocks that influence earnings through employment, job mobility, general productivity, or hours. Finally, we consider models of lifetime family income that integrate individual earnings, marriage (accounting for marital sorting), and earnings of a spouse (if present). We conclude by discussing directions for future work.

中文摘要

我们回顾了关于个人收入和家庭收入动态与分布的研究。我们从单变量收入模型开始，该模型在文献中占主导地位，并常在储蓄结构模型中被用作家庭收入的外生组成部分。我们提出了一个线性模型的版本，该版本涵盖了文献中使用的大多数设定，然后讨论了近期强调非正态冲击、非线性和年龄依赖过程以及异质性模型参数的论文。与基本模型相比，近期工作提供了对收入波动性本质更为丰富的描述。接着，我们转向基于工资、就业、工作流动性和工时的个人收入模型。这些多变量模型能够衡量收入永久性差异的来源，并区分通过就业、工作流动性、一般生产率或工时影响收入的冲击。最后，我们考虑了整合个人收入、婚姻（考虑婚配分类）和配偶收入（如有）的终身家庭收入模型。我们通过讨论未来工作的方向来结束本文。

Expectations Data in Structural Microeconomic Models

结构微观经济模型中的预期数据

Gizem Koşar, Cormac O'Dea

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

A growing literature uses now widely-available data on beliefs and expectations in the estimation of structural models. In this chapter, we review this literature, with an emphasis on models of individual and household behavior. We first show how expectations data have been used to relax strong assumptions about beliefs and outline how they can be used in estimation to substitute for, or as a complement to, data on choices. Next, we discuss the literature that uses different types of expectations data in the estimation of structural models. We conclude by noting directions for future research.

中文摘要

越来越多的文献在估计结构模型时使用现已广泛可得的信念和预期数据。在本章中，我们回顾了这些文献，重点关注个人和家庭行为模型。我们首先展示了预期数据如何被用来放宽关于信念的强假设，并概述了如何在估计中使用它们来替代或补充选择数据。接下来，我们讨论了在结构模型估计中使用不同类型预期数据的文献。最后，我们指出了未来研究的方向。

Center Pivot Irrigation Systems as a Form of Drought Risk Mitigation in Humid Regions

中心支轴灌溉系统作为湿润地区干旱风险缓解的一种形式

Daniel J. Cooley, Steven M. Smith

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

Irrigation in the Eastern US receives little attention compared to the West, but farmers in humid states of the US, traditionally reliant on rainfall, have more than tripled irrigation since 1978. We examine this trend in Illinois where there has been a nearly threefold increase in center pivot irrigation systems (CPIS) installations since 1988. Specifically, we analyze where and when CPIS installations occur and their benefits in terms of annual crop yield, irrigated acreage, crop selection, and reduction in drought-related insurance payouts. To do so, we create a novel data set derived from a deep learning model capable of automatically identifying the location of CPIS during drought years along with annual county level crop, weather, and insurance data. The results indicate CPIS installations in Illinois are significantly more common over alluvial aquifers after droughts. Additionally, counties with a higher presence of CPIS do not have higher average crop yields, a shift to more water intensive crops, or an expansion of cropland. However, in drought years CPIS presence does have a significant positive effect on corn yield and a significant negative effect on indemnity payments for both soybeans and corn. The results provide insights into an emerging trend of irrigation in humid regions, raising potential policy considerations for crop insurance and signaling a potential need to address water rights as demand increases.

中文摘要

与西部相比，美国东部的灌溉问题很少受到关注，但传统上依赖降雨的美国湿润州农民自1978年以来已将灌溉面积增加了两倍多。我们在伊利诺伊州考察了这一趋势，自1988年以来，该州中心支轴灌溉系统的安装量增加了近三倍。具体而言，我们分析了中心支轴灌溉系统安装的地点和时间，以及它们在年度作物产量、灌溉面积、作物选择和减少与干旱相关的保险赔付方面的效益。为此，我们创建了一个新颖的数据集，该数据集源自一个深度学习模型，能够自动识别干旱年份中心支轴灌溉系统的位置，并结合了年度县级作物、天气和保险数据。结果表明，伊利诺伊州的中心支轴灌溉系统在干旱后于冲积含水层上显著更为常见。此外，中心支轴灌溉系统存在率较高的县，其平均作物产量并未更高，也未转向更耗水的作物或扩大耕地面积。然而，在干旱年份，中心支轴灌溉系统的存在确实对玉米产量有显著的正向影响，并对大豆和玉米的赔付金额有显著的负向影响。这些结果揭示了湿润地区灌溉的新兴趋势，为作物保险提出了潜在的政策考量，并表明随着需求增加，可能需要解决水权问题。

Internal Disagreement and Disruptive Technologies

内部分歧与颠覆性技术

Joshua S. Gans

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper models the adoption by established firms of technologies that are internally disruptive in that different parts of an organization stand to lose or gain from adoption. When agents disagree with a decision they impose costs on the firm. The paper shows that any resistance to change this yields is necessarily accompanied by others aggrieved should change not occur. Thus, the firm cannot avoid disagreement costs regardless of whether they adopt the technology or not. In some cases, depending on their ability to impose costs, such firms may be more likely to adopt technologies as a result of internal disagreement.

中文摘要

本文对成熟企业采用那些在内部具有颠覆性的技术进行了建模，这种颠覆性体现在组织的不同部门会因采用该技术而受损或获益。当代理人不同意某项决策时，他们会给企业带来成本。本文表明，由此产生的任何变革阻力都必然伴随着如果变革不发生则其他方会感到不满。因此，无论企业是否采用该技术，都无法避免分歧成本。在某些情况下，取决于代理人施加成本的能力，这类企业可能因内部分歧而更有可能采用技术。

A Theory of Visionary Disruption

愿景式颠覆理论

Joshua S. Gans

NBER 工作论文 · 理论与方法 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Exploitation of disruptive technologies often requires resource deployment that creates conflict if there are divergent beliefs regarding the efficacy of a new technology. This arises when a visionary agent has more optimistic beliefs about a technological opportunity. Exploration in the form of experiments can be persuasive when beliefs differ by mitigating disagreement and its costs. This paper examines experimental choice when experiments need to persuade as well as inform. It is shown that, due to resource constraints, persuasion factors more highly for entrepreneurial than incumbent firms. However, incumbent firms, despite being able to redeploy resources using authority, are constrained in adoption as exploration cannot mitigate the costs of disagreement

中文摘要

颠覆性技术的利用通常需要资源部署，如果对新技术的有效性存在不同信念，就会产生冲突。当一位有远见的代理人对技术机会持有更乐观的信念时，这种情况就会出现。当信念不同时，以实验形式进行的探索可以通过减少分歧及其成本而具有说服力。本文考察了当实验需要既说服又告知时的实验选择。研究表明，由于资源约束，说服因素对创业企业比对在位企业更为重要。然而，尽管在位企业能够利用权威重新部署资源，但它们在采用方面受到限制，因为探索无法减轻分歧的成本。

The Effects of Public Housing on Children: Evidence from Colombia

公共住房对儿童的影响：来自哥伦比亚的证据

Adriana Camacho, Valentina Duque, Michael Gilraine, Fabio Sanchez

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 哥伦比亚

Abstract

We analyze the effect of Colombia's ambitious "Free Housing" program on children's educational outcomes. The program was generous, giving free housing to beneficiaries in desirable areas. We evaluate the program by leveraging housing lotteries and linking applicants to their children. We find that public housing increases high school graduation by seven percentage points – a seventeen percent increase relative to the control mean – and boosts exit exam scores and college-going. Using a survey to explore mechanisms, lottery winners report better environmental conditions and shorter commute times. Their children also attend better schools and live in neighborhoods with less crime.

中文摘要

我们分析了哥伦比亚雄心勃勃的“免费住房”项目对儿童教育成果的影响。该项目十分慷慨，向理想地区的受益者免费提供住房。我们通过利用住房抽签并将申请者与其子女关联来评估该项目。我们发现，公共住房使高中毕业率提高了七个百分点——相对于对照组均值增加了百分之十七——并提高了毕业考试分数和大学入学率。通过一项调查来探索机制，抽签获胜者报告了更好的环境条件和更短的通勤时间。他们的子女也就读于更好的学校，并居住在犯罪率较低的社区。

No. 9论文详情NBER w30089

The Rest of the World’s Dollar-Weighted Return on U.S. Treasurys

世界其他地区对美国国债的美元加权回报率

Zhengyang Jiang, Arvind Krishnamurthy, Hanno Lustig

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

Since 1980, foreign investors have timed their purchases and sales of U.S. Treasurys to yield particularly low returns. Their annual “dollar-weighted” returns, measured by the internal rate of return on their purchases and sales of Treasury bonds, are over 3.26 percentage points (pp) lower than a buy-and-hold strategy over the same horizon. Their returns are 1.62 pp lower than the returns earned by domestic investors. We also explore the heterogeneity across foreign investors, and find that official investors and developing country investors underperform more than other foreign investors. Our results are consistent with theories in which foreign investors are price-inelastic buyers of safe dollar assets, and increase their demand for dollar assets in stress periods.

中文摘要

自1980年以来，外国投资者通过择时买卖美国国债，获得了异常低的回报。以他们买卖美国国债的内部收益率衡量的年度“美元加权”回报率，比同期买入并持有策略的回报率低3.26个百分点以上。他们的回报率比国内投资者的回报率低1.62个百分点。我们还探讨了外国投资者之间的异质性，发现官方投资者和发展中国家投资者的表现比其他外国投资者更差。我们的结果与以下理论一致：外国投资者是安全美元资产的价格无弹性买家，并且在压力时期会增加对美元资产的需求。

Grads on the Go: Measuring College-Specific Labor Markets for Graduates

流动的毕业生：衡量大学特定毕业生劳动力市场

Johnathan G. Conzelmann, Steven W. Hemelt, Brad Hershbein, Shawn M. Martin, Andrew Simon, Kevin M. Stange

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

This paper introduces a new measure of the labor markets served by colleges and universities across the United States. About 50 percent of recent college graduates are living and working in the metro area nearest the institution they attended, with this figure climbing to 67 percent in-state. The geographic dispersion of alumni is more than twice as great for highly selective 4-year institutions as for 2-year institutions. However, more than one-quarter of 2-year institutions disperse alumni more diversely than the average public 4-year institution. In one application of these data, we find that the average strength of the labor market to which a college sends its graduates predicts college-specific intergenerational economic mobility. A Penny for Your Thoughts

一分钱换你的想法

W. Walker Hanlon, Stephan Heblich, Ferdinando Monte, Martin B. Schmitz

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 英国

Abstract

How do communication costs affect the production of new ideas and inventions? To answer this question, we study the introduction of the Uniform Penny Post in Great Britain in 1840. This reform replaced the previous system of expensive distance-based postage fees with a uniform low rate of one penny for sending letters anywhere in the country. The result was a large spatially-varied reduction in the cost of communicating across locations. We study the impact of this reform on the production of scientific knowledge using citation links constructed from a leading academic journal, the Philosophical Transactions, and the impact on the development of new technology using patent data. Our results provide quantitative causal estimates showing how a fall in communication costs can increase the rate at which scientific knowledge is exchanged and new ideas and technologies are developed. This evidence lends direct empirical support to an extensive theoretical literature in economic growth and urban economics positing that more ideas can emerge from communication between individuals.

Boundary Discontinuity Methods and Policy Spillovers

边界不连续方法与政策溢出效应

Ekaterina S. Jardim, Mark C. Long, Robert Plotnick, Emma van Inwegen, Jacob L. Vigdor, Hilary Wething

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The boundary discontinuity method of causal inference may yield misleading results if a policy’s impacts do not stop at the border of the implementing jurisdiction. We use geographically precise longitudinal employment data documenting worker job-to-job mobility to study policy spillovers in the context of three local minimum wage increases. Estimated spillover impacts on wages and hours are statistically significant, geographically diffuse, and sufficient to create concern regarding interpretation of results even using not-immediately-adjacent regions as controls. Spillover effects appear less concerning with smaller interventions or those or adopted in a smaller jurisdiction.

中文摘要

The Labor Market Impacts of Technological Change: From Unbridled Enthusiasm to Qualified Optimism to Vast Uncertainty

技术变革的劳动力市场影响：从盲目乐观到审慎乐观再到巨大不确定性

David Autor

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

This review considers the evolution of economic thinking on the relationship between digital technology and inequality across four decades, encompassing four related but intellectually distinct paradigms, which I refer to as the education race, the task polarization model, the automation-reinstatement race, and the era of Artificial Intelligence uncertainty. The nuance of economic understanding has improved across these epochs. Yet, traditional economic optimism about the beneficent effects of technology for productivity and welfare has eroded as understanding has advanced. Given this intellectual trajectory, it would be natural to forecast an even darker horizon ahead. I refrain from doing so because forecasting the “consequences” of technological change treats the future as a fate to be divined rather than an expedition to be undertaken. I conclude by discussing opportunities and challenges that we collectively face in shaping this future.

中文摘要

The Endowment Effect and Collateralized Loans

禀赋效应与抵押贷款

Kevin Carney, Michael Kremer, Xinyue Lin, Gautam Rao

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 肯尼亚

Abstract

Collateral requirements play an important role in credit markets. This paper shows that the endowment effect—the phenomenon where owing a good increases one's valuation of it—inhibits demand for loans which use a borrower's existing assets as collateral. Using a field experiment in Kenya, we show that borrowers instead strongly prefer loans collateralized using the new durable assets being financed by the loans themselves. They are willing to pay 9% per month higher interest for such Same-Asset Collateralized Loans (SACLs) despite the endowed and new assets being randomized, and thus similarly valued before ownership. Our findings imply that assets which are difficult to use as collateral—which cannot be financed by SACLs—will be invested in less, even if the borrower has other collateral. We argue that borrowers' preference for SACLs is driven by naivete: they initially perceive that they have little to lose when offered a SACL, but subsequently come to develop an attachment to the new asset, resulting in high repayment effort. Consistent with this, borrowers underestimate their future attachment to an asset before owning it, and SACLs do not have higher default rates despite having higher demand. We derive the conditions under which offering consumers SACLs increases or conversely decreases borrower welfare.

中文摘要

抵押品要求在信贷市场中扮演着重要角色。本文表明，禀赋效应——即拥有某物品会提高对其估值——抑制了以借款人现有资产作为抵押品的贷款需求。通过在肯尼亚进行的一项实地实验，我们发现借款人反而强烈偏好以贷款本身所融资的新耐用资产作为抵押品的贷款。尽管被赋予的资产和新资产是随机分配的，因此在所有权之前估值相似，但借款人仍愿意为这种“同资产抵押贷款”（SACL）每月多支付9%的利息。我们的研究结果表明，难以用作抵押品的资产——即无法通过SACL融资的资产——将减少投资，即使借款人拥有其他抵押品。我们认为，借款人对SACL的偏好是由天真驱动的：他们最初认为在获得SACL时几乎没有什么可损失的，但随后逐渐对新资产产生依恋，从而导致较高的还款努力。与此一致的是，借款人在拥有资产之前低估了自己未来的依恋程度，并且尽管SACL需求更高，但其违约率并未更高。我们推导出了向消费者提供SACL会增加或反而降低借款人福利的条件。
