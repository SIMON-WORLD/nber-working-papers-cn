Making Everyone’s Preferences Count: A Prospect Theory Approach to Valuing Health Improvements

让每个人的偏好都得到考量：前景理论在健康改善价值评估中的应用

Karen Mulligan, Drishti Baid, Jason N. Doctor, Darius N. Lakdawalla

健康与医疗 美国

Abstract

Health technology assessment bodies and policymakers have grappled with the issue of whose preferences – the general healthy population or the patient population – should be incorporated into traditional cost-effectiveness analysis. This debate arises because healthy individuals and patients often value the same health state differently, and cost-effectiveness will undervalue health-related quality of life (HRQoL) for individuals with chronic illness or disability because it cannot account for adaptation. Prospect theory (PT) provides a framework that permits adaptation since it assumes individuals evaluate outcomes relative to a reference point. We estimate utility over HRQoL in a PT framework in a nationally representative U.S. population and compare directly with utility estimates in an EU framework from the same population. Consistent with previous studies in the monetary and health domains, we find evidence of probability weighting bias and loss aversion over health. Using the PT utility estimates, we demonstrate how PT can be incorporated into healthcare value assessment and present the resulting implications for medical decision making.

中文摘要

卫生技术评估机构和政策制定者一直在争论应将谁的偏好——普通健康人群还是患者群体——纳入传统的成本效果分析。这一争论源于健康个体和患者往往对相同的健康状态赋予不同的价值，且成本效果分析无法解释适应性，因而会低估患有慢性疾病或残疾个体的健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）。前景理论（PT）提供了一个允许适应性的框架，因为它假设个体是相对于参考点来评估结果的。我们在具有全国代表性的美国人群中，于前景理论框架下估计了HRQoL上的效用，并直接与来自同一人群的欧盟框架下的效用估计进行比较。与先前在货币和健康领域的研究一致，我们发现了概率加权偏差和健康损失厌恶的证据。利用前景理论的效用估计，我们展示了如何将前景理论纳入医疗价值评估，并呈现了其对医疗决策的启示。
