Industry Distress Anomaly

行业困境异象

Hui Chen, Winston Wei Dou, Hongye Guo, Yan Ji

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

We document a robust industry-level distress anomaly in which more distressed industries earn significantly lower expected equity returns. The anomaly is distinct from the firm-level distress anomaly (Campbell, Hilscher and Szilagyi, 2008). It remains significant after controlling for firm-level distress but disappears in placebo industries formed by randomly reshuffling firms across actual industries. Both theoretically and empirically, we show that competition-distress feedback amplifies the exposure of industry profit margins and equity returns to discount-rate shocks. Industries with greater idiosyncratic left-tail risk are more distressed but exhibit weaker competition-distress feedback. This effect reduces their exposure to discount-rate shocks and thus lowers their expected equity returns.

中文摘要

我们记录了一个稳健的行业层面困境异象，其中更困境的行业预期股票收益显著更低。该异象不同于企业层面困境异象（Campbell, Hilscher和Szilagyi, 2008）。在控制企业层面困境后，它仍然显著，但在通过随机重新洗牌企业跨实际行业形成的安慰剂行业中消失。在理论和实证上，我们表明竞争-困境反馈放大了行业利润率和股票收益对贴现率冲击的敞口。具有更大特质左尾风险的行业更困境，但表现出更弱的竞争-困境反馈。这种效应降低了它们对贴现率冲击的敞口，从而降低了其预期股票收益。
