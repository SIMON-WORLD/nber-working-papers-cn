The Equilibrium Impact of Credit Frictions: Evidence from Default Risk Using Firm-Level Data

信贷摩擦的均衡影响：基于企业层面数据的违约风险证据

Timothy J. Besley, Peter John Lambert, Isabelle A. Michalski-Roland, John Van Reenen

金融与资产定价 英国

Abstract

This paper examines the impact of credit frictions arising from firm-level default risk on aggregate economic performance. We build a micro-to-macro model with heterogeneous firms and sector-specific production functions, showing that perceived default risk is a sufficient statistic for credit frictions. Using UK administrative data (2004–2019) matched to S&P risk measures, counterfactual estimates reveal that relaxing frictions raises output by 25% and wages by 23%. Ignoring equilibrium wage adjustments overstates output gains, while fixed-capital misallocation approaches understate them. Most gains reflect aggregate capital accumulation. Credit frictions remain above pre-crisis levels, reshape firm size dynamics, increase misallocation across firms, and dampen productivity growth over time.

中文摘要

本文考察了由企业层面违约风险产生的信贷摩擦对总体经济表现的影响。我们构建了一个具有异质性企业和部门特定生产函数的微观到宏观模型，表明感知违约风险是信贷摩擦的充分统计量。使用英国行政数据（2004–2019年）与S&P风险度量相匹配，反事实估计显示，放松摩擦可使产出提高25%，工资提高23%。忽略均衡工资调整会高估产出收益，而忽略固定资本错配则会低估它们。大部分收益反映了总资本积累。信贷摩擦仍高于危机前水平，重塑了企业规模动态，加剧了企业间的错配，并随着时间的推移抑制了生产率增长。
