Stress Testing Cardiac Care Markets through Deregulation

通过放松管制对心脏护理市场进行压力测试

Daniel R. Arnold, Michael R. Richards, Yashaswini Singh, Christopher M. Whaley

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

The diffusion of technological innovation depends on incentives, regulations, and firms’ strategic behaviors. We study these intersections within cardiac procedure markets following Medicare’s expansion of non-hospital facility options for treatment, enabled by clinical advancements. State-level regulations restrain federal pro-competition policy. Where market entry occurs, business stealing is concentrated among the lowest cost treatment settings, rather than high-cost hospitals––increasing Medicare spending by approximately $2.5 million. Medicare policy also generates externalities for untargeted procedures and other payers, except when hospitals and physicians are vertically integrated. Federal rulemaking interacts with and is mitigated by complex market dynamics––including in potentially unanticipated ways.

中文摘要

技术创新的扩散取决于激励、法规和企业的战略行为。我们研究了心脏手术市场中这些因素的交叉点，背景是医疗保险（Medicare）因临床进步而扩大了非医院设施的治疗选择。州级法规限制了联邦促进竞争的政策。在市场进入发生的地方，业务窃取集中在成本最低的治疗环境中，而不是高成本医院——这使医疗保险支出增加了约250万美元。医疗保险政策还对未针对的手术和其他支付方产生外部性，除非医院和医生是垂直整合的。联邦规则制定与复杂的市场动态相互作用并受其影响——包括可能以意想不到的方式。
