Choosing To Fail: Managerial Liability, Risk Management and Voluntary Exits of Banks

选择失败：管理层责任、风险管理与银行的自愿退出

Haelim Anderson, Charles W. Calomiris, Jennifer S. Rhee

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

We study how extended stockholder liability shaped bank resolution during and after the Panic of 1893, comparing California state banks (unlimited liability) with national banks (double liability). Using newly assembled data linking California state bank presidents to census records and national bank stock ownership data from Examination Reports, we measure managers’ personal exposures to extended liability. For California state banks, traditional fundamentals predict involuntary liquidations but do not explain voluntary exits. Instead, voluntary exits are driven by managers’ liability exposure, local economic risk, and personal wealth, consistent with managers responding to unlimited liability by initiating preemptive, orderly resolutions. For national banks under double liability, voluntary and involuntary liquidations are more similar, and personal exposure plays a smaller role in risk management. These findings show that substantial personal liability exposure can operate for modern prudential tools like compensation clawbacks and living wills. However, such extended liability also magnifies credit contractions during downturns, highlighting a fundamental tradeoff between micro-stability and macroeconomic fragility in regulatory design.

中文摘要

我们研究了1893年恐慌期间及之后，扩展的股东责任如何塑造银行处置，比较了加利福尼亚州银行（无限责任）与国家银行（双重责任）。利用新汇编的数据，将加利福尼亚州银行行长与人口普查记录以及来自检查报告的国家银行股票持有数据联系起来，我们衡量了管理者对扩展责任的个人敞口。对于加利福尼亚州银行，传统的基本面因素可以预测非自愿清算，但不能解释自愿退出。相反，自愿退出是由管理者的责任敞口、地方经济风险和个人财富驱动的，这与管理者响应无限责任而主动发起预防性、有序处置的行为一致。对于双重责任下的国家银行，自愿和非自愿清算更为相似，个人敞口在风险管理中的作用较小。这些发现表明，大量的个人责任敞口可以发挥类似于现代审慎工具（如薪酬追回和生前遗嘱）的作用。然而，这种扩展责任也会放大衰退期间的信贷收缩，凸显了监管设计中微观稳定与宏观脆弱性之间的根本权衡。
