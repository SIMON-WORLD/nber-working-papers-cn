Demand for Safety in the Crypto Ecosystem

加密生态系统中的安全需求

Murillo Campello, Angela Gallo, Lira Mota, Tammaro Terracciano

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the demand for safety and liquidity in the crypto ecosystem. We do so under a framework in which a representative investor allocates liquidity across stablecoin deposits in lending pools and traditional safe assets (e.g., MMF shares). Our model delivers three main predictions: (i) the stablecoin deposit premium co-moves with the Treasury premium when investors value the safety and liquidity services of stablecoins; (ii) increases in Treasury supply reduce the stablecoin deposit premium; and (iii) drops in the perceived safety and liquidity of stablecoin deposits (e.g., due to de-pegs or hacker attacks) reduce their premium. Using granular data from hundreds of DeFi pools spanning multiple protocols, tokens, and blockchains, we find evidence supporting these predictions. Investors treat stablecoin deposits as money-like instruments that are borderless and permissionless, yet as fragile as other forms of privately produced safe assets.

中文摘要

我们研究了加密生态系统中对安全性和流动性的需求。我们在一个代表性投资者在借贷池中的稳定币存款和传统安全资产（如货币市场基金份额）之间分配流动性的框架下进行研究。我们的模型得出三个主要预测：（i）当投资者重视稳定币的安全性和流动性服务时，稳定币存款溢价与国债溢价共同变动；（ii）国债供给增加会降低稳定币存款溢价；（iii）稳定币存款感知安全性和流动性下降（例如由于脱钩或黑客攻击）会降低其溢价。利用来自数百个DeFi池的细粒度数据，涵盖多种协议、代币和区块链，我们找到了支持这些预测的证据。投资者将稳定币存款视为类似货币的工具，具有无国界和无许可的特点，但与其他形式的私人生产的安全资产一样脆弱。
