The Environmental Costs of Sanctions: Flaring and Venting in Venezuela

制裁的环境成本：委内瑞拉的燃除与放空

Michele Fioretti, Kavanaugh FitzPatrick, Alessandro Iaria

环境与气候 伊朗、美国、委内瑞拉

Abstract

Economic sanctions on oil-producing autocracies change not only the quantity of oil but also how it is produced. Combining field-level production data with satellite measurements of gas flaring and atmospheric methane across six Latin American countries from 2012 to 2024, we study the U.S. sanctions on Venezuela. Per-barrel gas flaring in Venezuela rose about two-and-a-half-fold even as production fell by two-thirds, a pattern no unsanctioned neighbor shows, so environmental damage under sanctions is about two and a half times what a proportional decline in output would predict. Three channels generate this gap: the exit of small, dirty fields (composition) is more than offset by surviving fields running their infrastructure past design capacity (strain) and cutting their operating budgets (maintenance). We identify strain from the response of satellite-measured methane to the world price of naphtha, the imported diluent Venezuela's extra-heavy crude requires, and maintenance from a November 2022 U.S. Treasury license that allowed only Chevron to resume operations at its Venezuelan fields. A similar pattern of rising per-barrel emissions despite falling output appears under the sanctions on Iran (after 2018).

中文摘要

对石油生产型专制国家的经济制裁不仅改变了石油的数量，也改变了石油的生产方式。结合2012年至2024年间六个拉丁美洲国家的油田层面生产数据与卫星测量的天然气燃除和大气甲烷数据，我们研究了美国对委内瑞拉的制裁。委内瑞拉的每桶天然气燃除量上升了约2.5倍，即使产量下降了三分之二，这一模式在未受制裁的邻国中均未出现，因此制裁下的环境损害约为按产量比例下降所预测的2.5倍。三个渠道导致了这一差距：小型、污染严重油田的退出（构成效应）被幸存油田超设计能力运行基础设施（超负荷效应）和削减运营预算（维护效应）所抵消，且后两者的影响超过了前者。我们通过卫星测量的甲烷对石脑油（委内瑞拉超重原油所需的进口稀释剂）世界价格的响应来识别超负荷效应，并通过2022年11月美国财政部仅允许雪佛龙恢复其在委内瑞拉油田运营的许可证来识别维护效应。在制裁伊朗（2018年后）的情况下，也出现了类似的每桶排放量上升而产量下降的模式。
