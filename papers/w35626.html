Outside Options and Labor Supply: Evidence from the Gig Economy

外部选择与劳动力供给：来自零工经济的证据

Sydnee Caldwell, Emily Oehlsen

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

We use randomized pay experiments among Uber drivers, paired with a natural experiment in access to a competitor, to examine how outside options shape labor supply to the firm. When hours are flexible, the firm-specific labor supply elasticity combines a market-hours component and a firm-substitution component. Access to a single competing platform nearly doubles drivers’ firm-specific elasticity and cuts the implied monopsony markdown from 68% to about 50%. The same experiments identify sex differences: women are about twice as elastic to the market as men, yet no less elastic to their employer.

中文摘要

我们利用优步司机中的随机薪酬实验，并结合进入竞争对手的自然实验，来考察外部选择如何塑造企业对劳动力的供给。当工作时间灵活时，企业特定的劳动力供给弹性包含一个市场工时成分和一个企业替代成分。进入一个单一竞争平台几乎使司机的企业特定弹性翻倍，并将隐含的买方垄断加价折扣从68%降至约50%。同样的实验识别出性别差异：女性对市场的弹性约为男性的两倍，但对雇主的弹性却并不低于男性。
