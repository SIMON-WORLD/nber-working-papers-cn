A Deep Case for Shallow Integration

浅层一体化的深层理由

John Sturm Becko, Arnaud Costinot

环境与气候 全球/多国

Abstract

We study international cooperation in a general environment with pecuniary and non-pecuniary externalities across countries. We derive two main theorems, each demonstrating that market-access commitments, a form of shallow integration, are sufficient to implement globally efficient policies. When all cross-border externalities are pecuniary, the first theorem provides a new and unifying perspective on a series of results by Bagwell and Staiger. When some cross-border externalities are non-pecuniary, the second theorem shows that market-access commitments, properly extended, still sustain global efficiency. These extended market-access commitments reinterpret cross-border externalities as additional goods that are traded and priced at their social values. We discuss the implications for international cooperation on climate change and geopolitics.

中文摘要

我们研究了一个具有跨国货币性外部性和非货币性外部性的一般环境中的国际合作问题。我们推导出两个主要定理，每个定理都表明市场准入承诺（一种浅层一体化形式）足以实施全球有效的政策。当所有跨境外部性都是货币性时，第一个定理为Bagwell和Staiger的一系列结果提供了新的统一视角。当某些跨境外部性是非货币性时，第二个定理表明，适当扩展的市场准入承诺仍然能够维持全球效率。这些扩展的市场准入承诺将跨境外部性重新解释为按其社会价值进行交易和定价的额外商品。我们讨论了这对气候变化和地缘政治国际合作的影响。
