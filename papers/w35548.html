Reserves and the Buyer of Last Resort

准备金与最后买家

Frederic Boissay, Harald Uhlig

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine the role of central bank reserves and public liquidity when secondary markets may freeze. Central bank reserves help intermediaries purchase assets during stress but crowd out investment. Under laissez-faire, intermediaries hold insufficient reserves, overlooking how aggregate liquidity reduces freeze risk. We propose a "market-backstop principle", akin to Bagehot’s principle for intermediaries. It combines state-contingent buyer-of-last-resort interventions to restore trading with modest liquidity requirements to limit moral hazard. The welfare benefits of restoring market functioning exceed the fiscal costs of interventions. We explore implications for the size and composition of central bank balance sheets.

中文摘要

我们研究了当二级市场可能冻结时，中央银行准备金和公共流动性的作用。中央银行准备金帮助中介机构在压力时期购买资产，但会挤出投资。在自由放任下，中介机构持有的准备金不足，忽视了总流动性如何降低冻结风险。我们提出了一个“市场最后支持原则”，类似于针对中介机构的巴杰特原则。该原则将基于状态依赖的最后买家干预以恢复交易，与适度的流动性要求相结合以限制道德风险。恢复市场运作的福利收益超过了干预的财政成本。我们探讨了对中央银行资产负债表规模和构成的影响。
