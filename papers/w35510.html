Who is Afraid of Eurobonds?

谁害怕欧元债券？

Francesco Bianchi, Qingyuan Fang, Leonardo Melosi, Anna Rogantini Picco

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

The current euro area policy framework conflates short-run stabilization with long-run fiscal sustainability, exposing members to deflationary and inflationary tail risks. We employ an estimated euro area model to analyze an alternative framework that separates these objectives. A centralized Treasury issues Eurobonds to finance countercyclical stabilization, while national governments retain responsibility for long-term fiscal sustainability. The Treasury can coordinate with the monetary authority in case of a large recession, with no need to suspend fiscal rules at the national level. The arrangement functions as an automatic stabilizer, eliminating the tail risks of deflation and fiscal stagflation.

中文摘要

当前的欧元区政策框架将短期稳定与长期财政可持续性混为一谈，使成员国面临通缩和通胀的尾部风险。我们使用一个估计的欧元区模型来分析一个分离这些目标的替代框架。一个中央财政部发行欧元债券以资助反周期稳定，而国家政府则保留对长期财政可持续性的责任。在大规模衰退的情况下，财政部可以与货币当局协调，无需在国家层面暂停财政规则。该安排作为一种自动稳定器运作，消除了通缩和财政滞胀的尾部风险。
