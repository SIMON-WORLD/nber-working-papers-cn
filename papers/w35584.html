The Displacement Effects of Domestic Outsourcing

国内外包的替代效应

Mayara Felix, Michael B. Wong

公共财政与税收 巴西

Abstract

Evidence that domestic outsourcing lowers pay comes largely from on-site transfers, in which workers move to a contractor but keep the same jobs. Displacement is rarely observed: whether workers lose their jobs, where they go, how earnings evolve. In Brazil’s 1993–1994 pro-outsourcing reforms, which differentially affected security guards, such transfers were rare; firms instead used occupational layoffs, shedding their guards while keeping other workers. Displaced guards’ employment recovered within five years, but many changed occupations and wages stayed about 12% lower. Lifetime losses average 1.2 to 1.5 years of pre-layoff earnings, concentrated among workers from high-wage firms, reflecting lost premia.

中文摘要

关于国内外包降低薪酬的证据主要来自现场转移，即工人转移到承包商处但保留相同工作。替代效应很少被观察到：工人是否失业、他们去向何处、收入如何演变。在巴西1993-1994年支持外包的改革中，这些改革对保安产生了差异化影响，此类转移很少见；企业转而采用职业性裁员，解雇保安而保留其他工人。被替代的保安的就业在五年内恢复，但许多人改变了职业，工资仍低约12%。终身损失平均为裁员前收入的1.2至1.5年，集中在来自高工资企业的工人中，反映了损失的溢价。
