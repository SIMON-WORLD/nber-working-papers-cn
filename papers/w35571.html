Winning is Contagious: Social Learning and the Dynamics of Union Support

胜利具有传染性：社会学习与工会支持动态

Ellora Derenoncourt, Arindrajit Dube, Suresh Naidu, Heather Sarsons, Niharika Singh

环境与气候 未指明区域

Abstract

Union growth has historically happened in spurts. We combine union election data from 1961–2025 with a large-scale information experiment involving 9,000 low-wage workers to study the role of social learning in union growth. Observationally, close election wins cause future wins within state-industries. Experimentally, information about recent union wins increases union support and beliefs about co-worker support, with effects concentrated among workers with weak information environments and low baseline coworker support. In contrast, information about labor market tightness has little effect. Our results highlight social learning as a mechanism for union growth.

中文摘要

工会增长在历史上是间歇性爆发的。我们将1961年至2025年的工会选举数据与一项涉及9,000名低工资工人的大规模信息实验相结合，研究社会学习在工会增长中的作用。从观察上看，势均力敌的选举胜利会导致州内行业未来的胜利。在实验中，关于近期工会胜利的信息增加了工会支持和关于同事支持的信念，其影响集中在信息环境薄弱和同事基线支持度低的工人中。相比之下，关于劳动力市场紧张程度的信息影响甚微。我们的结果强调了社会学习作为工会增长的一种机制。
