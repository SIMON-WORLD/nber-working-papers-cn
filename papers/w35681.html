Spillover Effects of Transfer Programs

转移支付项目的溢出效应

Travis Baseler, Jules Gazeaud, Erik Snowberg

公共财政与税收 未指明区域

Abstract

We derive four facts from experiments on the spillover effects of cash and in-kind transfer programs onto non-recipients: (1) average spillovers on consumption and asset value are negligible; (2) negative psychological spillovers emerge in the longer term among households randomly excluded from transfers by within-village lotteries; (3) these spillovers are concentrated in consumption-poor households; and (4) they do not appear when the selection of transfer recipients is eligibility-based. These findings are consistent with household preferences against within-village randomization of transfers, and point to additional benefits of cluster-level randomized designs over household-level ones.

中文摘要

我们从关于现金和实物转移支付项目对非受益者溢出效应的实验中得出四个事实：（1）对消费和资产价值的平均溢出效应可以忽略不计；（2）在通过村内抽签随机排除在转移支付之外的家庭中，长期会出现负面的心理溢出效应；（3）这些溢出效应集中在消费贫困家庭中；（4）当转移支付受益者的选择基于资格标准时，这些溢出效应不会出现。这些发现与家庭偏好反对村内随机分配转移支付的观点一致，并指出了集群层面随机设计相对于家庭层面设计的额外优势。
