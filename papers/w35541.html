Financial Interdependence and Currency Internationalization

金融相互依存与货币国际化

Zhengyang Jiang

金融与资产定价 中国相关

Abstract

Does financial opening necessarily lead to currency internationalization? To study the competition between incumbent and rising powers under financial interdependence, we develop a model of asset demand with microfounded network effects. Search frictions with currency-specialized intermediaries generate distinct notions of liquidity at asset-market and currency-area levels, which jointly shape the trajectory of currency competition. In the U.S.-China context, China at early stages of financial development benefits from pooling its assets with the dollar area, which reinforces the status quo. As China's financial markets deepen, RMB issuance allows China to internalize network effects and erode the dollar's dominance, triggering a discrete shift toward fragmentation. This transition is further shaped by sanctions, financial repression, and third-country responses, highlighting how financial interdependence transforms cooperation into rivalry in the evolution of the international financial order.

中文摘要

金融开放是否必然导致货币国际化？为研究金融相互依存下在位大国与新兴大国之间的竞争，我们构建了一个具有微观基础网络效应的资产需求模型。与货币专业化中介机构的搜索摩擦在资产市场层面和货币区域层面产生了不同的流动性概念，这些概念共同塑造了货币竞争的轨迹。在中美背景下，处于金融发展早期阶段的中国从将其资产与美元区合并中获益，这强化了现状。随着中国金融市场的深化，人民币发行使中国能够内部化网络效应并削弱美元的支配地位，从而引发向碎片化的离散转变。这一转变还受到制裁、金融抑制和第三国反应的影响，凸显了金融相互依存如何在国际金融秩序的演变中将合作转化为竞争。
