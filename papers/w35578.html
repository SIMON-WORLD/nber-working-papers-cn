Reconstructing a Century of U.S. Corporate Bonds: Credit Risk in Historical Perspective

重构美国公司债券百年史：历史视角下的信用风险

Mohammad Ghaderi, Sébastien Plante, Nikolai Roussanov, Sang Byung Seo

金融与资产定价 美国

Abstract

Do corporate bond investors earn compensation for bearing credit risk? We construct a new historical corporate bond database spanning 128 years to estimate a corporate bond counterpart to the equity risk premium. Combining hand-collected archival data with modern sources, we assemble a panel of over 100,000 bonds and 7 million observations. While recent samples suggest corporate bond excess returns largely reflect the term premium, our long sample reveals a sizable and statistically significant credit risk premium. Credit spreads predict future corporate bond returns and macroeconomic aggregates, though their ability to forecast business cycle fluctuations weakens when prewar data are included.

中文摘要

公司债券投资者是否因承担信用风险而获得补偿？我们构建了一个跨越128年的新历史公司债券数据库，以估算与股票风险溢价相对应的公司债券溢价。通过将手工收集的档案数据与现代数据源相结合，我们汇编了一个包含超过100,000只债券和700万条观测值的面板数据集。虽然近期样本表明公司债券超额收益在很大程度上反映了期限溢价，但我们的长期样本揭示了一个规模可观且统计显著的信用风险溢价。信用利差能够预测未来的公司债券回报和宏观经济总量，尽管当纳入战前数据时，其预测商业周期波动的能力有所减弱。
