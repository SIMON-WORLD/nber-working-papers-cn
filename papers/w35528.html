Beliefs That Predict Returns and Beliefs That Attract Flows: Policy Insights and Sentiment Catering in Mutual Funds

预测收益的信念与吸引资金的信念：中国共同基金的政策洞察与情绪迎合

Zhenyu Gao, Wei Xiong, Jian Yuan

金融与资产定价 中国相关

Abstract

Using mandatory Outlook reports from Chinese mutual funds, we decompose each fund’s equity-market beliefs into macro fundamentals, government policy, and residual sentiment. Policy beliefs, rather than macro beliefs, predict subsequent market returns and help explain the positive alphas observed in China’s mutual fund industry. By contrast, residual sentiment strongly shapes equity allocations and attracts investor flows, even though it does not predict returns. Using maturing wealth management products as an instrument for future aggregate fund flows, we show that funds strategically position their stated beliefs to cater to investors. Overall, our analysis reveals a core tension in active asset management: skilled managers generate performance through policy insights while attracting flows through sentiment alignment.

中文摘要

利用中国共同基金的强制性展望报告，我们将每只基金的股票市场信念分解为宏观基本面、政府政策和残余情绪三个部分。政策信念而非宏观信念能够预测后续市场收益，并有助于解释中国共同基金行业观察到的正阿尔法。相比之下，残余情绪虽然不能预测收益，但强烈影响股票配置并吸引投资者资金流入。我们使用即将到期的理财产品作为未来总体资金流入的工具变量，表明基金会策略性地调整其陈述的信念以迎合投资者。总体而言，我们的分析揭示了主动资产管理中的一个核心矛盾：有技能的经理人通过政策洞察创造业绩，同时通过情绪迎合吸引资金流入。
