The Cov-lite Liquidity Advantage, Regulatory Pressures, and the Evolution of the Leveraged Loan Market

宽松契约流动性优势、监管压力与杠杆贷款市场的演变

Robert Prilmeier, René M. Stulz

金融与资产定价 全球/多国

Abstract

Following the global financial crisis (GFC), regulators made it harder for banks to retain leveraged loan exposure. We conjecture their actions increased the share of leveraged loan issuances with no maintenance covenants (cov-lite loans) because such loans are easier to sell. We find that, post-GFC, the share of cov-lite loan issuance increased more for banks facing stricter regulation, failing to pass a stress test outright, or exhibiting greater vulnerability to the severely adverse stress test scenario. We show that, as expected from theory, cov-lite loans have a liquidity advantage that lowers their credit spread and is higher for private firms.

中文摘要

在全球金融危机之后，监管机构使银行更难保留杠杆贷款敞口。我们推测，他们的行动增加了无维护契约贷款（宽松契约贷款）在杠杆贷款发行中的份额，因为此类贷款更容易出售。我们发现，危机后，对于面临更严格监管、未直接通过压力测试或对严重不利压力测试情景表现出更大脆弱性的银行，宽松契约贷款发行的份额增加更多。我们表明，正如理论预期，宽松契约贷款具有流动性优势，这降低了其信用利差，且对私营企业而言更高。
