Benchmark Mineability and the Financing of AI Innovation

基准可挖掘性与人工智能创新的融资

Alex Chan

AI 与数字经济 未指明区域

Abstract

Public AI benchmarks steer research and allocate investments. They are therefore market designs. Public examples can reveal the process behind a private final test, while a finite public score cannot cover a broad task space inherent to general intelligence. I show how both gaps become profitable when scores move capital and how targeted effort erodes the signal used by later investors. The market design lesson is to separate development from certification: publish practice tasks, but choose the investment-consequential generator after the submitted system's evaluation policy is fixed.

中文摘要

公共人工智能基准引导研究并分配投资。因此，它们是市场设计。公共示例可以揭示私人最终测试背后的过程，而有限的公共分数无法覆盖通用智能固有的广泛任务空间。我展示了当分数驱动资本时，这两个缺口如何变得有利可图，以及有针对性的努力如何侵蚀后来投资者使用的信号。市场设计的教训是将开发与认证分开：发布实践任务，但在提交系统的评估策略固定后，选择影响投资的生成器。
