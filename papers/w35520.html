Retail Betting Markets

零售博彩市场

Scott R. Baker, Justin Balthrop, Mark J. Johnson, Jason D. Kotter, Kevin Pisciotta

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper examines the rapid expansion and convergence of retail betting markets. We analyze market design elements, discuss economic utility, and highlight shared behavioral drivers of sports betting markets, prediction markets, and retail options trading. Our review underscores how technological innovation, behavioral biases, and regulatory arbitrage have shaped recent market evolution. We highlight important considerations for policy-makers facing a changing landscape and outline possibilities for further research.

中文摘要

本文考察了零售博彩市场的快速扩张与融合。我们分析了市场设计要素，讨论了经济效用，并强调了体育博彩市场、预测市场和零售期权交易共同的行为驱动因素。我们的综述强调了技术创新、行为偏差和监管套利如何塑造了近期市场演变。我们为面临不断变化格局的政策制定者指出了重要考量，并概述了进一步研究的可能性。
