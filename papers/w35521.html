Profits and Social Impacts: Complements vs. Tradeoffs for Lenders in Three Countries

利润与社会影响：三个国家贷款机构的互补性与权衡

Susan Athey, Bruno Fava, Dean Karlan, Adam Osman, Jonathan Zinman

国际贸易 菲律宾、南非

Abstract

The canonical approach to corporate governance posits a tradeoff between maximizing shareholder profits and maximizing other aspects of shareholder welfare such as social impact. Advances in machine learning that enhance the firm's ability to target specific customers may exacerbate or ameliorate this tradeoff. We estimate these tradeoffs using data from randomized microcredit approvals in South Africa, the Philippines, and Bosnia. We examine social impact on two dimensions: credit access (i.e., reaching disadvantaged groups that typically have less access to financial markets) and impact (i.e., treatment effects on household income). Two of the three lenders could have increased average loan profit margin by 7–9 percentage points through machine learning-based targeting. However, such targeting pushes against social impact goals vis-à-vis credit access; specifically, women and lower-income households would have been more excluded (the implied impact on borrowers' income, also central to the dual goal of social impact, is too imprecisely estimated to draw an inference). To gauge the magnitude of the first tradeoff, we examine how profits would change if the lenders altered whom they lent to within each quintile of baseline borrower income, thus holding the distribution broadly similar but fine-tuning the targeting within income bands. Such a constraint would lower the profit gains of targeting by about half. These findings highlight the importance of quantifying tradeoffs and complementarities when deciding what to maximize.

中文摘要

公司治理的经典方法假设股东利润最大化与股东福利的其他方面（如社会影响）最大化之间存在权衡。机器学习技术的进步增强了企业针对特定客户的能力，可能加剧或缓解这种权衡。我们利用南非、菲律宾和波斯尼亚随机小额信贷审批的数据来估计这些权衡。我们从两个维度考察社会影响：信贷获取（即覆盖通常难以获得金融市场的弱势群体）和影响（即对家庭收入的处理效应）。三家贷款机构中有两家可以通过基于机器学习的定向将平均贷款利润率提高7至9个百分点。然而，这种定向与社会影响目标（尤其是信贷获取）相冲突；具体而言，女性和低收入家庭将被更多排除在外（对借款人收入的隐含影响，也是社会影响双重目标的核心，因估计过于不精确而无法得出结论）。为衡量第一个权衡的规模，我们考察如果贷款机构在基线借款人收入的每个五分位内改变贷款对象，利润将如何变化，从而保持分布大致相似但在收入区间内微调定向。这种约束将使定向的利润收益减少约一半。这些发现强调了在决定最大化目标时量化权衡和互补性的重要性。
