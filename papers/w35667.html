NBER Working Paper w35667所在周报所在月度合集

A Theory of International Official Lending

国际官方贷款理论

Qing Liu, Zanhui Liu, Vivian Zhanwei Yue

金融与资产定价 全球/多国

Abstract

Official lending is large, senior, and countercyclical, continuing after sovereigns fall into arrears on private debt. We ask why sovereign finance exhibits this division of labor across creditors. In a production economy where a risk-averse sovereign privately allocates imported inputs, commitment is limited on both sides, and monitoring generates a noisy signal, the constrained-optimal allocation is decentralized by defaultable private debt, senior non-defaultable multilateral debt, and concessional bilateral debt whose relief is tied to the signal. Production remains distorted, but the sovereign is never excluded: official lending is monitored liquidity provision. A calibration reproduces procyclical private and countercyclical official debt.

中文摘要

官方贷款规模大、优先级高且具有逆周期性，在主权重陷私人债务违约后仍会继续。我们探究为何主权融资在债权人之间表现出这种分工。在一个风险厌恶的主权国家私下分配进口投入品的生产经济中，双方承诺均有限，且监督产生噪声信号，约束最优配置由可违约的私人债务、优先级的不可违约多边债务以及其减免与信号挂钩的优惠双边债务分散化实现。生产仍然扭曲，但主权国家永远不会被排除在外：官方贷款是受监督的流动性提供。校准重现了顺周期的私人债务和逆周期的官方债务。
