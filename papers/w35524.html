The Welfare Effects of Conditioning the Potential Duration of UI Benefits on Prior Work History

将失业保险金潜在期限与先前工作经历挂钩的福利效应

Leonard Goff, Simon Quach

宏观与货币财政 加拿大

Abstract

Most unemployment insurance (UI) systems condition the potential duration of benefits on claimants' work history, yet little evidence exists on whether doing so is socially optimal. We study this question using 25 regression discontinuities in Canada's UI system, where benefit duration increases discretely when prior hours worked crosses various thresholds. Using administrative records covering the universe of UI claims from 1997 to 2018, we estimate the fiscal effects of extending potential duration across the work-history distribution. On average, an additional week lengthens unemployment by 0.30 weeks and costs the government $\$96$. However, this average effect masks significant heterogeneity: the mechanical cost falls steeply with prior hours, while the behavioral cost increases modestly. Consequently, the fiscal externality of extending UI rises with labor market attachment. Embedding these estimates in a sufficient statistics framework, we find that the fiscal externality of a uniform extension is 76% larger than that of an extension targeted at claimants with short histories. Moreover, aggregate welfare can be raised at zero fiscal cost by redistributing benefits from high to low hour claimants.

中文摘要

大多数失业保险（UI）系统将福利的潜在期限与申请人的工作经历挂钩，但关于这样做是否具有社会最优性的证据很少。我们利用加拿大失业保险系统中的25个断点回归来研究这一问题，其中当先前工作时间跨越各种阈值时，福利期限会离散增加。利用覆盖1997年至2018年所有失业保险申请的行政记录，我们估计了在工作经历分布上延长潜在期限的财政效应。平均而言，额外一周会使失业期延长0.30周，并给政府带来96美元的成本。然而，这一平均效应掩盖了显著的异质性：机械成本随先前工作时间急剧下降，而行为成本则适度增加。因此，延长失业保险的财政外部性随劳动力市场依附程度而上升。将这些估计嵌入充分统计量框架，我们发现统一延长的财政外部性比针对短工作经历申请人的延长高出76%。此外，通过将福利从高工时申请人重新分配给低工时申请人，可以在零财政成本下提高总体福利。
