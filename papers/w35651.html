Falling Fertility: The Changing Value of Freedom, Fulfillment, and Family

生育率下降：自由、成就与家庭价值的变迁

Raquel Fernández, Inés Berniell, Milagros Onofri

其他主题 全球/多国

Abstract

Fertility has fallen in nearly all high-income countries. People also report wanting fewer children: between 2012 and 2022 the ideal number of children fell from 2.42 to 2.31 and the share of respondents intending a child within three years fell from 28 to 21 percent over a similar period. Why? Using two cross-national surveys that ask respondents about their attitudes and their fertility preferences, both fielded twice roughly a decade apart, we decompose each decline into the contribution of a broad and competing set of attitudes. Three stand out: children are increasingly seen as interfering with the freedom of parents; views on whether mothers of young children should work have become markedly more progressive and account for a substantially larger share of the decline among the tertiary-educated; and fewer people believe that women or men need children to lead a fulfilled life. This last attitude changed most over the decade and is the largest contributor to the fall in intended fertility, above all among less-educated women. The changing division of household work and increasing work-family conflict have minor contributions to changing fertility preferences.

中文摘要

几乎所有高收入国家的生育率都在下降。人们也报告希望生育更少的孩子：在2012年至2022年间，理想子女数从2.42降至2.31，而在类似时期内，表示打算在三年内生育孩子的受访者比例从28%降至21%。原因何在？利用两项跨国调查（每项调查大约相隔十年进行了两次），询问受访者的态度和生育偏好，我们将每次下降分解为一系列广泛且相互竞争的态度所做出的贡献。有三点尤为突出：儿童日益被视为干扰父母的自由；对于年幼孩子的母亲是否应该工作的看法已显著变得更加进步，并且在受过高等教育的人群中，这一因素在生育率下降中占显著更大的份额；以及更少的人认为女性或男性需要孩子才能过上充实的生活。最后一种态度在过去十年中变化最大，并且是预期生育率下降的最大贡献因素，尤其是在受教育程度较低的女性中。家庭劳动分工的变化和日益加剧的工作-家庭冲突对生育偏好变化的贡献较小。
