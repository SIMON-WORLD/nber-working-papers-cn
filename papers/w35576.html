Do Children's Genes Evoke Educational Expectations? Direct Genetic Effects on Parents' and Teachers' College Encouragement

儿童基因会引发教育期望吗？基因对父母和教师大学鼓励的直接效应

Philipp Dierker, Dalton Conley

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Parents’ and teachers’ expectations and aspirations are critical drivers of educational outcomes, yet what causally shapes these expectations remains unclear. In the present study, we address this question by deploying the random experiment of meiosis: namely, conditional on parental genotype, offspring genotype is random, allowing for the unconfounding of unique child characteristics from the more general family and household environment. Using the Wisconsin Longitudinal Study, we employ Mendelian imputation to estimate the direct genetic effects of 18 polygenic indices on college encouragement, net of parental genetic background. The genetic propensity for educational attainment and mathematical ability predict stronger expectations of college attendance, while smoking behavior predicts weaker expectations. Psychiatric traits show no effects after multiple-testing correction, indicating that responses are concentrated on cognitive and behavioral traits. Mediation analysis finds that prospectively measured teachers’ encouragement accounts for roughly a quarter of the genetic effects on educational attainment for cognitive traits, but not for psychiatric traits. Genetic effects on expectations are amplified among high-SES parents and first-born children. Thus, genetic influences on educational expectations are narrow in trait scope and uneven across families, with encouragement serving as one channel linking genetic differences to educational inequality.

中文摘要

父母和教师的期望与抱负是教育成果的关键驱动因素，然而，究竟是什么因果性地塑造了这些期望仍不清楚。在本研究中，我们通过利用减数分裂的随机实验来回答这一问题：即在父母基因型给定的条件下，子代基因型是随机的，从而能够将独特的儿童特征从更一般的家庭和住户环境中分离出来。利用威斯康星纵向研究，我们采用孟德尔插补法来估计18个多基因指数对大学鼓励的直接遗传效应，并剔除父母遗传背景的影响。教育成就和数学能力的遗传倾向预测了更强的大学入学期望，而吸烟行为则预测了较弱的期望。经过多重检验校正后，精神特质未显示出效应，表明反应集中在认知和行为特质上。中介分析发现，前瞻性测量的教师鼓励约占认知特质对教育成就遗传效应的四分之一，但对精神特质则无此作用。遗传对期望的影响在高社会经济地位父母和头胎子女中被放大。因此，遗传对教育期望的影响在特质范围上是狭窄的，且在家庭间是不均匀的，鼓励作为连接遗传差异与教育不平等的一个渠道。
