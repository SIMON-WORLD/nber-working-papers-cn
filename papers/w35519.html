Counterproductive Sustainable Investing: The Impact Elasticity of Brown and Green Firms

适得其反的可持续投资：棕色与绿色企业的影响弹性

Samuel M. Hartzmark, Kelly Shue

环境与气候 未指明区域

Abstract

We develop a new measure of impact elasticity: the change in a firm's environmental impact due to a change in its cost of capital. We find that reducing green firms' financing costs leads to minimal impact changes, while increasing brown firms' financing costs causes significant negative impact changes. Thus, sustainable investing strategies that shift capital from brown to green firms contain a counterproductive channel that makes brown firms more brown without making green firms more green. A mistaken focus on percentage reductions in emissions rewards already-green firms for trivial reductions in emissions and gives brown firms weak incentives to improve.

中文摘要

我们开发了一种新的影响弹性度量：企业环境影响因其资本成本变化而产生的变化。我们发现，降低绿色企业的融资成本导致的影响变化微乎其微，而提高棕色企业的融资成本则会引起显著的负面影响变化。因此，将资本从棕色企业转向绿色企业的可持续投资策略包含一个适得其反的渠道，该渠道使棕色企业变得更棕，而并未使绿色企业变得更绿。对减排百分比降低的错误关注奖励了本已绿色的企业，因其微不足道的减排量，同时给棕色企业提供了改进的微弱激励。
