The Effects of SNAP Sugary Drink Restrictions on Consumption and Welfare

SNAP含糖饮料限制对消费和福利的影响

Hunt Allcott, Amy Finkelstein, Anna Grummon, Matthew J. Notowidigdo

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

As of July 2026, 23 states had approved policies that disallow spending of SNAP benefits on sugary drinks. The effects of such restrictions on consumption are unclear, however, since most households can simply switch to buying sugary drinks with non-SNAP funds. Using a difference-in- differences design with nationwide grocery purchase panel data, we estimate that restrictions in the first 10 states reduced the average SNAP household’s retail purchases of excluded drinks by 12.4 percent (standard error = 1.0) over the first half of 2026. In states that excluded only some sugary drinks, SNAP households partially substituted to non-excluded drinks. Surveys we carried out before and after implementation show that the restrictions increased SNAP recipients’ perceptions of stigma. We combine our estimated consumption reductions with external parameters to model welfare effects given an over-consumption internality and a fiscal externality via public health care spending. In our model, excluding all sugary drinks from SNAP nationwide would provide benefits of about $1.1 billion per year, of which about 70 percent is from reduced health care costs.

中文摘要

截至2026年7月，已有23个州批准了禁止使用SNAP福利购买含糖饮料的政策。然而，此类限制对消费的影响尚不明确，因为大多数家庭可以简单地改用非SNAP资金购买含糖饮料。利用全国杂货购买面板数据，采用双重差分设计，我们估计前10个州的限制措施在2026年上半年使平均SNAP家庭对受限饮料的零售购买量减少了12.4%（标准误=1.0）。在仅排除部分含糖饮料的州，SNAP家庭部分转向了未被排除的饮料。我们在实施前后进行的调查显示，这些限制增加了SNAP受助人对污名化的感知。我们将估计的消费减少量与外部参数相结合，在存在过度消费内部性和通过公共医疗支出产生财政外部性的情况下，对福利效应进行建模。在我们的模型中，在全国范围内将SNAP中所有含糖饮料排除在外，每年将带来约11亿美元的收益，其中约70%来自医疗成本的降低。
