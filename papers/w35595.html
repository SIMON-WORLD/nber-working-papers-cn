NBER Working Paper w35595所在周报所在月度合集

Dynamic Effects of Trade

贸易的动态效应

Benny Kleinman, Ernest Liu, Stephen J. Redding

国际贸易 全球/多国

Abstract

The existence of aggregate welfare gains from trade is one of the classic insights from international economics. However, these conventional welfare gains are static, in the sense that they take existing factor supplies and production technologies at a point in time as given. Recent research has highlighted dynamic effects of trade through endogenous factor accumulation and technological innovation over time, although there remains less consensus about the quantitative magnitude of these dynamic effects than about their static counterparts. Key insights from this research are that short-run and long-run responses to trade liberalization differ; capital accumulation amplifies the impact of international trade shocks; trade can facilitate technology adoption and the diffusion of ideas by changing the composition of suppliers; effects on lifetime welfare differ substantially from those on steady-state welfare, once transition dynamics are taken into account; and market failures can either magnify the overall welfare gains from trade or rationalize activist trade and industrial policies.

中文摘要

贸易带来总体福利收益的存在是国际经济学中的经典见解之一。然而，这些传统的福利收益是静态的，因为它们将某一时点的现有要素供给和生产技术视为给定。最近的研究强调了贸易通过内生要素积累和技术创新随时间推移产生的动态效应，尽管对这些动态效应的量化程度尚未达成共识，不如对其静态对应效应那样一致。这项研究的关键见解是：贸易自由化的短期和长期反应不同；资本积累放大了国际贸易冲击的影响；贸易可以通过改变供应商构成来促进技术采用和思想传播；一旦考虑转型动态，对终身福利的影响与对稳态福利的影响存在显著差异；市场失灵既可以放大贸易的整体福利收益，也可以为积极的贸易和产业政策提供理由。
