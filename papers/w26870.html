German Long-Term Health Insurance: Theory Meets Evidence

德国长期健康保险：理论与证据的相遇

Juan Pablo Atal, Hanming Fang, Martin Karlsson, Nicolas R. Ziebarth

健康与医疗 德国

Abstract

German long-term health insurance (GLTHI) represents the largest market for private long-term health insurance contracts in the world. We show that GLTHI’s contract design coincides with the optimal dynamic contract for individuals with constant lifetime income profiles. After estimating the key ingredients of a life-cycle model, we find that, under a variety of assumptions, GLTHI achieves welfare that is at most 4% lower than for the optimal contract. Relative to the gains of replacing short-term contracts with either long-term contract, this welfare gap is smallest when reclassification risk is high.

中文摘要

德国长期健康保险（GLTHI）是世界上最大的私人长期健康保险合同市场。我们表明，GLTHI的合同设计与具有恒定终身收入曲线的个人的最优动态合同一致。在估计了生命周期模型的关键要素后，我们发现，在各种假设下，GLTHI实现的福利最多比最优合同低4%。相对于用任何一种长期合同替代短期合同所带来的收益，当重分类风险高时，这一福利差距最小。
