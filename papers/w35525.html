Evidence of How Electronic Reporting and Automated Auditing Affects Regulatory Compliance and Environmental Performance

电子报告与自动审计对监管合规和环境绩效影响的证据

Wayne B. Gray, Ronald Shadbegian, Ann Wolverton

环境与气候 美国

Abstract

We investigate whether, by providing better and more timely information, the first mandatory online reporting and auditing program for wastewater discharge releases in the U.S. improved facility compliance and environmental performance, while also allowing state regulators to more effectively monitor and enforce regulations. Examining reporting programs with automated feedback features can also offer insights into the potential for artificial intelligence-based tools to improve compliance and environmental performance. Difference-in-difference results suggest that the program resulted in significant increases in the completeness of reporting and reductions in discharges but also greater reported violations. We find that effects are larger for minor dischargers and publicly owned facilities. We also find evidence consistent with the more efficient targeting of inspections by state authorities towards plants with a history of recent noncompliance, which could be a potential mechanism driving these results.

中文摘要

我们研究美国首个强制性废水排放在线报告和审计项目是否通过提供更好、更及时的信息改善了设施合规性和环境绩效，同时使州监管机构能够更有效地监督和执行法规。研究具有自动反馈功能的报告项目也可以为基于人工智能的工具改善合规性和环境绩效的潜力提供见解。双重差分结果表明，该项目显著提高了报告的完整性并减少了排放量，但也增加了报告的违规行为。我们发现，对小型排放者和公有设施的影响更大。我们还发现证据表明，州当局更有效地将检查瞄准有近期违规历史的工厂，这可能是驱动这些结果的潜在机制。
