NBER Working Paper w35600所在周报所在月度合集

Recall Fluency, Beliefs and Behavior

回忆流畅性、信念与行为

Pedro Bordalo, Nicola Gennaioli, Matthew O'Brien, Andrei Shleifer

健康与医疗 美国

Abstract

The US Health and Retirement Study collects data on participants’ recall fluency (RF), their ability to recall words from a list, a standard measure of memory used by psychologists. Controlling for multiple demographic characteristics as well as IQ, people with a higher RF are more optimistic about stock returns, the price of their homes, and their life expectancy. We derive the predictions of a standard memory model and find that, consistent with them, the beliefs of higher RF people about each target domain are more sensitive to cues, to experiences in that domain, but also to irrelevant experiences in other domains, especially those similar to the target. These effects, in turn, carry to investment and retirement choices. RF emerges as an economically relevant personal trait.

中文摘要

美国健康与退休研究收集了参与者回忆流畅性（RF）的数据，即他们从列表中回忆单词的能力，这是心理学家使用的标准记忆测量指标。在控制了多种人口统计学特征以及智商之后，回忆流畅性较高的人对股票回报、房屋价格和预期寿命更为乐观。我们推导了一个标准记忆模型的预测，并发现与这些预测一致的是，回忆流畅性较高的人对每个目标领域的信念对线索、对该领域的经验以及与其他领域（尤其是与目标领域相似的领域）无关的经验都更为敏感。这些效应进而传导至投资和退休选择。回忆流畅性成为一个具有经济相关性的个人特质。
