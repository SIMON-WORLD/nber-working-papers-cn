Can We Build More Effective Place-Based Policies? Theory and Evidence

我们能否构建更有效的地点导向政策？理论与证据

Matthew Freedman, David Neumark

理论与方法 未指明区域

Abstract

Amid renewed policy interest, the academic literature on place-based programs has expanded dramatically in recent years. In this essay, we review recent theoretical and empirical work in an effort to assess when and why place-based job creation policies succeed or fail. Drawing on theory, we describe key dimensions on which place-based programs vary and discuss evidence on the consequences of different design choices. We conclude with several lessons for the next generation of place-based programs.

中文摘要

在政策兴趣重燃之际，关于地点导向项目的学术文献近年来急剧扩展。在这篇文章中，我们回顾了最近的理论和实证工作，以评估地点导向的就业创造政策何时以及为何成功或失败。借鉴理论，我们描述了地点导向项目变化的关键维度，并讨论了不同设计选择后果的证据。最后，我们为下一代地点导向项目总结了几点经验教训。
