Observability and Social Interactions in Consumption

消费中的可观察性与社会互动

Tullio Jappelli, Luigi Pistaferri

理论与方法 意大利

Abstract

Using a randomized survey experiment in the Italian Survey of Consumer Expectations, we study how observability and commonality shape responses to a €1,000 employer bonus. Making an idiosyncratic bonus observable to coworkers lowers the marginal propensity to consume by 3 percentage points. Conditional on observability, extending the bonus to all coworkers raises it by 6 percentage points. Under a social-interactions model, the latter effect implies a social-interaction parameter of 0.16 and a multiplier of 1.2. Thus, consumption responses depend not only on resources but also on whether income shocks are private, public, or shared within a salient peer group.

中文摘要

通过在意大利消费者预期调查中进行随机调查实验，我们研究了可观察性和共同性如何影响对1000欧元雇主奖金的反应。使个人特定奖金对同事可见，会使边际消费倾向降低3个百分点。在可观察的条件下，将奖金扩展到所有同事则会使边际消费倾向提高6个百分点。在社会互动模型下，后一种效应意味着社会互动参数为0.16，乘数为1.2。因此，消费反应不仅取决于资源，还取决于收入冲击是私人的、公共的，还是在显著的同伴群体内共享的。
