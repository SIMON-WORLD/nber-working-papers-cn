Investing in Mothers? The Long-Run Impact of a Universal Child Care Program on Maternal Work and Income

投资于母亲？普及儿童保育计划对母亲工作与收入的长期影响

Michael Baker, Jonathan Gruber, Kevin S. Milligan

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

We provide new estimates of the effects of universal child care on the long-term labor outcomes of mothers, documenting the life-cycle impact of Quebec’s universal, subsidized child care program on maternal labor supply, earnings, social program benefit receipt and tax remittances. We find an enduring positive impact on mothers’ employment long after children have aged out of the preschool years. There is also a long-run impact on earnings which grows to be twice as large as the participation effect. This arises because of growth in the intensity of labor force participation and a rise in hourly wages over time, suggesting that initial investments in work have multiplier effects later in women’s careers. There is clear evidence of occupational upgrading but most of the growth is within occupation, including the impact of more labor market experience in the years after children are out of child care. We show that, as a result, tax remittances grow over the lifecycle, and social assistance and employment insurance receipts fall. Our estimate of the fiscal balance of these effects reveals that the net present value of the flow of fiscal benefits may recapture between 75 and 117 percent of the upfront costs of the program.

中文摘要

我们提供了普及儿童保育对母亲长期劳动结果影响的新估计，记录了魁北克普及、补贴儿童保育计划对母亲劳动力供给、收入、社会计划福利领取和税收汇款的整个生命周期影响。我们发现，在儿童早已超过学龄前年龄后，对母亲就业的积极影响持续存在。收入也有长期影响，其增长幅度是参与效应的两倍。这是因为劳动力参与强度的增长和小时工资随时间的上升，表明初始工作投资在女性职业生涯后期具有乘数效应。有明确的职业升级证据，但大部分增长发生在职业内部，包括儿童离开保育后几年中更多劳动力市场经验的影响。我们表明，因此，税收汇款在整个生命周期中增长，而社会援助和就业保险领取下降。我们对这些影响的财政平衡估计显示，财政收益流的净现值可能回收该计划前期成本的75%至117%。
