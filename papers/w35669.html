Fool's Gold? How the USD lost its Shine

愚人金？美元如何失去光泽

Kai Arvai, Nuno Coimbra, Marco Pinchetti

环境与气候 美国

Abstract

This paper investigates the determinants of international investors’ portfolio choices between gold and sovereign bonds in an environment shaped by economic and geopolitical shocks. We develop an endogenous portfolio choice model where reserve safety has a political dimension — sovereign bonds issued by the dominant reserve country are more liquid but exposed to the issuer’s sanctions authority, while gold offers sanctions protection at the cost of lower liquidity. Our model implies that US convenience yields fall during periods of high sanction risk, as safe-asset demand fragments along geopolitical lines. Empirically, periods of elevated geopolitical risk coincide with higher gold prices and 10-year Treasury yields. In such periods, the average composition of official reserves shifts toward gold, with countries less aligned with the US increasing their holdings to a greater extent.

中文摘要

本文研究了在经济和地缘政治冲击塑造的环境中，国际投资者在黄金和主权债券之间投资组合选择的决定因素。我们开发了一个内生投资组合选择模型，其中储备安全性具有政治维度——由主导储备货币国发行的主权债券流动性更强，但面临发行者制裁权力的风险，而黄金提供制裁保护，但流动性较低。我们的模型表明，在高制裁风险时期，美国的便利收益率下降，因为安全资产需求沿地缘政治路线分裂。实证上，地缘政治风险升高时期与黄金价格和10年期国债收益率上升同时发生。在这些时期，官方储备的平均构成转向黄金，与美国不结盟的国家增加其持有量的程度更大。
