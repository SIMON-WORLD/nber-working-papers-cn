The Impact of Suicide Hotlines on Health: Evidence from 988 Implementation

自杀热线对健康的影响：来自988实施的经验证据

Benjamin Hansen, Niketana Kannan, Jonathan Zhang

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper examines the impact of the National Suicide Hotline Designation Act, which introduced the universal 988 calling code and earmarked federal funding to suicide crisis call centers in July 2022. We use a difference-in-differences design that leverages pre-existing cross-state variation in crisis line capacity gaps, measured by the number of pre-988 calls routed to a state but not answered in-state. The rollout substantially improved crisis line operations in states with larger baseline capacity gaps: these states answered more calls in-state and increased their in-state answer rates. However, these operational gains did not translate into detectable reductions in suicide mortality. The mortality estimates are precise enough to rule out even modest short-run reductions. We also find no statistically significant improvements in broader population mental health outcomes. These findings suggest that expanding crisis line capacity alone may be insufficient to reduce suicide deaths.

中文摘要

本文考察了《国家自杀热线指定法案》的影响，该法案于2022年7月引入了通用的988呼叫代码，并指定联邦资金用于自杀危机呼叫中心。我们采用双重差分设计，利用各州在危机热线容量缺口方面的既有差异，该缺口以988实施前路由至某州但未在该州接听的呼叫数量来衡量。该法案的推出显著改善了基线容量缺口较大州的危机热线运营：这些州接听了更多本州呼叫，并提高了本州接听率。然而，这些运营上的改善并未转化为可检测到的自杀死亡率下降。死亡率估计的精度足以排除即使是温和的短期下降。我们还发现，在更广泛的人群心理健康结果方面没有统计学上显著的改善。这些发现表明，仅扩大危机热线容量可能不足以减少自杀死亡。
