Unpacking Treatment Effect Heterogeneity in a Large-Scale Dropout-Prevention Experiment

大规模辍学预防实验中的处理效应异质性解析

Thomas Dee, Chloe R. Gibbs, Rebecca Margolit-Chan, Lillian Morrell, James X. Sullivan

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Timely high school completion is a critical step in successful transitions to post-secondary schooling and the labor market. This randomized controlled trial, deployed in two urban, disadvantaged school districts, tests the effectiveness of a mature and comprehensive student-support program on an unusually large scale (n=2,528). While overall effects on graduation rates are small and statistically insignificant, exploratory analyses reveal notable variation by site and over time, mirroring heterogeneity in treatment timing and program implementation. These findings highlight the challenges of scaling interventions and raise concerns about generalizing results from small-scale, single-site evaluations.

中文摘要

及时完成高中学业是成功过渡到高等教育和劳动力市场的关键一步。这项随机对照试验在两个城市弱势学区开展，以异常大的规模（n=2,528）测试了一个成熟且全面的学生支持项目的有效性。虽然对毕业率的总体影响较小且统计上不显著，但探索性分析揭示了因地点和时间而异的显著差异，反映了处理时机和项目实施中的异质性。这些发现凸显了扩大干预规模的挑战，并引发了对从小规模、单地点评估中推广结果的担忧。
