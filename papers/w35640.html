Labor Market Power with Worker and Firm Heterogeneity

具有工人与企业异质性的劳动力市场势力

David W. Berger, Kyle F. Herkenhoff, Jaehun Jeong, Simon Mongey

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

How do firms set wages? How should governments set income taxes? If labor supply is inelastic to wages, firms can pay workers less than their marginal products, and governments can increase taxes without eroding the base. However, the structure of labor supply elasticities in the economy is complex. Recent empirics document variation across workers, firms, and margins (which firm to work at versus how many hours to work). To account for this rich structure of labor supply elasticities we extend the neoclassical model to include a discrete choice over which firm to work at, production complementarities and strategic interaction between heterogeneous, granular firms. In terms of wage setting, we find that novel effects of worker heterogeneity account for 78 percent of the variable component of labor supply elasticities and markdowns, and 89 percent of markdown differences between large and small firms. In terms of policy, higher progressivity makes labor supply less elastic, eroding the tax base by widening markdowns and worsening sorting. These channels (i) produce large declines in earnings following increases in marginal tax rates, consistent with empirical studies, and (ii) reduce optimal tax progressivity by one-third and associated welfare gains by two-thirds.

中文摘要

企业如何设定工资？政府应如何设定所得税？如果劳动力供给对工资无弹性，企业可以支付给工人的工资低于其边际产出，政府也可以提高税收而不会侵蚀税基。然而，经济中劳动力供给弹性的结构是复杂的。最近的实证研究记录了工人、企业和边际（选择在哪家企业工作与选择工作多少小时）之间的差异。为了解释劳动力供给弹性这种丰富的结构，我们将新古典模型扩展为包含对在哪家企业工作的离散选择、生产互补性以及异质性、颗粒型企业之间的战略互动。在工资设定方面，我们发现工人异质性的新效应解释了劳动力供给弹性和加价折价（markdowns）可变部分的78%，以及大企业与小企业之间加价折价差异的89%。在政策方面，更高的累进性使劳动力供给弹性降低，通过扩大加价折价和恶化分类匹配来侵蚀税基。这些渠道（i）导致边际税率提高后收入大幅下降，与实证研究一致，并且（ii）将最优税收累进性降低三分之一，相关的福利收益降低三分之二。
