Beyond Price: Rights Trading in Strategic Factor Markets

超越价格：战略要素市场中的权利交易

Anparasan Mahalingam, Matthew J. Higgins

公共财政与税收 未指明区域

Abstract

Research on strategic factor markets has largely treated the terms of exchange as price. We argue that many strategic transactions instead divide value through contractual bundles in which payment terms and control rights are interdependent. In university technology licensing, we theorize Rights Trading: contingent payment, future improvement claims, and commercialization scope operate as linked components of the contractual bundle through which surplus is allocated. Using 858 hand coded university–industry licenses, we find that contracts relying more heavily on royalty and milestone provisions grant licensees stronger future improvement claims and broader commercialization scope. The results persist across controls and fixed effects and are directionally consistent with complementary instrumental-variable, event-study, and selection-bound analyses. We further show that this relationship is stronger when university technology transfer offices have greater contracting experience, suggesting that contracting capability shapes value appropriation in strategic factor markets. More broadly, the study shows that value appropriation in strategic factor markets depends not only on what organizations pay for strategic resources, but also on how contracts allocate control over their future development and commercialization.

中文摘要

关于战略要素市场的研究在很大程度上将交换条件视为价格。我们认为，许多战略交易通过合同捆绑来分配价值，其中支付条款和控制权相互依赖。在大学技术许可中，我们提出权利交易理论：或有支付、未来改进主张和商业化范围作为合同捆绑的关联组成部分运作，通过该捆绑分配盈余。利用858份手工编码的大学-产业许可合同，我们发现更依赖特许权使用费和里程碑条款的合同授予被许可方更强的未来改进主张和更广泛的商业化范围。这些结果在控制和固定效应下持续存在，并与互补的工具变量、事件研究和选择边界分析方向一致。我们进一步表明，当大学技术转移办公室具有更丰富的合同经验时，这种关系更强，表明合同能力塑造了战略要素市场中的价值获取。更广泛地说，研究表明战略要素市场中的价值获取不仅取决于组织为战略资源支付什么，还取决于合同如何分配对其未来开发和商业化的控制权。
