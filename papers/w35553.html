Sharing the Burden: The Impacts of Pharmacy Sector Reforms on the Labor Market for Pharmacists in Portugal

分担负担：葡萄牙药剂师部门改革对药剂师劳动力市场的影响

David Card, Ana Rute Cardoso, Pedro Luis Silva

教育与劳动 葡萄牙

Abstract

In the depths of the 2010 financial crisis the Portuguese government enacted a series of reforms in its prescription drug reimbursement system. Over the next three years value added per pharmacist in retail pharmacies fell by a third, reflecting a rise in generic substitution and reductions in the markups allowed for prescription drugs. This massive shock led to pay cuts for incumbent pharmacists and the introduction of a two-tier pay system for new hires, with little or no reduction in the growth of employment. We use detailed longitudinal records from the Quadros de Pessoal data base, merged with information on collective bargaining settlements and firm financial records, to study the impacts of the reforms. We estimate that between 2009 and 2022 average wages of pharmacists fell by a third; most of this reduction was due to lower wages for newly entering cohorts. The implied responsiveness of wages to productivity is substantially above most estimates in the rent-sharing literature, arguably reflecting the strong attachment of workers to the sector and the steady inflow of newly graduating pharmacists. Stepping back, we then consider how wages of pharmacists have diverged relative to wages of other health care professionals in Portugal, and the decisions of recent college applicants to enter the profession.

中文摘要

在2010年金融危机最严重时期，葡萄牙政府对其处方药报销体系实施了一系列改革。在接下来的三年里，零售药房每位药剂师创造的附加值下降了三分之一，这反映了仿制药替代的增加和处方药允许加价的降低。这一巨大冲击导致在职药剂师的工资削减，并为新员工引入了两级薪酬体系，而就业增长几乎没有减少。我们使用来自Quadros de Pessoal数据库的详细纵向记录，并结合集体谈判协议和企业财务记录的信息，来研究这些改革的影响。我们估计，在2009年至2022年间，药剂师的平均工资下降了三分之一；这一减少的大部分是由于新进入群体的工资降低。工资对生产率的隐含响应性大大高于租金分享文献中的大多数估计，这可能反映了工人对该部门的强烈依附以及新毕业药剂师的稳定流入。退一步说，我们然后考虑药剂师的工资相对于葡萄牙其他医疗保健专业人员的工资如何分化，以及近期大学申请者进入该职业的决策。
