Pollution Pricing in Equilibrium: Production, Reallocation, and Aggregate Impacts

均衡中的污染定价：生产、再配置与总体影响

R. Andrew Butters, Jackson Dorsey, Ivan Rudik

环境与气候 未指明区域

Abstract

How much does pricing pollution cut aggregate emissions, and at what cost? We develop a microfounded equilibrium model of firm production and abatement that can be estimated from standard firm financial and emissions data, without observing abatement effort directly. The model captures three adjustment margins: short-run input substitution, reallocation of output from dirty to cleaner firms, and long-run clean technology adoption. Applying it to the first 17 years of the European Union Emissions Trading System (EU-ETS), we find input substitution is rigid: firms cannot cut emissions in the short run without cutting output. Reallocation instead drives nearly all of the short-run aggregate emissions response, concentrated in sectors with heterogeneous emissions intensities, like power and cement, or with elastic demand, like refining. Clean technology adoption also responds causally to permit price shocks, reducing firms’ emissions intensity over the five years that follow. Doubling the permit price cuts aggregate emissions 23% in the short run and 31% in the longer run after induced clean technology adoption. In the short run, output falls by 4%, primarily because carbon costs pass from upstream polluting industries like electricity to downstream producers. In the long run, output falls just 2.5%, as clean technology adoption dampens these supply-chain impacts. Carbon pricing thus delivers substantial emissions reductions with moderate output effects, and much of the economic burden falls on downstream firms through higher upstream input costs.

中文摘要

污染定价能在多大程度上减少总体排放，成本又是多少？我们开发了一个基于微观基础的企业生产和减排均衡模型，该模型可以利用标准的企业财务和排放数据进行估计，而无需直接观察减排努力。该模型捕捉了三个调整边际：短期投入替代、产出从高污染企业向清洁企业的再配置，以及长期清洁技术采用。将其应用于欧盟排放交易体系（EU-ETS）的前17年，我们发现投入替代是刚性的：企业无法在不削减产出的情况下在短期内减少排放。相反，再配置驱动了几乎所有短期总体排放响应，集中在排放强度异质性较大的行业（如电力和水泥）或需求弹性较大的行业（如炼油）。清洁技术采用也对配额价格冲击产生因果响应，在随后的五年内降低了企业的排放强度。将配额价格提高一倍，短期内总体排放减少23%，在诱导清洁技术采用后的较长期内减少31%。短期内，产出下降4%，主要原因是碳成本从电力等上游污染行业传导至下游生产商。长期来看，产出仅下降2.5%，因为清洁技术采用抑制了这些供应链影响。因此，碳定价以适度的产出效应实现了可观的减排，而大部分经济负担通过上游投入品成本上升转嫁给了下游企业。
