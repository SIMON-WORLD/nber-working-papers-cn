Childhood Cognitive and Behavioral Impairments and Adult Health

童年认知和行为障碍与成年健康

Hope Corman, Kelly Noonan, Nancy Reichman

健康与医疗 美国

Abstract

We use U.S. nationally representative longitudinal data to estimate the effects of cognitive and behavioral impairments in childhood on health in adulthood. We focus on work-limiting health conditions and fair or poor self-rated health but also consider adequacy of sleep, body weight, and health behaviors (use of various substances and preventive health care) as potential pathways. In household fixed effects models, we find that both impairments are strongly associated with having a work-limiting health condition in adulthood; specifically, cognitive impairments increase the probability of a work-limiting condition by 70% and behavioral impairments increase the probability by 60%. Individuals with cognitive impairments in childhood are 43% more likely to report being in fair or poor health in adulthood compared to individuals without cognitive impairments. The effects of cognitive impairments on health do not appear to operate through insufficient sleep, obesity, or health behaviors. Individuals with childhood behavioral impairments are no more likely to report being in fair or poor health but are more likely to get insufficient sleep and to be obese, compared to individuals without behavioral impairments, suggesting that insufficient sleep and obesity could potentially lie on the pathway from behavioral impairments in childhood to work-limiting health conditions in adulthood.

中文摘要

我们使用美国全国代表性的纵向数据，估计童年期认知和行为障碍对成年期健康的影响。我们重点关注限制工作的健康状况和自评健康为一般或较差，但也考虑睡眠充足度、体重和健康行为（使用各种物质和预防性医疗保健）作为潜在途径。在家庭固定效应模型中，我们发现这两种障碍都与成年期存在限制工作的健康状况密切相关；具体而言，认知障碍使限制工作状况的概率增加70%，行为障碍使其概率增加60%。与没有认知障碍的个体相比，童年期有认知障碍的个体在成年期报告自评健康为一般或较差的可能性高出43%。认知障碍对健康的影响似乎并非通过睡眠不足、肥胖或健康行为起作用。与没有行为障碍的个体相比，童年期有行为障碍的个体报告自评健康为一般或较差的可能性并不更高，但更可能睡眠不足和肥胖，这表明睡眠不足和肥胖可能处于从童年期行为障碍到成年期限制工作的健康状况的路径上。
