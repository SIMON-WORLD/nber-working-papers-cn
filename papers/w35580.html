Cautious Careers: Job Mobility under Incomplete Markets

谨慎的职业：不完全市场下的工作流动性

Alex Clymo, Piotr Denderski, Yusuf Mercan, Benjamin Schoefer

金融与资产定价 美国

Abstract

Job mobility is risky, workers are risk averse, and insurance markets are incomplete. This paper studies how these features curb and distort job-to-job transitions by making workers excessively cautious: they place too much weight on job safety over wage and productivity gains. We demonstrate this tradeoff by eliciting employed workers’ wage-safety indifference curves in a custom, representative survey. On average, employed US workers require a 1.63% pay raise to accept each additional percentage point of annual unemployment risk in a new job. We assess the macroeconomic consequences of our mechanism by embedding it into a general equilibrium search model. Jobs differ in both wages (productivity) and unemployment risk, and risk-averse workers self-insure against unemployment risk through a non-state-contingent bond while searching on and off the job. We find that a complete markets counterfactual would boost job mobility by 12% and productivity by 0.19%. We also highlight a new role for unemployment insurance: it encourages employed workers to accept risky but high-productivity offers, thereby increasing productivity (by 1.3%) and job creation—as well as job loss and unemployment.

中文摘要

工作流动是有风险的，工人是风险厌恶的，保险市场是不完全的。本文研究了这些特征如何通过使工人过度谨慎来抑制和扭曲工作转换：他们过于重视工作安全性而非工资和生产率收益。我们通过在一项定制的代表性调查中引出在职工人的工资-安全无差异曲线来证明这种权衡。平均而言，美国在职工人需要1.63%的加薪才能接受新工作中每年额外一个百分点的失业风险。我们通过将我们的机制嵌入一般均衡搜索模型来评估其宏观经济后果。工作既在工资（生产率）上不同，也在失业风险上不同，风险厌恶的工人通过非状态依赖债券进行自我保险以应对失业风险，同时在岗和离岗搜索。我们发现，完全市场反事实将使工作流动性提高12%，生产率提高0.19%。我们还强调了失业保险的新作用：它鼓励在职工人接受有风险但高生产率的报价，从而提高生产率（提高1.3%）和就业创造——以及失业和失业率。
