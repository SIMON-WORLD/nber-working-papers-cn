Taxpayer Behavior in the Age of AI: A Field Experiment on Property Tax Appeals

人工智能时代的纳税人行为：房产税上诉的实地实验

Justin E. Holz, Ricardo Perez-Truglia, Andrew Simon, Alejandro Zentner

公共财政与税收 未指明区域

Abstract

Navigating the tax system often requires information and judgment that taxpayers may lack. Although taxpayers sometimes rely on human experts, AI could provide comparable assistance at substantially lower cost. Yet there is no causal evidence on how taxpayers use AI tax agents or how these tools affect their behavior. We fill this gap with a field experiment in the context of property tax appeals. We recruited a sample of 645 households in Dallas County, Texas. All households received access to a website providing personalized information, filing instructions, and supporting evidence for an appeal. Half were randomly assigned to a version of the website that also included an AI chatbot capable of answering questions and providing guidance tailored to their circumstances. Chatbot take-up was high: 78% initiated a conversation. Access to the chatbot increased the probability of filing an appeal without a human agent by 9.1 pp, from 41.4% to 50.5%. Evidence from clickstream data and conversation transcripts suggests that the chatbot helped households exercise judgment—that is, evaluate and act on the available information. The increase in appeal filing was smaller among less-advantaged households, providing suggestive evidence that the chatbot widened existing disparities. Our results demonstrate the potential of AI tax assistance while highlighting that who benefits depends not only on access, but also on how taxpayers use the technology.

中文摘要

应对税收制度通常需要纳税人可能缺乏的信息和判断力。尽管纳税人有时依赖人类专家，但人工智能可以以低得多的成本提供类似的帮助。然而，关于纳税人如何使用人工智能税务代理以及这些工具如何影响其行为，目前尚无因果证据。我们通过在房产税上诉背景下的实地实验填补了这一空白。我们招募了德克萨斯州达拉斯县的645户家庭作为样本。所有家庭都可以访问一个提供个性化信息、申请说明和支持证据的网站。其中一半家庭被随机分配到一个包含人工智能聊天机器人的网站版本，该机器人能够回答问题并根据其具体情况提供指导。聊天机器人的使用率很高：78%的家庭发起了对话。使用聊天机器人使无人工代理提交上诉的概率增加了9.1个百分点，从41.4%提高到50.5%。来自点击流数据和对话记录的证据表明，聊天机器人帮助家庭行使判断力——即评估现有信息并采取行动。在弱势家庭中，上诉提交的增加幅度较小，这提供了提示性证据，表明聊天机器人扩大了现有的差距。我们的结果展示了人工智能税务援助的潜力，同时强调谁受益不仅取决于获取途径，还取决于纳税人如何使用该技术。
