The Effects of Ads on Beliefs and Implications for Consumer Search

广告对信念的影响及其对消费者搜索的启示

Jean-Pierre H. Dubé, Ilya Morozov, Franklin She, Anna Tuchman

理论与方法 未指明区域

Abstract

We study how in-store display ads communicate information about a product’s price and quality, both directly through ad content and indirectly through inferences consumers draw from the presence and content of the ad. We run an incentivized experiment in a simulated online store, randomizing ad exposure, ad content, and the advertised product’s price, while also eliciting participants’ beliefs and choices. Display ads shift beliefs about price and quality, even when the ad copy does not directly reveal either attribute. We provide evidence that these belief shifts have a causal effect on searches and purchases of the advertised product. Using a stylized search model, we show that while visibility explains most of the ad effects, belief updating explains a statistically and economically significant share. Casting ad content choice as an information design problem, we show that advertisers may find it optimal to conceal product information even when it exceeds consumers’ expectations.

中文摘要

我们研究了店内展示广告如何通过广告内容直接传达产品价格和质量信息，以及消费者如何从广告的存在和内容中推断信息。我们在一个模拟的在线商店中进行了一项激励性实验，随机分配广告曝光、广告内容和广告产品的价格，同时收集参与者的信念和选择。即使广告文案没有直接揭示价格或质量属性，展示广告也会改变消费者对价格和质量的信念。我们提供的证据表明，这些信念变化对广告产品的搜索和购买产生了因果效应。通过一个简化的搜索模型，我们表明，虽然可见性解释了大部分广告效应，但信念更新解释了统计上和经济上显著的份额。将广告内容选择视为一个信息设计问题，我们表明，即使广告内容超出消费者的预期，广告商也可能发现隐藏产品信息是最优的。
