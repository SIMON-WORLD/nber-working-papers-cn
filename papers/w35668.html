Labor Mobility and the Level of Unemployment in a Currency Union

劳动力流动性与货币联盟中的失业水平

Erin P. Gibson, Christopher L. House, Christian Proebsting, Linda L. Tesar

金融与资产定价 美国

Abstract

Unemployment rates are substantially higher and more volatile in the euro area relative to the United States. We ask to what extent the lack of cross-country labor mobility can account for unemployment dynamics in Europe. Our analytical model incorporates downward nominal wage rigidity and an endogenous migration decision. Firms are unable to freely adjust wages during economic contractions, generating an asymmetric distribution of unemployment over the business cycle. The model is calibrated to the dynamics of unemployment and net migration in a typical euro area country. An increase in labor mobility to that observed in the United States and holding all other parameters fixed would reduce the volatility of euro area unemployment by 28% and return over 1,000,000 unemployed to the workforce. The welfare cost to a typical euro area country of the currency union is 4.1 percent of permanent consumption; increasing labor mobility reduces this cost to about 3.55 percent.

中文摘要

欧元区的失业率显著高于美国，且波动性更大。我们探究跨国劳动力流动性的缺乏在多大程度上可以解释欧洲的失业动态。我们的分析模型包含了名义工资刚性下行和内生移民决策。企业在经济收缩期间无法自由调整工资，从而在商业周期中产生不对称的失业分布。该模型根据典型欧元区国家的失业和净移民动态进行了校准。将劳动力流动性提高到美国观察到的水平，并保持所有其他参数不变，将使欧元区失业率的波动性降低28%，并使超过1,000,000名失业者重返劳动力队伍。货币联盟对典型欧元区国家的福利成本为永久消费的4.1%；提高劳动力流动性可将这一成本降低至约3.55%。
