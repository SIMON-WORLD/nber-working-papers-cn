Tariff-Dispersion Shocks: The Mean is the Variance

关税离散度冲击：均值即方差

Stephanie Schmitt-Grohé, Martín Uribe

国际贸易 美国

Abstract

This paper studies the joint behavior over time of the mean and dispersion of U.S. import tariff rates across goods at the HTS 6-digit level, as well as the macroeconomic consequences of the shocks that drive these two moments. We document that the cross-sectional mean and the cross-sectional dispersion of import tariffs move closely together over time. Using a state-space model, we find that both are driven primarily by common shocks, while mean- and dispersion-specific tariff shocks play a negligible role. Thus, effectively, there are no separate mean-tariff shocks or tariff-dispersion shocks; there are only mean-dispersion tariff shocks. These common shocks account for a sizable share of movements in imports and the trade balance but only a small fraction of movements in output. A positive transitory mean-dispersion tariff shock reduces the import-to-output ratio and improves the trade-balance-to-output ratio; a positive permanent mean-dispersion tariff shock also reduces the import-to-output ratio but has a muted effect on the trade-balance-to-output ratio. Positive transitory mean-dispersion tariff shocks are expansionary, whereas positive permanent mean-dispersion tariff shocks are contractionary in the short run and expansionary in the medium run.

中文摘要

本文研究了美国进口关税税率在HTS 6位码商品层面的均值和离散度随时间的联合行为，以及驱动这两个矩的冲击的宏观经济后果。我们记录了进口关税的横截面均值和横截面离散度随时间紧密同步变动。使用状态空间模型，我们发现两者主要由共同冲击驱动，而均值特定和离散度特定的关税冲击作用可忽略不计。因此，实际上不存在独立的均值关税冲击或关税离散度冲击；只有均值-离散度关税冲击。这些共同冲击对进口和贸易余额的变动有相当大的解释力，但对产出的变动解释力很小。正向的暂时性均值-离散度关税冲击降低了进口与产出之比，并改善了贸易余额与产出之比；正向的永久性均值-离散度关税冲击也降低了进口与产出之比，但对贸易余额与产出之比的影响较弱。正向的暂时性均值-离散度关税冲击是扩张性的，而正向的永久性均值-离散度关税冲击在短期内是收缩性的，在中期内是扩张性的。
